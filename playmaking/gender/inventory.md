# Gender material inventory

This inventory records possibilities within the scope of the [Sourcebook](sourcebook.md). The Pattern selects attributed standing and leaves the remaining material available for authors to develop.

| Material | KHAI type | Source or design basis | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Standing conferred through gendered expectations | Position | West and Zimmerman; Ridgeway and Correll | Offered by the Pattern |
| Reader, person read, or witness who contests a reading | Persona | Interactional accountability; theatrical roles | Authored by the Play |
| Office, committee room, or threshold that makes standing matter | Place | Risman's institutional and interactional dimensions; theatrical setting | Authored by the Play |
| Name on an application, badge, or visible credential | Piece | Application label in Moss-Racusin et al.; badges and credentials are theatrical extensions | Optional carrier of a cue |
| Shortlisting, recognition, or correction | Process | Evaluation experiment and interactional accountability | Authored where the Play needs repeated or rule-governed action |
| A consequential decision under pressure | Plot | Theatrical composition drawing on the mechanisms above | Authored by the Playwright |
| What a person tries to gain or keep under that reading | Plan | Theatrical possibility | Available when it serves the Play |
| Register of attention or discomfort | Pitch | Theatrical possibility | Available when it serves the Play |

The legacy paired Male and Female Positions are migration inputs. Local expressions can include presumed authority or pressure to accommodate where the Play grounds them. A reading can also be contested, ambiguous, or absent. Other KHAI types remain available where the material calls for them.
