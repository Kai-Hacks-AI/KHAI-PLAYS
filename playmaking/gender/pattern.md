# Gender

Gender models the standing a setting can confer or withhold when someone interprets a cue as gendered.

## Ground

Uses the [OPERA Dramaturgy contract](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS/blob/b188b40f09095e8b5a4c1df9f9cb2d5e4ba32736/playmaking/opera/references/dramaturgy.md). The [Sourcebook](sourcebook.md) documents the research and the [inventory](inventory.md) shows the available material. The Pattern can use any KHAI type.

## Root

The anchor and offered object is [Attributed Standing](position-attributed-standing.md), a Position. Link this Pattern from the Play. For each relevant Plot, link the authored files that identify:

- Who makes the reading and in what setting.
- What cue they can observe and when it becomes available.
- What treatment changes because of the reading, and how the Company can make that change observable.

## Open

The Playwright sets the concrete cue, local stakes, readers, and the outcome space. Different readers can interpret the same cue differently. Performers can accept the treatment, challenge it, redirect it, or respond another way within the Plot.

## Weave

Stage the authored cue and give the Company room to act on or resist the resulting treatment. If a required reader, cue, or consequence is missing, identify the gap for the Playwright before staging that connection.
