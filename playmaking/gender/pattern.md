# Gender

Gender models the standing a setting can confer or withhold when someone interprets a cue as gendered.

## Ground

Uses the [OPERA Dramaturgy contract](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS/blob/b188b40f09095e8b5a4c1df9f9cb2d5e4ba32736/playmaking/opera/references/dramaturgy.md). The [Sourcebook](sourcebook.md) documents the research and the [inventory](inventory.md) shows the available material. The Pattern can use any KHAI type.

## Root

[Gender](position-gender.md) is the anchor. The Pattern offers two playable Positions: [Female](position-female.md) and [Male](position-male.md).

Link this Pattern from the Play. For each relevant Plot, link the chosen Position from the Persona's Projection and establish what the Persona projects into the room. A simple statement such as “Owen is a [man](position-male.md)” is sufficient. Add any Persona-specific details alongside the link in Projection. The linked Position remains shared. Keep the projection available independently of who receives it. People may notice it, read it differently, or miss it entirely. Fix a particular reception in the Play or Plot only when a cue or later action depends on it. Where the Persona conceals a different reading, use Shadow for that concealed material. Link the authored files that identify:

- What is projected into the room.
- What cues are observable and when they become available.
- What situation puts the offered Position under pressure and what choices or costs it makes possible.

## Open

The two offered expressions are reusable starting points. The Play establishes where a reading is active, whether it precedes speech, and whether it persists or changes. A Persona may encounter different readings from different people.

The Playwright sets the concrete cues, local stakes, and conditions for action. Different readers can interpret the same cue differently. Performers can accept the treatment, challenge it, redirect it, or respond another way within the Plot.

## Weave

Stage the authored cue and give the Company room to act on or resist the resulting treatment. If a reception, cue, or consequence required by the authored Plot is missing, identify the gap for the Playwright before staging that connection.
