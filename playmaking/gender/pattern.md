# Gender

Gender models the standing a setting can confer or withhold when someone interprets a cue as gendered.

## Ground

Uses the [OPERA Dramaturgy contract](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS/blob/b188b40f09095e8b5a4c1df9f9cb2d5e4ba32736/playmaking/opera/references/dramaturgy.md). The [Sourcebook](sourcebook.md) documents the research and the [inventory](inventory.md) shows the available material. The Pattern can use any KHAI type.

## Root

[Gender](position-gender.md) is the anchor. The Pattern offers two playable Positions: [Female](position-female.md) and [Male](position-male.md).

The Director links the root in Promptbook Knowledge when using Gender in a production:

> Every person has a [gender](position-gender.md).

The Playwright links the expression in each relevant Persona's Projection. A simple statement such as “Owen is a [man](position-male.md)” is sufficient. Add any Persona-specific details alongside the link. Use Shadow for concealed material where it matters.

## Open

The Playwright authors the story dynamics: the situation, stakes, permissions, cues, and choices. Projections are available to the room. People can notice them, read them differently, or miss them. The shared Positions supply expectations and possible pressures for the Company to work with.

## Weave

The Director uses Promptbook Knowledge and the linked Persona expressions when playing the authored situation. Let the Company's choices establish how the reading affects this performance.
