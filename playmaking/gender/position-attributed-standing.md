# Attributed Standing

## Taxonomy

A Position conferred in a specific setting through a reader's gendered interpretation of a cue.

## Owner

KHAI Gender Pattern.

## Has

The access, credibility, responsibility, or degree of deference that a reader confers in the linked Plot.

## Orders

The local expectation attached to that standing. The linked authored material specifies who can enforce or contest it and how.

## Loses

The locally authored cost of having the reading imposed, challenged, or withdrawn.

## Drives

A choice about how to act under the reading when the Plot makes its consequences matter.
