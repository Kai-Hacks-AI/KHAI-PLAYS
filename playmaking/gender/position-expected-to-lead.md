# Expected to Lead

## Taxonomy

[Gender](pattern.md), an expression of [Attributed Standing](position-attributed-standing.md).

## Owner

KHAI Gender Pattern.

## Has

The reader gives the holder the floor and treats their direction as actionable before asking them to establish their authority.

## Orders

Take responsibility for direction when the group needs a decision. A reader who imposes this Position treats stepping aside as evidence of weakness or lack of competence.

## Loses

The freedom to defer to someone else without putting the authority the reader has granted at risk.

## Drives

The holder can use the opening to direct the group, hand the decision to someone else and face the reader's judgment, or challenge the assumption that the decision is theirs.
