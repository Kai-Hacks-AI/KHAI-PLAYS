# Female

## Taxonomy

An expression of [Gender](position-gender.md) for a room that reads the holder as female and applies the expectations described here.

## Owner

KHAI Gender Pattern.

## Has

People turn to the holder to absorb friction and keep the group working. They bring requests and disputes to the holder without first asking whose responsibility they are. In rooms that enact it this way, the attention can arrive uninvited.

## Orders

Make room for the request and smooth over resistance. When the holder declines, a reader who imposes this Position treats the refusal as a breach of cooperation and makes the holder account for it.

## Loses

The freedom to refuse an extra obligation on the same terms as someone the reader does not expect to accommodate.

## Drives

The holder can take on the work, name the unequal demand and risk the reader's response, or redirect the request to someone whose duty it is.
