# Gender

## Taxonomy

A Position within the [Gender Pattern](pattern.md). Its offered expressions are [Female](position-female.md) and [Male](position-male.md).

## Owner

KHAI Gender Pattern.

## Has

A reading the room applies to the Persona. It changes what others grant, expect, or withhold. The reading can be active on entry or emerge during the interaction.

## Orders

While the reading holds, the room treats the Persona's choices in light of the expectations it attaches to that expression. The Play establishes the readers, their cues, and how they apply or contest the reading.

## Loses

The ability to have an action considered independently of that reading by those applying it. The cost lasts as long as their reading shapes the encounter.

## Drives

The Persona can take up the standing, resist its demands, try to change the reading, or act around it. The other Personas' responses follow the Play's local wiring.
