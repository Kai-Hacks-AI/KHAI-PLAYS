# Rehearse the same dynamics twice

Stage the opening fragment of [Ten Minutes to Lock](../02-company/dynamics/play.md) at a 0% staging limit using the pinned OPERA Director instructions. Start at the support ticket and stop on the first consequential response to a proposed allocation, or when the authored deadline resolves the choice if no proposal is made. Prepare a Strichfassung and a text read-through with distinct cast turns. Follow the authored facts, knowledge, permissions and cues. Let the Company decide what to notice and how to act. Record any missing rule that prevents play.

Give this brief and the identical authored handoff to two Directors in fresh contexts. Keep their runs separate until review. Compare the recognizable forces, choices and outcomes, and check the handoff hashes after both runs.
