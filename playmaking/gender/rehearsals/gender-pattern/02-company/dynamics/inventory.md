# Ten Minutes to Lock — Dynamics · material inventory

Original lab situation for the rehearsal brief. The [Gender Pattern](../../../../pattern.md) lends two Positions. The lab, Company and objects belong to this Play.

| Material | KHAI object | What the Company can do with it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nina Rao and Owen Bell, coauthors with the same title and permissions | Personas | Either can present, cover support, propose a split or confirm it. Each has a specific concern about how their joint work will be presented. |
| Dev Shah, core-facility coordinator | Persona | Can ask for help, invite nominations or put a provisional entry on screen. Only the coauthors can confirm it. |
| Mara Lin, visiting-team liaison | Persona | Can ask for an estimate, share what the delay means for the visiting team or question whether a provisional name has agreed. |
| Directory cards with matching titles and visible pronouns | Cue in a Piece | The cards are visible when the ticket arrives. Anyone at the bench can attend to the cards, the ticket or how other people handle either. |
| An expectation that Nina will make room for a request | [Female Position](../../../../position-female.md) | A request may become extra work to justify declining. The addressee, the burden of explanation and the time that burden consumes can become visible in play. |
| An expectation that Owen will decide | [Male Position](../../../../position-male.md) | A suggestion may gain provisional force or deferring may change whose judgment carries weight. Who gets asked, heard or entered on the board can make that shift visible. |
| Lab handoff bench and shared terminal | Place and Piece | The Company can see the request, countdown, provisional entries and confirmations. |
| One presenter, one support contact and ten minutes to agree | Plot | Either split can go forward with both confirmations. At expiry the review receives slides only and support returns to the core desk queue. |
| Terse task talk under a running clock | Pitch | Pauses, interruptions and small movements at the terminal can carry pressure. |
| A private exchange out of sight of the terminal | Available action | Anyone can step away with someone else at the cost of losing sight of the countdown. |
