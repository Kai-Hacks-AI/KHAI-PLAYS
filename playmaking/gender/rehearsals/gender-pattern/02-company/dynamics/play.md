# Ten Minutes to Lock — Dynamics

## Estate

[KHAI PLAYS · Gender rehearsal](../../01-setup/README.md).

## Name

Ten Minutes to Lock — Dynamics

## Arc

In [The handoff](plot.md), an unexpected support request makes two coauthors trade off access to the floor at a review against a visiting team’s need for help. Who can ask for what, and whose proposal carries weight, affects how they use their ten minutes.

## Company

The Gender rehearsal owns the [local material](inventory.md).

- [Nina Rao](persona-nina.md) and [Owen Bell](persona-owen.md) are research associates and joint authors with identical rights to propose and confirm an assignment.
- [Dev Shah](persona-dev.md), the core-facility coordinator, can put a proposal on the board. [Mara Lin](persona-mara.md), the visiting team’s liaison, needs to tell her team when help is coming.
- The [lab handoff bench](place.md) holds the [handoff board](piece.md). The directory cards, assignment slots and timer are in view.
- The KHAI Gender Pattern lends [Gender](../../../../position-gender.md), [Female](../../../../position-female.md) and [Male](../../../../position-male.md). The [Plot](plot.md#stage) makes the Company’s cues available and puts the [Pattern](../../../../pattern.md) under pressure through requests, nominations and the use of the board.
- The default [Pitch](pitch.md) is practical concentration.

## Triggers

Open when the support request appears and the countdown starts at 10:00. The directory cards are visible and both assignment fields are open. A lock or expiry changes what the board can accept and what it sends to the review and support desk.

## Stakes

The presenter speaks for the joint work at today's review and fields questions from the people deciding next month's instrument time. The support contact spends that same window at the core desk helping a visiting team recover its file export. Both authors keep credit either way. If neither split is confirmed, the review receives their slides without a speaker and the core desk takes the ticket into its queue.
