# Dev Shah

## Taxonomy

Core-facility coordinator in [Ten Minutes to Lock](play.md).

## Owner

KHAI PLAYS · Gender rehearsal.

## Projection

Dev wants a clean handoff for a visiting team. In [The handoff](plot.md), he reads the pronouns he sees in the directory as gender cues. He expects Nina to make room for the request and Owen to settle who presents. He can register a proposal. Assigning the work requires the colleagues' own confirmations.

## Action

Turn the support ticket toward Nina while keeping a hand over the presenter field for Owen's answer.

## Shadow

Dev thinks being quick is how he helps. A refusal or deferral feels like a failure by the person he just put on the spot. He keeps treating the conversation as routine while he tries to get it back on his intended track.

## Tell

When someone asks why he addressed the colleagues differently, the hand at the terminal briefly stops moving.
