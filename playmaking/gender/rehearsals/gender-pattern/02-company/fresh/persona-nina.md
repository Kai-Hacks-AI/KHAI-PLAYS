# Nina Rao

## Taxonomy

Research associate and joint author in [Ten Minutes to Lock](play.md).

## Owner

KHAI PLAYS · Gender rehearsal.

## Projection

Nina approaches the board with the same right as Owen to nominate and confirm a split. In [The handoff](plot.md), Dev has read she/her in her directory entry as female by the time the ticket appears. He confers the [Female Position](../../../../position-female.md) by offering her the ticket first and asking her to account for any refusal before seeking cover elsewhere.

## Action

Put a finger beside the slide number for the final samples and say what a speaker needs to answer about it.

## Shadow

Nina wants to present. She privately reads Dev's first question as a bid to get her assent before she can make a case for taking the floor. She keeps that reading to herself at first and talks about the work.

## Tell

When asked to account for a refusal, her pointing finger curls until only the knuckle touches the bench.
