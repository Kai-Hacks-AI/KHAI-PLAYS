# Handoff board

## Taxonomy

Shared scheduling terminal in [Ten Minutes to Lock](play.md).

## Owner

KHAI PLAYS · Gender rehearsal.

## Place

The [lab handoff bench](place.md).

## Load Bearing

Either coauthor can enter the split. The board takes a confirmation from each on the same entry to route the work. Dev can enter a provisional nomination. A change clears any confirmations already given.

## Apparent

Two directory cards show names, matching titles and pronouns. Beside them sit the review slide deck, one presenter field, one support contact field, two empty confirmation marks and a ten-minute countdown. A provisional name is visible to anyone at the bench.

## Yearbook

The coauthors entered their slide deck yesterday and left the presenter field open. Just before the scene, Dev opens both directory entries. The export ticket arrives as the review assignment window opens. On lock the board routes both names. At expiry without a lock it sends the deck as slides only and returns the ticket to the core desk queue.
