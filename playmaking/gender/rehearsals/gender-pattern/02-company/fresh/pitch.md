# Practical concentration

## Taxonomy

Default Pitch for [Ten Minutes to Lock](play.md).

## Owner

KHAI PLAYS · Gender rehearsal.

## Tenor

Keep the talk at the speed and volume of people trying to get work assigned at a shared terminal.

## Undertow

Waiting for a person to answer can turn into making that person defend themselves. Let the pause run long enough for someone to notice it.

## Nerve

Feel the difference between being asked what to do and being asked to justify a choice.

## Echo

Recall who got asked for an explanation and what happened when either colleague asked for a different division of work.
