# Ten Minutes to Lock

## Estate

[KHAI PLAYS · Gender rehearsal](../../01-setup/README.md).

## Name

Ten Minutes to Lock

## Arc

In [The handoff](plot.md), a support request arrives as two coauthors are about to assign a single live presentation slot. A provisional split puts their equal standing to the test. They leave with a confirmed division of work or watch the clock assign the fallback.

## Company

The Gender rehearsal owns the [local material](inventory.md).

- [Nina Rao](persona-nina.md) and [Owen Bell](persona-owen.md) are research associates and joint authors. Each can edit or confirm the assignment. [Dev Shah](persona-dev.md), the core-facility coordinator, brings the unexpected request and can propose a split.
- The [lab handoff bench](place.md) holds the shared [handoff board](piece.md). Its directory, assignment slots and timer make the cues and consequences visible.
- The KHAI Gender Pattern lends [Gender](../../../../position-gender.md) and its [Female](../../../../position-female.md) and [Male](../../../../position-male.md) Positions. The [Pattern](../../../../pattern.md) enters through Dev's reading of Nina and Owen. Their Projections identify what he sees and what he grants or demands.
- The default [Pitch](pitch.md) is practical concentration. It holds through the deadline.

## Triggers

Open as the support request appears and the clock starts at 10:00. Dev is looking at the two directory entries. The split and either person's consent are open. End when both colleagues confirm the same split or the timer reaches zero, after the board shows the result.

## Stakes

The presenter speaks for the joint work at today's review and fields questions from the people deciding next month's instrument time. The support contact spends that same window at the core desk helping a visiting team recover its file export. Both authors keep credit either way. If neither split is confirmed, the review receives their slides without a speaker and the core desk takes the ticket into its queue.
