# The handoff

## Taxonomy

The sole Plot of [Ten Minutes to Lock](play.md).

## Owner

KHAI PLAYS · Gender rehearsal.

## Cue

At the [lab handoff bench](place.md), the review portal is collecting a single presenter for joint work by [Nina](persona-nina.md) and [Owen](persona-owen.md). An unexpected request for live help with a visiting team's export appears on the [handoff board](piece.md). It overlaps the review slot exactly. The assignment window is ten minutes long.

## Action

Nina wants the reviewer to hear a limitation she found while running the final samples. Owen wants a presenter who can defend the model when reviewers ask about the residuals. Either knows the work well enough to cover both. [Dev](persona-dev.md) wants a named support contact before the visiting team gives up waiting. He begins by offering the colleagues different kinds of standing even though the board gives them identical permissions.

## Stage

1. Nina and Owen stand at either end of the bench where both can reach the board. Dev is at the terminal with Nina's and Owen's directory entries open side by side. The entries show the same title, Research Associate, with she/her beside Nina's name and he/him beside Owen's. Dev reads these as female and male respectively. Both coworkers can see the screen. Only Dev knows what he makes of it at the start.
2. At 10:00 on the countdown the ticket appears beside the empty presenter field. Dev turns the screen toward Nina and asks her to take the ticket. He asks Owen who should present, with a hand over the provisional entry field. If Owen names a presenter, Dev enters the name as a working entry immediately, before requesting Nina's view. Either colleague can change it.
3. If Nina declines while Dev holds his [Female reading](../../../../position-female.md), Dev keeps the ticket turned toward her and asks her to explain why she cannot cover it before he asks for another volunteer. She can answer, redirect, challenge the demand or let the question hang while the clock runs. If Owen passes the nomination to Nina while Dev holds his [Male reading](../../../../position-male.md), Dev takes his hand off the presenter field and asks Owen if he's ready to defend the work. Dev gives Nina the floor and treats a renewed nomination from Owen as a suggestion he wants justified before he enters it.
4. After either response all three can change course. Either colleague can nominate either split directly on the board and both must confirm it to lock. A revision clears both confirmations. Dev can revise his treatment, repair an interruption, or keep pressing.
5. On the second matching confirmation, the board displays the presenter and support contact and closes. At zero without it, the board displays “Slides only” and “Core desk queue.” End on the visible result and the Company's immediate response.

## Tension

Nina wants the limitation on the record and is deciding whether explaining a refusal will take the time she needs to make that case. Owen knows Nina can answer the review questions and wants to see if putting her forward will cost them his leverage with Dev. Dev wants to leave the exchange thinking he helped two people settle a logistical problem. The [Personas](play.md#company) hold their private readings and physical tells.
