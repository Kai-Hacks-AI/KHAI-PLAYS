# Dev Shah

## Taxonomy

Core-facility coordinator in [Ten Minutes to Lock — Dynamics](play.md).

## Owner

KHAI PLAYS · Gender rehearsal.

## Projection

Dev wants a clean handoff for a visiting team. He can register a proposal. Assigning the work requires the colleagues' own confirmations.

## Action

Bring the support ticket into view beside the presenter field and ask what can go on the board.

## Shadow

Dev thinks a name on the board means the problem is nearly solved. He tends to spend his attention on getting a name into a field and can lose track of whose agreement he has.

## Tell

When a solution he thinks is close starts to slip, his hand hangs above the terminal.
