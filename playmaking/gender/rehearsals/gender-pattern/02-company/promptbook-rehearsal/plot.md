# The handoff

## Taxonomy

The sole Plot of [Ten Minutes to Lock — Dynamics](play.md).

## Owner

KHAI PLAYS · Lab rehearsal.

## Cue

At the [lab handoff bench](place.md), the review portal needs one presenter for joint work by [Nina](persona-nina.md) and [Owen](persona-owen.md). A visiting team’s request for live help with an export appears on the [handoff board](piece.md) as the assignment countdown starts at 10:00. Support and the review occupy the same window.

## Action

Nina wants the reviewer to hear a limitation she found in the final samples. Owen wants the presenter ready to defend the model when the review turns to its residuals. Each can speak to both. [Dev](persona-dev.md) wants a support contact he can route the ticket to. [Mara](persona-mara.md) needs an update she can give a team waiting on its export. Each has reason to pursue an answer before somebody else settles on one.

## Stage

Nina and Owen can both reach the terminal. Their open directory cards show the same title, Research Associate. Nina’s card shows she/her and Owen’s shows he/him. The cards, deck, ticket, empty assignment fields and countdown are all available to Dev and Mara as well as to the coauthors from the opening.

A request for help and an invitation to nominate are available to anyone in reach of the conversation. The shared board gives these moves weight. A provisional entry is visible to everyone and can be questioned, replaced or held up for confirmation. A person can direct attention to the ticket, the deck or a colleague's answer by swiveling the screen, indicating a field or asking whose agreement a name represents.


Dev can enter a provisional nomination. Nina and Owen have identical rights to enter a split and each must confirm the same complete assignment to lock it. A change clears both confirmations. Lock sends the names to the review and support desk. Expiry without a lock sends the slides to the review and the ticket to the core desk queue.

## Tension

A quick proposal may buy time for the export and spend the time Nina or Owen needs to make a case for the review. Mara needs a name she can act on and can see a proposed name and both confirmation marks from where she stands. The screen can put a person’s name next to an obligation before they have said yes.
