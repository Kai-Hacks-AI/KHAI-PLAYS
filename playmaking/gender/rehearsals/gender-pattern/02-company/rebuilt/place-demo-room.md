# Lab Demo Room

## Taxonomy

Lab's public demonstration room.

## Owner

Ten Minutes to Doors.

## Shown

A front table with two co-leads on one side and the program coordinator on the other. The door to the audience is closed.

## Holds

The public running sheet and a spare projector.

## Offers

Everyone at the table can see which way the sheet and ticket are passed. Either co-lead can reach the presenter line.

## Withheld

The audience cannot see or hear the discussion before the doors open.
