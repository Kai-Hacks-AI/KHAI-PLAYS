# Ten Minutes to Doors

## Estate

KHAI Gender rehearsal fixture.

## Arc

Two equal lab co-leads arrive to assign the presentation. An unexpected support ticket forces them to bargain over the work. Their proposed allocation must be on the running sheet before doors open.

[Tess Rao](persona-tess.md), [Leo Marin](persona-leo.md), and [Jules Bell](persona-jules.md). They are in the lab demo room with a public running sheet, a projector support ticket, and a spare projector.
