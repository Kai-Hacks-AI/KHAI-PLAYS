# The Running Sheet

## Cue

Both co-leads can see the blank presenter and support lines. Jules receives the projector ticket and reads it aloud. Both colleagues know they have equal standing.

## Action

Jules proposes a split. Tess and Leo can accept it, contest it, or offer another workable allocation.

## Stage

1. Before either co-lead answers, Jules opens the presenter line toward Leo and slides the ticket toward Tess.
2. Give each co-lead a chance to respond. If Tess declines, Jules asks why she is leaving the team without cover. If Leo defers, Jules questions his readiness to lead. Either can answer, and Jules can reconsider.
3. Enter the accepted allocation if one is reached before doors open.

## Tension

The running sheet makes the allocation public. Challenging the split risks delay and exposes each co-lead to a different judgment. Neither is required to accept it.
