# Ten Minutes to Lock — Room Projection · material inventory

Original lab situation based on the rehearsal brief. The [Gender Pattern](../../../../pattern.md) and its offered Positions supply the mechanism. The lab, Personas, objects and choices below are original to this Play.

| Material | KHAI object | Action it makes possible |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nina Rao and Owen Bell, joint authors with the same title and permissions | Two Personas | Either can present, handle support, make a nomination or consent to a split. Each brings a different reason to want the floor. |
| Dev Shah, core-facility coordinator | Persona | Reads the lab directory entries, routes the request and controls which nominee is heard first. Has no authority to assign either colleague. |
| Directory entries viewed by Dev before the opening | Cue in a Piece | Nina's entry shows she/her and Owen's shows he/him. Dev reads these as gender cues and converts his reading into different expectations. The displayed pronouns supply observable evidence for Dev's reading without establishing either person's identity. |
| Dev turns to Nina for support, then demands an account if she declines | [Female Position](../../../../position-female.md) | Nina can accept, decline, redirect or call attention to the terms. Dev can maintain or revise his treatment. |
| Dev treats Owen's nomination as the working answer, then makes him earn back the floor if he defers | [Male Position](../../../../position-male.md) | Owen can nominate, defer, cite their equal standing or insist Dev hear Nina first. Dev can maintain or revise his treatment. |
| Lab handoff bench with a live countdown and one shared terminal | Place | The Company can see which task each person has been penciled in for and who has consented. The clock keeps a dispute consequential. |
| Combined session and support board | Piece | Makes a new support request and both assignments visible. Holds a tentative entry, two confirmations and the deadline fallback. |
| Ten minutes to name presenter and support contact | Plot | Both colleagues can agree to either split. If time expires without agreement, both lose the live slot and the core desk takes the support request. |
| Terse task talk under a running clock | Pitch | Allows discomfort to surface in pauses, interruptions and the physical handling of the board. |
| A private conversation, a late intervention from a supervisor, a second support request | Unused possibilities | Could redistribute information or raise pressure. The live clock and the first request already let this Company play the choice. |

- **Additional Persona:** Mara Lin, a visiting-team liaison with a sightline to the board. Ticket progress is her focus. She can react to the allocation without picking up either Gender Projection.
