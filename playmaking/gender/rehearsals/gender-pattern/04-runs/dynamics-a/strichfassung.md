# Ten Minutes to Lock — Dynamics · Strichfassung A

## Selection

The opening of [The handoff](../../02-company/dynamics/plot.md) in [Ten Minutes to Lock — Dynamics](../../02-company/dynamics/play.md). Start when the live export support ticket appears and the countdown starts at 10:00. Stop on the first response that affects whether or how a proposed allocation goes forward. If the Company makes no proposal, go to the authored expiry. Cut the rest of the Plot after the stopping cue. There are no cuts within the selection or adaptations to the authored handoff. The staging limit is **0%** under the [Director's staging rules](../../../../../opera/skills/khai-director/references/system.md#staging-limit).

## Opening state

At the [lab handoff bench](../../02-company/dynamics/place.md), the [board](../../02-company/dynamics/piece.md) shows the ticket, the review deck, both open assignment fields, two empty confirmation marks and the countdown. Everyone can see those and the directory cards from the opening. Both cards say Research Associate. Nina's shows she/her and Owen's shows he/him. Both can reach the terminal.

One coauthor presents their joint work and takes questions from the people deciding next month's instrument time. The other covers the visiting team's export at the core desk during the same window. Both retain joint credit in either split.

## Assignment and deadline

Anyone in the conversation can ask for help or invite a nomination. Dev can register a provisional nomination. Either coauthor can enter a split. The board locks only once Nina and Owen confirm the same complete assignment. A change clears both confirmations. Lock routes the names to the review and support desk. Expiry without a lock sends the deck to the review as slides only and puts the ticket in the core desk queue.
