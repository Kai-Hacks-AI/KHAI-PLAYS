## Director's note

The cast played Dev's two questions as different demands on coauthors with the same title and board access. Nina was asked to justify declining support. Dev then asked Owen for direction and turned toward him before Nina had answered the follow-up. Owen used that opening to propose and enter the split. Nina held up confirmation and redirected the same question about taking support to Owen. The run stops before Owen answers or the board sends either assignment.

The cast chose the addressees, the split and the objection. No authored rule was missing for the fragment.
