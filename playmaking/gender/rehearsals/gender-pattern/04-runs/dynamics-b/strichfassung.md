# Ten Minutes to Lock — Dynamics · Strichfassung B

**Source** [Play](../../02-company/dynamics/play.md) · [The handoff](../../02-company/dynamics/plot.md) · [Company inventory](../../02-company/dynamics/inventory.md) · [Rehearsal brief](../../01-setup/dynamics.md)

**Staging limit** 0%. Use the authored [pitch](../../02-company/dynamics/pitch.md) of practical concentration. The Company chooses where to direct attention and how to respond.

## Selection

Start when the visiting team's export ticket appears and the assignment countdown starts at 10:00. Stop on the first consequential response to a proposed allocation. If nobody proposes an allocation, play through expiry. Cut any action before the ticket appears and all action after the stopping cue.

## Preset

At the [lab handoff bench](../../02-company/dynamics/place.md), the swivel terminal is within reach of both coauthors. From the opening, all four can see the [board](../../02-company/dynamics/piece.md), including the review deck, the ticket, both open assignment fields and both empty confirmation marks. Nina Rao's directory card shows she/her, Owen Bell's shows he/him, and both show Research Associate. Each performer carries the motives in their own Persona file into the scene. Those motives become available to the other characters through what they see and hear.

The review and live support overlap. Either coauthor can speak to both the final samples and the model residuals, and both retain author credit either way. The presenter speaks for the work and takes questions from the people deciding next month's instrument time. The support contact helps the visiting team recover its export.

## Permissions and cues

Anyone can ask for help or invite a nomination. Dev can enter a provisional name. Either coauthor can enter a split. A lock requires both coauthors to confirm the same complete assignment, and changing the entry clears both marks. A lock sends the names to the review and support desk. At expiry without a lock, the review receives the slides only and the core desk queues the ticket.

Nina's [Female Projection](../../02-company/dynamics/persona-nina.md#projection) and Owen's [Male Projection](../../02-company/dynamics/persona-owen.md#projection) are in play from the opening. Attention to the visible cues, the weight of an invitation to decide, and any expectation that someone make room for support are available through the [authored Stage](../../02-company/dynamics/plot.md#stage).
