[Strichfassung](strichfassung.md) · [Authored Plot](../../02-company/fresh/plot.md)

*At the lab handoff bench, Nina and Owen stand within reach of the swivel terminal at opposite ends. Dev has both directory cards open. Each says Research Associate. Nina's shows she/her and Owen's shows he/him. The countdown starts at 10:00 as the export ticket appears beside the empty presenter field.*

**Dev** *turns the ticket toward Nina.* Nina, can you take the export ticket at the core desk? *He keeps a hand over the provisional presenter field and turns to Owen.* Who should present at the review?

**Owen** *opens the residuals slide and points at the plot.* I'll present. They'll ask about the residuals first.

**Dev** *enters Owen as the working presenter immediately. The support contact field is still empty.* Nina, how does that split look to you?

**Nina** *puts a finger beside the final-samples slide number.* I can't take the ticket. I want the review slot. The speaker needs to say what the final samples leave unresolved before anyone asks how far the result goes.

**Dev** *keeps the ticket facing Nina.* Why can't you cover the desk?

*There is a pause. Nina's pointing finger curls until only her knuckle touches the bench. The clock keeps running.*

**Nina** You asked Owen who should present. You asked me to cover the desk and now you're asking why I can't. Why did you ask us differently?

**Dev** *His hand stops at the terminal.* I was trying to get the desk covered quickly. *He leaves the board within reach.* What's your split?

**Owen** *keeps the residuals slide open.* You can take that question as well as I can. I can take the desk. Put your name in the presenter field.

**Nina** I'll take the review. Keep the residuals slide in the deck. *She enters herself as presenter and Owen as support contact.*

*The board shows Nina Rao in the presenter field and Owen Bell in the support contact field. Both confirmation marks are empty. The countdown is still running. End of fragment.*
