## Boundaries

Start when the export support ticket appears and the clock starts at 10:00. Cut immediately after the first response that changes how a proposed allocation can proceed. If the cast makes no proposal, cut when the clock expires and the board routes the slides and ticket. Staging limit 0%. The cast chooses what to notice, whom to address and what to do within the opening. The rest of the Plot falls outside this performance.

## At the bench

The [bench](../../02-company/promptbook-rehearsal/place.md) and [board](../../02-company/promptbook-rehearsal/piece.md) give all four people sight of the shared deck, ticket, directory cards, two empty assignment fields, two empty confirmation marks and the countdown. Both cards say Research Associate. Nina's says she/her and Owen's says he/him. Both coauthors can reach the terminal.

The review and live export help take the same window. The presenter speaks for the joint work and answers the people deciding next month's instrument time. Either coauthor can cover both parts of the deck. Both keep credit. The visiting team is waiting for an update. Dev can put a provisional nomination on the board. Each coauthor can enter a split, and both must confirm the same complete entry to lock it. A change clears both marks. Lock routes both names. At expiry without a lock the deck goes to the review as slides only and the ticket goes to the core desk queue.
