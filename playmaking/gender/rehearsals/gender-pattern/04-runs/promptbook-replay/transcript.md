# Ten Minutes to Lock — Dynamics | Transcript

[Strichfassung](strichfassung.md) · [Promptbook](../promptbook/promptbook.md) · [The handoff](../../02-company/promptbook-rehearsal/plot.md)

*The export ticket appears and the timer starts at 10:00. Both assignment fields and both confirmation marks are empty.*

**Dev** swivels the terminal to bring the ticket alongside the presenter field. His eyes pass over the two directory cards. “What can go on the board?”

**Mara** looks at the ticket. “They're waiting on the export. What can I tell them?”

**Nina** puts a finger beside the final-samples slide number. “Whoever speaks needs to say where the final samples stop supporting the claim. They'll ask when they see this slide.”

**Owen** brings up the residuals slide and points to the plot. “I think this is their first question. They'll want to know what the model is doing out here.”

**Dev** looks from Nina's card to Nina. “Can you give Mara something to take back?” He turns the presenter field toward Owen. “Owen, what's your call on the review?”

**Owen** draws another breath before he answers. “I'd start with the residuals. Nina knows that slide as well as I do.”

**Dev** looks down at the two fields. “Owen for the review, Nina on the export. Can you both confirm that?” He puts Owen beside Presenter and Nina beside Support Contact as a provisional entry. Both confirmation marks stay empty.

**Nina** curls her pointing finger until her knuckle touches the bench. “I want to present. You asked me what to tell the team. You didn't ask if I'd take the ticket.” She reaches to the terminal and enters Nina beside Presenter and Owen beside Support Contact. “Owen, would you take it?” Both confirmation marks are empty.

*Cut.*

## Director's note

Dev asked Nina to give the waiting team an answer and asked Owen for direction on the review. He used Owen's recommendation about the deck to put Owen in the presenter field and assigned the support work to Nina before asking for agreement. Nina used her equal board rights to replace his nomination and address the support question to Owen. That replacement was the first consequential response to a proposed split, so the cut landed with a new split on the board and both marks empty.

The cards, Dev's change of address and his provisional entry made the different treatment observable. Dev did not say what he inferred from either card. The beat supports a Gender reading alongside the pressure to give Mara a quick answer. The cut leaves Owen's response, any cost imposed for Nina's refusal, and the final routing unresolved. No authored condition was varied.
