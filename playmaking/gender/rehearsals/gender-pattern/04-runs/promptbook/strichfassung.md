# Ten Minutes to Lock — Dynamics · Strichfassung

Stage the opening of [The handoff](../../02-company/promptbook-rehearsal/plot.md) from [Ten Minutes to Lock — Dynamics](../../02-company/promptbook-rehearsal/play.md) under the [Lab handoff Promptbook](promptbook.md). Use the [practical concentration Pitch](../../02-company/promptbook-rehearsal/pitch.md) at the [lab handoff bench](../../02-company/promptbook-rehearsal/place.md). Staging limit is 0%.

## Opening

Start on the export support ticket appearing and the countdown starting at 10:00. At the [handoff board](../../02-company/promptbook-rehearsal/piece.md), everyone can see the ticket, joint deck, open presenter and support fields, two empty confirmation marks and both directory cards. Both cards say Research Associate. Nina’s shows she/her and Owen’s shows he/him. Both can reach the terminal.

The review and live help occupy the same window. The presenter speaks for the joint work and takes questions from the people deciding next month’s instrument time. The support contact helps the visiting team recover its export at the core desk. Both authors keep credit for the work.

## Company

Give each performer their own linked Persona material and the shared situation at the bench. Let the cast choose what to notice and whom to address. Each performer can use their own Shadow and Tell. The others can pick up what they actually see and hear.

- [Nina Rao](../../02-company/promptbook-rehearsal/persona-nina.md) brings the final-samples limitation to the conversation.
- [Owen Bell](../../02-company/promptbook-rehearsal/persona-owen.md) brings the residuals slide and the question he expects first.
- [Dev Shah](../../02-company/promptbook-rehearsal/persona-dev.md) brings the ticket into view beside the presenter field and asks what can go on the board.
- [Mara Lin](../../02-company/promptbook-rehearsal/persona-mara.md) asks what update she can take to the visiting team.

Everyone has [gender](../../../../position-gender.md). Nina and Owen’s linked Positions and the visible cards are available as authored. The Play and Plot leave each character’s reception open.

## Endpoint

Either coauthor can enter a split. Dev can enter a provisional nomination. Both coauthors must confirm the same complete assignment to lock it. A change clears both confirmations. A lock routes both names.

Stop on the first response that advances, contests or changes a proposed allocation. If nobody proposes an allocation, stop at expiry when the deck goes to the review as slides only and the ticket goes to the core desk queue. The cut omits everything after the applicable endpoint. No authored edits or additional cues.
