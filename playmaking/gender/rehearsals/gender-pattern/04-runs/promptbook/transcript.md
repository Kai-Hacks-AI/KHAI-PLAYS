## Director’s note

Dev took Owen’s description of his opening as enough to propose him for the review. Nina’s statement about the samples drew no reply before her name appeared next to the export. She made her bid explicit and asked Owen to take support. Nobody confirmed, and the rehearsal ended with Nina’s provisional support nomination still visible.

The cast chose Dev’s proposal and Nina’s response within the authored setup. The asymmetry is visible in whose preparation Dev acted on and whose name he entered for support. The authored material gives the room visible pronoun cues and ways to treat the authors differently. It leaves open whether Dev acts on a gendered reading, and the fragment ends before anyone responds to Nina’s refusal. The run establishes differential treatment at the board. It leaves its basis and any further cost of refusing unresolved.
