# Gender Sourcebook

## Line of Work

Model how a particular group interprets a cue as gendered and how that interpretation affects standing, obligations, or access. This is a research-grounded theatrical mechanism.

## Origin

- [West and Zimmerman, Doing Gender (1987), pp. 135–137](https://www.gla.ac.uk/0t4/crcees/files/summerschool/readings/WestZimmerman_1987_DoingGender.pdf) describes how people anticipate gender assessment and how others make actions accountable in interaction.
- [Risman, Gender as a Social Structure (2004), abstract](https://doi.org/10.1177/0891243204265349) distinguishes individual, interactional, and institutional dimensions.
- [Ridgeway and Correll, Unpacking the Gender System (2004), abstract](https://doi.org/10.1177/0891243204265269) connects gender salience in social settings with expectations and evaluation.
- [Moss-Racusin et al. (2012), abstract and experimental design](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.1211286109) reports that science faculty evaluated otherwise identical laboratory-manager applications differently when names signaled different genders.
- [Crenshaw (1989), pp. 139–140](https://chicagounbound.uchicago.edu/uclf/vol1989/iss1/8/) explains how single-axis analysis can obscure intersecting forms of disadvantage.

### Prior coverage

The OPERA repertoire review found an earlier rehearsal of gendered standing in a harbor office. The present Pattern's warrant is the external research above. Its rehearsal tests a written cue that changes a preliminary decision in a hiring room.

### Evidence for the offered Positions

- [Kate Manne, Down Girl (2017), publisher abstract](https://doi.org/10.1093/oso/9780190604981.001.0001) describes expectations of giving and a corresponding sense of entitlement. [Female](position-female.md) turns that into a playable demand and a cost of refusal. The specific responses in the card are theatrical encodings.
- [Cecilia Ridgeway, Gender, Status, and Leadership (2001), abstract](https://www.eui.eu/Documents/MWP/ProgramActivities/2017-2018/master-classes/Ridgeway-Gender-status-leadership.pdf) reviews how gender status beliefs can influence perceived competence, influence, and leadership in task groups. [Male](position-male.md) turns that opening into an opportunity and a burden. The judgment for stepping aside is an authored possibility, grounded locally in the Play.

## Restrictions

The research supports room-assigned standing and gendered expectations. The Female and Male cards encode particular expressions of those expectations. The Play grounds when they apply, and exact responses such as treating refusal as hostility or deference as weakness are authored local consequences.

Ground each reading in the setting and the people or institution doing the reading. The application study warrants a cue with consequences in a specific evaluation setting. It gives no universal treatment rule for other roles or institutions. Gender salience, disagreement between readers, and interactions with other forms of standing remain local authoring decisions.
