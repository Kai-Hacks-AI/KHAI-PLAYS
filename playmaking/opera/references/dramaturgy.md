# Dramaturgy · TO GROW

Use Dramaturgy to bring a reusable Pattern into a Play. A Pattern describes how something works, what material it offers, and what a Play must supply for it to work there. The Playwright authors that connection in the Play’s own material.

## Ground

Work with Opera’s existing types and their responsibilities. A Pattern can offer a Position, Process, Piece, or another Opera object. Each object keeps the meaning and responsibilities of its type.

## Root

Name the Pattern’s anchor and its type. State the scope of each requirement. A requirement can apply to the whole Play, one Plot, or each relevant object. Link to the Pattern where the Play takes it on. Identify any offered material the Company uses and where the Play fulfils each requirement.

## Open

The Pattern author records what the Pattern offers and what it needs from a Play. The Playwright decides how the requirements become specific roles, situations, objects, and cues. Keep each requirement’s working details in the Play, Plot, or Company member that owns them. Link to that file wherever the details are needed. Resolve any conflict with the authored material before staging and leave performers their open choices.

A Pattern can be composed from other Patterns. Name and link its constituents, explain what their interaction adds, and state which requirements the Play must fulfil, including requirements carried through from the constituents. The Playwright uses the same contract for a composed Pattern.

## Weave

The Director stages the authored connection. Use the Play’s actual roles, circumstances, and cues to make the Pattern visible in action. If an essential condition is missing or conflicts with the Play, describe the gap for the Playwright.

For example, Gender is rooted in Position. It describes how a room reads a Persona before they speak. A Play using Gender establishes the room doing the reading, links the applicable expression in each Persona’s Projection, and uses Shadow when a different reading is concealed. The Plot gives that reading a way to become visible through action. The Playwright decides what the reading means in this room and leaves the Persona’s response open.
