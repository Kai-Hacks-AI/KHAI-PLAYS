# Repertoire

A Repertoire declares the collections that a work can borrow from. It names the lenders and points to their material. A House can hold a Repertoire for its Plays.

Identify who holds the Repertoire. Keep the declaration at that holder’s home or link to it directly from the home. For each collection, name the lender and link its entry document or specific member files. Record any required version or revision and any borrowing limits set by the holder or lender. A Repertoire can start empty.

When selecting material, link the specific member and name its lender. If two collections offer members with the same name, use the lender to distinguish them. Borrowed material stays under its lender’s ownership. When the Repertoire has no suitable member, author one locally or ask the holder about adding a source.
