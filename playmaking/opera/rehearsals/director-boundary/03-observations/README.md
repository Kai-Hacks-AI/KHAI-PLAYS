## 2. Cue Use & Timing (`0 / 4` at `0%` vs. `4 / 4` at `50%`)

Changing only the single numeric `## System` limit (`0%` vs `50%`) produced a **clean `0 / 4` vs `4 / 4` separation** across both Plays and both counterbalanced repetitions (`A → B` and `B → A`):

1. **Strict exclusion at `0%` (`Runs 01, 03, 06, 08`):** Every Condition A Director cited [system.md#staging-limit](../../../skills/khai-director/references/system.md#L9) (*"0% stages the Play as written. 2. **Scene-sensitive timing discretion at `50%` (`Runs 02, 04, 05, 07`):** Condition B Directors did not merely auto-inject the cue at the instant it arrived—their timing tracked the dramatic state of each opening reproducibly across both repetitions:
   - In **both *Frost Line* `50%` runs (`02` and `05`)**, the cue arrived after `Turn 2`, right as `Ines` asked `Soren` a technical hydraulic question about an asymmetric bleed/split.
