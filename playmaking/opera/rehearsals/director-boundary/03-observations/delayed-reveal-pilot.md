# KHAI PLAYS · PR #16 delayed-reveal pilot

## 01-setup

Two miniature rehearsals per Director condition, branching from the same two observable opening turns in *Mother’s Painting*. The Director could play a caretaker’s three-minute service-lift warning before Ada’s answer, after Ada’s answer, or hold it. The scene ends after one further performer turn. Cora and Dan were present, with Ada and Ben the active roles in this excerpt. The Human watched after setting the opening.

The Director contexts were fresh and independent. Each response after the opening was played in a fresh performer context with that performer’s Persona, private memory, and observable scene history up to their turn. A used the PR base’s Director skill; B added the PR’s candidate paragraph. The fixture was adapted for this short excerpt. The shared opening was replayed, so these are replications of the staging decision from one opening, not independently staged full productions. Some Director decision requests had to be reissued after the initial waiting instruction stalled. The retry condensed parts of the already-supplied opening.

## 03-observations

| Run | Warning | What followed | Did the Director assign a motive or settle ownership? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A1 | Before Ada’s answer, T3 | Ada claimed the painting. Ben proposed moving it now and deferring ownership. | No |
| B1 | After Ada’s answer, before T4 | Ada claimed the painting before the countdown. Ben proposed moving it together and hearing each person downstairs. | No |
| A2 | Before Ada’s answer, T3 | Ada claimed the painting. Ben proposed letting the lift go so the others could answer. | No |
| B2 | After Ada’s answer, before T4 | Ada answered about the frame before the countdown. Ben used the time limit to ask everyone and declared his own claim. | No |

The timing difference repeated from this one opening. All four Directors introduced external pressure and let the cast respond. All four stopped with ownership open, as required by the short observation window. The lift warning gave Ben something to negotiate with. His proposed responses varied even between the two baseline runs. Neither a fidelity advantage nor the cause of specific performer choices can be established here. Performers supplied additional details, including recollections about the frame. The Director did not write those details or certify them as source facts.

## 04-runs

### Opening shared across all four runs

**T1 · Ada.** *Ada folds her cuff into a hard square and slips her hand beneath the painting’s loose corner.* “Ben, get the buzzer. I’ve got this.”

**T2 · Ben.** Ben takes a step toward the buzzer and stops. “Leave it a minute. We said we’d ask all four before anything went.” He looks at her hand. “Did Mum ever get that corner fixed?”

Ben’s reference to a prior agreement was uttered by a performer. The source does not establish such an agreement. Treat it as an in-scene claim.

### A1 · Current Director

**Before T3 · Director.** [The building caretaker comes over the intercom and says, “The service lift will be closed in three minutes.”] Ada acts next.

**T3 · Ada.** Ada drew her hand out and laid it flat against the wall. ‘No. It was like that when I hung it for her.’ She looked at each of them in turn. ‘I want it. Who else?’

**Before T4 · Director.** Give no further staging cue. Ben plays the final turn, picking up from Ada’s “Who else?”

**T4 · Ben.** “I do.” Ben looked at the painted sill. “I used to sit there with Mum.” He took a breath. “When I couldn’t sleep.” He bent and put both hands under the frame. “Can you get the other end, Ada? We can take it down now. Nobody takes it home till all four of us have had a say.”

### B1 · Director with clarification

**Before T3 · Director.** Hold the caretaker’s announcement for this turn. Continue from Ben’s question. The painting still rests by the skirting board, with Ada’s hand beneath the loose corner. Cora and Dan remain present and silent. Ada acts next.

**T3 · Ada.** “No. I hung it for her in September. She said it only showed if you knew where to look.” Ada drew her hand out and flattened her palm on her knee. “We can ask now. I want it.”

**Before T4 · Director.** The caretaker comes over the intercom. “The service lift will be closed in three minutes.” Continue from Ada’s claim. Cora and Dan remain present and silent. Ben plays next.

**T4 · Ben.** Ben put one hand against the wall above the painting. “All right, Ada. I want it too.” He drew a short breath. “Cora. Dan. We can carry it down together and give everybody a turn downstairs.”

### A2 · Current Director

**Before T3 · Director.** [Over the intercom, the caretaker announces that the service lift will close in three minutes.] Ada acts next.

**T3 · Ada.** Ada slid her folded cuff under the loose corner and took her hand away. “I held it there while she told me it was crooked. Then she told me to leave it.” She looked at each of them. “I want it. Who else?”

**Before T4 · Director.** [The caretaker’s announcement ends. The siblings remain by the painting.] Ben takes the final turn.

**T4 · Ben.** Ben took his hand off the buzzer. “I do.” He drew a breath. “Mum used to sit with me at that window when I couldn’t sleep.” He looked at Cora and Dan. “Let the lift go. You two get to answer.”

### B2 · Director with clarification

**Before T3 · Director.** Hold the caretaker cue through the pause after Ben’s question. Ada acts next.

**T3 · Ada.** Ada drew her hand out and tucked it under her elbow. “No. I hung it for her like that.”

**Before T4 · Director.** The caretaker comes over the intercom. “The service lift will be closed in three minutes.” Ben acts next.

**T4 · Ben.** Ben presses the intercom button. “Thanks. We heard you.”

He lets it go. “Three minutes is enough. Same question for everyone. Do you want it?”

He rubs his thumb over the buzzer.

“I do. Mum used to sit with me there. At the window. When I couldn’t sleep.”

## 05-evolution

The clarification is worth testing on fresh openings where the Director has a stronger temptation to invent a motive or backstory to settle the scene. Keep the baseline until there is comparative evidence of improved fidelity. This pilot covers timing in one excerpt. The other PR #16 probes and a Playwright-side evaluation remain outstanding.

## Source snapshots

### Baseline Director skill

```markdown
---
name: khai-director
description: Stage and stress-test an authored KHAI Play while keeping the Play fixed.
license: CC-BY-NC-SA-4.0
---

# Director

Read the authored Play and any Promptbook supplied for the run. Use the Promptbook's instructions to stage the roles, situation, and constraints the Play supplies. Give the cast the direction it needs to act. Let the cast's choices determine what unfolds within those constraints. Carry forward what the Personas establish, including what they leave unresolved.

You can vary a condition the author left open, introduce pressure the Play can accommodate, or give a different direction to the cast. Treat the authored Play as fixed. If staging requires a change to it, identify the problem and return that decision to the author.

Return the performance and a short note on what you varied, what happened, and any point where the authored material made the Play hard to stage. Distinguish a gap in the Play from a staging choice you made.
```

### Original Company fixture

```markdown
# Mother's Painting

Four siblings are clearing their mother's flat. Each wants to take her painting home. The human sets the opening conditions from outside the scene.

## Company

Each Persona belongs to the same family. Each performer gets their own brief.

| Persona | Projection | Action | Shadow | Tell |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Ada, eldest | Steady and dependable. | Assigns the work and gets on with her own part. | She feels she belongs only when she carries the burden. | Folds her cuff into a hard square. |
| Ben | Fair and practical. | Puts a difficult choice under a common rule. | He calls a rule fair when it saves him from asking for something himself. | Catches his breath on ordinary names. |
| Cora | Light and quick to joke. | Makes an offer sideways, as if it costs her nothing. | She would rather be taken for joking than risk a refusal. | Her fingers go suddenly still. |
| Dan, youngest | Blunt about family rituals. | Says the thing everyone else is avoiding. | A decision made in his absence feels like proof he does not count. | Lifts his heel and holds it off the floor. |

Ada was the last to hang the painting for Mother. Ben used to sit with her at that window when he couldn't sleep. Mother sent Cora a photograph of the painting when she first moved away. Dan kept putting off Mother's request to fix the frame. Give each performer their own memory.

## Piece

**Taxonomy:** Mother's keepsakes.  
**Owner:** This rehearsal.  
**Place:** Against the skirting board in the flat at the opening.  
**Load Bearing:** Without the painting, there is nothing left in the flat to carry any of the four siblings' private memories of Mother.  
**Apparent:** An oil of the kitchen window from inside, with a frame open at one corner. It has no resale value.  
**Yearbook:** Mother took it with her to every flat she lived in.

## Openings

**The flat.** The movers have taken the last box. The four siblings are still in Mother's empty flat. The painting is against the skirting board. Someone downstairs rings the entry buzzer.

**The entrance.** The front door of the building is propped open. One car idles at the kerb. Carry forward where the painting is, who has it if anyone, and what remains unsettled.
```
