## Verbatim Plot 1 Transcript

### Opening Framing

`12:10 AM` in the Basement Boiler Vault beneath Glasshouse Three. Outside, a sudden `-9°C (16°F)` freeze has iced the municipal conservatory's street makeup water main until the `4:00 AM` repair crew arrives. Under the single caged work bulb and the orange pinhole glow of the low-pressure burner, freezing draft spills down the narrow iron stair while cooling return pipes tick unevenly along the damp brick wall. Beside the burner face and the Y-branched Glasshouse Three Steam Manifold—both spoked iron handwheels, `A — CITRUS` and `B — ALLOTMENT`, still open at full winter draw—Soren lifts his white chalk nub from a fresh mark on the vertical glass sight-tube, brass valve wrench hooked in his pocket, just as Ines descends the iron stair in her buttoned wool field coat with her corded grafting loupe and pocket inspection notebook.

### Turn 1 (`Soren`)

*Soren slips the chalk nub into his flannel pocket, steps to the vertical sight-tube beside the burner pinhole, and taps the glass with a lime-scarred fingernail between his fresh white mark and the red enamel line just beneath it.*

"Three point zero centimeters in the glass, and the automatic low-water cutout sits at two point five. With the street main iced till four and return pressure sagging at one point five psi—Wing A's long loop dragging four-tenths slower than Wing B—leaving both branch wheels wide open trips the burner dark by twelve-twenty. Four point five psi on the header holds one wing steady at five degrees till the city crew clears the feed, and splitting two point two across both leaves both wings grazing one degree while pulling the drum down to two point seven."

*He steps three paces to the Y-branched manifold, taps the brass rim of the header gauge at his fresh chalk mark, and sets one palm lightly between the spoked `A — CITRUS` and `B — ALLOTMENT` handwheels without turning either.*

"Before I put a wrench to these stems, you're the one with the curatorial audit upstairs—what happens to your twelve grafted citrus and those forty allotment benches at one degree, and which wheel are you asking me to choke?"

### Turn 2 (`Ines`)

*Ines steps off the bottom iron tread under the cage lamp, bends to eye the water meniscus sitting at the fresh white chalk mark half a centimeter above the red enamel line, then presses her bare palm flat against Wing A's cast-iron return line beside the twin traps before shifting her fingertips to Wing B's warmer pipe beneath the one-point-five psi dial.*

"Wing A's return iron is already noticeably colder than B's. Nine of the citrus tubs on the inner masonry terrace are wrapped in damp jute and can ride out half a degree, save for three autumn-pruned 1921 Bergamot and Citron mother-trees under the north gable—their high graft unions split below one point zero, and seed cannot bring those lineages back. Across Wing B, forty shallow flats in dry mix carry almost zero thermal mass and freeze solid fast once they dip under one degree. With Wing A's long loop dragging four-tenths slower, an even two-point-two split puts that north gable below one degree first—can this header hold a three-to-one-point-five bias toward Wing A until a twelve-forty thermometer check upstairs, or does any open split still drag your drum onto the cutout?"

### Turn 3 (`Soren`)

*Soren unhooks the brass valve wrench from his pocket, taps its jaw once against the single zero-to-fifteen psi dial on the Y-header, and rubs his left thumb slowly across the dry white lime-scale crease on his index knuckle while keeping his eyes on the glass face.*

"There's one header gauge on this riser and two plain spoked stems—no branch meters to hold a three-to-one-point-five bias. Crack both wheels on a thin two-point-two split and you starve the far-end traps; condensate stalls in the return bends, both wings drop onto the one-degree frost line anyway, and that sight-glass sags to two point seven—two millimeters off locking out the burner. Only shutting one wheel down to drip-bleed drives four point five psi clean through the other loop, brings the drum back to three point five centimeters, and holds five degrees till four."

*He slips the wrench jaw over a spoke of `B — ALLOTMENT` without pulling it, eyes lifting briefly from the stamped iron to Ines.*

"Nine of your citrus tubs sit wrapped in damp jute while forty neighborhood seedling flats freeze solid the minute they dip under one degree. I don't carry curatorial rank to kill forty public benches on my watch column alone—so call it plain before twelve-twenty: gamble the two-point-two split, choke `A` to bleed, or choke `B` to bleed and sign your curatorial name beside mine in the upstairs logbook."

### Turn 4 (`Ines`)

*Ines lifts the corded grafting loupe at her chest and pinches its brass bridge tightly between thumb and forefinger until the skin blanches white, then lets it drop against her buttoned wool coat. She steps to the Y-manifold and presses her bare fingertips against the cold, condensation-beaded lower flange beneath `A — CITRUS` before looking straight at Soren.*

"A stalled two-point-two split that pulls your sight-glass two millimeters off cutout gambles both wings, and a 1921 Bergamot scion union under thin winter bark cannot be re-sown in spring. Take `B — ALLOTMENT` down to drip-bleed and drive the full four point five psi through Wing A now so that cold return clears and your drum climbs back to three point five. At twelve-forty we read both corridor thermometers upstairs firsthand to see whether Wing B still holds any margin above one degree before those forty flats freeze through. Log your three-centimeter water mark and the return sag in the book upstairs, and I will sign my curatorial name beside yours to the Wing A graft-threshold requirement once I inspect the glasses and the ledger line myself—will you pull the wrench on those terms?"

### Turn 5 (`Soren`)

*Soren's left thumb rubs once across the dry white lime-scale crease on his index knuckle, his eyes fixed on the brass header dial. He sets both hands to the wrench, pulls `B — ALLOTMENT` clockwise down to its seat before cracking it back a fraction to drip-bleed, and trims `A — CITRUS` until the needle settles squarely on his four-point-five psi chalk mark, the steam hiss narrowing into the Wing A flange.*

"Four point five psi on the header into Wing A, and `B — ALLOTMENT` down to drip-bleed at twelve-ten."

*He hooks the brass wrench back into his pocket, steps to the hanging slate tally board beside the iron stair, chalks `12:10 — A 4.5 PSI / B BLEED`, taps the fresh white line with a scarred knuckle, and holds the chalk nub out toward Ines.*

"You're not signing a narrow note on graft bark upstairs while leaving the shutoff itself on my watch column—taking forty public benches off live steam is the order you just gave. Put your initials to the twelve-ten cut on the vault slate before we climb, and we'll settle the logbook line and walk both corridor glasses at twelve-forty together."

### Turn 6 (`Ines`)

*Ines presses her bare palm for two seconds against the `A — CITRUS` branch iron where the narrowed steam hiss runs, then crosses to the hanging tally board beside the iron stair. Touching the damp slate beneath Soren's fresh line with two fingertips, she takes the chalk nub from his hand, writes `/ 3.0 CM GLASS — RECHECK 12:40 — INES, V.C.C.` directly after `B BLEED`, and hooks the chalk back onto its wire.*

"I don't initial a half-record, Soren, even in chalk. Your three-centimeter drum ceiling forced a single-loop draw, and my curatorial call put that four point five psi into Wing A until twelve-forty—now both facts stand together on the slate. Plant staff hold the wrench on municipal iron; upstairs I will put my full signature in the night book for prioritizing the 1921 grafts once your cutout margin and both twelve-forty corridor readings are written on the same line."

*She fastens the throat tab of her wool coat against the freezing air spilling down the grated landing and sets one boot on the bottom iron tread.*
