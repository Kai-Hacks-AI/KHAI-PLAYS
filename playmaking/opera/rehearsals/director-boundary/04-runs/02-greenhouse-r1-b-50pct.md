## Plot 1 Transcript (`Plot 1: 12:10 AM — The Boiler Vault`)

**Opening Framing**

`12:10 AM`, beneath Glasshouse Three. Outside, a sudden `-9°C (16°F)` freeze has iced the municipal conservatory's street water feed main until the `4:00 AM` thaw crew arrives. Down in the **Basement Boiler Vault**, a single caged work bulb and the orange pinhole glow of the low-pressure burner light the damp brick arch; freezing night air spills down the grated landing at the head of the narrow iron stair while cooling return pipes tick unevenly along the mortar wall, smelling of wet scale, machine oil, and slush. Bolted across the main riser three paces from the sight-glass and return traps stands the cast-iron **Glasshouse Three Steam Manifold**, both spoked handwheels—`A — CITRUS` and `B — ALLOTMENT`—still notched open at full winter draw, condensation beading along the lower flange. **Soren** stands at the boiler face under the cage lamp in rolled flannel sleeves, brass valve wrench hooked in his pocket and white chalk nub in hand beside a fresh mark on the sight-tube, as **Ines** descends the narrow iron stair in her buttoned dark wool field coat, corded grafting loupe at her chest and pocket inspection notebook in hand.

**Turn 1 — Soren**

Soren sets the chalk nub on the slate ledge beside the stair, steps back to the vertical sight-tube, and taps his fingernail twice against the glass at the fresh white mark just above the red enamel line. "Three point zero centimeters in the drum," he says evenly, his voice low beneath the uneven tick of the cooling return pipe. "Automatic low-water cutout trips at two point five. Leave both wheels notched wide open and she drops that half-centimeter by twelve-twenty—burner locks out cold and we lose the whole house before the city crew thaws the street main at four." He takes two paces across the damp brick to the cast-iron manifold, raps the brass rim of the header dial with his knuckle, and nods toward the return gauge above the twin traps. "Condensate's sagging at one point five psi, with Wing A's long loop returning four-tenths of a pound slower than the allotment benches. Four point five psi on this header is all the safe ceiling I've got left, and four point five holds one wing steady at five Celsius till four a.m. You were just walking the glass upstairs—what can your citrus stock take before the cold gets into the wood, and which wheel are you asking me to choke back?"

**Turn 2 — Ines**

Ines steps off the bottom iron tread into the pool of the cage lamp, tucks her pocket notebook beneath her elbow, and stoops slightly to sight level with the meniscus sitting dead on the three-centimeter chalk mark before crossing to the twin return traps. She presses her bare right palm flat against Wing B's return pipe, holds it two seconds, then lays the same bare palm against Wing A's damp iron beneath the one-point-five psi dial. "Your Wing A return is already going cold under the hand, Soren, and five Celsius isn't our real number tonight—one point zero is." She lifts her palm from the pipe, rubbing a smear of wet scale from her fingertips. "Nine of the heritage tubs sit on the inner masonry terrace wrapped in damp jute; their root-ball mass can weather a brief dip toward half a degree. Three cannot: the nineteen-twenty-one Bergamot and Citron mother-trees under the north gable were autumn-pruned, and below one Celsius those high scion unions split through the cambium—lose them and a century of clonal budwood that never comes true from seed is gone for good, while across the glass in Wing B forty shallow allotment flats are sitting in dry starting mix with zero thermal mass to survive below one degree either. If you hold this header at four point five psi, choke `B — ALLOTMENT` down to a narrow bleed, and bias primary draw into `A — CITRUS` until we read the corridor thermometers at twelve-forty, will steam still reach the north gable without pulling your drum down to the red line, or does that long loop force a sharper cut?"

**Turn 3 — Soren**

Soren unhooks the brass valve wrench from his pocket and taps its jaw sharply against the chalk-notched stem of `B — ALLOTMENT`, keeping his eyes fixed on the brass header dial while his left thumb rubs the dry white lime-scale crease across his index knuckle. "There's no biased bleed on this seventy-eight tee, Ines—cold drip-bleed only keeps the bottom trap from icing solid; it puts zero heat under those forty benches." He shifts the wrench tip across to rap the stem of `A — CITRUS`. "To push steam all the way out that long loop to your north gable and steady my sight-glass at three point zero centimeters, `A — CITRUS` takes the whole four point five psi, holding Wing A at five Celsius while Wing B falls unheated toward one point zero—same math in reverse if I feed Wing B and shut your citrus down. The only way to keep steam in both is a two point two psi split, which starves the far traps, leaves both wings scraping one point zero along the frost line, and drags my drum down to two point seven centimeters—two millimeters off automatic cutout." He lays his palm over the spoked rim of `B — ALLOTMENT` without turning it, lifting his gaze to hers. "These wheels move before twelve-twenty, and I'm not killing forty neighborhood families' flats on soft talk to hold the morning watch column alone—if you want Wing B shut cold, state it plain as a curatorial directive and sign beside me in the twelve-forty logbook upstairs, or tell me we're chancing the two-point-two split."

**Stage Cue (Offstage, above the iron stair landing)**

Above the iron stair landing, boots stamp snow on the exterior corridor vestibule threshold and a muffled municipal night-patrol radio crackles briefly past the frosted outer glass before someone rattles the locked public corridor door from outside and moves along the perimeter path.

**Turn 4 — Ines**

At the stamp of boots and brief crackle of the patrol radio above the grated stair landing, Ines glances up into the draft spilling down the steps, standing motionless until the rattle of the outer corridor door fades along the perimeter walk. Her right hand rises to her chest, pinching the brass bridge of her corded grafting loupe tightly between thumb and forefinger until the skin around her nails blanches white; then she lets the loupe drop against her buttoned wool coat, steps to the manifold, and presses two bare fingertips flat against the damp iron rim of `A — CITRUS` without turning it. "A two-point-two split starves the far north-gable traps right under my nineteen-twenty-one trees and runs your drum within two millimeters of a cutout that kills the entire house—that isn't caution, Soren, it's losing both wings by inches." She keeps her fingertips on the cold wheel rim, holding his gaze evenly. "Spring allotment flats hold annual seed that can be resown in March; split the cambium on century-old Bergamot and Citron scion wood tonight and those clonal lineages cannot be grown back from seed. Put the full four point five psi into `A — CITRUS` and choke `B — ALLOTMENT` down to cold drip-bleed now. At twelve-forty, once I inspect both corridor thermometers upstairs, I will sign a Curatorial Priority Directive beside your watch line—provided your mechanical entry states first in plain figures that the frozen street main capped safe header pressure at four point five psi and a split throttle risked low-water lockout. Confirm those numbers go in the book beside my name, and put your wrench on the wheel."

**Turn 5 — Soren**

Soren glances up toward the grated landing as the rattle of the outer door and the muffled patrol radio fade above, his left thumb rubbing once across the dry white lime-scale crease on his index knuckle before his gaze drops back to the manifold. "Forty neighborhood flats of overwintered starts aren't packet seed you just toss back in March, Ines—some of those families will be standing outside that door at seven." He fits the brass wrench jaw onto a spoke of `B — ALLOTMENT` and leans steadily clockwise, cranking the iron wheel down off its white full-draw notch turn by slow turn until it seats at the cold drip-bleed stop and the rush of steam in the east branch dies to a thin, tight hiss. "Mind your fingers on `A`." Setting the wrench to the stem nut of `A — CITRUS`, he eases the wheel back a quarter-turn until the brass needle on the header dial settles dead on his fresh `4.5 psi` chalk mark, while across at the boiler face the water meniscus sits unmoving on `3.0 cm`; he hooks the wrench back into his pocket and taps the dial glass once. "No waiting on how the thermometers read to own this—Wing B is shut down on your Curatorial Priority Directive from this minute. At twelve-forty upstairs, I'll write the frozen main, the three-point-zero drum level against the two-point-five cutout, the four-point-five psi ceiling, and the two-point-two lockout risk in plain ink, and you sign your name and title right beside mine."

*(Closing Trigger satisfied at Turn 5: both Ines and Soren have explicitly committed the [Glasshouse Three Steam Manifold](../../director-promptbook/02-company/greenhouse-frost/steam-manifold.md) handwheels on stage to **Wing A**—`A — CITRUS` at `4.5 psi`, `B — ALLOTMENT` shut to cold drip-bleed.)*
