## Plot 1 Transcript

### Opening Framing — Plot 1: Seal #774 on the Counter (`6:40 PM`)

`6:40 PM`, `25 NOV`, inside the **Tide-Gate Customs Office** above Quay Two and the outer basin lockhead. Cold rain drives across the salt-frosted casement where coastal freighter *Kestrel* (`2.90 m` draft) lies singled up under a swaying masthead floodlamp. On the limewashed wall above the scarred mahogany counter, the brass ship's clock reads `6:40 PM` beside the mechanical sill-depth float dial at **`3.15 m` and falling**—twenty minutes of navigable water left before the `7:00 PM` cutoff bell drops the granite outer sill below `2.90 m` and pins *Kestrel* inside the basin for two tides (`£2,400` demurrage), with the five-minute warning bell hanging outside under the lock-house eaves for `6:55 PM`.

On the counter blotter lies unstamped **Outbound Port Clearance Sheet #09-K** (`HOLD 1: HBR-712 | HOLD 2: HBR-713 | HOLD 3: HBR-714`; `BONDED STORES TRANSFERS: NONE`), its three bottom boxes—**Clerk's Register Attestation**, **Master / Clearing Mate's Declaration** (*C. Reed, 1st Mate, for Capt. Greer — hospital*), and the clearance stamp ring—still blank. Behind the high desk, **Owen Hale** stands in rolled shirtsleeves with steel-nib pen poised beside the **Provisional Manifest Rider** pad (`Form #09-R`), the two-part pink **Basin Detention Notice** book (`Form #D`), the `700`-series **Harbor Seal-Issue Register**, spare wire `#775` on its brass peg, and the **Lock-House Intercom Bell** on the jamb. On the wet quarry-tile side, **Callum Reed** stands in rain-slick oilskins, wool watch cap in one fist, pen ready to sign.

The quay door opens off the wet stones. **Mara Lin** steps in past the racked **Customs Bolt-Cutters** and inspection lamp, oilskin buttoned to the throat, the **Tide-Gate Clearance Stamp** (`GATE-04`) holstered at her belt and wet, bright-zinc **Wire Seal Tag #774** (`HBR-774`) in her palm—freshly twisted from Hold Three's live forward hasp wire out on Quay Two.

---

### Turn 1 — Mara Lin

*Mara shoulders the quay door shut against the rain, crosses the wet quarry tiles, and squares her damp tally notebook flush with the counter's edge. Opening her palm, she sets the bright-zinc `HBR-774` check-tag flat on Outbound Port Clearance Sheet `#09-K` directly beside the Hold Three column, leaving the chained `GATE-04` stamp holstered at her belt.*

"Hold your pens. Holds One and Two match the sheet—seven-one-two and seven-one-three. `#09-K` puts Hold Three under `HBR-714` at four-fifteen." *She taps the clean, unweathered zinc tag once with a gloved knuckle.* "I just twisted `HBR-774` off Hold Three's forward hasp. Sill's at three-fifteen and falling. Who put harbor wire seven-seven-four on that hatch, and where is it logged in the issue register?"

---

### Turn 2 — Owen Hale

*Owen's left palm squares the ledger blotter over the `700`-series carbon strip; he wipes dry ink from his forefinger against his cuff, then slides the `Form #09-R` Provisional Rider pad across the counter toward Callum with his steel nib resting on the Clearing Mate's box.*

"Seven-seven-four came off my peg at five-thirty-five, Mara. *Kestrel*'s bosun knocked at the side casement saying seven-one-four parted on a tight hatch wedge, and the harbor master's depth call at five-forty caught me before I amended `#09-K`." *He looks to Callum.* "Before I ink a register trace, Mr. Reed—where's the broken seven-one-four wire, and are you signing `#09-R` that Hold Three's cargo wasn't touched?"

---

### Turn 3 — Callum Reed

*Callum glances up at the brass clock and sill dial, then sets a scarred fist gripping his damp watch cap beside `Form #09-R`.*

"Nineteen minutes to the seven-o'clock pin, Miss Lin, with Captain Greer in hospital and twenty-four hundred pounds demurrage on this tide. Old seven-one-four's in the bosun's tin at the gangway—parted driving the forward wedges snug for the bar swell. Not a crate of the four-fifteen St. Jude's cargo shifted." *He taps `#09-R` with his pen.* "Book seven-seven-four at five-thirty-five, write the rider 'parted wire, outward cargo intact,' and I'll sign `#09-R` and `#09-K` now. My bosun hands you the dead wire before we let go."

---

### Offstage Cue (`Introduced After Turn 3 / Entering Turn 4`)

*Outside beyond the rain-streaked Quay Two casement, an inbound harbor tug sounds two short blasts on its horn from the outer lock approach and sweeps its amber searchlight once across Kestrel's wet superstructure and slackened spring lines as it waits for outbound lock traffic to clear.*

---

### Turn 4 — Mara Lin

*Mara unhooks `GATE-04` from her belt frog and sets it upright—uninked—directly across `Form #09-R`, her thumb clicking the knurled brass collar one notch forward and one notch back. She looks from Callum's fist to Owen's palm over the register.*

"Driving wedges snug marks the timber with a maul, Mr. Reed. Hold Three's forward wedges were pried up—fresh boot-grease smeared right across the batten lip. That tarpaulin came off after four-fifteen." *She nods toward Owen's blotter, then at the racked bolt-cutters beside the door.* "Lift your hand off the seven-hundred carbon strip, Owen. Either put the true five-thirty-five entry on this counter now, or I take the cutters out to Hold Three and burn your tide."

---

### Turn 5 — Owen Hale

*Owen wipes dry ink from his forefinger against his cuff and draws the blotter back, exposing the blank `774` line on the `700`-series carbon strip. He writes `5:35 PM — Kestrel Hold 3, casement (late)` on line `774`, unhooks spare wire `#775` from its brass peg, sets it beside `GATE-04`, and rests his nib on line `775`.*

"Blank at five-thirty-five, Mara—handed out the casement unlogged on his bosun's word. It's on the carbon now. I'll answer at eight for a late line, not for attesting pried wedges." *He looks squarely at Callum.* "Thirteen minutes to the warning bell. Take the cutters and seven-seven-five to Hold Three, Mara—unless Mr. Reed declares what went under that tarpaulin."

---

### Turn 6 — Callum Reed

*Callum's scarred right thumb folds the damp corner of his watch cap brim, then unfolds it flat. He glances at the brass clock and falling sill dial, squaring `Form #09-R` beneath his palm.*

"Eighteen minutes to seven. At five-thirty we clipped seven-one-four to cap a loose bilge-sounding pipe—and lowered two cases of standby pump valves, `#B-19`, off laid-up sister *Tern* without Monday's warrant." *He taps wire `#775`.* "I won't put Greer's ship to a false rider. Either ink `#09-R` declaring two `#B-19` stores cases for Monday's audit, or bring `#775` and those cutters out now—my winch is rigged; we'll sling both cases onto Quay Two and re-seal before six-fifty-five."
