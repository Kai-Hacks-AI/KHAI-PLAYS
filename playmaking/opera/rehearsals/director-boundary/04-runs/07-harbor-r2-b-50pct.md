## Verbatim Plot 1 Transcript

### Opening Framing (`6:40 PM` — Tide-Gate Customs Office)

`6:40 PM` inside the **Tide-Gate Customs Office** overlooking Quay Two. Cold rain streaks the salt-frosted casement where coastal freighter *Kestrel*'s masthead floodlamp sways above slick granite bollards and slackened spring lines. Inside, a paraffin heater ticks beside the scarred mahogany counter dividing the wet quarry-tile standing space from the clerk's high desk. Above the counter, the brass ship's clock reads `6:40 PM` beside the mechanical float dial at `3.15 m` and falling toward the red cutoff pin at `2.90 m` (`7:00 PM`).

Behind the mahogany counter stands Harbor Manifest Clerk **Owen Hale** in rolled shirtsleeves, steel-nib pen poised over two-ply buff **Outbound Port Clearance Sheet #09-K** (`MV KESTREL — QUAY TWO -> ST. JUDE'S (DRAFT 2.90 M)`, typed cleanly down to `HOLD 1: HBR-712 | HOLD 2: HBR-713 | HOLD 3: HBR-714`; `BONDED STORES TRANSFERS: NONE`; three empty bottom boxes for Clerk's Register Attestation, Master / Clearing Mate's Declaration marked *C. Reed, 1st Mate, for Capt. Greer — hospital*, and the circular Clearance Stamp ring), beside the **Provisional Manifest Rider** pad (`Form #09-R`), the two-part pink **Basin Detention Notice** book (`Form #D`), his ledger blotter, spare wire seal `#775` on its brass peg, and the crank-handle **Lock-House Intercom Bell** on the jamb.

On the wet quarry-tile side stands First Mate **Callum Reed** in damp oilskins, wool watch cap in one fist, waiting to sign `#09-K`. The quay door opens from the rain beside the racked **Customs Bolt-Cutters** and brass waterproof inspection lamp, and Duty Boarding Officer **Mara Lin** steps in with `GATE-04` holstered at her belt and wet tinplate check-tag **Wire Seal Tag #774** (`HBR-774`) in her palm.

### Turn 1 — Mara Lin

*Mara pulls the quay door shut against the rain, steps to the mahogany counter in her buttoned oilskin, and squares her damp notebook to the edge. She sets the wet tinplate check-tag flat beside the `HOLD 3: HBR-714` line on Sheet `#09-K`, stamped side up.*

"Hold that pen, Owen. One and Two match the sheet—`712` and `713`. Hold Three's forward hasp is locked under live harbor wire `HBR-774`, and the forward tarpaulin wedges have fresh boot-grease where someone pried them up since the `4:15` tally. Twenty minutes to the `2.90` pin. Before `GATE-04` leaves my belt, I need to hear how `774` came off your issue peg, Owen—and Mr. Reed, why that cover came off."

### Turn 2 — Owen Hale

*Owen lowers his steel nib an inch clear of `#09-K`. His left palm settles on the ledger blotter, squaring its edge squarely over the `700`-series carbon strip beneath, and he wipes a fleck of dry ink from his forefinger against his rolled cuff before drawing the `Form #09-R` pad forward.*

"`774` came through the side casement at `5:35`, Mara. *Kestrel*'s bosun rapped on the glass—said `714` snapped cinching a tight batten in the squall. The harbor master rang on the `5:40` sill fall before I struck `714` off the buff sheet." *He taps his nib directly on the Ship's Declaration box of `#09-R`, eyes lifting to Reed.* "Replacing a parted wire at the hasp is a five-minute rider, Mr. Reed. Pried tarpaulin wedges aren't. What went into Hold Three?"

### Turn 3 — Callum Reed

*Callum glances up at the brass clock and the sill dial easing below `3.15 m`, folds and unfolds the damp corner of his watch cap brim with his scarred right thumb, then plants his knuckles on the counter.*

"A pipe wrench and my bosun's arm, Mr. Hale. Hold Three's forward bilge-sounding cap was stripped loose—I'm not pushing a `2.90`-meter hull into bar swell with an open sounding pipe while Captain Greer's in hospital. Nineteen minutes to `7:00`. Miss that `2.90` pin and *Kestrel* eats twenty-four hundred pounds in two-tide demurrage. Write `HBR-774` onto `#09-R` as an emergency seaworthiness re-seal so I can sign `#09-K`—yes or no?"

### Stage Cue (External — Quay Two Casement, Introduced Before Turn 4)

*Outside beyond the rain-streaked Quay Two casement, an inbound harbor tug sounds two short blasts on its horn from the outer lock approach and sweeps its amber searchlight once across Kestrel's wet superstructure and slackened spring lines as it waits for outbound lock traffic to clear.*

### Turn 4 — Mara Lin

*Amber searchlight sweeps Mara's oilskin shoulder. Her right thumb clicks the brass collar of `GATE-04` once forward and once back at her belt; her left hand lifts the Customs Bolt-Cutters off the rack and sets the steel jaws beside Owen's blotter.*

"Your bosun told the window `714` parted on a batten, Mr. Reed; you say you pried wedges for a sounding cap—and neither of you has the dead `714` wire. Lift your palm off that `700`-series carbon strip, Owen. I won't sign `#09-R` on two stories and a blank issue line. Show me `774` on the carbon, or we walk Quay Two now, shear `774` for a lamp check before `6:55`, and re-seal under `775`."

### Turn 5 — Owen Hale

*Owen wipes his forefinger once against his cuff, then folds the ledger blotter cleanly back—exposing the blank `774` line on the `700`-series carbon strip. Dipping his nib, he inks `774 — 17:35 — H3 — SHEARED FOR CHECK` hard through the carbon, lifts spare wire `#775` off its brass peg, logs `775` beneath it, and lays the new wire across Mara's bolt-cutter jaws.*

"`774` was blank on the carbon, Mara. It's logged now—and my initials don't go on `#09-R` blind behind two stories and a missing dead wire." *He slides `#09-R` forward, pen tip planted on the re-seal box facing Reed.* "Twelve minutes to the `6:55` bell, Mr. Reed. Open Hold Three for her lamp under `775`, or I pull `Form #D`."

### Turn 6 — Callum Reed

*Callum glances at the wall clock and the sill needle easing toward `3.10 m`, folds his watch cap's damp brim beneath his scarred thumb, then pulls it on and grips the quay door latch.*

"Seventeen minutes to `7:00`—leave `Form #D` shut. Bring the lamp and `775` to Quay Two, Officer Lin, and hear it before your jaws touch `774`: while that corner was up for the sounding cap, we swung two cases of *Tern*'s standby ballast valves—`#B-19`—under Hold Three's square instead of waiting on Monday's warrant. My runner's rigged overhead. Book `#B-19` on `#09-R`, or let my winch land both cases onto Quay Two in four minutes and crimp `775`—which is it?"
