# Director Clean-Room Note on `promptbook.md`

## 1. Keeping `promptbook.md` Play-Agnostic

To ensure a single `02-company/promptbook.md` can govern completely different ensemble chamber Plays unchanged, every rule is expressed exclusively through KHAI structural primitives and observable theatrical mechanics rather than world content:

- **Role and cast cardinality abstraction:** Performers are addressed as "two or more performer agents" inhabiting authored `Personas`, `Positions`, and `Plans`, avoiding fixed cast counts (e.g., duet vs. quartet), role titles, or relationship specifics.
- **Component optionality:** Setting, object, procedural, and tonal elements are referenced conditionally ("its authored situation, Place, present Personas, and any supplied Pieces or Processes", "any active Pitch", "every active Plan", "recorded Performance brought in on a Trigger") so Plays that omit a Piece, Process, Pitch, or loaned Performance still conform cleanly without dead instructions.
- **Venue-neutral physical language:** Instead of indoor-only idioms ("in the room", "behind closed doors"), observable boundaries are framed around "the shared Place" and "Plots where that Persona is present," fitting interior, exterior, or shifting multi-Place chamber works alike.
- **Situation-and-cue routing:** Intra-Plot turn-taking (`~4–6` brisk cast turns via *By a cue* or *By the scene*) and inter-Plot advancement (authored `Triggers` carrying established facts, Piece/Process states, and unresolved `Stakes`) depend entirely on what each Play supplies in its `Arc` and `Plots`, never on hardcoded dramatic beats or time limits.
- **Read-only outcome accounting:** End-of-run `Plan` assessment writes to the run's output/closing report (or lodged `transcript.md` + `performance.md` `Aftermath` when captured for loan) while explicitly forbidding edits to authored `Plan` or Company files, keeping shared Company assets clean across repeated runs and across multiple Plays.

## 2. Ambiguities and Tensions in `khai-director/references/promptbook.md` and the `HACKS` Guides

Reading only `khai-director/SKILL.md` and `khai-director/references/*.md` surfaced several ambiguities and structural tensions:

1. **Heading hierarchy and empty-chapter convention (`promptbook.md` vs. `performance.md`):**
   `performance.md` specifies an explicit `# Performance — TO LOAN` title with `##` subheadings, whereas `promptbook.md` lists the five HACKS chapters (`Human`, `Agent`, `Collaboration`, `Knowledge`, `System`) as links to files that each use `#` H1 headings, and says *"Leave a HACKS chapter empty if it adds nothing."* It is slightly ambiguous whether a Promptbook should use `# Promptbook` + `##` chapters or five top-level `#` headings, and whether an unused chapter should remain as an empty heading or be omitted. (Using `# Promptbook` with `##` chapters in fixed order matches `performance.md`.)
2. **Participatory framing vs. silent-witness runs (`promptbook.md`, `human.md`, `collaboration.md`):**
   `promptbook.md` asks *"how people participate"*, `human.md` asks *"how they join if they take part"*, and `collaboration.md` opens with *"Set how the human and agents work together."* For a silent-witness performance, "collaboration" between Human and Company is strictly pre-run framing (selecting Play, Pitch, and 0% Promptbook), silent observation during play, and post-run receipt/blocker escalation—so `Collaboration` has to explicitly specify *non-intervention* during play to satisfy its mandate cleanly.
3. **Director vs. Cast Performers collapsed under `Agent` (`agent.md`):**
   `agent.md` frames `Agent` primarily around role-play and narration (*"inhabit roles, speak, act, observe, narrate, or carry out the conditions they are given"*), without distinguishing the **Director agent** (who frames Plots, enforces context isolation, tracks Triggers, and assesses outcomes) from **Cast performer agents** (who must *not* narrate omnisciently or self-direct). Separating those two agent roles explicitly in `## Agent` was necessary to prevent role/orchestrator bleed.
4. **Narrow definition of "By a cue" and missing inter-Plot handoff guidance (`collaboration.md` vs. `SKILL.md`):**
   `collaboration.md` defines speaker selection only inside a single scene and narrows **By a cue** specifically to *"waits for a process to reach its cue, then responds,"* whereas `promptbook.md` (*"what cues a response or handoff"*) and `system.md` (*"supplied roles, situation, and cues"*) use *cue* more broadly, and `SKILL.md` relies heavily on `Arc` and `Triggers` for Plot-to-Plot handoffs. `collaboration.md` says nothing about Plot turn budgets or `Trigger`-driven transitions between Plots.
5. **Tension between `Pitch` tuning (`SKILL.md`) and the `0%` staging limit (`system.md`):**
   `SKILL.md` directs the Director to *"Use any active Pitch to tune pacing, attention, and staging,"* while `system.md` defines **0%** as *"stages the Play as written. The Director uses the supplied roles, situation, and cues"* and reserves *"vary staging and timing... and give different direction to the cast"* for **50%**. To reconcile them at **0%**, `promptbook.md` treats an active `Pitch` as part of the supplied Play/Human framing that focuses pacing and attention *within* the authored situation rather than licensing changes to authored facts, constraints, or cues.
6. **Cross-skill primitives and non-loaned `Plan` assessment location (`SKILL.md`, `knowledge.md`, `performance.md`):**
   `SKILL.md` links `../khai-playwright/references/pitch.md` (outside `khai-director/references/`) and uses terms (`Arc`, `Triggers`, `Stakes`, `Plot`), while Company primitives (`Persona`, `Piece`, `Place`, `Process`, `Position`, `Plan`) have no glossary in `khai-director`. Specifically, neither `SKILL.md` nor `knowledge.md` mentions assessing `Plans` at the end of a run or preserving authored files as read-only; only `performance.md` (`## Aftermath`) mentions recording *"what the Performance settled, what failed, and what remains open"* when lodging a `TO LOAN` card. Spelling out read-only `Plan` assessment in `## Knowledge` and `## System` closes that gap for both regular runs and captured Performances.
7. **Architectural context isolation vs. in-world knowledge rules (`knowledge.md`, `SKILL.md`):**
   `SKILL.md` states *"Do not give performers knowledge before it becomes available to them in play,"* and `knowledge.md` asks who can use or reveal information, what counts as evidence, and where persistent knowledge is recorded—but neither explicitly specifies **separate performer contexts** (preventing prompt-level leakage of offstage Plots or other roles' `Plans` and `Positions`) as distinct from instructing a single shared context to role-play ignorance.
