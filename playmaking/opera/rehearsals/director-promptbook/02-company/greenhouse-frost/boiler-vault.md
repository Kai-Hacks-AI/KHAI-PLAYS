# Basement Boiler Vault

## Taxonomy

Sub-grade steam plant beneath Glasshouse Three in [Frost Line at Glasshouse Three](play.md).

## Owner

[KHAI-PLAYS](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS)

## Shown

A low vaulted brick room lit by a single caged work bulb and the orange pinhole glow of the low-pressure burner; freezing night air pours down the grated landing at the head of the narrow iron stair while cooling return pipes tick unevenly along the damp mortar wall and smell of wet scale, machine oil, and slush.

## Holds

- The [Glasshouse Three Steam Manifold](steam-manifold.md) with its `0–15 psi` header gauge and twin spoked handwheels (`A — CITRUS` and `B — ALLOTMENT`).
- The vertical glass boiler sight-tube mounted beside the burner face, etched in red enamel at the `2.5 cm` automatic low-water safety cutout and freshly chalk-marked at `3.0 cm`.
- The brass condensate return gauge reading `1.5 psi` above the twin return traps (Wing A's longer cast-iron return lagging `0.4 psi` behind Wing B's shorter loop).
- A hanging slate tally board and chalk nub on a wire hook beside the iron stair to the ground-floor corridor.

## Offers

Immediate physical control of steam allocation via the [Glasshouse Three Steam Manifold](steam-manifold.md) to whoever turns the wheels, and direct visual verification of the `3.0 cm` sight-glass level, `2.5 cm` cutout line, and `1.5 psi` return pressure to [Soren](soren.md) from the start—or to [Ines](ines.md) as soon as she steps up to the boiler face under the cage lamp.

## Withheld

Any sight or touch of Wing A's citrus mother-trees, Wing B's forty neighborhood seedling benches, the corridor capillary thermometers, or the chained [Conservatory Night Logbook](night-logbook.md). Reaching the glasshouses or the ledger requires leaving the manifold wheels behind in the vault and climbing the iron stair.
