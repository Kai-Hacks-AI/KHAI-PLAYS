# Feed-Line Icing and Frost Drop

## Taxonomy

Thermal and mechanical steam-depletion Process in [Frost Line at Glasshouse Three](play.md).

## Owner

[KHAI-PLAYS](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS)

## Initiated by

A sudden `-9°C (16°F)` midnight freeze ices the conservatory's street water makeup main until the `4:00 AM` municipal thaw crew arrives. At `12:10 AM` in the [Basement Boiler Vault](boiler-vault.md), whoever checks the boiler face can tell the Process is underway from two readings: the tubular sight-glass has fallen to `3.0 cm` (`0.5 cm` above the red `2.5 cm` automatic low-water burner cutout line), and condensate return pressure has dropped to `1.5 psi` (with Wing A's long return loop lagging `0.4 psi` behind Wing B's shorter bench loop), capping safe continuous header draw at `4.5 psi`.

## Direction

Left at full winter draw on both wings, steam leaves the boiler faster than iced makeup water and lagging condensate return can replenish the drum, tripping the `2.5 cm` low-water cutout by `12:20 AM`, locking out the burner, and dropping both glasshouse wings below `-2.0°C (28°F)` well before `4:00 AM`. Once throttled within the `4.5 psi` ceiling, heat loss through Glasshouse Three's ridge glazing drives any unheated—or thinly split—wing down to the `1.0°C (34°F)` contact pin on the [Glasshouse Three Corridor](glasshouse-three-corridor.md) wall thermometers by `12:40 AM`, tripping the brass frost-drop alarm bell.

## Lever

The twin branch handwheels (`A — CITRUS` and `B — ALLOTMENT`) on the [Glasshouse Three Steam Manifold](steam-manifold.md) govern how the Process runs between `12:10 AM` and `12:40 AM` (while unlatching an inner wing door in Plot 2 can briefly bleed `6.0°C / 43°F` corridor air into one wing at the cost of chilling the corridor buffer):

- **Full triage to Wing A (`A — CITRUS` at `4.5 psi`, `B — ALLOTMENT` shut to drip-bleed):** Stabilizes boiler sight-glass at `3.5 cm` (`1.0 cm` safe margin above cutout). Wing A holds steady at `5.0°C (41°F)`; Wing B falls to `1.0°C (34°F)` on the corridor thermometer (`0.5°C / 33°F` at outer benches) at `12:40 AM`, tripping the frost-drop bell.
- **Full triage to Wing B (`B — ALLOTMENT` at `4.5 psi`, `A — CITRUS` shut to drip-bleed):** Stabilizes boiler sight-glass at `3.5 cm` (`1.0 cm` safe margin above cutout). Wing B holds steady at `5.0°C (41°F)`; Wing A falls to `1.0°C (34°F)` on the corridor thermometer (`0.5°C / 33°F` pooling at lower root-collars and the north gable) at `12:40 AM`, tripping the frost-drop bell.
- **Split throttle (`2.2 psi` divided across both `A — CITRUS` and `B — ALLOTMENT`):** Slows cooling in both wings but starves far-end steam traps so cooling condensate stalls in the return bends, pulling the boiler sight-glass down to `2.7 cm` (`2 mm` above cutout) with audible pipe knock; at `12:40 AM`, *both* thermometers hit the contact pin together (`Wing A 1.0°C / 34°F`, `Wing B 0.8°C / 33.5°F`), tripping the frost-drop bell.

Nothing inside the conservatory can restore full dual-wing pressure before the `4:00 AM` street main repair.

## Echo

By `12:40 AM`, Plot 1's manifold setting has inscribed a concrete physical state upstairs in the [Glasshouse Three Corridor](glasshouse-three-corridor.md): a ringing frost-drop bell, exact needle positions on the paired wall thermometers, visible frost or clear glass in Wing A (around the three `1921` north-gable citrus grafts vs. nine jute-wrapped inner tubs) and Wing B (across the outer rows of forty shallow neighborhood seedling flats), and an unanswered `12:40 AM` row in the [Conservatory Night Logbook](night-logbook.md).
