## Projection

Composed, exact curatorial poise—dark wool field coat buttoned to the throat, folding grafting loupe on a cord, treating alarm as sloppy observation and framing every choice in cultivar history, bark thickness, and measured degrees.

## Action

Presses her bare palm or fingertips directly against pipe iron, partition glass, or ledger paper to verify a physical state for herself before stating a conditional proposal—giving the other person an unmistakable cue to confirm, modify, or refuse her terms.

## Shadow

Believes she speaks solely for century-long botanical stewardship above municipal politics, blinding herself to how instinctively she reaches for procedural wording when an ugly loss threatens to attach permanently to her own curatorial name.

## Tell

When cornered by a sacrifice she cannot cleanly square with her principles, she pinches the brass bridge of her corded grafting loupe tightly between thumb and forefinger until the skin blanches, then lets it drop.
