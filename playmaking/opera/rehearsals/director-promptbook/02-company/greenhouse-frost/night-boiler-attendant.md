# Night Boiler Attendant

## Taxonomy

Municipal Conservatory Plant Operations role in [Frost Line at Glasshouse Three](play.md).

## Owner

[KHAI-PLAYS](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS)

## Has

Operating keys and mechanical custody of the [Basement Boiler Vault](boiler-vault.md), direct authority to set the [Glasshouse Three Steam Manifold](steam-manifold.md) wheels to prevent plant failure, and primary watch-signatory column in the [Conservatory Night Logbook](night-logbook.md).

## Orders

Keep boiler drum water safely above the `2.5 cm` automatic low-water safety cutout, maintain minimum freeze protection across municipal glasshouses until morning relief or repair crews arrive, and enter accurate half-hourly pressure readings and alarm events in the [Conservatory Night Logbook](night-logbook.md).

## Loses

Institutional standing to judge curatorial specimen value against public neighborhood allotment stock on botanical grounds, and disciplinary protection if the burner trips on low water or an unbacked valve cut destroys a wing without recorded curatorial authorization or mechanical necessity.

## Drives

Moves whoever holds the watch to protect the boiler's cutout margin first, anchor every argument in verifiable gauge numbers, and refuse to carry sole blame in the morning ledger for sacrificing living stock when higher curatorial authority is in the building.
