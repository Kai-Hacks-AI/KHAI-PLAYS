# Conservatory Night Logbook

## Taxonomy

Municipal shift watch and curatorial incident ledger in [Frost Line at Glasshouse Three](play.md).

## Owner

[KHAI-PLAYS](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS)

## Place

Chained to the sloped oak lectern beneath the paired wall thermometers in the [Glasshouse Three Corridor](glasshouse-three-corridor.md).

## Load Bearing

Without a completed night entry, the `4:00 AM` repair crew, Parks Superintendent, municipal plant union, allotment association, and lending herbarium have no authoritative record of boiler limits, valve settings, or who authorized overnight frost exposure—turning any plant loss into an unbacked breach of duty.

## Apparent

A heavy cloth-bound folio ruled into five columns—`Time`, `Drum / PSI`, `Wing A / Wing B`, `Action Taken`, and `Initials / Directive`—with an indelible black pen tethered on a brass bead chain; tonight's page shows neat half-hourly entries down to `11:00 PM — 3.6 cm / 6.0 psi | 7.5°C / 7.5°C | Routine winter draw | S.V.`, followed by an empty `11:30 PM / 12:00 AM` row above the blank `12:40 AM` frost-bell line.

## Yearbook

- **November 1996:** Chained permanently to the corridor lectern after an unlogged night damper failure killed thirty public cold-frame flats and staff memos contradicted each other at City Hall.
- **February 2021:** Re-ruled with the `Initials / Directive` column requiring any emergency steam diversion affecting accessioned trees or public benches to show either joint watch initials or a signed Curatorial Priority Directive.
- **Tonight, 11:00 PM:** Initialed `S.V.` by [Soren](soren.md) on routine rounds—the last ink on the page before feed-line flutter began at `11:45 PM` and the midnight line went unfilled.
