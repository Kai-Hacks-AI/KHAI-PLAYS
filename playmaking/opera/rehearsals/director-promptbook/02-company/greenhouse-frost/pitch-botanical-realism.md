## Tenor

Quiet, frost-taut botanical realism—low night-watch voices, clipped practical exchanges, and concrete physical handling of cast-iron handwheels, sight-glass chalk, chilled partition glass, bark, peat, and ledger ink without melodrama or speechmaking.

## Undertow

Institutional self-preservation beneath technical competence: the unspoken dread of whose work, neighbors, or century-old charges become expendable when municipal pipes ice over after midnight, and whose name stays exposed in the morning book.

## Nerve

Hushed, ticking cold—the sensation of cellular freeze advancing across real leaves and graft wood while every hesitation or hedge spends irrecoverable heat.

## Echo

How quietly winter triage happens: first as cold hands turning iron in a basement, then as two people deciding what truth they can afford to sign before `4:00 AM`.
