## Arc

Two midnight scenes thirty minutes apart turn a mechanical triage done blind in the basement into a visible reckoning under glass and in ink:

1. **[Plot 1: 12:10 AM — The Boiler Vault](plot-1-boiler-vault.md).** Down in the [Basement Boiler Vault](boiler-vault.md), street feed-line icing has choked makeup water until the `4:00 AM` municipal repair crew arrives. [Soren](soren.md) has just read the sight-glass drop and condensate return gauge when [Ines](ines.md) descends the iron stair from the cooling glasshouses. Acting before the automatic low-water safety cutout kills the burner altogether, they must commit the [Glasshouse Three Steam Manifold](steam-manifold.md) wheels to **Wing A** (twelve century-old grafted citrus mother-trees), **Wing B** (forty neighborhood allotment seedling benches), or a thin **split throttle** near the frost line—without being able to see the plants from the vault.
2. **[Plot 2: 12:40 AM — Glasshouse Three Corridor](plot-2-corridor-bell.md).** Thirty minutes later upstairs in the [Glasshouse Three Corridor](glasshouse-three-corridor.md), [Feed-Line Icing and Frost Drop](feed-line-freeze.md) has carried Plot 1's valve commitment through the pipes and tripped the brass frost-drop bell on the paired capillary wall thermometers. Standing where both wings are visible through the interior glass partitions, Soren and Ines face the chained [Conservatory Night Logbook](night-logbook.md)—deciding whether Plot 1's steam setting holds until `4:00 AM` and whose words, signature, directive, or refusal stay on the record for morning inspection.

## Triggers

- **Shared public premises at the start (`12:10 AM`):** A sudden `-9°C (16°F)` midnight freeze has iced the municipal conservatory's street water feed main. City water crews have confirmed they cannot thaw and restore makeup flow until `4:00 AM`. Glasshouse Three divides into **Wing A** (housing twelve century-old grafted citrus mother-trees) and **Wing B** (housing forty neighborhood allotment seedling benches). [Soren](soren.md) has the night boiler watch; [Ines](ines.md) has remained late in the complex completing a winter citrus rootstock and budwood audit.
- **Role-gated availability at `12:10 AM` (learnable by the other Persona only through observable disclosure or direct inspection in play):**
  - **Soren knows at curtain:** Just before `12:10 AM`, the boiler sight-glass dropped to `3.0 cm` (`0.5 cm` above the `2.5 cm` automatic low-water burner cutout) and condensate return sagged to `1.5 psi` (with Wing A's longer return loop lagging `0.4 psi` behind Wing B's). Leaving both manifold branch wheels at full winter draw trips the cutout by `12:20 AM` and freezes both wings dark; throttling to the `4.5 psi` safe ceiling holds **only one wing** safely at `5.0°C (41°F)` until `4:00 AM`, whereas a `2.2 psi` split throttle leaves both wings grazing `1.0°C (34°F)` while pulling the sight-glass down to `2.7 cm` (`2 mm` above cutout). Privately, Soren also knows he first spotted feed flutter at `11:45 PM` and spent twenty unlogged minutes flushing the strainer basket himself before calling upstairs—shaving `0.6 cm` off drum reserve without altering the one-wing ceiling. Ines can learn the gauge numbers by Soren's word or by stepping to inspect the sight-glass and header dial in [Basement Boiler Vault](boiler-vault.md); she can notice the unrecorded `11:30 PM / 12:00 AM` check row only if Soren mentions it or when inspecting the open [Conservatory Night Logbook](night-logbook.md) in Plot 2.
  - **Ines knows at curtain:** Between `11:50 PM` and `12:05 AM`, feeling the fin-pipes slacken upstairs, she observed that nine of Wing A's twelve mature citrus tubs sit on the inner masonry terrace wrapped in damp jute with enough root-ball thermal mass to survive a brief dip to `0.5°C–1.0°C (33°F–34°F)`, **except** three autumn-pruned `1921` Bergamot and Citron mother-trees by the north gable whose exposed high graft unions split irreversibly below `1.0°C (34°F)`—and crossing Wing B to reach the vault stair, she saw forty shallow community seed-saving and winter-start flats with dry thin potting mix and almost zero thermal mass that freeze solid rapidly below `1.0°C (34°F)`. Privately, Ines also knows her lending herbarium's multi-year municipal renewal is under review next week: signing a Curatorial Priority Directive that writes off forty public benches invites City Hall backlash, while losing a `1921` graft without putting a Wing A demand on the record damns her curation. Soren learns these botanical and institutional specifics only to the extent Ines speaks them in play or frost makes them visible behind the corridor glass in Plot 2.
- **Plot 1 start (`12:10 AM`):** Opens in [Basement Boiler Vault](boiler-vault.md) the moment Soren finishes chalking `3.0 cm` at the sight-glass and Ines comes down the iron stair into the vault. Both branch handwheels on the [Glasshouse Three Steam Manifold](steam-manifold.md) begin at full winter draw and must be committed before the `12:20 AM` cutout drop.
- **Plot 1 close and handoff:** Plot 1 ends as soon as Soren and Ines commit the [Glasshouse Three Steam Manifold](steam-manifold.md) wheels to **Wing A**, **Wing B**, or a **split throttle**. Thirty minutes pass (`12:10 AM` to `12:40 AM`) as [Feed-Line Icing and Frost Drop](feed-line-freeze.md) runs under that exact setting:
  - *Wing A committed:* Boiler sight-glass steadies at `3.5 cm`; at `12:40 AM`, Wing A holds `5.0°C (41°F)` while Wing B drops to `1.0°C (34°F)` (`0.5°C / 33°F` at outer benches), tripping the frost-drop bell with ice webbing lower panes and early frost curl on outer seedling trays.
  - *Wing B committed:* Boiler sight-glass steadies at `3.5 cm`; at `12:40 AM`, Wing B holds `5.0°C (41°F)` while Wing A drops to `1.0°C (34°F)` (`0.5°C / 33°F` around lower root-collars), tripping the frost-drop bell with hoarfrost feathering the north ridge panes above the three `1921` citrus grafts.
  - *Split throttle committed:* Sluggish condensate return pulls the boiler sight-glass to `2.7 cm` (`2 mm` above cutout) with audible return-pipe knock; at `12:40 AM`, *both* needles trip the frost-drop contact (`Wing A 1.0°C / 34°F`, `Wing B 0.8°C / 33.5°F`), riming both far gables at once.
- **Plot 2 start (`12:40 AM`):** Opens in [Glasshouse Three Corridor](glasshouse-three-corridor.md) as the brass frost-drop bell trips on the wall thermometers with both Soren and Ines present beside the partition windows and the open [Conservatory Night Logbook](night-logbook.md).
- **Plot 2 close (end of Play):** Plot 2 ends once both Soren and Ines have taken their observable stand on the `12:40 AM` entry in the [Conservatory Night Logbook](night-logbook.md)—whether by signing, counter-signing, entering a curatorial directive or dissent, or refusing the page—and settled whether Plot 1's manifold setting stands toward `4:00 AM` or one breaks from the lectern for the vault stair.

## Stakes

- **Living stock vs. living stock:** Wing A holds century-old grafted citrus mother-trees (`1919–1928`) whose scion cultivars cannot be regrown from seed if the high graft unions split; Wing B holds forty neighborhood households' overwintered seed-saving trays and spring allotment flats with no backup house and zero root-ball buffer.
- **Cutout survival vs. shared frost risk:** On the [Glasshouse Three Steam Manifold](steam-manifold.md), single-wing triage buys a safe `3.5 cm` boiler sight-glass cushion at the price of plunging the unheated wing onto the `1.0°C (34°F)` frost-drop line by `12:40 AM`; a split throttle refuses to abandon either wing in Plot 1 but starves condensate return to `2.7 cm` (`2 mm` above total burner cutout) and puts frost into *both* wings at once.
- **The morning record:** In Plot 2, the [Conservatory Night Logbook](night-logbook.md) turns mechanical valve turns into institutional truth—fixing whether Soren carries an unbacked watch loss (or exposed `11:45 PM` gap), whether Ines stakes or withholds her [Visiting Citrus Curator](visiting-citrus-curator.md) authority in ink, and whether either changes course once frost is visible on the glass. Both characters' choices remain genuinely open in both Plots.
