# Plot 1: 12:10 AM — The Boiler Vault

## Taxonomy

Opening Plot of [Frost Line at Glasshouse Three](play.md).

## Owner

[KHAI-PLAYS](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS)

## Cue

`12:10 AM` in the [Basement Boiler Vault](boiler-vault.md). Outside, a sudden `-9°C (16°F)` freeze has iced the municipal conservatory's street makeup feed main until the `4:00 AM` repair crew arrives. Under [Feed-Line Icing and Frost Drop](feed-line-freeze.md), [Soren](soren.md) has just read the boiler sight-glass drop (`3.0 cm`, only `0.5 cm` above the `2.5 cm` automatic low-water burner cutout) and condensate return gauge (`1.5 psi`) alone under the cage lamp: leaving both [Glasshouse Three Steam Manifold](steam-manifold.md) wheels wide open will trip the cutout by `12:20 AM`, while safe continuous header pressure (`4.5 psi`) can hold only **Wing A** or **Wing B** safely above freezing—unless they risk splitting `2.2 psi` thin steam across both wings near the frost line.

## Action

[Soren](soren.md), holding [Night Boiler Attendant](night-boiler-attendant.md), moves to get the manifold off lethal dual full-draw immediately, make [Ines](ines.md) confront the hard `2.5 cm` cutout ceiling, and prevent Wing B's forty neighborhood allotment benches from being discarded without accountability. Ines, holding [Visiting Citrus Curator](visiting-citrus-curator.md), comes down to find out why fin-pipe warmth is slackening upstairs, verify the boiler gauges for herself, and protect Wing A's century-old grafted citrus mother-trees. Their efforts collide at the `A — CITRUS` and `B — ALLOTMENT` handwheels, where every turn of margin given to one wing comes out of the other wing or out of the boiler's `5 mm` cutout cushion.

## Stage

At curtain (`12:10 AM`), [Soren](soren.md) stands alone beside the [Glasshouse Three Steam Manifold](steam-manifold.md) and boiler sight-glass in the [Basement Boiler Vault](boiler-vault.md) with his brass valve wrench and white chalk nub, both branch wheels still open at full winter draw. Fixed opening event: [Ines](ines.md) descends the narrow iron stair into the vault wearing her wool field coat with her corded grafting loupe and pocket inspection notebook, fresh from walking Wing A and Wing B between `11:50 PM` and `12:05 AM`. Plot 1 ends as soon as Soren and Ines commit the steam manifold handwheels to **Wing A**, **Wing B**, or a **split throttle**.

## Tension

Soren privately carries twenty unlogged minutes since `11:45 PM` spent trying to flush slush from the feed strainer himself before calling upstairs (shaving `0.6 cm` off the sight-glass cushion without changing the one-wing ceiling) and dreads being left alone on paper at `4:00 AM` for whichever wing freezes. Ines privately carries what she just saw under the glass—nine inner citrus tubs wrapped in damp jute have thermal mass, whereas the three `1921` north-gable mother-trees *and* all forty shallow neighborhood seed-saving and winter-start flats have almost none—alongside the knowledge that signing a public-bench sacrifice endangers her herbarium's city renewal next week while losing a `1921` graft without demanding Wing A steam damns her curation.
