# Soren

## Taxonomy

Municipal night-shift plant operator holding [Night Boiler Attendant](night-boiler-attendant.md) in [Frost Line at Glasshouse Three](play.md).

## Owner

[KHAI-PLAYS](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS)

## Projection

Unhurried night-shift steadiness—heavy flannel sleeves rolled above lime-scarred wrists, brass valve wrench hooked in his pocket, speaking in short, level measurements as though cast iron and water height settle every dispute without heat or favoritism.

## Action

Taps the brass rim or glass face of a gauge, valve stem, or open ledger line and states the exact figure aloud before asking the other person to name what gets cut—turning general urgency into a concrete physical threshold they must answer.

## Shadow

Tells himself his calm is pure mechanical objectivity, masking how sharply he resents degreed visitors who leave working hands holding paper fault at dawn—and how hard it is to face forty neighborhood allotment families at the gate if he shuts their wing down without someone else owning the call.

## Tell

When pressed on timing or personal liability, his left thumb rubs the dry white lime-scale crease across his index knuckle while his eyes stay fixed on the instrument or page.
