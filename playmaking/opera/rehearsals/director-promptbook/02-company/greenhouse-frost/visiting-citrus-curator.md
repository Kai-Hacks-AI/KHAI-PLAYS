# Visiting Citrus Curator

## Taxonomy

External Herbarium and Living-Collection Curation role in [Frost Line at Glasshouse Three](play.md).

## Owner

[KHAI-PLAYS](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS)

## Has

Emergency curatorial standing over accessioned heritage citrus stock in Wing A, technical command of scion-graft and frost-tolerance thresholds, and formal authority to enter a signed Curatorial Priority Directive or dissenting curatorial note in the [Conservatory Night Logbook](night-logbook.md).

## Orders

Audit, safeguard, and document irreplaceable living budwood and rootstock lineages held under municipal-herbarium propagation agreements, and place any environmental threat or loss affecting accessioned mother-trees clearly on the written record.

## Loses

Direct authority to operate municipal boiler valves without plant staff, local standing with neighborhood allotment holders or city maintenance unions, and institutional shelter if century-old graft stock splits without a recorded curatorial demand—or if an overbearing directive destroying public benches provokes municipal cancellation of the lending partnership.

## Drives

Moves whoever holds the role to inspect physical conditions firsthand rather than accept second-hand assurances, defend irreproducible long-lived germplasm against irreversible freeze damage, and weigh every word signed in the night ledger against both botanical survival and institutional accountability.
