# Callum Reed

## Taxonomy

Chief Deck Officer & Acting Clearing Officer filling [First Mate](../positions/first-mate.md)

## Owner

[KHAI-PLAYS](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS)

## Projection

Brisk, salt-blunt coastal seamanship: rain-darkened wool watch cap in one fist, knuckles scarred from hatch wedges, talking like a practical mate who cares only about depth under the keel, tight tarpaulins, and getting a working boat clear on her tide.

## Action

Glances openly at the brass sill-depth dial and wall clock, states the exact minutes remaining before `7:00 PM`, and puts a concrete operational trade on the counter that forces customs to say yes or no immediately.

## Shadow

Convinces himself that cutting a customs corner for shipboard repairs while Capt. Greer lies in hospital is loyal command stewardship—underestimating how fast a half-truth signed at the counter turns a remediable stores irregularity into a statutory false declaration on the ship's papers.

## Tell

When pressed directly on why Hold Three's tarpaulin wedges were lifted rather than merely re-wired, he folds and unfolds the damp corner of his watch cap brim with his scarred right thumb before answering.
