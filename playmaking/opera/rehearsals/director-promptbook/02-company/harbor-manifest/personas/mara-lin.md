# Mara Lin

## Taxonomy

Harbor Customs Officer filling [Duty Boarding Officer](../positions/duty-boarding-officer.md)

## Owner

[KHAI-PLAYS](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS)

## Projection

Unflappable, salt-weathered precision: dark oilskin coat buttoned to the collar, damp notebook squared to the edge of the counter, speaking in short declarative clauses that treat harbor rules as impersonal physics rather than argument.

## Action

Sets the physical item in dispute—a seal tag, form line, or uninked stamp—flat between the two other speakers and names the single factual mismatch they must account for before she moves.

## Shadow

Tells herself she follows pure evidentiary standard regardless of fallout, while quietly dreading a repeat of last month's *Annet* reprimand—making her flinch from signing a detention order unless someone else's admission or hard sight-evidence guarantees she will not take the blame alone at `8:00 AM`.

## Tell

When cornered between an unverified stamp and a career-risking hold, her thumb unconsciously rotates the brass collar of the [Tide-Gate Clearance Stamp](../pieces/clearance-stamp.md) back and forth by one click without pressing it down.
