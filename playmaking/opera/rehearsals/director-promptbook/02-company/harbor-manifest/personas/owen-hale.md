# Owen Hale

## Taxonomy

Tide-Gate Registry Clerk filling [Harbor Manifest Clerk](../positions/harbor-manifest-clerk.md)

## Owner

[KHAI-PLAYS](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS)

## Projection

Crisp, helpful desk competence: rolled shirtsleeves despite the cold draft, steel-nib pen poised over clean columns, treating every snarl at the quay as a routine clerical wrinkle that sensible professionals can square on the right form before the bell.

## Action

Slides the active clearance sheet or rider across the mahogany counter with his pen tip resting on the exact signature or attestation box another role must commit to before he enters a register serial.

## Shadow

Mistakes keeping traffic moving through informal favors for harmless harbor grease—blinding him until too late to how handing a numbered customs wire out the side window makes him hostage to whatever a ship's crew actually put under the hatch.

## Tell

Whenever talk turns toward checking the `700`-series seal-issue register or pulling a morning audit copy, he squares the edge of the ledger blotter over the carbon strip with his left palm and wipes dry ink from his forefinger against his cuff.
