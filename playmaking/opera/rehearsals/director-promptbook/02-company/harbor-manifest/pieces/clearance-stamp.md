# Tide-Gate Clearance Stamp

## Taxonomy

Harbor Boarding Officer Enforcement Kit ([Tide-Gate Customs Office](../places/tide-gate-customs-office.md))

## Owner

[KHAI-PLAYS](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS)

## Place

Holstered at [Mara Lin](../personas/mara-lin.md)'s belt when she steps into the [Tide-Gate Customs Office](../places/tide-gate-customs-office.md) at `6:40 PM`, and set beside its violet ink pad on the mahogany counter during final sign-off at `6:55 PM`.

## Load Bearing

Without its numbered impression (`GATE-04 / 25 NOV PM EBB`) in the clearance ring of [Outbound Port Clearance Sheet #09-K](../pieces/port-clearance-sheet.md), the lock-keeper is barred from opening the outer gate before the `7:00 PM` sill cutoff; once pressed down, it places [Mara Lin](../personas/mara-lin.md)'s personal badge number permanently behind *Kestrel*'s outbound cargo and seal state.

## Apparent

A heavy, salt-tarnished brass rotary stamp with a knurled date collar set to `25 NOV — PM EBB` and engraved die `CUSTOMS CLEARED · GATE-04`, chained to a worn leather belt frog.

## Yearbook

- **`14 OCT` — Four weeks prior (Known at start only to [Mara Lin](../personas/mara-lin.md); learnable by others only if she mentions it, though harbor staff know *Annet* was held last month):** Mara withholds `GATE-04` on the evening ebb and detains coaster *Annet* over an unlogged deck-locker wire; when dawn search proves the wire parted in heavy spray with no undeclared goods, the charterer lodges a wrongful-detention claim and the Port Collector warns Mara in writing that a second unsubstantiated ebb detention removes her from tide-gate duty.
- **`6:15 PM` tonight (Shared premise):** Mara sets the brass collar to `25 NOV — PM EBB` before walking out onto Quay Two in the rain to check *Kestrel*'s three cargo holds for the `7:00 PM` tide.
- **`6:40 PM` -> `7:00 PM` (Live in play):** Held back while [Wire Seal Tag #774](../pieces/wire-seal-tag-774.md) sits unresolved on the counter, remaining the final physical act required before `7:00 PM` to pass *Kestrel* through [Ebb Sill Clearance](../processes/ebb-sill-clearance.md).
