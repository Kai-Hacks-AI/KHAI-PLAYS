# Clipped Maritime Procedural Realism

## Taxonomy

 Procedural Realism — Working Harbor Chamber Drama ([Hold Three on the Ebb](../play.md))

## Owner

[KHAI-PLAYS](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS)

## Tenor

Brisk, clipped maritime procedural realism: spare coastal working speech anchored in seal serials, sill depths, form columns, and clock minutes, spoken by tired harbor and shipboard professionals in damp oilskins who treat paperwork as physical seamanship.

## Undertow

Cornered self-preservation: beneath every crisp citation of port rules or tidal seamanship, each person at the counter is privately calculating who absorbs the blame, the fine, or the ruined berth when tomorrow morning's ledger opens.

## Nerve

Taut, draining compression, as navigable water falls off the granite sill faster than three incompatible half-truths can be reconciled on one sheet of damp paper.

## Echo

The sharp click of either a brass clearance stamp or a pinned lock-gate bar—and the dry recognition that harbor order turns on what three pressured people agree to put in ink before the bell.
