# Tide-Gate Customs Office

## Taxonomy

Outer Basin Lockhead & Quay Two Precinct, Small Tidal Harbor ([Hold Three on the Ebb](../play.md))

## Owner

[KHAI-PLAYS](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS)

## Shown

Cold rain streaks the salt-frosted casement overlooking Quay Two, where coastal freighter *Kestrel*'s masthead Floodlamp sways above slick granite bollards and slackened spring lines. Inside, a paraffin heater ticks beside a scarred mahogany counter dividing the wet quarry-tile public standing space from the clerk's high desk; on the limewashed wall above the counter, a brass ship's clock (`6:40 PM` at Plot 1; `6:55 PM` at Plot 2) hangs beside a mechanical float dial showing water over the outer basin sill (`3.15 m` falling at `6:40 PM`; `2.95 m` falling at `6:55 PM`; red cutoff pin at `2.90 m` / `7:00 PM`), while the iron sill bell hangs directly outside under the lock-house eaves.

## Holds

- [Outbound Port Clearance Sheet #09-K](../pieces/port-clearance-sheet.md) laid on the mahogany counter blotter beside a carbon-backed **Provisional Manifest Rider** pad (`Form #09-R`), a two-part pink **Basin Detention Notice** book (`Form #D`), and the `700`-series **Harbor Seal-Issue Register & Carbon Strip** (kept on the clerk's side of the counter, with spare unbroken customs wire seal `#775` hanging on the brass peg beside it).
- [Tide-Gate Clearance Stamp](../pieces/clearance-stamp.md) and its inked violet pad in the boarding officer's leather belt-pouch / counter tray, alongside heavy steel **Customs Bolt-Cutters** and a brass waterproof inspection lamp racked by the quay door.
- A crank-handle **Lock-House Intercom Bell** on the clerk's jamb that signals the lock-gate crew forty yards away to either swing the outer gate on receipt of a stamped sheet or pin the red sill bar for the night.

## Offers

Gives all three roles a single dry counter under bright overhead light where a vessel's outbound clearance can be amended, co-signed, stamped, or refused within earshot of the sill bell: the [Harbor Manifest Clerk](../positions/harbor-manifest-clerk.md) controls the ledger side of the counter and form books, the [Duty Boarding Officer](../positions/duty-boarding-officer.md) controls the [Tide-Gate Clearance Stamp](../pieces/clearance-stamp.md) and cutters by the quay door, and the [First Mate](../positions/first-mate.md) stands at the public side of the counter within hailing reach of *Kestrel*'s deck crew out the door.

## Withheld

Keeps the interior of *Kestrel*'s **Hold Three** sixty yards out in the rain down Quay Two behind live wire seal `#774` and battened tarpaulin wedges: to see what actually sits beneath Hold Three's forward hatch boards, someone must leave the counter, carry the bolt-cutters onto the wet deck, shear `#774`, lift the hatch boards, and burn nearly the entire fifteen-minute margin before the `6:55 PM` warning bell. It also withholds the absent **Port Collector** (off duty until `8:00 AM` audit) and *Kestrel*'s injured **Master, Capt. Greer** (ashore overnight at the cottage hospital without telephone reach from the counter), forcing the three people in the room to sign or refuse on their own authority before `7:00 PM`.
