# Clear Kestrel Over the Evening Sill Without Wrecking the Ship's Standing

## Taxonomy

Individual Vessel Clearance Plan ([First Mate](../positions/first-mate.md))

## Owner

[Callum Reed](../personas/callum-reed.md)

## Direction

Walk out of the [Tide-Gate Customs Office](../places/tide-gate-customs-office.md) before the `7:00 PM` sill bell with [Mara Lin](../personas/mara-lin.md)'s [Tide-Gate Clearance Stamp](../pieces/clearance-stamp.md) on [Outbound Port Clearance Sheet #09-K](../pieces/port-clearance-sheet.md) so *Kestrel* clears the outer basin sill tonight and escapes `£2,400` in two-tide berth and charter demurrage—while navigating the unmanifested sister-ship pump spares in Hold Three without triggering a customs seizure or statutory false-declaration charge while Capt. Greer is in hospital.

## Orders

- **[Callum Reed](../personas/callum-reed.md) ([First Mate](../positions/first-mate.md)):** Press the twenty-minute ebb deadline on [Mara Lin](../personas/mara-lin.md) and [Owen Hale](../personas/owen-hale.md), test how much of the `5:30 PM` Hold Three opening must be put on the table to win clearance, and decide in both Plots whether to push for a **Provisional Manifest Rider**, accept or steer a **Quay Two spot check**, surrender or amend the disputed spares at the counter, or contest a **Basin Detention Notice** under formal demurrage protest.

## Implementation

- Callum personally knows—while Mara and Owen do not at `6:40 PM` unless disclosed or inspected—that with Master Capt. Greer ashore overnight at the cottage hospital with a crushed hand, Callum ordered `#714` clipped at `5:30 PM` for two reasons at once: to thread a loose bilge-sounding pipe cap inside Hold Three before facing tonight's bar swell, and to stow two stenciled wooden cases of standby ballast-pump valves (`MV TERN — BONDED MARINE VALVES #B-19`) swung across Quay Two from laid-up sister coaster *Tern* without waiting for Monday's bonded-stores transfer warrant. His boatswain fetched `#774` from Owen's side window at `5:35 PM` and crimped it on at `5:45 PM`, leaving the check-tag attached on the assumption Owen had updated `#09-K`.
- Missing the `7:00 PM` sill strands *Kestrel* (`2.90 m` draft) for two tides (`£2,400` demurrage, wiping out the St. Jude's voyage and threatening the boat's lay-up). Yet signing a clean rider falsely swearing nothing entered Hold Three after `4:15 PM` puts Callum's ticket on the line, whereas disclosing or hoisting off the two `#B-19` valve cases sacrifices *Kestrel*'s spare pump manifolds or risks stalling past `7:00 PM`.
- Callum's choices remain genuinely open in both Plots across rider terms, spot-check cooperation, emergency quay offload/stores declaration, or formal protest if held.
