# Reconcile Sheet #09-K and the 700-Series Register Before Cutoff

## Taxonomy

Individual Registry & Liability Plan ([Harbor Manifest Clerk](../positions/harbor-manifest-clerk.md))

## Owner

[Owen Hale](../personas/owen-hale.md)

## Direction

Bring [Outbound Port Clearance Sheet #09-K](../pieces/port-clearance-sheet.md) and serial `774` on the `700`-series **Harbor Seal-Issue Register** into defensible alignment before the `7:00 PM` sill bell so *Kestrel*'s watch paperwork closes tonight without leaving Owen's blank `5:35 PM` register line exposed to a hostile `8:00 AM` Collector audit—or pinning Owen's initials to undeclared cargo under Hold Three.

## Orders

- **[Owen Hale](../personas/owen-hale.md) ([Harbor Manifest Clerk](../positions/harbor-manifest-clerk.md)):** Steer counter paperwork across `6:40 PM` and `6:55 PM` so any form pulled—**Provisional Manifest Rider**, post-inspection amendment under `#775`, or **Basin Detention Notice**—either covers serial `774` as a documented quay replacement or forces [Callum Reed](../personas/callum-reed.md) and [Mara Lin](../personas/mara-lin.md) to share explicit written responsibility before Owen signs the clerk's attestation box.

## Implementation

- Owen personally knows—though neither Mara nor Callum has seen the register carbon behind the counter when Plot 1 opens—that at `5:35 PM` *Kestrel*'s boatswain knocked at his side window claiming `#714` parted on a hatch wedge in the rain; Owen handed spare wire `#774` off the back of his `700`-series strip without logging a mandatory Seal-Break Entry or calling Mara, meant to amend `#09-K` before `6:40 PM`, and forgot when the harbor master telephoned about tonight's sill depth.
- Crucially, Owen does **not** know at `6:40 PM` whether Callum's crew merely fixed a wedge or stowed something unmanifested in Hold Three while `#714` was off.
- Every option cuts two ways, leaving Owen's choice genuinely open in both Plots: a **Provisional Manifest Rider** clears the ship quietly unless Hold Three hides unmanifested goods or Mara demands the carbon register strip; a **Quay Two spot check** reveals what is actually in Hold Three before Owen attests the sheet, at the risk of running right up against the `6:55 PM` bell; an **overnight detention** triggers a mandatory `8:00 AM` Collector audit of the `700`-series carbon unless the Seal-Trace box is squared tonight.

## Targets

- `[ ]` Gauge from [Mara Lin](../personas/mara-lin.md) and [Callum Reed](../personas/callum-reed.md) in Plot 1 whether Hold Three was opened for more than a parted hatch wire before signing Owen's clerk attestation onto an unamended sheet.
- `[ ]` Commit in Plot 1 to whichever desk or quay procedure (provisional rider, Hold Three spot check, or detention notice) best contains the unlogged `5:35 PM` issue of `#774` given what surfaces at the counter.
- `[ ]` Before the `7:00 PM` sill bell in Plot 2, ensure serial `774` on the `700`-series register trace is accounted for on the final counter instrument without Owen singly attesting an unverified cargo tally.
