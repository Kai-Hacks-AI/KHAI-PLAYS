## Arc

Twenty minutes before the `7:00 PM` evening ebb drops the outer basin sill below coastal motor freighter *Kestrel*'s `2.90 m` loaded draft, a routine customs sign-off fractures into two compressed counter reckonings inside the [Tide-Gate Customs Office](places/tide-gate-customs-office.md).

In [Plot 1: Seal #774 on the Counter](plots/plot-1-the-replaced-tag.md) (`6:40 PM`), [Mara Lin](personas/mara-lin.md) steps in from rainy Quay Two carrying [Wire Seal Tag #774](pieces/wire-seal-tag-774.md) from *Kestrel*'s Hold Three where [Owen Hale](personas/owen-hale.md)'s typed [Outbound Port Clearance Sheet #09-K](pieces/port-clearance-sheet.md) still lists `#714`, just as [Callum Reed](personas/callum-reed.md) presses for his clearance stamp to escape two tides of berth demurrage (`£2,400`). Forced to act while navigable water drains off the sill, the three must commit to a procedure—drawing a **Provisional Manifest Rider**, cutting `#774` on the quay for a **Hold Three spot check**, or preparing an overnight **Basin Detention Notice**—before knowing the whole truth of what the other two did between `4:15 PM` and `6:40 PM`.

Fifteen minutes later, [Plot 2: The Five-Minute Sill Bell](plots/plot-2-the-five-minute-bell.md) (`6:55 PM`) opens in the same room as the five-minute warning bell clangs outside: whatever call they made at `6:40 PM` has burned fifteen minutes of water down to `2.95 m` and placed its physical consequence—the pulled `700`-series seal-register carbon with its blank `774` line, or the sheared wire and spot-check tally exposing sister coaster *Tern*'s two unmanifested bonded valve cases (`#B-19`)—flat on the mahogany counter. Plot 1 commits the Company to how the discrepancy is tested under falling water; Plot 2 forces all three to sign, stamp, offload, or detain with the exposed result staring up from the blotter before the `7:00 PM` cutoff bell makes *Kestrel*'s passage or stranding irreversible.

  - *Callum's `5:30 PM` Hold Three opening:* Callum starts knowing he had `#714` clipped both to thread a loose bilge-sounding pipe cap and to stow two stenciled wooden cases of laid-up sister coaster *Tern*'s standby ballast-pump valves (`MV TERN — BONDED MARINE VALVES #B-19`) without waiting for Monday's bonded-stores transfer warrant; Mara and Owen learn of the sounding pipe or the `#B-19` valve cases only if Callum discloses them in play or if Plot 1 commits to the Quay Two spot check, which brings the tally of both items onto the counter at `6:55 PM`.

## Stakes

The Company is fighting over **whether *Kestrel* clears the `7:00 PM` sill under [Ebb Sill Clearance](processes/ebb-sill-clearance.md) and whose name absorbs the legal and financial risk of Hold Three's broken seal chain**.

- **In [Plot 1](plots/plot-1-the-replaced-tag.md):** The stakes turn on *how the unknown is priced against twenty minutes of falling water*—trading speed on paper (a provisional rider over [Outbound Port Clearance Sheet #09-K](pieces/port-clearance-sheet.md)) against physical certainty on the wet quay (cutting the live twin of [Wire Seal Tag #774](pieces/wire-seal-tag-774.md) off Hold Three) or preemptive containment (pulling detention papers before `6:55 PM`).
- **In [Plot 2](plots/plot-2-the-five-minute-bell.md):** With fifteen minutes spent and the physical record exposed on the mahogany counter, the stakes sharpen into *irreversible personal and operational commitment*: pressing or withholding the [Tide-Gate Clearance Stamp](pieces/clearance-stamp.md) decides `£2,400` in two-tide demurrage and *Tern*'s `#B-19` pump valves for [Callum Reed](personas/callum-reed.md), the `8:00 AM` Collector audit of the blank `774` register line for [Owen Hale](personas/owen-hale.md), and boarding-watch survival after *Annet* for [Mara Lin](personas/mara-lin.md).
