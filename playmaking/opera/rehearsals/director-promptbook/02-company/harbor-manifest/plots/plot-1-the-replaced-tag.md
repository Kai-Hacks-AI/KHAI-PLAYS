# Plot 1: Seal #774 on the Counter

## Taxonomy

Opening Plot of [Hold Three on the Ebb](../play.md)

## Owner

[KHAI-PLAYS](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS)

## Cue

`6:40 PM` inside the [Tide-Gate Customs Office](../places/tide-gate-customs-office.md). Rain drives against the Quay Two glass; the mechanical float dial reads `3.15 m` and falling under [Ebb Sill Clearance](../processes/ebb-sill-clearance.md), leaving twenty minutes before the `7:00 PM` sill bell drops water below *Kestrel*'s `2.90 m` draft and strands her on two tides of berth demurrage (`£2,400`). [Callum Reed](../personas/callum-reed.md) stands at the mahogany counter pressing to sign [Outbound Port Clearance Sheet #09-K](../pieces/port-clearance-sheet.md) and get his clearance stamp as [Mara Lin](../personas/mara-lin.md) steps in from the wet quay carrying [Wire Seal Tag #774](../pieces/wire-seal-tag-774.md)—just twisted from a replacement harbor wire seal on Hold Three where Owen's typed sheet still reads `HBR-714`.

## Action

- **[Mara Lin](../personas/mara-lin.md)** ([Duty Boarding Officer](../positions/duty-boarding-officer.md)) puts [Wire Seal Tag #774](../pieces/wire-seal-tag-774.md) down beside `#09-K`, withholds the [Tide-Gate Clearance Stamp](../pieces/clearance-stamp.md), and presses both [Owen Hale](../personas/owen-hale.md) and [Callum Reed](../personas/callum-reed.md) to explain how an unlogged `700`-series harbor seal replaced `#714` and why Hold Three's tarpaulin wedges show fresh boot-grease.
- **[Callum Reed](../personas/callum-reed.md)** ([First Mate](../positions/first-mate.md)) drives to keep *Kestrel* on tonight's ebb before `7:00 PM`, pressing for an immediate path to the stamp while weighing how much of Hold Three's `5:30 PM` opening he can safely disclose or keep back.
- **[Owen Hale](../personas/owen-hale.md)** ([Harbor Manifest Clerk](../positions/harbor-manifest-clerk.md)) works to steer the discrepancy toward a controllable counter disposition without exposing his unlogged `5:35 PM` side-window seal handoff or attesting blind to whatever *Kestrel*'s crew put under the hatch.
- Their efforts collide at the counter: none of the three can clear or close `#09-K` alone, and every minute spent arguing eats into the single fifteen-minute window left if anyone means to cut `#774` on Quay Two before the `6:55 PM` warning bell.

## Stage

- **Already at the counter (`6:40 PM`):** [Owen Hale](../personas/owen-hale.md) behind the high desk with [Outbound Port Clearance Sheet #09-K](../pieces/port-clearance-sheet.md) (`HOLD 3: HBR-714`), the **Provisional Manifest Rider** pad, the **Basin Detention Notice** book, spare wire seal `#775`, and the `700`-series register carbon kept on his side of the blotter; [Callum Reed](../personas/callum-reed.md) on the wet tile side in oilskins, watch cap in fist, pen ready to sign as clearing mate for hospitalized Capt. Greer.
- **Entering on the opening beat (`6:40 PM`):** [Mara Lin](../personas/mara-lin.md) pushes through the quay door from the rain carrying wet [Wire Seal Tag #774](../pieces/wire-seal-tag-774.md) in her palm, the [Tide-Gate Clearance Stamp](../pieces/clearance-stamp.md) holstered at her belt, and the customs bolt-cutters racked beside her at the door. Out on Quay Two, Hold Three remains sealed under live wire loop `#774`.
- **Closing commitment:** The Plot ends as soon as the three commit on stage to one of the three tide-gate calls—clearing on a **Provisional Manifest Rider**, breaking Hold Three's hatch seal `#774` on the quay for a **spot check before the bell**, or **detaining *Kestrel* inside the basin overnight** (see [Hold Three on the Ebb — Triggers](../play.md#triggers)).

## Tension

- **[Mara Lin](../personas/mara-lin.md)** saw pried tarpaulin wedges and fresh boot-grease on Hold Three at `6:35 PM`, proving the hatch was opened rather than merely re-tagged, yet carries a written Port Collector warning after last month's clean *Annet* hold that makes a second unbacked detention career-fatal ([Verify Hold Three Without an Unbacked Hold or Blind Stamp](../plans/mara-verify-before-stamp.md)).
- **[Owen Hale](../personas/owen-hale.md)** privately handed spare wire `#774` out his side window to *Kestrel*'s boatswain at `5:35 PM` without logging a Seal-Break Entry or calling Mara, then forgot to amend `#09-K`—leaving line `774` blank on his register carbon and leaving him ignorant of whether *Kestrel*'s crew stowed undeclared goods under that wire ([Reconcile Sheet #09-K and the 700-Series Register Before Cutoff](../plans/owen-reconcile-the-register.md)).
- **[Callum Reed](../personas/callum-reed.md)** ordered `#714` clipped at `5:30 PM` both to cap a loose bilge-sounding pipe and to stow two cases of laid-up sister coaster *Tern*'s bonded bronze pump valves (`MV TERN — BONDED MARINE VALVES #B-19`) without Monday's transfer warrant, while `£2,400` in two-tide demurrage hangs over the boat if she misses `7:00 PM` ([Clear Kestrel Over the Evening Sill Without Wrecking the Ship's Standing](../plans/callum-clear-the-sill.md)).
