# Plot 2: The Five-Minute Sill Bell

## Taxonomy

Closing Plot of [Hold Three on the Ebb](../play.md)

## Owner

[KHAI-PLAYS](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS)

## Cue

`6:55 PM` in the same [Tide-Gate Customs Office](../places/tide-gate-customs-office.md)—fifteen minutes after Plot 1. Outside under the lock-house eaves, the iron **five-minute sill warning bell** clangs once; on the wall dial, water over the granite sill stands at `2.95 m` and dropping fast toward the `2.90 m` red pin (`7:00 PM`) under [Ebb Sill Clearance](../processes/ebb-sill-clearance.md). The lock-gate crew stands on the intercom awaiting either a stamped [Outbound Port Clearance Sheet #09-K](../pieces/port-clearance-sheet.md) to warp *Kestrel* straight through or an executed **Basin Detention Notice** / `7:00 PM` cutoff to pin the sill bar for the night, as the concrete physical outcome of Plot 1's call lands on the mahogany counter.

## Action

- **[Mara Lin](../personas/mara-lin.md)** ([Duty Boarding Officer](../positions/duty-boarding-officer.md)), with the [Tide-Gate Clearance Stamp](../pieces/clearance-stamp.md) unholstered beside its violet pad, drives to lock down a legally defensible final disposition before `7:00 PM`—deciding whether the physical evidence and signatures now on the counter justify pressing `GATE-04` onto `#09-K` (with or without binding caveats/quay discharge) or holding *Kestrel* in the basin.
- **[Owen Hale](../personas/owen-hale.md)** ([Harbor Manifest Clerk](../positions/harbor-manifest-clerk.md)) confronts the exposed `700`-series register trace on the counter and drives to ensure that whichever document closes the watch—stamped rider/amendment or finalized detention notice—does not leave his `5:35 PM` blank line carrying sole blame at `8:00 AM`.
- **[Callum Reed](../personas/callum-reed.md)** ([First Mate](../positions/first-mate.md)), with *Kestrel*'s crew standing by single lines on Quay Two and five minutes of water left under her keel, drives to win lock-gate release—deciding whether to sign the counter terms, sacrifice or declare the `#B-19` valve spares on Quay Two, or refuse ruinous terms and put a formal demurrage protest on the record.
- All three choices remain genuinely open in these final five minutes: a provisional clearance can still collapse into detention if someone balks at signing the exposed trace, a spot check's mixed find can either clear via emergency stores action/quay offload or trigger a hold, and a preliminary detention draft can either harden into an overnight lock-down or flip to conditional clearance before the `7:00 PM` bell.

## Stage

All three Personas ([Mara Lin](../personas/mara-lin.md), [Owen Hale](../personas/owen-hale.md), [Callum Reed](../personas/callum-reed.md)) are present at the mahogany counter in the [Tide-Gate Customs Office](../places/tide-gate-customs-office.md) as the `6:55 PM` warning bell rings, with [Tide-Gate Clearance Stamp](../pieces/clearance-stamp.md) and [Wire Seal Tag #774](../pieces/wire-seal-tag-774.md) on the blotter alongside the exact physical outcome produced by Plot 1's call:

1. **If Plot 1 committed to a Provisional Manifest Rider:** Owen lays **Provisional Manifest Rider `#09-K/R`** clipped to [Outbound Port Clearance Sheet #09-K](../pieces/port-clearance-sheet.md) alongside the mandatory `700`-series **Seal-Issue Carbon Strip** pulled to enter `#774`'s serial—visibly exposing the blank, uninitialed `774` register line on the counter while `#09-K/R` awaits Callum's binding ship declaration, Owen's register attestation, and Mara's stamp before `7:00 PM`.
2. **If Plot 1 committed to breaking Hold Three's hatch seal for a quay spot check:** Mara and Callum step back in from rainy Quay Two at `6:55 PM` as Mara drops **sheared wire `#774`** and her wet **Spot-Check Tally Slip** onto `#09-K` beside Owen's spare wire seal **`#775`**: under Hold Three's forward square they found no contraband cargo, a freshly threaded **bilge-sounding pipe cap**, and **two unmanifested wooden cases stenciled `MV TERN — BONDED MARINE VALVES #B-19`** sitting directly under the cargo runner within two-minute hoist reach of Quay Two, requiring immediate sign-off on offload, emergency stores endorsement under `#775`, or detention before `7:00 PM`.
3. **If Plot 1 committed to detaining *Kestrel* inside the basin overnight:** Owen sets two-part pink **Basin Detention Notice `#D-14`** stapled over `#09-K` alongside the pulled `700`-series **Seal-Issue Carbon Strip** needed to complete `#D-14`'s mandatory **Statutory Cause & Seal-Trace Box**—exposing the blank `774` register line while *Kestrel* still lies singled up beside the open lockhead with five minutes of navigable water left if `#D-14` is either executed and served or withdrawn in favor of conditional sign-off on `#09-K` before `7:00 PM`.

## Tension

- **[Mara Lin](../personas/mara-lin.md)** holds the final physical gate (`GATE-04` stamp vs. lock-house stand-down) with five minutes left: stamping an uncorroborated or irregular sheet puts her badge on the hook at St. Jude's and before the Collector, while executing `#D-14` without ironclad cause on the form triggers the exact reprimand threshold set after *Annet* ([Verify Hold Three Without an Unbacked Hold or Blind Stamp](../plans/mara-verify-before-stamp.md)).
- **[Owen Hale](../personas/owen-hale.md)** can no longer keep the `700`-series register carbon out of sight once final form serials hit the counter at `6:55 PM`, forcing him to decide in plain view how to sign or condition line `774` before `8:00 AM` audit copies lock ([Reconcile Sheet #09-K and the 700-Series Register Before Cutoff](../plans/owen-reconcile-the-register.md)).
- **[Callum Reed](../personas/callum-reed.md)** faces the final five minutes before `2.90 m` traps *Kestrel* for two tides (`£2,400`), forced to weigh parting with *Tern*'s `#B-19` pump valves, signing a binding declaration on `#09-K`, or taking a ruinous stranding onto the record while Capt. Greer lies ashore ([Clear Kestrel Over the Evening Sill Without Wrecking the Ship's Standing](../plans/callum-clear-the-sill.md)).
