# Duty Boarding Officer

## Taxonomy

Harbor Customs & Tide-Gate Enforcement Service ([Tide-Gate Customs Office](../places/tide-gate-customs-office.md))

## Owner

[KHAI-PLAYS](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS)

## Has

Sole physical custody of the [Tide-Gate Clearance Stamp](../pieces/clearance-stamp.md) and customs bolt-cutters; statutory authority to walk any berthed vessel's deck, twist check-tags from hatch seals, shear a customs wire for an on-quay hold spot check, co-sign a **Provisional Manifest Rider** noting a verified seal exception, or issue a **Basin Detention Notice** ordering the lock-gate stood down.

## Orders

Verify that every cargo hold seal on a departing vessel matches the serial numbers attested on [Outbound Port Clearance Sheet #09-K](../pieces/port-clearance-sheet.md) before applying the [Tide-Gate Clearance Stamp](../pieces/clearance-stamp.md); refuse an unbacked stamp whenever a hatch seal has been replaced or a tarpaulin disturbed without logged cause; and ensure any **Basin Detention Notice** states a concrete, verifiable ground on the form before holding a commercial vessel past a navigable tide.

## Loses

The comfort of deferring judgment to higher authority during an evening ebb watch, and the ability to edit the harbor manifest or seal-issue register unilaterally—every clearance or detention requires a counter document numbered by the [Harbor Manifest Clerk](../positions/harbor-manifest-clerk.md) and carries the officer's personal badge number into tomorrow's Port Collector review.

## Drives

Pushes whoever holds it to test discrepancies in the open before ink hits paper—weighing the career hazard of stamping an uninspected hatch that hides undeclared goods against the equal hazard of stranding a working coaster on a detention order that collapses under morning inspection.
