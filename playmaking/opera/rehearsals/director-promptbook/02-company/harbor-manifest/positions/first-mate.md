# First Mate

## Taxonomy

Deck Command & Clearing Watch, Coastal Motor Freighter *Kestrel* ([Hold Three on the Ebb](../play.md))

## Owner

[KHAI-PLAYS](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS)

## Has

Direct command of *Kestrel*'s deck crew, hatch battens, and cargo winch on Quay Two; temporary ship's clearing authority in the absent Master's stead to sign outbound declarations, provisional manifest riders, emergency stores amendments, or formal demurrage protests on [Outbound Port Clearance Sheet #09-K](../pieces/port-clearance-sheet.md); and first-hand operational custody of what was stowed or repaired aboard after the stevedores knocked off.

## Orders

Deliver *Kestrel* safely over the outer basin sill on the scheduled ebb without incurring avoidable berth demurrage or losing charter hire; keep holds seaworthy for open coastal water; and sign accurate ship's customs declarations without exposing the vessel, owner, or absent Master to cargo seizure or statutory false-declaration penalties.

## Loses

Access to customs stamps, harbor seal ledgers, or unilateral lock-gate release—the vessel cannot cast off from Quay Two or enter the lock without customs sign-off—and loses the cover of a senior captain on scene whenever an irregular deck decision comes due at the counter.

## Drives

Presses whoever holds it to secure outbound clearance before the falling sill strands the ship, trading hard on time, demurrage liability, and practical seamanship while weighing how much deck-side irregularity can be disclosed, offloaded, or signed onto a rider without forfeiting the tide or the ship's standing.
