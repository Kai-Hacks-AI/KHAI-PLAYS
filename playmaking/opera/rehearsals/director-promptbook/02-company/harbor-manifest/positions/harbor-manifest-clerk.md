# Harbor Manifest Clerk

## Taxonomy

Harbor Customs & Tide-Gate Registry Desk ([Tide-Gate Customs Office](../places/tide-gate-customs-office.md))

## Owner

[KHAI-PLAYS](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS)

## Has

Custody of [Outbound Port Clearance Sheet #09-K](../pieces/port-clearance-sheet.md), the `700`-series **Harbor Seal-Issue Register & Carbon Strip**, spare unissued wire seals (`#775`), the **Provisional Manifest Rider** pad (`Form #09-R`), the **Basin Detention Notice** book (`Form #D`), and the lock-house intercom bell at the counter jamb.

## Orders

Prepare outbound clearance sheets from signed loading tallies; log every issued `700`-series customs wire seal by time, vessel, hold, and recipient before it leaves the counter; attest that seal serials and bonded-stores lines reconcile before passing a sheet for the [Duty Boarding Officer](../positions/duty-boarding-officer.md)'s stamp; and enter a complete register trace whenever a provisional rider or detention notice is drawn.

## Loses

Power to stamp a vessel clear or physically inspect a hold on the quay, as well as any off-the-record margin once a form serial is pulled—because both a **Provisional Manifest Rider** and a **Basin Detention Notice** attach the carbon seal-register trace for review by the Port Collector at `8:00 AM`.

## Drives

Moves whoever holds it to reconcile discrepant paperwork cleanly inside the room before an audit or demurrage dispute pulls the underlying register carbon—favoring workable desk amendments when safe, yet balking whenever attesting a line would pin another role's cargo or enforcement risk onto the clerk's initials.
