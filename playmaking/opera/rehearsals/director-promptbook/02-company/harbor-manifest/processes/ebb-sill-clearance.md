# Ebb Sill Clearance

## Taxonomy

Harbor Tidal Lock & Outbound Customs Operations ([Hold Three on the Ebb](../play.md))

## Owner

[KHAI-PLAYS](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS)

## Initiated by

The evening ebb dropping the outer basin float gauge through `3.15 m` at `6:40 PM` while *Kestrel* (`2.90 m` loaded draft) lies singled up at Quay Two awaiting outbound customs clearance in the [Tide-Gate Customs Office](../places/tide-gate-customs-office.md). Everyone in the office can track its advance directly on the wall clock and mechanical sill-depth dial, punctuated aloud by the single iron **five-minute sill warning bell** outside at `6:55 PM` and the double **sill cutoff bell** at `7:00 PM`.

## Direction

Drains `0.25 m` of navigable water off the granite outer basin sill across twenty minutes (`3.15 m` at `6:40 PM` -> `2.95 m` at `6:55 PM` -> below `2.90 m` at `7:00 PM`), steadily narrowing what can still be physically verified on Quay Two or amended on [Outbound Port Clearance Sheet #09-K](../pieces/port-clearance-sheet.md) before the lock-keeper must either pass *Kestrel* through the outer gate or pin the sill bar shut for the night.

## Lever

Driven by the falling coastal ebb, which no harbor official or ship's officer can slow or pause. Its operational outcome at the lockhead is steered solely by what lands completed on the counter before `7:00 PM`:
- **To pass *Kestrel* out on the ebb:** [Outbound Port Clearance Sheet #09-K](../pieces/port-clearance-sheet.md) (either reconciled by a co-signed **Provisional Manifest Rider** `#09-K/R` or updated after a physical hatch spot check and re-seal under `#775`) must carry the [Harbor Manifest Clerk](../positions/harbor-manifest-clerk.md)'s register attestation, the [First Mate](../positions/first-mate.md)'s ship declaration, and the [Duty Boarding Officer](../positions/duty-boarding-officer.md)'s [Tide-Gate Clearance Stamp](../pieces/clearance-stamp.md) before `7:00 PM`.
- **To halt clearance immediately or by default:** Executing a **Basin Detention Notice** (`#D-14`)—or reaching the `7:00 PM` cutoff bell with `#09-K` unstamped—stands down the lock-gate crew and pins the sill bar across the outer gate.

## Echo

At `7:00 PM`, the threshold hardens into irreversible physical fact: either *Kestrel* warps clear over the outer sill for St. Jude's carrying the legal liability of whatever declarations, riders, cargo offloads, or seal traces were inked in the [Tide-Gate Customs Office](../places/tide-gate-customs-office.md), or the sill falls beneath her keel and traps her inside the basin across low water and the night flood—locking in two tides of berth demurrage (`£2,400`), a broken coastal charter window, and an `8:00 AM` Port Collector inspection of both Hold Three and the `700`-series seal register.
