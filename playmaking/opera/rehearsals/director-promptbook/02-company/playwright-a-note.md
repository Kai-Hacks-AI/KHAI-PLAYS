## 2. How Linking the Reusable `../promptbook.md` Shaped `play.md` and Company Files

Because `../promptbook.md` already governs silent-witness mode, `~4–6` turn pacing, observable-only cast turns, isolated performer contexts, evidence rules, read-only Plan assessment, and a **0% staging limit**, none of those mechanics belonged in `greenhouse-frost/`. At the same time, writing against a **0% staging limit** and strict context isolation sharpened four authoring choices inside the Play and Company files:

1. **Complete three-branch physics for `0%` carry-forward (`feed-line-freeze.md`, `play.md`, `plot-2-corridor-bell.md`):** Plot 2 opens on the `12:40 AM` frost-drop bell regardless of whether Plot 1 chose Wing A, Wing B, or a split throttle. To prevent any staging gap at `0%`, `feed-line-freeze.md` and `Triggers` define why leaving both wheels wide open trips the `2.5 cm` cutout by `12:20 AM` and why *all three* valid manifold commitments trip the `1.0°C (34°F)` frost-drop contact at `12:40 AM` (on Wing B's needle, Wing A's needle, or both needles together with `2.7 cm` sight-glass sag).
2. **Self-contained role-gated seeding (`plan-soren-watch.md`, `plan-ines-curation.md`, `play.md`):** Because `../promptbook.md` seeds performers with shared public premises plus their own `Persona`, `Position`, and `Plans`—while withholding `play.md`'s overview and the other Persona's private files—each character's private `12:10 AM` starting facts (Soren's sight-glass/return readings and unlogged `11:45 PM` strainer delay; Ines's `11:50 PM–12:05 AM` observations of the three exposed `1921` north-gable grafts vs. forty zero-mass allotment trays and next week's renewal vote) were placed directly in each role's `Plan` (`Implementation`) and mirrored in `play.md` (`Triggers`) with explicit availability rules for when the other Persona can learn them.
3. **Inspection-ready `Piece` and `Place` evidence (`night-logbook.md`, `boiler-vault.md`, `glasshouse-three-corridor.md`):** Because `../promptbook.md` treats verifiable `Piece` states as stage evidence, `night-logbook.md` (`Apparent`) places an unfilled `11:30 PM / 12:00 AM` check line visibly above the `12:40 AM` row so Ines can discover the gap organically on stage without omniscient leakage.
4. **Open-choice `Plans` and two-party Plot 2 closure:** To keep choices genuinely open while supporting end-of-run `Plan` assessment, `[ ]` targets test whether each Persona puts real constraints on stage and secures backing or record integrity rather than mandating a single wing. Similarly, Plot 2's closing `Trigger` requires *both* Personas to take their observable stand on the `12:40 AM` logbook entry and valve continuation so one character writing first cannot end the Plot before the other responds.

## 3. Any Pull Toward Play-Specific Promptbook Instructions

I felt two brief pulls toward Play-specific staging instructions:
- specifying who takes Turn 1 when Ines descends the iron stair in Plot 1 or when the frost-drop bell chatters at `12:40 AM` in Plot 2; and
- instructing the Director how to update corridor thermometer readings from Plot 1's valve state.

In both cases, resisting that pull proved cleaner: `../promptbook.md`'s existing "By a cue / By the scene" handoff handles Turn 1 once `Stage` fixes the opening entrance and ringing alarm, and `feed-line-freeze.md` (`Process`) plus `play.md` (`Triggers`) express the thermometer outcomes properly as in-world mechanics rather than Promptbook rules. No Play-specific Promptbook additions were needed.
