## 2. How Linking an External Reusable `promptbook.md` Shaped a 3-Persona Play

- **Clean separation between staging protocol and dramatic material:** Because `../promptbook.md` already governs silent-witness Human participation, `~4–6` brisk observable-only turns per Plot, cue/scene turn handoffs, isolated performer contexts, read-only `Plan` assessment, and the `0%` staging limit, `play.md` needed zero mechanics prose. Everything in `harbor-manifest/` stayed inside world facts, roles, objects, cues, and open stakes.
- **Triangular role lock for 3-Persona turn flow:** Under `../promptbook.md`'s scene-and-cue turn selection, a 3-Persona chamber scene risks collapsing into a two-way argument that sidelines the third character unless the dramatic machinery requires all three. I structured the three `Positions`, `Pieces`, and `Persona` `Actions` as an interlocking triangle: Mara holds the clearance stamp and bolt-cutters, Owen holds `#09-K`, the rider/detention pads, and the `700`-series seal register, and Callum holds ship's declaration authority and deck custody on Quay Two. No two Personas can clear or close `#09-K` bilaterally.
- **Three-way asymmetric knowledge under isolated performer seeding:** Because `../promptbook.md` seeds each performer with shared public premises plus only that role's own `Persona`, `Position`, and private `Plan` (withholding `play.md`'s top-level overview and uninspected `Piece` `Yearbook` entries), I did two things:
  1. Explicitly separated shared `6:40 PM` premises from role-gated facts and stated when each private fact becomes learnable on stage (`play.md` `Company` and `Piece` `Yearbook` entries).
  2. Placed each character's own pre-`6:40 PM` lived history (Mara's pried-wedge sight and *Annet* reprimand; Owen's unlogged `5:35 PM` side-window `#774` handoff; Callum's `5:30 PM` sounding-pipe cap and *Tern*'s `#B-19` bonded valve cases) directly in that character's own `Plan` as well as Plot `Tension`, while keeping `Targets` open across rider, spot check, conditional offload/endorsement, or detention.
- **Zero-invention branch outcomes across the 15-minute ellipsis (`0%` limit):** Because `0%` staging forbids the Director from inventing what lies inside Hold Three on a spot check—or improvising if a `~4–6` turn exchange ends in a 2–1 split—`Triggers` and `Plot 2` (`Stage`) explicitly author the physical counter state at `6:55 PM` for all three Plot 1 commitments (provisional rider + blank `774` carbon; sheared `#774` + `#775` + spot-check slip showing the capped sounding pipe and two `#B-19` valve crates; detention notice `#D-14` + blank `774` trace) so every branch stays stageable at `0%` while leaving all three characters' Plot 2 choices genuinely open.

## 3. Any Pull Toward Play-Specific Promptbook Instructions?

I felt two brief pulls and resolved both cleanly inside the Play and Company files without wishing to touch `../promptbook.md`:
1. **3-cast turn participation:** A momentary pull to add a rule ensuring all three Personas speak before Plot 1's commitment Trigger fires. Making the three `Positions` procedurally interdependent—and giving each `Persona` a physical counter `Action` that cues the others—solved this organically through character and situation rather than Promptbook rules.
2. **Handling the 15-minute Quay Two spot check at `0%`:** A momentary pull to write Director procedural notes on what a hatch inspection uncovers between Plots. Treating Hold Three's contents and the `700`-series register state as authored `Piece` `Yearbook` facts and explicit Plot 1 -> Plot 2 `Triggers` handled the ellipsis cleanly without leaking Play-specific rules into a reusable Promptbook.
