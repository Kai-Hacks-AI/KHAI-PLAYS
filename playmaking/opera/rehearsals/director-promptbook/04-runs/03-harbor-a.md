# Director’s Note

## (a) Where the Shared `promptbook.md` Worked Cleanly on a 3-Persona Play

- **Silent-witness ensemble chamber framing (`## Human`, `## Agent`).** Running [Hold Three on the Ebb](../02-company/harbor-manifest/play.md) under the shared [Promptbook](../02-company/promptbook.md) (`0%` staging limit) and [Clipped Maritime Procedural Realism](../02-company/harbor-manifest/pitches/maritime-procedural-realism.md) sustained taut, uninterrupted procedural momentum across three separate performer contexts. The `Observable behavior override` (`## System`) worked cleanly across all thirteen turns: every performer expressed cornered self-preservation strictly through physical tells (Mara clicking the `GATE-04` date collar, Owen squaring the blotter over the carbon strip and wiping ink on his cuff, Callum folding his damp watch-cap brim) and clipped coastal counter bargaining, with zero inner monologue or meta-narration.
- **Three-way turn routing (`By a cue` + `By the scene`).** The two routing rules in `## Collaboration` prevented two-speaker ping-pong from stranding the third role in a three-person chamber room. `By a cue` cleanly governed procedural thresholds—Mara's `6:40 PM` entrance with `#774`, Owen's closing Plot 1 seal-prep beat, Mara's `6:55 PM` spot-check return on the warning bell, Owen's `6:59 PM` register closure upon return of check-tag `#775`, and Mara's final `GATE-04` stamp—while `By the scene` carried the pressure around the triangle in between (`M → O → C → M → C → O` in Plot 1; `M → C → O → M → C → O → M` in Plot 2).
- **Epistemic gating and stage evidence (`## Knowledge`).** Seeding each performer strictly with the shared `6:40 PM` public premises and their isolated role files preserved genuine three-way asymmetry until observable play shifted it:
  - Mara's `6:35 PM` boot-grease observation entered shared play via her Plot 1 Turn 1 disclosure, whereas her private *Annet* Collector reprimand never leaked and remained pure subtextual pressure preventing both a blind rider and an unbacked `#D-14` hold.
  - Owen's `5:35 PM` side-window handoff entered first as an uncorroborated verbal explanation in Plot 1 Turn 2 (while his palm still covered the carbon strip) and became shared physical evidence only when he folded back the blotter in Turn 6 to issue `#775`.
  - Callum tested a half-truth in Plot 1 Turn 3 (bilge-sounding pipe cap only), pivoted to pre-cut verbal disclosure of `MV TERN — BONDED MARINE VALVES #B-19` in Turn 5 only when Mara lifted the customs bolt-cutters at the door, and saw that claim harden into verified counter evidence only when Mara's wet Spot-Check Tally Slip landed on `#09-K` at `6:55 PM`.
- **`Triggers` carry-forward at `0%`.** The `6:40 PM → 6:55 PM` transition cleanly married Plot 1's emergent counter state (Owen's already-exposed `700`-series carbon strip and `775` row) with the Play's fixed `Quay Spot Check` branch outputs (sheared `#774`, Spot-Check Tally Slip, `#B-19` under Hold Three's two-minute cargo runner, sill dial at `2.95 m`) without inventing external complications.

## (b) Micro-Decisions and `0%` Boundary Notes (Routing Space vs. Authored Seams)

1. **Routing dual-addressed turns in a three-cornered room *(Director routing choice within open `promptbook.md` space, not an authored gap)*.** In a 3-Persona procedural collision, characters frequently challenge both counterparts in a single turn (e.g., Mara in Plot 1 Turn 1 looking from Callum's pen to Owen's `700` register; Mara in Plot 1 Turn 4 instructing Owen on `#775` while issuing Callum a quay-door ultimatum; Owen in Plot 2 Turn 3 demanding Callum's recipient initials while asking Mara for a remarks-line ruling). Selecting which pressured Persona answered first was the primary micro-decision:
   - After Plot 1 Turn 1, routing to Owen (`Turn 2`) let harbor seal custody narrow Callum's maneuver room before Callum spoke (`Turn 3`).
   - After Plot 1 Turn 4, routing to Callum (`Turn 5`) answered the doorway ultimatum first so Owen (`Turn 6`) could react to both `#B-19` and `#775` before the pair stepped into the rain.
   - After Plot 2 Turn 3, routing to Mara (`Turn 4`) before Callum (`Turn 5`) respected a real contractual dependency: Callum had conditioned his signature on `#B-19` not being logged as seized goods, so Mara's remarks-line ruling had to hit `#09-K/R` before Callum could sign truthfully.
2. **`~4–6` turn allocation across three Personas (`6` turns in Plot 1, `7` turns in Plot 2) *(Promptbook edge elasticity vs. 3-role signature chains)*.** Plot 1 settled cleanly in `6` turns (`2` turns per Persona). In Plot 2, completing final clearance without stacking multiple exchanges into one turn required `7` turns because the Play's own role gates ([Duty Boarding Officer](../02-company/harbor-manifest/positions/duty-boarding-officer.md), [First Mate](../02-company/harbor-manifest/positions/first-mate.md), [Harbor Manifest Clerk](../02-company/harbor-manifest/positions/harbor-manifest-clerk.md), and [Tide-Gate Clearance Stamp](../02-company/harbor-manifest/pieces/clearance-stamp.md)) enforce a strict sequential chain after the `6:55 PM` opening (`Mara sets terms` → `Callum orders hoist & states condition` → `Owen drafts #09-K/R & logs row 774` → `Mara inks remarks ruling & sends #775 out` → `Callum initials row 774, signs declarations, & delivers tag #775` → `Owen signs Clerk's Register Attestation prior to stamp` → `Mara presses GATE-04`).
