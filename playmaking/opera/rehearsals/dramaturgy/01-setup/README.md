# SCORE rehearsal plan — Dramaturgy and Patterns

Can a Playwright use the [Dramaturgy reference](../../../references/dramaturgy.md) to turn a Pattern’s requirements into authored material that a fresh Director can stage?

## Cases

1. **Gender.** Use the legacy [Gender authoring guidance](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai/blob/c8c56c7e843eac2eb33709d06ad1d7aca4dd8c00/packages/engines/gender/playwright_instructions.md) and [Gender materials](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai/tree/c8c56c7e843eac2eb33709d06ad1d7aca4dd8c00/packages/engines/gender). Author a short harbor Plot. Identify the room doing the reading, author how each Persona carries the room’s reading under Projection, and use Shadow where the brief calls for a different reading to be concealed. Give the reading a visible consequence in the opening beat while leaving each performer’s response open.
2. **Clime.** Use [Clime](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai/tree/c8c56c7e843eac2eb33709d06ad1d7aca4dd8c00/packages/composites/clime), [Weather](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai/tree/c8c56c7e843eac2eb33709d06ad1d7aca4dd8c00/packages/engines/weather), and [Climate](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai/tree/c8c56c7e843eac2eb33709d06ad1d7aca4dd8c00/packages/engines/climate). Author a short Plot where today’s conditions matter against what the Place was built to handle. Show how the two constituent Patterns interact and what the Play supplies for the combined effect. Leave the cast’s response open.

## Run and record

- Pin the authoring brief and source files for each case. Use a fresh Playwright for each case. Supply the brief, Dramaturgy, the Playwright skill, and relevant Pattern material. Save the authored Play and its links under `02-company/`.
- Use a fresh Director and isolated performers for each case. Start the Director at the authored Play with its linked material and a Promptbook with a 0% staging limit. Keep the authoring conversation out of the staging context. Save the opening beats and any requests for missing information under `04-runs/`.
- For each case, give a fresh Director a copy with one documented requirement left unfulfilled. Record whether the Director identifies the gap and where it belongs. Keep the expected gap out of the Director’s brief.
- Under `03-observations/`, trace each requirement to the authored object that fulfils it and to its observable effect, or record where that trace breaks. For Clime, check that the interaction between Weather and Climate reaches the stage. In both cases, record whether performer decisions remained open.
- Under `05-evolution/`, propose the smallest change the evidence warrants to the Dramaturgy reference or the Pattern’s authoring guidance.
