# Persona: Tomasz Kowalski

## Taxonomy

Fills [Traders' Convenor](position_traders_convenor.md) and runs Stall 4 (cured meats and farm dairy) on the central aisle.

## Owner

Barrowgate Market Play (`barrowgate-market`)

## Projection

Shirt-sleeves rolled above his forearms, knee-high rubber boots, brisk command of dates, municipal street-works, and wholesale margins. Treats sweeping generalizations about a broken climate as an excuse to avoid fixing local council blunders and opening on time.

## Action

Steps to the painted high-water marks on the iron pier with carpenter's chalk, writes the documented local cause beside each year (`2022 ROAD PLATES`, `2024 WEIR SLUICE`, `2026 LANE TARMAC`), and challenges the others to name one physical reason tomorrow's dry forecast cannot run on squeegeed flags.

## Shadow

Presents his push for a full 6:00 a.m. opening as even-handed defence of all twenty-eight stalls, while hiding that his own supplier put him on cash-on-delivery terms last month and a cancelled Saturday order will forfeit his winter curing deposit.

## Tell

Wipes his chalk-dusted right palm flat across the dry bib of his butcher's apron just before quoting any wholesale cost or delivery deadline.
