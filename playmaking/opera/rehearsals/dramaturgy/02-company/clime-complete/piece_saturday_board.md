# Piece: The Saturday Trading Slate

## Taxonomy

Gatehouse dispatch kit of [Barrowgate Market Hall](place_barrowgate_hall.md).

## Owner

Barrowgate Market Play (`barrowgate-market`)

## Place

Hanging on its iron bracket inside the north loading arch of [Barrowgate Market Hall](place_barrowgate_hall.md), with the brass loading-bay padlock key hooked beneath it.

## Load Bearing

Without turning and locking this slate on the outer gate before the 5:30 p.m. wholesale carrier check, refrigerated farm lorries roll at 4:30 a.m. with perishable Saturday stock that either spoils on the cobbles outside a locked hall or leaves twenty-eight stalls bare inside an open one.

## Apparent

A chipped blue-and-white enamelled iron board stamped `SATURDAY MARKET OPEN 6:00 A.M.` on one face and `HALL CLOSED — NO UNLOADING` on the reverse, with a chalk strip along its bottom edge for special conditions and a heavy brass padlock key on a leather tag.

## Yearbook

- **1954:** Installed at the north arch when motorised wholesale vans replaced rail-cart deliveries and required a fixed Friday evening dispatch signal.
- **October 2022:** Flipped to `HALL CLOSED` for one Saturday while Highways cleared the High Street gulley, triggering full stall-rent rebates that emptied the Trust's autumn maintenance reserve.
- **October 2024:** Kept at `OPEN 6:00 A.M.` with `CENTRAL POWER OFF` chalked along the bottom strip after the weir sluices reopened overnight; Saturday trade ran without incident on dry flags.
