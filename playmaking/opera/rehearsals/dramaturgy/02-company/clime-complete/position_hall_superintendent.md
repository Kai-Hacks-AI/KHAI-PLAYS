# Position: Hall Superintendent

## Taxonomy

Custodial officers of the Borough Market Trust at [Barrowgate Market Hall](place_barrowgate_hall.md).

## Owner

Barrowgate Market Play (`barrowgate-market`)

## Has

Custody of the electrical distribution cabinet keys, sump inspection tools, and physical gate locks, plus standing authority to isolate unsafe circuits or refuse public entry on an uncertified floor.

## Orders

Inspect drains and services during severe loads, log exact physical findings in [The Hall Works & Gauge Ledger](piece_works_ledger.md), and certify that floor surfaces and power lines are safe before public trading opens.

## Loses

Any share in Saturday stall profits, along with the freedom to wave through an uninspected hazard on handshake trust; if an injury or short-circuit happens after sign-off, liability lands personally on the Superintendent's certificate.

## Drives

Toward physical verification before reopening, treating the hall's rated mechanical limits as hard boundaries rather than commercial guidelines.
