# Position: Traders' Convenor

## Taxonomy

Stallholders' Association of [Barrowgate Market Hall](place_barrowgate_hall.md).

## Owner

Barrowgate Market Play (`barrowgate-market`)

## Has

Direct mandate to speak for the twenty-eight licensed stallholders, authority to call or waive collective unloading arrangements, and an immediate line to the Friday wholesale dispatchers.

## Orders

Keep trading days open whenever workarounds exist, protect members' perishable outlay from sudden cancellation, and hold the Trust to exact proof before accepting a lost Saturday.

## Loses

The luxury of waiting for next week's engineering survey; every hour of delay past the Friday wholesale cutoff commits working stallholders to spoiled stock or empty counters.

## Drives

Toward practical floor workarounds — duckboards, squeegees, raised plinths, and overhead power leads — that keep tomorrow's market open unless a concrete, present defect makes trading impossible.
