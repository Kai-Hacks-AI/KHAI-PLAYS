## 1. Dramaturgy Rationale (`TO GROW`)

This Play brings the composed **Clime** Pattern (`fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/composites/clime/process_clime.md`) and its constituent **Weather** (`fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/weather/process_weather.md`) and **Climate** (`fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/climate/process_climate.md`) engines into *The Sunken Flags* (`play/play.md`) following Opera's Dramaturgy contract (`references/dramaturgy.md`).

### Ground — Opera Types and Scoped Ownership

Each Company member keeps the native responsibilities of its Opera type so no file duplicates another object's private or scoped material:

- **Borrowed `Process` Patterns (`fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/...`):** `Clime` and its three bridges (`process_clime_day.md`, `process_clime_terms.md`, `process_clime_run.md`), together with constituent `Weather` channels/bands and `Climate` modes, remain untouched canon owned by **KAI HACKS AI** (`ChBrain/khai`).

- **`Pitch` (`play/pitch_practical_reckoning.md`):** Tunes the Play's register to unsentimental civic practicality and explicitly keeps weather out of symbolic/pathetic-fallacy work.
- **`Play` (`play/play.md`):** Serves as the entry point linking the Estate, Arc, Company (with lender attribution for borrowed `ChBrain/khai` Processes), Triggers, and Stakes.

### Root — Composed Pattern Anchor, Constituent Interaction, and Requirement Scopes

- **Pattern Anchor & Type:** Rooted in the composed `Process` Pattern **[Clime](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/composites/clime/process_clime.md)** and its three bridge `Process`es — **[Clime, the Day](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/composites/clime/process_clime_day.md)**, **[Clime, the Terms](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/composites/clime/process_clime_terms.md)**, and **[Clime, the Run](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/composites/clime/process_clime_run.md)** — wired onto **[Barrowgate Market Hall](clime-complete/place_barrowgate_hall.md)** under `## Shown` and declared for the Company in **[play.md](clime-complete/play.md)** (`## Company`).
- **Constituent Patterns & What Their Interaction Adds:**
  - Constituent 1: **[Weather](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/weather/process_weather.md)** models the transient atmospheric load pressing on a place across five live channels over a standing seasonal floor. Alone, `Weather` gives a physical force hitting a building today, not what that day implies or why the building's defences were sized as they were.
  - Constituent 2: **[Climate](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/climate/process_climate.md)** models the multi-decadal atmospheric regime (`Regime`, `Seasonality`, `Extremity`, `Shift`) reflected in long-run adaptation. Alone, `Climate` gives the standing odds and baseline frame, which never flood a room on any afternoon.
  - **Added Value of Composition (`Clime`):** `Clime` adds no third physical force; it wires **draw** (`Weather`) against **odds** (`Climate`) to stage the **asymmetric inference problem** between lived experience and statistical record. The Terms do the structural governing (determining whether 48 mm of rain is routine or catastrophic via the hall's built culvert bore) while remaining invisible to direct sense; the Day does all the physical damage while remaining almost silent about what distribution produced it; and the Run (three flooded October Fridays in five years) is the only evidence living decision-makers hold while adaptation choices still matter, yet is too short to settle statistically and individually explicable one event at a time.

### Open — Authoring Choices, Conflict Resolution, and Performer Freedom

- **Eliminating false exits without removing disagreement:** In a flooded building, performers could sidestep `Clime` entirely if a clogged drain grate explained the flood away as simple janitorial neglect. Opening `play/plot_the_deluge_floor.md` (`## Stage`) with Maren Holt carrying a clean, leaf-free brass strainer basket from the north sump resolves that dodge immediately while preserving Tomasz Kowalski's accurate external explanations (the newly tarred Sawyer's Lane runoff today, pinned weir sluices in 2024, Highways road plates in 2022).
- **Two-Plot progression (`play/plot_the_deluge_floor.md` → `play/plot_the_saturday_slate.md`):** Plot 1 stages the hall under peak `Deluge` so the mismatch between live load and 1908 culverts is undeniable on the floor. Plot 2 starts once the rain slackens and the culverts begin drawing the water down twenty minutes before the 5:30 p.m. wholesale carrier lock: because tomorrow's forecast is dry and the surface pool is receding, reopening becomes practically tempting right when the third silt mark on the pier makes trusting the old baseline hardest.
- **Open choice:** All three Personas have coherent institutional and private reasons to push for full opening, full closure, or conditional partial trading (e.g., perimeter plinths and overhead festoon power only), and complete freedom to disagree to the final bell over whether three October floods in five years signify a shifted regime or three local coincidences.

### Step 1 — Source Claim (Attributed Research Distinct from Pattern Adaptation)

- **Attributed research claims (as recorded in `REFERENCES.md`):**
  - *Lorenz (1963; 1969):* In a deterministic nonperiodic flow with sensitive dependence on initial conditions, arbitrarily small differences in starting state grow without bound so particular atmospheric states are unpredictable beyond a horizon of days, whereas the long-run statistics on the system's attractor remain well defined.
  - *Hasselmann (1976):* Fast, effectively random weather fluctuations act as forcing integrated by slow system components, so long-run climate variability is what accumulated weather becomes over time.
  - *Köppen (1884 onward; 1923) & Olgyay (1963) / Givoni (1969):* A place's baseline climate class is defined by multi-decadal temperature and moisture averages rather than any single event or season, and built architecture acts as a physical modifier sized to temper expected elemental loads.
- **Pattern authors' adaptation (`process_clime.md`, `process_clime_day.md`, `process_clime_terms.md`, `playwright_instructions.md`):**
  - Translates the Lorenz–Hasselmann–Köppen relationship into a dramatic asymmetry between **a draw** (`Clime, the Day` / `Weather`) and **the odds** (`Clime, the Terms` / `Climate`): because the standing regime is knowable only at second hand from multi-decade records yet governs everything built before the afternoon arrived, while today's extreme weather is completely felt yet carries minimal diagnostic information about the distribution it came from, physical flood damage in a scene is caused not by raw rainfall millimetres alone but by the **mismatch** between the live load falling now and the recorded regime the place was built to shed.

### Step 4 — Expected Observable Beat

In the opening minutes of **[The Deluge Floor](clime-complete/plot_the_deluge_floor.md)**:

1. **Tomasz** demands the north sump be cleared so the central aisle can drain and keep chiller power for tomorrow's 6:00 a.m. market, treating the four and a half inches of water as a routine blockage under heavy rain.
2. **Maren** drops the dripping, completely clean brass strainer basket onto the weigh-table next to **[The Hall Works & Gauge Ledger](clime-complete/piece_works_ledger.md)** and points to the `22 MM/HR` 1908 brass plate above the open sump: nothing clogged the drain — forty-eight millimetres in seventy minutes simply doubled the shed rate the 1908 twin twelve-inch pipes were built for, drowning the sunken aisle while leaving **Priya's** three-inch perimeter plinth dry by half an inch.
3. Rather than ending the argument, that proof sharpens it: because one afternoon of `Water, Deluge` cannot tell anyone whether the 1908 distribution has shifted or merely delivered a rare draw compounded by Sawyer's Lane tarmac runoff, **Tomasz**, **Maren**, and **Priya** immediately split — without anyone misreading the physical facts — over whether the hall's standing terms still hold for tomorrow morning.
