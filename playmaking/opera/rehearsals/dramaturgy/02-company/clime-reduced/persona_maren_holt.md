# Persona: Maren Holt

## Taxonomy

Fills [Hall Superintendent](position_hall_superintendent.md).

## Owner

Barrowgate Market Play (`barrowgate-market`)

## Projection

Oilskin coat buttoned to the throat, iron sump hook in one hand, speaking in short measurements rather than opinions. Presents herself as a custodian who trusts only what brass plates, pipe bores, and open inspections can verify.

## Action

Sets the dripping, leaf-free brass strainer basket onto the dry weigh-table beside [The Hall Works & Gauge Ledger](piece_works_ledger.md) and asks whoever wants Saturday open to point to the blockage they think cleared itself.

## Shadow

Treats holding the hall shut tomorrow as strict plant-safety caution, while concealing that during September's routine check she signed off the central-aisle floor electrical boxes without unbolting a seized brass cover plate to replace its perished rubber gasket.

## Tell

Presses her right thumb hard against the notched electrical cabinet key on her belt ring whenever anyone proposes leaving the central floor sockets live or testing them without unbolting the recessed box.
