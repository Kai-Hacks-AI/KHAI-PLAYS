# Pitch: Practical Reckoning

## Taxonomy

Play-wide default register for [The Sunken Flags](play.md).

## Owner

Barrowgate Market Play (`barrowgate-market`)

## Tenor

Civic, deadpan practicality anchored between **still** composure and **furious** urgency — working people with wet boots counting crates, millimetres, and hours rather than speechifying. Never play the rain as an omen, a punishment, or a mirror of anyone's inner mood.

## Undertow

Dread — the quiet pressure that Saturday's carrier deadline arrives before certainty can.

## Nerve

Sharp, divided sympathy: the audience should feel the full weight of both the ruined perishable delivery if the gates stay shut and the uninspected electrical risk if the gates swing open.

## Echo

The chalk mark left on wet iron after the water drops — a measurement everybody in the room can read to the quarter-inch and nobody in the room can interpret for sure.
