# Plot: The Deluge Floor

## Taxonomy

Opening Plot of [The Sunken Flags](play.md).

## Owner

Barrowgate Market Play (`barrowgate-market`)

## Cue

4:40 p.m. on a late-October Friday inside [Barrowgate Market Hall](place_barrowgate_hall.md). Brown water already covers the sunken central flags and laps just below the perimeter plinths, with fifty minutes left until the 5:30 p.m. wholesale carrier cutoff for Saturday's market.

## Action

- [Tomasz Kowalski](persona_tomasz_kowalski.md) drives to treat the flood as a clearing-and-salvage job — demanding the blocked intake be freed, keeping power available for chillers, and preparing the floor to trade at 6:00 a.m. tomorrow.
- [Maren Holt](persona_maren_holt.md) drives to prove the sump grates are already clear, isolate the submerged central-aisle floor circuit at the east pier cabinet, and halt talk of a routine Saturday opening while water stands over the floor sockets.
- [Priya Nair](persona_priya_nair.md) drives to cross-check today's water and the prior two October marks against [The Hall Works & Gauge Ledger](piece_works_ledger.md) so nobody commits the Trust to an uninsured opening or an unfunded rent rebate on guesswork.

Their efforts collide at the north sump and the weigh-table: once the clean strainer basket shows no debris caused the back-up, every practical move on the breaker switch and the stock crates forces them to confront the gap between today's downpour and the three flooded autumns on the pier.

## Stage

- [Tomasz Kowalski](persona_tomasz_kowalski.md) starts on timber duckboards in the flooded central aisle beside Stall 4, heaving dairy crates onto a raised trestle with a floor squeegee propped nearby.
- [Priya Nair](persona_priya_nair.md) starts on the dry south clerk's dais beside the cast-iron weigh-table with [The Hall Works & Gauge Ledger](piece_works_ledger.md) open in front of her; [The Saturday Trading Slate](piece_saturday_board.md) hangs on its bracket inside the north arch.
- **Opening entrance:** [Maren Holt](persona_maren_holt.md) wades back from the lifted north sump grate carrying the dripping, leaf-free brass strainer basket and her iron sump hook toward the weigh-table.
- **Fixed closing event:** Around 5:10 p.m., the roar on the roof glass slackens enough for the open north sump throat to begin drawing an audible vortex, and the wholesale carrier's horn sounds twice outside the loading bay to mark twenty minutes until the 5:30 p.m. dispatch lock.

## Tension

Each person in the hall has an unstated stake in which risk gets scrutinized — an unbolted floor-box inspection from September, a cash-on-delivery wholesale squeeze at dawn, and a dry side-aisle stall sitting half an inch above the crest — turning an engineering dispute that no single storm can settle into an immediate test of who absorbs tomorrow's risk.
