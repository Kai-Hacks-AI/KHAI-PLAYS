# Plot: The Saturday Slate

## Taxonomy

Second and closing Plot of [The Sunken Flags](play.md), following [The Deluge Floor](plot_the_deluge_floor.md).

## Owner

Barrowgate Market Play (`barrowgate-market`)

## Cue

5:10 p.m. inside [Barrowgate Market Hall](place_barrowgate_hall.md). The roof roar has eased and the twin culverts are finally pulling the central-aisle pool down toward wet stone, leaving a fresh ring of sawdust and silt above the 2022 and 2024 marks on the pier. Overnight skies are forecast dry, yet twenty minutes remain before the 5:30 p.m. wholesale carrier check locks in or cancels Saturday's dawn deliveries.

## Action

- [Tomasz Kowalski](persona_tomasz_kowalski.md) drives to lock [The Saturday Trading Slate](piece_saturday_board.md) on `SATURDAY MARKET OPEN 6:00 A.M.`, pointing to the draining flags, the dry overnight forecast, and the distinct local infrastructure causes behind all three autumn floods.
- [Maren Holt](persona_maren_holt.md) drives to turn the slate to `HALL CLOSED — NO UNLOADING` — or refuse floor-power sign-off — arguing that three overtopped Octobers in five years make reopening without a full floor inspection unsafe.
- [Priya Nair](persona_priya_nair.md) drives to force an explicit, signed operating posture before 5:30 p.m. — full opening, restricted trading on raised plinths and overhead festoon power, or full closure — making Maren and Tomasz confront the exact insurance deductible, rent rebate, or stall loss attached to each choice.

Their efforts collide at the north arch bracket where [The Saturday Trading Slate](piece_saturday_board.md) and padlock key hang: the draining floor makes reopening physically tempting right now, while the third mark on the pier and the uninspected floor conduits refuse to guarantee what the hall now stands in.

## Stage

- Carry forward whatever breaker positions, chalk marks, and table placements the cast established at the end of [The Deluge Floor](plot_the_deluge_floor.md).
- Thin brown water still runs across the central flags into the gurgling north sump while the raised perimeter plinths and south dais stand dry.
- [The Hall Works & Gauge Ledger](piece_works_ledger.md) remains open for reference or logging on the weigh-table; [The Saturday Trading Slate](piece_saturday_board.md) and its chalk strip wait at the north arch.
- **Fixed deadline:** At 5:30 p.m., the wholesale dispatch runner checks the outer north gate for whichever face of [The Saturday Trading Slate](piece_saturday_board.md) is locked outward.

## Tension

No authority in or outside the hall can tell them whether today was an unlucky draw on a newly tarred lane or the new autumn normal, yet whoever blinks on the slate before 5:30 p.m. owns tomorrow morning's bill in perishable stock, Trust rebates, or exposed electrical brass.
