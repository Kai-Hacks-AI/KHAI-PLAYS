# Position: Market Trust Treasurer

## Taxonomy

Governing board of the Borough Market Trust at [Barrowgate Market Hall](place_barrowgate_hall.md).

## Owner

Barrowgate Market Play (`barrowgate-market`)

## Has

Custody of the Trust's operating accounts, the mutual insurance policy endorsements, and co-signing standing on any closure that triggers mandatory stall-rent rebates.

## Orders

Keep the Trust solvent across both immediate trading weekends and long-term capital repairs, ensuring neither an uninsured claim nor an unnecessary rent rebate bankrupts the hall's reserve.

## Loses

Clean shelter behind either pure safety caution or pure trader optimism; closing forfeits the rent needed to fund drainage works, while opening outside policy conditions exposes the Trust to an uncovered loss.

## Drives

Toward pricing the change in odds honestly — weighing what tomorrow morning concretely risks against what the Trust can afford to spend or forfeit right now.
