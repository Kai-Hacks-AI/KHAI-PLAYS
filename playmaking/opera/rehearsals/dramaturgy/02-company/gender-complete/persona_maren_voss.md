# Persona: Captain Maren Voss

## Taxonomy

Visiting Shipmaster of the coastal freighter *Sable Tern*.

## Owner

Greyhaven Harbor House.

## Projection

Salt-stiffened master's frieze greatcoat cut square in the shoulder, tarred sea-cap set low over close-cropped hair, heavy sea-boots, and a brass-cornered clearance pouch tucked under one arm. Before Voss speaks a word, the room assigns [Male](../fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/gender/position_male.md): the bearing of a sea captain whose bodily arrival is read on sight as a rightful claim on command space, conferring unasked authority and expecting Voss to direct the exchange without hesitation.

## Action

Without speaking, steps straight into the unlatched **Masters' Chart Well** and sets the *Sable Tern*'s leather clearance pouch flat against the mahogany rail beside the [Tide-Gate Clearance Ledger](piece_clearance_ledger.md), stopping half a pace back from the hooded brass lamp's direct glare.

## Shadow

Conceals [Female](../fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/gender/position_female.md) beneath the salt-heavy coat and weathered sea-habit — a reading Voss has kept covered across coastal ports since taking over the *Sable Tern* on an older family master's ticket whose physical-particulars column cannot survive prolonged, hostile comparison if a port room decides to enforce a contradiction. Voss's blind spot is trusting that the momentum of a conferred master's entrance will push a hurried flood-tide desk into stamping the folio before anyone slows down to scrutinize the person holding the pen.

## Tell

As the counter lamp catches the signature column of the [Tide-Gate Clearance Ledger](piece_clearance_ledger.md), Voss's left thumb hooks inside the heavy frieze cuff to tug the wool down over a narrow wrist while angling the throat and jawline slightly out of the lamp's cone.
