# Piece: Tide-Gate Clearance Ledger

## Taxonomy

Greyhaven Port Warden's Statutory Clearance Kit.

## Owner

Greyhaven Harbor House.

## Place

Open beneath the hooded brass lamp on the central counter of the [Outer Bar Clearance Office](place_harbor_office.md).

## Load Bearing

Without a stamped flood-tide line and countersignature in this folio, the breakwater signal tower will not dip the bar pennant for an outbound hull; sailing unentered forfeits coastal insurance and empowers the harbor cutter to seize the vessel's registry.

## Apparent

A calfskin-bound folio laid open across twin ruled pages — vessel draft and bar margin on the left, master's name, certificate particulars, and wet signature column on the right — flanked by two brass hand-stamps (*CLEARED ON FLOOD* in black ink, *HELD FOR SURVEY* in red) chained to the mahogany rail.

## Yearbook

- **Three winters back:** After the collier *Brinewake* grounded on the ebb and blocked the channel for four days, the harbor board added the right-hand master's particulars and bare-hand countersignature column beside every draft entry.
- **Dawn today, first flood:** The outgoing mail packet's clearance line was stamped on a wafer-thin shoal margin, its wet black ink still drying two lines above the blank row awaiting the next outbound vessel.
- **Now, twenty minutes before ebb:** The blank line sits directly under the brass lamp's cone between the two chained stamps.
