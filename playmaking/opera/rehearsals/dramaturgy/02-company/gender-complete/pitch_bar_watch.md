# Pitch: Bar Watch

## Taxonomy

Play-wide default Pitch for [Before the Bar Closes](play.md).

## Owner

Greyhaven Harbor House.

## Tenor

**Dread** — Play the counter exchange with the taut, measured compression of a harbor clock running out against rising shoal water, where small procedural gestures carry institutional weight.

## Undertow

Brisk maritime practicality: oilskins, chalked soundings, and brass stamps handled as ordinary morning work even as the ground shifts underneath both figures.

## Nerve

The prickle of watching two people step into social frames the room built before they arrived, knowing a single misjudged move under the lamp cannot be taken back.

## Echo

How quickly an ordinary public counter sorts bodies into claim and accommodation before a single word enters the air.
