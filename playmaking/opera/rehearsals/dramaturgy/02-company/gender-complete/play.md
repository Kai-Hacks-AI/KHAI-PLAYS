## Name

*Before the Bar Closes*

## Arc

A single high-pressure encounter, [The Flood-Gate Desk](plot_opening_clearance.md), tracks the twenty-minute window before ebb current closes Greyhaven's outer sandbar. What begins as a silent pre-speech sorting at the harbor counter turns a routine vessel clearance into a test of authority, exposure, and nerve.

## Triggers

- **Start of [The Flood-Gate Desk](plot_opening_clearance.md):** Morning flood slack at Greyhaven; the outer bar signal mast shows one black ball (twenty minutes of safe water remaining). [Warden Clara Dane](persona_clara_dane.md) stands on duty at the [Tide-Gate Clearance Ledger](piece_clearance_ledger.md) inside the [Outer Bar Clearance Office](place_harbor_office.md) as [Captain Maren Voss](persona_maren_voss.md) crosses the wet threshold from the quay. Fixed at the start are the physical state of the counter, the silent opening beat in which the room assigns and displays its reading of both Personas before either speaks, and the twenty-minute tide limit. Everything either performer says or chooses after that silent opening beat is open.
- **End of [The Flood-Gate Desk](plot_opening_clearance.md):** The Plot ends when [Warden Clara Dane](persona_clara_dane.md) stamps clearance or detention in the [Tide-Gate Clearance Ledger](piece_clearance_ledger.md), when [Captain Maren Voss](persona_maren_voss.md) withdraws the *Sable Tern*'s papers from the counter, or when the bar signal drops to close the tide gate.

## Stakes

Control of the *Sable Tern*'s passage across Greyhaven's outer bar — held materially in the [Tide-Gate Clearance Ledger](piece_clearance_ledger.md) — and the standing of both figures in front of a watching port room. In [The Flood-Gate Desk](plot_opening_clearance.md), Voss risks either missing the flood tide (defaulting on the *Sable Tern*'s coastal charter) or inviting lamp-lit scrutiny at the counter that jeopardizes command altogether, while Dane risks either letting a deep-laden hull ground on a razor-thin bar margin or absorbing institutional blowback from a port room ready to treat her refusal as personal hostility rather than lawful command.
