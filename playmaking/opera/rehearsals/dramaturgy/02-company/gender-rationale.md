# Dramaturgy Rationale & Trace — *Before the Bar Closes*

This document records the [Dramaturgy](../../../references/dramaturgy.md) rationale (`Ground`, `Root`, `Open`, `Weave`) for bringing the pinned **Gender** Pattern ([README](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/gender/README.md), `ChBrain/khai` @ `c8c56c7e843eac2eb33709d06ad1d7aca4dd8c00`) into [Before the Bar Closes](gender-complete/play.md), followed by one explicit four-step trace from attributed source claim to observable stage beat.

## Dramaturgy Rationale

### Ground

The Gender Pattern works entirely within Opera's existing object types and preserves each type's distinct responsibility:

- **Position (`TO HOLD`):** All three runtime files offered by the Pattern — [Gender](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/gender/position_gender.md), [Female](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/gender/position_female.md), and [Male](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/gender/position_male.md) — are Opera `Position` objects (`Has`, `Orders`, `Loses`, `Drives`). They remain untouched canon owned by **KAI HACKS AI** (`CC-BY-NC-4.0`), modeling gender not as inner psychology or anatomy, but as standing, obligations, losses, and friction imposed by a room before speech.
- **Play (`ENACTS`):** [play/play.md](gender-complete/play.md) owns the `Estate`, `Name`, `Arc`, `Company` (declaring both locally authored objects under Greyhaven Harbor House and borrowed Pattern Positions under **KAI HACKS AI**), `Triggers`, and `Stakes`, without duplicating individual Personas' private `Shadow` fields or assigned expressions.
- **Place (`TO SHOW`):** [Outer Bar Clearance Office](gender-complete/place_harbor_office.md) owns the physical and social apparatus of "the room, the eyes doing the assigning" ([playwright_instructions.md](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/gender/playwright_instructions.md)) — the counter split between the **Masters' Chart Well** and the **Petitioners' Bench**, the watchroom glass transom, and the propped-open inner doorway — without naming Persona-specific reads.
- **Persona (`TO PAST`):** [Captain Maren Voss](gender-complete/persona_maren_voss.md) and [Warden Clara Dane](gender-complete/persona_clara_dane.md) each own their respective `Projection`, `Action`, `Shadow`, and `Tell`. Each carries its room-assigned Gender expression in `Projection`, and only Voss carries a concealed Gender expression in `Shadow`.
- **Piece (`TO PLAY`):** [Tide-Gate Clearance Ledger](gender-complete/piece_clearance_ledger.md) owns the material weight of the clearance register, bare-hand countersignature column, and chained stamps under the counter lamp.
- **Plot (`TO CAST`) & Pitch (`TO TUNE`):** [The Flood-Gate Desk](gender-complete/plot_opening_clearance.md) places the Company under a twenty-minute tide deadline and sequences the silent opening beat in `Stage`, while [Bar Watch](gender-complete/pitch_bar_watch.md) sets the Play's default register without altering facts or performer choices.

### Open

The Pattern defines what holding [Female](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/gender/position_female.md) or [Male](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/gender/position_male.md) grants, demands, forfeits, and drives toward, while leaving open what concrete institution performs the reading, how institutional rank interacts with gendered reading, what object puts the gap under pressure, and how each performer answers.

- **Local Playwright decisions:**
  - **Separating institutional office from the room's read:** Giving Deputy Warden Clara Dane statutory custody of the [Tide-Gate Clearance Ledger](gender-complete/piece_clearance_ledger.md) while the harbor room still reads her as [Female](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/gender/position_female.md) — and making Captain Maren Voss the petitioner whose arrival the room reads on sight as [Male](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/gender/position_male.md) — ensures the pre-speech gender assignment cannot be confused with formal harbor rank.
  - **Making the concealed reading materially consequential:** Voss's concealed [Female](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/gender/position_female.md) reading in `Shadow` collides directly with the [Tide-Gate Clearance Ledger](gender-complete/piece_clearance_ledger.md)'s bare-hand countersignature and physical-particulars column under the hooded brass lamp (`Withheld` in [play/place_harbor_office.md](gender-complete/place_harbor_office.md)). If Voss steps back from the conferred [Male](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/gender/position_male.md) claim, the room penalizes the abdication as weakness or evasion; if Voss presses straight into the lamp cone, close inspection risks triggering the room's enforcement against contradiction.
- **Resolving structural conflicts before staging:**
  - *Opera `Persona` `Shadow` requirement vs. Gender Pattern `Shadow` constraint:* Opera's [Persona reference](../../../skills/khai-playwright/references/persona.md) requires `## Shadow` on every Persona card, whereas [playwright_instructions.md](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/gender/playwright_instructions.md) specifies *"do use Shadow only when a persona hides a read different from the one it carries"* (and the brief calls for one Persona with a concealed reading in `Shadow`). We resolved this cleanly by giving Clara Dane a concrete procedural blind spot in `## Shadow` (her unlogged dawn bar-margin waiver on the mail packet) with no Gender link, while wiring Voss's concealed [Female](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/gender/position_female.md) read in `## Shadow`.
  - *No uncast speaking extras:* The harbor watch and off-duty pilots operate through the [Outer Bar Clearance Office](gender-complete/place_harbor_office.md)'s glass transom and braided gate cord rather than requiring uncast dialogue, keeping the two-Persona Company self-contained for staging.
- **Performers' open choices:** After the fixed pre-speech opening beat, neither performer's path is scripted. Dane can enforce a hard inspection and absorb the port room's friction against non-accommodation, map a procedural detour around confrontation ([position_female.md](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/gender/position_female.md) `Drives`), or clear the vessel on conditions. Voss can wield the conferred [Male](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/gender/position_male.md) momentum across the rail, step back and absorb the room's penalty for hesitation ([position_male.md](fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/gender/position_male.md) `Drives`), or negotiate directly with Dane.

### Weave

When staging from [play/play.md](gender-complete/play.md), the Director initializes [The Flood-Gate Desk](gender-complete/plot_opening_clearance.md) inside [Outer Bar Clearance Office](gender-complete/place_harbor_office.md) under [Bar Watch](gender-complete/pitch_bar_watch.md) and runs the three-part pre-speech `Stage` sequence before the first line of dialogue:

1. **Dane's pre-speech read made visible:** Dane stands at the open [Tide-Gate Clearance Ledger](gender-complete/piece_clearance_ledger.md), flanked by the watch's dumped wire tray of unsorted pilot chits on her blotter and the propped-open doorway to the absent Harbor Master's chair behind her — visually enacting the room's assumption of clerical accommodation before she utters a syllable.
2. **Voss's pre-speech read made visible:** As Voss crosses the threshold in salt-stiffened master's frieze, the watchroom beyond the transom pulls the braided cord to unlatch the **Masters' Chart Well** gate before Voss speaks — visually enacting conferred command space opening without asking.
3. **Silent counter collision and open handoff:** Dane's silent `Action` (sweeping the chits aside and pulling the ledger and chained stamps squarely before her chest across the inner-door sightline) and `Tell` (white-knuckled thumb on the stamp rim; single glance at the bar-depth slate) meet Voss's silent `Action` (stepping into the opened command bay and laying the clearance pouch flat beside the ledger just shy of the lamp glare) and `Tell` (left thumb tugging the heavy wool cuff over a narrow wrist while angling the jaw from the lamp cone). From that beat onward, the Director applies the Pattern's social friction through the watching room whenever either Persona tests, drops, or contradicts their assigned frame, leaving the clearance decision open.
