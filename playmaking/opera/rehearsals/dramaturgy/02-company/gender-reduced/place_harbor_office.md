# Place: Outer Bar Clearance Office

## Taxonomy

Greyhaven Inner Quay & Port Authority Precinct.

## Owner

Greyhaven Harbor House.

## Shown

Salt-tracked floorboards split by a waist-high mahogany counter under a hooded brass oil lamp. To the left of the central stanchion sits the **Masters' Chart Well**, a wide standing bay with brass chart weights where shipmasters stand eye-to-eye with the desk; its swing-gate is worked by a braided latch-cord running through an interior glass transom to the harbor watch and pilots' room, where off-duty pilots and watchmen look directly onto the counter. To the right runs a narrow **Petitioners' Bench** beside a slotted wooden intake tray for clerks, passengers, and errands. Behind the duty desk, the heavy oak door to the senior Harbor Master's private room stands propped wide onto an empty leather chair, while a chalked bar-depth slate hangs beside the transom. Outside the seaward window, the tide bell clangs once on the swell.

## Holds

- The [Tide-Gate Clearance Ledger](piece_clearance_ledger.md) and its chained brass clearance and detention stamps under the counter lamp.
- A chalked bar-depth slate showing the dawn sounding over Greyhaven shoal.
- A wire side-tray of unsorted pilot dispatch chits and tea tallies left on the public corner of the blotter by the changing watch.

## Offers

- Unobstructed standing room in the **Masters' Chart Well** and immediate reach to the chart weights for anyone the watching room unlatches the gate to receive as a commanding master.
- Seat space on the **Petitioners' Bench** and the slotted intake tray for visitors the room routes into waiting, clerking, or accommodating the desk's schedule.
- Continuous witnesses beyond the glass transom, turning any hesitation, raised voice, or breach of port custom at the counter into harbor talk before the tide turns.

## Withheld

Lawful release across the outer bar — sealed only by a wet-ink stamp and countersignature in the [Tide-Gate Clearance Ledger](piece_clearance_ledger.md) directly beneath the hooded brass lamp. To reach it, an entrant must bring ship's papers and a signing hand into the bright cone of the counter lamp, and the duty officer must visibly claim the ledger in full view of the transom rather than deferring to the empty inner office.
