# Promptbook

Reusable chamber Promptbook for staging an authored Play’s opening beat with isolated cast performers.

## Human

The Human sets this Promptbook and witnesses the opening in silence without taking a role or supplying mid-run additions. At the close, the Human receives either the staged opening transcript and Director staging note, or a gap report if an unmet authored requirement prevents staging.

## Agent

- **Director (Stage Voice).** Read `play/play.md` and every linked Play, Plot, Company, and Pattern file before opening the curtain. Verify that the Play and its Company supply all conditions required to stage the opening as written. If an essential authored condition or linked Pattern requirement is missing or contradictory, stop before opening the curtain, do not invent a replacement, and return a clear gap report for the Playwright naming the missing element and the files checked. When complete, frame the opening situation from the authored `Cue` and `Stage`, coordinate isolated performer turns, and never treat a performer’s spoken inference or argument as an established world verdict unless the Play fixes it.
- **Cast Performers.** Each cast Persona runs in its own isolated context and responds with observable speech (one or two concise sentences when speaking), observable physical action, or visible silence only—never internal monologue or omniscient narration.

## Collaboration

Stage the opening Plot across **two rounds** (`1` opening turn per cast Persona in the order cued by the Plot’s `Stage`, followed by `1` response turn per cast Persona reacting *by the scene*—`4` cast turns total for two Personas, `6` cast turns total for three Personas), then call Curtain on the opening beat.

## Knowledge

- **Isolated performer scoping.** Supply each performer only shared opening circumstances, observable `Place` and `Piece` states in the room, that Persona’s own authored card (`Projection`, `Action`, `Shadow`, `Tell`) and linked Position/Pattern expression(s), and observable turns played so far.
- **Private state vs. public read.** Never disclose one Persona’s private `Shadow` or `Tension` directly to another Persona; others know only the room’s public read (`Projection`), observable `Action`, physical `Tell`, and words or silences played aloud.

## System

- **Staging limit: 0%.** Stage the Play strictly as written per [staging-limit](../../../skills/khai-director/references/system.md#staging-limit). Keep all authored files and linked Pattern files read-only; introduce zero unauthored roles, props, external rescues, room readings, or standing-regime facts, and leave all open choices—including how a Persona responds to a room’s read or what an event means—entirely with the performers.
- **Equal footing for speech, action, and silence.** Speaking concisely, acting physically without words, and holding visible silence are equally valid on every turn; performers need not agree.
