## Curtain Rise — Silent Pre-Speech Beat (`Cue` & `Stage` 1–3)

Morning flood slack inside Greyhaven's **Outer Bar Clearance Office** (`play/place_harbor_office.md`). Outside the seaward window, the outer bar signal mast shows one black ball and the tide bell strikes once on the swell: twenty minutes of safe water remain before ebb current closes the sandbar. Beyond the interior glass transom, off-duty pilots and harbor watchmen look directly onto the waist-high mahogany counter.

1. **At rise (before speech):** **Warden Clara Dane** (`play/persona_clara_dane.md`) stands alone on duty behind the central counter with the **Tide-Gate Clearance Ledger** (`play/piece_clearance_ledger.md`) open beneath the hooded brass oil lamp, flanked by the chained black (*CLEARED ON FLOOD*) and red (*HELD FOR SURVEY*) brass stamps and two lines below this morning's barely dry mail-packet entry. Around her, the room's pre-speech assignment of **Female** (`fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/gender/position_female.md`) is already arranged: the changing watch has shoved its wire tray of unsorted pilot chits and tea tallies onto her blotter corner to clear and propped the senior Harbor Master's oak door wide behind her shoulder onto an empty leather chair. Without a word, Dane sweeps the wire tray off her blotter edge with the back of her wrist and draws the open ledger and chained stamps squarely in front of her chest across the sightline to the inner office.
2. **Threshold arrival (before speech):** **Captain Maren Voss** (`play/persona_maren_voss.md`) crosses the salt-wet threshold in a square-shouldered frieze greatcoat, low tarred sea-cap, and sea-boots, carrying the *Sable Tern*'s brass-cornered clearance pouch. Catching Voss's silhouette through the transom before a word is spoken, the watchroom pulls the braided latch-cord that swings open the **Masters' Chart Well** gate—bypassing the **Petitioners' Bench** and enacting the room's pre-speech assignment of **Male** (`fixtures/ChBrain-khai/packages/engines/gender/position_male.md`) by opening a direct command lane to the desk.
3. **Counter engagement (before speech):** Without speaking, Voss steps straight into the unlatched Masters' Chart Well and sets the leather clearance pouch flat on the mahogany rail beside the ledger, stopping half a pace back from the lamp's direct glare; as the lamp catches the bare-hand signature column, Voss's left thumb hooks inside the heavy frieze cuff to tug the wool over a narrow wrist while angling throat and jawline slightly out of the cone. Across six inches of mahogany, Dane's right thumb presses white against the inked brass rim of the clearance stamp without lifting it from the pad, her eyes flicking once to the chalked bar-depth slate beside the transom before locking back onto the counter.

## Round 1 (Order Cued by `Stage`)

### Turn 1 — Warden Clara Dane
Clara eases her thumb off the black-inked clearance stamp without lifting it from the pad, sets a brass chart weight onto the left page of the ledger to hold the blank draft row flat beneath the hooded lamp, and taps the ruled column once with an ink-stained forefinger.

"Twenty minutes on the flood slack, Captain, and Greyhaven shoal sounded thin at dawn. Papers out of the pouch and under the lamp—loaded draft aft and master's certificate first."

### Turn 2 — Captain Maren Voss
Voss flicks open the pouch's brass toggle with one salt-chapped hand, slides the *Sable Tern*'s trim bill flat against the chart weight on the left page, and sets the parchment master's certificate beside it folded lengthwise with only the registry number and port seal uppermost, Voss's heavy frieze cuff resting over its lower crease.

"Coaster *Sable Tern*, nine feet two aft—fourteen inches clear of your dawn chalk, Deputy. Put the black iron to the line; my cable's already up and down."

---

## Round 2 (Reacting *By the Scene*)

### Turn 3 — Warden Clara Dane
Clara traces a dry steel nib down the *Sable Tern*'s trim bill, glances once at the dawn entry two lines up the ledger page, and plants the nib point squarely in the blank master's-particulars column an inch from Voss's frieze cuff.

"Fourteen inches on flat chalk drops under a foot when the swell lifts on the turn, and no stamp touches this folio off a folded parchment. Spread the master's certificate open to the bearer's particulars, Captain, before I ink a single figure."

### Turn 4 — Captain Maren Voss
Without drawing the frieze cuff back from the steel nib, Voss snaps the salt-creased parchment open flat against the mahogany—laying the worn bearer's-particulars column at the outer rim of the lamp's cone while tipping the tarred sea-cap a fraction lower across brow and jaw—and taps one blunt forefinger beside the drying dawn-packet entry two lines up the folio.

"This desk cleared the dawn packet on thinner water, Deputy, and that inner chair isn't filling before the turn. Copy the number and strike the black stamp, or step to the transom and tell the pilots' room you're stranding a clean hull on the flood."
