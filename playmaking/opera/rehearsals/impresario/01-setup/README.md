# SCORE rehearsal plan — House and Repertoire

Can an Impresario establish a House and hand a Playwright enough information to assemble a Company with borrowed and locally authored material?

Give a fresh Impresario a Human brief for a House with two supplied collections. Each collection offers a customs-inspection Process with the same name. Give the sources distinct lender names and stable links. Ask the Impresario to establish the House and declare the collections approved in the brief.

Give a fresh Playwright the resulting House and Repertoire links and a brief for a harbor Play. Ask for an Estate link, one borrowed inspection Process and one locally authored seal Piece. The brief should identify which lender's inspection procedure the Play needs. Keep the original Impresario conversation out of the Playwright context.

Record the brief and outputs under SCORE. Check that the House declaration names the owner and both lenders, the Company links the intended lender's Process, and the seal belongs to the Play. Note any extra information the Playwright had to request and where the skill guidance should change.
