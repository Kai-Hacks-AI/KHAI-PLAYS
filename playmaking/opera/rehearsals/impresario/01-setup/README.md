# SCORE rehearsal — House and Repertoire

Can a fresh Playwright identify a Play’s House, find its Repertoire, and choose the intended lender when two collections use the same name?

## Inputs

The Human commissions a harbor Play from Harbor House, with the Human as House owner. The Human approves two collections: [Harbor Office](../02-company/harbor-office/inspection.md) and [Shipowners’ Guild](../02-company/shipowners-guild/inspection.md). Each offers a Process named Inspection. Pin this rehearsal’s revision before handing over the links.

## Run

1. Give a fresh Impresario the House brief and the collection links. Ask for a stable House home, a House owner, and a Repertoire declaration naming and linking both lenders. Save the result under `02-company/`.
2. Start a fresh Playwright with the House and a Play brief: a visiting captain presents a damaged seal to a boarding officer before the tide turns. Use **Harbor Office’s Inspection**, then author the specific damaged seal as a local Piece. Supply the House link and the brief. Let the Playwright locate the Repertoire from the House. Keep the Impresario’s conversation and the scoring criteria out of the Playwright’s context.
3. Give a fresh reviewer the resulting `play.md` as the entry point. Ask which House is responsible and where the selected Process comes from. The reviewer should be able to follow `Estate` to the House and `Company` to the chosen lender’s exact Process.

## Record

Save the input briefs, House declaration, and authored Play under `02-company/`. Save the three responses and any requests for missing information under `04-runs/`. Record under `03-observations/` whether the Estate link identifies the responsible House, whether its Repertoire can be found, whether Company distinguishes the two lenders, and whether the local Piece has separate ownership. Record any needed Impresario or Playwright guidance under `05-evolution/`.
