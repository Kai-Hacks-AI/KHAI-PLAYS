# Harbor House

## Home

Harbor House is the House responsible for its commissioned harbor Plays. Playwrights commissioning work under Harbor House link this home under Estate.

## Owner

The Human.

## Shared Collection Governance

The Human approves which external collections Harbor House makes available and approves changes to its shared collections (subject to confirmation of any additional authorized approvers).

## Repertoire

Plays under Harbor House may draw from the collections declared in the [Harbor House Repertoire](repertoire.md).
