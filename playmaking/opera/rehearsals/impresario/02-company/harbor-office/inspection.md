# Inspection

## Taxonomy

A Harbor Office Process for checking a seal at the quay.

## Owner

Harbor Office.

## Initiated by

A vessel presents its seal at the quay.

## Direction

The boarding officer compares the seal with the manifest and records any discrepancy.

## Lever

The officer may ask to inspect the seal directly before recording a finding.

## Echo

The record preserves what the officer observed and what remains unresolved.
