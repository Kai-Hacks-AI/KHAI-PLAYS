# Inspection

## Taxonomy

A Shipowners’ Guild Process for a shipboard check before arrival.

## Owner

Shipowners’ Guild.

## Initiated by

The first mate prepares the ship for arrival.

## Direction

The mate compares the seal with the ship’s own loading record.

## Lever

The mate can call a second crew member to witness the check.

## Echo

The ship retains its record of the check and any discrepancy.
