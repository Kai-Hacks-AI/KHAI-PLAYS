## 2. Needed Guidance Refinements Surfaced by the Rehearsal

### A. `khai-playwright` (`references/play.md` & `SKILL.md`) — Missing Repertoire & Borrowed-Source Rules
- **Observed dependency in Step 2 ([02-playwright.md](../04-runs/02-playwright.md#L3-L8)):** Currently, [house-repertoire.md](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS/blob/da3de5361f2f264105e5cfcb54373b6192a0b16d/playmaking/opera/skills/khai-impresario/references/house-repertoire.md#L5-L7) states that *"The Playwright decides which member a particular Play borrows and names its source in the Company"* and *"Keep borrowed material under its lender's ownership."* However, **neither [khai-playwright/SKILL.md](../../../skills/khai-playwright/SKILL.md) nor [khai-playwright/references/play.md](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS/blob/da3de5361f2f264105e5cfcb54373b6192a0b16d/playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/references/play.md#L5-L22) mentions a House’s Repertoire, naming a borrowed member’s lender in `Company`, or preserving lender ownership vs. local Play/House ownership**.
- **Why Step 2 succeeded here:** In Step 1, the Impresario echoed those rules inside [harbor-house/repertoire.md](../02-company/harbor-house/repertoire.md#L13) (`## Governance` and `## Shared Names Across Declared Collections`), which the Step 2 Playwright read and followed (`"Following ... the Repertoire’s governance rule for shared names, the Play ... names Harbor Office explicitly as lender and owner in Company"`, [02-playwright.md](../04-runs/02-playwright.md#L7)). Had a House written a terse Repertoire listing only lender names and links, `khai-playwright` alone would not have instructed the Playwright to name the lender in `Company` or distinguish borrowed ownership from local Piece ownership.
- **Recommended minimal addition to [khai-playwright/references/play.md](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS/blob/da3de5361f2f264105e5cfcb54373b6192a0b16d/playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/references/play.md):**
  1. Under `## Estate` (`#L5-L7`), add: *"Follow the House’s Repertoire when selecting external collections available to the Play."*
  2. Under `## Company` (`#L17-L20`), add: *"When borrowing an object from a declared collection, link the borrowed file, name its lender in Company (especially when collections share a name), and leave the borrowed file under its lender’s ownership while giving locally authored objects local House or Play ownership."*

### B. `khai-impresario` (`SKILL.md` & `references/house-repertoire.md`) — Guaranteeing House → Repertoire Linkability
- **Observed handoff nuance ([01-setup/README.md](../01-setup/README.md#L12) vs. [khai-impresario/SKILL.md](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS/blob/da3de5361f2f264105e5cfcb54373b6192a0b16d/playmaking/opera/skills/khai-impresario/SKILL.md#L11)):** `khai-impresario/SKILL.md` tells the Impresario to *"Give the Playwright the House and Repertoire links when commissioning a Play,"* while Step 2 tested supplying **only** the House link (`harbor-house/house.md`) and letting the Playwright locate the Repertoire from the House (matching a Reviewer who follows `Estate` from `play.md`).
- **Why it worked and how to lock it in:** [house-repertoire.md](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS/blob/da3de5361f2f264105e5cfcb54373b6192a0b16d/playmaking/opera/skills/khai-impresario/references/house-repertoire.md#L3) says *"Keep the declaration at the House's home."* When the Impresario split `house.md` and `repertoire.md` into separate files in `harbor-house/`, it added `## Repertoire` in `house.md` linking `[Harbor House Repertoire](repertoire.md)` ([harbor-house/house.md](../02-company/harbor-house/house.md#L15-L17)). Making this explicit in [house-repertoire.md](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS/blob/da3de5361f2f264105e5cfcb54373b6192a0b16d/playmaking/opera/skills/khai-impresario/references/house-repertoire.md#L3)—*"Keep the declaration in the House’s home file or link to it directly from the House’s home"*—ensures `Estate` always leads to the Repertoire even when they live in separate files.

### C. `khai-impresario` (`references/house-repertoire.md`) — Link Concrete Entry Files Rather Than Bare Directories
- **Observed friction in Step 3 ([03-reviewer.md](../04-runs/03-reviewer.md#L91)):** In [harbor-house/repertoire.md](../02-company/harbor-house/repertoire.md#L20-L29), the Impresario wrote `Source (local): [../harbor-office/](../harbor-office/) — [../harbor-office/inspection.md](../harbor-office/inspection.md)`. Because `harbor-office/` and `shipowners-guild/` have no `README.md` index document, bare directory links do not resolve to a Markdown page on static viewers.
- **Recommended minimal clarification in [house-repertoire.md](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS/blob/da3de5361f2f264105e5cfcb54373b6192a0b16d/playmaking/opera/skills/khai-impresario/references/house-repertoire.md#L3):** Clarify *"a link to the source"* as linking to the collection’s index document or borrowed member file(s) rather than a bare directory path when no index document exists.
