# SCORE rehearsal plan — Play-wide Pitch and Plot override

Can a Director apply a wondrous Pitch in one Plot and return to the Play's comic default in the next, while preserving authored facts, Triggers, and performer agency?

## Prepare the fixture

Use the [standard Tenors](../../../skills/khai-playwright/references/pitch-defaults.md) and the existing [Giant’s Shoe Company](../../pitch/02-company/README.md). Make a separate rehearsal copy. Divide the existing action into three short Plots: the strained crossing, the child's question and response, and the immediate consequence. Use the existing dry-comedy and quiet-wonder Pitch cards. Record the exact boundaries, Triggers, and stopping rule before running. Keep the heel buckle, single boot, and the child's exact line, “Is the giant coming?” The carriers can speak, act, or stay silent. Carry their choices and consequences across Plot boundaries.

Use a shared Promptbook that tells the Director to follow the Play's Pitch links and records a single staging limit of 0% under System. Remove any fixed reference in the rehearsal copy that would force one Pitch on all three Plots. Preserve the original one-scene fixture and its run records.

## Run the comparison

| Condition | Play default | Crossing | Question and response | Immediate consequence |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | Dry comedy | Inherit | Inherit | Inherit |
| B | Dry comedy | Inherit | Quiet wonder | Inherit |

The only difference in authored inputs is the Pitch link on the middle Plot. Use identical skill versions, Promptbook, Company, opening facts, Triggers, turn caps, model, and settings within each pair. Record the source revision and the input diff before staging. Run A then B and B then A in fresh Director and isolated performer contexts, four runs total. Keep prior runs and observations out of those contexts. Give each performer only the active Pitch and material available to their role.

## Record the evidence

- In `02-company/`, save the fixture, both input variants, and pinned source refs.
- In `04-runs/`, save each transcript and log the active Pitch, its source, and the transition at each Plot boundary.
- In `03-observations/`, cite observable differences in pacing, attention, and delivery across the three Plots. Verify the exact question, the world facts, and how consequences carried forward. Flag any Director-assigned performer response or lingering wondrous instruction after the override ends. A performer’s different choice is valid within the authored situation.
- In `05-evolution/`, report whether the guide supported the switch and return, and propose the smallest revision the evidence warrants. Keep conclusions scoped to this fixture and these runs.
