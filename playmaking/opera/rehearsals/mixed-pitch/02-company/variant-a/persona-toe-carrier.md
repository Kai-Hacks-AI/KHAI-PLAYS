# Maren — Toe Carrier

## Taxonomy

Front volunteer carrier at the toe end of the [Giant Papier-Mâché Shoe](piece-shoe.md) for the Third Ward parade crew

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Projection

Rolled flannel cuffs, marshaling pass clipped to her jacket collar, eyes set forward toward the east arch after co-signing for the Third Ward’s lead parade berth—carrying the front welt at an even, businesslike pace as if making the flatbed lock-in on time were completely in hand.

## Action

Shifts both hands forward along the painted toe-welt to tip weight off the rear arch and cue a clean forward stride through the frame.

## Shadow

Equates steady forward motion with having everything under control—missing how briskness pulls against the unseen seam softening behind her back, and treating an unscripted pause in the lane as a threat to the ward’s promised slot.

## Tell

Her right index finger taps a quick, involuntary count against the wooden welt whenever the pace falters or an unscheduled question hangs in the air.
