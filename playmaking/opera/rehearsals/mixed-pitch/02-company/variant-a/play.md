# The Giant’s Shoe

Staged under the shared [Promptbook](../promptbook.md).

## Estate

[KHAI-PLAYS](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS)

## Name

*The Giant’s Shoe*

## Arc

Three continuous moments across [Market Square](place-square.md) turn a failing parade carry into an unscripted encounter at eye level and an immediate physical reckoning on open stone:

1. **[Plot 1: The Strained Crossing](plot-1-strained-crossing.md).** Halfway across [Market Square](place-square.md)—thirty yards short of the east arch flatbed and three paces from the dry fountain plinth—the Tuesday-night paste patch under the [Giant Papier-Mâché Shoe](piece-shoe.md) begins creasing inward over [Tomas](persona-heel-carrier.md)’s hands while [Maren](persona-toe-carrier.md) sets pace at the toe welt.
2. **[Plot 2: Question and Response](plot-2-question-and-response.md).** As their stride brings the eight-foot boot alongside the central fountain’s chalk marshaling edge, a child steps down beside the lane, looks up at the boot, and asks verbatim, “Is the giant coming?”—catching both volunteers mid-load with no giant built behind them and the heel seam yielding.
3. **[Plot 3: Immediate Consequence](plot-3-immediate-consequence.md).** Carrying forward whatever pace, grip, route, words, or silence they just committed to beside the child, Maren and Tomas take the next decisive strides under the warming brass band’s pressure, settling the immediate fate of the softening heel, the crossing, and the child watching from the chalk line.

## Triggers

- **Start of [Plot 1: The Strained Crossing](plot-1-strained-crossing.md):** Fixed mid-crossing opening in [Market Square](place-square.md), thirty yards short of the east arch flatbed and three paces from the waist-high fountain plinth. Only this single boot exists for the Third Ward entry. As the damp heel seam begins buckling inward over Tomas’s fingers, Tomas takes Turn 1 and Maren takes Turn 2 (`2` carrier turns).
- **End of Plot 1 -> Start of [Plot 2: Question and Response](plot-2-question-and-response.md):** Closes after Turn 2 as their stride brings the boot abreast of the central fountain’s chalk line, carrying forward their exact grip, cadence, and heel strain. Plot 2 opens immediately as a child steps beside the flank of the boot on the chalk edge, looks up at the eight-foot shoe, and asks verbatim: “Is the giant coming?”
- **End of Plot 2 -> Start of [Plot 3: Immediate Consequence](plot-3-immediate-consequence.md):** Plot 2 runs `2–3` carrier turns (`hard cap 3`), leaving both volunteers free to speak, act without words, or stay silent, and closes once both have committed their response to the question and their immediate handling of the shoe. Plot 3 opens on the very next beat—carrying forward what was said, withheld, or adjusted on the boot—as brass warm-ups beyond the east arch tighten toward step-off.
- **End of Plot 3 (Close of Play):** Runs `2` carrier turns (`1` turn each for Maren and Tomas) executing the immediate physical consequence for the buckling heel, their route (`straight lane` toward the east arch vs. `plinth` brace or pause), and the child watching at the chalk line, then ends cleanly at Curtain.

## Stakes

- **The heel seam vs. the marshaling clock:** Pressing straight across the remaining thirty yards of [Market Square](place-square.md) protects Maren’s co-signed lead parade berth at the risk of folding Tomas’s damp heel patch through in front of the crowd; slowing, tilting, or veering three paces off-line to the fountain plinth spares the shell at the risk of stalling in plain view and forfeiting step-off.
- **The lone boot at eye level:** Neither volunteer built a giant behind the [Giant Papier-Mâché Shoe](piece-shoe.md)—whatever they say, gesture, or hold back when the child asks “Is the giant coming?” lands simultaneously on the watching child and on the strained papier-mâché between their hands.
