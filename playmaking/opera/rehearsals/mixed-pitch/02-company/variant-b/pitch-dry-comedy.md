# Pitch — The Giant’s Shoe

## Taxonomy

[The Giant’s Shoe](play.md)

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Tenor

Play the question with the dry practicality of a parade logistics problem.

## Undertow

The strain of failing papier-mâché in tired hands while someone beside the lane watches closely enough to take the boot at face value.

## Nerve

A catch of suspense over whether the softening heel can survive whatever answer, gesture, or silence the carriers risk mid-step.

## Echo

The sight of one oversized shoe crossing open stone, and how much of a giant depends on how two tired people carry the weight.
