# Plot 1: The Strained Crossing

## Taxonomy

Opening Plot of [The Giant’s Shoe](play.md) (inherits the Play’s default Pitch).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Cue

Halfway across [Market Square](place-square.md), thirty yards short of the waiting flatbed beyond the east arch and three paces inside the waist-high stone plinth by the dry central fountain. Brass instruments warm up out of sight beyond the arch in damp autumn air while early onlookers line the low rope barriers. Beneath the rear counter of the eight-foot [Giant Papier-Mâché Shoe](piece-shoe.md), Tuesday night’s uncured flour-paste patch begins creasing inward over [Tomas](persona-heel-carrier.md)’s knuckles.

## Action

[Tomas](persona-heel-carrier.md) works at the rear rim to support the softening heel seam with his palms and check their stride before the damp shell folds through, while [Maren](persona-toe-carrier.md) leads forward at the painted wooden toe-welt to hold an even, businesslike marshaling pace toward the east arch lock-in. Their efforts pull against one another through the rigid wooden carry-welt between them.

## Stage

At curtain, Maren (front toe-welt, facing east) and Tomas (rear heel counter, watching the seam directly) carry the single [Giant Papier-Mâché Shoe](piece-shoe.md) knee-high along the white chalk marshaling lane; no second float or giant follows behind them. Fixed sequence (`2` carrier turns): Tomas takes Turn 1 as the heel patch yields over his fingers; Maren takes Turn 2 from the toe welt in response to whatever drag, cadence shift, or word reaches her, bringing the flank of the boot abreast of the central fountain’s chalk edge.

## Tension

Tomas feels every fresh inward crease as a public verdict on his parish-shed build after promising the workshop crew their lone boot would ride the lead wagon upright, making him reluctant to announce aloud in the open square that his patch is failing. Maren co-signed for the Third Ward’s lead berth after last year’s forfeit and reads any unscheduled slackening in the lane as a threat to making step-off before the marshal scratches their entry.
