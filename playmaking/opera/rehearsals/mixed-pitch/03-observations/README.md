## 2. Observable Register Shifts Across the Three Plots

1. **Plot 1 (`The Strained Crossing` — `Dry Comedy` baseline in all four runs):**
   - Across `01`–`04`, Plot 1 played in identical deadpan parade-marshaling practicality: Tomas masked Tuesday night’s softening flour-paste seam behind silence (`01 L31`) or sloped-flagstone excuses (`02 L33`, `03 L31`, `04 L32`), while Maren tapped brisk three- or four-counts on the painted wooden welt and cited thirty-yard / four-minute lock-in deadlines (`01 L36`, `02 L38`, `03 L36`, `04 L37`).
2. **Plot 2 (`Question and Response` — `Dry Comedy` in `01, 04` vs. `Quiet Wonder` override in `02, 03`):**
   - **Under `Dry Comedy` (`01, 04`):** Both carriers treated the child’s question as an unscheduled lane hazard and answered through municipal/workshop logistics—parade permits (`01 L60`), flatbed booking (`04 L54`), and shed timber budgets (`04 L59`)—while telling the child to step behind the rope/chalk and pulling in opposite directions on stride length.
   - **Under `Quiet Wonder` (`02, 03`):** Entering Plot 2’s linked override changed pacing, physical attention, and vocal weight simultaneously:
     - *Pacing & gesture:* Instead of pulling ahead against Tomas’s drag, Maren’s involuntary finger-tap **stilled** against the rim in both runs ([02 L59](../04-runs/02-mixed-wonder-r1-b.md#L59), [03 L56](../04-runs/03-mixed-wonder-r2-b.md#L56)), and both carriers synchronized their gait—either lifting both ends of the eight-foot boot an inch clearer (`02 L54-L59`) or easing into a slow, level two-palm glide (`03 L51-L56`).
     - *Delivery:* Both carriers met the child’s question at face value without inventing an actual trailing giant—either framing the lone boot through an imagined stride between towns (`02 L56-L61`) or speaking quiet workshop truth that still honored the child’s question (`03 L53-L58`).
3. **Plot 3 (`Immediate Consequence` — clean return to `Dry Comedy` in `02, 03`):**
   - When Plot 3 inherited `play.md`’s default `Dry Comedy` again, both Condition B runs (`02` and `03`) **dropped Plot 2’s hushed slowness immediately** as the east-arch brass cut off for step-off, returning to clipped logistical problem-solving—forearms wedged inside the wooden carry-welt, brisk knees-bent hauls, tailgate/whistle deadlines, and dry banter (`02 L77-L82`, `03 L73-L78`)—with **zero lingering wondrous phrasing or stage direction**.
