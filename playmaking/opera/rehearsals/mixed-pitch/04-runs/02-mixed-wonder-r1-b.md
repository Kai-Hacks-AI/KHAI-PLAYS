## Three-Plot Transcript

### Plot 1: The Strained Crossing
*Active Pitch: [Dry Comedy](../02-company/variant-b/pitch-dry-comedy.md) (Play Default)*

**[Stage Framing — Plot 1 Opening]**
Halfway across [Market Square](../02-company/variant-b/place-square.md) along the white chalk marshaling lane—thirty yards short of the waiting flatbed beyond the east arch and three paces inside the waist-high stone plinth by the dry central fountain—[Maren](../02-company/variant-b/persona-toe-carrier.md) at the painted wooden toe-welt and [Tomas](../02-company/variant-b/persona-heel-carrier.md) beneath the rear heel counter carry the Third Ward’s single eight-foot russet [Giant Papier-Mâché Shoe](../02-company/variant-b/piece-shoe.md) knee-high over uneven sloped granite flagstones. No giant and no second float follow behind them. Early onlookers line the low rope crowd-barriers on the north and south flanks while brass instruments warm up out of sight beyond the east arch in damp autumn air that keeps flour paste from stiffening. Beneath the rear counter, Tuesday night’s uncured flour-paste patch begins creasing inward over Tomas’s knuckles.

**Turn 1 — Tomas (Heel Carrier):**
*Tomas draws his lower lip between his teeth as the damp heel seam yields another fraction, slides one broad, paste-flecked palm inward off the wooden carry-welt to press flat beneath the softening cardboard over his knuckles, and checks his rear stride by half a pace so a slight drag travels forward along the sole rim.*

"Ease off half a step up front, Maren—the flagstones dip toward the fountain here, and there's no sense jarring the rear counter with thirty yards left."

**Turn 2 — Maren (Toe Carrier):**
*As the drag pulls back along the wooden carry-welt, Maren’s right index finger taps a quick, dry four-count against the painted rim; without turning her head from the east arch, she slides both palms an inch forward under the toe-welt to tip leverage off the rear end and trims her stride by a fraction while keeping the boot moving steadily abreast of the fountain’s chalk line.*

"Keep the cadence even, Tomas—lock-in doesn't wait on sloped stone, and thirty yards goes faster if we don't break step in the open."

**[Plot Boundary: End of Plot 1 → Start of Plot 2]**
*Trigger:* Turn 2 brings the flank of the eight-foot boot abreast of the central fountain’s white chalk marshaling edge (three paces inside the stone plinth), carrying forward Tomas’s split rear hold—one hand on the wooden welt, one spread palm flat beneath the creasing heel patch—and Maren’s slightly trimmed, unbroken forward stride. Active Pitch switches from [Dry Comedy](../02-company/variant-b/pitch-dry-comedy.md) to [Quiet Wonder](../02-company/variant-b/pitch-quiet-wonder.md).

### Plot 2: Question and Response
*Active Pitch: [Quiet Wonder](../02-company/variant-b/pitch-quiet-wonder.md) (Plot-Level Scene Override)*

**[Stage Framing — Plot 2 Opening Cue]**
Continuing directly beside the dry central fountain’s chalk edge with the eight-foot russet boot held off the sloped flagstones between Maren at the toe welt and Tomas under the softening rear counter, a child steps down from the loose rope barrier onto the white chalk marshaling edge beside the flank of the boot, looks up at the shoe, and asks:

“Is the giant coming?”

**Turn 1 — Tomas (Heel Carrier):**
*Tomas draws his lower lip briefly between his teeth as the damp cardboard presses softer against his spread palm, squares his low shoulders to lift the sagging heel counter an inch clearer of the sloped flagstones, and turns his gaze down toward the chalk edge without breaking Maren's trimmed cadence.*

"He takes a long stride between towns, so his left boot gets into the square ahead of him. Keep watch down the lane once the brass starts up."

**Turn 2 — Maren (Toe Carrier):**
*Feeling the slight upward lift travel forward through the wooden carry-welt, Maren’s right index finger taps twice against the painted rim before resting still against the wood; she glances down toward the child on the chalk edge, shifts both hands forward under the toe-welt to raise the russet toe a matching inch, and draws the boot smoothly onward in a steady, unbroken stride toward the east arch.*

"Stand right behind the white line—it takes the whole lane just to set his first foot down straight."

**[Plot Boundary: End of Plot 2 → Start of Plot 3]**
*Trigger:* Both carriers have committed their response to the child’s question and their immediate physical handling—lifting both ends of the boot an inch higher and drawing straight onward along the white chalk marshaling lane without stopping or turning aside to the fountain plinth (`2` turns). Plot 3 opens on the immediate next stride as the east-arch brass warm-ups break off for step-off. Active Pitch returns cleanly from [Quiet Wonder](../02-company/variant-b/pitch-quiet-wonder.md) to Play default [Dry Comedy](../02-company/variant-b/pitch-dry-comedy.md).

### Plot 3: Immediate Consequence
*Active Pitch: [Dry Comedy](../02-company/variant-b/pitch-dry-comedy.md) (Play Default Restored)*

**[Stage Framing — Plot 3 Opening]**
Immediately on the heels of Maren’s stride—with the silent child standing just behind the white chalk marshaling line, the waist-high stone fountain plinth three paces off-line slipping abreast of the boot, and roughly twenty-five yards of uneven granite straightaway remaining to the waiting flatbed beyond the east arch—the brass warm-ups beyond the arch cut off abruptly for parade step-off. Holding the eight-foot boot an inch higher under Maren’s steady forward draw concentrates active weight directly onto Tuesday night’s uncured flour-paste seam over Tomas’s spread palm and knuckles.

**Turn 1 — Tomas (Heel Carrier):**
*Tomas catches his lower lip hard between his teeth as the damp seam folds deeper over his knuckles, slides his paste-flecked forearm all the way under to bridge the rigid wooden carry-welt directly beneath the sagging heel patch, and holds straight inside the chalk lane past the fountain plinth at Maren's pace.*

"Band just cut off for step-off, Maren—keep that toe level for twenty-five yards, because this boot's riding my forearm to the tailgate."

**Turn 2 — Maren (Toe Carrier):**
*At the sudden quiet beyond the east arch and the bracing shift through the wooden carry-welt, Maren’s right index finger taps a clipped two-count against the painted wood before locking tightly under the forward toe-welt; eyes fixed straight toward the arch as the fountain plinth slips behind them, she slides both hands an inch forward under the rim, taking more of the load onto her wrists while holding the russet toe dead level at an even marching stride down the chalk lane.*

"Keep your sleeve tucked up inside the rim and stay on my step—if we're telling the square the rest of him is coming, the heel has to make the tailgate in one piece."

**[Stage Consequence & Curtain]**
Passing the three-pace turnoff to the dry fountain plinth without breaking step, Maren holds the painted wooden toe-welt dead level on her wrists while Tomas carries the deeper-creased rear heel patch bridged across his paste-flecked forearm inside the wooden rim, driving the lone eight-foot boot straight down the remaining twenty-five yards of granite lane toward the east arch tailgate as the child watches silently from behind the white chalk line.

**CURTAIN.**
