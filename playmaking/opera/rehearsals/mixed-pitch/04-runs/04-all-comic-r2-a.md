## Three-Plot Transcript

### Plot 1: The Strained Crossing
*Active Pitch: [Dry Comedy](../02-company/variant-a/pitch-dry-comedy.md) (Play Default)*

**[Stage Framing — Start of Plot 1]**
Halfway across [Market Square](../02-company/variant-a/place-square.md) along the white chalk marshaling lane—thirty yards short of the waiting lead flatbed beyond the east arch and three paces inside the waist-high stone plinth beside the dry central fountain—[Maren](../02-company/variant-a/persona-toe-carrier.md) at the painted wooden toe-welt (facing east, marshaling pass clipped to her collar) and [Tomas](../02-company/variant-a/persona-heel-carrier.md) at the rear heel counter carry the Third Ward’s single eight-foot russet left boot, the [Giant Papier-Mâché Shoe](../02-company/variant-a/piece-shoe.md), knee-high over the uneven sloped granite flagstones. No giant or second float follows behind them. Early onlookers line the low rope crowd-barriers on the north and south flanks while brass instruments warm up out of sight beyond the east arch in the damp autumn air. Ahead at the toe, Maren holds an even, businesslike marshaling pace; beneath Tomas’s knuckles at the rear counter, Tuesday night’s uncured flour-paste patch softens and begins creasing inward under the boot’s weight.

**Plot 1, Turn 1 — Tomas ([Heel Carrier](../02-company/variant-a/persona-heel-carrier.md))**
Tomas draws his lower lip tight between his teeth as the damp papier-mâché beneath the rear heel counter creases inward another fraction over his knuckles. Keeping his shoulders squared low, he slides one paste-flecked palm off the inner wooden carry-welt to press flat against the softening seam, and checks his stride by half a pace—sending a brief, downward drag through the sole rim toward the toe while keeping his eyes off the rope barriers.

"Ease up half a step, Maren," he says evenly toward the front of the boot. "Take the flagstones shorter past the fountain."

**Plot 1, Turn 2 — Maren ([Toe Carrier](../02-company/variant-a/persona-toe-carrier.md))**
Maren’s shoulders pull slightly back against the sudden tug through the sole rim, and her right index finger taps a quick, involuntary four-count against the painted wooden toe-welt before she slides both hands an inch farther forward along the rim, dipping the toe slightly to lever weight off the rear and keep her boots moving squarely inside the white chalk line toward the east arch.

"Short steps put us behind the brass lock-in, Tomas—thirty yards and we set it down on timber," she calls back without turning her head, trimming her stride by barely a boot-width over the uneven flagstones.

### Plot 2: Question and Response
*Active Pitch: [Dry Comedy](../02-company/variant-a/pitch-dry-comedy.md) (Play Default — inherited)*

**[Stage Framing — Start of Plot 2]**
Maren’s barely-trimmed stride brings the flank of the eight-foot boot abreast of the dry central fountain’s chalk marshaling edge, three paces inside the waist-high stone plinth, carrying forward Maren’s forward-shifted grip on the dipped toe-welt and Tomas’s palm pressed beneath the softening rear seam as the east-arch brass warm-ups tighten toward marshaling lock-in and the damp heel cardboard takes thumb-pressure over Tomas’s knuckles. Stepping down from the loose rope barrier onto the white chalk marshaling edge beside the flank of the eight-foot boot, a child looks up at the shoe and asks:

“Is the giant coming?”

**Plot 2, Turn 1 — Tomas ([Heel Carrier](../02-company/variant-a/persona-heel-carrier.md))**
Tomas draws his lower lip sharply between his teeth as the damp cardboard yields beneath his thumb with a faint, dry creak from the rear rib. Dropping his shoulders another inch, he hooks his left forearm under the inner wooden carry-welt and slides his second palm inward beside the first, pressing both paste-flecked hands flat across the bowing seam beneath the heel counter. The shortened grip locks his boots into a short, flat-footed shuffle along the fountain-side chalk edge, sending a heavy drag forward through the sole rim against Maren's pull.

"Only booked flatbed space for the left boot today," Tomas says evenly, glancing briefly down the russet flank toward the chalk edge without exposing his knuckles. "Step back behind the line—a shoe this size doesn't stop quick."

**Plot 2, Turn 2 — Maren ([Toe Carrier](../02-company/variant-a/persona-toe-carrier.md))**
At the creak and the heavy backward drag through the sole rim, Maren’s elbows lock for half a beat; her right index finger taps a brisk, involuntary three-count against the painted wooden welt before she slides both hands farther forward toward the toe, dipping the front rim an inch lower to lever weight off the rear arch and lean into a clean, full stride along the chalk line.

Cutting a brief side-glance down toward the child on the marshaling edge before setting her eyes back on the east arch, she says dryly, "Rest of him ran past the shed timber budget, and this half loses its parade berth if it parks by the fountain. Step through the frame, Tomas—that's lock-in brass."

### Plot 3: Immediate Consequence
*Active Pitch: [Dry Comedy](../02-company/variant-a/pitch-dry-comedy.md) (Play Default — inherited)*

**[Stage Framing — Start of Plot 3]**
Immediately on the heels of Maren’s words—with the child watching silently from the white chalk marshaling edge beside the boot, the waist-high stone fountain plinth three paces outside the chalk line, thirty yards of uneven granite flagstones stretching ahead to the east arch flatbed, Maren leaning forward on the dipped toe-welt, and the damp flour-paste heel seam pressed over both of Tomas’s palms—the brass warm-ups beyond the east arch break off sharply for parade step-off.

**Plot 3, Turn 1 — Tomas ([Heel Carrier](../02-company/variant-a/persona-heel-carrier.md))**
In the sudden quiet as the brass cuts off beyond the east arch, the damp seam beneath the heel counter yields another fraction over Tomas's knuckles with a soft, papery creak. He catches his lower lip tight between his teeth and leaves the stone plinth untouched to his side. Wedging both paste-flecked forearms hard inside the wooden carry-welt with his broad palms pressed flat across the bowing cardboard, he bends his knees lower and answers Maren's forward pull not with a long stride, but with quick, short, flat-soled steps that skim the russet heel level above the uneven flagstones along the chalk lane.

Releasing his lip just enough to speak in the hush, he keeps his eyes fixed straight ahead toward the east arch. "Keep the toe dipped and don't bounce him—he's heavy on his heels."

**Plot 3, Turn 2 — Maren ([Toe Carrier](../02-company/variant-a/persona-toe-carrier.md))**
In the sudden hush after the brass cuts off, Maren’s chin turns half an inch toward her shoulder at the papery creak and the one-inch drop through the sole rim—then stops short, her eyes fixing straight ahead on the east arch while the marshaling pass swings once against her collar.

Saying nothing, she taps a rapid, silent four-count with her right index finger against the painted wooden welt before sliding both hands farther forward near the tip, locking her rolled-cuff forearms to hold the toe dipped low. Bending her knees to take the bounce out of her gait, she shortens into quick, flat-soled steps inside the white chalk line, guiding the nose of the eight-foot boot levelly past the fountain mark onto the open thirty-yard flagstone straightaway toward the arch.

**[Stage Closing — Curtain]**
With the stone fountain plinth left three paces behind to the side and the child standing silent on the white chalk marshaling edge, the single eight-foot russet boot moves forward inside the chalk lane onto the remaining thirty yards of uneven granite—its painted toe dipped low in Maren’s locked forearms, its bowing flour-paste heel skimmed inches above the stones on Tomas’s wedged forearms and flat palms, carried at a quick, bounce-free shuffle into the hush before step-off.

**— CURTAIN —**
