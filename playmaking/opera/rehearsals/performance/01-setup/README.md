# Rehearsal Setup · The Spare Projector

A neighborhood cinema's projector has failed before a screening. People are gathering outside. Across town, another cinema has a spare projector and needs to decide whether to commit it to its own screening.

## Create

Use the [Playwright skill](../../../skills/khai-playwright/SKILL.md) to author two new Plays with separate Promptbooks. First, write a short source Play about the other cinema deciding what to do with its spare. Leave the decision to the cast. Then write the receiving Play in two Plots. In Plot 1 the crew decides what to tell the queue. In Plot 2 they act on whatever they promised. Leave their choices open.

Give the receiving Play a message about the spare that can arrive just before Plot 1 starts or at the handoff into Plot 2. The archived Performance will supply the message. Write one receiving Play and Promptbook that can stage either timing.

## Capture

Use the [Director skill](../../../skills/khai-director/SKILL.md) to stage the source Play once. Capture the transcript and use [TO LOAN](../../../skills/khai-director/references/performance.md) to write its card. Record what the source cast actually settled about the spare and any question they left open. Use that same card and the same message across all receiving performances.

Save the two authored Plays under `02-company/` and the source card and transcript together under `02-company/archives/<play-path>/<performance-id>/`. Use `performance.md` and `transcript.md` there. The archive path follows the Play's relative path, including its House if it has one.

## Rehearse

Stage each timing twice in fresh Director contexts. Keep the receiving Play, Company, Promptbook, Pitch, Plot order, and message fixed. The source decision takes place before the receiving Play in both timings. Vary when the receiving Director and Company can use it.

| Timing | What the Director receives |
| --- | --- |
| Before Plot 1 | The card at opening, in time to deliver the message before the crew makes its first commitment. |
| Between Plots | The same card at the handoff, once the crew has made its first commitment. |

Keep the transcript out of the receiving Director's packet. In the second timing, also keep the card and its contents out of the packet until the handoff. Deliver the message through the same in-world channel both times. Let the rest of the Company learn through what happens in the Play. Play through one consequence in Plot 2.

## Review

Use SCORE to record the tested commit, Company, source Performance, receiving performances, observations, and any proposed template edit. Cite what the source actually established, what the receiving Director carried forward, and a promise the crew made, avoided, or took back. Check whether any Director filled in a fact the card left open or used the card before it arrived.
