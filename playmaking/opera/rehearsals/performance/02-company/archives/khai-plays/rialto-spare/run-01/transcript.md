Twenty minutes to the `7:15 PM` lobby door call for Rialto's `7:30 PM` archival 35mm double-feature. Warm ozone, hot machine oil, and steady 24-fps chatter fill [The Rialto Projection Booth](../../../../khai-plays/rialto-spare/places/rialto-booth.md); rain streaks the alley transom beside the freight landing.

- **Machine 1** stands healthy and threaded with Reel 1.
- **Machine 2** is seven minutes into its loaded 24-fps [Machine 2 Warm-Up Run](../../../../khai-plays/rialto-spare/processes/machine-two-warmup.md) (engaged at `6:48 PM` after this afternoon's oil-seal weep and grabby take-up clutch): at the intermittent sight-glass, oil is coming up to operating heat with a thin dark bead resting along the lower seal lip clear of the gate, and the weighted test loop pulls a slight rhythmic hitch on the take-up arm once per turn without dropping tension.
- **[The Bench Spare](../../../../khai-plays/rialto-spare/pieces/bench-spare-unit.md)** sits strapped to its two-handle birch carry skid beside the freight door, wearing Tuesday's green bench-test tag—its skid-rail spring clip empty where the Astor's step-down pedestal collar shim normally rides.
- On the wall phone console between them, the red message lamp blinks beside the handset queued to the Astor Cinema's booth extension: four minutes ago (`6:51 PM`), Astor projectionist Mara Chen left word that her sole 35mm head stripped its intermittent drive gear ahead of their `7:30 PM` show, a wet Friday crowd is lining up under the Astor marquee, and a cargo hatchback is idling in the Astor alley if Rialto will release the spare. Cross-town river bridges are backed up `20–30` minutes in the rain.
- **On stage:** Tomas Voss stands crouched by Machine 2's oil sight-glass. Nadia Osei steps off the top of the booth stair carrying the house-open clipboard.

### Turn 1 — Nadia Osei (`6:55 PM`)

*Nadia steps off the top of the booth stair in her rain-darkened blazer, adjusts the low-volume walkie clipped at her lapel, and checks the wall clock at 6:55 PM against the blinking red lamp on the phone console. "Mara's got a wet crowd under the Astor marquee and a hatchback idling in her alley, Tomas, and with the river bridges backed up twenty to thirty minutes, stalling here is the same as telling her no. I unlock our lobby doors at 7:15, which means if it takes both of us to muscle this skid down the freight stair for a curb handoff, we need a call on the table by seven flat. Tell me straight what Two is giving you—is that seal staying clear of the gate enough to release the spare, or what's the hard showstopper?"

*She cuts her eyes back up to the wall clock's sweep hand and snaps the brass clip on her house clipboard shut with her thumb.*

*Tomas crouches beside Machine 2's intermittent housing, pulls the grease pencil from behind his ear to tick a reference mark against the sight-glass rim, and rests two fingers briefly against the take-up arm roller as the test loop hitches past, then straightens. "Oil just hit operating heat at eight minutes—there's a dark bead sitting on the lower seal lip clear of the gate, and the take-up clutch throws one slight hitch per turn without dropping tension. Before my initials clear that spare to leave the building, Two finishes a full fifteen-minute soak to 7:03 with that oil bead dead still, we verify whether there's a usable shim in this bench drawer or sitting on Mara's pedestal, and the skid goes down the stair before your 7:15 doors so nobody pulls me off booth duty."

*He crosses two paces to the birch carry skid, taps the empty steel spring clip on the rail with his folded feeler gauge, unhooks Tuesday's green bench-test tag from the spare's igniter housing, and sets the tag flat on the phone console shelf directly in front of Nadia. Stepping back to the rewind bench, he slides open the steel parts drawer and shifts a tray of set-screws aside toward the rear dividers.*

### Turn 4 — Tomas Voss (`6:58 PM`)

*Tomas nudges the steel parts drawer shut with his hip, looks back to Machine 2 where the take-up arm gives its slight once-per-turn hitch under the weighted loop, and holds silent for a beat. "Ten minutes at full heat didn't smooth that clutch out—five more minutes to 7:03 won't cure grabbing cork, and a once-per-turn hitch on a light loop will snatch perforations under the rim weight of two-thousand-foot archival reels. "Mara, it's Nadia in the Rialto booth with Tomas at 6:59—stand your alley hatchback down, because we can't release the bench spare tonight. Machine Two still has an oil-seal weep at the sight-glass and a once-per-turn hitch on the take-up clutch at full operating heat, so our backup head stays strapped here on live standby for four unspliced borrowed archival reels at 7:30, and last November's step-down pedestal collar shim isn't in our bench drawer.
