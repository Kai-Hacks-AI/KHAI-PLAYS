# Authoring Note — *Rialto Spare* & *The Spare Projector*

## Reference Choices

Both Plays were authored under House folder `khai-plays` (`Estate: KHAI-PLAYS`) using the supplied `khai-playwright` references:

- **Play (`ENACTS`) & Promptbook (`HACKS`):** Each Play pairs `ENACTS.md` (entry point, arc, company inventory, triggers, stakes) with a self-contained `PROMPTBOOK.md` (`Human`, `Agent`, `Collaboration`, `Knowledge`, `System`).
- **Plots (`TO CAST`):** *Rialto Spare* uses one brisk `4–5` turn Plot ([the-cross-town-call.md](khai-plays/rialto-spare/plots/the-cross-town-call.md)) ending on a verbatim outgoing booth-line message. *The Spare Projector* uses two `4–5` turn Plots: [01-what-we-tell-the-line.md](khai-plays/the-spare-projector/plots/01-what-we-tell-the-line.md) (ending as soon as the crew commits to and delivers/posts its statement to the queue) and [02-keeping-the-door.md](khai-plays/the-spare-projector/plots/02-keeping-the-door.md) (`15–20` minutes later past billed curtain, enacting one concrete consequence of that promise).
- **Personas (`TO PAST`) & Competing Plans (`TO DOIT`):** Each Play casts two crew Personas with distinct blind spots (`Shadow`), deliberate cues (`Action`), and involuntary physical signs (`Tell`), backed by competing `[ ]`-targeted Plans so lending, conditioning, holding, refusing, delaying, standing by, refunding, or walking a promise back all remain live choices in play.
- **Places (`TO SHOW`) & Pieces (`TO PLAY`):** Rather than relying on abstract meta-rules alone, epistemic separation in *The Spare Projector* is built into [astor-threshold.md](khai-plays/the-spare-projector/places/astor-threshold.md) and [astor-booth-phone.md](khai-plays/the-spare-projector/pieces/astor-booth-phone.md) (a silent-strobe booth phone inside the sound-buffered upper booth doorway out of sight and earshot of the lobby doors) paired with [lobby-easel-and-till.md](khai-plays/the-spare-projector/pieces/lobby-easel-and-till.md) (a public felt board and shallow `~18`-ticket cash float at the glass entrance). In *Rialto Spare*, [bench-spare-unit.md](khai-plays/rialto-spare/pieces/bench-spare-unit.md) carries a concrete physical caveat—a missing Astor step-down pedestal collar shim—alongside Rialto's own changeover vulnerability.
- **Processes (`TO IDLE`) & Pitches (`TO TUNE`):** [machine-two-warmup.md](khai-plays/rialto-spare/processes/machine-two-warmup.md) puts live mechanical heat on Rialto's choice without pre-deciding whether Machine 2 holds all night; [sidewalk-queue-swell.md](khai-plays/the-spare-projector/processes/sidewalk-queue-swell.md) and [promise-reckoning.md](khai-plays/the-spare-projector/processes/promise-reckoning.md) carry whatever the Astor crew announces in Plot 1 across the `15–20` minute gap into Plot 2.
- **Omitted Type (`Position — TO HOLD`):** Neither Play reassigns roles or rotates office-holders across scenes; `Persona ## Taxonomy` and role-assigned `Plan ## Orders` carry each crew member's standing cleanly without redundant files.

## Seams & Friction Noticed

1. **Linking an un-run `TO LOAN` archive path from `ENACTS ## Triggers`:**
   `play.md` (`## Triggers`) asks the Playwright to link the recorded Performance a Play draws on, while `performance.md` (`TO LOAN`) specifies keeping `performance.md` beside `transcript.md` in an Archive folder without defining the archive path convention itself. When authoring source and receiving Plays together before capture, the receiving Play must forward-link a not-yet-created path (`../../archives/khai-plays/rialto-spare/run-01/performance.md`) derived from external rehearsal setup conventions, creating one expected unresolved link until Capture runs.
2. **Expressing variable arrival timing inside fixed `ENACTS` and `Plot` headings:**
   `play.md` (`## Triggers`: *"say when it enters"*) and `plot.md` (`## Stage`: *"Specify the order of fixed events when it matters"*) read most naturally when a borrowed Performance enters at one fixed point. Staging both **Before Plot 1** and **Between Plots** from a single unchanged Play and Promptbook required keeping `Plot ## Cue` timing-neutral while writing explicit two-mode fixed-event switches in `ENACTS ## Triggers`, both `Plot ## Stage` sections, and `PROMPTBOOK` (`## Human`, `## Collaboration`, `## Knowledge`, `## System`)—and guarding against accidental double-delivery in Plot 2 when **Before Plot 1** is active or premature leakage in Plot 1 when **Between Plots** is active.
3. **Carrying an exact in-world message across `TO LOAN` without `transcript.md`:**
   Because the receiving Director never receives `transcript.md`, any verbatim booth-phone message must survive inside `performance.md` (`## Aftermath` and `## Note`). `performance.md` frames `Aftermath` around settled/failed/open outcomes and `Note` around what later productions should know, without naming verbatim in-world artifacts; *Rialto Spare*'s `PROMPTBOOK ## Knowledge` bridges this seam by explicitly requiring the outgoing booth message, settled terms, and unresolved operational unknowns to be recorded for the Performance card.
4. **Protecting open `Aftermath` unknowns across a between-Plot time jump:**
   A `15–20` minute jump into Plot 2 creates natural narrative pressure for a Director to resolve offstage transit or hardware questions during the ellipsis. Aligning the world's time constants (`20–30` minute wet bridge traffic in *Rialto Spare* vs. a `15–20` minute Plot 1-to-Plot 2 gap landing just past 7:30 PM in *The Spare Projector*) with explicit `PROMPTBOOK ## Knowledge` and `## System` bans on invented updates keeps `Aftermath`'s open questions genuinely open on stage.
