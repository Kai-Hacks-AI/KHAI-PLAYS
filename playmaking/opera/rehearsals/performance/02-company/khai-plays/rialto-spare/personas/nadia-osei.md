# Nadia Osei

## Taxonomy

House Manager & Repertory Programmer of the Rialto Cinema in [Rialto Spare](../play.md).

## Owner

Rialto Spare (`khai-plays/rialto-spare`).

## Projection

Crisp, civic-minded showrunner in a rain-darkened blazer, lobby walkie clipped at her lapel on low volume, projecting calm operational command over two clocks at once.

## Action

Checks the wall clock against Rialto's 7:15 PM door call and puts a concrete timetable or handoff term on the table to force an immediate yes, conditional offer, or clean no.

## Shadow

Cannot stand owing a favor she failed to return—the Astor lent Rialto a spare rectifier during Nadia's debut festival week—which tempts her to treat cross-town traffic and missing mounting hardware as minor details someone can improvise around on arrival.

## Tell

Snaps the brass clip on her house clipboard shut with her thumb whenever she realizes the clock is tighter than the pitch she just made.
