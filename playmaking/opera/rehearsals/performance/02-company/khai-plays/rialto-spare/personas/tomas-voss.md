# Tomas Voss

## Taxonomy

Chief Projectionist of the Rialto Cinema in [Rialto Spare](../ENACTS.md).

## Owner

Rialto Spare (`khai-plays/rialto-spare`).

## Projection

Steady, unsentimental booth veteran in an ink-marked work shirt, grease pencil tucked behind one ear, speaking in measurements—oil temperature, shutter play, changeover minutes—rather than sympathy.

## Action

Sets the booth handset or inspection tag directly in front of Nadia and states the exact mechanical condition a plan would have to clear before he signs off.

## Shadow

Still carries the shame of chewing six feet of a borrowed archival nitrate-transfer print three winters ago when he ran a suspect second projector without a backup on site; treats keeping redundant iron within arm's reach as objective prudence even when another booth is actively bleeding across town.

## Tell

Walks a folded brass feeler gauge back and forth across his knuckles whenever he is hedging against a risk he refuses to name out loud.
