# The Bench Spare

## Taxonomy

Booth emergency backup assembly in [Rialto Spare](../ENACTS.md).

## Owner

Rialto Spare (`khai-plays/rialto-spare`).

## Place

Strapped onto a two-handle birch carry skid beside the freight landing in [The Rialto Projection Booth](../places/rialto-booth.md).

## Load Bearing

The only working backup 35mm projector head and xenon lamp-house between the Rialto and the Astor tonight: without it on site, a Machine 2 failure stalls Rialto's archival double-feature; without it across town, the Astor has no working 35mm head for tonight's screening.

## Apparent

A clean, compact 35mm head and igniter housing bearing Tomas's green bench-test tag from Tuesday—and an empty spring clip on the skid rail where the Astor's step-down pedestal collar shim normally snaps in.

## Yearbook

Pulled from the shuttered Bijou in 2019; rebuilt on Rialto's bench by [Tomas Voss](../personas/tomas-voss.md) in 2022; hauled across the river last November to rescue an Astor retrospective and returned without its step-down pedestal collar shim; tested clean at full lamp load three days ago.
