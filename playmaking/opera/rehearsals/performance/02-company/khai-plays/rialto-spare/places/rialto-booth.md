# The Rialto Projection Booth

## Taxonomy

Upper projection booth of the Rialto Cinema in [Rialto Spare](../play.md).

## Owner

Rialto Spare (`khai-plays/rialto-spare`).

## Shown

Warm ozone, hot machine oil, and steady 24-fps chatter fill a narrow concrete room above the balcony; rain streaks the alley transom beside the freight stair, and the red message lamp blinks on the wall phone console.

## Holds

Machine 1 threaded with Reel 1 of tonight's archival feature, Machine 2 running hot under test, [The Bench Spare](../pieces/bench-spare-unit.md) strapped on its birch carry skid by the freight door, and the hardwired booth telephone/text console linked to the Astor Cinema's booth line.
