# Rescue the Astor Screening

## Taxonomy

Cross-house mutual-aid Plan in [Rialto Spare](../play.md).

## Owner

Pursued by [Nadia Osei](../personas/nadia-osei.md) in [Rialto Spare](../play.md).

## Direction

Give the stranded Astor Cinema a workable path to [The Bench Spare](../pieces/bench-spare-unit.md)—or an immediate, unambiguous decision they can act on—before their 7:30 PM queue breaks up in the rain.

## Orders

- [Nadia Osei](../personas/nadia-osei.md): Pin down concrete handoff logistics, timing, and return conditions before Rialto's 7:15 PM lobby call absorbs the crew.
- [Tomas Voss](../personas/tomas-voss.md): Separate real mechanical showstoppers from worst-case caution and specify what the Astor projectionist must bring or expect if [The Bench Spare](../pieces/bench-spare-unit.md) moves.

## Implementation

Cross-town bridge traffic is running `20–30` minutes in the rain, Rialto has no spare booth operator to send across town for mounting, and the Astor crew needs an answer now while their crowd is still at the door.

## Targets

- [ ] A definite decision on [The Bench Spare](../pieces/bench-spare-unit.md) is settled before Rialto's 7:15 PM door call.
- [ ] Practical handoff terms, timing, or refusal reasons are stated plainly enough for the Astor crew to decide what to tell their queue.
- [ ] One concrete voice or text message is transmitted to the Astor booth phone before Curtain.
