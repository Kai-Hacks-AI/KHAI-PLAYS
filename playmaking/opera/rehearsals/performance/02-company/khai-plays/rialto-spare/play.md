# Rialto Spare

## Name

*Rialto Spare*

## Arc

One brisk evening Plot, [The Cross-Town Call](plots/the-cross-town-call.md), catches the Rialto Cinema twenty minutes before doors open on its own Friday 35mm double-feature. Word has just come in from the neighborhood Astor Cinema across the river that its sole projector failed with an audience already lining up outside. Before unlocking their own house, the Rialto's projectionist and house manager must settle what to do with their spare unit and put one clear answer onto the Astor's booth phone line.

## Triggers

[The Cross-Town Call](plots/the-cross-town-call.md) starts at 6:55 PM in [The Rialto Projection Booth](places/rialto-booth.md), twenty minutes before Rialto's 7:15 PM house-open call for a 7:30 PM archival double-feature. Fixed at the opening:

- Four minutes ago, Astor Cinema projectionist Mara Chen left an urgent voice message on the Rialto booth line: the Astor's main 35mm projector stripped its intermittent drive gear ahead of their 7:30 PM show, a wet Friday crowd is gathering under their marquee, and she has a cargo hatchback idling in the Astor alley if Rialto can release [The Bench Spare](pieces/bench-spare-unit.md).
- Rialto's Machine 1 is healthy and threaded with Reel 1, while Machine 2 is mid-test in [Machine 2 Warm-Up Run](processes/machine-two-warmup.md) after showing an oil-seal weep and a grabby take-up clutch that afternoon.
- Rain is backing up cross-town river bridges (`20–30` minutes curb-to-curb), and the Astor's step-down pedestal collar shim is missing from [The Bench Spare](pieces/bench-spare-unit.md)'s skid clips.

Leave the crew's decision completely open: they may lend the spare immediately, lend it under strict conditions or warnings, hold it pending a benchmark on Machine 2, or keep it at Rialto. The Plot ends after roughly `4–5` brisk cast turns as soon as Tomas and Nadia settle their decision and send one short, concrete voice or text message to the Astor Cinema's booth phone—leaving intact whatever operational uncertainties cannot be settled inside Rialto's booth.

## Stakes

Rialto and the Astor share one working backup 35mm head on a rainy Friday night. Sending [The Bench Spare](pieces/bench-spare-unit.md) out the freight stair leaves Rialto's archival double-feature exposed if [Machine 2 Warm-Up Run](processes/machine-two-warmup.md) betrays a failing seal or clutch mid-show; keeping or delaying it leaves the Astor's gathering crowd facing a dark screen across town. [The Cross-Town Call](plots/the-cross-town-call.md) forces Tomas and Nadia to decide which risk they will own and what exact terms or refusal they will put on the record to the Astor booth line.
