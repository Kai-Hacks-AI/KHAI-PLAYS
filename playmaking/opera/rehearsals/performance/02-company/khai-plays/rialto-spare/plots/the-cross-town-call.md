# The Cross-Town Call

## Taxonomy

Single Plot of [Rialto Spare](../ENACTS.md).

## Owner

Rialto Spare (`khai-plays/rialto-spare`).

## Cue

6:55 PM in [The Rialto Projection Booth](../places/rialto-booth.md), twenty minutes before lobby doors unlock for Rialto's 7:30 PM archival 35mm double-feature. Machine 2 chatters through [Machine 2 Warm-Up Run](../processes/machine-two-warmup.md) while the red message lamp blinks beside the booth phone: across the river at the Astor Cinema, projectionist Mara Chen has just called in with a stripped intermittent gear, a wet Friday crowd forming outside her doors, and a hatchback waiting in the Astor alley if Rialto will part with [The Bench Spare](../pieces/bench-spare-unit.md).

## Action

[Tomas Voss](../personas/tomas-voss.md) drives to protect Rialto's changeover capability under [Safeguard the Double-Bill](../plans/safeguard-the-double-bill.md) before committing the only backup head in the building, while [Nadia Osei](../personas/nadia-osei.md) drives under [Rescue the Astor Screening](../plans/rescue-the-astor-screening.md) to get the Astor an actionable lifeline—or at minimum a fast, honest answer—before 7:15 PM locks both houses into tonight's grid. Their efforts collide over [The Bench Spare](../pieces/bench-spare-unit.md) and what exact words go back onto the Astor booth line.

## Stage

[Tomas Voss](../personas/tomas-voss.md) starts at Machine 2's oil sight-glass with [The Bench Spare](../pieces/bench-spare-unit.md) strapped to its birch skid beside the freight landing. [Nadia Osei](../personas/nadia-osei.md) enters up the booth stair carrying the house-open clipboard after hearing lobby chatter that the Astor broke down. The booth phone handset sits on the wall console between them, queued to dial or text the Astor booth extension. The Plot closes as soon as they settle their call on the spare and transmit one verbatim message to the Astor booth line.

## Tension

Tomas knows he has not checked whether the Astor's missing pedestal collar shim is anywhere in Rialto's parts drawer and dreads gambling an archival print on Machine 2 without a safety net; Nadia knows the Astor bailed out her first festival week with a loaner rectifier and resists being the programmer who left a sister screen dark in the rain.
