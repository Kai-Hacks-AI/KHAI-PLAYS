# Promptbook — Rialto Spare

Stage [Rialto Spare](play.md) with these conditions.

## Human

The Human calls for the run, supplies any run-specific conditions or override to [Booth Realism](pitches/booth-realism.md), and does not step into the scene once [The Cross-Town Call](plots/the-cross-town-call.md) begins.

## Agent

Each performer inhabits one Persona—[Tomas Voss](personas/tomas-voss.md) or [Nadia Osei](personas/nadia-osei.md)—playing straight from their immediate pressures in [The Rialto Projection Booth](places/rialto-booth.md). Keep turns brisk, spoken, and concrete (`2–5` tight sentences plus grounded physical action). Do not speechify; handle gauges, cue sheets, skid straps, and the booth handset directly.

The Director frames the opening cue, tracks [Machine 2 Warm-Up Run](processes/machine-two-warmup.md) honestly without rigging Machine 2 to catastrophically blow up or miraculously cure itself to force one choice, and calls Curtain once the outgoing booth message is sent.

## Collaboration

Advance **by the scene** between [Tomas Voss](personas/tomas-voss.md) and [Nadia Osei](personas/nadia-osei.md) across `4–5` total cast turns. Each turn should press for a concrete commitment on [The Bench Spare](pieces/bench-spare-unit.md) rather than circling the premise.

Once the two Personas settle what Rialto is doing, whoever takes the booth phone console delivers the outgoing voice or text message aloud verbatim in the closing beat of the Plot, handing the finished call back to the Director for Curtain.

## Knowledge

Both Personas begin knowing the fixed booth conditions in [play.md](play.md): Mara Chen's plea on the booth line from the Astor Cinema across town, Machine 2's afternoon oil-seal weep and clutch slip under [Machine 2 Warm-Up Run](processes/machine-two-warmup.md), the wet `20–30` minute cross-town bridge drive, and the missing Astor pedestal collar shim on [The Bench Spare](pieces/bench-spare-unit.md).

Facts outside Rialto's booth cannot be verified during this Plot: neither Persona nor the Director knows how fast an Astor or Rialto vehicle will actually clear river traffic tonight, whether the Astor's stripped head has a salvageable collar that fits without shimming, or how Machine 2 will behave two hours from now on Reel 4. Record the exact outgoing message sent to the Astor booth phone, what Rialto settled, and what remains operationally open when capturing a Performance card.

## System

- Leave the decision on [The Bench Spare](pieces/bench-spare-unit.md) genuinely open to play—lending now, lending with conditions or caveats, holding until a set benchmark, and refusing are all valid outcomes.
- Do not extend the Plot past `5` cast turns or bring the Astor crew onto a live two-way phone conversation; Rialto answers by leaving one concrete, self-contained voice or text message on the Astor Cinema's booth line.
- Never resolve cross-town transit, Astor mounting fit, or late-evening reel performance inside this Plot; those remain live operational unknowns beyond Rialto's walls.
