# Promptbook — The Spare Projector

Stage [The Spare Projector](ENACTS.md) with these conditions.

## Human

The Human sets the run's arrival timing (**Before Plot 1** or **Between Plots**), supplies the archived Performance card [Rialto Spare · run-01](../../archives/khai-plays/rialto-spare/run-01/performance.md) at the moment matching that timing, names any override to [Wet Marquee](pitches/wet-marquee.md), and does not perform in the scenes once play begins.

## Agent

Each performer inhabits one crew Persona—[Mara Chen](personas/mara-chen.md) or [Devon Reyes](personas/devon-reyes.md)—with brisk, spoken turns (`2–5` sentences plus immediate physical action in [The Astor Lobby & Booth Stairwell](places/astor-threshold.md)). Never pre-script whether the crew promises a delay, holds standby, issues rain-checks, cancels outright, keeps its word, or walks a promise back.

The Director frames each Plot's opening cue, delivers Rialto's booth message strictly at the run's scheduled timing, registers external crowd pressure from [Sidewalk Queue Swell](processes/sidewalk-queue-swell.md) and [Promise Reckoning](processes/promise-reckoning.md) through brief, concrete lobby/door cues rather than adding a third main Persona, and ensures [Keeping the Door](plots/02-keeping-the-door.md) plays through one concrete front-of-house or screening consequence before calling Curtain.

## Collaboration

Advance **by the scene** between [Mara Chen](personas/mara-chen.md) and [Devon Reyes](personas/devon-reyes.md) across `4–5` brisk cast turns in each Plot:

- **Plot 1 ([What We Tell the Line](plots/01-what-we-tell-the-line.md)):** If the timing is **Before Plot 1**, the Director opens by delivering Rialto's message on [The Booth Extension Phone](pieces/astor-booth-phone.md) to Mara alone before the first turn; if **Between Plots**, the Director opens with the booth phone dark and silent. Close Plot 1 immediately once the crew commits to and posts or speaks its first public announcement to the queue at [The Marquee Easel & Cash Till](pieces/lobby-easel-and-till.md).
- **Handoff & Plot 2 ([Keeping the Door](plots/02-keeping-the-door.md)):** Advance the clock `15–20` minutes (~7:35–7:38 PM) and set [Promise Reckoning](processes/promise-reckoning.md) in motion from Plot 1's exact announcement. If the timing is **Between Plots**, deliver the exact same Rialto message via [The Booth Extension Phone](pieces/astor-booth-phone.md) to Mara alone on Plot 2's opening cue; if **Before Plot 1**, do not deliver a second message. Close Plot 2 after `4–5` brisk turns once the crew acts on its Plot 1 promise and plays through one concrete consequence with the queue or screening.

## Knowledge

Enforce strict channel separation and `TO LOAN` card boundaries at every step:

- **Single in-world channel:** Rialto's message enters the world exclusively through [The Booth Extension Phone](pieces/astor-booth-phone.md) inside the booth doorway to [Mara Chen](personas/mara-chen.md) alone—at Plot 1 opening in **Before Plot 1**, or at the Plot 2 handoff in **Between Plots**. [Devon Reyes](personas/devon-reyes.md) and the sidewalk queue have zero direct access to the booth line and can learn of Rialto's reply only through what Mara says or does onstage.
- **No anticipation in Between Plots:** During Plot 1 of a **Between Plots** run, neither Persona nor the Director knows whether Rialto will lend, condition, delay, or refuse the spare; the crew knows only that their projector stripped its drive gear and Mara left word at Rialto ten minutes ago with no reply yet.
- **Card-only authority:** Draw Rialto's message, settled terms, and unresolved caveats strictly from `Aftermath` and `Note` in [Rialto Spare · run-01](../../archives/khai-plays/rialto-spare/run-01/performance.md). Never read, consult, or import details from a source `transcript.md`. Any question that `performance.md` left open—such as cross-town travel time in rain, pedestal collar fit on the Astor mount, or how Rialto's own projector holds later—remains genuinely unknown in play and may not be settled by invented updates.

## System

- Keep [ENACTS.md](ENACTS.md), Company files, [Wet Marquee](pitches/wet-marquee.md), Plot order, and the exact Rialto booth message identical across both arrival timings; vary only when [Rialto Spare · run-01](../../archives/khai-plays/rialto-spare/run-01/performance.md) arrives (**Before Plot 1** vs. **Between Plots**).
- Never consult a source `transcript.md`, never use or foreshadow `performance.md` before its scheduled arrival, and never resolve offstage facts that `performance.md` left open (no invented follow-up calls from Rialto, no surprise van arrivals solving transit, and no offstage hardware verdicts beyond what is already present at the Astor).
- Keep each Plot to roughly `4–5` cast turns; require a committed public statement to the queue to end Plot 1 and one enacted front-of-house or screening consequence to end Plot 2.
