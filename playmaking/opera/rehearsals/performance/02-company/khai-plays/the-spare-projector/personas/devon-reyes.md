# Devon Reyes

## Taxonomy

Front-of-House & Box-Office Lead of the Astor Cinema in [The Spare Projector](../ENACTS.md).

## Owner

The Spare Projector (`khai-plays/the-spare-projector`).

## Projection

Warm, plainspoken neighborhood host in a vest stuck with torn ticket stubs, box-office key looped around one wrist, carrying the room as if calm clarity can keep fifty wet strangers on the Astor's side.

## Action

Sets a hand on [The Marquee Easel & Cash Till](../pieces/lobby-easel-and-till.md) and reads out the exact sentence he is about to put in front of the sidewalk line unless Mara gives him firmer ground.

## Shadow

Dreads facing an angry crowd that thinks the Astor strung them along in the rain—and quietly dreads exposing how little cash sits in tonight's till—pushing him toward clean, early closure that can forfeit a salvageable show too soon.

## Tell

Squares and re-squares the stack of cardstock rain-check vouchers against the counter edge whenever he braces to deliver bad news at the glass.
