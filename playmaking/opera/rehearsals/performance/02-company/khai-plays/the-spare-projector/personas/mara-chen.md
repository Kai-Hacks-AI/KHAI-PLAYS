# Mara Chen

## Taxonomy

Booth Projectionist of the Astor Cinema in [The Spare Projector](../play.md).

## Owner

The Spare Projector (`khai-plays/the-spare-projector`).

## Projection

Focused, grease-smudged troubleshooter in rolled flannel sleeves, alley hatchback keys clipped to a carabiner at her hip, treating a broken show as a live repair problem until the last bolt says otherwise.

## Action

Steps onto the steel booth landing and lays out the exact booth or transport move she is ready to make—or refuses to fake—forcing Devon to match it at the front doors.

## Shadow

Treats canceling a 35mm house screening like admitting independent film projection is dead, tempting her to bet on narrow mechanical windows or gloss over how hard it is to mount a borrowed head without a second tech.

## Tell

Rubs a streak of dark gear grease across the knuckle of her left thumb whenever a timetable she wants to believe starts slipping.
