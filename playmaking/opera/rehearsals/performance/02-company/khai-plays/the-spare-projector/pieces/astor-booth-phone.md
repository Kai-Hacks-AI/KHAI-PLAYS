# The Booth Extension Phone

## Taxonomy

Booth-to-booth communications handset and message console in [The Spare Projector](../ENACTS.md).

## Owner

The Spare Projector (`khai-plays/the-spare-projector`).

## Place

Bolted to the cinderblock wall just inside the sound-buffered booth doorway at the top of [The Astor Lobby & Booth Stairwell](../places/astor-threshold.md).

## Load Bearing

The sole live channel connecting the Astor to the Rialto Cinema across town: without it, no cross-town reply about the spare projector can reach the Astor crew, and because its ringer is wired to a silent booth flasher for screenings, nobody down in the lobby knows when a message lands unless [Mara Chen](../personas/mara-chen.md) checks it and carries word downstairs.

## Apparent

A scuffed beige wall telephone and single-line text/voicemail display beside a silent amber strobe lamp, its coiled cord stretched from years of one-handed booth calls.

## Yearbook

Wired onto a dedicated booth extension in 2014 after a lobby ring bled into a quiet matinee; used last November when [Mara Chen](../personas/mara-chen.md) coordinated a borrowed Rialto head across the river; dialed out to Rialto ten minutes before [What We Tell the Line](../plots/01-what-we-tell-the-line.md) opens.
