# The Marquee Easel & Cash Till

## Taxonomy

Front-of-house public notice and box-office kit in [The Spare Projector](../play.md).

## Owner

The Spare Projector (`khai-plays/the-spare-projector`).

## Place

Set immediately inside the glass street entrance at the foot of [The Astor Lobby & Booth Stairwell](../places/astor-threshold.md).

## Load Bearing

Turns private crew talk into a binding house commitment: whatever [Devon Reyes](../personas/devon-reyes.md) posts on the felt easel or announces at the door sets the crowd's legal and social claim on tonight's show, while the shallow cash till and cardstock rain-check book set hard physical limits on how many immediate cash refunds the Astor can pay out on the spot.

## Apparent

A brass-trimmed black felt letterboard on a wooden A-frame beside the box-office counter, paired with a locked steel petty-cash tray holding roughly eighteen tickets' worth of small bills and a rubber-banded stack of stamped Astor rain-check passes.

## Yearbook

Set out before every sold-near-full Friday bill since the Astor reopened as a neighborhood single-screen in 2021; emptied down to modest opening float this afternoon after a distributor courier C.O.D. payment; pulled up to the glass doors by [Devon Reyes](../personas/devon-reyes.md) minutes after the main projector sheared its gear.
