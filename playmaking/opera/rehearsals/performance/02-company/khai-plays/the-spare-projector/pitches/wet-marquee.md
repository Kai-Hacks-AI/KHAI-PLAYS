# Wet Marquee

## Taxonomy

Default production Pitch for [The Spare Projector](../play.md).

## Owner

The Spare Projector (`khai-plays/the-spare-projector`).

## Tenor

Grounded front-of-house urgency under a ticking showtime clock and wet glass doors.

## Undertow

Fierce attachment to keeping a neighborhood movie house alive—pulling against the embarrassment of overpromising to neighbors standing in the rain.

## Nerve

Exposed accountability: every sentence posted at the door in Plot 1 waits outside to collect in Plot 2.

## Echo

How differently a promise weighs when information arrives before you give your word versus fifteen minutes too late.
