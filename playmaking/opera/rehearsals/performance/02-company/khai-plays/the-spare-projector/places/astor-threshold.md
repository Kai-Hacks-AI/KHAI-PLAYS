# The Astor Lobby & Booth Stairwell

## Taxonomy

Split-level front threshold and upper booth access of the Astor Cinema in [The Spare Projector](../ENACTS.md).

## Owner

The Spare Projector (`khai-plays/the-spare-projector`).

## Shown

Rain-blurred neon through twin glass street doors, silhouettes of queued patrons shifting under dripping umbrellas outside, cold terrazzo underfoot, and a short open steel stair rising eight steps to the sound-buffered projection booth doorway.

## Holds

[The Marquee Easel & Cash Till](../pieces/lobby-easel-and-till.md) beside the front glass doors below, [The Booth Extension Phone](../pieces/astor-booth-phone.md) mounted just inside the heavy booth door above, the crippled main 35mm pedestal inside the booth, and an exit push-bar leading out the back alley where Mara's hatchback sits parked.

## Offers

Face-to-face conference on the stair landing out of the sidewalk's earshot for [Mara Chen](../personas/mara-chen.md) and [Devon Reyes](../personas/devon-reyes.md), immediate public posting or door announcements to the queue for Devon, and private booth-phone access plus back-alley vehicle egress for Mara.

## Withheld

Any way for Devon at the glass doors—or the crowd on the sidewalk—to hear or see [The Booth Extension Phone](../pieces/astor-booth-phone.md) inside the sound-buffered booth doorway unless Mara brings word down the stairs, and any shelter for the sidewalk queue unless the front doors are unlocked to let them into the lobby.
