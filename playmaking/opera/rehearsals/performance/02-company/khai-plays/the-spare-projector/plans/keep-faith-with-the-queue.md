# Keep Faith with the Queue

## Taxonomy

Front-of-house trust and box-office solvency Plan in [The Spare Projector](../ENACTS.md).

## Owner

Pursued by [Devon Reyes](../personas/devon-reyes.md) in [The Spare Projector](../ENACTS.md).

## Direction

Give the wet Friday crowd outside [The Astor Lobby & Booth Stairwell](../places/astor-threshold.md) a timely, truthful commitment before showtime slips past in silence, and stand behind—or transparently amend—that commitment in Plot 2 without breaking the cash till or the Astor's good name.

## Orders

- [Devon Reyes](../personas/devon-reyes.md): Force a clear public statement onto [The Marquee Easel & Cash Till](../pieces/lobby-easel-and-till.md) before 7:30 PM, manage the thin cash float and rain-check vouchers, and face the line directly when Plot 1's promise comes due.
- [Mara Chen](../personas/mara-chen.md): Give Devon straight facts from the booth and [The Booth Extension Phone](../pieces/astor-booth-phone.md) so the house never strings neighbors along on guesswork.

## Implementation

[The Marquee Easel & Cash Till](../pieces/lobby-easel-and-till.md) holds roughly eighteen cash refunds in small bills alongside stamped rain-check passes; unlocking the lobby doors brings wet patrons inside where every word on the stairs carries.

## Targets

- [ ] A clear, specific statement is delivered or posted to the queue at the end of Plot 1 rather than leaving the doors silent.
- [ ] Refunds, rain-checks, seating, or standby terms stay within what [The Marquee Easel & Cash Till](../pieces/lobby-easel-and-till.md) and the two-person crew can actually honor.
- [ ] In Plot 2, the crew plays through one concrete front-of-house consequence with the queue—keeping, backing up, or owning a change to Plot 1's word openly.
