# The Spare Projector

## Estate

[KHAI-PLAYS](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS)

## Name

*The Spare Projector*

## Arc

Two linked evening Plots track one Friday failure across twenty minutes at the neighborhood Astor Cinema. In [What We Tell the Line](plots/01-what-we-tell-the-line.md), the main 35mm projector has stripped its drive gear shortly before a 7:30 PM screening, a wet crowd is building under the marquee, and the two-person crew must commit to a public statement at the doors. In [Keeping the Door](plots/02-keeping-the-door.md), fifteen to twenty minutes later past billed curtain, the crew stands in the direct wake of what they told the crowd and must act on that promise—carrying it through, adapting it, or taking it back—through one concrete consequence at the threshold. Ordering the two Plots turns spoken words at the glass into a debt that comes due minutes later, sharpening whenever word from across town arrives only after the first promise is already posted.

## Triggers

Fixed at opening: before 6:51 PM the Astor's sole 35mm projector sheared its intermittent drive gear; at 6:51 PM [Mara Chen](personas/mara-chen.md) called the cross-town Rialto Cinema booth line asking whether they could spare their portable 35mm unit; at 7:18 PM [What We Tell the Line](plots/01-what-we-tell-the-line.md) opens with [Sidewalk Queue Swell](processes/sidewalk-queue-swell.md) gathering outside for the 7:30 PM screening.

This Play draws on the archived `TO LOAN` Performance [Rialto Spare · run-01](../../archives/khai-plays/rialto-spare/run-01/performance.md), staged under one of two arrival timings set for the run:

- **Before Plot 1:** [Rialto Spare · run-01](../../archives/khai-plays/rialto-spare/run-01/performance.md) enters on the opening cue of [What We Tell the Line](plots/01-what-we-tell-the-line.md), delivering Rialto's outgoing message through [The Booth Extension Phone](pieces/astor-booth-phone.md) to [Mara Chen](personas/mara-chen.md) alone before the crew makes any commitment to the queue.
- **Between Plots:** [What We Tell the Line](plots/01-what-we-tell-the-line.md) runs while [The Booth Extension Phone](pieces/astor-booth-phone.md) remains completely silent—the crew knows only that Mara called across town with zero reply yet. [Rialto Spare · run-01](../../archives/khai-plays/rialto-spare/run-01/performance.md) enters at the handoff into [Keeping the Door](plots/02-keeping-the-door.md), delivering the exact same message through [The Booth Extension Phone](pieces/astor-booth-phone.md) to [Mara Chen](personas/mara-chen.md) alone right as Plot 2 opens, after the crew's first commitment to the queue is already made.

Leave the crew's choices completely open in both Plots. [What We Tell the Line](plots/01-what-we-tell-the-line.md) (`~4–5` brisk turns) ends as soon as the crew commits to and posts or speaks its statement at [The Marquee Easel & Cash Till](pieces/lobby-easel-and-till.md), initiating [Promise Reckoning](processes/promise-reckoning.md). Fifteen to twenty minutes elapse into [Keeping the Door](plots/02-keeping-the-door.md) (`~4–5` brisk turns, ~7:35–7:38 PM), which ends once the crew acts on what they promised and plays through one concrete consequence with the queue or screening.

## Stakes

The Astor's survival rests on neighborhood trust, tonight's door receipts in [The Marquee Easel & Cash Till](pieces/lobby-easel-and-till.md), and whether a real screening can still hit the sheet without burning the crowd waiting in the rain. [What We Tell the Line](plots/01-what-we-tell-the-line.md) stakes the house's word at the front glass—promising a delay, holding standby, or calling off the show—while [Keeping the Door](plots/02-keeping-the-door.md) forces [Mara Chen](personas/mara-chen.md) and [Devon Reyes](personas/devon-reyes.md) to pay for, uphold, or walk back that word against what [The Booth Extension Phone](pieces/astor-booth-phone.md) actually delivers and leaves unresolved.
