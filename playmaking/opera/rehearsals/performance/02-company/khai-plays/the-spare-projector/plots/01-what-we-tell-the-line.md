# What We Tell the Line

## Taxonomy

Plot 1 of [The Spare Projector](../ENACTS.md).

## Owner

The Spare Projector (`khai-plays/the-spare-projector`).

## Cue

7:18 PM in [The Astor Lobby & Booth Stairwell](../places/astor-threshold.md), twelve minutes before the billed 7:30 PM Friday screening. Twenty minutes ago the Astor's sole 35mm projector sheared its intermittent drive gear; outside under the dripping marquee, [Sidewalk Queue Swell](../processes/sidewalk-queue-swell.md) is pressing umbrellas and ticket stubs up to the lobby glass; ten minutes ago [Mara Chen](../personas/mara-chen.md) left an urgent call on the cross-town Rialto booth line asking after their spare 35mm unit.

## Action

[Devon Reyes](../personas/devon-reyes.md) drives under [Keep Faith with the Queue](../plans/keep-faith-with-the-queue.md) to put an honest, defensible announcement at the front glass before 7:30 PM traps the crowd in wet silence, while [Mara Chen](../personas/mara-chen.md) drives under [Hold the Screening Together](../plans/hold-the-screening-together.md) to keep tonight's screening alive if any workable path exists without lying about the dead pedestal upstairs. Their efforts collide over what exact commitment—delayed start, standby hold, rain-checks, or outright cancellation and refunds—goes out through [The Marquee Easel & Cash Till](../pieces/lobby-easel-and-till.md).

## Stage

[Devon Reyes](../personas/devon-reyes.md) starts down by the locked glass entrance beside [The Marquee Easel & Cash Till](../pieces/lobby-easel-and-till.md); [Mara Chen](../personas/mara-chen.md) starts at the top of the short steel stair inside the booth doorway beside [The Booth Extension Phone](../pieces/astor-booth-phone.md), grease on her cuffs and the alley hatchback keys on her belt.

Fixed event order at opening: in a **Before Plot 1** run, Rialto's message arrives on [The Booth Extension Phone](../pieces/astor-booth-phone.md) for Mara alone right before she steps onto the stair landing; in a **Between Plots** run, [The Booth Extension Phone](../pieces/astor-booth-phone.md) stays dark and silent throughout Plot 1. Either way, Devon starts knowing only that the main gear sheared and Mara called across town. Plot 1 ends as soon as the crew commits to and speaks or posts its statement to the queue at the front doors.

## Tension

Mara knows stripping the Astor's dead head off its pedestal and fitting any loaner unit is a two-person lift with zero margin for mismatched collars; Devon knows the petty-cash drawer in [The Marquee Easel & Cash Till](../pieces/lobby-easel-and-till.md) cannot cover a full sidewalk stampede of cash refunds without running dry mid-line.
