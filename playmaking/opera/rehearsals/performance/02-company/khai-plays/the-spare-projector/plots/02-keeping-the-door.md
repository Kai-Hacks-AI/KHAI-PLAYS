# Keeping the Door

## Taxonomy

Plot 2 of [The Spare Projector](../ENACTS.md).

## Owner

The Spare Projector (`khai-plays/the-spare-projector`).

## Cue

Fifteen to twenty minutes after Plot 1 (~7:35–7:38 PM, now past the billed 7:30 PM showtime) in [The Astor Lobby & Booth Stairwell](../places/astor-threshold.md). Heavy rain drums on the marquee while [Promise Reckoning](../processes/promise-reckoning.md) presses directly against the front doors: the crowd is now acting on whatever statement the crew delivered at the close of [What We Tell the Line](01-what-we-tell-the-line.md), expecting the Astor to make good on its word right now.

## Action

[Mara Chen](../personas/mara-chen.md) and [Devon Reyes](../personas/devon-reyes.md) must execute, salvage, or walk back the commitment they made in Plot 1—reconciling [Hold the Screening Together](../plans/hold-the-screening-together.md) and [Keep Faith with the Queue](../plans/keep-faith-with-the-queue.md) with what Rialto's booth message actually established and what it left unresolved. Their efforts collide over who takes the hit right now: the waiting crowd, the cash drawer, the alley pickup run, or tonight's bill.

## Stage

[Devon Reyes](../personas/devon-reyes.md) stands at [The Marquee Easel & Cash Till](../pieces/lobby-easel-and-till.md) facing the immediate front-door fallout of Plot 1's promise; [Mara Chen](../personas/mara-chen.md) starts at the booth doorway beside [The Booth Extension Phone](../pieces/astor-booth-phone.md) and the crippled projector mount.

Fixed event order at opening: in a **Between Plots** run, Rialto's message arrives now on [The Booth Extension Phone](../pieces/astor-booth-phone.md) for Mara alone right on Plot 2's opening cue, before Devon or the queue knows any word came in; in a **Before Plot 1** run, that message is already in play from Plot 1 and no new call arrives. Any operational question left open by the archived Performance remains unresolved onstage. Plot 2 ends once the crew acts on its promise and plays through one concrete consequence with the queue or screening.

## Tension

Devon faces neighbors at the glass who remember the exact words posted fifteen minutes ago and will spot any hedge or backtrack instantly; Mara knows every minute spent arguing on the stairs or pulling bolts in the booth burns whatever narrow mechanical or transit window might still exist—if one exists at all.
