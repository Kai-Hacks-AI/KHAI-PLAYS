# Promise Reckoning

## Taxonomy

Post-announcement crowd and box-office follow-through in [The Spare Projector](../ENACTS.md).

## Owner

The Spare Projector (`khai-plays/the-spare-projector`).

## Initiated by

The crew delivering or posting its Plot 1 statement to the queue at [The Marquee Easel & Cash Till](../pieces/lobby-easel-and-till.md), visible fifteen to twenty minutes later in how the crowd now stands at or inside [The Astor Lobby & Booth Stairwell](../places/astor-threshold.md) past 7:30 PM.

## Direction

Converts Plot 1's words into an immediate operational demand: if the crew promised a delayed start or standby wait, restless patrons press for doors, seats, or proof of progress; if the crew called off the show or offered refunds/rain-checks, patrons line up at the till and disperse down the wet block.

## Lever

Driven by the exact terms promised in Plot 1, the passing of the 7:30 PM curtain mark, and the limits of [The Marquee Easel & Cash Till](../pieces/lobby-easel-and-till.md); shaped—or thrown into friction—by how the crew acts once Rialto's message on [The Booth Extension Phone](../pieces/astor-booth-phone.md) is weighed.

## Echo

Leaves one concrete, irreversible mark on tonight's house by Curtain of [Keeping the Door](../plots/02-keeping-the-door.md): a crowd seated or held on honest terms, a refund/voucher line settled or strained at the counter, a promised delay walked back face-to-face, or a salvage run committed under real cost.
