# Sidewalk Queue Swell

## Taxonomy

Pre-show front-door crowd buildup in [The Spare Projector](../play.md).

## Owner

The Spare Projector (`khai-plays/the-spare-projector`).

## Initiated by

Advance ticket-holders and Friday walk-ups arriving under the marquee fifteen minutes before the billed 7:30 PM curtain while the street doors of [The Astor Lobby & Booth Stairwell](../places/astor-threshold.md) remain locked past normal seating time.

## Direction

Thickens along the wet sidewalk and presses faces and umbrellas up to the lobby glass, turning impatient glances at the blank felt board on [The Marquee Easel & Cash Till](../pieces/lobby-easel-and-till.md) into sharp knocks for an explanation.

## Lever

Driven by steady rain and the approaching 7:30 PM start time; slowed briefly only when the crew posts or speaks a clear, credible commitment at the doors, and worsened by visible hesitation or mixed signals inside the lobby.

## Echo

Forces the crew in [What We Tell the Line](../plots/01-what-we-tell-the-line.md) to go on the record before 7:30 PM, locking in the public expectation that feeds [Promise Reckoning](promise-reckoning.md).
