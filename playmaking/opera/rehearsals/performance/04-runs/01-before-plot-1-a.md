## Plot 1 — [What We Tell the Line](../02-company/khai-plays/the-spare-projector/plots/01-what-we-tell-the-line.md)

### Opening Cue (`7:18 PM` · Before Plot 1)

*Friday evening, twelve minutes before the billed `7:30 PM` 35mm screening in [The Astor Lobby & Booth Stairwell](../02-company/khai-plays/the-spare-projector/places/astor-threshold.md). At `6:58 PM`, the Astor's sole 35mm projector sheared its intermittent drive gear; at `7:08 PM`, [Mara Chen](../02-company/khai-plays/the-spare-projector/personas/mara-chen.md) called the cross-town Rialto Cinema booth line asking after their portable 35mm spare. Down inside the locked twin glass street doors on the cold terrazzo, [Devon Reyes](../02-company/khai-plays/the-spare-projector/personas/devon-reyes.md) stands beside [The Marquee Easel & Cash Till](../02-company/khai-plays/the-spare-projector/pieces/lobby-easel-and-till.md) while [Sidewalk Queue Swell](../02-company/khai-plays/the-spare-projector/processes/sidewalk-queue-swell.md) presses dripping umbrellas and advance ticket stubs up to the rain-blurred glass, rapping at the blank felt board.*

*Eight steel steps above, just inside the sound-buffered projection booth doorway—inaudible and invisible to Devon downstairs and to the sidewalk crowd—the silent amber strobe lamp beside [The Booth Extension Phone](../02-company/khai-plays/the-spare-projector/pieces/astor-booth-phone.md) flashes with the incoming voice message from [Rialto Spare · run-01](../02-company/archives/khai-plays/rialto-spare/run-01/performance.md). Mara Chen alone lifts the scuffed beige handset and hears Nadia Osei's recorded message verbatim:*

> "Mara, it's Nadia in the Rialto booth with Tomas at 6:59—stand your alley hatchback down, because we can't release the bench spare tonight. Machine Two still has an oil-seal weep at the sight-glass and a once-per-turn hitch on the take-up clutch at full operating heat, so our backup head stays strapped here on live standby for four unspliced borrowed archival reels at 7:30, and last November's step-down pedestal collar shim isn't in our bench drawer. Make the call for your 7:30 crowd now before anyone burns twenty to thirty minutes on the river bridges, and if you hold the screening over for tomorrow, leave word after our last reel clears tonight so we can make good on festival week."

### Turn 1 — [Mara Chen](../02-company/khai-plays/the-spare-projector/personas/mara-chen.md) (`7:18 PM`)

*Mara sets the beige handset back onto its wall cradle inside the booth doorway, unclips the hatchback keys from her hip carabiner to shove them into her trouser pocket, and rubs a streak of dark gear grease across the knuckle of her left thumb. She comes three steps down the open steel stair and leans over the rail toward the terrazzo lobby, pitching her voice between them and short of the knocking glass doors.*

**MARA CHEN:** "Don't put a delay up on that felt board, Devon—Nadia just left word on the booth line, and Rialto can't release their spare head tonight. Their Machine Two has an oil-seal weep and a take-up clutch hitch with four unspliced archival reels going up at 7:30, and last November's step-down pedestal collar shim isn't even in their bench drawer. Tonight's screening is dead on the pedestal, and I'm not running the hatchback across the bridges for nothing or stringing a wet sidewalk along on a show I can't mount. We've only got about eighteen tickets' worth of cash in that tray plus the rain-check stack, and Nadia said to leave word after their last reel clears if we try holding over for tomorrow—so tell me how you're posting the cancellation at the door, and I'll come down to work the line beside you."

### Turn 2 — [Devon Reyes](../02-company/khai-plays/the-spare-projector/personas/devon-reyes.md) (`7:19 PM`)

*Lobby & Door Cue ([Sidewalk Queue Swell](../02-company/khai-plays/the-spare-projector/processes/sidewalk-queue-swell.md)): Eleven minutes to `7:30 PM`. Rain sheets down the neon-lit street glass. Under the marquee, nearly forty wet advance ticket-holders and Friday walk-ups crowd shoulder to shoulder; knuckles rap twice against the locked right-hand door leaf, and two damp Friday stubs press flat against the glass directly opposite the blank felt easel.*

*Devon squares the rubber-banded stack of stamped cardstock rain-check passes against the edge of the box-office counter—once, twice—keeping the wooden A-frame angled back toward the stairwell so the silhouettes pressing stubs to the wet street glass can't read the felt board yet. With the box-office key glinting on its wrist loop, he sets his palm flat on the brass trim of the easel and looks up the steel steps at Mara.*

**DEVON REYES:** "Thank God you told me straight before I hung a fifteen-minute hold on that glass, Mara. Listen to the exact line I'm ready to pin up right now: *7:30 35MM SCREENING CANCELLED — MAIN PROJECTOR GEAR FAILURE — RAIN-CHECK PASSES OR REFUNDS INSIDE.* Here's where that bites us: there are nearly forty wet people under that marquee and only eighteen tickets' worth of small bills left in this tray after this afternoon's courier C.O.D. Before I letter this board or touch that door lock, tell me straight—is that missing collar shim sitting in your booth bench here so a Saturday holdover is even real, or do we keep tomorrow out of our mouths completely and work out how we cover ticket nineteen when the cash drawer runs dry?"

### Turn 3 — [Mara Chen](../02-company/khai-plays/the-spare-projector/personas/mara-chen.md) (`7:21 PM`)

*Mara comes down the remaining five steel steps onto the cold terrazzo floor, rubs the dark streak of gear grease across her left thumb knuckle once against her rolled flannel cuff, and stops beside the wooden A-frame, tapping the blank felt board near Devon's hand.*

**MARA CHEN:** "Keep Saturday completely out of our mouths—I haven't torn through the back parts bins for that collar shim yet, pulling our dead head to check the pedestal takes both of us lifting, and we don't even know if Rialto's Machine Two survives its four reels tonight. Letter it *7:30 35MM CANCELLED — MAIN PROJECTOR GEAR FAILURE — RAIN-CHECK PASSES & LIMITED TILL INSIDE*, and tell the whole line aloud the second you crack the doors that this afternoon's print courier drained our float to eighteen cash payouts, so anyone past eighteen who won't take a stamped rain-check gets a dated, initialed ticket stub we honor in cash tomorrow. Put the letters up and turn the lock to get them out of the wet—I'm standing right here at the counter to answer for the booth face-to-face, and once the lobby clears, you and I are heading upstairs to search the bins and lift that dead head."

### Turn 4 — [Devon Reyes](../02-company/khai-plays/the-spare-projector/personas/devon-reyes.md) (`7:22 PM`)

*Devon slots white plastic letters briskly across the black felt grooves—`7:30 35MM CANCELLED — MAIN PROJECTOR GEAR FAILURE — RAIN-CHECK PASSES & LIMITED TILL INSIDE`—pivots the brass-trimmed A-frame flush against the left street glass toward the umbrellas outside, unlocks the steel petty-cash tray with the key on his wrist loop, and squares the unbanded stack of stamped rain-check passes once against the counter edge before striding to the right-hand door, throwing the thumb-turn deadbolt, and pulling the wet glass leaf wide.*

**DEVON REYES:** "That's clean and we can stand behind every word of it—keep a pen ready right beside me at the tray. Evening, everyone—come inside out of the rain and let me give it to you straight so nobody wastes another minute on the sidewalk: our sole 35mm projector sheared its drive gear in the booth, we have no backup machine in the house, and tonight's 7:30 screening is cancelled. If you walked up hoping to buy at the door, we're deeply sorry for the wet trip; if you're holding paid Friday stubs, line up inside at the counter where Mara and I have stamped Astor rain-check passes ready right now. I'll be plain about our drawer, too—settling a print courier C.O.D. this afternoon left us eighteen cash refunds on hand, so once bill eighteen goes out, anyone who prefers cash over a rain-check leaves with a dated stub signed by both of us for full cash payout right here tomorrow."

*Fifteen minutes elapse past the billed `7:30 PM` showtime as heavy rain drums on the Astor marquee. Inside the open right-hand door, the felt board on [The Marquee Easel & Cash Till](../02-company/khai-plays/the-spare-projector/pieces/lobby-easel-and-till.md) still reads `7:30 35MM CANCELLED — MAIN PROJECTOR GEAR FAILURE — RAIN-CHECK PASSES & LIMITED TILL INSIDE`. Across the past quarter hour, roughly two dozen wet patrons have exchanged stubs for stamped cardstock rain-check passes and dispersed down the block, while [Devon Reyes](../02-company/khai-plays/the-spare-projector/personas/devon-reyes.md) has counted out small bills from the steel petty-cash tray until refund eighteen empties the last paper dollar, leaving only loose coins.*

*Moments earlier, [Mara Chen](../02-company/khai-plays/the-spare-projector/personas/mara-chen.md) stepped eight stairs up to the booth doorway to kill power on the crippled projector's lamp rectifier. Beside her, [The Booth Extension Phone](../02-company/khai-plays/the-spare-projector/pieces/astor-booth-phone.md) stays dark and silent—no second message has arrived, the dead 35mm head remains bolted to its pedestal, and the back parts bins sit unsearched until the lobby clears. Down on the puddled terrazzo, [Promise Reckoning](../02-company/khai-plays/the-spare-projector/processes/promise-reckoning.md) comes due among the final six soaked Friday stub-holders standing at the counter.*
