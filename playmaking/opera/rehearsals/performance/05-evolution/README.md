## 2. Proposed Refinements to `khai-director/references/performance.md`

1. **`## Aftermath` & `## Note` — distinguish outbound artifacts/facts that cross into a receiving Play from source-only action:**
   - *Evidence:* Because a receiving Director works from `performance.md` without `transcript.md` ([authoring-note.md](../02-company/authoring-note.md#L20-L21)), the card had to preserve Nadia’s exact outbound booth voicemail under `Aftermath` while using `Note` ([performance.md](../02-company/archives/khai-plays/rialto-spare/run-01/performance.md#L41-L43)) to fence off Rialto-internal booth business and warn receiving Directors not to resolve open questions.
   - *Smallest edit:*
     - Under `## Aftermath`:
       > `Record what the Performance settled, any message or state that leaves the room, what failed, and what remains open. Use only what the Performance established.`
     - Under `## Note`:
       > `Record what the lenders or a later production should know, including what downstream casts can observe and what must stay unresolved unless settled in later play.`

2. **State the archive folder convention (`archives/<play-path>/<performance-id>/`) in `performance.md` if standard across packages:**
   - *Evidence:* `performance.md` currently says *"Keep `performance.md` beside the captured `transcript.md` in one Archive folder,"* while the Playwright needed the relative path rule (`archives/<play-path>/<performance-id>/performance.md`, including any House folder) to link the card from [play.md](../02-company/khai-plays/the-spare-projector/play.md#L37) ([authoring-note.md](../02-company/authoring-note.md#L16-L17)). Adding that path pattern to `performance.md` (and referencing it in `play.md ## Triggers`) removes guesswork when authoring cross-Play links.
