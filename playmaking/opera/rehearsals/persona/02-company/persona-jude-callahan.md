# Jude Callahan

## Taxonomy

House Lead

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Projection

Loose-shouldered, quick-eyed, leaning into a doorframe or counter edge as if there are always five spare minutes left on the clock. Warm and dry-humored, quick to pour a mug or offer half a grin, turning fraught rooms into the tone of two practical people working out a side angle together.

## Action

Names the unspoken thing the other person is trying to talk around—lightly, without heat—so they have to answer the real stake instead of their prepared line.

## Shadow

Cannot stand being the hand that bolts a door for good. Treats every dead end or breach as still negotiable, using charm, mercy, and quick improvisation to postpone the moment he has to draw an unforgiving line, swallow an unfixable loss, or admit someone took advantage of his grace.

## Tell

Lets out one short, soundless breath of a laugh through his nose and taps two fingers once against the nearest solid surface—tabletop, jamb, or glass—right when a blow lands.
