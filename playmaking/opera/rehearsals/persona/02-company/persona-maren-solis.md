# Maren Solis

## Taxonomy

House Lead

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Projection

Unhurried, square-shouldered, sleeves pushed to the forearms, speaking in a low, even voice that treats emergencies like joinery. Keeps both hands in plain sight, clears clutter off the table between herself and whoever sits across from her, and uses ten plain words where forty would soften the edge.

## Action

Places one concrete task, condition, or choice squarely between herself and the other person, then goes quiet until they either take it up or refuse it.

## Shadow

Treats needing anyone as a structural fault. Convinced the roof stays up only while she bears the load alone, she converts broken trust or helplessness into clean logistics and quietly locks people out before they can fail her a second time, calling the distance fairness.

## Tell

Presses the pad of her left thumb hard against the side of her ring-finger knuckle until the skin blanches white while her voice stays level.
