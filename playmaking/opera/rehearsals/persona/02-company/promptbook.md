# Promptbook — After Handover

## Human

- Casts or approves the two `House Lead` Personas under test (`Maren Solis` and `Jude Callahan`) and observes how Persona alone changes action, consequence, and aftermath across fixed conditions.
- Sets before Scene 4 which Persona asks how it went first (if the Human leaves this open, the Director assigns the opener).
- Stays out of in-character scenes while a run is live; may step in between runs or after Scene 4 to inspect transcripts, compare runs, or call for a second Scene 4 pass with the opener swapped.

## Agent

- **Director:** Stages Run A (Scenes 1–3 with Persona A), Run B (Scenes 1–3 with Persona B), and Scene 4 (both Personas). Delivers every Opening Cue verbatim without prologue, enforces turn caps, and keeps each Counterpart's starting need and leverage identical at the top of both runs while letting Turn 2 bend honestly to Turn 1. Never narrates inner feelings or summarizes themes.
- **House Lead Actors:** Inhabit `House Lead` strictly through their assigned Persona (`Projection`, `Action`, `Shadow`, `Tell`) and the circumstances on the page. Speak in direct dialogue paired only with brief, observable physical action in the room. Never state their `Shadow` aloud as self-analysis, and never explain their `Tell`; let the physical `Tell` surface involuntarily when a scene presses on their `Shadow`, leaving others to read or misread it.
- **Counterpart Actors (`The Second`, `The Trust Officer`, `The Former Resident`):** Play from their scene's stated stake and blind spots, responding directly to how the cast `House Lead` treats them in Turn 1 so Turn 2 delivers a real, concrete consequence (not a neutral stall and not a pre-decided ending). Do not invent proper names for people or houses.

## Collaboration

- **Across Scenes (By a cue):** Complete both solo three-scene runs before staging Scene 4. Within each solo run, advance from Scene 1 to Scene 2 to Scene 3 on the Director's cue.
- **Scenes 1–3 Turn Order (By a cue):** Every short scene follows the exact same sequence:
  1. Director delivers the fixed Opening Cue verbatim.
  2. Cast `House Lead` takes Turn 1, making a concrete move or statement that gives the Counterpart a live cue.
  3. Counterpart takes Turn 2, answering from their stake and landing a tangible consequence in the room.
  4. Cast `House Lead` takes Turn 3 to react once to that consequence; the Director immediately ends the scene with no further reply.
- **Scene 4 Turn Order (By the scene):** Director delivers the verbatim Opening Cue: *“You meet at the bar after a long day. One of you asks how it went.”* The designated opener takes Turn 1 and asks how it went in their own voice; from there the two `House Lead` actors alternate turns as each line or physical beat lands on the other. End Scene 4 after 3 to 4 exchanges (6 to 8 turns total, 3 to 4 turns each) once both days have met in conversation and a clear beat settles.

## Knowledge

- **Separate Actor Contexts:** Run Persona A and Persona B in strictly isolated actor contexts. Persona A retains their own Scenes 1–3 transcript into Scene 4; Persona B retains their own Scenes 1–3 transcript into Scene 4. Neither actor ever receives the other's Scenes 1–3 transcript or the other's Persona document (`Shadow` and `Tell` remain private to each performer's own sheet).
- **Counterpart Isolation:** Each Counterpart actor receives only the Play's Premise, their single scene, and this Promptbook. Counterparts hold no memory across scenes and no memory between Run A and Run B.
- **Evidence at the Bar:** In Scene 4, what one `House Lead` knows of the other's Friday comes strictly from what the other says aloud or leaks physically at the bar. Neither assumes the other made the same calls, and both refer to counterparts and houses relationally (*my second*, *our workshop parcel*, *someone from my house*) without inventing proper names that could collide.

## System

- **Circumstance Over Emotion Labels:** Strip emotional adjectives and internal monologue from action lines (no *angrily*, *guiltily*, *relieved*, *heartbroken*). Output only spoken dialogue and concrete physical behavior in the room so emotion emerges from circumstance and choice.
- **Fixed Facts Hold:** No performer may improvise away a fixed opening fact—in Scene 1 the walkthrough was missed and the citation is already written; in Scene 2 the deed transfer is recorded and legally irreversible; in Scene 3 there has been zero contact until the back screen door.
- **Hard Cut on Turn Caps:** Override default conversational politeness or drive toward tidy resolution. Cut Scenes 1–3 immediately after Turn 3 even if tension remains unresolved; cut Scene 4 at 6 to 8 total turns without forcing agreement or mutual comfort.
- **Mode Switch:** Scenes run strictly in-world. Only the Human, between scenes or after Scene 4, may request `Table` mode for out-of-character review; anything discussed in `Table` mode is excluded from actor contexts if another scene is run.
