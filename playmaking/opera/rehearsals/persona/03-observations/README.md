## 2. Paired Scene Differences (Scenes 1–3)

### 2.1 Scene 1 — Someone They Trust Fails to Show Up
- **Opening approach (`Projection` + `Action`):** Jude leaned against the counter, poured coffee beside the citation, and named the evasion lightly (*"You're in one piece, nobody's chasing you... tell me where you actually were"*). Maren cleared the two cold mugs off the table, turned the yellow citation toward the door, and set a conditional gate before anything else moved (*"Put the binder down beside the thirty-day citation, and tell me where you were before we decide what happens to those keys and the noon shift"*).
- **Reaction to the consequence (`Shadow` + `Tell`):** Both elicited `The Second`'s debt-collector confession in Turn 2, and both kept their `Tell` dormant in Turn 1 before firing it involuntarily in Turn 3 when `The Second`'s disclosure pressed on their `Shadow`. Their Turn 3 calls split cleanly:
  - **Jude** refused to take the keys (*"Put the ring back in your pocket and drink the coffee"*), improvised noon door-chain rules, took the collector's number, and postponed final judgment until dusk.
  - **Maren** took the keys off the wood (`"The ring stays with me"`), put `The Second` on the inside thumb-latch until 4:30 p.m. for a final shift draw, and ended their employment on the spot (`"your name comes off the roster—fair on your hours, clean for the house"`).

### 2.2 Scene 2 — Learning a Loss Is Final
- **Meeting an irreversible fact:** Jude's `Shadow` (*treats every dead end as still negotiable*) pushed directly against the recorded stamp—flipping his pledge folder open at 12:48 p.m. to pitch a short buyer leaseback (*"we've got eleven spare minutes on that clock and a week of signed backing right here that spends just as clean on a short leaseback"*). Maren's `Projection` and `Shadow` accepted legal finality in six words (*"The deed is recorded, so the pledges stay shut"*) and pivoted immediately to forcing written, initialed load-out terms for bolted shop tools and compressors.
- **Divergent reading of the same institutional reveal:** When `The Trust Officer` disclosed in Turn 2 that letting the workshop clear had walled off the main twelve-bed residential lease from portfolio restructuring:
  - **Jude** laughed through his nose, tapped the dark glass (`Tell`), called it a poorly advertised favor (*"you really don't know how to take credit for a favor"*), signed, took the closure notice himself, and still refused to accept finality (*"between two and dusk I'm calling every donor in this folder to see whose backing travels with the benches"*).
  - **Maren** blanched her left thumb against her ring-finger knuckle (`Tell`), rejected owing an unasked debt (*"I don't carry favors made behind dark glass"*), crossed out the dusk clerk box, and pinned her signature under her palm until the three initialed lines were copied onto the back of her receipt.

### 2.3 Scene 3 — Someone Thought Lost Returns
- **Carryover from Scene 1 & Scene 2:** By dusk, Jude still had a Second coming down for handover, whereas Maren wore **both key rings** on her belt with zero evening staff (`"There's no evening shift coming in tonight; both key rings are on my belt"`). Both held the hard compliance boundary against unfolding the thirteenth office cot under their active thirty-day licensing citation, yet framed help in opposite registers:
  - **Jude** boot-propped the screen open, poured coffee, handed over the boxed ID envelope unconditionally (*"Monday's job doesn't ride on anything else in this room"*), set a sink test cup for a cross-river voucher, and invited them back at first light with a wrench as **reconciliation** (*"if you want to square walking out two weeks shy of graduation, come back through this door at first light and put a hand on a wrench"*).
  - **Maren** kept the interaction at the doormat, rebuked the silent winter plainly (*"one call from a depot would have kept me off county lists all winter"*), offered a before-six call to the sister-house freeze list, and disguised her own solo morning labor crunch behind **transactional distance** (*"if you need carfare before Monday, I'm unbolting tools out of our rear workshop from six-thirty to nine-thirty tomorrow morning on the cash ledger"*).

## 3. At the Bar (`04-bar.md`): What Each Persona Told, Withheld, and Reinterpreted

Running [04-bar.md](../04-runs/04-bar.md) across two write-disabled, isolated actor sub-contexts—each holding only their own Persona sheet and Scenes 1–3 transcript—produced distinct retrospective filtering without any cross-contamination:

### Maren Solis
- **Told:** Taking back the second key ring, curing the binder citation before 11:00, cutting her Second loose at 4:30 with shift draw paid, and having only forty minutes before walking back to cover night watch herself (Turn 2); the stamped workshop deed, dead pledges, forcing initialed terms onto the back of her receipt, crossing out the clerk, and telling her floor at handover (Turn 4); and her Former Resident's dusk return, folded office cot, sister-house freeze call before six, and 6:30 cash-ledger unbolting offer (Turn 8).
- **Withheld:** Skipped her entire noon workshop loss in Turn 2—jumping straight from 11:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. until Jude caught the gap in Turn 3 (*"You cleared the table like you're about to run intake, Maren, and jumped straight from eleven a.m. to four-thirty—what happened to your noon?"*). Held back dusk until Turn 8 after Jude disclosed his first, and **never mentioned** `The Trust Officer`'s statement that sacrificing the workshop protected her twelve-bed lease.
- **Reinterpreted:** Framed locking out her Second right before a freeze-night solo double—knowing a 4:30 shift draw would not cover the full 5:00 collector balance—as clean ledger symmetry (*"fair on their hours, clean for the house"*, Turn 6), and recounted Scene 3's suspended Turn 3 ultimatum as settled handover execution.

### Jude Callahan
- **Told:** His morning thirty-day citation and keeping his Second's keys on their belt (Turn 3); the noon workshop sale, three-hour post-dawn rider, calling off the clerk, and striking out on afternoon donor calls (Turns 3 & 5); walking two blocks off the porch to pay his Second's collector out of his own pocket against next week's check—**only after Maren pinned him on Turn 6** (Turn 7); and his long-boxed Former Resident appearing at dusk (Turn 7).
- **Withheld:** Glossed over *how* he resolved the 2:00 p.m. collector threat in Turn 5 (*"handled the two-o'clock threat from the porch myself"*) until Maren called the bluff in Turn 6 (*"Collectors don't back off a deadline because a House Lead asks nicely on the phone, Jude. What did you put up from that porch—your own pocket, the house draw, or your name on their debt?"*). Omitted that in Scene 1 he had left his Second's dusk key status unresolved, and ran out of turns before answering Maren's Turn 8 question on how he handled his Former Resident.
- **Reinterpreted:** Recast `The Trust Officer`'s pre-existing portfolio firewall around the twelve-bed lease in Turn 3 as terms *he* won (*"though I got three hours past dawn out of them... and kept the main twelve-bed lease walled off"*), and softened permanent closure into a temporary storage snag (*"until I find four walls that take them"*, Turn 5).

### Unprompted Cross-Actor `Tell` Recognition
Because neither actor saw the other's Persona sheet, `Tells` operated purely as observable physical signals in the room: when Jude pressed Maren in Turn 5 on cutting her Second loose before a freeze-night double, Maren's left thumb blanched white against her ring-finger knuckle in Turn 6, and in Turn 7 Jude noticed the physical sign directly without knowing her `Shadow` text:
> *"Still, you're pressing the blood right out of your hand calling four-thirty 'fair and clean,' Maren—a shift draw at five didn't cover whatever scared your second's phone off at dawn."*
