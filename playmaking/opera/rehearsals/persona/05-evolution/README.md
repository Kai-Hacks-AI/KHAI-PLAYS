# Evolution: TO PAST Persona

Evidence-linked assessments and smallest proposed edits for **Playwright**, **Promptbook (HACKS)**, and **Persona (TO PAST)**, anchored to concrete moments from authoring and the staged runs in [04-runs/](../04-runs/).

---

## 1. Playwright (`playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/SKILL.md`)

- **Where the authoring workflow helped:**
  Separating `For a Persona, use TO PAST` from `For a Play, use the Promptbook guide` let the Playwright deliver two contrasting `House Lead` Personas, a four-scene Play ([play.md](../02-company/play.md)), and its Promptbook ([promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md)) in one pass without needing clarification—solving the parallel-day bar constraint cleanly by setting independent transitional houses across a city network on quarter-end freeze Friday.
- **Where it left a choice unclear:**
  1. **Play-only wording holdover during Persona authoring:** Line 9 of `khai-playwright/SKILL.md` updates the first sentence to *"author the Play or Persona they request"*, then immediately reverts in the third sentence to *"Use them to decide **what the Play must contain**"*—leaving Persona-only requests outside the completeness and clarification check.
  2. **Unresolved hard cuts jumping into a later scene:** In [01-jude-failed-to-show.md](../04-runs/01-jude-failed-to-show.md) Turn 3, Jude deferred the 2:00 p.m. debt-collector confrontation to the afternoon, and in [03-maren-return.md](../04-runs/03-maren-return.md) Turn 3 Maren ended on an unanswered conditional (`"Set your bag by the radiator if I'm dialing that call, or turn the bolt behind you"`) right on the mandatory Turn 3 cut. When [04-bar.md](../04-runs/04-bar.md) opened *after* evening handover, Maren pressed Jude on what happened at 2:00 p.m. (Turn 6), forcing Jude's actor in Turn 7 to invent offstage afternoon facts (walking two blocks off the porch to pay the collector from his own pocket) that never occurred on-page.
- **Smallest proposed edit to `playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/SKILL.md`:**
  - On line 9, replace `"Use them to decide what the Play must contain"` with:
    ```markdown
    Use them to decide what the work must contain
    ```
  - On line 15 (`For a short Play...`), append one sentence for multi-scene continuity:
    ```markdown
    If a later scene opens after an unresolved cut, state what settles offstage between them.
    ```

## 2. Promptbook (`02-company/promptbook.md` & Reusable HACKS `references/knowledge.md`)

- **Keep in [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md):**
  - `System → Circumstance Over Emotion Labels` (*"Strip emotional adjectives and internal monologue from action lines... Output only spoken dialogue and concrete physical behavior in the room"*): kept all seven runs grounded in physical props (coffee mugs, coasters, key rings, recording stamps) rather than named feelings.
  - `Knowledge → Separate Actor Contexts` (*"`Shadow` and `Tell` remain private to each performer's own sheet"*): directly enabled Jude in [04-bar.md](../04-runs/04-bar.md) Turn 7 to spot Maren's blanched knuckle live across the booth (*"you're pressing the blood right out of your hand calling four-thirty 'fair and clean,' Maren"*) and draw his own conclusion without seeing her Persona file.
- **Rewrite in [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md) (`## Knowledge → Counterpart Isolation`):**
  - *Moment:* In [01-jude-failed-to-show.md](../04-runs/01-jude-failed-to-show.md) Turn 1, `play.md` Scene 1 (`Reasons to Act`) printed `The Second`'s secret overdue debt collector directly on the scene page while `promptbook.md` only isolated Counterparts from Persona sheets—omitting the reverse rule hiding Counterpart `Reasons to Act` from `House Lead`. Only because the Director redacted Counterpart `Reasons to Act` as a staging choice could Jude say *"You're in one piece, nobody's chasing you"* and let `The Second`'s Turn 2 (*"Somebody is chasing me, actually"*) land genuinely (the same asymmetric leak recurred in [02-maren-final-loss.md](../04-runs/02-maren-final-loss.md) around the dusk clerk offer and [03-maren-return.md](../04-runs/03-maren-return.md) around the fallen-through sublet).
  - *Proposed rewrite in [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md) (`## Knowledge`):*
    ```markdown
    - **Counterpart & Intra-Scene Isolation:** Each Counterpart receives only the Play's Premise, shared opening facts, their own Reasons to Act, and this Promptbook (holding no memory across scenes or runs). Within Scenes 1–3, withhold the Counterpart's Reasons to Act from House Lead so unvoiced motives enter only through play.
    ```
- **Smallest proposed edit to the reusable HACKS template (`playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/references/knowledge.md`):**
  - **Affected chapter:** `Knowledge` (`references/knowledge.md`) — confirming the same cross-partition finding observed in `promptbook-chapters`.
  - **Proposed wording (append one sentence):**
    ```markdown
    Keep role-private motives and secrets out of shared Play sections, or name exactly what the Director withholds from each role at curtain-up.
    ```

## 3. Persona (`02-company/persona-*.md` & Reusable TO PAST `references/persona.md`)

- **Details that gave the actors something concrete to play (Keep):**
  - **Maren's `Action` + `Shadow` ([persona-maren-solis.md](../02-company/persona-maren-solis.md)):** *"Places one concrete task, condition, or choice squarely between herself and the other person, then goes quiet"* paired with *"converts broken trust or helplessness into clean logistics... calling the distance fairness."* In [03-maren-return.md](../04-runs/03-maren-return.md) Turn 3, despite holding both key rings alone with zero evening staff ahead of a 6:30 a.m. shop strip-out, Maren offered morning unbolting pay framed strictly as *the Former Resident's* need for Monday carfare rather than admitting she needed help.
  - **Jude's `Action` + `Tell` ([persona-jude-callahan.md](../02-company/persona-jude-callahan.md)):** *"Names the unspoken thing the other person is trying to talk around—lightly, without heat"* paired with the nasal breath-laugh and two-finger tap. In [04-bar.md](../04-runs/04-bar.md) Turn 3, Jude immediately caught Maren skipping noon (*"jumped straight from eleven a.m. to four-thirty—what happened to your noon?"*), and in Turn 7 his `Tell` fired at the exact instant Maren pinned him into admitting he paid off his Second's collector from his own pocket.
- **Details to cut or rewrite in the authored Personas:**
  1. **Furniture-bound habit in [persona-maren-solis.md](../02-company/persona-maren-solis.md) (`## Projection`):**
     - *Moment:* `"clears clutter off the table between herself and whoever sits across from her"` produced table-clearing in Scene 1 ([01-maren-failed-to-show.md](../04-runs/01-maren-failed-to-show.md) Turn 1: two cold mugs), Scene 2 ([02-maren-final-loss.md](../04-runs/02-maren-final-loss.md) Turn 1: parcel file and pledges), and Scene 4 ([04-bar.md](../04-runs/04-bar.md) Turn 2: drink menu and salt shaker), and collided with Scene 3 ([03-maren-return.md](../04-runs/03-maren-return.md)), which stands at a latched back-kitchen screen door with no table between them—requiring Director intervention to translate the habit onto a doormat jar crate and counter towels rather than inventing furniture.
     - *Rewrite in [persona-maren-solis.md](../02-company/persona-maren-solis.md):* Replace `"clears clutter off the table between herself and whoever sits across from her"` with `"clears space between herself and whoever stands or sits across from her"`.
  2. **Emotion/tone adjective in [persona-jude-callahan.md](../02-company/persona-jude-callahan.md) (`## Projection`):**
     - *Moment:* `"Warm and dry-humored"` clashed with `System → Circumstance Over Emotion Labels` in [01-jude-failed-to-show.md](../04-runs/01-jude-failed-to-show.md), requiring the Director to instruct the actor to render it through props and line cadence rather than tonal adverbs.
     - *Cut/rewrite in [persona-jude-callahan.md](../02-company/persona-jude-callahan.md):* Replace `"Warm and dry-humored, quick to pour a mug"` with `"Quick to pour a mug or answer tension with dry understatement"`.
- **Smallest proposed edit to the reusable TO PAST template (`playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/references/persona.md`):**
  - **Affected sections:** `Projection` and `Tell`.
  - **Moments:** (a) Maren's table-bound `Projection` straining the standing screen-door scene in [03-maren-return.md](../04-runs/03-maren-return.md); and (b) in [04-bar.md](../04-runs/04-bar.md), the Director had to add explicit pre-scene direction restraining `Tell` frequency so actors held their `Tell` dormant until a line genuinely struck their `Shadow` (Turns 6 and 7) instead of firing it on routine turns.
  - **Proposed wording in `references/persona.md`:**
    - Under `## Projection`, replace `Show what the Persona presents to the room. Make it concrete.` with:
      ```markdown
      Show what the Persona presents in posture, speech, and use of space across any room. Make it observable without tying it to one setting.
      ```
    - Under `## Tell` (building on `a7375d0`'s current `Give one involuntary physical sign, distinct from the deliberate move in Action. It can surface in the same beat. Let others notice that something is off and draw their own conclusions.`), make dormancy explicit on non-pressure beats:
      ```markdown
      Give one involuntary physical sign, distinct from the deliberate move in Action, that stays dormant until the Shadow is under pressure. It can surface in the same beat. Let others notice that something is off and draw their own conclusions.
      ```

## 4. Cross-Skill Seam (`playmaking/opera/skills/khai-director/SKILL.md`)

- **Moment:** Across all seven runs ([01-jude-failed-to-show.md](../04-runs/01-jude-failed-to-show.md) through [04-bar.md](../04-runs/04-bar.md)), Directors had to hold **both** the Play and the cast TO PAST Personas fixed while keeping each Persona's `Shadow` and `Tell` out of counterpart contexts—yet `khai-director/SKILL.md` currently names only `the authored Play and any Promptbook`.
- **Smallest proposed edit to `khai-director/SKILL.md` (lines 9 and 11):**
  - Line 9: `Read the authored Play, any Personas cast in its roles, and any Promptbook supplied for the run.`
  - Line 11: `Treat the authored Play and Personas as fixed.`
