# The Second's Key Ring

## Taxonomy

House Key Set

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Place

On the house's side-entry office table, within reach between the House Lead and the Second beside the thirty-day provisional citation and the night-watch binder.

## Load Bearing

With no third staffer on the roster, a twelve-bed house cannot run two shifts on one set of keys. Without this ring, no deputy can unlock, secure, or relieve the doors while the House Lead is downtown or off watch—forcing noon intake onto an inside latch or chain and collapsing freeze-night coverage onto one person's belt.

## Apparent

A tarnished brass split ring on a belt clip, holding duplicate keys for the front lock, side door, and office alongside a small brass tag stamped 2. Dry from a coat pocket and still warm from a closed hand, the keys show light wear along the grooves—ordinary shift hardware against the office wood.

## Yearbook

- **Six months ago:** Entrusted to the Second after the House Lead personally vouched for them over board hesitation, turning the spare shift set into a personal guarantee.
- **Last night:** Carried home alongside the bagged night-watch binder and maintenance receipts on a promise to arrive thirty minutes before the morning licensing walkthrough, before an overdue personal debt collector threatened to show up on the house steps.
- **Dawn through walkthrough today:** Kept in a coat pocket beside a powered-off phone while the Second paid half the balance off-site to keep the collector away from inspection—leaving the other half due this afternoon while the House Lead walked the inspector through without the binder and took a thirty-day provisional citation.
- **Twenty minutes after walkthrough:** Carried in through the side door and set within reach on the office table beside the citation and binder, with noon intake uncovered, a downtown trust deadline before midday, and the House Lead's call still unmade.
