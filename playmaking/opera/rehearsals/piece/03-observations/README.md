## 3. Authoring & Staging Friction Observed

1. **Temporal Anchor Ambiguity in `Place` and `Yearbook` During Authoring:**
   Before drafting [piece-second-key-ring.md](../02-company/piece-second-key-ring.md), `khai-playwright` paused once to ask which point in time `Place` (*"Say where the Piece is now"*) and `Yearbook` should anchor to:
   > *"1. Maren Solis's house (post-Friday... now clipped as the second ring on Maren's own belt) ... 2. Jude Callahan's house (post-Friday... still on his Second's belt) ... 3. Scene 1 curtain-up / pre-divergence... Which ring and point in time should this Piece anchor to?"*
   Answering to anchor at Scene 1 when the keys are set on the office table resolved the fork immediately, with zero manual edits to the returned Piece.
2. **Asymmetric Secret Leakage Inside `Yearbook` ([02-with-piece.md](../04-runs/02-with-piece.md) Director's Note):**
   Because `piece-second-key-ring.md`'s `Yearbook` traces *"how the Piece came to carry that weight"* chronologically through last night's debt-collector threat and dawn's off-site half-payment, Run 2's Director flagged that handing the standalone Piece sheet to both cast contexts at Scene 1's *original* doorway Opening Cue would leak `The Second`'s hidden motive to `House Lead` before Turn 1 (avoided here only because this rehearsal entered at the post-disclosure table cue).
3. **Link Normalization:** [01-without-piece.md](../04-runs/01-without-piece.md) included `file:///tmp/opera-piece-<hash>/...` links to its isolated staging folder; those targets are normalized to relative links into [../../persona/02-company/](../../persona/02-company/) in [04-runs/01-without-piece.md](../04-runs/01-without-piece.md) with zero text changes.
