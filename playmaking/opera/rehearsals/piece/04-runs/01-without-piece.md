## Staged Performance — *After Handover*, Scene 1 (*After Walkthrough*)

**Cast:**
- **House Lead:** `Maren Solis` ([persona-maren-solis.md](../../persona/02-company/persona-maren-solis.md))
- **Counterpart:** `The Second` ([play.md](../../persona/02-company/play.md))

**Opening Cue**
Setting the night-watch binder beside the thirty-day provisional citation, your Second opens their other hand and sets the second set of house keys down on the office table within reach between you: “Every log and receipt licensing asked for is inside that cover, and I don't make five o'clock's balance without my hours here. Slide those keys back across the wood and I'll clear the citation and hold noon intake through handover—or take the ring off the table right now and I'll clear out while you still have an hour before you head downtown.”

**Turn 1 — House Lead (`Maren Solis`)**
She pushes a pen cup and loose papers to the far corner of the office table, clearing the wood between them. With her right hand she draws the key ring back across the table and sets it beside her own forearm. She squares the night-watch binder alongside the thirty-day citation, then rests both hands on the bare wood, the pad of her left thumb pressing against the side of her ring-finger knuckle until the skin goes white. “The keys stay on my side. I’ll forfeit the workshop parcel downtown and take noon intake myself. Sit that chair until four-thirty, match those receipts to every line on the citation, and sign out the full shift’s hours so you clear five o’clock—then your time here is done. Sit, or take the door.”

**Turn 2 — The Second**
The Second unbuttons their dry coat, pulls out the chair across from her, and sits, keeping both hands on their own half of the table away from the key ring beside her forearm. Reaching to the far corner of the wood, they take a pen from the cup, draw the night-watch binder and the thirty-day citation in front of them, and unclip the stack of maintenance receipts from inside the cover. “I didn’t just shut my phone off to keep that collector off the porch. I shut it off so I wouldn’t have to call you at dawn and prove the board right six months ago—I gambled I could pay half, make it back before licensing knocked, and keep it out of this house. Then I walked in late and tried to use your downtown closing to pry the keys back.” They align the top receipt with the first numbered line on the citation and set the pen point to the margin. “I’ll have every line of this citation matched before noon. Don’t pay me to sit a chair till four-thirty while you forfeit the workshop parcel—put me on bed-turn and kitchen prep under your eye through intake so I’m earning the hours on that sheet. At four-thirty I’ll sign out, settle the balance six blocks over for good, and walk—unless clearing this desk and working the floor without keys buys me a shot at bottom roster tomorrow.”

**Turn 3 — House Lead (`Maren Solis`)**
Her left hand stays where it is on the wood, thumb pad pressed into the side of her ring-finger knuckle until the skin around the joint stands white. With her right hand she lifts the second set of keys from the table, drops the ring into her front pocket, then draws the shift sheet from the corner stack and sets it at the center line beside the citation before placing her empty right hand back on the bare wood. “There is no bottom roster in a two-staff house. Every shift here carries keys, and a phone that goes dark at dawn doesn’t hold this threshold twice. Match every line on that citation, turn the intake beds, and get kitchen prep on before noon. At four-thirty I’ll sign out your shift and put six months of completed hours in writing so we’re square—and at four-thirty you walk.”

*[Hard cut]*
