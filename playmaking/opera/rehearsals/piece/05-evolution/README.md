## 1. Smallest Suggested Edit to `references/piece.md` (`Place` & `Yearbook`)

- **What worked as written (Keep):**
  - **`Load Bearing`** (*"Say what would fail in the world if this Piece were removed"*) directly changed both characters' strategic options: without the Piece ([01-without-piece.md](../04-runs/01-without-piece.md) Turn 1), Maren assumed taking the keys forced her to forfeit her downtown workshop closing; with the Piece ([02-with-piece.md](../04-runs/02-with-piece.md) Turn 1–2), Maren tried to run noon intake off the inside latch while going downtown, and `The Second` recognized that inside-latch trap and walked out onto the stoop, forcing the exact operational collapse `Load Bearing` predicted.
  - **`Apparent`** (*"Show what someone can observe before they know what the Piece carries"*) turned generic `"keys"` into playable belt-clip brass hardware that clicked audibly against Maren's own ring in [02-with-piece.md](../04-runs/02-with-piece.md) Turn 3.
- **Where `references/piece.md` left a choice unclear or leaked private history:**
  1. **Unanchored `"now"` in `## Place` (Authoring pause):** During Step 1 authoring, `Say where the Piece is now` forced `khai-playwright` to stop and ask whether `"now"` meant curtain-up, mid-scene on the office table, or post-run after Scene 4.
  2. **Unpartitioned secrets in `## Yearbook` ([02-with-piece.md](../04-runs/02-with-piece.md) Director's Note §3):** Tracing how the ring came to carry its weight put `The Second`'s private debt-collector threat and dawn half-payment into `Yearbook`; handing a standalone Piece brief to the whole cast at a normal Scene 1 opening leaks unrevealed role secrets unless `Yearbook` marks who knows private entries.
- **Smallest proposed edit to `playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/references/piece.md`:**
  - Under `## Place`, replace `Say where the Piece is now and link its Place if it has a file.` with:
    ```markdown
    Say where the Piece starts when the scene opens, and link its Place if it has a file.
    ```
  - Under `## Yearbook`, append one sentence to `Trace how the Piece came to carry that weight in a few moments placed in time.`:
    ```markdown
    Trace how the Piece came to carry that weight in a few moments placed in time. Mark any moment that only one role knows at the opening.
    ```
