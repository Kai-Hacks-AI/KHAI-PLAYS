# Tomas — Heel Carrier

## Taxonomy

Rear volunteer carrier at the heel end of the [Giant Papier-Mâché Shoe](piece-shoe.md) for the Third Ward parade crew

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Projection

Dried paste flecks at his wrists, shoulders squared low beneath the rear counter, carrying the end he ribbed and patched himself in the parish shed with unhurried shop-floor steadiness after promising the workshop crew their boot would ride the lead wagon upright.

## Action

Slides one broad palm inward from the wooden rim to press directly beneath the softening heel seam, checking the rear stride by half a pace so his partner feels the change through the boot.

## Shadow

Takes every fresh crease in the papier-mâché as a public verdict on his workmanship—guarding the failing patch with his hands rather than admitting aloud in the open square how close the heel is to folding through.

## Tell

He draws his lower lip between his teeth whenever the damp cardboard yields another fraction over his knuckles.
