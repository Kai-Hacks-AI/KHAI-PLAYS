# Giant Papier-Mâché Shoe

## Taxonomy

Third Ward Autumn Parade Entry Kit

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Place

Carried mid-crossing across [Market Square](place-square.md), held knee-high off the flagstones between two volunteer carriers.

## Load Bearing

Without the shoe reaching the waiting flatbed beyond the east arch intact, the Third Ward forfeits its lead marshaling berth in the Autumn Parade, stranding six weeks of parish-shed volunteer build work before step-off.

## Apparent

An eight-foot russet papier-mâché work boot with painted brass eyelets, rope laces thick as wrists, and an inner wooden carry-welt along the sole rim. Under the rear heel counter, a damp seam has gone soft and begun creasing inward over the rear carrier’s hands, bowing closer to the stones every few strides.

## Yearbook

- **Six weeks ago:** When timber and shed space fell short for a full twelve-foot figure, the Third Ward workshop scaled its build down to a single oversized left boot meant to ride the lead flatbed on its own.
- **Tuesday night:** After a roof leak dampened the rear ribbing in the parish shed, the heel arch was repatched late at night with heavy flour paste that never fully cured in the wet autumn air.
- **Two minutes ago:** Lifting the boot clear of its sawhorses at the west curb cracked an inner rib behind the heel; halfway across the square, the softened shell began taking thumb-pressure and folding inward under its own weight.
