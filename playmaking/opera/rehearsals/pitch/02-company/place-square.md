# Market Square

## Taxonomy

Old Town Parade Route, between the West Staging Curb and the East Arch

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Shown

A broad span of uneven granite flagstones sloped gently toward a dry central fountain; white chalk marshaling lines marking the parade lane; early onlookers gathered along rope barriers on the north and south flanks; brass instruments warming up out of sight beyond the east arch; damp autumn air that keeps flour paste from stiffening.

## Holds

- [Giant Papier-Mâché Shoe](piece-shoe.md), carried mid-lane thirty yards short of the east arch.
- A waist-high stone plinth beside the dry fountain rim, three paces outside the chalk marshaling line.
- Low rope crowd-barriers flanking the lane, loose enough for a small bystander to step down onto the chalk edge beside passing crews.

## Offers

- **To the carriers:** A clear thirty-yard straightaway to the waiting flatbed beyond the east arch if they hold pace, or the waist-high fountain plinth three paces off-line where the buckling heel could be braced at the cost of breaking stride in front of the crowd.
- **To onlookers along the rope:** Unobstructed sightlines down to the underside of the boot and close enough footing at the chalk edge to walk beside the carriers and speak without raising a voice.

## Withheld

- **Cover or a hidden rest inside the lane:** Nothing along the chalk path conceals the softening heel or takes the boot’s weight unseen. Reaching the flatbed on time requires crossing the remaining open stone under strain; sparing the heel early requires veering off-line or stopping in plain view of everyone watching.
