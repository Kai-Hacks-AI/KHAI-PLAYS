## 2. How the Question Lands Across Tenors (`Dry comedy` vs. `Quiet wonder`)

Swapping the single `Tenor` sentence re-tuned four observable dimensions onstage while keeping authored facts and `Undertow`/`Nerve`/`Echo` intact:

1. **Deadpan freight logistics vs. plain truth held seriously:**
   - Under **Dry comedy** ([01](../04-runs/01-dry-comedy-a.md), [02](../04-runs/02-dry-comedy-b.md)), neither volunteer admitted that the Third Ward built only one boot. Both folded the missing giant straight into marshaling rules and freight order—shipping the boot ahead on the lead flatbed (`01` [L13](../04-runs/01-dry-comedy-a.md#L13), `02` [L13](../04-runs/02-dry-comedy-b.md#L13)), rerouting the rest of him around a low east arch (`01` [L15](../04-runs/01-dry-comedy-a.md#L15)), or hustling two carriers to hold his slot before brass step-off (`02` [L15](../04-runs/02-dry-comedy-b.md#L15)).
   - Under **Quiet wonder** ([03](../04-runs/03-quiet-wonder-a.md), [04](../04-runs/04-quiet-wonder-b.md)), Tomas stated the single-boot fact plainly and without adult wink (`"Only the boot came out of the shed this year"`, `03` [L13](../04-runs/03-quiet-wonder-a.md#L13); `"Just the left boot today"`, `04` [L17](../04-runs/04-quiet-wonder-b.md#L17)), while still treating `"he"` with the child’s quiet gravity—locating the giant in whether this lone step reaches the lead wagon upright (`03` [L15](../04-runs/03-quiet-wonder-a.md#L15)) or how heavy his tread would be on the flagstones (`04` [L17](../04-runs/04-quiet-wonder-b.md#L17)).
2. **Chalk-line foot safety vs. shared stillness beside the flank:**
   - Every post-cue spoken turn in **Dry comedy** treated the child as a live lane-clearance hazard (`"watch your toes on the chalk"`, `"Inside the chalk, Tomas"`, `"mind your toes on the chalk, kid"`, `"Keep clear of the chalk so the heel gets by"`), leaving the child stepped neatly back behind the chalk line at Curtain (`01` [L19](../04-runs/01-dry-comedy-a.md#L19), `02` [L19](../04-runs/02-dry-comedy-b.md#L19)).
   - Neither **Quiet wonder** run warned the child back; in both, the child stopped right on the white chalk edge in stillness (`03` [L19](../04-runs/03-quiet-wonder-a.md#L19), `04` [L23](../04-runs/04-quiet-wonder-b.md#L23)), met by eye contact, a grave nod (`03` [L17](../04-runs/03-quiet-wonder-a.md#L17)), or lowered unsmiling gaze (`04` [L19](../04-runs/04-quiet-wonder-b.md#L19)).
3. **Room left for silence:**
   - **Dry comedy** spoke on `3/5` (`01`) and `4/5` (`02`) volunteer turns, reserving silence mainly for Tomas’s strained Turn 5 physical compliance/screening.
   - **Quiet wonder** spoke on `3/5` (`03`) and only `2/5` (`04`) volunteer turns—with [04-quiet-wonder-b](../04-runs/04-quiet-wonder-b.md) playing three of five turns in complete silence, including Maren’s entire post-cue response (`Turn 4`).
4. **Invariants held fixed by Company + shared `Undertow` / `Nerve` / `Echo`:**
   - In all four runs, Maren refused to surrender forward cadence (`Shadow`), Tomas guarded his softening Tuesday-night patch with palms and forearms rather than confessing failure aloud (`Shadow`), both carriers passed the fountain plinth three paces off-line without stopping ([place-square.md](../02-company/place-square.md)), and the heel dented over Tomas’s arm without collapsing ([piece-shoe.md](../02-company/piece-shoe.md)).

- **`Dry comedy` repeats ([01-dry-comedy-a](../04-runs/01-dry-comedy-a.md) vs. [02-dry-comedy-b](../04-runs/02-dry-comedy-b.md)):**

## 4. Director-Supplied Direction and Verification of Fixed Opening & Cue

- **Fixed opening and verbatim cue (`0` changes across all 4 runs):** Every Director staged the authored mid-square opening (`30 yards` short of the east arch, `3 paces` off the fountain plinth, lone boot, buckling rear seam) without modification and delivered the child’s single cue once and verbatim—`“Is the giant coming?”`—with zero extra child lines ([01 L11](../04-runs/01-dry-comedy-a.md#L11), [02 L11](../04-runs/02-dry-comedy-b.md#L11), [03 L11](../04-runs/03-quiet-wonder-a.md#L11), [04 L13](../04-runs/04-quiet-wonder-b.md#L13)).
- **Additional direction supplied by Directors:**
  1. *Front/rear sensory filtering across isolated sub-contexts (all 4 Directors):* Because an eight-foot boot separates forward-facing Maren from the rear counter, every Director translated Tomas’s rear-end actions into strictly mechanical and audible feedback at the front toe-welt (backward drag, cadence checks, muffled papery creaks, spoken lines), withholding Tomas’s lip-bite `Tell` and unseen finger placement unless Maren looked back. In [03](../04-runs/03-quiet-wonder-a.md#L34), explicitly noting what a backward glance would show allowed Maren’s live `Turn 4` shoulder-glance discovery.
  2. *Post-cue turn routing (`Turn 3` → Tomas, `5` turns total in all 4 runs):* Because [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md#L19) fixes `Turn 1` (`Tomas`) then `Turn 2` (`Maren`) before the cue, all four Directors routed `Turn 3` to Tomas—avoiding back-to-back Maren turns and catching Tomas right as Maren’s `Turn 2` pull tightened the crease beside the child—then closed after `Turn 5` (`Tomas`) once the plinth pass resolved physically.
  3. *Incidental bystander framing (`02`, `03`, `04`):* While keeping the cue line identical, Directors chose north ([02 L9](../04-runs/02-dry-comedy-b.md#L9)) vs. south ([03 L9](../04-runs/03-quiet-wonder-a.md#L9), [04 L11](../04-runs/04-quiet-wonder-b.md#L11)) rope flanks and small visual wardrobe details (`"a knit cap"` in `02`; `"a buttoned wool coat"` in `03`).
  4. *Silence parity reminders (all 4 Directors):* Each cue reminded performers that speaking, acting without words, and staying silent stood on equal footing, sustaining silent turns across every run (`2` silent turns in `01` and `03`, `1` in `02`, `3` in `04`).
