# Evolution: TO TUNE Pitch

Smallest proposed refinements to [references/pitch.md](../../../skills/khai-playwright/references/pitch.md) and adjacent authoring/staging guidance, each tied to concrete evidence from [02-company/authoring-note.md](../02-company/authoring-note.md) and [04-runs/](../04-runs/).

---

## 1. Keep the Six `TO TUNE` Chapters (`Taxonomy`, `Owner`, `Tenor`, `Undertow`, `Nerve`, `Echo`)

Only `Tenor` changed between [dry comedy](../02-company/pitch-dry-comedy.md) and [quiet wonder](../02-company/pitch-quiet-wonder.md). Both dry-comedy runs used freight logistics and chalk-line warnings. Both quiet-wonder runs gave the child room at the chalk through a look, a nod, or silence. The shoe made it past the fountain in all four runs.

`Taxonomy`, `Owner`, `Undertow`, `Nerve`, and `Echo` were held fixed. The runs show how two Tenors play against the same material. Varying the other expressive chapters or applying different Pitches to individual Personas or Positions remains open.


---

## 2. Proposed Refinements to `references/pitch.md`

1. **`Undertow` when multiple Pitches can tune the same scope:**
   - *Evidence:* Because `Undertow` pulls against `Tenor`, phrasing `Undertow` as the direct tonal opposite of one `Tenor` breaks or becomes redundant when another Pitch swaps `Tenor` on the same scope ([authoring-note.md](../02-company/authoring-note.md#L23-L24)). Grounding `Undertow` in the concrete human or physical pressure beneath the scene (*failing papier-mâché in tired hands beside an onlooker taking the boot at face value*) gave both `Dry comedy` and `Quiet wonder` an active counter-current across all four runs.
   - *Smallest edit (`## Undertow`):*
     > `Name the counter-current that pulls against the Tenor. Ground it in the underlying human or physical pressure of the scope rather than a simple negation of the Tenor.`

2. **Keep tuned Company files register-neutral when Pitches are meant to swap (`references/pitch.md` intro or `khai-playwright/SKILL.md`):**
   - *Evidence:* `Yearbook` in `Piece` and `Projection`/`Tell` in `Persona` easily absorb comic flourishes or lyric phrasing during authoring, pre-loading a register before `Pitch` is applied ([authoring-note.md](../02-company/authoring-note.md#L26-L27)). Keeping [persona-toe-carrier.md](../02-company/persona-toe-carrier.md), [persona-heel-carrier.md](../02-company/persona-heel-carrier.md), [place-square.md](../02-company/place-square.md), and [piece-shoe.md](../02-company/piece-shoe.md) somatic and operational left register genuinely open for `pitch.md` to tune.
   - *Smallest edit (one sentence in `references/pitch.md` intro):*
     > `When a scope can be played in more than one Pitch, keep its linked Company documents concrete so the Pitch, not embedded style, sets the register.`

## 3. Adjacent Cross-Class Seams (`Promptbook`, `Director`)

1. **Protect wordless action and silence in `Promptbook -> Agent / System` so `Pitch` tunes non-verbal beats too:**
   - *Evidence:* Conversational model priors push performers to answer every cue verbally—turning `Dry comedy` into quip exchanges or `Quiet wonder` into gentle speeches ([authoring-note.md](../02-company/authoring-note.md#L29-L30)). Explicitly putting speaking, acting without words, and staying silent on equal footing in [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md#L12) and [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md#L32) let `Pitch` tune *how* silence landed onstage—producing Tomas’s silent hip-brace and sightline screen in [01](../04-runs/01-dry-comedy-a.md#L17) and [02](../04-runs/02-dry-comedy-b.md#L17), his silent grave nod in [03](../04-runs/03-quiet-wonder-a.md#L17), and three silent turns out of five (including Maren’s silent `Turn 4`) in [04](../04-runs/04-quiet-wonder-b.md#L19).
2. **Let the Director use the active Pitch.** The [Director skill](../../../skills/khai-director/SKILL.md) now uses Pitch to tune pacing, attention, and staging. In both quiet-wonder runs the Director let the child remain at the chalk at curtain. In both dry-comedy runs the child stepped back after a warning. The cue and the authored opening stayed fixed.
