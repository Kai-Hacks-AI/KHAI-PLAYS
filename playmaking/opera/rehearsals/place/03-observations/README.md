# Observations: TO SHOW Place

Twelve single-scene runs staged the exchange over Mother's painting under uniform stopping conditions (all twelve ran ten turns inside their assigned Place after a single Human opening). Runs [01](../04-runs/01-flat-a.md)–[04](../04-runs/04-entrance-baseline-b.md) compare two authored Places hosting the same Company, Piece, and Promptbook; runs [03](../04-runs/03-entrance-baseline-a.md)–[12](../04-runs/12-entrance-withheld-b.md) hold *The Building Entrance* and its Human opening fixed while varying one sentence at a time across `Shown`, `Holds`, `Offers`, and `Withheld`. Two runs per condition give concrete examples of what each Place or chapter change enables, taxes, or forecloses, while also showing different sibling choices under identical conditions.

## 2. Two Places Hosting the Same Exchange (`Mother's Flat` vs. `The Building Entrance`)

Holding the cast, Piece, and Promptbook fixed across [Mother's Flat](../02-company/place-mothers-flat.md) ([01](../04-runs/01-flat-a.md)–[02](../04-runs/02-flat-b.md)) and [The Building Entrance](../02-company/place-building-entrance.md) ([03](../04-runs/03-entrance-baseline-a.md)–[04](../04-runs/04-entrance-baseline-b.md)) shifted how claims were made, what props carried leverage, and what privacy cost:

- **Direct room witnesses vs. locked-away history:** In *Mother's Flat*, the room held the physical referents of the siblings' memories (`Holds`/`Offers`), so Ben walked to the uncurtained kitchen windowsill in both [01](../04-runs/01-flat-a.md) (Turn 3) and [02](../04-runs/02-flat-b.md) (Turns 3, 6), Ada touched the bare picture hook inside the pale plaster mark ([01](../04-runs/01-flat-a.md) Turn 5; [02](../04-runs/02-flat-b.md) Turn 5), and Dan pressed the canvas back into that pale rectangle ([01](../04-runs/01-flat-a.md) Turn 10). Also, because *Mother's Flat* explicitly withheld *chores, tools, or other keepsakes* left by the movers, performers could not invent spare packing material as they had in unplaced runs (*“not even a biro left in the flat to write it down,”* [02](../04-runs/02-flat-b.md) Turn 4).
- **Irreversible lock-out and purchased privacy at the entrance:** At *The Building Entrance*, Ada dropped the flat keys into the brass return slot on Turn 1 of every Entrance run ([03](../04-runs/03-entrance-baseline-a.md)–[12](../04-runs/12-entrance-withheld-b.md)), putting Mother's rooms permanently out of reach. Because the wedged-open door and running car exposed the siblings to the street (`Withheld`), speaking plainly required a two-step physical sacrifice in both baseline runs ([03](../04-runs/03-entrance-baseline-a.md) Turns 5–7; [04](../04-runs/04-entrance-baseline-b.md) Turns 5–7): pulling the wooden wedge and stepping out to switch off the idling engine.
- **Different choices under identical conditions:** Within *Mother's Flat* alone, [01-flat-a](../04-runs/01-flat-a.md) ended in a two-handed stalemate against the plaster mark while Ada held a two-minute intercom truce, whereas under the exact same conditions in [02-flat-b](../04-runs/02-flat-b.md), Cora blocked the interior door lock to ask without a joke (Turn 9) and Dan placed the painting into her hands and buzzed the street door open (Turn 10).

## 3. Single-Sentence Variants on *The Building Entrance*

Comparing [03](../04-runs/03-entrance-baseline-a.md)–[04](../04-runs/04-entrance-baseline-b.md) against each variant shows how a one-sentence change played out. The differences include what the sentence introduced and how the Director staged it.

1. **`Shown` ([variant-shown.md](../02-company/variant-shown.md) — [05](../04-runs/05-entrance-shown-a.md), [06](../04-runs/06-entrance-shown-b.md)):**
   Changing *cold street air* to *steady rain blowing onto the outer tiles* made unprotected outdoor transit visibly destructive to bare oil and split pine. That closed off walkout bluffs: when Ada tucked the canvas under her lapel in [06-entrance-shown-b](../04-runs/06-entrance-shown-b.md) (Turns 9–10) and challenged Ben (*“either let me out into the rain or unlock the boot for me”*), Ben latched the heavy door shut and pocketed his keys (*“that open frame isn't going out into the wet”*).
2. **`Holds` ([variant-holds.md](../02-company/variant-holds.md) — [07](../04-runs/07-entrance-holds-a.md), [08](../04-runs/08-entrance-holds-b.md)):**
   Adding *a folded mover's blanket with brown parcel tape* at the foot of the stairs created a contestable secondary object before anyone touched the fragile frame. Cora intercepted the blanket and tape ahead of Ada in [07-entrance-holds-a](../04-runs/07-entrance-holds-a.md) (Turn 2, giving Ben material to propose a four-strip tape draw on Turn 5), while Ada seized them in [08-entrance-holds-b](../04-runs/08-entrance-holds-b.md) (Turns 1, 5, 9) and Cora blocked the lower rail with her shoe so the frame could not be lowered onto Ada's spread blanket. Because `Withheld` was unchanged, holding wrapping material altered vestibule leverage without unlocking neutral loading at the kerb.
3. **`Offers` ([variant-offers.md](../02-company/variant-offers.md) — [09](../04-runs/09-entrance-offers-a.md), [10](../04-runs/10-entrance-offers-b.md)):**
   In all six runs where `Offers` only named steadying or shifting the painting along the doorway wall ([03](../04-runs/03-entrance-baseline-a.md)–[08](../04-runs/08-entrance-holds-b.md)), siblings kept hands, shoes, or coats on or beside the frame at the threshold. Replacing that single bullet with setting the painting flat on *a dry ledge behind the stairwell turn* with hands off was taken up in **both** runs: by Dan on Turn 7 of [09-entrance-offers-a](../04-runs/09-entrance-offers-a.md) (which freed Ada and Ben to step clear and open the door and boot toward Cora on Turns 9–10) and by Ada on Turn 10 of [10-entrance-offers-b](../04-runs/10-entrance-offers-b.md) (establishing hands-off neutral ground inside the shut vestibule). The new sentence introduced both the dry ledge and the option of setting the painting there. The runs show those changes together. To test the option alone, establish the ledge in both versions.
4. **`Withheld` ([variant-withheld.md](../02-company/variant-withheld.md) — [11](../04-runs/11-entrance-withheld-a.md), [12](../04-runs/12-entrance-withheld-b.md)):**
   Adding the approaching traffic warden put an immediate price on stalling the idling car without agreement. In **both** runs, blocked from loading the canvas into his boot and unwilling to drive off empty, Ben switched off his ignition in view of the warden and took a parking penalty notice under his windscreen wiper ([11](../04-runs/11-entrance-withheld-a.md) Turns 5–8; [12](../04-runs/12-entrance-withheld-b.md) Turn 6). The Directors staged the warden’s approach and chose when the ticket appeared after Ben shut off the engine. The siblings still chose differently once that cost was paid: in [11](../04-runs/11-entrance-withheld-a.md), Cora's offer to reimburse the ticket stung Ben into dropping his rotation rule and claiming the canvas for his own wall; in [12](../04-runs/12-entrance-withheld-b.md), Ada treated the ticket as taken *“for all four of us”* (Turn 9), leading Dan, Ada, and Ben to align on a hand-to-hand rotation starting with Dan's repair (Turns 8–10).

## 4. Director Choices and Authoring Seams Observed

- **Settling open ownership at curtain-up:** Every Entrance Director independently assigned the single idling car to Ben and the flat keys at the return slot to Ada before Turn 1 so four isolated performers would not invent conflicting owners; four Directors ([06](../04-runs/06-entrance-shown-b.md), [07](../04-runs/07-entrance-holds-a.md), [08](../04-runs/08-entrance-holds-b.md), [12](../04-runs/12-entrance-withheld-b.md)) noted that [place-building-entrance.md](../02-company/place-building-entrance.md) mentions *“a waiting driver”* in `Withheld` #1 alongside *“stall the idling car”* in the same bullet.
- **`Offers` vs. `Withheld` overlap on the door wedge:** In [place-building-entrance.md](../02-company/place-building-entrance.md), kicking the wooden wedge free appears under `Offers` (*what someone can do freely*) while pulling the wedge to speak in private appears under `Withheld` (*what costs the brisk kerbside goodbye*).
- **Psychological phrasing in `Withheld`:** Where `Withheld` named internal habits (*“give up logistical rules or sideways jokes”*), performers occasionally stated their own Shadows aloud (for example, Cora explaining why she jokes in [02-flat-b](../04-runs/02-flat-b.md) Turn 9 and [03-entrance-baseline-a](../04-runs/03-entrance-baseline-a.md) Turn 9, and Ben explaining why he made a rule in [02-flat-b](../04-runs/02-flat-b.md) Turn 6 and [06-entrance-shown-b](../04-runs/06-entrance-shown-b.md) Turn 7); where `Withheld` named observable spatial costs (the return slot, the shut door, the parking ticket in [11](../04-runs/11-entrance-withheld-a.md)–[12](../04-runs/12-entrance-withheld-b.md)), choices stayed physical. *(Minor transcript note: in [11-entrance-withheld-a](../04-runs/11-entrance-withheld-a.md) Turn 9, the Director noted dropping the word `it` from `putting it off` when assembling the log; Dan's verbatim `putting it off` is restored cleanly.)*
