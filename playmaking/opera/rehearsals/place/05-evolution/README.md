## 1. Keep the Four Operational Chapters (`Shown`, `Holds`, `Offers`, `Withheld`)

Varying one sentence at a time on [place-building-entrance.md](../02-company/place-building-entrance.md) showed each operational chapter doing distinct work onstage:
- **`Shown`** changed immediate sensory jeopardy without adding props (steady rain foreclosing Ada's lapel walkout in [06-entrance-shown-b](../04-runs/06-entrance-shown-b.md) Turn 10).
- **`Holds`** added a contestable prop that shifted vestibule leverage (Cora seizing the mover's blanket and parcel tape in [07-entrance-holds-a](../04-runs/07-entrance-holds-a.md) Turn 2; Ada spreading the blanket against Cora's shoe in [08-entrance-holds-b](../04-runs/08-entrance-holds-b.md) Turns 5–10).
- **`Offers`** opened a hands-off move that broke the doorway clinch in both runs (Dan in [09-entrance-offers-a](../04-runs/09-entrance-offers-a.md) Turn 7 and Ada in [10-entrance-offers-b](../04-runs/10-entrance-offers-b.md) Turn 10 laying the painting flat on the dry stairwell ledge).
- **`Withheld`** exacted a visible price when stalling met an approaching warden (Ben switching off his engine and taking a windscreen penalty ticket in both [11-entrance-withheld-a](../04-runs/11-entrance-withheld-a.md) Turns 5–8 and [12-entrance-withheld-b](../04-runs/12-entrance-withheld-b.md) Turn 6).

## 2. Changes to `references/place.md`

- **Offers:** Say what this Place makes available, and to whom. The doorway wedge makes privacy possible, while gaining it costs the siblings an open exit. The ledge variant introduced both a surface and an option. KAIWorlds also uses Offers for encounters, viewpoints, and role-specific opportunities. [The Allotment Path](https://github.com/ChBrain/KAIWorlds/blob/95be8a21398815f4890e5c5ee161a2373c371fa7/allotment/place_the_path.md) · [The Meeting Room](https://github.com/ChBrain/KAIWorlds/blob/95be8a21398815f4890e5c5ee161a2373c371fa7/misfits/place_the_meeting_room.md)
- **Withheld:** Name what the Place keeps out of reach and what someone must visibly do or give up to reach it. The shut door and the parking ticket gave the cast something visible to act on. [Withheld A](../04-runs/11-entrance-withheld-a.md) · [Withheld B](../04-runs/12-entrance-withheld-b.md)

## 3. Adjacent Seam (`khai-director/SKILL.md` & Open Spatial Control)

- *Evidence:* Four Entrance Directors ([06](../04-runs/06-entrance-shown-b.md), [07](../04-runs/07-entrance-holds-a.md), [08](../04-runs/08-entrance-holds-b.md), [12](../04-runs/12-entrance-withheld-b.md)) flagged that [place-building-entrance.md](../02-company/place-building-entrance.md) left open whether the idling car was controlled by a sibling or an offstage driver; all ten Entrance runs stayed coherent only because each Director fixed control of the car and keys at curtain-up before cueing isolated performers.
- *Alignment note:* Alongside `Persona` and `Piece`, include `Place` among the fixed Company elements read in [khai-director/SKILL.md](../../../skills/khai-director/SKILL.md), and let the Director settle open control of held items at curtain-up when staging isolated performers.
