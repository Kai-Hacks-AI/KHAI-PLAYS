# Rehearsal Setup · The Last Airing

A community radio station goes off air at midnight. Its host, producer, and sound engineer have promised the neighborhood a final message. They have a printed copy in the studio. A guest is bringing the recording. At 23:50 the guest calls to say she will arrive at 23:59. The full message takes two minutes.

## Create

Use the [Playwright skill](../../../skills/khai-playwright/SKILL.md) to author a short Play and Promptbook, three distinct Personas and their station Positions, the studio as a Place, the printed message and recording as Pieces, and the final broadcast as a Process. Give the cast reasons to care about whose voice goes out and what gets heard. Keep the call in the Play and the cast's shared operating conditions in the Company.

Use [TO DOIT](../../../skills/khai-playwright/references/plan.md) to author two Plans for the same broadcast. Give them the same Direction and observable Targets, including getting the full message on air before midnight. Give each Plan its own Orders and any conditions specific to its approach.

| Plan | Before the call |
| --- | --- |
| Read live | Prepare the host to read the printed message. |
| Hold for the recording | Keep the slot for the guest and assign who can call the switch to the printed message and when. |

## Rehearse

Use the [Director skill](../../../skills/khai-director/SKILL.md). Run each Plan twice in fresh contexts. Keep the Company, casting, opening, and what the cast can learn fixed. Give the cast only the active Plan. Play one preparation move, then deliver the same call at 23:50 in each run. Play through the broadcast or midnight cutoff. The cast decides what to do with the Plan under pressure.

## Review

Use SCORE to record the tested commit, Company, runs, observations, and any proposed template edit. Cite one preparation move and one response to the call per run. Look for a commitment the Plan made actionable, who took responsibility when conditions changed, and what the Director had to supply. Record target verdicts from what went on air and carry forward any target still open. Both Plans may lead to the same broadcast.
