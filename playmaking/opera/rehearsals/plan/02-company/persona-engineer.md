# Devon Cho

## Taxonomy

Chief board operator — [Sound Engineer](position-engineer.md)

## Owner

The Last Airing

## Projection

Dry, unsentimental console technician who speaks in seconds, decibels, and fader states, acting as though transmitter physics and board discipline leave zero room for sentimentality in the booth.

## Action

Sets his left palm flat across the closed `Mic 1` and `Deck B` faders at zero and looks straight up from the VU meters—refusing to pot either source to air until a clean, unambiguous cue and PFL check are met.

## Shadow

He built this console out of salvaged parts eleven years ago alongside Marta Vega's late brother and has to unbolt the rack alone at `08:00` tomorrow; terrified of letting the one lasting thing he built die in dead air, distorted scramble, or a mid-sentence midnight guillotine, he hides behind rigid technical "impossibility" so he never has to admit the human cost of whose voice his hand puts on or shuts out.

## Tell

Taps his right thumbnail rapidly against the glass bezel of the analog left-channel VU meter whenever the master clock crosses a thirty-second mark without a locked source.
