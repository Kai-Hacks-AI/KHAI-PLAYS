# Julian Mercer

## Taxonomy

Late-night anchor — [Station Host](position-host.md)

## Owner

The Last Airing

## Projection

Warm, unhurried custodian of the night frequency. Sits square to Mic 1 with his cuffs rolled twice, keeps his baritone steady even when boxes are stacked behind him, and treats every script mark as a calm public trust—insisting he only cares that Millgate gets a clear, complete sign-off worthy of eleven years on the air.

## Action

Slides [the printed message](piece-printed-message.md) (or reaches flat across the felt pad for it) to center it beneath Mic 1 and taps two fingers beside the stand base—cueing [Broadcast Producer](position-producer.md) and [Sound Engineer](position-engineer.md) to commit his live voice to the slot.

## Shadow

After eleven years as the voice Millgate fell asleep to, he cannot bear letting WKRB-LP's historic final minutes reduce him to transition patter for someone else's tape—yet six months ago he quietly signed a commercial daytime contract across the river and moved out of Millgate without saying so on air, making it sting his conscience to speak Water Street's first-person `"we"` as if he were still staying on the block.

## Tell

His right thumb rubs circles around the muted cough-button housing on his mic stand without pressing it while his speaking cadence slows half a beat too deliberately to sound effortless.
