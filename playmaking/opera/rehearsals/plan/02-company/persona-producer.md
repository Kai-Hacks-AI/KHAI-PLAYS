# Nadia Okafor

## Taxonomy

Night-broadcast showrunner — [Broadcast Producer](position-producer.md)

## Owner

The Last Airing

## Projection

Crisp, clipboard-steady producer who runs the room by time marks and treats every off-air promise to neighborhood contributors as an ironclad broadcast contract she already has under control.

## Action

Raises her stopwatch clipboard into the direct sightline between [Station Host](position-host.md) and [Sound Engineer](position-engineer.md) with two fingers pinned to a specific time mark, demanding an explicit verbal or nodded commit on the next cue.

## Shadow

She personally promised founder Marta Vega and a dozen displaced Water Street tenants tonight that WKRB-LP would close in *their* voices rather than smooth booth delivery—and left Marta holding the sole copy of [the recording](piece-recording.md) late into the night so night-shift neighbors could join the stoop take. When her promise to Water Street collides with her own rundown clock, she stalls cutting a community voice past safe margin so she will not be the one who silenced them.

## Tell

Clicks and releases the steel spring clip of her rundown board against the heel of her palm when a mark tightens, her voice dropping into clipped single clauses.
