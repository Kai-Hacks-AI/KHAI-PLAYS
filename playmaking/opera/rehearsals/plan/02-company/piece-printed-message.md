# The Printed Message

## Taxonomy

Sign-off broadcast script kit for [The Final Broadcast](process-final-broadcast.md)

## Owner

The Last Airing

## Place

In [Studio A](place-studio.md), clipped flat inside the broadcast folder at [Broadcast Producer](position-producer.md)'s side of the console desk.

## Load Bearing

Without it, the booth has no complete text of WKRB-LP's promised two-minute farewell—and Millgate loses the exact Friday mutual-aid address, tenant dispatch location, and closing roll of station builders if [the recording](piece-recording.md) does not air in full before `00:00:00`.

## Apparent

Two sheets of heavy cream paper in 14-point serif type headed `"STILL ON THE BLOCK — WKRB-LP 89.9 FM SIGN-OFF (2:00)"`, bracketed in red grease pencil into four `:30` blocks at intelligible broadcast pace:
- **Block 1 (`:00–:30`)**: Night-shift salutation to Millgate kitchens, cabs, and stoops tuning in for the last airing.
- **Block 2 (`:30–1:00`)**: Plain-spoken account of the building buyout and why `"we on Water Street"` refused a gag clause when surrendering the antenna.
- **Block 3 (`1:00–1:30`)**: Concrete neighborhood handoff—Fridays at `19:00` in St. Jude's basement on Baltic Street, plus the printed tenant notice board at Morales Hardware.
- **Block 4 (`1:30–2:00`)**: Roll of neighbors who raised the mast eleven years ago, ending with a blue-ink line in Marta Vega's block capitals: `"THE TOWER GOES DARK; THE BLOCK STAYS LIT."`

## Yearbook

- `18:40 tonight` — Drafted at 14 Water Street by station founder Marta Vega and Nadia Okafor so the station's last two minutes would leave a working neighborhood map rather than a eulogy.
- `21:15 tonight` — Printed in [Studio A](place-studio.md) just before the office printer was packed; Marta inked the closing line across the bottom margin before taking the portable recorder back to Water Street.
- `23:30 tonight` — Bracketed into four `:30` timing marks by Nadia (`2:00` total; rushing past `1:50` slurs the Baltic Street and Morales Hardware details) and placed in the console folder in [Studio A](place-studio.md).
