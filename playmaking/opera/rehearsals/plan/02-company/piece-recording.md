# The Recording

## Taxonomy

Sign-off field audio kit for [The Final Broadcast](process-final-broadcast.md)

## Owner

The Last Airing

## Place

In transit through Millgate with station founder Marta Vega toward [Studio A](place-studio.md), expected at the studio ahead of the closing slot (not yet in the building at `23:49:00`).

## Load Bearing

Without it, Marta Vega's actual founder's voice and the chorus of twelve displaced Water Street tenants recorded on their stoop never go out over 89.9 FM—any studio fallback replaces the living voices of the block with a single booth voice reading [the printed message](piece-printed-message.md).

## Apparent

A scuffed station USB flash drive on a brass keyring labeled `"WKRB FIELD 2"` in white gaffer tape, containing one uncompressed two-minute stereo WAV file (`MILLGATE_FINAL.wav`, `2:00` exact across the same four `:30` sections as [the printed message](piece-printed-message.md)) that requires physical insertion into `Deck B` and at least `15 seconds` of directory scan and PFL level verification before air.

## Yearbook

- `Seven winters ago` — Brass-tagged by Devon Cho after surviving a harbor sleet storm during the boil-water emergency broadcasts, becoming WKRB-LP's trusted street-reporting stick.
- `21:20 tonight` — Checked out from [Studio A](place-studio.md) with the handheld field recorder to Marta Vega so Water Street could record the sign-off in its own voices.
- `22:45 tonight` — Captured in one unbroken `2:00` take under the rain awning at 14 Water Street—Marta's raspy voice delivering Blocks 1–4 over a distant river tug horn and twelve tenants speaking `"The tower goes dark; the block stays lit"` in unison—then transferred onto the USB stick for Marta's walk to [Studio A](place-studio.md).
