# Studio A

## Taxonomy

Second-floor storefront station of WKRB-LP 89.9 FM, Millgate

## Owner

The Last Airing

## Shown

A cramped single-room combo broadcast booth above the shuttered Millgate Bakery, smelling of warm rack transformers and wet wool. Rain streaks the window over empty streetcar tracks; taped cardboard moving boxes line the back wall; a red `ON AIR` lamp glows over the wired-glass stairwell door; a digital red LED master clock wired to the transmitter rack counts seconds toward `00:00:00` while analog VU needles sway softly to `Deck A`'s closing instrumental bed.

## Holds

A four-channel broadcast console (`Mic 1` host boom, `Mic 2` table guest mic, `Deck A` cart player running the closing instrumental loop until `23:57:30`, `Deck B` USB media deck with PFL cue bus requiring 15 seconds to scan and level-check an inserted drive); a copper screening landline handset with a silent amber flasher (no broadcast phone-patch hybrid—handset only); [the printed message](piece-printed-message.md) inside the desk broadcast folder; a stopwatch clipboard and red grease pencils; an open `"WATER STREET / ST. JUDE'S ARCHIVE"` manila envelope resting atop the moving boxes by the door; and the sealed master-clock transmitter relay set to cut RF carrier power at `00:00:00` sharp.

## Offers

Gives [Sound Engineer](position-engineer.md) direct hands on all four channel faders, PFL buttons, and `Deck B`; gives [Station Host](position-host.md) `Mic 1`, a felt-topped script desk, and an unobstructed view of the master clock; gives [Broadcast Producer](position-producer.md) the screening handset, rundown clipboard, custody of [the printed message](piece-printed-message.md), and two steps' reach to the stairwell door; and gives any open console channel immediate broadcast reach across Millgate on 89.9 FM until `00:00:00`.

## Withheld

- **Anything past `00:00:00`**: The transmitter's license-cutoff relay is slaved to the master clock and drops carrier to flat static at `00:00:00`—no fader or switch in the booth can extend air time by a single second.
- **Remote audio transfer or phone-to-air patching**: Studio broadband was disconnected yesterday for move-out and the phone hybrid burned out last winter; external audio such as [the recording](piece-recording.md) can only enter physically through the stairwell door and clear 15 seconds of `Deck B` scan and PFL check before air.
- **Off-air privacy during a live mic**: Because console and microphones share one un-partitioned room, opening `Mic 1` or `Mic 2` puts any spoken argument, chair scrape, or door latch onto 89.9 FM unless the crew communicates by silent hand cues or pulls the fader down first.
