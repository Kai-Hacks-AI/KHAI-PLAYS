# Plan — Final Broadcast

## Taxonomy

Sign-off broadcast execution plan

## Owner

[The Final Broadcast](process-final-broadcast.md) in [The Last Airing](play.md)

## Direction

Put WKRB-LP 89.9 FM's promised two-minute farewell message on the air in full before the `00:00:00` transmitter cutoff shuts the station down permanently, so Millgate hears the complete sign-off—including the Water Street mutual-aid handoff and closing names—without dead air or a severed ending.

## Orders

- **[Sound Engineer](position-engineer.md)**: By the ten-minute mark (`23:50:00`), arm `Deck B` (USB input) on PFL standby as the reserved primary channel for [the recording](piece-recording.md) in [The Final Broadcast](process-final-broadcast.md), keeping `Mic 1` closed while the sign-off slot is held for Marta Vega.
- **[Broadcast Producer](position-producer.md)**: By the ten-minute mark (`23:50:00`), reserve the `23:57:30` sign-off slot on the rundown for [the recording](piece-recording.md) and retain [the printed message](piece-printed-message.md) in the producer's folder as standby copy; [Broadcast Producer](position-producer.md) alone holds authority to call a switch from [the recording](piece-recording.md) to [the printed message](piece-printed-message.md), and must call that switch no later than **`23:57:30`** (leaving `0:30` for `Mic 1` handoff and PFL check before the **`23:58:00`** `2:00`-runtime floor).
- **[Station Host](position-host.md)**: By the ten-minute mark (`23:50:00`), remain on standby at `Mic 1` without taking or marking [the printed message](piece-printed-message.md), ready to introduce [the recording](piece-recording.md) briefly or—only when [Broadcast Producer](position-producer.md) calls the switch—take [the printed message](piece-printed-message.md) for PFL check and a full `2:00` live read.
- **All Booth Positions**: If [the recording](piece-recording.md) enters [Studio A](place-studio.md) before the switch mark, [Broadcast Producer](position-producer.md) hands it straight to [Sound Engineer](position-engineer.md) for `15-second` `Deck B` PFL check and air; if [Broadcast Producer](position-producer.md) calls the switch, move immediately to `Mic 1` PFL check so all four `:30` blocks begin airing no later than `23:58:00`.

## Implementation

- [The recording](piece-recording.md) carries `2:00` of Water Street's actual voices (`MILLGATE_FINAL.wav`) and requires physical delivery through [Studio A](place-studio.md)'s stairwell door plus at least `15 seconds` of `Deck B` scan and PFL check before fader-up.
- [Studio A](place-studio.md)'s master-clock relay cuts RF carrier power automatically at `00:00:00`; any `2:00` airing (`Deck B` or `Mic 1`) that starts after `23:58:00` will either slur Block 3's handoff details or be severed mid-sentence at midnight.
- Neither [Station Host](position-host.md) nor [Sound Engineer](position-engineer.md) may unilaterally cancel the held `Deck B` slot before `23:57:30` without [Broadcast Producer](position-producer.md)'s switch call; if switched, `Mic 1` still requires a `5–10 second` PFL check before going live.
- Splitting the `2:00` farewell mid-air between `Mic 1` and an un-cued `Deck B` risks dead air, room bleed, and dropped blocks inside [Studio A](place-studio.md)'s un-partitioned room.

## Targets

- [ ] The console channel used to deliver the final message (`Mic 1` or `Deck B`) passes a pre-fader listen (`PFL`) level check before its fader goes live to 89.9 FM.
- [ ] All four `:30` blocks of the two-minute final message—salutation, buyout account, St. Jude's and Morales Hardware handoff, and closing roll—air intelligibly on 89.9 FM before `00:00:00`.
- [ ] Marta Vega's authorship and the Water Street neighbors' role in the farewell are heard explicitly on 89.9 FM—either in Marta's recorded voice or by spoken on-air attribution—before `00:00:00`.
- [ ] 89.9 FM reaches the `00:00:00` carrier cutoff with clean broadcast continuity: no dead air of `3 seconds` or more, no hot-mic booth dispute on air, and no voice or recording cut off mid-sentence by the relay.
- [ ] [The printed message](piece-printed-message.md) and, once in [Studio A](place-studio.md), [the recording](piece-recording.md) are placed together in the `"WATER STREET / ST. JUDE'S ARCHIVE"` envelope for Friday's mutual-aid handoff.
