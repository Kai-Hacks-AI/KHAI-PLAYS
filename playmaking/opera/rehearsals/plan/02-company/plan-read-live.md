# Plan — Final Broadcast

## Taxonomy

Sign-off broadcast execution plan

## Owner

[The Final Broadcast](process-final-broadcast.md) in [The Last Airing](play.md)

## Direction

Put WKRB-LP 89.9 FM's promised two-minute farewell message on the air in full before the `00:00:00` transmitter cutoff shuts the station down permanently, so Millgate hears the complete sign-off—including the Water Street mutual-aid handoff and closing names—without dead air or a severed ending.

## Orders

- **[Broadcast Producer](position-producer.md)**: By the ten-minute mark (`23:50:00`), place [the printed message](piece-printed-message.md) on [Station Host](position-host.md)'s desk stand, confirm the four `:30` pacing marks and explicit spoken attribution to Marta Vega and Water Street, and lock `23:57:30` on the rundown for the live studio read.
- **[Station Host](position-host.md)**: By the ten-minute mark (`23:50:00`), take [the printed message](piece-printed-message.md) at `Mic 1`, deliver a brief voice check for PFL calibration, and stand ready to read all four `:30` blocks live at broadcast pace starting at `23:57:30` (and no later than `23:58:00`).
- **[Sound Engineer](position-engineer.md)**: By the ten-minute mark (`23:50:00`), calibrate `Mic 1` on PFL for [Station Host](position-host.md)'s voice and arm `Mic 1` as primary source for [The Final Broadcast](process-final-broadcast.md); at `23:57:30` (hard start ceiling `23:58:00`), fade `Deck A` down and pot `Mic 1` to air.
- **All Booth Positions**: Keep `Mic 1`'s `2:00` live read uninterrupted through Block 4 once committed; do not swap to `Deck B` unless [the recording](piece-recording.md) is physically in [Studio A](place-studio.md) and verified end-to-end on `Deck B` PFL by `23:55:00`.

## Implementation

- [The printed message](piece-printed-message.md) is already in [Studio A](place-studio.md) and requires a full `2:00` (`23:58:00` latest start) at steady broadcast pace so listeners can copy the Baltic Street and Morales Hardware handoff details in Block 3.
- [Studio A](place-studio.md)'s master-clock relay cuts RF carrier power automatically at `00:00:00`; preparing `Mic 1` now eliminates courier and media-scan risk in the station's final minutes.
- External USB media on `Deck B` requires at least `15 seconds` of directory scan and PFL verification after entering [Studio A](place-studio.md) and may not displace the prepared `Mic 1` read once the `23:55:00` verification mark passes.
- While `Mic 1` is hot in [Studio A](place-studio.md)'s single room, all off-air coordination and door arrivals must use silent hand cues so booth noise never bleeds onto 89.9 FM.

## Targets

- [ ] The console channel used to deliver the final message (`Mic 1` or `Deck B`) passes a pre-fader listen (`PFL`) level check before its fader goes live to 89.9 FM.
- [ ] All four `:30` blocks of the two-minute final message—salutation, buyout account, St. Jude's and Morales Hardware handoff, and closing roll—air intelligibly on 89.9 FM before `00:00:00`.
- [ ] Marta Vega's authorship and the Water Street neighbors' role in the farewell are heard explicitly on 89.9 FM—either in Marta's recorded voice or by spoken on-air attribution—before `00:00:00`.
- [ ] 89.9 FM reaches the `00:00:00` carrier cutoff with clean broadcast continuity: no dead air of `3 seconds` or more, no hot-mic booth dispute on air, and no voice or recording cut off mid-sentence by the relay.
- [ ] [The printed message](piece-printed-message.md) and, once in [Studio A](place-studio.md), [the recording](piece-recording.md) are placed together in the `"WATER STREET / ST. JUDE'S ARCHIVE"` envelope for Friday's mutual-aid handoff.
