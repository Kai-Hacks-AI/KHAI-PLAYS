## Fixed at the Opening (`23:49:00`)

It is **`23:49:00`** on the last night of community radio station **WKRB-LP 89.9 FM**. Eleven minutes remain before the sealed master-clock relay cuts transmitter carrier power permanently at **`00:00:00`**.

- [Julian Mercer](persona-host.md), [Nadia Okafor](persona-producer.md), and [Devon Cho](persona-engineer.md) are inside [Studio A](place-studio.md) under the lit red `ON AIR` lamp.
- `Deck A` is currently airing the closing instrumental bed, which runs down to its end-of-bed tone at **`23:57:30`**. `Mic 1`, `Mic 2`, and `Deck B` are closed at zero.
- The crew has promised Millgate a two-minute (`2:00`) sign-off message in the closing window before midnight. [The printed message](piece-printed-message.md) sits inside [Broadcast Producer](position-producer.md)'s folder on the console desk.
- Station founder **Marta Vega** recorded that same `2:00` text on Water Street with twelve displaced neighbors onto [the recording](piece-recording.md) and is expected at [Studio A](place-studio.md) ahead of the closing slot. **At `23:49:00`, no one in the booth knows of any delay.**
- The crew begins under [the active Plan](plan.md).

## What the Performers Decide

Once play begins, the three booth Personas decide in real time:
- How they carry out, enforce, strain against, renegotiate, or depart from [the active Plan](plan.md) through the powers and limits of their Positions;
- Who holds [the printed message](piece-printed-message.md), when `Mic 1` or `Deck B` is checked on PFL, whether and when a switch is called, and when [The Final Broadcast](process-final-broadcast.md) goes live on 89.9 FM;
- Whose voice goes out over 89.9 FM, whether all four `:30` blocks clear air at intelligible pace before `00:00:00`, how Marta Vega and Water Street are attributed (or omitted), and how the booth handles Marta's arrival at the door while the clock runs out.

## Action & World Cues

### 1. Opening Preparation (`23:49:00`–`23:50:00`)

Open at **`23:49:00`** with `Deck A`'s instrumental bed playing softly on the monitors and the cast unaware of any delay. Play **one preparation move** across the booth under [the active Plan](plan.md)'s pre-`23:50:00` `Orders` (one concise beat in which each of the three Personas acts or responds once to set the booth as [the active Plan](plan.md) directs). Immediately upon completing that preparation move, advance the master clock to **`23:50:00`**.

### 2. The `23:50` Call (`23:50:00`)

At **`23:50:00`**, the silent amber lamp on [Studio A](place-studio.md)'s copper screening handset flashes. When answered, deliver **this exact verbatim call** from **Marta Vega** over street rain before the payphone disconnects:

> *"Nadia—it's Marta, I'm at the Canal payphone, my coin's about to drop. The crosstown bus died in the rain with all of us on it—I'm walking as fast as my knees will carry me with the USB stick in my coat. We got all twelve Water Street voices on it, Nadia, two minutes clean, just like you promised them. I cannot get up your stairs a second before 23:59—23:59 sharp I'm through that door. Don't erase us on the last night—"* *(the payphone clicks dead)*

If no one answers within ten seconds, the lamp stops flashing at `23:50:10`, and at `23:50:20` it flashes once more to deliver the exact same verbatim call before clicking dead.

### 3. The Reckoning & Sign-Off Window (`23:50:15`–`23:58:59`)

Play the booth's response and console preparation toward midnight:
- **`23:57:30` (`Deck A` End-of-Bed Cue)**: `Deck A`'s closing instrumental loop reaches its end warning tone and stops. Unless [Sound Engineer](position-engineer.md) has already faded `Deck A` down for an active channel—or immediately brings up `Mic 1`, `Mic 2`, `Deck B`, or re-loops `Deck A`—audible dead air begins on 89.9 FM after `3 seconds`.
- **`23:58:00` (`2:00` Runtime Ceiling)**: Exactly `2:00` remain before the `00:00:00` relay cutoff—the final second at which a complete `2:00` message (`:30` per block across Blocks 1–4) can start and still finish intelligibly before carrier drop.

### 4. The `23:59` Arrival (`23:59:00`)

If [Studio A](place-studio.md) is still running at **`23:59:00`**, the stairwell wired-glass door latches open and **Marta Vega** steps inside, rain-soaked and breathless, holding out [the recording](piece-recording.md) (`"WKRB FIELD 2"` USB stick) toward the console while looking straight from the lit red `ON AIR` lamp to [Nadia Okafor](persona-producer.md), [Julian Mercer](persona-host.md), and [Devon Cho](persona-engineer.md).
- If `Mic 1` or `Mic 2` is open when the door opens or if anyone speaks aloud in the room, that sound airs live on 89.9 FM.
- Loading [the recording](piece-recording.md) into `Deck B` and checking PFL takes at least `15 seconds` from handoff (`23:59:15` earliest clean `Deck B` fader-up). Marta follows silent or spoken cues from the booth honestly (`Mic 2` live greeting, USB handoff, or silent witness) until `00:00:00`.

### 5. Midnight Cutoff & Closing Booth Beat (`00:00:00`–`00:00:30`)

At **`00:00:00` sharp**, the transmitter rack's master-clock relay clunks open. The red `ON AIR` lamp over the door snaps dark, and the off-air monitor speaker drops into flat static—severing any word or recording still in progress mid-syllable on 89.9 FM.

Play **one brief closing beat inside [Studio A](place-studio.md)** (`00:00:00`–`00:00:30`) in the sudden static so the crew (and Marta, if present) register what actually went out over Millgate and what stayed trapped in the booth. Then review [the active Plan](plan.md)'s `Targets`: record `[x]`, `[F]`, `[W]`, or `[-]` for every target settled by what went on air or happened in the booth, and leave `[ ]` open to carry forward for any target still unresolved at curtain (closing the Plan only if every target has a verdict).
