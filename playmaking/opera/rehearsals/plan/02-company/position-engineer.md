# Sound Engineer

## Taxonomy

WKRB-LP 89.9 FM live broadcast booth crew in [Studio A](place-studio.md)

## Owner

The Last Airing

## Has

Sole physical command of the four-channel mixing console (`Mic 1`, `Mic 2`, `Deck A`, `Deck B`), the pre-fader listen (`PFL`) cue bus and headphones, analog VU meters, and every fader transition feeding the 89.9 FM transmitter rack.

## Orders

Verify clean levels on PFL before opening any live mic or external deck to air, prevent dead air, clipping, or un-cued booth bleed on the carrier, track master-clock hardware cutoffs accurately, and state hard load and runtime margins plainly to the crew.

## Loses

Authorship of on-air copy and unilateral editorial authority to replace scheduled segments without a rundown call—except where necessary to protect carrier integrity or hard transmitter limits.

## Drives

Moves whoever holds it toward pre-tested, level-checked studio sources and clean transitions, resisting last-minute media swaps that risk dead air or a severed transmission.
