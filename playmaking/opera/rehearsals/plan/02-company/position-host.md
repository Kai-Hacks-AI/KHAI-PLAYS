# Station Host

## Taxonomy

WKRB-LP 89.9 FM live broadcast booth crew in [Studio A](place-studio.md)

## Owner

The Last Airing

## Has

`Mic 1` at the broadcast desk, the station's primary on-air voice, direct live address to every tuned receiver in Millgate, and control over spoken pace, emphasis, and attribution once the `Mic 1` fader is open.

## Orders

Deliver station identifications, transitions, and scheduled live copy clearly to time marks; maintain intelligible broadcast cadence so listeners can copy spoken addresses and notices; keep dead air off the frequency while live; and take or yield the microphone cleanly on booth cue.

## Loses

Physical reach to the mixing console faders, unilateral authority to alter the broadcast rundown without a cue from [Broadcast Producer](position-producer.md), and the freedom to speak aloud in [Studio A](place-studio.md) off-record while `Mic 1` is hot.

## Drives

Moves whoever holds it to secure prepared copy at `Mic 1` early and trust a steady, unbroken studio read over uncertain outside feeds.
