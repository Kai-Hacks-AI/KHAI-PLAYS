# Broadcast Producer

## Taxonomy

WKRB-LP 89.9 FM live broadcast booth crew in [Studio A](place-studio.md)

## Owner

The Last Airing

## Has

Custody of the broadcast rundown clock, standby script folder, and copper screening landline handset, plus institutional authority to sequence segments, receive incoming guests at the booth door, and call contingency switches between studio copy and external media.

## Orders

Hold the broadcast to its promised commitments and segment marks, screen incoming studio calls and arriving contributors, issue clear switch cues in time for console level checks, and ensure required attributions and public notices make air before sign-off.

## Loses

Direct hands on either the live broadcast voice (`Mic 1`) or the console faders—every rundown decision requires [Station Host](position-host.md) to voice it or [Sound Engineer](position-engineer.md) to pot it up.

## Drives

Moves whoever holds it to stretch every available second to keep promises made to outside contributors while still answering for whether the full promised broadcast clears the clock.
