# The Final Broadcast

## Taxonomy

WKRB-LP 89.9 FM license sign-off and midnight transmitter shutdown

## Owner

The Last Airing

## Initiated by

Starts when [Sound Engineer](position-engineer.md) fades down `Deck A`'s closing instrumental loop in [Studio A](place-studio.md) and pots up `Mic 1`, `Mic 2`, or `Deck B` to put the sign-off segment onto 89.9 FM—or automatically at `23:57:30` when `Deck A`'s closing loop reaches its end-of-bed tone, opening the final `2:30` sign-off window before midnight. Whoever runs the console can tell it has been met when `Deck A` drops below program level or hits `23:57:30` while the red `ON AIR` lamp stays lit and the master clock counts down toward `00:00:00`.

## Direction

Advances in real clock seconds toward the irreversible `00:00:00` transmitter relay cutoff, sending whatever signal is on an open channel fader—all four `:30` blocks of [the printed message](piece-printed-message.md), [the recording](piece-recording.md) on `Deck B`, live spoken attribution, rushed speech, room bleed, or dead carrier hum—out across Millgate until RF power drops at midnight.

## Lever

- **Drives it**: An open channel fader (`Mic 1`, `Mic 2`, or `Deck B`) preceded by a PFL level check (`5–10 seconds` for a live mic check; `15 seconds` minimum from door handoff to scan and PFL-check `Deck B` USB audio) and begun by **`23:58:00`** so all four `:30` blocks (`2:00` total) clear at intelligible broadcast pace before `00:00:00`.
- **Slows or degrades it**: Holding the slot past `23:57:30` with no open program source (`3+ seconds` produces audible dead air); late physical arrival and `15-second` USB cueing on `Deck B`; mid-message fader switches between `Mic 1` and `Deck B`; rushing the `2:00` script in under `1:50` (which slurs the Baltic Street and Morales Hardware details in Blocks 3–4); or speaking aloud in [Studio A](place-studio.md) while a microphone channel is hot.
- **Stops it**: Pulling all console faders to zero stops program audio while leaving empty carrier on air; at **`00:00:00` sharp**, the master-clock relay cuts transmitter carrier power permanently—nothing can pause, slow, or extend the `00:00:00` cutoff.

## Echo

At `00:00:00`, the red `ON AIR` lamp clicks dark and 89.9 FM turns permanently to static across Millgate. Whatever cleared the open faders intelligibly before `00:00:00`—the complete four-block farewell or a severed fragment, booth voice or Water Street's recorded voices (or a live sequence of both), honest attribution or uncredited first-person claim, clean sign-off or on-air confusion—stands irreversibly as WKRB-LP's final airing, leaving everyone inside [Studio A](place-studio.md) facing what made it out and what was cut off in the room.
