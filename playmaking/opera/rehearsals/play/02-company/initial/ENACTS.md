## Name

*Low Water at Lock Four*

## Arc

*Low Water at Lock Four* unfolds across two tightly linked evening Plots at Lock Four on an autumn canal under municipal drought restriction:

1. [Part 1: Dusk on the Lock Wall](plot-1-dusk-on-the-lock-wall.md) sets the operational decision on the masonry coping at dusk while the upper gates remain shut and the upper pound sits barely half an inch above the red municipal intake mark. It forces an immediate commitment among three live paths—locking the heavy barge through on a full draw, holding the barge above the lock overnight, or attempting a shallow/partial draw—before Lock Four closes for the night.
2. [Part 2: The Dusk Gauge Call](plot-2-the-dusk-gauge-call.md) picks up twenty minutes later inside the lock house after gravity, hull draft, and gate iron have turned Part 1's commitment into physical fact on the chamber and gauge. When the night water-bailiff rings for the dusk reading, lockkeeper and skipper must confront what goes over the wire and whose signature backs the closing line in the ledger.

Their order turns an operational gamble on the water into an evidentiary reckoning in ink: Part 1 decides what happens to the pound and the cargo; Part 2 decides how that outcome is spoken, owned, or contested on the official record.

## Company

- **Personas:**
  - [Lena](persona-lena.md) — lockkeeper on duty at Lock Four, carrying the brass sluice windlass key, a hand lantern, and responsibility for keeping the upper pound at or above the municipal intake mark until midnight.
  - [Bram](persona-bram.md) — independent barge skipper working the last scheduled evening passage with perishable autumn pears, carrying his bow check-line, cargo bills, and grower credit riding on the morning wholesale market.
- **Places:**
  - [Lock Four Wall](place-lock-wall.md) — the dusk coping, shut upper mitre gates, sluice gear, staff gauge, and upper lay-by where Part 1 plays out.
  - [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md) — the cramped brick watch room beside the chamber where Part 2 plays out around the district telephone and desk ledger.
- **Pieces:**
  - [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md) — the enameled staff gauge in the upper wing wall marked at forty-two inches for the town's upstream water intake.
  - [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) — Bram's sixty-foot iron motor-barge laden deep with unrefrigerated orchard crates.
  - [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md) — the district wire on which the night water-bailiff calls for the dusk pound reading.
  - [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md) — the bound passage-and-gauge book requiring both lockkeeper's and skipper's signatures at dusk close.
- **Processes:**
  - [Drawing the Chamber](process-chamber-draw.md) — letting water from the thin upper pound into Lock Four's chamber on a full or partial draw (or leaving it uninitiated if the gates stay shut).
  - [The Dusk Gauge Check](process-dusk-gauge-call.md) — the bailiff's telephone audit twenty minutes after dusk close and the co-signing of the closing ledger line.
- **Plans:**
  - [Hold the Municipal Intake Mark](plan-hold-the-intake-mark.md) — [Lena](persona-lena.md)'s effort to keep the pound at or above the red mark until midnight and close the book cleanly.
  - [Make the Morning Market](plan-make-the-morning-market.md) — [Bram](persona-bram.md)'s effort to clear Lock Four on the final scheduled slot before his fruit spoils and without absorbing fault on paper.
- **Positions:** None separate from [Lena](persona-lena.md) and [Bram](persona-bram.md), who hold their working posts throughout both Plots.
- **Pitch:** [Working-Waterway Realism](pitch-working-waterway-realism.md) is the default Pitch across the entire Play and both Plots unless the Director selects another Pitch when staging a run.

## Triggers

- **Opening [Part 1: Dusk on the Lock Wall](plot-1-dusk-on-the-lock-wall.md):** Fixed at curtain—autumn dusk at [Lock Four Wall](place-lock-wall.md); upper gates shut; upper pound resting half an inch above the red stripe on [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md); [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) tied nose-in at the upper lay-by on the last scheduled passage before night closure. Both performers' choices remain completely open.
- **Ending Part 1 and Handoff:** Part 1 runs roughly 4–5 cast turns and ends the moment [Lena](persona-lena.md) and [Bram](persona-bram.md) commit to locking the barge through on a full draw, holding the barge above the lock overnight, or trying a shallow/partial draw via [Drawing the Chamber](process-chamber-draw.md). Twenty minutes pass between scenes while the physical consequence of that commitment settles on the pound, sill, chamber, and barge.
- **Opening [Part 2: The Dusk Gauge Call](plot-2-the-dusk-gauge-call.md):** Both [Lena](persona-lena.md) and [Bram](persona-bram.md) are inside [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md) with [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md) open on the desk when [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md) rings to start [The Dusk Gauge Check](process-dusk-gauge-call.md).
- **Ending Part 2:** Part 2 runs roughly 4–5 cast turns and ends once the dusk reading has been given on [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md) and both [Lena](persona-lena.md) and [Bram](persona-bram.md) have settled what they write, sign, qualify, or refuse to sign in [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md).

## Stakes

The Play fights over who absorbs the cost of a half-inch drought margin when public water rules collide with working-boat livelihoods.

- In [Part 1: Dusk on the Lock Wall](plot-1-dusk-on-the-lock-wall.md), every commitment carries a real price on [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md) and [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md): a full draw clears the barge toward market while pulling the pound down to or below the town's red intake mark before midnight; holding shut protects the mark while stranding perishable crates overnight; a shallow/partial draw squeezes water and sill clearance alike.
- In [Part 2: The Dusk Gauge Call](plot-2-the-dusk-gauge-call.md), the stakes move onto [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md) and [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md): whether [Lena](persona-lena.md) and [Bram](persona-bram.md) back the same story in speech and ink, shade a borderline figure, file a written protest, or leave an unsigned line that puts lock cottage, skipper's ticket, or grower debt on the block.
