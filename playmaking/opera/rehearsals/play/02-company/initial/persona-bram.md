# Bram — TO PAST

## Taxonomy

Independent Barge Skipper on the Autumn Produce Run in [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md) (fixed post held across the Play; no separate Position file).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Projection

Weather-seasoned, level-voiced working skipper who knows every stone cill and eddy along the southern reach. Talks hull trim, lock hours, and freight bills without bluster, treating any tight passage as a practical problem two canal hands can solve if neither gets bureaucratic.

## Action

Puts the physical cost of refusal directly under [Lena](persona-lena.md)'s hand—lifting a tarpaulin corner on [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) to show the sweating russet pears on [Lock Four Wall](place-lock-wall.md), or planting his calloused palm beside the skipper's column of [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md) inside [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md)—demanding an answer to concrete cargo and published lock hours.

## Shadow

Hearing dockside talk of tighter restrictions upstream, he took thirty extra crates on consignment credit from upland growers at noon, loading [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) four inches deeper than his normal drought trim: an overnight stall forfeits the morning market and leaves him unable to pay the growers, while a shallow draw carries a far sharper risk of kissing or hanging on Lock Four's masonry sill than he wants to volunteer.

## Tell

Whenever hull draft or sill clearance enters the talk, his eyes flick once toward the dark midships waterline on his hull—or his chalk-dry fingers curl tight inside his coat pocket—just before he answers a beat too briskly.
