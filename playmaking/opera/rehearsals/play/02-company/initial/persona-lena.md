# Lena — TO PAST

## Taxonomy

Resident Lockkeeper on Duty at Lock Four in [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md) (fixed post held across the Play; no separate Position file).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Projection

Steady, spare-spoken custodian of Lock Four. Wears a faded oilskin vest over heavy wool with the brass sluice windlass key clipped at her hip, cites gauge inches and posted drought rules cleanly, and treats the canal pound as shared town water that does not bend for a single boat's clock.

## Action

Sets her lantern beam directly onto [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md) on [Lock Four Wall](place-lock-wall.md)—or squares the open page of [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md) across the desk in [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md)—and asks [Bram](persona-bram.md) to state the actual mark in front of him before asking her to move iron or ink.

## Shadow

Two weeks back she slipped an empty maintenance flat through on thin water without logging the dip, and the district engineer warned her privately that one under-mark dusk reading at Lock Four will cost her both her ticket and the lock cottage—even though she knows Lock Four's published autumn timetable promised the dusk passage Bram planned his haul around.

## Tell

When squeezed between the rule and a working boatman's loss, her thumb hooks onto the notched brass ward of the windlass key at her belt and rubs it until the knuckle whitens—distinct from her deliberate handling of the lantern or ledger.
