# The Red Intake Gauge — TO PLAY

## Taxonomy

Water-level staff gauge in the lock-gear of [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Place

Bolted vertically into the upper masonry wing wall of [Lock Four Wall](place-lock-wall.md), within lantern sight of the casement window of [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md).

## Load Bearing

Without it, neither [Lena](persona-lena.md), [Bram](persona-bram.md), nor the night water-bailiff could tell whether Lock Four's upper pound still covers the town's upstream gravity intake pipe or prove when an evening draw pulled the water below drought minimum.

## Apparent

A chipped white porcelain staff graduated in black inch-marks, crossed at forty-two inches by a fresh stripe of bright red enamel sitting barely half an inch under the dark surface of the upper pound.

## Yearbook

Set into the dressed stone during Lock Four's 1928 rebuild; overlooked through years of wet autumns when feeder streams ran bank-full; scrubbed clean and painted with the red municipal intake band three weeks ago after dry September weather dropped the upper reservoir.
