# Bram's Orchard Barge — TO PLAY

## Taxonomy

Working canal freight hull in [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Place

Snubbed nose-in at the upper lay-by of [Lock Four Wall](place-lock-wall.md) when [Part 1: Dusk on the Lock Wall](plot-1-dusk-on-the-lock-wall.md) opens; twenty minutes later during [Part 2: The Dusk Gauge Call](plot-2-the-dusk-gauge-call.md), resting wherever Part 1's commitment left it—down in the lower reach, held above the shut upper gates, or settled inside/across the chamber after a partial draw.

## Load Bearing

Without this deep-laden hull and its unrefrigerated fruit arriving on the final scheduled slot, Lock Four shuts down at dusk with its half-inch pound margin untouched and no collision between town water and river freight.

## Apparent

A sixty-foot riveted iron motor-barge riding low to her rubbing strake beneath tarped stacks of slatted orchard crates that breathe out the warm, sweet scent of ripe autumn pears into the cold dusk air.

## Yearbook

Riveted in 1954 for aggregate haulage before [Bram](persona-bram.md) planked her hold for valley produce; worked twelve punctual October harvests down the southern flight; loaded four inches deeper than her usual drought trim at noon today when upland growers talked Bram into taking thirty extra crates on consignment credit.
