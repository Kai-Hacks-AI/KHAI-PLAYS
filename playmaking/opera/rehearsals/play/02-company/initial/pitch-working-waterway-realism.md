# Working-Waterway Realism — TO TUNE

## Taxonomy

Default production register for [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md), governing both [Part 1: Dusk on the Lock Wall](plot-1-dusk-on-the-lock-wall.md) and [Part 2: The Dusk Gauge Call](plot-2-the-dusk-gauge-call.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Tenor

Taut, spare working-waterway realism: practical exchanges clipped to water inches, paddle iron, hull trim, fruit spoilage, and ledger ink between two working people who know the machinery and do not waste breath on speeches.

## Undertow

Quiet class-and-craft kinship between a resident lockkeeper and an independent skipper who have worked the same canal for years and both feel municipal rules tightening from shore offices miles away—pulling against their immediate need to protect their own livelihood.

## Nerve

The dry-mouth tension of watching a half-inch margin run out at dusk, where every reasonable-sounding compromise pushes real physical or legal loss onto one of the two people standing there.

## Echo

The weight of wet iron settling shut and dry ink sitting in [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md)—knowing both people still have to face each other on the next trip up the flight.
