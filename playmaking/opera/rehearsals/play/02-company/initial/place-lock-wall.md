# Lock Four Wall — TO SHOW

## Taxonomy

Chamber coping and upper lay-by of Lock Four on the autumn canal, three stone steps outside [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md) in [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Shown

Cold October dusk dropping fast over tarred oak mitre gates shut tight against the upper pound; flat black water licking half an inch above a wet red enamel band on white porcelain; the sweet, heavy smell of ripe pears rising off a deep-laden iron hull snubbed at the upper lay-by; dry, bleached weed lines crusted along drought-shrunk stonework.

## Holds

Shut upper and lower mitre gates, iron sluice racks and pawls, granite mooring bollards, [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md) bolted into the upper wing wall, and [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) moored nose-in above the upper gates.

## Offers

Unobstructed sightlines to both [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md) and the barge's waterline; mechanical control of [Drawing the Chamber](process-chamber-draw.md) to [Lena](persona-lena.md), who carries the windlass key; mooring and warping leverage to [Bram](persona-bram.md), who works the bow and stern lines from the coping.

## Withheld

Enough spare depth in the upper pound to fill Lock Four's chamber cleanly without pulling the water down to or under the forty-two-inch municipal intake mark. Reaching the lower canal tonight requires spending that last half-inch of upper-pound water or risking a deep hull over the masonry sill on a short draw; preserving the mark untouched requires leaving Bram's perishable cargo penned above shut gates until morning.
