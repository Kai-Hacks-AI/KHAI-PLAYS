# Make the Morning Market — TO DOIT

## Taxonomy

Individual freight-passage Plan within [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md).

## Owner

[Bram](persona-bram.md) in [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md) (KHAI-PLAYS).

## Direction

Get [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) through Lock Four on the night's last scheduled passage so its perishable orchard crates reach the morning wholesale market unspoiled, and leave Lock Four without a ledger entry that shifts drought-breach or delay liability onto the boat.

## Orders

- **[Bram](persona-bram.md):** Press his scheduled arrival on [Lock Four Wall](place-lock-wall.md) before night lock-close; work bow and stern lines if [Drawing the Chamber](process-chamber-draw.md) starts or protect his claim if held above the gates; audit what [Lena](persona-lena.md) reports during [The Dusk Gauge Check](process-dusk-gauge-call.md); sign, qualify, or withhold his counter-signature in [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md) to protect cargo and hull.

## Implementation

[Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) carries unrefrigerated autumn pears and sits four inches deeper than Bram's normal drought trim under thirty extra consignment crates; only [Lena](persona-lena.md)'s windlass key can open the sluices; holding overnight misses the morning market run, while a shallow draw risks hanging up on the masonry sill.
