# Part 1: Dusk on the Lock Wall — TO CAST

## Taxonomy

Opening Plot (Part 1 of 2) of [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md), followed by [Part 2: The Dusk Gauge Call](plot-2-the-dusk-gauge-call.md) and tuned by [Working-Waterway Realism](pitch-working-waterway-realism.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Cue

October dusk on [Lock Four Wall](place-lock-wall.md) under an active municipal drought restriction. The upper mitre gates stand shut. Still water in the upper pound rests barely half an inch above the forty-two-inch stripe on [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md), which [Lena](persona-lena.md) is under orders to hold until midnight. [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) has arrived on the last scheduled passage before Lock Four closes for the night, riding deep under tarped crates of autumn pears bound for the morning market.

## Action

[Lena](persona-lena.md) drives toward [Hold the Municipal Intake Mark](plan-hold-the-intake-mark.md), defending the thin margin above the town intake pipe through midnight. [Bram](persona-bram.md) drives toward [Make the Morning Market](plan-make-the-morning-market.md), pressing to lock down on the published final slot before an overnight tie-up ruins his unrefrigerated fruit. Their efforts collide at the sluice rack over [Drawing the Chamber](process-chamber-draw.md): drawing a full chamber for Bram's heavy hull will pull the upper pound right down to—or just below—the red mark; refusing passage leaves Bram's cargo stranded overnight above the shut gates; attempting a shallow/partial draw tries to shave the drop on thin sill clearance. Either performer may hold firm, bargain, or gamble; the Plot closes in roughly 4–5 cast turns as soon as Lena and Bram commit to one of those three courses.

## Stage

At curtain on [Lock Four Wall](place-lock-wall.md), [Lena](persona-lena.md) stands beside the shut upper gate balance beam with her brass windlass key clipped to her belt and a lit hand lantern set near [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md). [Bram](persona-bram.md) steps onto the coping from the foredeck of [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) with his bow check-line in hand. Fixed sequence: the upper gates begin shut and [Drawing the Chamber](process-chamber-draw.md) has not started; no water enters the chamber unless Lena sets her key to the sluice rack; the scene cuts immediately once both commit to full lockage, holding above the lock overnight, or trying a shallow/partial draw.

## Tension

[Lena](persona-lena.md) privately carries an unlogged low-water dip from two weeks ago and a quiet warning from the district engineer that one under-mark dusk reading will cost her the lock cottage—even though she knows the canal's own published timetable promised Bram this final dusk passage. [Bram](persona-bram.md) privately carries thirty extra orchard crates taken on credit at noon, sitting his barge four inches deeper than his usual drought trim—meaning an overnight stall forfeits money he owes upland growers, while a shallow draw runs a sharp risk of grounding on Lock Four's masonry sill.
