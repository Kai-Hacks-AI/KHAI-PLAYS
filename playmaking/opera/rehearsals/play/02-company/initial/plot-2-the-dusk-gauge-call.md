# Part 2: The Dusk Gauge Call — TO CAST

## Taxonomy

Closing Plot (Part 2 of 2) of [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md), following twenty minutes after [Part 1: Dusk on the Lock Wall](plot-1-dusk-on-the-lock-wall.md) and tuned by [Working-Waterway Realism](pitch-working-waterway-realism.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Cue

Inside [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md) twenty minutes after Part 1's commitment. Outside in the dark, the chamber, [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md), and [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md) sit in whatever physical state Part 1 left behind—a full draw with the barge down below and the pound at or under the red mark, shut gates with the pound safe and the fruit barge stranded above, or the tight, scraped aftermath of a shallow/partial draw. Before either person has signed off for the night, the twin bells on [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md) ring to start [The Dusk Gauge Check](process-dusk-gauge-call.md): the night water-bailiff is on the wire asking for Lock Four's dusk gauge reading.

## Action

[Lena](persona-lena.md) pursues the closing end of [Hold the Municipal Intake Mark](plan-hold-the-intake-mark.md) while [Bram](persona-bram.md) pursues the closing end of [Make the Morning Market](plan-make-the-morning-market.md). Their efforts collide across eight feet of floor between [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md) and [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md): Lena must give a dusk gauge reading aloud on the district line with Bram listening in the room, and both must decide how—or whether—to sign the closing line in [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md) on whatever Part 1 left in the chamber and on the gauge. Both performers remain genuinely free across roughly 4–5 cast turns to report and co-sign raw numbers, round a hairline reading, add a written protest or exception note, or withhold a signature.

## Stage

Inside [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md), [Lena](persona-lena.md) stands between the casement overlooking [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md) and the ringing [Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md). [Bram](persona-bram.md) stands just inside the door beside the sloped desk where [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md) lies open with nib pen and blotter. Fixed sequence: (1) at curtain, [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md) rings with the night water-bailiff calling for the dusk gauge figure and final passage status; (2) Lena answers the wire within Bram's hearing; (3) the call concludes and both confront the signature columns of [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md) to close the Play.

## Tension

[Lena](persona-lena.md) knows that reporting an under-mark reading on [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md) invites the citation she was warned would cost her post, yet shading a figure or logging a clean refusal puts her in [Bram](persona-bram.md)'s hands when the pen reaches his counter-signature column. Bram knows that co-signing an unqualified hold-over or a faulted partial draw leaves him carrying grower debt, spoilage, or sill blame alone, yet forcing a ruinous report onto the wire will not move his crates an inch closer to the morning market.
