# Drawing the Chamber — TO IDLE

## Taxonomy

Evening lockage water-transfer Process within [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Initiated by

[Lena](persona-lena.md) fitting her brass windlass key to the upper sluice rack on [Lock Four Wall](place-lock-wall.md) and lifting one or both gate paddles—either for a full chamber fill or a checked shallow/partial draw—while [Bram](persona-bram.md) tends the lines on [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md). If Lena and Bram commit in [Part 1: Dusk on the Lock Wall](plot-1-dusk-on-the-lock-wall.md) to holding the barge above the lock overnight, this Process stays idle and the upper gates stay shut.

## Direction

Draws water out of the drought-thin upper pound into Lock Four's chamber until either chamber and upper pound equalize for a full passage or Lena drops the paddles early to cap the upper-pound drop on a partial draw.

## Lever

Driven by gravity head from the upper pound rushing through the sluice culverts; slowed by cracking a single paddle a few ratchet teeth at a time while checking [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md); stopped by knocking off the iron pawl to drop the paddles shut before equalization—at the direct price of shallower water over the masonry upper sill.

## Echo

After a full draw, [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) locks down into the lower reach cleanly, and the upper pound settles right on or just below the forty-two-inch stripe on [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md). After a shallow/partial draw, the upper pound shaves down to the lip of the red mark while Bram's deep-laden keel scrapes or hangs on the upper sill, leaving an irregular draw and physical strain standing behind [Part 2: The Dusk Gauge Call](plot-2-the-dusk-gauge-call.md).
