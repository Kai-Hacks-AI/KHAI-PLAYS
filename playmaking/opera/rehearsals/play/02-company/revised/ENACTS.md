## Name

*Low Water at Lock Four*

## Arc

*Low Water at Lock Four* unfolds across two tightly linked evening Plots at Lock Four on an autumn canal under municipal drought restriction:

1. [Part 1: Dusk on the Lock Wall](plot-1-dusk-on-the-lock-wall.md) sets the operational decision on the masonry coping at dusk while the upper gates remain shut, the upper pound sits barely half an inch above the red municipal intake mark, and a wire-sealed auxiliary key inside [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md) guards enough standing mill-race water for one lockage. It forces an immediate commitment among four live paths—locking the heavy barge through on an unassisted full draw, breaking the numbered seal on [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md) for a bypass-fed full draw, holding the barge above the lock overnight, or attempting a shallow/partial draw—before Lock Four closes for the night.
2. [Part 2: The Dusk Gauge Call](plot-2-the-dusk-gauge-call.md) picks up twenty minutes later inside the lock house after gravity, hull draft, gate iron, and lead wire have turned Part 1's commitment into physical fact on the chamber, gauge, and wall peg. When the night water-bailiff rings for the dusk check, lockkeeper and skipper must confront what goes over the wire and whose signature backs the closing gauge and seal line in the ledger ahead of morning inspection.

Their order turns an operational gamble on the water into an evidentiary reckoning in ink: Part 1 decides what happens to the pound, the mill-race seal, and the cargo; Part 2 decides how that outcome is spoken, owned, or contested on the official record.

## Company

- **Personas:**
  - [Lena](persona-lena.md) — lockkeeper on duty at Lock Four, carrying the brass sluice windlass key, a hand lantern, and responsibility for keeping the upper pound at or above the municipal intake mark until midnight and accounting for Lock Four's sealed reserve gear.
  - [Bram](persona-bram.md) — independent barge skipper working the last scheduled evening passage with perishable autumn pears, carrying his bow check-line, cargo bills, and grower credit riding on the morning wholesale market.
- **Places:**
  - [Lock Four Wall](place-lock-wall.md) — the dusk coping, shut upper mitre gates, sluice gear, mill-race culvert spindle, staff gauge, and upper lay-by where Part 1 plays out.
  - [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md) — the cramped brick watch room beside the chamber holding the wire-sealed bypass key on its peg, where Part 2 plays out around the district telephone and desk ledger.
- **Pieces:**
  - [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md) — the enameled staff gauge in the upper wing wall marked at forty-two inches for the town's upstream water intake.
  - [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) — Bram's sixty-foot iron motor-barge laden deep with unrefrigerated orchard crates.
  - [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md) — the T-handled auxiliary culvert key hanging on a peg inside [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md) under numbered lead-wire seal `04-19`.
  - [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md) — the district wire on which the night water-bailiff calls for the dusk pound and seal check.
  - [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md) — the bound passage, gauge, and seal book requiring both lockkeeper's and skipper's signatures at dusk close before morning inspection.
- **Processes:**
  - [Drawing the Chamber](process-chamber-draw.md) — letting water from the upper pound into Lock Four's chamber on a full or partial draw (or leaving it uninitiated if the gates stay shut).
  - [Feeding the Mill-Race Bypass](process-mill-race-bypass.md) — breaking numbered seal `04-19` on [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md) to feed enough old mill-race water into the upper pound to cover one lockage without dropping below the red mark.
  - [The Dusk Gauge Check](process-dusk-gauge-call.md) — the bailiff's telephone audit twenty minutes after dusk close and the co-signing of the closing gauge and seal line ahead of morning inspection.
- **Plans:**
  - [Hold the Municipal Intake Mark](plan-hold-the-intake-mark.md) — [Lena](persona-lena.md)'s effort to keep the pound at or above the red mark until midnight and close the telephone and ledger record cleanly before morning inspection.
  - [Make the Morning Market](plan-make-the-morning-market.md) — [Bram](persona-bram.md)'s effort to clear Lock Four on the final scheduled slot before his fruit spoils and without absorbing drought-breach or seal-break fault on paper.
- **Positions:** None separate from [Lena](persona-lena.md) and [Bram](persona-bram.md), who hold their working posts throughout both Plots.
- **Pitch:** [Working-Waterway Realism](pitch-working-waterway-realism.md) is the default Pitch across the entire Play and both Plots unless the Director selects another Pitch when staging a run.

## Stakes

The Play fights over who absorbs the cost of a half-inch drought margin when public water rules and sealed emergency reserves collide with working-boat livelihoods.

- In [Part 1: Dusk on the Lock Wall](plot-1-dusk-on-the-lock-wall.md), every commitment carries a real price across [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md), [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md), and [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md): an unassisted full draw clears the barge toward market while pulling the pound down to or below the town's red intake mark; a bypass-fed full draw clears the barge and preserves the red mark at the irreversible cost of breaking numbered lead-wire seal `04-19`; holding shut protects both mark and seal while stranding perishable crates overnight; a shallow/partial draw keeps the seal intact while squeezing pound margin and sill clearance alike.
- In [Part 2: The Dusk Gauge Call](plot-2-the-dusk-gauge-call.md), the stakes move onto [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md) and [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md) before morning inspection checks the peg: whether [Lena](persona-lena.md) and [Bram](persona-bram.md) back the same account of water height and seal `04-19` in speech and ink, shade a borderline figure or emergency cause, file a written protest, or leave an unsigned or mismatched record that puts lock cottage, skipper's ticket, or grower debt on the block.
