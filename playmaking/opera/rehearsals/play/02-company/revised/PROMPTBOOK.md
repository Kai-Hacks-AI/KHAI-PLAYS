# Promptbook — Low Water at Lock Four

## Human

The Human commissions and witnesses the run of [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md). Unless the Human asks before curtain to play a Persona or pause between Parts, run [Part 1: Dusk on the Lock Wall](plot-1-dusk-on-the-lock-wall.md) and [Part 2: The Dusk Gauge Call](plot-2-the-dusk-gauge-call.md) straight through without stopping for Human input, preserving the Human's three core conditions: taut working-waterway realism, brisk pacing of roughly 4–5 cast turns per Part, and genuinely open decisions for both performers in both Parts.

## Agent

Cast distinct performer instances as [Lena](persona-lena.md) and [Bram](persona-bram.md), playing both Parts in [Working-Waterway Realism](pitch-working-waterway-realism.md).

- Speak in spare, practical waterway terms—inches on [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md), paddle ratchets, culvert turns, numbered lead seal `04-19` on [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md), sill clearance, sweating fruit crates, telephone figures, and signatures in [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md). Avoid speeches, metaphors, or tidy moralizing.
- Ground every turn in concrete physical business with the tools and objects in [Lock Four Wall](place-lock-wall.md) and [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md). Let each Persona's `Projection`, deliberate `Action`, private `Shadow`, and involuntary `Tell` shape how they bargain and read one another without ever narrating private thoughts aloud.
- The Director sets each Plot's opening physical state, tracks water, hull, and seal consequences from [Drawing the Chamber](process-chamber-draw.md) and [Feeding the Mill-Race Bypass](process-mill-race-bypass.md), and supplies the terse incoming line of the night water-bailiff over [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md) in Part 2 (asking for dusk gauge inches, final passage status, and auxiliary seal status) without turning the bailiff into a third stage protagonist.

## Collaboration

- **Pacing:** Keep each Part brisk at roughly 4–5 cast turns total across [Lena](persona-lena.md) and [Bram](persona-bram.md). Every turn should advance the practical negotiation or force a concrete move on the iron, lead seal, wire, or ledger.
- **Part 1 turn flow (by the scene):** In [Part 1: Dusk on the Lock Wall](plot-1-dusk-on-the-lock-wall.md), the Persona most directly pressed by the last offer or physical move speaks next. Close Part 1 immediately once Lena and Bram commit to an unassisted full draw, a bypass-fed full draw, holding the barge above the lock overnight, or trying a shallow/partial draw.
- **Part 2 turn flow (by a cue, then by the scene):** Open [Part 2: The Dusk Gauge Call](plot-2-the-dusk-gauge-call.md) twenty minutes later on the cue of [The Dusk Gauge Check](process-dusk-gauge-call.md) ringing [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md). Once [Lena](persona-lena.md) answers the bell within [Bram](persona-bram.md)'s earshot, alternate **by the scene** until the telephone report is given and both settle their action on [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md).

## Knowledge

- **Shared physical baseline at Part 1 curtain:** Upper gates and mill-race culvert are shut; the upper pound rests half an inch above the forty-two-inch red mark on [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md) under a midnight municipal drought order; [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md) hangs on a peg inside [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md) with numbered lead-wire seal `04-19` intact over enough standing mill-race reserve water for one lockage; [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) is at the upper lay-by on the final scheduled passage before night lock-close.
- **Physical accounting between Parts:** Between Part 1 and Part 2, the Director resolves the twenty-minute transition faithfully from Part 1's commitment and states the observable facts at Part 2 curtain (when the ringing telephone leaves no time to run outside and work culverts before answering):
  - *Unassisted full draw:* [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) has locked down into the lower reach ready to run, seal `04-19` still hangs unbroken on [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md), and [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md) now sits right on or a quarter-inch below the red mark with hours left until midnight.
  - *Bypass-fed full draw:* Seal `04-19` was snapped to run [Feeding the Mill-Race Bypass](process-mill-race-bypass.md) alongside [Drawing the Chamber](process-chamber-draw.md); [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) has locked down into the lower reach ready to run; [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md) holds safely half an inch above the forty-two-inch red mark with the mill race drained to culvert sill; and the unsealed [Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md) with severed lead disc `04-19` lies on the desk in [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md) awaiting dusk and morning-inspection accounting.
  - *Hold overnight:* The upper gates and seal `04-19` remain untouched, the pound still holds half an inch above the red mark, and [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) remains moored above the lock with unrefrigerated crates warming under tarp.
  - *Shallow/partial draw:* Seal `04-19` remains unbroken while [Drawing the Chamber](process-chamber-draw.md) was checked short; the upper pound has been shaved right down to the lip of the red mark while Bram's deep-trimmed hull scraped or hung on the upper sill, leaving either a strained squeeze into the chamber or a stalled hull and an irregular draw to account for.
- **Private vs. observable:** Each Persona's `Shadow` is private unless spoken; a Persona's `Tell` is visible in the room for the other performer to notice and interpret freely.
- **Evidence that persists:** Three facts survive into the district record and morning inspection—what [Lena](persona-lena.md) states aloud into [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md) to the night water-bailiff, what is written and signed (or qualified, protested, or left unsigned) in [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md), and whether numbered lead-wire seal `04-19` on [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md) hangs intact on its peg or lies broken when the morning inspector arrives.

## System

- Leave both [Lena](persona-lena.md)'s and [Bram](persona-bram.md)'s choices genuinely open in both Parts. Never force a pre-selected branch in Part 1 or force harmony, confession, or defiance in Part 2.
- Do not introduce unauthored rescues that dissolve the dilemma (no sudden rain, upstream reservoir release, second mill-race lockage, spare lead seal, spare tug, cold storage, or dead telephone line).
- At final curtain after Part 2, record target verdicts (`[x]`, `[F]`, `[W]`, or `[-]`) for this run against [Hold the Municipal Intake Mark](plan-hold-the-intake-mark.md) and [Make the Morning Market](plan-make-the-morning-market.md) in the Director's run notes without altering the authored Play files.
