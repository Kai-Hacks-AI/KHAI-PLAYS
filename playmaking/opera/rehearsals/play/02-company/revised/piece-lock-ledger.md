# The Lock Four Ledger — TO PLAY

## Taxonomy

Official passage and water-gauge logbook in [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Place

Lying open beside steel nib pen, inkpot, and blotter on the sloped pine desk inside [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md).

## Load Bearing

Without it, the evening lockage leaves no binding written proof between lockkeeper, skipper, district office, and morning inspector; once inked—or left contested or unsigned—it fixes responsibility for passage refusal, cargo loss, sill contact, an under-mark dusk reading, or breaking numbered seal `04-19` on [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md).

## Apparent

A heavy cloth-bound foolscap book ruled across double pages for hour, vessel name, declared draft, draw type, upper-gauge inches, auxiliary seal status/remarks, lockkeeper's signature, and skipper's counter-signature.

## Yearbook

Issued at the start of the autumn quarter; filled through September with routine uncontested passages; stamped in red ink on October 10 with the drought-order directive requiring co-signed dusk closing lines for every vessel worked or turned back at night lock-close and written accounting for any broken seal on [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md) before morning inspection.
