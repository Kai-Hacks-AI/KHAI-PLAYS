# The Mill-Race Bypass Key — TO PLAY

## Taxonomy

Sealed auxiliary water-control gear in [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Place

Hanging from an iron wall peg beside the pinned drought circular inside [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md) when [Part 1: Dusk on the Lock Wall](plot-1-dusk-on-the-lock-wall.md) opens, three stone steps from the mill-race culvert spindle on [Lock Four Wall](place-lock-wall.md); during [Part 2: The Dusk Gauge Call](plot-2-the-dusk-gauge-call.md), either still hanging wire-sealed on its peg or lying unsealed beside [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md) with its severed lead tag if used between Parts.

## Load Bearing

Without it, [Feeding the Mill-Race Bypass](process-mill-race-bypass.md) cannot run and no reserve water from the old mill race can enter Lock Four's upper pound, leaving no physical way to pass [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) on a full draw without pulling the pound down to or below the red stripe on [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md); without its numbered lead-wire seal, [Lena](persona-lena.md) could tap that reserve quietly without having to account for it on [The Dusk Gauge Check](process-dusk-gauge-call.md), in [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md), and at morning inspection.

## Apparent

A heavy T-handled wrought-iron socket key on a whitewashed brick peg, wired through its shaft eye to a stapled iron wall ring by a loop of thin lead wire crimped shut with a flat disc stamped `04-19`.

## Yearbook

Forged to turn the side-culvert valve when Lock Four shared headwater with the nineteenth-century gristmill; retained as a dry-summer make-up key after the mill closed; wired to its peg under numbered lead seal `04-19` by the district water inspector on October 10 under drought rules requiring any broken seal to be declared on the dusk telephone check and logged in [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md) before morning towpath inspection.
