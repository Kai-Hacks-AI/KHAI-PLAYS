# The Lock-House Wall Phone — TO PLAY

## Taxonomy

District canal telephone link in [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Place

Bolted to the whitewashed brick beside the casement window inside [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md).

## Load Bearing

Without it, the district water office could not audit Lock Four's pound height at dusk, leaving [Lena](persona-lena.md) overnight seepage until dawn instead of forcing an immediate spoken figure twenty minutes after lock-close while [Bram](persona-bram.md) stands in the room.

## Apparent

A black bakelite wall box with twin brass bells at the crown, a crank at the side, and a heavy corded receiver worn smooth where wet thumbs grip it.

## Yearbook

Strung on creosote poles along the towpath to link the seven locks of the flight; used most seasons for ice jams and weir warnings; repurposed this month for nightly dusk gauge roll-calls by the night water-bailiff under the municipal drought restriction.
