# Lock Four House — TO SHOW

## Taxonomy

Duty watch room beside the chamber of Lock Four on the autumn canal, three stone steps back from [Lock Four Wall](place-lock-wall.md) in [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Shown

A cramped whitewashed brick room eight feet across, lit by a single brass wick lamp; damp wool coats steaming on wall pegs beside a heavy T-handled iron key wired to its peg under numbered lead seal `04-19`; a narrow casement window looking straight out onto the dark coping and the lantern over [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md); the twin bells of [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md) cutting into the quiet twenty minutes after dusk close.

## Holds

[The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md) hanging wire-sealed on a wall peg beside the pinned municipal drought-restriction circular, [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md) bolted beside the casement, a sloped pine desk bearing [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md) with steel nib pen and inkpot, and direct window sight back onto [Lock Four Wall](place-lock-wall.md).

## Offers

Three steps from the coping during Part 1, access to [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md) if [Lena](persona-lena.md) chooses to break seal `04-19` and start [Feeding the Mill-Race Bypass](process-mill-race-bypass.md); during Part 2, light, ink, and wire to complete [The Dusk Gauge Check](process-dusk-gauge-call.md) so Lena can answer the night water-bailiff's call and both [Lena](persona-lena.md) and [Bram](persona-bram.md) can inspect, sign, qualify, or contest the evening gauge and seal entry in [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md).

## Withheld

Distance or secrecy between spoken report, broken lead, and written proof. In an eight-foot room, [Bram](persona-bram.md) hears every syllable [Lena](persona-lena.md) gives the water-bailiff on [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md), Lena sees every mark or refusal Bram makes on [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md), and neither can reverse what Part 1 left in the chamber or re-crimp numbered seal `04-19` on [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md) before morning inspection.
