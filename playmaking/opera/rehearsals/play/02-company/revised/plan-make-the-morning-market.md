# Make the Morning Market — TO DOIT

## Taxonomy

Individual freight-passage Plan within [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md).

## Owner

[Bram](persona-bram.md) in [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md) (KHAI-PLAYS).

## Direction

Get [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) through Lock Four on the night's last scheduled passage so its perishable orchard crates reach the morning wholesale market unspoiled, and leave Lock Four without a telephone or ledger record that shifts drought-breach, broken-seal, or delay liability onto the boat.

## Orders

- **[Bram](persona-bram.md):** Press his scheduled arrival—and, if needed, the reserve water available via [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md) and [Feeding the Mill-Race Bypass](process-mill-race-bypass.md)—on [Lock Four Wall](place-lock-wall.md) before night lock-close; work bow and stern lines if [Drawing the Chamber](process-chamber-draw.md) starts or protect his claim if held above the gates; audit what [Lena](persona-lena.md) reports during [The Dusk Gauge Check](process-dusk-gauge-call.md); sign, qualify, or withhold his counter-signature in [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md) to protect cargo and hull.

## Implementation

[Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) carries unrefrigerated autumn pears and sits four inches deeper than Bram's normal drought trim under thirty extra consignment crates; only [Lena](persona-lena.md) controls the sluice windlass key and the wire-sealed [Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md) inside [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md); holding overnight misses the morning market run, a shallow draw risks hanging up on the masonry sill, and breaking seal `04-19` requires dusk and ledger accounting that could scrutinize his hull's draft.

## Targets

- [ ] Secure a dusk commitment on [Lock Four Wall](place-lock-wall.md) that moves [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) toward the lower reach instead of stranding it overnight above shut gates.
- [ ] Clear Lock Four's upper sill and chamber without grounding the hull or losing the fruit load to overnight spoilage.
- [ ] Ensure [Lena](persona-lena.md)'s spoken report on [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md) does not pin an unauthorized draw, overloaded draft, or rule breach on his vessel.
- [ ] Settle his signature column in [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md)—including any entry accounting for seal `04-19`—so he retains clean standing with growers and canal authorities.
