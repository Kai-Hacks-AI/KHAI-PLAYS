# Part 1: Dusk on the Lock Wall — TO CAST

## Taxonomy

Opening Plot (Part 1 of 2) of [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md), followed by [Part 2: The Dusk Gauge Call](plot-2-the-dusk-gauge-call.md) and tuned by [Working-Waterway Realism](pitch-working-waterway-realism.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Cue

October dusk on [Lock Four Wall](place-lock-wall.md) under an active municipal drought restriction. The upper mitre gates stand shut. Still water in the upper pound rests barely half an inch above the forty-two-inch stripe on [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md), which [Lena](persona-lena.md) is under orders to hold until midnight. Three steps inside [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md), [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md) hangs on its wall peg under numbered lead-wire seal `04-19`, guarding enough standing water in the old mill race to cover a single lockage. [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) has arrived on the last scheduled passage before Lock Four closes for the night, riding deep under tarped crates of autumn pears bound for the morning market.

## Action

[Lena](persona-lena.md) drives toward [Hold the Municipal Intake Mark](plan-hold-the-intake-mark.md), defending the thin margin above the town intake pipe and her clean standing at the post. [Bram](persona-bram.md) drives toward [Make the Morning Market](plan-make-the-morning-market.md), pressing to lock down on the published final slot before an overnight tie-up ruins his unrefrigerated fruit. Their efforts collide over [Drawing the Chamber](process-chamber-draw.md) and [Feeding the Mill-Race Bypass](process-mill-race-bypass.md): an unassisted full draw clears Bram's heavy hull while pulling the upper pound down to or below the red mark; breaking seal `04-19` on [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md) feeds enough mill-race water to lock the barge through without dropping below the red mark, at the cost of a broken numbered seal that must be answered for at dusk and in [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md) before morning inspection; refusing passage strands Bram's cargo overnight above shut gates; a shallow/partial draw keeps the seal intact while risking sill contact on thin clearance. Either performer may hold firm, bargain, or gamble; the Plot closes in roughly 4–5 cast turns as soon as Lena and Bram commit to one of those four courses.

## Stage

At curtain on [Lock Four Wall](place-lock-wall.md), [Lena](persona-lena.md) stands beside the shut upper gate balance beam with her brass sluice windlass key clipped to her belt and a lit hand lantern set near [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md); three steps behind her inside [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md), [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md) hangs wire-sealed on its peg near the doorway, opposite the sunken mill-race culvert spindle beside the upper wing wall. [Bram](persona-bram.md) steps onto the coping from the foredeck of [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) with his bow check-line in hand. Fixed sequence: upper gates and mill-race culvert begin shut, seal `04-19` begins unbroken, and neither [Drawing the Chamber](process-chamber-draw.md) nor [Feeding the Mill-Race Bypass](process-mill-race-bypass.md) has started; the scene cuts immediately once both commit to an unassisted full draw, a bypass-fed full draw, holding above the lock overnight, or trying a shallow/partial draw.

## Tension

[Lena](persona-lena.md) privately carries an unlogged low-water dip from two weeks ago and a quiet warning from the district engineer that another dusk breach will cost her the lock cottage—making both an under-mark gauge reading and an unbacked break of numbered seal `04-19` dangerous to her post, even though she knows the canal's published timetable promised Bram this final dusk passage. [Bram](persona-bram.md) privately carries thirty extra orchard crates taken on credit at noon, sitting his barge four inches deeper than his usual drought trim—meaning an overnight stall forfeits money he owes upland growers, a shallow draw sharply risks grounding on Lock Four's masonry sill, and asking for the sealed mill-race reserve invites questions in ink about why his hull needed full emergency water.
