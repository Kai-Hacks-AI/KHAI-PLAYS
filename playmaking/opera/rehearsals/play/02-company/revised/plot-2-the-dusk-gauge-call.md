# Part 2: The Dusk Gauge Call — TO CAST

## Taxonomy

Closing Plot (Part 2 of 2) of [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md), following twenty minutes after [Part 1: Dusk on the Lock Wall](plot-1-dusk-on-the-lock-wall.md) and tuned by [Working-Waterway Realism](pitch-working-waterway-realism.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Cue

Inside [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md) twenty minutes after Part 1's commitment. Outside in the dark, the chamber, [Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md), and [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md)—and inside the room, [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md)—sit in whatever physical state Part 1 left behind: an unassisted full draw with the barge below and the pound at or under the red mark; a bypass-fed full draw via [Feeding the Mill-Race Bypass](process-mill-race-bypass.md) with the barge below, the pound safely above the red mark, and numbered lead-wire seal `04-19` snapped ahead of morning inspection; shut gates with pound and seal untouched and the fruit barge stranded above; or the scraped aftermath of a shallow/partial draw. Before either person has signed off for the night, the twin bells on [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md) ring to start [The Dusk Gauge Check](process-dusk-gauge-call.md).

## Action

[Lena](persona-lena.md) pursues the closing end of [Hold the Municipal Intake Mark](plan-hold-the-intake-mark.md) while [Bram](persona-bram.md) pursues the closing end of [Make the Morning Market](plan-make-the-morning-market.md). Their efforts collide across eight feet of floor between [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md), [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md), and [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md): Lena must give Lock Four's dusk gauge reading and auxiliary-seal status aloud on the district line with Bram listening in the room, and both must decide how—or whether—to write and co-sign the closing gauge and seal line in [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md) before morning inspection. Both performers remain genuinely free across roughly 4–5 cast turns to report and co-sign raw facts, shade a borderline figure or seal reason, enter a written protest or qualification, or withhold a signature.

## Stage

Inside [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md), [Lena](persona-lena.md) stands between the casement overlooking [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md) and the ringing [Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md). [Bram](persona-bram.md) stands just inside the door beside the sloped desk where [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md) lies open with nib pen and blotter; [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md) either hangs wire-sealed on its wall peg or lies on the desk beside severed lead disc `04-19` if used between Parts. Fixed sequence: (1) at curtain, [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md) rings with the night water-bailiff calling for the dusk gauge figure, final passage status, and seal check; (2) Lena answers the wire within Bram's hearing; (3) the call concludes and both confront the signature and remarks columns of [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md) to close the Play.

## Tension

[Lena](persona-lena.md) knows that an under-mark reading—or a broken numbered seal `04-19` left unreported on [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md) or unbacked in [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md) when morning inspection checks the peg—invites the citation she was warned would cost her post, yet how she justifies the evening puts her in [Bram](persona-bram.md)'s hands at the counter-signature column. Bram knows that co-signing an unqualified hold-over, a faulted partial draw, or a seal-break entry blaming his hull's deep draft leaves him carrying grower debt, sill fault, or overload exposure, yet burning Lena on the wire will not help his standing on the canal.
