# The Dusk Gauge Check — TO IDLE

## Taxonomy

Nightly municipal drought-audit Process within [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Initiated by

Twenty minutes after dusk lock-close, the twin brass bells on [The Lock-House Wall Phone](piece-wall-phone.md) ring inside [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md) at the start of [Part 2: The Dusk Gauge Call](plot-2-the-dusk-gauge-call.md) as the night water-bailiff calls down the flight for Lock Four's dusk reading, last-passage status, and auxiliary seal report.

## Direction

Moves from the ringing telephone to [Lena](persona-lena.md)'s spoken dusk gauge figure and seal report on the district wire—spoken within three paces of [Bram](persona-bram.md)—and immediately onto the open closing row of [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md), where spoken account, physical seal `04-19` on [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md), and written signatures must meet or diverge before morning inspection.

## Lever

Driven by the night water-bailiff working sequentially down the canal flight ahead of the midnight municipal intake window and backed by the morning inspector's physical check of [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md) peg; slowed only by a ring or two while Lena glances toward [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md); cannot be dodged by ignoring the bell, since an unanswered lock house dispatches the bailiff down the towpath in person.

## Echo

Leaves an official dusk gauge and auxiliary-seal report logged at the district water office and leaves [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md) either co-signed in agreement, signed with a written protest or seal/draft qualification, split between phone report and physical ledger/seal state, or missing a signature ahead of morning inspection.
