# Feeding the Mill-Race Bypass — TO IDLE

## Taxonomy

Auxiliary reserve-water feed Process paired with [Drawing the Chamber](process-chamber-draw.md) within [Low Water at Lock Four](ENACTS.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Initiated by

Twisting or snapping numbered lead-wire seal `04-19` to lift [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md) off its wall peg inside [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md) and turning the sunken mill-race culvert spindle beside the upper wing wall of [Lock Four Wall](place-lock-wall.md)—confirmed at once by standing water rumbling through the brick side-culvert into the upper pound. If [Lena](persona-lena.md) and [Bram](persona-bram.md) commit to an unassisted draw, a shallow draw, or an overnight hold in [Part 1: Dusk on the Lock Wall](plot-1-dusk-on-the-lock-wall.md), this Process stays idle and seal `04-19` hangs unbroken.

## Direction

Feeds impounded reserve water from the old mill race into Lock Four's upper pound to replace the chamber volume drawn off by [Drawing the Chamber](process-chamber-draw.md) across one full lockage.

## Lever

Driven by the limited head of standing water above the old mill-race culvert invert (enough for a single chamber fill and no second draw); metered and shut tight again with [The Mill-Race Bypass Key](piece-mill-race-key.md) once the chamber equalizes; breaking numbered lead-wire seal `04-19` cannot be reversed, re-threaded, or hidden from morning inspection.

## Echo

[Bram's Orchard Barge](piece-orchard-barge.md) locks down into the lower reach on full sill clearance while Lock Four's upper pound remains safely above the forty-two-inch mark on [The Red Intake Gauge](piece-intake-gauge.md), and the old mill race drains down to culvert sill; inside [Lock Four House](place-lock-house.md), the unsealed key and severed lead disc `04-19` remain on the desk to be accounted for during [The Dusk Gauge Check](process-dusk-gauge-call.md) and signed into [The Lock Four Ledger](piece-lock-ledger.md) before morning inspection.
