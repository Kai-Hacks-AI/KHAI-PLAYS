## (c) Friction Noticed in `SKILL.md` and References During a Focused Multi-Plot Revision

1. **Deciding whether a new `Piece` also warrants a new `Process`:** `SKILL.md` advises *"Create or revise only the objects the brief calls for"* alongside *"A Place can hold a Piece the cast uses when a Process starts."* When a focused brief names a new physical object ("add a wire-sealed auxiliary mill-race bypass key... which can feed enough reserve water...") that triggers its own world-state change, starting condition, and irreversible trace, `SKILL.md` leaves implicit whether the Playwright should mint a paired `Process` file (`process-mill-race-bypass.md`) or stretch an existing `Process` (`process-chamber-draw.md`) whose mechanism and direction differ.
2. **Hidden prose blast radius beyond link updates:** `SKILL.md` (`## Deliver the work`) says *"For a focused brief, return the requested objects and update the links affected by the change."* In a tightly coupled multi-Plot Play, adding one operational option in Plot 1 with an audit consequence in Plot 2 invalidates closed-world enumerations ("three courses," "two records," or `Place` `Withheld` statements claiming no spare water exists) across 11 of 17 existing files—including `PROMPTBOOK.md`'s inter-Plot physical accounting table and `place-lock-wall.md`'s `Withheld` section—even where existing links were not broken. Guidance on auditing closed-world constraints (`Withheld`, `Triggers`, `Echo`, and `Promptbook -> Knowledge` handoff rules) during revisions would prevent subtle contradictions.
3. **Single-"now" wording in `Piece` and `Place` templates across branching Plots:** `references/piece.md` (`## Place: Say where the Piece is now`) and `references/place.md` (`## Shown: Show what someone entering can sense before anyone acts`) assume a static present moment. When a Piece's location and physical appearance in Plot 2 depend on an open choice made in Plot 1 (still wire-sealed on the wall peg vs. lying unsealed beside its severed lead tag on the desk), the Playwright must invent conditional phrasing without guidance on whether `Place`/`Shown` should state Plot 1 curtain baseline alone or track cross-Plot state branches.
