## 3. What Changed Onstage After the Focused Revision

With Plot order and `Working-Waterway Realism` held fixed, introducing [piece-mill-race-key.md](../02-company/revised/piece-mill-race-key.md) and [process-mill-race-bypass.md](../02-company/revised/process-mill-race-bypass.md) shifted both Plots without softening the conflict:

1. **Part 1 shifted from physical loss vs. refusal to conditional seal-breaking leverage:**
   - In **Initial** ([01](../04-runs/01-initial-a.md), [02](../04-runs/02-initial-b.md)), `42½"` could not cover a full chamber without breaching `42"`, forcing physical damage or stranding in **both** runs: `01` ground `60` feet of riveted iron on the granite sill (`[F]` on Bram Target 2) and shaved the pound to `41 15/16"` on ripples (`[W]` on Lena Target 1); `02` stranded the pear cargo overnight (`[F]` on Bram Targets 1 & 2).
   - In **Revised** ([03](../04-runs/03-revised-a.md), [04](../04-runs/04-revised-b.md)), Bram pointed to the mill-race spindle on `Turn 3` in **both** runs, and Lena turned seal `04-19` into an upfront bargaining gate on `Turn 4`—extracting Bram’s `+4"` draft confession on the wall (`01` had kept `3'10"` hidden until Part 2 after hitting the sill) before snapping `04-19` on `Turn 6` so both barge and `42½"` intake mark cleared Part 1 intact.
2. **The `Triggers` handoff reversed physical leverage entering Part 2:**
   - In `01` (barge penned in chamber awaiting lower paddles) and `02` (barge moored above shut gates), Lena entered Part 2 still holding physical gate control over Bram’s boat.
   - In `03` and `04`, the bypass-fed full draw put Bram’s barge **already floating free below the tail-bridge** at Part 2 curtain while severed lead disc `04-19` sat irreversibly on Lena’s desk—leaving Lena reliant on Bram’s honour and counter-signature to back her broken seal before morning inspection (`03` [L124](../04-runs/03-revised-a.md#L124)).
3. **Part 2 shifted from physical casualty accounting to audit & paper alignment:**
   - In `01`–`02`, Part 2 negotiated who owned physical harm already suffered (a rubbed sill or a warming hold of fruit).
   - In `03`–`04`, Part 2 negotiated *why* `04-19` had to be cut: Lena could only justify snapping `04-19` to the morning inspector by recording that Bram’s deep draft (`42"` in `03`; `38"` in `04`) made a checked draw unsafe, colliding directly with Bram’s upstream Lock Three declaration (`03`) or Monday flight-permit exposure (`04` `[F]` on Target 4, prompting Lena’s first onstage confession of her own `Shadow` warning at `04` [L124](../04-runs/04-revised-b.md#L124)).
