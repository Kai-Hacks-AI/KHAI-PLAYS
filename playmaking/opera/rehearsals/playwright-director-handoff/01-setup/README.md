# Rehearse the Playwright–Director handoff

Run each role in fresh context. Give the Director the Playwright's completed handoff and a production brief. Keep the reviewer prompts below out of the role briefs.

## Source-based run

Give the Playwright the title *The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse* and [Project Gutenberg ebook 21](https://www.gutenberg.org/files/21/21-h/21-h.htm). Scope the source to the complete fable in that edition. Ask for the material inventory and Sourcebook first, then the Play and Plot files for the full arc.

Give the Director the authored files and ask for a short staging of one continuous fragment within a single Plot in the town house. Ask for the Strichfassung and a performed run. Keep authored facts fixed.

## Original run

Give the Playwright this brief: Two people arrive at a closed ferry terminal. One has a key and the other knows why the ferry stopped running. Ask for the material inventory first, then an original Play and its Plot files.

Give the Director the authored handoff and ask for one fragment that can be performed using only the starting context and cues captured in the Strichfassung.

## Review

- For the fable, compare the inventory with the source from beginning to end. Check the country visit, the town visit, both interruptions, and the Country Mouse's final decision. Check the Sourcebook links and rationale for selected and unused objects. Mark material invented or inferred where relevant.
- For both runs, check that the material inventory was delivered before composition and that the Play and Plot files use it. Check that enough authored material is present to support a different staging selection.
- Compare each Strichfassung with its Play and Plot files. Check that its boundaries resolve to a fragment, its starting cues are sufficient, and the performance respects the authored facts and the staging limit. Check that the authored files are unchanged.
- Record the inventory, authored files, Strichfassung, performance, and review findings with the run. Treat missing evidence as an open result.
