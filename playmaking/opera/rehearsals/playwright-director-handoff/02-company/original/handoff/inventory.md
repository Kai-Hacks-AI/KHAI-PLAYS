# Material Inventory — *Slack Water at Marrow Slip*

## Brief and Premise

**Brief:** *Two people arrive at a closed ferry terminal. One has a key and the other knows why the ferry stopped running.*

**Generated Premise:** Five nights after the mainland-to-Kestrel Island ferry (*M/V Gullswake*) abruptly ceased service behind a laminated *"CLOSED — RAMP HYDRAULIC INSPECTION"* notice, two waterfront workers converge in sleet at the padlocked **Marrow Slip Terminal** forty minutes before the **23:20 low-water slack**:

- **Maren Holt**, the island cooperative's **Bonded Shore Agent**, arrives with an unreturned **#2 Brass Harbour Master Key** and a time-critical **Dispensary Cold-Chain Case**. Because Coast Guard crews do not run suspended commercial clinic freight without an active maritime emergency, Maren intends to unlock the terminal, record custody on the office sheet, and take the cooperative's **16-Foot Aluminum Work Skiff** across Marrow Narrows during the fifteen-minute slack-water window.
- **Doyle Kerr**, *Gullswake*'s **Suspended Chief Marine Engineer**, is already under the dark waiting-room eaves with a flat pry bar and no key. He knows the ramp hydraulics are intact: on Friday's 21:14 fog arrival, *Gullswake* ran with a dead starboard radar and an expired hull-inspection extension, struck the outer east guide-crib, limped behind Kestrel Island to hide an uninsured casualty, and sheared an **iron-bolted crib piling** so it sits submerged less than a foot beneath the low-water chop directly across the slip mouth. Inside the terminal's bolted **Night Drop Box** lies Doyle's signed **Friday Engine-Bell & Fathometer Envelope**, which he dropped through the brass slot that night before losing his nerve for five days.

Neither can get what they came for alone. Maren holds the only key to the Waiting Room, the inner **Ticket & Winch Office**, and the **Night Drop Box**, and is forty minutes from dropping an aluminum skiff onto an unmarked underwater spike. Doyle holds the knowledge that can save her hull—and unlock a Coast Guard hazard response via the harbour VHF radio—at the cost of blowing open a five-day cover-up that threatens his license and permanently shutters the ferry company before tomorrow's **07:00** adjusters' cleanout.

## Timeline, Facts, and Discovery Rules

### Fixed Times and Facts (Where Choices and Handoffs Depend on Them)
- **Friday, Nov 20, 21:14:** *M/V Gullswake* (starboard radar unserviceable, hull-inspection extension expired Nov 18) strikes the outer east timber-and-iron guide-crib at Marrow Slip in fog, ripping its starboard ballast tank and shearing a bolted piling stub across the slip approach at `-0.2 ft` chart datum.
- **Friday, Nov 20, 23:50:** Management posts *"CLOSED — RAMP HYDRAULIC INSPECTION"*, tows *Gullswake* behind Kestrel Island, and collects crew keys. Before walking out, Doyle drops [piece-bell-log-envelope.md](piece-bell-log-envelope.md) into the Winch Office Night Drop Box.
- **Wednesday, Nov 25, 19:00:** Mainland pharmacy courier hands [piece-cold-chain-case.md](piece-cold-chain-case.md) to Maren; thermal pack rated stable until **01:00 Thursday**.
- **Wednesday, Nov 25, 22:40 (Play Start — Plot 1):** Ebb tide at `+1.4 ft` running 4 knots out; Maren arrives at [place-waiting-room.md](place-waiting-room.md) and finds Doyle under the eaves.
- **Wednesday, Nov 25, ~23:00 (Plot 2 Window):** Tide passing `+0.3 ft`; inner [place-winch-office.md](place-winch-office.md) entered (or contested).
- **Wednesday, Nov 25, 23:20–23:35 (Plot 3 Window):** Fifteen-minute low-water slack at `-0.6 ft` datum. Current drops below 0.5 knots; sheared east piling sits 8–10 inches beneath the chop (visible as an iron-bolt eddy only if the slipway floodlight is switched on).
- **Wednesday, Nov 25, 23:35:** Flood tide surges back into Marrow Slip, ending small-skiff launch safety until morning.
- **Thursday, Nov 26, 07:00:** Company adjusters arrive to empty the Winch Office Night Drop Box; cooperative receiver audits Maren's bond and keys.

*(Conversational pacing inside each Plot remains loose so performers can bargain, conceal, test, and pivot naturally up to the tide cues.)*

### Information Asymmetry, Uncertain Recollection, and When the Company Can Learn Material
- **At Play Start (Plot 1):**
  - **Maren knows** she holds [piece-master-key.md](piece-master-key.md), that [piece-cold-chain-case.md](piece-cold-chain-case.md) expires at **01:00**, and how to operate the davit hoist at slack water. **Maren mistakenly believes** the ferry stopped for a routine ramp-cylinder stall and assumes *M/V Gullswake* is tied up at the mainland repair yard down-coast rather than hidden behind Kestrel Island.
  - **Doyle knows** the exact bearing and depth of the sheared east crib-piling, why *Gullswake* is hidden behind Kestrel Island, and that dropping the skiff down the center cradle at low tide will gut its hull. **Doyle is uncertain** whether Port Superintendent Halvorsen already emptied the Night Drop Box over the weekend—he cannot verify [piece-bell-log-envelope.md](piece-bell-log-envelope.md) is still inside until [piece-master-key.md](piece-master-key.md) opens the box in Plot 2.
- **Discovery Gates in Play:**
  1. **Plot 1:** Either Persona can learn what the other carries (`Master Key`, `Cold-Chain Case`, pry bar) through observation or confrontation at the Waiting Room / Dutch door. Maren can learn the hydraulic notice is false if Doyle discloses it to keep her off the hoist or trade for entry.
  2. **Plot 2:** Unlocking the Winch Office lets both inspect the live hoist, VHF breaker, and bolted Drop Box. Opening the Drop Box with [piece-master-key.md](piece-master-key.md) confirms [piece-bell-log-envelope.md](piece-bell-log-envelope.md) is still inside and lets either read the **21:14 Nov 20** bell tape and fathometer trace firsthand.
  3. **Plot 3:** Throwing the slipway floodlight breaker at **23:20** (`-0.6 ft`) visibly reveals the swirling boil and iron drift-bolt of the sheared east piling thirty feet off the ramp toe, independently corroborating Doyle's warning while lighting the closed terminal to the shore road.

## Open Performer Decisions Preserved Across the Arc

The authored Plots will set forces in motion without scripting a predetermined resolution:
1. **Access & Bargain (Plot 1 -> Plot 2):** How Maren uses monopoly over [piece-master-key.md](piece-master-key.md)—admitting Doyle on conditions, forcing his explanation before turning the inner lock, or attempting to shut him out.
2. **Custody & Disclosure (Plot 2):** Who takes physical possession of [piece-bell-log-envelope.md](piece-bell-log-envelope.md) once unlocked, and whether either throws the **Harbour VHF Channel 16** switch to report the five-day-old navigable hazard (securing official Coast Guard transit for the clinic case while exposing Doyle's delayed report and triggering federal ferry impoundment).
3. **The 23:20 Slack-Water Commitment (Plot 3):** Whether they launch [piece-work-skiff.md](piece-work-skiff.md) together along the west fender line with Doyle conning past the submerged piling, call Coast Guard over VHF with the Envelope in hand, stand down from launching to preserve life/hull at the cost of the unassisted crossing, or part on adversarial terms before the **23:35** flood turn.
