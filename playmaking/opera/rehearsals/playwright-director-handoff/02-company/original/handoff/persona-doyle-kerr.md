# Doyle Kerr

## Taxonomy

Twenty-year coastal diesel officer holding [Licensed Chief Marine Engineer](position-chief-engineer.md) on standby suspension. Pursues [Recover the Bell-Log Envelope](plan-recover-bell-log.md).

## Owner

[Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md)

## Projection

Unhurried, salt-bitten mechanical authority: heavy wool watch cap, grease worked permanently into knuckle creases, a 14-inch flat pry bar held low against his thigh like a routine shop tool rather than a burglar's iron. He speaks in clearances, tide heights, and metal fatigue as if patience alone keeps amateurs alive.

## Action

Places himself physically between a hasty hand and the gear—citing an exact measurement from the tide board, hoist chain, or hull gauge—to slow the room down and make the other person ask what he knows before touching a control.

## Shadow

Tells himself five days of paid silence were just pragmatic seamanship to let a local ferry line survive till Friday's private salvage tug—refusing to face that leaving a sheared, iron-bolted piling unmarked in Marrow Slip has already turned him from reluctant witness into accomplice.

## Tell

Whenever Coast Guard reporting rules, marine licenses, or Friday night's crossing time (**21:14**) come near the surface, he scrubs the back of his scarred left knuckles slowly across his jawbone as if wiping diesel soot he cannot get off.
