# Maren Holt

## Taxonomy

Mainland shore courier and former relief purser filling the [Bonded Shore Agent](position-bonded-agent.md) role. Pursues [Slack-Water Crossing](plan-slack-water-crossing.md).

## Owner

[Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md)

## Projection

Foul-weather poise and brisk waterfront competence: oilskin cuffs snapped tight, headlamp angled down so it never blinds another worker, setting the [Sealed Dispensary Cold-Chain Case](piece-cold-chain-case.md) level on dry timber before speaking. She acts as though the closed terminal is merely an inconvenient night shift she still has every right to run.

## Action

Steps straight to the physical threshold—testing a lock cylinder with the [Harbour Master Key](piece-master-key.md), laying a pen beside a manifest sheet, or checking a hoist pin—to force whoever is in the room to either help her move or state aloud why they are standing in her way.

## Shadow

Believes that because she is willing to take the cold water and dark crossing on her own back for Kestrel Island's clinic, her unauthorized entry costs nobody else anything—ignoring how turning an unreturned key after suspension entangles bystanders, co-signers, and whoever shares the room when the **07:00** receiver audit arrives.

## Tell

Inside her right coat pocket—or openly at her side once drawn—she presses the pad of her thumb hard against the serrated brass teeth of the [Harbour Master Key](piece-master-key.md) whenever someone's timeline or excuse fails to add up, leaving pale notches across her skin.
