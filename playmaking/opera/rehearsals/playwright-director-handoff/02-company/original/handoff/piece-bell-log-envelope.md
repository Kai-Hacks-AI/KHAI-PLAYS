# Friday Engine-Bell & Fathometer Envelope

## Taxonomy

Shipboard casualty record created under [Licensed Chief Marine Engineer](position-chief-engineer.md) within [Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md).

## Owner

[Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md)

## Place

Locked inside the bolted steel Night Drop Box on the wall of [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md).

## Load Bearing

Contains the only contemporaneous physical proof that *M/V Gullswake* struck the east guide-crib at **21:14 Nov 20** while ordered underway radar-blind on an expired hull extension—and establishes the exact depth trace of the sheared piling blocking Marrow Slip. If company adjusters clear it at **07:00**, management can bury the casualty or pin fault on the engine room; if produced to authorities, it proves the wreck while timestamping [Doyle Kerr](persona-doyle-kerr.md)'s five-day delay in calling Coast Guard.

## Apparent

Nine-by-twelve manila clasp envelope stiffened with folded thermal chart paper, thumb-printed with black marine diesel grease and marked across the flap in indelible marker: `M/V GULLSWAKE — ENG LOG 20 NOV — HOLD FOR MASTER`.

## Yearbook

- **Friday, Nov 20, 21:14:** Thermal fathometer strip records a hard shoal spike off the east crib piling simultaneously with a full-astern bell order in *Gullswake*'s engine room.
- **Friday, Nov 20, 23:52:** Ordered onto paid standby before surrendering his terminal keys, [Doyle Kerr](persona-doyle-kerr.md) seals the signed bell carbon and fathometer tear-sheet inside the envelope and drops it through the brass slot into [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md).
- **Wednesday, Nov 25, 22:40:** Still lying unseen at the bottom of the steel Night Drop Box ahead of Thursday's **07:00** adjuster cleanout.
