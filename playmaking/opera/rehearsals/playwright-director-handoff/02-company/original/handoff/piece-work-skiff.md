# Slipway Work Skiff

## Taxonomy

Cooperative harbor utility craft governed from [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md) and [Covered Slipway Apron](place-slipway-apron.md) within [Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md).

## Owner

[Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md)

## Place

Suspended six feet above the center launch cradle on twin chain davits in [Covered Slipway Apron](place-slipway-apron.md).

## Load Bearing

The sole operable boat inside the locked mainland terminal tonight. Lowered straight down the center cradle at `-0.6 ft` low water, its `.100`-gauge riveted aluminum bottom drives directly onto the sheared iron-bolted east piling thirty feet off the ramp toe; walked slowly along the west fender line during the **23:20–23:35** slack, it can clear the obstruction only if guided past the pile bearing.

## Apparent

Unpainted 16-foot square-bow aluminum work skiff stenciled `CO-OP TENDER #1`, fitted with a manual-start 25-hp outboard, two cedar oars lashed under the thwarts, a brass garboard plug wired to the transom, and a portable red tank sloshing half full.

## Yearbook

- **September 2025:** Hauled onto the slipway davits as the cooperative's winter line-handling and emergency freight tender.
- **Friday, Nov 20, 23:45:** Hoisted six feet clear of the tide and chained at the drum inside [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md) when *M/V Gullswake* was towed behind Kestrel Island.
- **Wednesday, Nov 25, 22:40:** Hanging dry under the corrugated roof as [Ebb Tide to Low-Water Slack](process-ebb-to-slack.md) pulls the water down toward the unmarked piling beneath its bow.
