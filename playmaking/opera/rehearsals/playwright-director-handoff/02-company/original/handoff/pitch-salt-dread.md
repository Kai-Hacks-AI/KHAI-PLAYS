# Salt Dread

## Taxonomy

Play-wide default Pitch for [Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md), governing [Plot 1 — The Threshold](plot-01-the-threshold.md) and [Plot 2 — The Night Drop](plot-02-the-night-drop.md).

## Owner

[Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md)

## Tenor

**Dread** — quiet maritime foreboding: sleet ticking on dark glass, brass locks clicking in an unheated building, and black ebb water dropping inch by inch toward timber and iron hidden just below the chop.

## Undertow

Wary working-waterfront solidarity—two people who know cold rope, bad bosses, and winter islands recognizing that neither made this mess from greed, even while checking each other's hands.

## Nerve

Taut, breathless calculation: the sense that every unlocked latch, switched breaker, or half-told truth narrows the room and brings the hidden wreck closer to the hull.

## Echo

The sound of falling tidewater sucking around an unmarked piling in the dark while shore lights stay off.
