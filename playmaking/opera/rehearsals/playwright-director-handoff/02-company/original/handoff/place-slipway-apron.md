# Covered Slipway Apron

## Taxonomy

Waterside launch deck beyond the [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md), opening directly onto Marrow Narrows within [Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md).

## Owner

[Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md)

## Shown

Sleet drumming deafeningly on high corrugated iron; the clank of taut davit chains swaying in wind gusts; black water sucking down barnacle-crusted pilings under [Ebb Tide to Low-Water Slack](process-ebb-to-slack.md); white-painted tide numerals bolted to the west timber dolphin (`+1.4` at 22:40, dropping toward `-0.6` at 23:20).

## Holds

- [Slipway Work Skiff](piece-work-skiff.md) suspended six feet clear of the water on twin chain davits over the center launch cradle.
- Coiled three-quarter-inch polypropylene hauling line bent to the west fender-wall cleats.
- Manual davit brake pin and boarding ladder bolted inside the west catwalk.

## Offers

- The only sheltered small-craft launch point inside the locked harbour during the **23:20–23:35** slack-water window, reachable to anyone who has unlatched the [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md) waterside door and davit pawl.

## Withheld

- Safe center-channel passage and unlit sight of the wreck hazard: thirty feet off the ramp toe along the center-east track, the sheared iron-bolted guide-piling sits eight to ten inches beneath the `-0.6 ft` chop—invisible in the dark unless the [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md) sodium floodlight is switched on at dead low, and passable without hull rupture only by walking the [Slipway Work Skiff](piece-work-skiff.md) tight along the west fender line with two coordinated hands or exact local bearings.
