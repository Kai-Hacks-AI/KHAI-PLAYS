# Marrow Slip Waiting Room

## Taxonomy

Landside entry hall of Marrow Slip Terminal, fronting the [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md) within [Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md).

## Owner

[Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md)

## Shown

Sleet rattling hard against salt-frosted clerestory panes; the sour smell of wet creosote rising through floorboards over the ebb tide; a laminated notice taped inside the front glass reading *"SERVICE SUSPENDED — RAMP HYDRAULIC INSPECTION"*; fresh white wood splinters and flat-iron pry marks along the exterior transom latch.

## Holds

- Curved cedar passenger benches bolted to cracked linoleum.
- Wall-mounted coinless shore payphone (live dial tone for local mainland exchanges only; island underwater tie-line clicks dead).
- Heavy steel Dutch door with a brass mortise cylinder separating the Waiting Room from the [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md).

## Offers

- Dry cover out of the northeast squall and an unobstructed sightline through frosted side glass toward the empty parking apron and padlocked municipal road gate.
- Clean, undamaged entry from the leeward porch to whoever carries the [Harbour Master Key](piece-master-key.md).

## Withheld

- Every working control of the terminal—the Night Drop Box, VHF radio, floodlight panel, manifest pad, and davit hoist inside the [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md). Reaching them requires visibly turning the [Harbour Master Key](piece-master-key.md) in the Dutch door's brass mortise lock or loud, destructive prying that splinters the jamb before **07:00** inspection.
