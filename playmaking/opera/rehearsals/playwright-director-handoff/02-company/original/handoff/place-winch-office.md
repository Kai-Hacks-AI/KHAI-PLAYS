# Ticket & Winch Office

## Taxonomy

Inner operations cage between [Marrow Slip Waiting Room](place-waiting-room.md) and the [Covered Slipway Apron](place-slipway-apron.md) within [Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md).

## Owner

[Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md)

## Shown

Cold smell of heavy gear grease and damp carbon paper; a narrow counter overlooking the dark slipway through salt-streaked glass; the unlit red indicator lamp above the marine breaker panel; the brass drop slot above a quarter-inch steel Night Drop Box bolted straight through the wall studs.

## Holds

- Bolted steel Night Drop Box containing the [Friday Engine-Bell & Fathometer Envelope](piece-bell-log-envelope.md), keyed to the [Harbour Master Key](piece-master-key.md).
- 25-watt Harbour VHF Base Radio (tuned to Channel 16 / 22A, currently dark at the wall breaker) and adjacent Slipway Floodlight toggle.
- Primary geared chain-hoist drum and ratchet pawl governing the [Slipway Work Skiff](piece-work-skiff.md) outside on the [Covered Slipway Apron](place-slipway-apron.md).
- Cooperative carbon manifest pad and corded ballpoint pen clipped to the ticket wicket.

## Offers

- Immediate VHF Channel 16 reach to Sector Coast Guard once the radio breaker is thrown.
- High-wattage sodium floodlight illumination across the slip mouth and painted tide board.
- Mechanical leverage to slacken or lock the davit hoist chain from under cover.

## Withheld

- Physical access to the [Friday Engine-Bell & Fathometer Envelope](piece-bell-log-envelope.md) inside the welded Night Drop Box unless someone turns the [Harbour Master Key](piece-master-key.md) in its cylinder.
- Secrecy once power is used: throwing the floodlight breaker lights the closed terminal roof visibly from the harbour road, and keying VHF Channel 16 puts a timestamped transmission on Sector Coast Guard's tape.
