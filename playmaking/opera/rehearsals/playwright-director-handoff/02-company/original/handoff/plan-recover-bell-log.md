# Recover the Bell-Log Envelope

## Taxonomy

Individual evidence-recovery and damage-containment plan within [Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md).

## Owner

[Doyle Kerr](persona-doyle-kerr.md) in [Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md)

## Direction

Gain entry to [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md), retrieve the [Friday Engine-Bell & Fathometer Envelope](piece-bell-log-envelope.md) from the Night Drop Box before company adjusters empty it at **07:00**, and keep anyone from launching blindly onto the sheared east guide-piling—ideally without triggering an immediate Coast Guard casualty investigation into his five-day silence under [Licensed Chief Marine Engineer](position-chief-engineer.md).

## Orders

- **[Doyle Kerr](persona-doyle-kerr.md) ([Licensed Chief Marine Engineer](position-chief-engineer.md)):** Get past the locked Dutch door into [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md) (originally by pry bar; once [Maren Holt](persona-maren-holt.md) arrives, by persuading or bargaining for her [#2 Brass Harbour Master Key](piece-master-key.md)), open the bolted Night Drop Box, secure the [Friday Engine-Bell & Fathometer Envelope](piece-bell-log-envelope.md), and prevent an unguided center-cradle launch of the [Slipway Work Skiff](piece-work-skiff.md).

## Implementation

- Lacks a key; forcing the steel Dutch door or welded quarter-inch Night Drop Box with a 14-inch pry bar is slow, loud, and leaves unmistakable break-in scars.
- Cannot stop [Maren Holt](persona-maren-holt.md) from lowering the [Slipway Work Skiff](piece-work-skiff.md) at **23:20** on vague warnings alone; disclosing the sheared piling to save her hull puts his five-day cover-up and the Harbour VHF Channel 16 radio into her reach, colliding head-on with [Slack-Water Crossing](plan-slack-water-crossing.md).

## Targets

- [ ] Gain entry to [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md) and open the Night Drop Box before **07:00**.
- [ ] Take physical custody of the [Friday Engine-Bell & Fathometer Envelope](piece-bell-log-envelope.md) so management cannot destroy it or alter fault.
- [ ] Prevent the [Slipway Work Skiff](piece-work-skiff.md) from striking the submerged east guide-piling during [Ebb Tide to Low-Water Slack](process-ebb-to-slack.md).
- [ ] Avoid an unshielded VHF Channel 16 report that exposes five days of unreported hazard without the log in his hands.
