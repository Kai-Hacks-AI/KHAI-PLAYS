## Taxonomy

## Implementation

- Must keep the [Sealed Dispensary Cold-Chain Case](piece-cold-chain-case.md) dry and unbroken below `8.0°C` (**01:00** limit).
- Must launch within the **23:20–23:35** slack window before the flood tide turns into the slip.
- Assumes at outset that the slipway approach is clear water and that only ferry hydraulic repairs—not a submerged channel obstruction—halted *M/V Gullswake*; collides directly with [Recover the Bell-Log Envelope](plan-recover-bell-log.md) the moment [Doyle Kerr](persona-doyle-kerr.md) blocks the hoist or reveals the sheared east piling.
