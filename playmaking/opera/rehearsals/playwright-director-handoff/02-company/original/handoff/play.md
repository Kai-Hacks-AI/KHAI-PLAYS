# Slack Water at Marrow Slip

## Estate

Authored locally for **Marrow Point Stage** as a standalone original production. All Company members, Plots, Plans, and Pitches belong to this work; no external Repertoire collections are borrowed. The Director selects or authors the governing Promptbook when staging a run.

## Name

*Slack Water at Marrow Slip*

## Arc

Three consecutive thresholds carry two shore workers from the sleet-swept exterior of a closed mainland ferry terminal down to the black water of its obstructed launch slip over fifty-five minutes (**22:40–23:35**):

1. **[Plot 1 — The Threshold](plot-01-the-threshold.md)** (*22:40–23:00*, [Marrow Slip Waiting Room](place-waiting-room.md)): An arrival collision over physical access. The woman with the key catches the crewman without one at a scored window; neither can pass the inner steel Dutch door cleanly without reckoning with the other's presence.
2. **[Plot 2 — The Night Drop](plot-02-the-night-drop.md)** (*23:00–23:20*, [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md)): A custody and disclosure reckoning. Unlocking the inner cage puts the bolted Night Drop Box, the dark VHF Channel 16 radio, the slipway floodlight, and the skiff hoist drum in the same room—forcing Friday night's concealed collision out from behind the fake *"RAMP HYDRAULIC INSPECTION"* sign.
3. **[Plot 3 — Slack Water](plot-03-slack-water.md)** (*23:20–23:35*, [Covered Slipway Apron](place-slipway-apron.md)): An irreversible fifteen-minute commitment at dead low water (`-0.6 ft`), when the current slacks enough to cross while dropping the surface to inches above a sheared iron-bolted piling—requiring both performers to settle how the boat, the radio, and the casualty record meet the **23:35** flood turn.

Their order turns an apparent trespass dispute (Plot 1) into an evidentiary standoff with live communication switches (Plot 2), and finally into a physical seamanship and conscience test under a closing tidal clock (Plot 3).

## Triggers

- **Fixed Opening Conditions (22:40 Wednesday, Nov 25):** Five nights earlier (**21:14 Friday, Nov 20**), *M/V Gullswake* ran radar-blind on an expired hull extension, struck the outer east guide-crib, hid behind Kestrel Island to conceal an uninsured casualty, and sheared an iron-bolted piling stub across the slip approach at `-0.2 ft` datum. Management posted *"SERVICE SUSPENDED — RAMP HYDRAULIC INSPECTION"* and collected crew keys; before leaving at **23:52 Friday**, Doyle dropped [Friday Engine-Bell & Fathometer Envelope](piece-bell-log-envelope.md) into the Night Drop Box. At **22:40 Wednesday**, Maren believes only ramp hydraulics failed; Doyle knows the wreck hazard and bearings, though he cannot know whether the Envelope is still inside the Drop Box until it is unlocked.
- **Start of [Plot 1 — The Threshold](plot-01-the-threshold.md) (22:40):** Triggered as [Maren Holt](persona-maren-holt.md) arrives at [Marrow Slip Waiting Room](place-waiting-room.md) with [#2 Brass Harbour Master Key](piece-master-key.md) and [Sealed Dispensary Cold-Chain Case](piece-cold-chain-case.md), catching [Doyle Kerr](persona-doyle-kerr.md) with his pry bar beside the scored transom (`+1.4 ft` ebb).
- **Handoff 1 -> 2 (~23:00):** Triggered when Maren unlocks the steel Dutch door into [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md) (admitting Doyle on terms, contesting entry, or testing his warning), when Doyle forces the jamb, or as the tide crosses `+0.3 ft`.
- **Discovery Gates in [Plot 2 — The Night Drop](plot-02-the-night-drop.md) (23:00–23:20):** Entering [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md) exposes the Night Drop Box, dark VHF/floodlight breakers, manifest pad, and hoist drum. Turning [#2 Brass Harbour Master Key](piece-master-key.md) in the Night Drop Box reveals [Friday Engine-Bell & Fathometer Envelope](piece-bell-log-envelope.md) intact inside and allows either Persona to read the **21:14 Nov 20** bell carbon and fathometer spike.
- **Handoff 2 -> 3 (23:20):** Triggered when the dolphin tide board reaches `-0.6 ft` (start of low-water slack) and either Persona opens the waterside door / unlatches the davit pawl onto [Covered Slipway Apron](place-slipway-apron.md), keys VHF Channel 16, or forces a final custody stand at the water's edge.
- **Discovery & Curtain in [Plot 3 — Slack Water](plot-03-slack-water.md) (23:20–23:35):** Switching on the Slipway Floodlight at `-0.6 ft` visibly reveals the eddy and rusted drift-bolt of the sheared east piling thirty feet off the ramp toe. The Play ends at **23:35** when the flood tide surges back into Marrow Slip—or earlier once an irreversible launch, Coast Guard broadcast, or departure locks the night's outcome. All performer bargains, disclosures, custody splits, and launch/radio choices remain open.

## Stakes

The production fights over **who absorbs the cost when working people inherit an institutional cover-up—and whether urgent care for an isolated island is bought by risking a blind dark crossing or blowing open a wreck that can sink a ferry line and an engineer's license**:

- **In [Plot 1 — The Threshold](plot-01-the-threshold.md):** Custody of the threshold and [Maren Holt](persona-maren-holt.md)'s [Bonded Shore Agent](position-bonded-agent.md) surety bond collide with [Doyle Kerr](persona-doyle-kerr.md)'s need to get inside without leaving a crowbarred door—putting [#2 Brass Harbour Master Key](piece-master-key.md) at stake.
- **In [Plot 2 — The Night Drop](plot-02-the-night-drop.md):** Possession of [Friday Engine-Bell & Fathometer Envelope](piece-bell-log-envelope.md) and control of the Harbour VHF Channel 16 switch put Doyle's [Licensed Chief Marine Engineer](position-chief-engineer.md) credential and the ferry company's survival directly against official Coast Guard intervention for Kestrel Island.
- **In [Plot 3 — Slack Water](plot-03-slack-water.md):** The hull of [Slipway Work Skiff](piece-work-skiff.md), the **01:00** viability of [Sealed Dispensary Cold-Chain Case](piece-cold-chain-case.md), and two human lives on black water hang on the fifteen-minute window of [Ebb Tide to Low-Water Slack](process-ebb-to-slack.md) before **23:35** closes the narrows and **07:00** brings the morning reckoning.
