# Plot 1 — The Threshold

## Taxonomy

Opening Plot of [Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md), tuned by default to [Salt Dread](pitch-salt-dread.md).

## Owner

[Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md)

## Cue

**Wednesday, Nov 25, 22:40** at [Marrow Slip Waiting Room](place-waiting-room.md). Sleet lashes the dark clerestory glass above the laminated *"SERVICE SUSPENDED — RAMP HYDRAULIC INSPECTION"* placard. Underneath the floorboards, [Ebb Tide to Low-Water Slack](process-ebb-to-slack.md) tears seaward at `+1.4 ft`—leaving forty minutes until the **23:20** slack-water window and two hours and twenty minutes on the [Sealed Dispensary Cold-Chain Case](piece-cold-chain-case.md).

## Action

- **[Maren Holt](persona-maren-holt.md)** (holding [Bonded Shore Agent](position-bonded-agent.md), pursuing [Slack-Water Crossing](plan-slack-water-crossing.md)) drives straight toward unlocking the steel Dutch door into [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md) so she can log her cargo and prep the [Slipway Work Skiff](piece-work-skiff.md)—demanding that [Doyle Kerr](persona-doyle-kerr.md) either account for the fresh pry marks on the transom and step clear of her bond, or state plain business.
- **[Doyle Kerr](persona-doyle-kerr.md)** (holding [Licensed Chief Marine Engineer](position-chief-engineer.md), pursuing [Recover the Bell-Log Envelope](plan-recover-bell-log.md)) drives toward getting Maren's [#2 Brass Harbour Master Key](piece-master-key.md) turned in that same Dutch door *with himself included* while talking her off any plan to drop the skiff tonight—colliding at the brass mortise lock, which she will not open for an unexplained break-in and he cannot let her pass through toward a blind launch.

## Stage

1. **Fixed at Rise (22:40):** [Doyle Kerr](persona-doyle-kerr.md) stands under the leeward porch eaves beside the scored exterior transom latch, carrying a 14-inch flat pry bar and no key. The inner Dutch door to [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md) is locked.
2. **Entrance:** [Maren Holt](persona-maren-holt.md) rounds the corner from the footpath carrying the [Sealed Dispensary Cold-Chain Case](piece-cold-chain-case.md) by its handle and the [#2 Brass Harbour Master Key](piece-master-key.md) in her right oilskin pocket, catching Doyle in the spill of her angled headlamp before unlocking the Waiting Room side door.
3. **In Reach:** Once inside [Marrow Slip Waiting Room](place-waiting-room.md), both can see the laminated hydraulic-inspection placard, the fresh wood splinters at the transom, the shore-only payphone (island tie-line dead), and the locked Dutch door. Neither can see inside the Night Drop Box or inspect the slipway tide board closely until the Dutch door opens.
4. **Handoff Cue to Plot 2 (~23:00):** Closes when [Maren Holt](persona-maren-holt.md) turns the [#2 Brass Harbour Master Key](piece-master-key.md) in the Dutch door (on shared terms, conditional bargain, or contested entry), when Doyle forces the lock, or if Maren refuses entry past **23:00** as the ebb drops toward `+0.3 ft`.

## Tension

- **[Maren Holt](persona-maren-holt.md)** assumes *M/V Gullswake* is down-coast for routine hydraulic cylinder work and does not yet know her cooperative's #2 key was overlooked during Friday's crew key seizure—or that any turn of her key tonight lands squarely on tomorrow's **07:00** receiver audit alongside Doyle's pry marks.
- **[Doyle Kerr](persona-doyle-kerr.md)** knows the hydraulic placard is a company lie hiding Friday's **21:14** crib strike and the sheared iron-bolted piling across the slip mouth, and privately fears Superintendent Halvorsen may already have emptied the [Friday Engine-Bell & Fathometer Envelope](piece-bell-log-envelope.md) from the Drop Box—keeping him reluctant to confess a five-day cover-up before he knows whether the proof still exists behind that door.
