# Plot 2 — The Night Drop

## Taxonomy

Middle Plot of [Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md), following [Plot 1 — The Threshold](plot-01-the-threshold.md) and tuned by default to [Salt Dread](pitch-salt-dread.md).

## Owner

[Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md)

## Cue

**Wednesday, Nov 25, ~23:00** inside [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md). [Ebb Tide to Low-Water Slack](process-ebb-to-slack.md) pulls the narrows past `+0.3 ft` toward dead low twenty minutes away. Every contested lever sits within arm's reach in one cramped cage: the cooperative manifest pad, the dark Harbour VHF Base Radio and Slipway Floodlight breakers, the geared chain-hoist drum holding the [Slipway Work Skiff](piece-work-skiff.md), and the bolted steel Night Drop Box keyed to Maren's [#2 Brass Harbour Master Key](piece-master-key.md).

## Action

- **[Maren Holt](persona-maren-holt.md)** ([Bonded Shore Agent](position-bonded-agent.md), [Slack-Water Crossing](plan-slack-water-crossing.md)) moves to log the [Sealed Dispensary Cold-Chain Case](piece-cold-chain-case.md) on the wicket pad and free the davit hoist chain for the **23:20** launch—using her hold on the [#2 Brass Harbour Master Key](piece-master-key.md) and the VHF Channel 16 breaker to extract the whole truth of why Doyle is desperate to open the Night Drop Box and keep her off the water.
- **[Doyle Kerr](persona-doyle-kerr.md)** ([Licensed Chief Marine Engineer](position-chief-engineer.md), [Recover the Bell-Log Envelope](plan-recover-bell-log.md)) drives to get the Night Drop Box unlocked, put his hands on the [Friday Engine-Bell & Fathometer Envelope](piece-bell-log-envelope.md), and stop Maren from releasing the center-cradle hoist—colliding over who holds the Envelope once opened and whether the VHF radio or floodlight breaker gets thrown.

## Stage

1. **Entry State (~23:00):** Both Personas enter (or contest the doorway of) [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md) from whatever terms ended [Plot 1 — The Threshold](plot-01-the-threshold.md). [Maren Holt](persona-maren-holt.md) brings the [#2 Brass Harbour Master Key](piece-master-key.md) and [Sealed Dispensary Cold-Chain Case](piece-cold-chain-case.md); [Doyle Kerr](persona-doyle-kerr.md) brings his pry bar and exact bearings of Friday's wreck.
2. **Discovery — The Night Drop Box:** If the [#2 Brass Harbour Master Key](piece-master-key.md) turns the Night Drop Box cylinder, both discover the [Friday Engine-Bell & Fathometer Envelope](piece-bell-log-envelope.md) is still inside, untouched since **23:52 Friday**. Opening its clasp lets either read the signed **21:14 Nov 20** full-astern bell carbon and thermal fathometer spike off the east crib piling.
3. **Power & Radio Choices in Reach:** Throwing the VHF breaker powers Channel 16 (audible squelch and live Sector Coast Guard watch, though no transmission goes out unless someone keys the mic); throwing the Slipway Floodlight toggle lights the [Covered Slipway Apron](place-slipway-apron.md) and the harbour roofline simultaneously.
4. **Handoff Cue to Plot 3 (23:20):** Closes as the painted dolphin gauge outside hits `-0.6` (**23:20** low-water slack begins) and either Persona unlatches the waterside door / davit pawl toward [Covered Slipway Apron](place-slipway-apron.md), keys VHF Channel 16, or locks custody of the Envelope and key for a final stand at the slip edge.

## Tension

- **[Doyle Kerr](persona-doyle-kerr.md)** faces the trap inside his own proof: the [Friday Engine-Bell & Fathometer Envelope](piece-bell-log-envelope.md) exonerates him of Friday's radar-blind helm strike, *yet* handing it to Coast Guard tonight—after five days of paid silence—exposes his failure under [Licensed Chief Marine Engineer](position-chief-engineer.md) to report a submerged channel spike promptly.
- **[Maren Holt](persona-maren-holt.md)** realizes that if the slip is genuinely blocked by an unmarked piling, a solo center-cradle drop destroys the [Slipway Work Skiff](piece-work-skiff.md) and the [Sealed Dispensary Cold-Chain Case](piece-cold-chain-case.md) alike—meaning her only remaining paths before **01:00** are forcing a federal hazard call on VHF Channel 16 (burning the ferry company and putting Doyle's ticket on the block) or trusting Doyle on the dark apron to walk the hull down the west fender line together.
