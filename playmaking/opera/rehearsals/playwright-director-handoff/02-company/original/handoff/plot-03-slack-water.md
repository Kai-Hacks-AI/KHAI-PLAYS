# Plot 3 — Slack Water

## Taxonomy

Closing Plot of [Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md), following [Plot 2 — The Night Drop](plot-02-the-night-drop.md) and calling [Slack Resolve](pitch-slack-resolve.md).

## Owner

[Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md)

## Cue

**Wednesday, Nov 25, 23:20** on [Covered Slipway Apron](place-slipway-apron.md), with the waterside door open back into [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md). [Ebb Tide to Low-Water Slack](process-ebb-to-slack.md) reaches dead low (`-0.6 ft` on the west dolphin board): the four-knot rip falls eerie and flat under drumming sleet, opening a fifteen-minute crossing window until the **23:35** flood turn while leaving barely eight inches of black chop over the sheared iron-bolted east guide-piling thirty feet off the ramp toe.

## Action

- **[Maren Holt](persona-maren-holt.md)** ([Bonded Shore Agent](position-bonded-agent.md), [Slack-Water Crossing](plan-slack-water-crossing.md)) drives for an irrevocable, survivable commitment before **23:35**—either putting the [Slipway Work Skiff](piece-work-skiff.md) safely past the west fender wall with the [Sealed Dispensary Cold-Chain Case](piece-cold-chain-case.md) aboard, or triggering an official Coast Guard hazard-and-freight response backed by hard proof.
- **[Doyle Kerr](persona-doyle-kerr.md)** ([Licensed Chief Marine Engineer](position-chief-engineer.md), [Recover the Bell-Log Envelope](plan-recover-bell-log.md)) drives to decide what his twenty-year ticket is actually for when a hull and a crewmate's trust hit the water—either physically taking the davit brake / west hauling line (or helm) to conn past the piling he left unmarked, standing beside a Channel 16 hazard report, or refusing launch and walking out with whatever custody of the [Friday Engine-Bell & Fathometer Envelope](piece-bell-log-envelope.md) he has won.

## Stage

1. **Entry State (23:20):** Both Personas carry forward the exact custody state of [#2 Brass Harbour Master Key](piece-master-key.md), [Sealed Dispensary Cold-Chain Case](piece-cold-chain-case.md), and [Friday Engine-Bell & Fathometer Envelope](piece-bell-log-envelope.md), plus whatever breaker state (floodlight / VHF) was set in [Plot 2 — The Night Drop](plot-02-the-night-drop.md).
2. **Visual Corroboration at Dead Low (23:20–23:30):** If the Slipway Floodlight is on (or switched on now), a swirling flat boil and two inches of rusted iron drift-bolt break the trough thirty feet off the center-east ramp toe—confirming in plain sight that a straight cradle launch guts the [Slipway Work Skiff](piece-work-skiff.md), while showing narrow dark clearance tight against the west fender cleats.
3. **Mechanical Constraints on Launch:** Dropping the [Slipway Work Skiff](piece-work-skiff.md) straight off the davits without tending the west fender hauling line drives the bow onto the east piling; clearing the west wall requires two coordinated moves (one easing the davit brake / hauling line while the other fends or cons past the thirty-foot mark) or aborting the launch in favor of VHF Channel 16 / shore retreat.
4. **Fixed Closing Boundary (23:35):** At **23:35**, the first flood surge slaps loud against the outer pilings and sets drift chop back into the slip mouth, closing unassisted small-craft launch for the night and ending the Play on the irreversible state achieved.

## Tension

- **[Maren Holt](persona-maren-holt.md)** must confront her blind spot under [Bonded Shore Agent](position-bonded-agent.md): she cannot cross Marrow Narrows cleanly "on her own back" tonight—either she shares real physical or legal jeopardy with [Doyle Kerr](persona-doyle-kerr.md), or she prioritizes principle/safety at the risk of the **01:00** clinic ceiling.
- **[Doyle Kerr](persona-doyle-kerr.md)** knows that whether he steps into the [Slipway Work Skiff](piece-work-skiff.md), keys VHF Channel 16 himself, or walks back up the dark road holding the [Friday Engine-Bell & Fathometer Envelope](piece-bell-log-envelope.md), the five-day fiction that he was merely "waiting for Friday's tug" ends when the **23:35** flood comes in.
