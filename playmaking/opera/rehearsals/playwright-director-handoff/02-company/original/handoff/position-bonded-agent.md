# Bonded Shore Agent

## Taxonomy

Mainland shore-custody role for the Kestrel Island Fishermen's & Clinic Cooperative within [Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md).

## Owner

[Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md)

## Has

- Authorised custody of a numbered [Harbour Master Key](piece-master-key.md) for off-hours freight handling at [Marrow Slip Waiting Room](place-waiting-room.md) and [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md).
- Signature authority on the cooperative's carbon manifest pad to log perishable clinic cargo such as the [Sealed Dispensary Cold-Chain Case](piece-cold-chain-case.md) and dispatch the [Slipway Work Skiff](piece-work-skiff.md) on cooperative business.

## Orders

- Record time, seal number, and key number on the Winch Office manifest pad whenever unlocking the terminal or moving cooperative equipment after hours.
- Deliver signed cold-chain cargo within thermal tolerance or return it unbroken to mainland cold storage before spoilage.
- Surrender keys, logs, and equipment tallies intact at the cooperative's receiver audit (**07:00 Thursday**).

## Loses

- Personal $15,000 surety bond and island cooperative standing if terminal locks are forced, unlogged outsiders enter under the agent's key, or the [Slipway Work Skiff](piece-work-skiff.md) is damaged in an unrecorded launch.
- Plausible deniability: any turn of the #2 key after Friday's terminal closure ties that night's events directly to the holder's name at the morning audit.

## Drives

Pushes whoever holds it to treat urgent island deliveries as legitimate work rather than trespass—insisting on clean paper, signed receipts, and making the crossing on schedule even when normal ferry service has collapsed.
