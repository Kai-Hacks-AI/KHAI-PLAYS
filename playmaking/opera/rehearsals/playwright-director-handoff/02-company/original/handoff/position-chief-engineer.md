# Licensed Chief Marine Engineer

## Taxonomy

Certificated vessel officer standing in the Kestrel Crossing ferry service within [Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md).

## Owner

[Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md)

## Has

- Statutory authority to record engine-room bell orders, hull casualties, and machinery defects in the vessel's official log—as captured in the [Friday Engine-Bell & Fathometer Envelope](piece-bell-log-envelope.md).
- Technical command of propulsion, ballast state, and slipway winch loads, plus recognized standing with Sector Coast Guard on VHF Channel 16 when reporting a marine casualty or navigable hazard.

## Orders

- Log every helm-to-engine bell order accurately and preserve casualty records against alteration.
- Report any grounding, structural strike, or submerged obstruction in a navigable approach to maritime authorities without delay, even when vessel owners order paid standby silence.

## Loses

- Shore keys and active watch access once placed on company standby.
- Coast Guard merchant mariner credential and pension eligibility if an inquiry establishes the engineer concealed an unmarked channel hazard—or career employment on the coast if the engineer blows open an uninsured wreck that liquidates the employer.

## Drives

Pulls the holder between self-preservation and seamanship: protecting the written record that proves the bridge—not the engine room—drove a defective vessel into the crib, while dreading both a sunken boat on an unmarked piling and the federal inquiry that follows a late report.
