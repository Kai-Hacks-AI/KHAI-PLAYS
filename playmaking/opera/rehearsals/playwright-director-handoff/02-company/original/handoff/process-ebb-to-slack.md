# Ebb Tide to Low-Water Slack

## Taxonomy

Semidiurnal coastal tidal cycle through Marrow Narrows shaping [Covered Slipway Apron](place-slipway-apron.md) within [Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md).

## Owner

[Slack Water at Marrow Slip](play.md)

## Initiated by

The play opens at **22:40** with a four-knot spring ebb sucking seaward under the terminal pilings; anyone looking out from [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md) or [Covered Slipway Apron](place-slipway-apron.md) can read `+1.4` on the west dolphin tide board and hear black water tearing past the ramp toe.

## Direction

Draws the water level down two vertical feet over forty minutes (`+1.4 ft` at **22:40** -> `+0.3 ft` at **23:00** -> `-0.6 ft` chart datum across the **23:20–23:35** slack window), killing the four-knot rip so a small outboard can cross Marrow Narrows, while simultaneously dropping the surface until only eight to ten inches of sleet-chop cover the sheared iron-bolted east guide-piling—before reversing at **23:35** into a rising flood tide that drives chop and drift ice hard back into the slip.

## Lever

Driven by lunar spring tide and northeast wind setup; nothing in human hands can slow or halt the water itself. Performers can act *around* its visibility and geometry: throwing the [Ticket & Winch Office](place-winch-office.md) floodlight toggle at `-0.6 ft` reveals the dark eddy and rusted drift-bolt breaking the trough thirty feet off the ramp toe, and working the davit hoist and west fender hauling line during the fifteen-minute slack alters where the [Slipway Work Skiff](piece-work-skiff.md) meets the channel.

## Echo

Once **23:35** passes and the flood rip sets into the slip mouth, unassisted small-skiff launch closes until morning light, the [Sealed Dispensary Cold-Chain Case](piece-cold-chain-case.md) is trapped ashore unless Coast Guard transit has been summoned, and the submerged piling slips back out of visual reach under rising dark water.
