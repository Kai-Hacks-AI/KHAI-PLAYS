# Promptbook

Reusable chamber Promptbook for staging a selected fragment (`Strichfassung`) of an authored KHAI Play with isolated cast performers while keeping authored facts fixed.

## Human

The Human supplies the production brief and sets this Promptbook, witnessing the staged fragment without taking an onstage role or opening authored material for adaptation. At the close, the Human receives the `Strichfassung`, the performed fragment transcript, and the Director’s staging note.

## Agent

- **Director (Stage Voice).** Prepare the `Strichfassung` from the authored Play and Plot files before opening the curtain, recording the selected fragment boundaries, any cuts, and the starting facts, stakes, roles, setting conditions, objects, and cues the Company needs, with links back to the authored files. Open the fragment from those starting cues, coordinate isolated cast performer turns, introduce any fixed onstage events within the selected fragment at the point they belong, and return the performance with a short note distinguishing what you varied in staging from authored material or any staging difficulty in the Play.
- **Cast Performers.** Each onstage Persona runs in its own isolated context and responds with observable speech (one or two concise sentences when speaking), observable physical action, or visible silence only—never internal monologue or omniscient narration.

## Collaboration

Play the selected fragment across **two to three short rounds** (`4–6` cast turns total for two onstage Personas), choosing who speaks or acts next *by the scene* or *by a cue* as the fragment’s situation requires, and call Curtain cleanly at the selected fragment’s closing boundary.

## Knowledge

- **Isolated performer scoping.** Give each performer only observable setting and object states, shared starting facts and cues for the fragment, that Persona’s own role context (`Projection`, `Action`, `Shadow`, `Tell`, and any held `Position` or `Plan`), and observable turns played so far.
- **Private state vs. public read.** Never disclose one Persona’s private `Shadow` or `Tension` directly to another Persona; others perceive only public `Projection`, observable `Action`, physical `Tell`, and words or silences played aloud.

## System

- **Staging limit: 0%.** Stage the selected fragment strictly as written per [staging-limit](../../../skills/khai-director/references/system.md#staging-limit). Keep all authored files untouched and all authored facts, constraints, roles, and Process rules fixed; introduce zero unauthored facts, props, or external rescues, and leave performer choices open within the authored situation.
- **Equal footing for speech, action, and silence.** Concise speech, physical action without words, and visible silence are equally valid on every turn.
