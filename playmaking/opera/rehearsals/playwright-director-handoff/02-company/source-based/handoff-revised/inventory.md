# Material Inventory — *The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse*

## Edition, Scope, and Access Audit

- **Primary work:** Aesop, *The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse*, in *Aesop's Fables*, translated by George Fyler Townsend, produced by David Widger, Project Gutenberg eBook `#21` (`https://www.gutenberg.org/files/21/21-h/21-h.htm`; Release Date June 25, 2008; original plain-text release September 30, 1991; `21-h.htm` updated October 28, 2016).
- **Agreed scope:** The complete fable as printed in Project Gutenberg eBook `#21` (pinned verbatim at [`../../sources/gutenberg-21-town-and-country-mouse.md`](../../sources/gutenberg-21-town-and-country-mouse.md) per [`../playwright-brief.md`](../playwright-brief.md)), situated immediately after *The Goat and the Ass* and immediately before *The Wolf, the Fox, and the Ape*. ## Clause-by-Clause Source Decomposition

| Beat ID | Verbatim Source Anchor ([`gutenberg-21-town-and-country-mouse.md`](../../sources/gutenberg-21-town-and-country-mouse.md)) | Dramatic Material Available |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `S1` | “A COUNTRY MOUSE invited a Town Mouse, an intimate friend, to pay him a visit and partake of his country fare.” | Prior warm intimacy between equals; deliberate act of country hospitality; Country Mouse begins in the role of host. |
| `S2` | “As they were on the bare plowlands, eating there wheat-stocks and roots pulled up from the hedgerow, the Town Mouse said to his friend, ‘You live here the life of the ants, while in my house is the horn of plenty. I am surrounded by every luxury, and if you will come with me, as I wish you would, you shall have an ample share of my dainties.’” | Exposed rural setting (`bare plowlands`, `hedgerow`); coarse harvested meal (`wheat-stocks and roots pulled up`); Town Mouse's status sting (`life of the ants`) paired with generous invitation (`horn of plenty`, `every luxury`, `ample share of my dainties`). |
| `S3` | “The Country Mouse was easily persuaded, and returned to town with his friend.” | Yielding of the host's pride to curiosity and appetite; transition from country ground to the town house. |
| `S4` | “On his arrival, the Town Mouse placed before him bread, barley, beans, dried figs, honey, raisins, and, last of all, brought a dainty piece of cheese from a basket.” | Role swap: Town Mouse becomes host; seven-part culinary escalation culminating in a prized `dainty piece of cheese from a basket`. |
| `S5` | “The Country Mouse, being much delighted at the sight of such good cheer, expressed his satisfaction in warm terms and lamented his own hard fate.” | High-water mark of Town Mouse's persuasion: visual/olfactory delight before eating, open praise, and Country Mouse's public repudiation of his own rural lot. |
| `S6` | “Just as they were beginning to eat, someone opened the door, and they both ran off squeaking, as fast as they could, to a hole so narrow that two could only find room in it by squeezing.” | Timing cut (`just as they were beginning to eat`); first unseen human intrusion (`someone opened the door`); vocal panic (`squeaking`); physical indignity and bodily pressure (`a hole so narrow that two could only find room in it by squeezing`). |
| `S7` | “They had scarcely begun their repast again when someone else entered to take something out of a cupboard, whereupon the two Mice, more frightened than before, ran away and hid themselves.” | Second approach aborted (`scarcely begun their repast again`); distinct second intruder with an errand (`someone else entered to take something out of a cupboard`); escalated fear (`more frightened than before`) and flight off the open boards into complete hiding inside the dark rear recess of the narrow hole. |
| `S8` | “At last the Country Mouse, almost famished, said to his friend: ‘Although you have prepared for me so dainty a feast, I must leave you to enjoy it by yourself. It is surrounded by too many dangers to please me. I prefer my bare plowlands and roots from the hedgerow, where I can live in safety, and without fear.’” | Bodily irony (`almost famished` beside untouched luxury); courteous acknowledgement of hospitality (`prepared for me so dainty a feast`); decisive parting over incompatible terms of living (`safety, and without fear` vs surrounded dainties). |

## Unused, Consolidated, and Deferred Material Possibilities

| Candidate Material | Source Basis | Disposition | Rationale |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **The Door-Opener & The Cupboard-Searcher** (`Persona` files) | `S6` (“someone opened the door”), `S7` (“someone else entered to take something out of a cupboard”) | **Left unused as Personas**; encoded via [`process-household-intrusion.md`](process-household-intrusion.md) | Townsend leaves both humans nameless and indifferent to the mice. Casting them as speaking Personas with open choices would break mouse-eye scale and turn an intimate two-hander into a domestic farce about householders. |
| **The Ants** (`Persona` or `Process`) | `S2` (“You live here the life of the ants”) | **Left unused** (rhetorical image only) | In the text, ants are solely Town Mouse's metaphor for joyless drudgery, not present creatures on the plowlands. |
| **The Road to Town** (`Place` / transit `Plot`) | `S3` (“returned to town with his friend”) | **Left unused as Place/Plot**; handled in [`play.md`](play.md) `Triggers` | Townsend bridges the journey in six words without obstacle or choice; staging a travel scene would dilute the clean contrast between the two tables. |
| **Separate Narrow-Hole `Place`** (`place-narrow-hole.md`) | `S6` (“a hole so narrow that two could only find room in it by squeezing”), `S7` (“ran away and hid themselves”) | **Consolidated** inside [`place-town-larder.md`](place-town-larder.md) | Keeping both the splintered mouth and the pitch-dark inner recess of the single wainscot squeeze-hole inside the same `Place` as the spread lets the mice smell the honey and see the cheese from the crack's mouth after Wave 1 and jam themselves completely out of sight inside that same hole on Wave 2 without inventing a second refuge. |
| **Separate `Piece` files for Bread, Barley, Beans, Dried Figs, Honey, Raisins, Basket, and Cupboard** | `S4`, `S7` | **Consolidated** into [`place-town-larder.md`](place-town-larder.md) (`Holds`) + focal [`piece-basket-cheese.md`](piece-basket-cheese.md) | Authoring eight near-identical food/furniture files would fragment stage business. Townsend himself singles out the finale (“and, last of all, brought a dainty piece of cheese from a basket”), making the cheese the natural load-bearing `Piece` while the six preceding foods dress the Place. |
| **Guest `Position`** (`position-guest.md`) | `S1`, `S4`, `S8` | **Left unused** | [`position-host.md`](position-host.md) already defines the asymmetrical duty at each table, and leaving the guest unbound by a formal Position preserves maximum freedom to critique, convert, or walk out. |
| **Country Mouse Pre-Set Return `Plan`** | `S8` | **Left unused** | Giving Country Mouse a pre-authored Plan to return home would undercut his genuine persuasion in `S3` and earnest self-pity in `S5`. Leaving him without a counter-Plan lets his Plot 3 choice emerge live under hunger and fear. |
