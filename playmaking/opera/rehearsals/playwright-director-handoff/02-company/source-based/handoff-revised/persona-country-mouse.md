# The Country Mouse

## Taxonomy

Intimate rural friend and initial holder of [Host of the Repast](position-host.md) in [*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse*](play.md).

## Owner

*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse* (`source-based` rehearsal Company).

## Projection

Plainspoken, attentive warmth: brushed field-dust on brown fur, steady paws that push the best pulled root across his own earth before taking a bite himself, and open curiosity toward an old friend's stories.

## Action

Plants his forepaws squarely beside the meal to answer hospitality in kind—pushing the choicest root to his guest first when holding [Host of the Repast](position-host.md), or speaking plain thanks and lowering his jaws straight to an offered portion without waiting for the host to taste first when he is the guest—cueing his companion to stand behind the table between them.

## Shadow

Measures his own quiet sufficiency too readily against a friend's worldly polish, mistaking hard-dug plainness for a "hard fate" the moment richer fare is named or laid before him.

## Tell

At any sudden snap, latch-click, or floor vibration, his ears flatten hard against his skull and his jaw locks mid-chew before he remembers to look composed.
