# The Dainty Cheese from the Basket

## Taxonomy

Crowning delicacy of the seven-part town banquet in [*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse*](play.md).

## Owner

*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse* (`source-based` rehearsal Company).

## Place

Brought from the woven basket onto the open floorboards of [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](place-town-larder.md).

## Load Bearing

Without this prized wedge sitting exposed on the boards, [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) could not clinch [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md)'s conversion before the first alarm, nor would walking away "almost famished" in [Famished in Cover](plot-03-second-footstep.md) cost a real, watering appetite.

## Apparent

A pale, buttery wedge smelling sharply of cream and salt, fetched last of all from a basket to outshine the bread, barley, beans, dried figs, honey, and raisins already laid out, and too wide to drag into the narrow wainscot hole.

## Yearbook

- **Before the country journey:** Scouted inside the household basket by [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) as proof that his house holds the horn of plenty.
- **On arrival in town:** Carried out last of all with ceremonial flourish in [The Cheese from the Basket](plot-02-horn-of-plenty.md), then abandoned unbitten on the open floor when the door swings wide.
