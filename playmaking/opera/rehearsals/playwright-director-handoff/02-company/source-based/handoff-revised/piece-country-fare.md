# Wheat-Stocks and Hedgerow Roots

## Taxonomy

Foraged rural meal in [*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse*](play.md).

## Owner

*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse* (`source-based` rehearsal Company).

## Place

Laid out beside the hedgerow scrape on [The Bare Plowlands and Hedgerow](place-bare-plowlands.md).

## Load Bearing

Without this tough, edible store, [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) would be fleeing starvation rather than choosing between working sufficiency and perilous luxury—leaving him nowhere viable to return to in [Famished in Cover](plot-03-second-footstep.md).

## Apparent

Dry, pale wheat-stocks paired with knotted roots still dusted with dark plowland loam; fibrous, unscented beside town sweets, and slow to chew.

## Yearbook

- **Harvest edge:** Gleaned stalk by stalk from the stripped furrows after the plow passed.
- **Morning of the visit:** Pulled fresh from under the hedgerow bank by [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) and wiped with his paws as his best offering for an intimate friend.
