# Host of the Repast

## Taxonomy

Table standing within [*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse*](play.md), held first by [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) in [Fare on the Bare Plowlands](plot-01-bare-plowlands.md) and transferred to [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) in [The Cheese from the Basket](plot-02-horn-of-plenty.md) and [Famished in Cover](plot-03-second-footstep.md).

## Owner

*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse* (`source-based` rehearsal Company).

## Has

Authority to choose the ground, lay out the fare in whatever order best shows the household's worth, and invite the guest to eat on the house's terms.

## Orders

Keep the guest fed and sheltered at the host's own risk; set the finest portion within the guest's reach and bid the guest begin before eating one's fill, joining in beside them once the guest bends to taste; answer for any interruption or shortage that spoils the meal.

## Loses

The freedom to grumble at the larder, snatch the first bite ahead of an unserved guest, or flee into narrow cover without exposing one's own home as meager or unsafe.

## Drives

Pushes whoever holds it to vindicate their entire way of life through a finished, unhurried meal—turning every untouched morsel or forced retreat into a personal indictment.
