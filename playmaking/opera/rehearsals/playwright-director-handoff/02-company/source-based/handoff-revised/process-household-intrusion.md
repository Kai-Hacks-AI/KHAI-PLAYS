# Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard

## Taxonomy

Domestic human traffic cycle across [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](place-town-larder.md) in [*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse*](play.md).

## Owner

*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse* (`source-based` rehearsal Company).

## Initiated by

Starts on each wave as soon as both [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) and [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) are out on the open floorboards beside the spread in [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](place-town-larder.md) and **either** mouse lowers head or jaws to take a bite or taste—first in [The Cheese from the Basket](plot-02-horn-of-plenty.md) (once [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](piece-basket-cheese.md) is laid out and [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) has responded to the spread: "just as they were beginning to eat") and again in [Famished in Cover](plot-03-second-footstep.md) after they emerge from the hole ("scarcely begun their repast again"). Simultaneous eating is not required: if the host waits courteously beside the spread while the guest bends first to taste, whoever runs the Process can see the trigger met on that single turn before a mouthful is swallowed. *(In [Famished in Cover](plot-03-second-footstep.md), if [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) refuses outright to leave the narrow hole after coaxing, Wave 2 still enters across the exposed spread for the cupboard.)*

## Direction

Moves deeper into the room with each wave: Wave 1 swings the outer door wide across the threshold, driving both mice off the boards squeaking to the mouth of the single narrow wainscot squeeze-hole where two fit only by squeezing; Wave 2 brings a second person all the way across the floorboards to open the cupboard and take something out, driving both mice off the open spread (or back from the hole's mouth) all the way into the pitch-dark inner recess of that same narrow wainscot hole behind the skirting board so they stay hidden out of sight of the cupboard.

## Lever

Driven by the ordinary, indifferent errands of the unseen human household. Mouse speed, stillness, and squeezing into the wainscot crack can keep the pair from being crushed or caught, and delaying a return to the open boards postpones the next brush with boots—but neither mouse can slow, bargain with, or stop the household's comings and goings.

## Echo

Leaves [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](piece-basket-cheese.md) and the six accompanying dainties lying abandoned in plain view on the floorboards after at most an aborted first taste, while both mice end up wedged breathless in the dark rear of the narrow wainscot hole—more frightened than before, and with [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) pushed from eager appetite to near-famine.
