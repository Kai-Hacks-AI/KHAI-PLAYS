# The Town Mouse

## Taxonomy

Intimate urban friend, pursuer of [Convert the Country Friend to Town Luxury](plan-share-the-horn-of-plenty.md), and holder of [Host of the Repast](position-host.md) in the town scenes of [*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse*](play.md).

## Owner

*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse* (`source-based` rehearsal Company).

## Projection

Sleek, affectionate generosity: quick, groomed whiskers, the easy confidence of someone who sleeps near honey and figs, and an eager pride in rescuing an old friend from drudgery.

## Action

Sweeps an arm or nose past what is already on the ground to present one finer delicacy held in reserve, cueing his companion to compare the old life with the "horn of plenty" now on offer.

## Shadow

Treats chronic household terror as an unremarkable tax on refinement, unable to admit—even to himself—that a feast he can only snatch between bolts into a crack is not mastery of the house.

## Tell

Even at the height of boasting or playing the grand host, the tip of his tail ticks toward the nearest bolt-hole and one eye flicks to the bottom seam of the door.
