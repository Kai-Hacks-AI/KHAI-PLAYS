# Table Pride and Friendly Irony

## Taxonomy

Play-wide default Pitch for [*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse*](play.md), governing [Fare on the Bare Plowlands](plot-01-bare-plowlands.md) and [The Cheese from the Basket](plot-02-horn-of-plenty.md) until superseded in [Famished in Cover](plot-03-second-footstep.md).

## Owner

*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse* (`source-based` rehearsal Company).

## Tenor

**Comic.** Play the hospitality, table ceremony, and lifestyle comparisons with warm, observant social comedy—two fond friends taking dead-serious pride in roots, raisins, and manners until a swinging door deflates the pomp in half a heartbeat.

## Undertow

Real hunger and sudden status shame: how quickly an honest life looks pitiable when measured beside effortless sweetness, and how thin polite confidence turns when survival enters the room.

## Nerve

Wry, affectionate recognition at watching vanity and generosity tangle across a shared meal.

## Echo

The quiet dignity of enough once the dazzle of someone else's abundance wears off.
