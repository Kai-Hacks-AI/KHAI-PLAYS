# Skirting-Board Panic

## Taxonomy

Scene-level Pitch called by [Famished in Cover](plot-03-second-footstep.md) within [*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse*](play.md).

## Owner

*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse* (`source-based` rehearsal Company).

## Tenor

**Dread.** Play the town room from two inches off the floorboards: giant latches, booming soles, cupboard shadows, and ribs crushed into splintered wood where every step onto open boards feels like stepping under a boot.

## Undertow

Absurd, mouth-watering irony—starving almost to faintness within whisker-reach of honey, figs, and [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](piece-basket-cheese.md).

## Nerve

Breathless constriction in the chest paired with the hollow ache of an interrupted stomach.

## Echo

Clean, unhurried relief at trading rich scraps under terror for bare earth without fear.
