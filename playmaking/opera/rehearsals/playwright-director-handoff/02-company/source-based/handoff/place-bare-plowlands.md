# The Bare Plowlands and Hedgerow

## Taxonomy

Open rural field-margin setting for [Fare on the Bare Plowlands](plot-01-bare-plowlands.md) and offstage refuge invoked in [Famished in Cover](plot-03-second-footstep.md), within [*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse*](play.md).

## Owner

*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse* (`source-based` rehearsal Company).

## Shown

Turned, stripped furrows stretching flat to a tangled thorn hedgerow; cold soil underfoot, dry chaff in the wind, and long, unobstructed sightlines where nothing heavy can approach unseen or unheard.

## Holds

[Wheat-Stocks and Hedgerow Roots](piece-country-fare.md) stacked cleanly beside a shallow scrape under the hedge-bank, plus dry clods and root-tangles wide enough for two mice to sit side by side without touching shoulders.

## Offers

Unhurried quiet, open air, and warning from fifty paces off—allowing both [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) and [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) to chew, argue, and linger in the open without fear of sudden doors.

## Withheld

Sweetness, softness, and effortless surplus: every mouthful here must be tugged from stiff earth or gleaned stalk by stalk, and anyone craving figs, honey, or cheese must abandon open ground and travel to [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](place-town-larder.md).
