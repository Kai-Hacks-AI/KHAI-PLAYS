# The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole

## Taxonomy

Interior domestic room setting for [The Cheese from the Basket](plot-02-horn-of-plenty.md) and [Famished in Cover](plot-03-second-footstep.md), subject to [Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard](process-household-intrusion.md), within [*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse*](play.md).

## Owner

*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse* (`source-based` rehearsal Company).

## Shown

Polished floorboards warm with indoor air and rich with the mingled scent of dried fruit, grain, honey, and aged rind—bounded on one side by a heavy door seam, on another by a tall cupboard, and along the skirting board by a splintered wainscot hole barely wider than a single mouse's ribs.

## Holds

An arranged spread of bread, barley, beans, dried figs, honey, and raisins; a woven provision basket; [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](piece-basket-cheese.md); the closed cupboard above; and the narrow wainscot squeeze-hole at floor level.

## Offers

Concentrated luxury without field labour to [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) and his guest [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md)—seven distinct dainties within a few tail-lengths on smooth boards.

## Withheld

Peace to swallow a meal: the feast lies on exposed boards between door and cupboard, while cover inside the narrow hole is so tight that two mice can occupy it only by bruisingly squeezing together with empty mouths and no room to carry food inside.
