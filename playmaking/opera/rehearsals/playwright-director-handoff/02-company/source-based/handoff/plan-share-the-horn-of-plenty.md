# Convert the Country Friend to Town Luxury

## Taxonomy

Hospitality and persuasion campaign inside [*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse*](play.md).

## Owner

[The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md), acting first as visiting friend in [Fare on the Bare Plowlands](plot-01-bare-plowlands.md) and then as [Host of the Repast](position-host.md) in [The Cheese from the Basket](plot-02-horn-of-plenty.md) and [Famished in Cover](plot-03-second-footstep.md).

## Direction

Draw [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) away from "the life of the ants" on [The Bare Plowlands and Hedgerow](place-bare-plowlands.md) and prove—through an ample, shared feast in [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](place-town-larder.md)—that town luxury is the superior way to live right now.

## Orders

- **[The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md):** Contrast coarse [Wheat-Stocks and Hedgerow Roots](piece-country-fare.md) with the town house's "horn of plenty" to win the journey; assume [Host of the Repast](position-host.md) on arrival and stage bread, barley, beans, dried figs, honey, and raisins before presenting [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](piece-basket-cheese.md) last of all; after each alarm, steady his guest and coax him back to finish the repast.

## Implementation

Depends on [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) coming willingly as an intimate friend and actually eating enough of the spread to feel satisfied rather than famished. Operates on exposed floorboards governed by [Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard](process-household-intrusion.md), where the host controls the menu but never the door latch or cupboard.

## Targets

- `[ ]` Persuade [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) to leave [The Bare Plowlands and Hedgerow](place-bare-plowlands.md) and accompany him to the town house.
- `[ ]` Win [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md)'s warm delight and open dissatisfaction with his country lot by presenting the seven-part spread crowned with [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](piece-basket-cheese.md).
- `[ ]` Complete the shared repast closely enough that [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) accepts town hazards as worth the dainties rather than departing for the plowlands.
