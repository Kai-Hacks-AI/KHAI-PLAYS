# Play — ENACTS

## Estate

*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse* (`source-based` rehearsal House). All Plots and Company objects in this folder are newly authored local material grounded in George Fyler Townsend's Project Gutenberg eBook `#21` translation of Aesop, documented in the [Sourcebook](sourcebook.md) and [Material Inventory](inventory.md) from the [pinned primary source](../../sources/gutenberg-21-town-and-country-mouse.md). No external Repertoire members are borrowed. No specific Promptbook is mandated by the brief; the Director selects or authors the Promptbook when staging.

## Name

*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse*

## Arc

Three sequential Plots turn two meals between old friends into a lived test of what makes a life bearable:

1. [Fare on the Bare Plowlands](plot-01-bare-plowlands.md) opens in unhurried safety over coarse roots and wheat-stocks, where friendly hospitality gives way to urban comparison and the wager to visit town.
2. [The Cheese from the Basket](plot-02-horn-of-plenty.md) reverses host and guest in the town house, carrying [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) to the height of delight and self-pity over a seven-part feast crowned with cheese—only to snap shut at the very first bite when the door opens and crushes both friends squeaking into a narrow crack.
3. [Famished in Cover](plot-03-second-footstep.md) begins inside that cramped hole with the cheese still lying in reach, drives a second attempt straight into a cupboard intrusion and sharper terror, and forces a decision on an empty stomach rather than in comfort.

Their order matters: because [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) first hosts in peace (Plot 1) and then publicly disowns his "hard fate" at the sight of town plenty (Plot 2), his reckoning in Plot 3 is neither sour grapes nor untested caution—it is earned across two aborted meals while almost famished in front of untouched cheese.

## Company

All Company files below are locally owned by this Play:

- **Personas:**
  - [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) — begins on home soil holding [Host of the Repast](position-host.md) with [Wheat-Stocks and Hedgerow Roots](piece-country-fare.md); wants to honour his friend well and, once tempted, see whether life truly holds sweeter fare without toil.
  - [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) — enters as intimate guest pursuing [Convert the Country Friend to Town Luxury](plan-share-the-horn-of-plenty.md), then assumes [Host of the Repast](position-host.md) in town with [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](piece-basket-cheese.md); wants to rescue his friend from ant-like drudgery and vindicate his life of curated plenty.
- **Position:**
  - [Host of the Repast](position-host.md) — held by [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) in Plot 1 and transferred to [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) upon arrival in Plot 2 through Plot 3.
- **Places:**
  - [The Bare Plowlands and Hedgerow](place-bare-plowlands.md) — rural setting for Plot 1 and offstage refuge at stake in Plot 3.
  - [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](place-town-larder.md) — indoor setting for Plots 2 and 3, holding the six laid-out provisions (bread, barley, beans, dried figs, honey, raisins), basket, cupboard, door threshold, and narrow squeeze-hole.
- **Pieces:**
  - [Wheat-Stocks and Hedgerow Roots](piece-country-fare.md) — held on [The Bare Plowlands and Hedgerow](place-bare-plowlands.md) in Plot 1.
  - [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](piece-basket-cheese.md) — brought out last in [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](place-town-larder.md) in Plot 2 and left exposed on the floorboards into Plot 3.
- **Process:**
  - [Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard](process-household-intrusion.md) — offstage human household traffic active across Plots 2 and 3.
- **Plan:**
  - [Convert the Country Friend to Town Luxury](plan-share-the-horn-of-plenty.md) — pursued by [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) across all three Plots.
- **Pitches:**
  - **Default Pitch:** [Table Pride and Friendly Irony](pitch-fable-table.md) (`comic` Tenor) governs the Play by default across [Fare on the Bare Plowlands](plot-01-bare-plowlands.md) and [The Cheese from the Basket](plot-02-horn-of-plenty.md).
  - **Plot 3 Pitch:** [Skirting-Board Panic](pitch-footstep-dread.md) (`dread` Tenor) applies when [Famished in Cover](plot-03-second-footstep.md) begins inside the narrow hole and carries through the cupboard intrusion and reckoning. The Director may select a different Pitch when staging a run.
- **What the Company knows and when:**
  - At curtain-up, both Personas know each other as intimate friends; [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) knows [The Bare Plowlands and Hedgerow](place-bare-plowlands.md) and [Wheat-Stocks and Hedgerow Roots](piece-country-fare.md), while [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) already knows [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](place-town-larder.md), its basket stores, and the fact that humans use the door and cupboard (though his `Shadow` leads him to discount that peril).
  - [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) learns of the town dainties only by report in Plot 1, sees the seven-part spread and [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](piece-basket-cheese.md) directly on arrival in Plot 2, learns how narrow the wainscot refuge is only when Wave 1 of [Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard](process-household-intrusion.md) drives both mice into it at the end of Plot 2, and learns that the alarm is recurring household routine when Wave 2 strikes at the cupboard in Plot 3.

## Triggers

- **Fixed at the start of the Play:** [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) has invited his intimate friend [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) to [The Bare Plowlands and Hedgerow](place-bare-plowlands.md) and set out [Wheat-Stocks and Hedgerow Roots](piece-country-fare.md) while holding [Host of the Repast](position-host.md). Both friends' choices—how hard to press, whether to boast or yield, whether to risk another dash for food, and how to part—remain open.
- **Plot 1 ([Fare on the Bare Plowlands](plot-01-bare-plowlands.md)):**
  - *Starts:* Both mice sit down on the bare plowlands to eat [Wheat-Stocks and Hedgerow Roots](piece-country-fare.md).
  - *Ends & hands off to Plot 2:* Closes when [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) agrees (eagerly or conditionally) to accompany [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) back to town to test his promised "horn of plenty," transferring the scene immediately to their arrival in [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](place-town-larder.md). *(If [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) refuses outright after [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) has spent every argument, the Play can close early on the plowlands with Targets 1–3 of [Convert the Country Friend to Town Luxury](plan-share-the-horn-of-plenty.md) marked `[F]` / `[-]`, though [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md)'s `Shadow` strongly inclines him toward curiosity.)*
- **Plot 2 ([The Cheese from the Basket](plot-02-horn-of-plenty.md)):**
  - *Starts:* Both mice arrive in [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](place-town-larder.md), where [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) assumes [Host of the Repast](position-host.md) and lays out the six provisions followed last of all by [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](piece-basket-cheese.md).
  - *Ends & hands off to Plot 3:* The moment both mice lower their heads to begin eating, Wave 1 of [Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard](process-household-intrusion.md) opens the door; both bolt squeaking into the narrow wainscot hole by squeezing. Once both are wedged breathless inside with [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](piece-basket-cheese.md) left unbitten on the open floor, Plot 2 hands off directly into Plot 3 under [Skirting-Board Panic](pitch-footstep-dread.md).
- **Plot 3 ([Famished in Cover](plot-03-second-footstep.md)):**
  - *Starts:* The outer door closes and quiet returns outside the narrow hole while hunger gnaws harder at [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md).
  - *Mid-Plot trigger:* If the mice venture back onto the boards and scarcely begin their repast again, Wave 2 of [Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard](process-household-intrusion.md) brings someone else in to take something from the cupboard, driving both mice into deeper cover more frightened than before. *(If [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) refuses even to leave the narrow hole, Wave 2 still enters for the cupboard across the exposed spread while they watch from the crack.)*
  - *Ends the Play:* Closes once the second intruder exits and the famished reckoning resolves—whether [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) leaves the dainty feast behind to return to [The Bare Plowlands and Hedgerow](place-bare-plowlands.md) in safety, convinces [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) to come away too, or risks staying on town boards—allowing every target in [Convert the Country Friend to Town Luxury](plan-share-the-horn-of-plenty.md) to receive its final verdict.

## Stakes

The production fights over what counts as a bearable life—and whether intimate friendship can hold when each friend's home indicts how the other lives:

- **In [Fare on the Bare Plowlands](plot-01-bare-plowlands.md):** [Wheat-Stocks and Hedgerow Roots](piece-country-fare.md) and [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md)'s self-respect as [Host of the Repast](position-host.md) are on the line; yielding to [Convert the Country Friend to Town Luxury](plan-share-the-horn-of-plenty.md) puts rural sufficiency on trial against unseen urban splendour.
- **In [The Cheese from the Basket](plot-02-horn-of-plenty.md):** [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](piece-basket-cheese.md) seems to settle the argument in town's favour—drawing [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) to repudiate his own country lot—until Wave 1 of [Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard](process-household-intrusion.md) turns triumph into bodily panic inside a crack barely wide enough for two.
- **In [Famished in Cover](plot-03-second-footstep.md):** With stomachs empty after Wave 2, the performers make the choice that defines the run: [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) must choose between [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](piece-basket-cheese.md) under chronic fear in [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](place-town-larder.md) and coarse [Wheat-Stocks and Hedgerow Roots](piece-country-fare.md) without fear on [The Bare Plowlands and Hedgerow](place-bare-plowlands.md), while [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) must decide whether he can watch an intimate friend walk out hungry or admit what his "horn of plenty" actually costs.
