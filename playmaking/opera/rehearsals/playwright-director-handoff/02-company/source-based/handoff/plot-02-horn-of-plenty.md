# The Cheese from the Basket

## Taxonomy

Second Plot of [*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse*](play.md), following [Fare on the Bare Plowlands](plot-01-bare-plowlands.md) and tuned by default to [Table Pride and Friendly Irony](pitch-fable-table.md).

## Owner

*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse* (`source-based` rehearsal Company).

## Cue

Newly arrived inside [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](place-town-larder.md), [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) now holds [Host of the Repast](position-host.md) and must make good on his promise of "the horn of plenty" before a dusty, hungry guest whose expectations he has raised sky-high.

## Action

[The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) drives toward Target 2 of [Convert the Country Friend to Town Luxury](plan-share-the-horn-of-plenty.md), building a culinary crescendo to dazzle his friend into disowning rural life; [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) takes in the spread, voices warm delight and laments his own "hard fate," and reaches eagerly to eat at last—whereupon their shared triumph collides head-on with Wave 1 of [Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard](process-household-intrusion.md) and a bolt-hole barely wide enough for two bodies.

## Stage

Both Personas enter [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](place-town-larder.md). Fixed event order:
1. [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) places bread, barley, beans, dried figs, honey, and raisins before his guest, and last of all fetches [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](piece-basket-cheese.md) onto the open boards.
2. [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) responds to the sight of the spread before either mouse finishes a proper mouthful.
3. The instant both mice begin to eat, Wave 1 of [Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard](process-household-intrusion.md) fires: someone opens the door, forcing both mice to run squeaking to the narrow wainscot hole where two can fit only by squeezing, leaving [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](piece-basket-cheese.md) exposed outside.

## Tension

[The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) has just openly lamented his own country life seconds before the door bursts open—leaving his words hanging absurdly in the dark as his ribs are crushed into the crack—while [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md), bound by [Host of the Repast](position-host.md), must squeeze in beside his terrified guest without admitting that his grand house belongs to someone else's boots.
