# Famished in Cover

## Taxonomy

Closing Plot of [*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse*](play.md), following [The Cheese from the Basket](plot-02-horn-of-plenty.md); shifts the scene register to [Skirting-Board Panic](pitch-footstep-dread.md).

## Owner

*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse* (`source-based` rehearsal Company).

## Cue

Wedged shoulder-to-shoulder inside the narrow hole of [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](place-town-larder.md) after the first door-scare subsides, both mice stare out at [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](piece-basket-cheese.md) and the untouched spread; [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) has still swallowed almost nothing and is growing sharper-hungered by the minute, while [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) still holds [Host of the Repast](position-host.md) over an uneaten feast.

## Action

[The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) pushes to salvage Target 3 of [Convert the Country Friend to Town Luxury](plan-share-the-horn-of-plenty.md), treating the first alarm as a passing trifle to coax his guest back onto the open boards and keep him in town; [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) tests whether a meal can actually be eaten here, then—after Wave 2 strikes—weighs empty-bellied terror beside untouched dainties against returning to [The Bare Plowlands and Hedgerow](place-bare-plowlands.md) and [Wheat-Stocks and Hedgerow Roots](piece-country-fare.md). Their efforts collide over whether a feast ringed by panic is luxury at all.

## Stage

Both Personas start squeezed inside the narrow hole of [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](place-town-larder.md) with [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](piece-basket-cheese.md) lying in view on the floorboards. Fixed event order if they venture back out to eat:
1. Scarcely have they begun their repast again when Wave 2 of [Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard](process-household-intrusion.md) fires: someone else enters the room to take something out of the cupboard.
2. Both mice—more frightened than before—scramble away into deeper hiding without carrying [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](piece-basket-cheese.md).
3. Once the cupboard intruder withdraws, [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md), now almost famished, and [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) face the open reckoning: risk a third venture onto the boards, stay on town terms, or part ways between town dainties and country safety.

## Tension

[The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) genuinely loves his friend yet cannot condemn the room's terror without demolishing his own pride as [Host of the Repast](position-host.md), while [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) must speak across real affection and a cramping, famished stomach—turning down a feast generously prepared for him without turning an intimate friend into an enemy.
