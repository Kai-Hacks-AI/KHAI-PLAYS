# Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard

## Taxonomy

Domestic human traffic cycle across [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](place-town-larder.md) in [*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse*](play.md).

## Owner

*The Town Mouse and the Country Mouse* (`source-based` rehearsal Company).

## Initiated by

Starts the instant both [The Town Mouse](persona-town-mouse.md) and [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) settle openly beside the spread on the floorboards of [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](place-town-larder.md) and lower their heads to eat—first in [The Cheese from the Basket](plot-02-horn-of-plenty.md) ("just as they were beginning to eat") and again in [Famished in Cover](plot-03-second-footstep.md) ("scarcely begun their repast again").

## Direction

Moves deeper into the room with each wave: Wave 1 swings the outer door wide across the threshold, driving both mice squeaking into the narrow wainscot hole where two fit only by squeezing; Wave 2 brings a second person all the way across the boards to open the cupboard and take something out, forcing the mice out of exposed cover into deeper hiding.

## Lever

Driven by the ordinary, indifferent errands of the unseen human household. Mouse speed, stillness, and squeezing into cracks can keep the pair from being crushed or caught, and delaying a return to the open boards postpones the next brush with boots—but neither mouse can slow, bargain with, or stop the household's comings and goings.

## Echo

Leaves [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](piece-basket-cheese.md) and the six accompanying dainties lying untouched in plain view while both mice end up wedged in the dark—more frightened than before, short of breath, and with [The Country Mouse](persona-country-mouse.md) pushed from eager appetite to near-famine.
