## Verbatim Performed Transcript

### Curtain Up — Opening Cue

**[Director — Stage Voice]:** Across [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/place-town-larder.md), the heavy outer door clicks shut; the human footsteps from Wave 1 recede beyond the threshold, and warm indoor quiet settles back over the polished floorboards where bread, barley, beans, dried figs, honey, raisins, and [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/piece-basket-cheese.md) gleam untouched a few tail-lengths from the splintered wainscot hole. Inside that narrow crack, [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-town-mouse.md) and an empty-stomached [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-country-mouse.md) start wedged shoulder-to-shoulder.

---

### Round 1 *(Handoff: by the scene)*

**Turn 1 — [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-town-mouse.md):**
*Letting out a tight, shallow breath against the splintered wood, the Town Mouse swallows hard, smooths one quick paw over his whiskers, and edges half a body-length out onto the open floorboards—one eye flicking to the dark seam beneath the heavy door while the tip of his tail ticks back toward the hole—before sweeping his nose past the figs and honey toward the pale, buttery wedge of cheese.*

"Nothing at all, old friend—only someone passing the threshold, as they do in a great house. Come, take the first bite of the basket-cheese while it is soft."

**Turn 2 — [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-country-mouse.md):**
*Unwedging his bruised ribs inch by inch from the splintered wainscot, the Country Mouse creeps onto the open floorboards with his belly held low to the wax and his ears still flattened before forcing them upright; his nostrils flare sharply at the salt-and-cream scent of the pale wedge, and with a quick glance toward the towering dark seam of the door, he uses both steady brown paws to nudge a crumbled flake from the corner of the basket-cheese toward his friend before folding his paws back against his hollow sides.*

"Does that great timber open so without warning often, cousin—or have we truly got the room to ourselves now? Taste a crumb with me first, for my heart is still knocking harder than my stomach."

---

### Round 2 *(Handoff: by the scene)*

**Turn 3 — [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-town-mouse.md):**
*Stepping out beneath the tall shadow of the cupboard, the Town Mouse draws in the sharp scent of salt and cream until his hollow flanks contract, yet nudges the crumbled flake back against the rich, buttery point of the wedge and sweeps a paw past a drop of honey—one eye flicking to the dark seam under the door while the tip of his tail ticks toward the splintered hole.*

"A step at odd hours is only the custom of a great house, cousin—a small enough thing beside gnawing dry roots in a bare furrow. Take the creamy heart of it with the honey first; I will not touch a crumb until you have had a proper mouthful."

**Turn 4 — [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-country-mouse.md):**
*Dropping his gaze from the towering shadow of the cupboard to his own field-dusted paws, the Country Mouse swallows hard, dips the crumbled flake of basket-cheese into the gleaming drop of honey, and parts his jaws to take the rich morsel onto his tongue—yet freezes with his teeth barely indenting the soft rind, one ear swiveling toward the dark crack beneath the door where his friend's eye keeps darting.*

"Plain roots they were indeed, old friend, and this smells sweeter than summer clover—yet why does your tail still point toward the wainscot while you bid me feast?"

### Fixed Mid-Fragment Event — Wave 2 of [Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/process-household-intrusion.md)

**[Director — Stage Voice]:** *Scarcely have the Country Mouse's teeth touched the honeyed crumb when the door latch snaps and the heavy door swings wide again. This time someone else strides straight into the room across the polished floorboards—heavy soles booming past the exposed spread and [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/piece-basket-cheese.md) right to the tall cupboard above them—pulling the cupboard door open and reaching inside to take something out.*

---

### Round 3 *(Handoff: by a cue)*

**Turn 5 — [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-town-mouse.md):**
*At the snap of the latch and boom of the approaching soles, the Town Mouse lets out one sharp squeak; his claws skitter wildly on the waxed boards in a half-lunge toward the pale wedge of cheese before recoiling empty-mouthed beneath the swinging shadow of the cupboard door. Whipping round, he bolts through the splintered wainscot crack into the deeper recess behind the skirting board—sweeping one frantic paw inward to clear the narrow mouth—and flattens his sleek belly into the dust. Pressed tight against the rough timber in dead silence, he clamps both trembling paws over his empty muzzle, wide eyes fixed through the crack on the giant sole planted beside the abandoned feast while his hollow ribs heave without a sound.*

**Turn 6 — [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-country-mouse.md):**
*At the crack of the latch his ears slam flat to his skull and his jaw locks so violently that the honeyed crumb drops untasted from his teeth onto the waxed boards; scrambling backward as the giant sole crashes down beside the pale wedge of cheese, he hurls himself headlong through the splintered wainscot gap, scraping brown fur on the jagged wood until his bruised ribs jam hard against the Town Mouse in the dark recess. Pinned there in dead silence with a smear of untasted honey gleaming on his chin and his hollow flanks shuddering against his friend's shoulder, he stares wide-eyed through the narrow slit at the towering boot leather eclipsing the feast, holding his breath until his chest visibly aches.*

**[Director — Stage Voice]:** `Curtain` at the close of `Stage` step `2` of [Famished in Cover](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/plot-03-second-footstep.md#stage)—both mice wedged breathless and more frightened than before in deeper hiding with empty stomachs, while [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/piece-basket-cheese.md) lies unbitten beside the householder's boot under the open cupboard.

## Director Staging Note

### What Happened Onstage
- **Live collision of `Position`, `Action`, and `Tell`:** Without sharing private `Shadow` or `Plan` state across performers, the authored mechanics generated acute dramatic friction on their own. On Turn 2, [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-country-mouse.md) enacted his authored `Action`—nudging a flake of [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/piece-basket-cheese.md) toward his host and waiting with folded paws for [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-town-mouse.md) to share the first bite—which ran straight into [Host of the Repast](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/position-host.md#orders) (`place the finest portion within the guest's reach before eating one's fill`). On Turn 3, [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-town-mouse.md) pushed the crumb back with honey and refused to eat ahead of his guest, only for [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-country-mouse.md) on Turn 4 to catch his friend's observable `Tell` aloud (*"why does your tail still point toward the wainscot while you bid me feast?"*) at the exact instant his teeth indented the honeyed rind.
- **Equal footing of action and visible silence under `Skirting-Board Panic`:** Once Wave 2 struck at the cupboard, both isolated performers independently dropped speech in Round 3 in favour of physical flight and breathless silence. On Turn 5, [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-town-mouse.md) aborted a half-lunge for the cheese and bolted first into deeper cover—paying the exact status penalty encoded in [Host of the Repast](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/position-host.md#loses) (`bolt first into narrow cover without exposing one's own home as meager or unsafe`)—and on Turn 6 [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-country-mouse.md)'s locking jaw (`Tell`) dropped the honeyed crumb untasted onto the floorboards, leaving Target 3 (`[ ]`) of [Convert the Country Friend to Town Luxury](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/plan-share-the-horn-of-plenty.md) openly unfulfilled at `Curtain`.

### What Was Varied or Selected in Staging vs. Authored Material (`Staging Limit: 0%`)
- **Kept strictly fixed (`0%` adaptation):** Every setting detail, the seven-part spread crowned by [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/piece-basket-cheese.md), both Personas' roles, [Host of the Repast](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/position-host.md), [Convert the Country Friend to Town Luxury](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/plan-share-the-horn-of-plenty.md), [Skirting-Board Panic](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/pitch-footstep-dread.md), and the Wave 2 cupboard trigger of [Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/process-household-intrusion.md) were staged strictly as authored in `handoff/`, with zero added props, predators, or external rescues.
- **Selected by the Director within `0%` staging discretion:**
  1. **Fragment cut ([`strichfassung.md`](strichfassung.md)):** Selected Plot 3's opening `Cue` through `Stage` steps `1–2` and cut `Stage` step `3` (the intruder's exit and post-alarm parting reckoning) so that `6` cast turns could play the coaxing from cover and the live Wave 2 cupboard intrusion without rushing two separate dramatic movements.
  2. **Turn order and handoff mode ([Promptbook — Collaboration](../../02-company/promptbook.md#collaboration)):** Cued [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-town-mouse.md) first *by the scene* in Rounds 1–2 as [Host of the Repast](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/position-host.md) answering for the interrupted table, and cued him first *by a cue* in Round 3 because he had stepped directly beneath the cupboard shadow in Turn 3—putting his obligation not to bolt ahead of his guest under immediate pressure.

### Points Where the Authored Material Made Staging Hard (Play Gaps vs. Staging Choices)
1. **Underspecified geometry of "deeper hiding" (authored Play gap in [`place-town-larder.md`](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/place-town-larder.md)):**
   [`plot-03-second-footstep.md#stage`](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/plot-03-second-footstep.md#stage) (`step 2`) and [`process-household-intrusion.md#direction`](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/process-household-intrusion.md#direction) direct the mice to scramble *"out of exposed cover into deeper hiding"* on Wave 2, whereas [`place-town-larder.md#shown`](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/place-town-larder.md#shown) and [`#holds`](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/place-town-larder.md#holds) name only the single splintered wainscot squeeze-hole (plus basket, cupboard, and door). Under `Staging limit: 0%`, rather than inventing a second mouse-hole or unauthored furniture, the Director bridged this minimally as a deeper recess behind that same splintered skirting-board opening so both mice remained cramped in view of the boards.
2. **Reciprocal "you eat first" courtesy vs. simultaneous eating trigger (interaction of authored rules + Director pacing choice):**
   [`process-household-intrusion.md#initiated-by`](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/process-household-intrusion.md#initiated-by) initiates Wave 2 when **both** mice lower their heads to eat ("scarcely begun their repast again"), while [`position-host.md#orders`](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/position-host.md#orders) forbids [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-town-mouse.md) to eat before his guest has eaten and [`persona-country-mouse.md#action`](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-country-mouse.md#action) prompts [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-country-mouse.md) to push a portion back and wait for his companion first. In sequential isolated turns, that mutual deference delayed the bite across Round 1 and meant only [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-country-mouse.md)'s teeth indented the honeyed cheese on Turn 4 while his host waited beside him; firing Wave 2 on that indentation rather than adding another round was a **Director timing choice** to honour the `4–6` turn chamber limit, though the courtesy standoff itself comes directly from the two authored files.
3. **Note for uncut Plot 3 productions — third-attempt Process silence (authored gap outside our selected fragment):**
   Although cut by our [`strichfassung.md`](strichfassung.md), [`plot-03-second-footstep.md#stage`](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/plot-03-second-footstep.md#stage) (`step 3`) explicitly permits performers to *"risk a third venture onto the boards,"* while [`process-household-intrusion.md`](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/process-household-intrusion.md) specifies only Wave 1 and Wave 2. A future `0%`-limit production staging the full Plot would need Playwright guidance on whether a third venture triggers further household traffic or lets the mice eat unmolested.
