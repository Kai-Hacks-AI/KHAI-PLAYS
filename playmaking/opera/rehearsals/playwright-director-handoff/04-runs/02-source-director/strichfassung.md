## Selected Fragment Boundaries

- **Selected Plot:** Plot 3 — [Famished in Cover](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/plot-03-second-footstep.md), set inside [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/place-town-larder.md).
- **Opening boundary (`Curtain Up`):** Plot 3's opening `Cue` ([`plot-03-second-footstep.md#cue`](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/plot-03-second-footstep.md#cue); [`play.md#triggers`](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/play.md#triggers)). The heavy outer door has just clicked shut after Wave 1 of [Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/process-household-intrusion.md) aborted the very first bite of the feast. Quiet returns to the room while [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-town-mouse.md) and [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-country-mouse.md) remain wedged shoulder-to-shoulder inside the splintered wainscot squeeze-hole, staring out at [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/piece-basket-cheese.md) and the six untouched provisions on the polished floorboards.
- **Included span:** Plot 3 `Cue` through `Stage` steps `1` and `2` ([`plot-03-second-footstep.md#stage`](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/plot-03-second-footstep.md#stage)):
  1. From cramped cover, [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-town-mouse.md) moves to steady his guest and coax him back onto the open boards to finish the repast, while a sharper-hungered [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-country-mouse.md) decides whether and how to venture back out toward [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/piece-basket-cheese.md).
  2. When the mice venture out and have scarcely begun their repast again *(or, per [`play.md#triggers`](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/play.md#triggers), across the exposed spread even if [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-country-mouse.md) refuses to leave the narrow hole)*, Wave 2 of [Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/process-household-intrusion.md) fires: someone else enters the room to take something out of the cupboard.
  3. Both mice—more frightened than before—scramble into deeper hiding without carrying [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/piece-basket-cheese.md).
- **Closing boundary (`Curtain`):** Immediately upon completion of `Stage` step `2`, with both mice wedged breathless and empty-mouthed in deeper cover while the second householder stands at the open cupboard above the abandoned spread and [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/piece-basket-cheese.md).

## Starting Conditions for the Company

### Active Pitch
- [Skirting-Board Panic](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/pitch-footstep-dread.md) (`dread` Tenor; `Undertow` of mouth-watering irony inches from honey, figs, and cheese; `Nerve` of breathless rib-constriction paired with an interrupted stomach), superseding the Play's default [Table Pride and Friendly Irony](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/pitch-fable-table.md) per [`play.md#company`](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/play.md#company).

### Setting and Objects Onstage
- **Place:** [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/place-town-larder.md) — warm polished floorboards scented with dried fruit, grain, honey, and aged rind, bounded by a heavy door seam on one side, a tall closed cupboard on another, a woven basket, a splintered wainscot hole barely wider than one mouse's ribs (where both currently squeeze shoulder-to-shoulder with no room for food), and deeper cover further back.
- **Onstage Piece & Spread:** [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/piece-basket-cheese.md) — a pale, buttery wedge smelling of cream and salt, sitting unbitten on the open floorboards beside bread, barley, beans, dried figs, honey, and raisins, and too wide to drag into the wainscot hole.
- **Offstage Contrast Held in Memory:** [The Bare Plowlands and Hedgerow](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/place-bare-plowlands.md) and [Wheat-Stocks and Hedgerow Roots](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/piece-country-fare.md) (coarse, slow-chewed fare eaten in open sightlines without fear).

### Stakes in the Fragment
- Whether [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-town-mouse.md), bound by [Host of the Repast](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/position-host.md), can steady his shaken friend and reclaim the exposed table as "the horn of plenty," and whether [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-country-mouse.md)'s sharpening hunger and affection draw him back onto boards governed by someone else's boots ([`play.md#stakes`](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/play.md#stakes); [`plot-03-second-footstep.md#action`](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/plot-03-second-footstep.md#action)).

  - [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-town-mouse.md) now serves as host over bread, barley, beans, dried figs, honey, raisins, and [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/piece-basket-cheese.md).
  - Seconds after [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-country-mouse.md) praised the spread and lamented his "hard fate," someone opened the door just as both lowered their heads to eat, driving both squeaking into this narrow wainscot crack where two fit only by bruisingly squeezing together.
  - Neither mouse has swallowed a proper mouthful; the outer door has just clicked shut and the room outside the crack is quiet again, with [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/piece-basket-cheese.md) gleaming a few tail-lengths out on the boards.

## Cues and Round Plan (`3` Rounds / `6` Cast Turns)

1. **Opening Cue & Round 1 (`Turns 1–2`, chosen *by the scene*):**
   - Outside the splintered wainscot crack, the outer door latches shut and footsteps fade; sweet scent of honey, figs, and [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/piece-basket-cheese.md) drifts into the hole while both mice press rib-to-rib.
   - [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-town-mouse.md) goes first (bound by [Host of the Repast](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/position-host.md) to answer for the interrupted meal), followed by [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-country-mouse.md).
2. **Round 2 (`Turns 3–4`, chosen *by the scene*):**
   - Both Personas play out whether they creep back onto the polished floorboards to resume the repast beside [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/piece-basket-cheese.md) or hold back at the wainscot edge.
3. **Fixed Mid-Fragment Process Cue (`Wave 2` of [Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/process-household-intrusion.md)):**
   - Fires the instant both mice have scarcely begun their repast again on the floorboards *(or enters across the spread if [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/persona-country-mouse.md) refuses to leave the hole)*: a second householder opens the door, strides across the floorboards to the tall cupboard, pulls the cupboard door wide, and reaches inside to take something out.
4. **Round 3 (`Turns 5–6`, chosen *by a cue* from `Wave 2`) & `Curtain`:**
   - Both Personas respond directly to the cupboard intrusion—scrambling into deeper hiding more frightened than before without carrying [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff/piece-basket-cheese.md)—and the Director calls `Curtain` cleanly at the end of `Stage` step `2`.
