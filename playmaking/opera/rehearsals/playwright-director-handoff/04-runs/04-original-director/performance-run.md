## Verbatim Performed Fragment Transcript

### Round 1 — Leeward Porch & Locked Side Door (`22:40`, `+1.4 ft` Ebb)

> **Stage Voice (Opening Cue):** Sleet lashes the salt-frosted clerestory glass of Marrow Slip Waiting Room above the laminated *"SERVICE SUSPENDED — RAMP HYDRAULIC INSPECTION"* notice while the four-knot spring ebb roars seaward beneath the porch boards at `+1.4 ft`. Rounding the footpath with the wire-sealed blue cold-chain cooler (`5.8°C — CEILING 8.0°C (01:00)`) in her left hand and the `#2 Brass Harbour Master Key` in her right oilskin pocket, Maren Holt catches Chief Engineer Doyle Kerr in the downward spill of her angled headlamp under the leeward porch eaves beside fresh white wood splinters and flat-iron pry marks on the exterior transom latch, a 14-inch flat pry bar held low against his thigh beside the locked Waiting Room side door.

**Turn 1 — Maren Holt:**

Maren halts just inside the leeward eaves, keeping her headlamp angled down off Doyle's eyes onto the dry porch planks as she sets the blue cold-chain cooler level by her boot. Inside her right oilskin pocket her thumb presses hard into the teeth of the brass key while her gaze tracks from the fresh white wood splinters on the transom latch down to the flat iron against his thigh, then steps square to the locked side door.

"Thought you were down-coast on the *Gullswake* yard job, Chief. Any turn of my key tonight puts those fresh splinters on my bond at seven sharp—so tell me plain why you're taking a bar to the transom."

**Turn 2 — Doyle Kerr:**

Doyle eases the flat iron an inch lower against his wool thigh, scrubs the back of his scarred left knuckles slowly along his jawbone, and tracks his gaze from her pocketed right hand down to the `5.8°C` strip on the wire-sealed cooler. He takes one measured half-step back from the splintered transom, giving her clear room at the side-door lock while keeping his boot braced on the dry threshold plank.

"There's no yard job, Maren—Halvorsen pulled our keys Friday night, and my signed engine-bell sheet is sitting in that Night Drop Box ahead of his seven o'clock adjusters. Your cooler's already climbing past five-point-eight for the eleven-twenty slack, so get us out of the sleet and I'll sign your co-op pad for the wood—before you lay a hand on that skiff hoist."

### Round 2 — Crossing into Marrow Slip Waiting Room (`Stage 2` -> `Stage 3`)

> **Stage Voice (Cue):** Sleet drives harder under the porch eaves while the `+1.4 ft` four-knot ebb pulls loud beneath the floorboards. Once the outer side door clicks open, the unheated Marrow Slip Waiting Room opens before them—curved bolted cedar benches on cracked linoleum, the laminated *"SERVICE SUSPENDED — RAMP HYDRAULIC INSPECTION"* placard taped inside the front glass, the coinless wall payphone, and the locked heavy steel Dutch door with its brass mortise cylinder fronting the inner Ticket & Winch Office.

**Turn 3 — Maren Holt:**

Maren draws the `#2` brass key on its cracked red cork tag, unlocks the Waiting Room side door with a sharp metallic click, steps over the cracked linoleum to set the cooler level on a bolted cedar bench, and walks straight to the locked steel Dutch door. Stopping with the key held an inch short of the inner brass cylinder, she presses the pad of her thumb hard against the serrated teeth until the skin pales and turns toward Doyle in the open doorway.

"You don't warn a shore agent off her own skiff hoist and talk seven o'clock adjusters without giving me the whole story, Doyle. Set that iron down by the door and tell me plain where *Gullswake* actually is—and why you're warning me off the tender—before my key touches this inner lock."

**Turn 4 — Doyle Kerr:**

Doyle steps inside over the threshold, pushes the side door shut against the sleet, and lays the fourteen-inch pry bar flat on the cracked linoleum beside the outer jamb. He walks unhurriedly past the laminated hydraulic-inspection notice to halt two paces short of the steel Dutch door, scrubbing the back of his scarred left knuckles slowly along his jawbone before letting both grease-creased hands hang open in plain view.

"*Gullswake* tore her starboard ballast tank on the east guide-crib Friday night, and they've got her moored dark behind Kestrel waiting on a private tug. That strike sheared an iron-bolted piling at minus point-two datum thirty feet off the ramp—eight inches under tonight's low chop where it'll gut your aluminum skiff—so turn the lock and let's see if Halvorsen left my bell sheet in the box."

### Round 3 — Standoff at the Locked Steel Dutch Door (`Stage 3`)

> **Stage Voice (Cue):** Inside the unheated Waiting Room, sleet ticks sharp against the frosted clerestory glass above the *"SERVICE SUSPENDED — RAMP HYDRAULIC INSPECTION"* notice while the four-knot ebb pulls seaward under the floorboards toward the `23:20` slack. On the bolted cedar bench sits the wire-sealed cooler; Maren stands at the locked steel Dutch door facing Doyle across the cracked linoleum.

**Turn 5 — Maren Holt:**

Maren holds still for a beat over the roar of the ebb beneath the linoleum, her gaze cutting once to the laminated hydraulic notice taped inside the sleet-frosted glass and down to the pale brass-notches pressed across her thumb. She slides the `#2` key home into the steel Dutch door's mortise cylinder without turning it, resting her palm flat against the cold steel plate as she faces Doyle.

"Halvorsen left an unbuoyed iron stub in my launch lane with two weeks of island insulin on that bench, and putting my key in his drop box ties a fifteen-thousand-dollar bond straight to your fight at seven. You sign my manifest for the transom splinters and con *Tender One* clear of that east-crib piling on the eleven-twenty slack, or this lock stays shut."

**Turn 6 — Doyle Kerr:**

Doyle's gaze cuts once to the wire-sealed blue cooler on the cedar bench and holds still for a beat while sleet ticks hard against the frosted clerestory glass. Slowly, he scrubs the back of his scarred left knuckles along his jawbone, then steps forward to stop a clean arm's length short of her palm on the steel Dutch door, planting his boots square over the trembling floorboards.

"I'll sign your co-op pad first thing, Maren, and if the slip floodlight shows clean water along the west pilings off the cradle line, I'll work the hoist and con *Tender One* out myself at eleven-twenty. If that iron stub chokes the west gap too, I'll put my own Chief's ticket on Channel Sixteen for your insulin—right after you turn that brass and we see what's left in the Drop Box."

> **Stage Voice — CURTAIN (`Stage 3` Closing Boundary):** Curtain falls cleanly on the Waiting Room side of the locked steel Dutch door—the `#2 Brass Harbour Master Key` seated unturned in the brass mortise cylinder beneath Maren's palm, Doyle's pry bar lying flat back by the outer door jamb, the `Sealed Dispensary Cold-Chain Case` resting level on the cedar bench, and the threshold bargain spoken aloud before either crosses into `Ticket & Winch Office`.

## Director's Staging Note

### 1. Authored Material Held Fixed vs. Staging Selections (`Staging limit: 0%`)

- **Held Fixed (`0%` adaptation):** Every authored timestamp (`22:40` rise, `23:20–23:35` slack, `01:00` thermal ceiling, `07:00` audit/adjuster cleanout, Friday's `21:14` strike and `23:50–23:52` shutdown), tidal measurement (`+1.4 ft` 4-knot ebb, `-0.2 ft` sheared east piling `8–10 inches` under `-0.6 ft` chop), setting constraint, Piece detail (`5.8°C` cooler strip, `#2` key on cracked red cork tag), Position rule, Plan, and Pitch ([Salt Dread](../../02-company/original/handoff/pitch-salt-dread.md)) stayed completely untouched. Performers were briefed and cued **solely** from [strichfassung.md](strichfassung.md).
- **Director's Selections Within `0%` Discretion:**
  1. *Fragment choice:* Selected `Stage 1` through `Stage 3` of [Plot 1 — The Threshold](../../02-company/original/handoff/plot-01-the-threshold.md) (`22:40–~22:48`) rather than a fragment from [Plot 2](../../02-company/original/handoff/plot-02-the-night-drop.md) or [Plot 3](../../02-company/original/handoff/plot-03-slack-water.md). Because Plots 2 and 3 explicitly inherit contingent custody, disclosure, and breaker states from prior Plots and no prior `Performance` (`TO LOAN`) card exists in the handoff, staging Plot 2 or Plot 3 standalone under a `0%` limit would have forced the Director to invent unplayed prior-scene bargains. Opening at `22:40` (`Stage 1–2`) required zero invented backstory.
  2. *Turn cadence & private-read filtering:* Coordinated 3 rounds (`6` cast turns total) *by the scene*, opening on Maren's headlamp catch (`Turn 1`) so Doyle had to answer for the fresh transom splinters (`Turn 2`). When Maren played the pocketed form of her `Tell` in `Turn 1` (*"Inside her right oilskin pocket her thumb presses hard into the teeth of the brass key"*), the cue relayed to Doyle translated that beat to its strictly visible surface (*"Her right hand presses hard inside her right oilskin pocket"*) so Doyle could not "see through" oilskin before Maren drew the key openly in `Turn 3`.

### 2. Where Authored Material Aided or Complicated Staging (Play Design vs. Staging Choice)

- **What Worked Cleanly:** The Play's physical thresholds (locked side door -> Waiting Room -> locked steel Dutch door -> bolted Drop Box) and inspectable Piece surfaces (`5.8°C — CEILING 8.0°C (01:00)` strip, `HARBOUR #2` cork float tag, fresh white transom splinters) gave isolated performers concrete, non-telepathic leverage on every turn.
- **Authored Friction Points Distinguished from Staging Choices:**
  1. *Semi-private phrasing of Maren's `Tell` (Play design detail):* [persona-maren-holt.md](../../02-company/original/handoff/persona-maren-holt.md) defines Maren's `Tell` as occurring *"Inside her right coat pocket—or openly at her side once drawn."* Because a `Tell` in KHAI is meant to be an observable physical leak rather than private narration, the pocketed variant sits right on the line between hidden state and public action; under isolated performer scoping, the Director had to strip the unseen brass-teeth detail when relaying `Turn 1` to Doyle until the key came out of her pocket in `Turn 3`.
  2. *Implicit prior acquaintance at `22:40` Rise (minor Play gap):* Maren's background as *"former relief purser"* and Doyle's twenty-year tenure as Chief Engineer imply two colleagues who already know each other's names and standing, yet [plot-01-the-threshold.md](../../02-company/original/handoff/plot-01-the-threshold.md) (`Stage 1–2`) does not state explicitly whether they recognize one another on sight through sleet and headlamp spill. Recording that recognition explicitly in [strichfassung.md](strichfassung.md) from their authored taxonomies kept `Turn 1` crisp, whereas a one-line clarification in `Plot 1 -> Stage 2` would eliminate any guesswork under `0%` staging.
  3. *Fast threshold velocity vs. single-Plot fragment boundary (staging-selection pressure, not a Play flaw):* Maren's authored `Action` (*"steps straight to the physical threshold—testing a lock cylinder"*) naturally drives her from the outer porch door to the inner Dutch door within a single turn (`Turn 3`). Keeping a `6`-turn run inside `Plot 1` (`Stage 1–3`) without crossing prematurely into `Ticket & Winch Office` (`Stage 4` / `Plot 2`) required explicit boundary framing in Round 3's stage notes so both performers played out the bargain on the Waiting Room side of the steel Dutch door rather than stepping into the next room mid-turn.
