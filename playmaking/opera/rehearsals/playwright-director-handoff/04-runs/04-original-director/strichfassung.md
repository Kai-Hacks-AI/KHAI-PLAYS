## Starting Context, Timeline, Process, and Stakes

### Fixed Chronology & Shared Waterfront Situation
- **Five nights prior (`Friday, Nov 20`):** Ferry service between the mainland and Kestrel Island aboard *M/V Gullswake* ceased behind a laminated notice taped inside the terminal glass: *"SERVICE SUSPENDED — RAMP HYDRAULIC INSPECTION"*. Ferry management collected crew keys at **23:50 Friday**.
- **Earlier tonight (`Wednesday, Nov 25, 16:30 & 19:00`):** County Hospital Pharmacy packed and wire-sealed Kestrel Island dispensary's two-week winter insulin and pediatric nebulizer replenishment (`16:30`), rated stable below `8.0°C` only until **01:00 Thursday**. At `19:00`, the shuttered ferry freight shed refused cargo and Coast Guard dispatch declined non-acute commercial freight transport without an active maritime emergency, leaving the case signed over to Maren Holt.
- **Fragment Rise (`Wednesday, Nov 25, 22:40`):** Both workers have slipped past the padlocked municipal road gate on foot in a northeast sleet squall—forty minutes before the **23:20–23:35** low-water slack window and two hours and twenty minutes before the **01:00** cold-chain ceiling.
- **Morning Reckoning (`Thursday, Nov 26, 07:00`):** Nine hours away—when the cooperative receiver audits Maren's `#2` key, manifest logs, equipment tallies, and `$15,000` surety bond, and company adjusters arrive to clear out the terminal's Night Drop Box.

### Active Process
- **[Ebb Tide to Low-Water Slack](../../02-company/original/handoff/process-ebb-to-slack.md):** Driven by lunar spring tide and northeast wind setup; nothing in human hands can slow or halt it. At **22:40**, a four-knot spring ebb tears seaward underneath the Waiting Room floorboards and pilings at **`+1.4 ft`** (dropping toward `+0.3 ft` at `23:00` and dead-low slack `-0.6 ft` across **23:20–23:35**, before reversing into a rising flood rip at **23:35** that shuts down small-skiff launches until morning).

### Fragment Stakes
- Custody of the threshold and [Maren Holt](../../02-company/original/handoff/persona-maren-holt.md)'s [Bonded Shore Agent](../../02-company/original/handoff/position-bonded-agent.md) `$15,000` surety bond collide head-on with [Doyle Kerr](../../02-company/original/handoff/persona-doyle-kerr.md)'s need to reach the inner office without leaving a crowbarred steel door and to stop Maren from dropping a thin aluminum hull onto an unmarked wreck spike—putting [#2 Brass Harbour Master Key](../../02-company/original/handoff/piece-master-key.md) and the truth behind the fake hydraulic-inspection sign immediately on the line.
