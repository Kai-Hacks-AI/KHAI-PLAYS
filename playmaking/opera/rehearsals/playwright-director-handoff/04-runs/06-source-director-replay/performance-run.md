## Verbatim Performed Transcript

### Curtain Up (`Cue`)

*Inside [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/place-town-larder.md), the heavy outer door has closed after the first alarm and quiet settles back over the warm, polished floorboards. A few tail-lengths out between the closed door seam and the tall closed cupboard lie bread, barley, beans, dried figs, honey, raisins, and—pale, buttery, and unbitten—[The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/piece-basket-cheese.md). Wedged shoulder-to-shoulder at the cramped, splintered mouth of the single narrow wainscot squeeze-hole, [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-town-mouse.md) and a sharp-hungered [Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-country-mouse.md) stare out at the untouched feast.*

---

### Round 1 (`Cue` → `Stage` Step `1`)

#### Turn 1 — [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-town-mouse.md) *(by the scene)*

*Letting out a tight, whistling breath against the splintered wainscot, The Town Mouse wriggles his sleek ribs free of the cramped mouth, slicks down his ruffled whiskers with two rapid strokes of his forepaws, and steps out onto the open floorboards as his hollow flanks give a sharp twitch. Halfway to the spread, one bright eye flicks to the dark seam beneath the heavy door and the tip of his tail ticks back toward the narrow hole, even as he sweeps an inviting paw past the figs and honey to nudge the pale, buttery wedge of basket-cheese nearer the skirting board.*

"Only a passing step in the passage, dear friend—nothing to keep us from our supper! Come out and take the first bite of this cream-cheese before the best of the savor cools."

#### Turn 2 — [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-country-mouse.md) *(by the scene)*

*Working his bruised, field-dusty ribs free of the splintered wainscot mouth on a shallow, catching breath, The Country Mouse inches out onto the gleaming floorboards—one ear still angled toward the dark seam under the towering door—until he plants both forepaws squarely beside the pale wedge of basket-cheese, his hollow flanks drawing tight.*
"You spare nothing for an old friend, and the mere smell of such cream-cheese makes my dry roots at home seem a hard, poor living indeed."
*Without waiting for his host to taste first, he lowers his head straight to the offered wedge and sinks his front teeth into its salted, buttery corner.*

---

### Stage Event — [Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/process-household-intrusion.md), Wave 2 (`Stage` Step `1` Trigger Met)

*With both mice out on the open floorboards beside the spread and [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-country-mouse.md) lowering his head to taste [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/piece-basket-cheese.md)—scarcely having begun their repast again and before a mouthful can be bitten free or swallowed—the outer door swings open and someone else from the household strides straight across the polished floorboards past the exposed spread, swings the tall cupboard door wide, and reaches inside to take something out.*

---

### Round 2 (`Stage` Step `2`)

#### Turn 3 — [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-country-mouse.md) *(by a cue)*

*At the first boom of the floorboards, The Country Mouse's ears flatten hard against his skull and his jaw locks tight before his teeth can sever a crumb, wrenching his muzzle back from the wedge with only a shine of salt-butter on his whiskers. Letting out one thin, strangled squeak, he scrambles flat-bellied for the skirting board, scrapes his bruised brown ribs violently through the splintered wainscot mouth, and forces himself all the way back into the pitch-dark recess behind the boards—hunched motionless in breathless, locked-jawed silence with his trembling flanks crushed against the raw wood.*

#### Turn 4 — [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-town-mouse.md) *(by a cue)*

*Whiskers snapping back at the boom of the floorboards, The Town Mouse's claws skitter wildly across the polished wood as he abandons the untouched wedge of cheese, shoots headlong through the splintered wainscot mouth, and rams his sleek ribs bruisingly into the remaining sliver of the pitch-dark recess. Crushed hard against the raw inner timber, he clamps his jaws shut to swallow a thin wheeze in his constricted chest, his hollow flanks jerking once in a silent hunger-cramp while the tip of his tail ticks stiffly in the dark and one wide, unblinking eye stays pinned through the narrow crack toward the bottom of the room.*

---

### Curtain (`End of Stage Step 2`)

*Above the polished floorboards, the second household figure stands at the open cupboard while [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/piece-basket-cheese.md) and the six accompanying dainties lie abandoned in plain view; behind the skirting board, both mice remain jammed breathless and empty-bellied inside the pitch-dark inner recess of the single narrow wainscot hole. **Curtain** (prior to `Stage` step `3`).*

### 2. What Happened in Play

- In Round 1, [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-town-mouse.md) wriggled out onto the open floorboards, flicked one eye to the door seam and ticked his tail toward the hole (`Tell`) while dismissing the first alarm as a passing step, nudged [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/piece-basket-cheese.md) toward the skirting board, and invited his guest to take the first bite. [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-country-mouse.md) crept onto the floorboards, planted his forepaws squarely beside the wedge, compared its creamy scent against his dry country roots, and lowered his jaws straight to the salted corner without waiting for his host to eat first.
- That turn visibly satisfied the authored `Initiated by` condition of Wave 2 in [Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/process-household-intrusion.md) (both mice out on the open floorboards beside the spread and either mouse lowering head or jaws to take a bite or taste before a mouthful is swallowed). Wave 2 fired immediately at the top of Round 2 as a second household member strode across the boards to open the cupboard.
- In Round 2, both isolated performers independently chose pure physical action and breathless silence under [Skirting-Board Panic](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/pitch-footstep-dread.md): [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-country-mouse.md)'s ears flattened and jaw locked (`Tell`) with only salt-butter on his whiskers as he jammed himself into the pitch-dark inner recess behind the skirting board, and [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-town-mouse.md) skittered off the boards to ram his sleek ribs beside him in the dark while the cheese stayed exposed under the cupboard.
