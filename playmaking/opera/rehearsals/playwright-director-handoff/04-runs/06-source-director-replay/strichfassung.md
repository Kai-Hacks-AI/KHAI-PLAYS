## Selected Boundaries & Cuts

- **Curtain Up (`Cue` of [Famished in Cover](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/plot-03-second-footstep.md)):** Opens inside [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/place-town-larder.md) just as the outer door closes and quiet returns after Wave 1 of [Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/process-household-intrusion.md). Both [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-town-mouse.md) and [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-country-mouse.md) start wedged shoulder-to-shoulder at the cramped, splintered mouth of the single narrow wainscot squeeze-hole, staring out at [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/piece-basket-cheese.md) and the six accompanying provisions on the open floorboards.
- **Played Span (`Stage` steps `1–2` of [Famished in Cover](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/plot-03-second-footstep.md)):** Covers the post-alarm coaxing and test of returning to the spread (`Stage` step `1`), the firing of Wave 2 of [Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/process-household-intrusion.md) once its authored initiation condition is visibly met onstage, and both mice bolting in heightened terror off the open boards (or recoiling from the hole's mouth) to jam themselves past the opening into the pitch-dark inner recess of the single narrow wainscot hole behind the skirting board without carrying [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/piece-basket-cheese.md) (`Stage` step `2`).
- **Curtain:** Called cleanly at the close of `Stage` step `2`, with the second household intruder at the open cupboard, [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/piece-basket-cheese.md) abandoned on the floorboards, and both mice wedged breathless in the dark rear crevice behind the skirting board.
- **Cuts:**
  - **Cut before Curtain Up:** Plot 1 ([Fare on the Bare Plowlands](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/plot-01-bare-plowlands.md)) and Plot 2 ([The Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/plot-02-horn-of-plenty.md)) are cut prior to `Curtain Up`; their established facts, role transfer, and unresolved stakes carry forward as starting conditions.
  - **Cut after Curtain:** `Stage` step `3` of [Famished in Cover](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/plot-03-second-footstep.md) (the cupboard intruder's exit and the famished reckoning over staying, risking a third dash, or departing for [The Bare Plowlands and Hedgerow](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/place-bare-plowlands.md)) is cut after `Curtain`.

## Carried-In Starting Facts & Unresolved Stakes

1. **Prior Country Meal & Wager (from [Fare on the Bare Plowlands](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/plot-01-bare-plowlands.md)):** Intimate friends [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-country-mouse.md) and [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-town-mouse.md) first shared coarse [Wheat-Stocks and Hedgerow Roots](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/piece-country-fare.md) in complete safety on [The Bare Plowlands and Hedgerow](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/place-bare-plowlands.md). Calling that life "the life of the ants," [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-town-mouse.md) promised "the horn of plenty" and persuaded his friend to come to town (completing Target 1 of [Convert the Country Friend to Town Luxury](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/plan-share-the-horn-of-plenty.md)).
2. **Interrupted Town Banquet (from [The Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/plot-02-horn-of-plenty.md)):** Assuming [Host of the Repast](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/position-host.md) in [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/place-town-larder.md), [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-town-mouse.md) set out bread, barley, beans, dried figs, honey, and raisins, crowning them last of all with [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/piece-basket-cheese.md). [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-country-mouse.md) expressed warm delight and lamented his own "hard fate" (completing Target 2 of [Convert the Country Friend to Town Luxury](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/plan-share-the-horn-of-plenty.md)). Just as they began to eat, Wave 1 of [Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/process-household-intrusion.md) swung the outer door wide, driving both mice squeaking into the mouth of the single narrow wainscot hole—so tight two fit only by bruisingly squeezing—before a mouthful could be swallowed.
3. **Active Stakes in This Fragment:**
   - [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-country-mouse.md) has still swallowed almost nothing, is growing sharper-hungered by the minute, and tests whether a meal can actually be eaten on these floorboards.
   - [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-town-mouse.md), bound by [Host of the Repast](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/position-host.md) and pursuing Target 3 of [Convert the Country Friend to Town Luxury](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/plan-share-the-horn-of-plenty.md), must treat the door-scare as a passing trifle and coax his guest back onto the open boards to complete the shared repast without exposing his home as unsafe.

## Onstage Company, Setting, Objects & Knowledge Scoping

- **Place:** [The Town House Cupboard-Room and Narrow Hole](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/place-town-larder.md)
  - *Observable state:* Warm polished floorboards smelling richly of dried fruit, grain, honey, and aged rind; bounded by the heavy closed door seam on one side, a tall closed cupboard on another, a woven provision basket, and along the skirting board the room's sole mouse cover—a single splintered wainscot squeeze-hole barely wider than one mouse's ribs, opening from a cramped floor-level mouth into a pitch-dark inner recess behind the skirting board with no room to drag food inside.
- **Load-Bearing Piece Onstage:** [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/piece-basket-cheese.md)
  - *Observable state:* A pale, buttery wedge smelling sharply of cream and salt, lying exposed and unbitten on the open floorboards beside the bread, barley, beans, dried figs, honey, and raisins, and too wide to drag into the narrow wainscot hole.
- **Cast & Isolated Scoping (per [Promptbook](../../02-company/promptbook.md)):**
  - **[The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-town-mouse.md):** Receives shared setting/object states and starting facts, his own `Projection`, `Action`, `Shadow`, and `Tell`, his active standing in [Host of the Repast](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/position-host.md), his active [Convert the Country Friend to Town Luxury](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/plan-share-the-horn-of-plenty.md) (Targets 1–2 met; Target 3 pending), his knowledge of the room and that household humans use the door and cupboard, and his side of Plot 3 `Tension`. Does **not** receive [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-country-mouse.md)'s private `Shadow` or `Tension`.
  - **[The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-country-mouse.md):** Receives shared setting/object states and starting facts (including memory of [The Bare Plowlands and Hedgerow](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/place-bare-plowlands.md) and [Wheat-Stocks and Hedgerow Roots](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/piece-country-fare.md)), his own `Projection`, `Action` (guest variant: answers hospitality with plain thanks and lowers his jaws straight to an offered portion without waiting for the host to taste first), `Shadow`, and `Tell`, and his side of Plot 3 `Tension`. Knows Wave 1's door-scare and the squeeze-hole's cramped mouth; does **not** know in advance that Wave 2 will strike at the cupboard and does **not** receive [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-town-mouse.md)'s private `Shadow`, `Plan`, or `Tension`.

## Cues & Round Coordination (`2–3` Rounds, `4–6` Cast Turns)

1. **Opening Cue (`Curtain Up`, Round 1):** Quiet returns outside the closed door while both mice remain wedged shoulder-to-shoulder at the splintered mouth of the narrow wainscot hole staring out at [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/piece-basket-cheese.md) as hunger sharpens in [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-country-mouse.md). Cue [The Town Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-town-mouse.md) first *by the scene* as [Host of the Repast](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/position-host.md), then [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-country-mouse.md) *by the scene*.
2. **Process Gate (`Stage` Step `1` — Wave 2 of [Household Footsteps at Door and Cupboard](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/process-household-intrusion.md)):**
   - *Strict `0%` condition:* Hold Wave 2 until **both** mice have visibly ventured out onto the open floorboards beside the spread **and** **either** mouse lowers head or jaws to take a bite or taste ("scarcely begun their repast again", before a mouthful is swallowed)—or until [The Country Mouse](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/persona-country-mouse.md) refuses outright to leave the narrow hole after coaxing.
   - *Firing:* The moment that authored condition is visibly met on a cast turn, introduce Wave 2 immediately *by a cue*: a second household human enters across the floorboards to open the tall cupboard and take something out. (If any cue conflict blocks the condition under `0%`, hold the cue and report the gap rather than forcing or bypassing it.)
3. **Retreat into Inner Recess & Closing Cue (`Stage` Step `2` -> `Curtain`):** Cue both mice *by a cue* under Wave 2's cupboard intrusion as they bolt off the open floorboards (or recoil from the mouth) more frightened than before, jamming themselves past the splintered mouth into the pitch-dark inner recess of the single narrow wainscot squeeze-hole behind the skirting board without [The Dainty Cheese from the Basket](../../02-company/source-based/handoff-revised/piece-basket-cheese.md). Call **Curtain** once both are wedged hidden inside that dark rear recess (`Stage` step `2` complete, prior to `Stage` step `3`).
