# Rehearsal Setup · The Wrong Name

Two volunteers are about to unveil a plaque at a neighborhood hall. One wants to keep the ceremony moving. The other checks the plaque and sees that the honoree’s name is misspelled. The honoree’s family is arriving.

## Create

Use the [Playwright skill](../../../skills/khai-playwright/SKILL.md) to author a short Play and Promptbook, two volunteer Personas, the hall as a Place, the plaque as a Piece, and the unveiling as a Process. Give the volunteers public responsibilities that collide. The volunteer who checks the plaque privately knows they signed off on the proof. At the check, only that volunteer sees the error. The family can learn about it through what happens onstage.

Use [TO CAST](../../../skills/khai-playwright/references/plot.md) to write two Plots with the same starting pressure, public aims, and private stakes. Vary only the order of the family’s entrance and the check in Stage.

| Plot | Order |
| --- | --- |
| Before the entrance | The volunteer checks the plaque and sees the error. After one beat, the family enters. |
| After the entrance | The family enters. After one beat, the volunteer checks the plaque and sees the error. |

## Rehearse

Use the [Director skill](../../../skills/khai-director/SKILL.md). Run each Plot twice in fresh contexts with the same Company and only the active Plot. Keep the opening and the entrance and discovery cues the same apart from their order. Let the volunteers decide what to do. Play through one consequence once both events have happened.

## Review

Use SCORE to record the tested commit, Company, runs, observations, and any proposed template edit. Cite a move or silence per run. Compare who knew what when, which action became available or costly, and how the order changed what the volunteers could do. Record any extra direction the Director supplied.
