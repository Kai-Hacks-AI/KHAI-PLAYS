# Authoring Note — *The Wrong Name*

## 1. Key Authoring Choices

- **Concrete Error & Credible Unseen Mounting (`piece-plaque.md`):** Honoree **Miriam Okafor** (`1948–2025`, printed accurately on forty half-sheet programs in the room) is cast across the plaque's 1.5-inch top bronze header as **`MARIAM OKAFOR`**. A single swapped vowel in large capitals is immediately legible from the reserved front row six feet away once unveiled—impossible to wave off as kerning—yet plausible for a rushed steward checking dates and body copy on a phone screen at 4:45 p.m. against a 5:00 p.m. foundry cutoff. Having [Theo Park](persona-plaque-lead.md) mount the late-arriving plaque with its protective kraft paper taped over the face and slide the paper out from behind the velvet drape explains why neither volunteer knows of the misspelling at Opening and gives Theo a natural physical reason to peek under the bottom corner at the Discovery Cue.
- **Colliding Civic Duties & Symmetrical Stakes (`persona-ceremony-lead.md`, `persona-plaque-lead.md`):** [Marta Solis](persona-ceremony-lead.md) holds the live microphone and the clock (her post-chaos Events Chair reputation, a hard 2:20 p.m. press-photo cutoff, a 2:30 p.m. youth-group turnover, and a personal morning promise to widower Samuel Okafor not to put him through a drawn-out fuss). [Theo Park](persona-plaque-lead.md) holds physical custody of the draw-cord, his printed name as Dedication Steward over \$1,400 in neighbor donations, personal gratitude to Miriam, and secret sole sign-off on the rushed proof after telling Marta and the board he checked every letter.
- **Stage Physics That Price Every Option (`place-hall.md`, `process-unveiling.md`):** Putting an oak lectern with a switched-on gooseneck mic two paces from a wall-bolted plaque, six feet in front of the Okafor family's reserved chairs, with forty hushed neighbors and no backstage wings gives whispers, hand-checks, cord-blocking, stalls, and unveiling distinct, observable public costs.
- **Split Discovery Cue & Pre-Check Epistemic Hygiene (`play.md`, `promptbook.md`):** Dividing the Discovery Cue into a **Public Stage View** (a brief one-inch check of the bottom hem for packing tape) and a **Private Visual View** (`MARIAM OKAFOR`, delivered only to Theo), while withholding `piece-plaque.md`'s hidden lettering from both performers prior to that cue, preserves true zero-knowledge behavior during Turns 1–2 of `plot-after-entrance.md` and strict one-sided knowledge at the moment of discovery in both Plots.

*(Template note: `process-unveiling.md` uses the exact `TO IDLE` headings defined in `references/process.md`: `Taxonomy`, `Owner`, `Initiated by`, `Direction`, `Lever`, `Echo`.)*

## 2. Seams, Overlaps, and Tensions Observed in `TO CAST` (`references/plot.md`)

1. **`## Stage` stretches between static tableau, entrances, and timed event ordering:**
   `references/plot.md` defines `Stage` as *"Place the Personas and their Pieces, and say who enters with what."* That prompt natively covers `t = 0` blocking and character entrances (Samuel and Tomi Okafor arriving with cane, chrysanthemums, and program), but has no explicit slot for **timed mid-scene sequence** (`First... After one beat...`) or for **non-entrance actions** (Theo checking beneath the cloth). To vary event order cleanly without touching `Cue`, `Action`, or `Tension`, `Stage` had to split internally into two paragraphs: static opening placement followed by ordered runtime events.
2. **`## Tension` must hold latent pre-check stakes rather than post-cue discoveries:**
   In an order-varying experiment, `Tension` (*"what a Persona carries privately that keeps the Action from going smoothly"*) cannot name a fact discovered mid-scene (`MARIAM OKAFOR`) without either leaking future knowledge into the pre-check beat of *After the entrance* or breaking invariance across the two Plots. `Tension` worked cleanly only when restricted to **pre-existing private liability** at Curtain-up (Theo's solo rush proof sign-off and assurance to the board; Marta's morning promise to Samuel), leaving the physical error stored in `Piece` until triggered in `Stage`.
3. **Boundary between `Plot ## Tension` and `Persona ## Shadow` (`TO PAST`):**
   `Persona ## Shadow` and `Plot ## Tension` overlap unless deliberately separated by timescale. Placing enduring dispositional blind spots in `Shadow` (Marta reading hesitation as stage fright; Theo quietly soloing tasks) and episode-specific private commitments in `Tension` (this morning's phone promise; eleven days ago's skipped second proof signature—though `Shadow` also needed enough concrete grounding to motivate `Tell`) kept both fields functional, though the seam between biographical history in `Persona`, object history in `Piece ## Yearbook`, and situational burden in `Plot ## Tension` invited deliberate deduplication.
4. **Triple collision on the word "Cue" across `Plot`, `Persona`, `Play`, and `Promptbook`:**
   In `TO CAST`, `## Cue` names the *starting Place and ambient pressure*; in `Persona — TO PAST`, `## Action` gives *"someone else a cue"*; in `Play` and `Promptbook ## Collaboration`, *Cue* names discrete runtime triggers (*Entrance Cue*, *Discovery Cue*). A reader moving from `Plot ## Cue` to `Play` runtime cues has to track two different meanings of the same term.
5. **Gating `Piece ## Yearbook` vs. `Piece ## Apparent` before discovery:**
   `Piece — TO PLAY` separates `## Apparent` (*what someone can observe before they know what the Piece carries*) from `## Yearbook` (*how the Piece came to carry that weight*). Keeping `## Apparent` strictly to the veiled velvet outline and placing the foundry's **`MARIAM OKAFOR`** casting error inside `## Yearbook` honored `TO PLAY` cleanly, while requiring `Promptbook ## Knowledge` to gate `## Yearbook`'s foundry-typo sentence out of isolated performer contexts until the Discovery Cue fires.
6. **Division of fixed structure vs. open play between `Plot` and `Play` / `Promptbook`:**
   `Plot` (`TO CAST`) stayed cleanly declarative—naming forces, collision geometry, blocking/event order, and private pressure—while `Play` held the invariant verbatim cue text and `Promptbook` held turn counting (`2` intervening turns + `2–4` open-exchange turns) and performer context isolation. Without `Play` and `Promptbook` carrying the beat count and epistemic split, `Plot ## Stage` would have had to absorb procedural Director instructions that do not belong in `TO CAST`.
