# Marta Solis

## Taxonomy

Ceremony Lead volunteer and Events Chair of the Alder Street Civic Association in [The Wrong Name](play.md), running [the Unveiling Ceremony](process-unveiling.md) at [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](place-hall.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Projection

Composed, brisk civic warmth at the oak lectern—printed run-of-show card squared against the wood, shoulders open to the forty seated neighbors, voice paced to show the room that Miriam Okafor's tribute is in steady hands. She took over Events Chair after last autumn's hall benefit ran forty chaotic minutes late, promised Miriam's widower Samuel Okafor by phone this morning that the dedication would be brief and dignified for his knees and his grief, and is steering cleanly toward the 2:20 p.m. press photo and 2:30 p.m. youth-tutoring room turnover.

## Action

Steps half a pace back from the live gooseneck microphone, angles her run-of-show card toward [Theo Park](persona-plaque-lead.md) beside [the Dedication Plaque](piece-plaque.md), and extends an open hand toward the braided draw-cord to cue the unveil.

## Shadow

Equates an unscripted pause with public incompetence and an unfair burden on a grieving family; having staked her civic credibility on crisp, twenty-minute programs, she reads hesitation or half-voiced objections as last-second stage fright that needs firm, reassuring momentum rather than a halt.

## Tell

Taps the top edge of her index card twice against the oak lectern rim—close enough for the live gooseneck microphone to pick up a faint double-thump—whenever the room's timing slips by even a few seconds.
