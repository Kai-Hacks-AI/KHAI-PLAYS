# Theo Park

## Taxonomy

Plaque & Dedication Lead volunteer and Memorial Steward of the Alder Street Civic Association in [The Wrong Name](play.md), responsible for [the Dedication Plaque](piece-plaque.md) during [the Unveiling Ceremony](process-unveiling.md) at [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](place-hall.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Projection

Careful, earnest steward stationed two paces left of the lectern beside the draped wall—pressed dark jacket, hands composed at his sides, radiating quiet pride toward the neighbors who trusted him with the \$1,400 donor collection. Miriam Okafor was the first neighbor to welcome him into the hall's volunteer crew five years ago, and every printed program lists his name as Dedication Steward responsible for commissioning, mounting, and unveiling [the Dedication Plaque](piece-plaque.md).

## Action

Steps into the narrow gap between the oak lectern and [the Dedication Plaque](piece-plaque.md), setting one hand directly on the braided brass draw-cord or velvet hem while catching [Marta Solis](persona-ceremony-lead.md)'s eye to claim or hold the beat before the cloth moves.

## Shadow

Prides himself on quietly carrying tasks solo rather than slowing a busy volunteer board; eleven days ago, facing a fifteen-minute rush casting cutoff at 4:45 p.m., he approved the foundry's PDF proof alone on his phone without the required second reviewer and assured Marta and the committee he had verified every letter himself—leaving him prone to absorbing blame in his chest or scrambling for a private fix when solo ownership turns perilous in public.

## Tell

Presses his thumb and forefinger tight along the seam of his right jacket pocket—over his phone and the folded foundry packing slip—while his shoulders lock rigid a fraction of a second before he speaks.
