# The Dedication Plaque

## Taxonomy

Permanent civic memorial fixture and centerpiece of [the Unveiling Ceremony](process-unveiling.md) in [The Wrong Name](play.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Place

Bolted at eye height into the front wall two paces left of the oak lectern in [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](place-hall.md), six feet in front of the four reserved family chairs.

## Load Bearing

Carries the hall's permanent public tribute to late Saturday-pantry founder **Miriam Okafor** (`1948–2025`). Forty neighborhood households pooled \$1,400 in small donations to cast it, every printed program in the room names *The Miriam Okafor Community Pantry Wall*, and [the Unveiling Ceremony](process-unveiling.md) gathers her widower Samuel Okafor, her daughter Tomi Okafor, forty neighbors, and the local weekly's photographer around revealing this bronze tablet. Without an unveiled plaque—or if the dedication fails in front of the hall—the promised centerpiece of the tribute collapses in public view.

## Apparent

An 18-by-24-inch rectangular outline bolted at eye height beneath a weighted navy velvet unveiling cloth hung from a short brass top rod, with a braided brass draw-cord looped at its right edge. Pulling the draw-cord parts and drops the velvet cleanly to reveal the entire plaque to the hall; lifting the weighted bottom corner an inch off the wall lets only someone standing flush beside it glimpse the face without exposing it to the chairs.

## Yearbook

- **Six weeks ago:** After Miriam Okafor's death, neighborhood donors raised \$1,400 through the Alder Street Civic Association for a cast-bronze memorial plaque, naming [Theo Park](persona-plaque-lead.md) Dedication Steward on the program.
- **Eleven days ago, 4:45 p.m.:** Re-keying the copy into its casting layout, the foundry entered **`MARIAM OKAFOR`** instead of **`MIRIAM OKAFOR`** across the 1.5-inch raised polished-brass top header (above `1948 – 2025` and *WHO FED ALDER STREET WITHOUT ASKING WHY*) and emailed a final PDF proof warning that the rush furnace slot closed at 5:00 p.m. Reading the dates and quote on his phone screen on the bus without the committee's required second reviewer, Theo replied *Approved for casting — no changes* over his own signature and later told [Marta Solis](persona-ceremony-lead.md) and the board he had checked every letter himself.
- **Today, 1:45 p.m.:** When the courier delivered the crate just before doors opened, Theo bolted the dark statuary-bronze plaque into the wall with the foundry's protective kraft paper still taped across the center so early arrivals would not see it, hung the navy velvet cloth over the top rod, and slid the kraft sheet out from underneath without lifting the cloth to inspect the bare bronze.
