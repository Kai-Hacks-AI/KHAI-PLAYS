# The Wrong Name

Two neighborhood volunteers stand at the front of [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](place-hall.md) at the point of unveiling [the Dedication Plaque](piece-plaque.md) for late pantry founder **Miriam Okafor** (`1948–2025`). [Marta Solis](persona-ceremony-lead.md) is at the live lectern microphone keeping [the Unveiling Ceremony](process-unveiling.md) moving on schedule; [Theo Park](persona-plaque-lead.md) stands beside the veiled plaque he personally signed off on for casting. ## Fixed Opening and Cues

Use the exact Opening and fixed cues below, firing the **Entrance Cue** and **Discovery Cue** in the exact order set in [plot.md](plot.md) (`## Stage`):

1. **Opening (2:12 p.m.):**
   Forty neighbors sit hushed in folding chairs inside [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](place-hall.md), holding ivory half-sheet programs titled *Honoring Miriam Okafor (1948–2025)*. At the oak lectern, [Marta Solis](persona-ceremony-lead.md) stands behind the live gooseneck microphone on the closing lines of her brief welcome, with the press photographer poised by the side window ahead of a hard 2:20 p.m. departure, a youth group booked for 2:30 p.m., and four paper-ribboned front-row chairs reserved for the Okafor family sitting empty six feet from the front wall. Two paces to Marta's left, [Theo Park](persona-plaque-lead.md) stands beside [the Dedication Plaque](piece-plaque.md), draped smooth beneath its weighted navy velvet cloth and braided brass draw-cord. Neither volunteer has seen the bare bronze face since casting.

2. **Entrance Cue:**
   At the rear of [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](place-hall.md), the double glass doors ease open. Miriam's widower Samuel Okafor, leaning lightly on his walking cane, and her daughter Tomi Okafor, carrying white chrysanthemums and a folded printed program, walk quietly down the center aisle as seated neighbors turn with warm nods, taking two of the reserved front-row chairs six feet directly in front of [the Dedication Plaque](piece-plaque.md).

3. **Discovery Cue:**
   - **Public Stage View (seen by Marta and the hall):** Beside [the Dedication Plaque](piece-plaque.md), [Theo Park](persona-plaque-lead.md) reaches down and angles the weighted bottom corner of the navy velvet cloth an inch off the wall, glancing underneath for a second as if checking that no foundry packing tape or cord snag remains, before letting the velvet settle flat over the plaque again. From the lectern and the chairs, only that brief hand check is visible; the bronze face stays hidden.
   - **Private Visual View (seen ONLY by [Theo Park](persona-plaque-lead.md)):** Behind the lifted corner of the navy velvet, the 1.5-inch raised polished-brass capitals across the top header line catch the light: **`MARIAM OKAFOR`**—not **`MIRIAM OKAFOR`**.

## Run Structure

1. **Set the Opening and Event 1:** Establish the **Opening**, then immediately fire whichever fixed cue comes `First` in [plot.md](plot.md) (`## Stage`).
2. **Play One Beat (`2` volunteer turns):** Give each volunteer one concise turn (`1` turn for [Marta Solis](persona-ceremony-lead.md) and `1` turn for [Theo Park](persona-plaque-lead.md)) under the exact knowledge state then in effect.
3. **Fire Event 2:** Immediately trigger the second fixed cue (`After one beat` in [plot.md](plot.md) `## Stage`).
4. **Open Volunteer Exchange (`2–4` volunteer turns):** Once both events have happened, let [Marta Solis](persona-ceremony-lead.md) and [Theo Park](persona-plaque-lead.md) play out what they do over `2–4` concise turns (`4–6` volunteer turns total across the run; `7` hard ceiling only if needed to finish a mid-beat exchange).
5. **One Immediate Consequence and Curtain:** Once the volunteers commit to an onstage course—or let [the Unveiling Ceremony](process-unveiling.md) carry forward—play through **one immediate consequence** in [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](place-hall.md) showing what happens to [the Dedication Plaque](piece-plaque.md) and how the Okafor family and assembled hall respond, then call **Curtain**.

## Open Decisions

What the volunteers choose is completely open. Either volunteer may speak into the live microphone, step off-mic to whisper, stay silent, pull the draw-cord, hold the velvet cloth down, stall, postpone, improvise a tribute, confess, cover, or collide in front of the room. Every path carries real onstage cost; none is favored by the Play.
