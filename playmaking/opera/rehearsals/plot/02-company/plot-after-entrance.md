# The Wrong Name — Plot

## Taxonomy

Dedication hour of [The Wrong Name](play.md), staging [the Unveiling Ceremony](process-unveiling.md) at [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](place-hall.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Cue

Inside [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](place-hall.md) at 2:12 p.m., forty seated neighbors hold printed programs honoring *Miriam Okafor (1948–2025)* as the room hushes for [the Unveiling Ceremony](process-unveiling.md). The oak lectern's gooseneck microphone is live, the local weekly's photographer must leave at 2:20 p.m., a youth tutoring group takes the room at 2:30 p.m., and the four paper-ribboned front-row chairs reserved for Miriam's widower Samuel Okafor and daughter Tomi Okafor sit six feet from the draped front wall.

## Action

[Marta Solis](persona-ceremony-lead.md) drives to carry [the Unveiling Ceremony](process-unveiling.md) smoothly from her closing welcome straight into the draw-cord pull and press photograph without a stall, keeping her morning promise to Samuel Okafor that the tribute would stay brief and dignified. [Theo Park](persona-plaque-lead.md) drives to steward [the Dedication Plaque](piece-plaque.md) properly so the neighborhood's permanent memorial to Miriam Okafor honors her under his printed stewardship. Their efforts collide across the two-pace gap between the live microphone and the navy velvet cloth: Marta holds the room's clock and public momentum, while Theo holds physical custody of the draw-cord and cloth—any move by Theo to inspect, hold, or interrupt the reveal breaks Marta's live ceremony before forty neighbors, and any push by Marta to pull the cord exposes Theo's plaque to every eye in the hall.

## Stage

At opening, [Marta Solis](persona-ceremony-lead.md) stands at the oak lectern in [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](place-hall.md) behind the live gooseneck microphone with her printed run-of-show card, and [Theo Park](persona-plaque-lead.md) stands two paces to her left beside [the Dedication Plaque](piece-plaque.md), veiled beneath its weighted navy velvet cloth and braided brass draw-cord with its bare bronze face unseen by either volunteer since casting.

First, at the Entrance Cue, Miriam's widower Samuel Okafor, leaning lightly on his walking cane, and her daughter Tomi Okafor, carrying white chrysanthemums and a folded printed program, enter through the rear double glass doors and walk down the center aisle into two of the reserved front-row chairs six feet from [the Dedication Plaque](piece-plaque.md). After one beat, at the Discovery Cue, [Theo Park](persona-plaque-lead.md) angles the weighted bottom corner of the navy velvet cloth an inch off the wall to check that no foundry packing tape or cord snag remains and sees the raised bronze header cast as **`MARIAM OKAFOR`** instead of **`MIRIAM OKAFOR`**—visible only to Theo before the velvet settles flat again.

## Tension

[Theo Park](persona-plaque-lead.md) privately carries sole sign-off on the foundry proof for [the Dedication Plaque](piece-plaque.md): eleven days ago, facing a fifteen-minute rush casting cutoff at 4:45 p.m., he approved the PDF on his phone without the committee's required second reviewer, assured [Marta Solis](persona-ceremony-lead.md) and the board he had verified every letter himself, and bolted the plaque up just before doors opened by sliding its kraft paper out from behind the velvet without viewing the bare bronze. Meanwhile, Marta privately promised Samuel Okafor this morning that the hall would not put his grieving family through a drawn-out or disorganized spectacle.
