# The Unveiling Ceremony

## Taxonomy

Fortieth-anniversary Saturday afternoon memorial dedication at [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](place-hall.md) in [The Wrong Name](play.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Initiated by

[Marta Solis](persona-ceremony-lead.md) switching on the oak lectern's gooseneck microphone at 2:10 p.m., quieting forty seated neighbors, and beginning her two-minute opening welcome while [Theo Park](persona-plaque-lead.md) takes his station beside [the Dedication Plaque](piece-plaque.md). Everyone in the room can tell the ceremony is running because conversations have ceased, printed programs are open on laps, the press photographer has stepped into position by the side wall, and Marta is on the final lines leading into the unveil.

## Direction

Advances straight from Marta's closing welcome into acknowledging Samuel and Tomi Okafor at the reserved front row, Marta's hand-cue to Theo, Theo pulling the braided brass draw-cord to drop the navy velvet cloth from [the Dedication Plaque](piece-plaque.md), reading the dedication aloud, and taking the 2:20 p.m. newspaper photograph before the 2:30 p.m. youth tutoring turnover.

## Lever

Driven forward by Marta's live-mic cadence, forty silent neighbors watching from six to thirty feet away, the photographer's hard 2:20 p.m. departure, and the 2:30 p.m. room handover. A volunteer can slow it for a single beat only by stretching a sentence at the lectern, tending to the front-row seating, or resting a steadying hand on the draw-cord; stopping, delaying, or redirecting the reveal requires someone to visibly hold the velvet cloth closed or speak an interruption into the hushed hall at real public cost.

## Echo

Once the ceremony runs its course, the navy velvet cloth has dropped from the front wall—putting whatever is cast on [the Dedication Plaque](piece-plaque.md) permanently into public view before Samuel and Tomi Okafor, forty program-holding neighbors, and the weekly newspaper's lens—or, if halted, leaves a still-covered wall in front of an assembled hall waiting for an explanation.
