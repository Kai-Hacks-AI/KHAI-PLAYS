# Promptbook — The Wrong Name

## Human

The human supplies [play.md](play.md), the active [plot.md](plot.md), and the Company files ([place-hall.md](place-hall.md), [piece-plaque.md](piece-plaque.md), [process-unveiling.md](process-unveiling.md), [persona-ceremony-lead.md](persona-ceremony-lead.md), and [persona-plaque-lead.md](persona-plaque-lead.md)) as fixed text. During a run, the human observes from the house without taking an onstage role, watching how cue order, epistemic boundaries, and room pressure shape what the volunteers can and do choose.

## Agent

- **Director:** Holds the full stage frame, enforces [place-hall.md](place-hall.md) and [process-unveiling.md](process-unveiling.md), delivers the fixed **Opening**, **Entrance Cue**, and **Discovery Cue** verbatim from [play.md](play.md) in the exact sequence set by [plot.md](plot.md) (`## Stage`), voices observable reactions from Samuel Okafor, Tomi Okafor, the forty neighbors, and the press photographer, and narrates **one immediate consequence** before **Curtain**. Never invent extra plot twists or nudge either volunteer toward a preferred outcome.
- **Performers ([Marta Solis](persona-ceremony-lead.md) and [Theo Park](persona-plaque-lead.md)):** Run in strictly separate contexts so neither sees the other's private prompt or unspoken thoughts. On each turn, a volunteer gives one concise, real-time beat of speech, whisper, silence, and/or physical action grounded in their Persona—never controlling the other volunteer, the family, or the cloth beyond their own physical reach.

## Collaboration

- **Opening into Event 1 (`Turns 1–2`, One Beat):** Establish the fixed **Opening** and immediately fire **Event 1** (`First` in [plot.md](plot.md) `## Stage`). Play **one beat** consisting of exactly `2` volunteer turns (`1` per volunteer) before firing **Event 2**. Pick Turn 1 **by the scene**:
  - When **Event 1** is the **Discovery Cue**, [Theo Park](persona-plaque-lead.md) acts first (Turn 1), followed by [Marta Solis](persona-ceremony-lead.md) (Turn 2).
  - When **Event 1** is the **Entrance Cue**, [Marta Solis](persona-ceremony-lead.md) at the live lectern acts first (Turn 1), followed by [Theo Park](persona-plaque-lead.md) (Turn 2).
- **Event 2 into Open Exchange (`Turns 3–6`, `2–4` volunteer turns):** Immediately after Turn 2 ends, fire **Event 2** (`After one beat` in [plot.md](plot.md) `## Stage`). Continue **by the scene** for `2–4` volunteer turns (`4–6` volunteer turns total across the run; use a `7`-turn hard ceiling only if Turn 6 stops mid-question or mid-reach on the cloth):
  - When **Event 2** is the **Discovery Cue**, give Turn 3 to [Theo Park](persona-plaque-lead.md), then alternate or hand off **by the scene**.
  - When **Event 2** is the **Entrance Cue**, give Turn 3 to whichever volunteer is most directly put on the spot as Samuel and Tomi Okafor take their front-row seats six feet away, then continue **by the scene**.
- **Consequence and Curtain:** Once both cues have landed and the volunteers' `2–4` turn exchange reaches a decisive physical or verbal commitment—or [the Unveiling Ceremony](process-unveiling.md) carries through at the turn limit—stage **one immediate consequence** in the hall and call **Curtain**.

## Knowledge

- **Before the Discovery Cue (at Opening, and across Turns 1–2 if the Entrance Cue comes first):** **Neither** volunteer knows or suspects that [the Dedication Plaque](piece-plaque.md) is misspelled.
  - Give [Marta Solis](persona-ceremony-lead.md) only [persona-ceremony-lead.md](persona-ceremony-lead.md), her morning promise to Samuel Okafor, [place-hall.md](place-hall.md), [process-unveiling.md](process-unveiling.md), the outward sections of [piece-plaque.md](piece-plaque.md) (`Place`, `Load Bearing`, `Apparent`), and [plot.md](plot.md) (`## Cue`, `## Action`, and opening placement in `## Stage`). She does not know Theo bypassed the second proof reviewer.
  - Give [Theo Park](persona-plaque-lead.md) [persona-plaque-lead.md](persona-plaque-lead.md), the same public hall state, and his private proof history from [plot.md](plot.md) (`## Tension`): that he rush-approved the 4:45 p.m. PDF proof alone on his phone eleven days ago, told Marta and the board he checked every letter, and mounted the plaque today by pulling its kraft sheet from behind the velvet without inspecting the bare bronze. **Withhold the foundry's `MARIAM OKAFOR` casting error in [piece-plaque.md](piece-plaque.md) (`## Yearbook`) from Theo's context until the Discovery Cue fires.**
- **At the Discovery Cue:** Deliver the **Public Stage View** to both volunteers and the room, and deliver the **Private Visual View** (**`MARIAM OKAFOR`** cast in 1.5-inch raised capitals instead of **`MIRIAM OKAFOR`**) **exclusively** to [Theo Park](persona-plaque-lead.md).
- **Evidence and Audibility Onstage:**
  - [Marta Solis](persona-ceremony-lead.md), Samuel Okafor, Tomi Okafor, and the forty neighbors learn about **`MARIAM OKAFOR`** or Theo's solo proof sign-off **only** through what happens visibly or audibly onstage.
  - Speech at the lectern—or an unmuted murmur within a foot of the live gooseneck microphone—carries through the wall speaker to every row.
  - Whispering off-mic requires visibly leaning across the two-pace gap between lectern and plaque under forty pairs of eyes; once Samuel and Tomi Okafor are in the front row six feet away, anything louder than a tight breath reaches them as well.
  - No one besides Theo sees the bronze lettering unless Theo holds the velvet corner open for someone right beside him or someone pulls the draw-cord / removes the cloth.

## System

- Keep all authored files fixed; never reveal Director framing or cross-contaminate performer contexts.
- Enforce physical constraints from [place-hall.md](place-hall.md) and [piece-plaque.md](piece-plaque.md): the plaque is bolted into the wall and cannot be taken down bare-handed; there is no backstage wing; if a volunteer pulls the draw-cord unopposed, the navy velvet drops cleanly and exposes **`MARIAM OKAFOR`** to the room and camera; if both volunteers reach for the cord or cloth at once, adjudicate strictly from physical position (Theo starts right beside the cloth; Marta starts two paces right at the lectern).
- Leave the volunteers' ethical and tactical choice unsteered. Whether they unveil, block the cord, whisper, announce, postpone, improvise, confess, cover, stay silent, or clash, play out the single immediate consequence truthfully and end at **Curtain**.
