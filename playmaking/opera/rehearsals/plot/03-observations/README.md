# Observations: TO CAST in *The Wrong Name*

Four isolated runs staged [play.md](../02-company/play.md) (*The Wrong Name*) from the same `2:12 p.m.` opening with the same seven Company files, public responsibilities, private stakes, **Entrance Cue**, and **Discovery Cue**. Runs [01-before-entrance-a](../04-runs/01-before-entrance-a.md) and [02-before-entrance-b](../04-runs/02-before-entrance-b.md) received only [plot-before-entrance.md](../02-company/plot-before-entrance.md); runs [03-after-entrance-a](../04-runs/03-after-entrance-a.md) and [04-after-entrance-b](../04-runs/04-after-entrance-b.md) received only [plot-after-entrance.md](../02-company/plot-after-entrance.md). Across both Plots, `Taxonomy`, `Owner`, `Cue`, `Action`, starting placement, and `Tension` were byte-for-byte identical; only the order of the plaque check and the family’s entrance in `## Stage` changed.

In all four runs, [the Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md) stayed covered beneath its navy velvet cloth at Curtain—preventing **`MARIAM OKAFOR`** from being unveiled before Miriam’s widower Samuel Okafor, daughter Tomi Okafor, and the newspaper camera—yet reversing the two events in `Stage` completely reshaped who knew what when, whether Marta trusted or verified Theo’s claim, who disclosed what to the family, and where the ceremony landed.

## 3. How Event Order Changed Available and Costly Moves (Across & Within Plots)

1. **Pre-positioning the whisper channel vs. discovering under an active unveil cue:**
   - In **Before the entrance** ([01](../04-runs/01-before-entrance-a.md), [02](../04-runs/02-before-entrance-b.md)), discovering the typo one beat early did not give enough time to resolve the crisis before the doors opened—Marta spent `Turn 2` diagnosing what she assumed was a snagged rod—but it **closed the physical and acoustic gap before the family sat down**. With Marta’s palm already over the gooseneck capsule on `Turn 2`, Theo could deliver a complete, credible confession (`MARIAM` + solo proof approval) on `Turn 3` before Marta ever welcomed the family or cued the cord.
   - In **After the entrance** ([03](../04-runs/03-after-entrance-a.md), [04](../04-runs/04-after-entrance-b.md)), Beat 1 committed both volunteers publicly: Marta had already spoken the unveiling cue into the live mic (`Turn 1`) and Theo had already greeted the family at the cord (`Turn 2`). Discovering `MARIAM OKAFOR` *after* that handoff made silent hesitation impossible—Theo immediately had to spend speech or gesture managing the front row and photographer (`Turn 3`) and could pass Marta only a bare three-word alarm without explaining *how* a verified plaque could be wrong.
2. **Immediate belief + Monday-recast push vs. skepticism + wall-side visual check:**
   - Because Marta in `01`–`02` heard Theo admit bypassing the second proof reviewer on `Turn 3`, she believed him immediately—putting her `Shadow` straight into pushing *through* the unveil (`"We recast it Monday—we're not halting this room in front of Samuel"`), which in turn forced Theo’s public `Turn 5` refusal and full confession in **both** `Before the entrance` repeats (`01` and `02` tracked the same six-turn structural arc, varying only whether Theo’s `Turn 1` signal was wholly silent mouthing in `01` vs. an off-mic murmur in `02`).
   - Because Marta in `03`–`04` heard `MARIAM` *without* the proof confession on `Turn 3`, her `Shadow` read Theo’s freeze as stage fright under a dark hem—driving **both** `After the entrance` runs into physical cloth-edge verification at the wall. Within `After the entrance`, Marta’s `Turn 4` choice split the trajectory: stepping directly to the wall on `Turn 4` in [03](../04-runs/03-after-entrance-a.md) gave her `Turn 6` to return to the mic and control the public disclosure (shielding Theo while trapping him earlier on `"No loose bolts—you already told them top pin"`), whereas re-cueing the cord on `Turn 4` in [04](../04-runs/04-after-entrance-b.md) delayed visual verification to `Turns 6–7` behind a switched-off mic until Tomi Okafor stepped up from the front row in the silence.

## 4. Extra Direction Supplied by the Directors

- **Invariant opening and cues (`0` text changes):** All four Directors delivered the `2:12 p.m.` Opening, **Entrance Cue**, and **Discovery Cue** verbatim from [play.md](../02-company/play.md), varying strictly their order per [plot.md](../02-company/plot.md) (`## Stage`).
- **On-disk epistemic isolation (all 4 runs):** Each volunteer performer ran in a separate subagent reading only spoiler-free `cast-marta/` or `cast-theo/` files (`0` mentions of `MARIAM` on disk), receiving only onstage observations and (for Theo at Discovery) the `Private Visual View`.
- **Pre-pull beat boundary on `Turn 2` in `After the entrance` ([03](../04-runs/03-after-entrance-a.md#L132), [04](../04-runs/04-after-entrance-b.md#L138)):** Because [play.md](../02-company/play.md#L22) opens on the closing lines of Marta’s welcome, Marta cued the unveil on `Turn 1` in both `After the entrance` runs; both Directors bounded Theo’s uninformed `Turn 2` to stepping into position with hands at the cord/hem *before* pulling so `Event 2` (`Discovery Cue`) could fire intact.
- **Physical cloth-tension and microphone rulings:** In [03](../04-runs/03-after-entrance-a.md#L133) and [04](../04-runs/04-after-entrance-b.md#L139), Directors enforced [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md#L32) by ruling that Marta’s one-inch bottom-corner tug stayed checked taut by Theo’s left-hand clamp until Theo deliberately slackened the edge on his next turn (`Turn 5` in `03`; `Turn 7` via the `7`-turn mid-question/mid-reach ceiling in `04`). In `01`, `02`, and `04`, Directors allowed firm palm-covering (or switching off the base toggle in `04` `Turn 6`) to mute breath-level whispers on the live gooseneck mic while keeping the leaning posture visible to the six-foot front row.
