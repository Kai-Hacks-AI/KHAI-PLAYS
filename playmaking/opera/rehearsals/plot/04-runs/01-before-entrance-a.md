# The Wrong Name — Staged Run (`run-01`)

- **Play:** [The Wrong Name](../02-company/play.md) ([promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md))
- **Active Plot:** [plot.md](../02-company/plot.md) (*Discovery Cue* first; *Entrance Cue* after one beat)
- **Place / Piece / Process:** [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](../02-company/place-hall.md) · [the Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md) · [the Unveiling Ceremony](../02-company/process-unveiling.md)
- **Cast:** [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md) (*Ceremony Lead*) · [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md) (*Plaque & Dedication Lead*)

---

## Opening (2:12 p.m.)

Forty neighbors sit hushed in folding chairs inside [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](../02-company/place-hall.md), holding ivory half-sheet programs titled *Honoring Miriam Okafor (1948–2025)*. At the oak lectern, [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md) stands behind the live gooseneck microphone on the closing lines of her brief welcome, with the press photographer poised by the side window ahead of a hard 2:20 p.m. departure, a youth group booked for 2:30 p.m., and four paper-ribboned front-row chairs reserved for the Okafor family sitting empty six feet from the front wall. Two paces to Marta's left, [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md) stands beside [the Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md), draped smooth beneath its weighted navy velvet cloth and braided brass draw-cord. Neither volunteer has seen the bare bronze face since casting.

## Event 1 — Discovery Cue

- **Public Stage View (seen by Marta and the hall):** Beside [the Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md), [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md) reaches down and angles the weighted bottom corner of the navy velvet cloth an inch off the wall, glancing underneath for a second as if checking that no foundry packing tape or cord snag remains, before letting the velvet settle flat over the plaque again. From the lectern and the chairs, only that brief hand check is visible; the bronze face stays hidden.
- **Private Visual View (seen ONLY by [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md)):** Behind the lifted corner of the navy velvet, the 1.5-inch raised polished-brass capitals across the top header line catch the light: **`MARIAM OKAFOR`**—not **`MIRIAM OKAFOR`**.

---

## One Beat (`Turns 1–2`)

### Turn 1 — [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md)

Theo's shoulders lock rigid as the velvet settles flat; his right thumb and forefinger press tight along the seam of his right jacket pocket while his left hand closes firmly over the braided brass draw-cord at the plaque's right edge, pinning it against the wall. He takes a crisp step into the narrow gap toward [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md)'s left side to enter her sightline, fixes his gaze on the side of her face, gives one tight, unmistakable shake of his head, and mouths silently off-angle from the gooseneck microphone: *"Hold on—don't cue it."*

### Turn 2 — [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md)

Marta taps the top edge of her printed run-of-show card twice against the oak lectern rim right beside the live gooseneck microphone, keeps her shoulders squared and a warm smile directed out over the chairs, and speaks smoothly into the mic: "And as we honor forty years of Miriam's Saturday pantry, we'll give our friend by the window just a moment to set his lens." Holding that composed posture, she presses her left palm firmly over the microphone head, leans half a pace left toward [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md), flicks a quick glance down to his hand pinning the brass draw-cord, and whispers tightly off-mic: *"Is the rod snagged? Ease your grip, Theo—we lose our photographer at two-twenty."*

*(Through the wall speaker above the aisle, Marta's two card-taps carry as a faint double-thump; by the side window, the press photographer lifts his camera an inch in a brief ready nod.)*

---

## Event 2 — Entrance Cue

At the rear of [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](../02-company/place-hall.md), the double glass doors ease open. Miriam's widower Samuel Okafor, leaning lightly on his walking cane, and her daughter Tomi Okafor, carrying white chrysanthemums and a folded printed program, walk quietly down the center aisle as seated neighbors turn with warm nods, taking two of the reserved front-row chairs six feet directly in front of [the Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md).

*(Six feet directly in front of the plaque and lectern, Samuel settles both hands atop his cane and looks up steadily toward the navy velvet cloth; beside him, Tomi rests the white chrysanthemums and her folded printed program across her knees.)*

---

## Open Volunteer Exchange (`Turns 3–6`)

### Turn 3 — [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md)

Theo dips his chin in a quiet, deferential nod toward Samuel and Tomi Okafor as they settle six feet away, keeping his left hand closed fast around the braided brass draw-cord against the wall. His shoulders stay locked rigid, right thumb and forefinger pinched hard on the seam of his right jacket pocket; angling his mouth just an inch from [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md)'s ear behind her muffled microphone, barely moving his lips so the breath won't carry to the front row, he whispers: *"Not the rod. The bronze says M-A-R-I-A-M—with an A. I missed it on the rush proof alone. Don't drop this cloth in front of Samuel."*

*(Six feet away in the front row, Samuel returns Theo's slight nod and waits attentively; Tomi's gaze flicks briefly between the veiled plaque and the two volunteers leaning close between lectern and wall, though Theo's tight breath does not carry to the chairs.)*

### Turn 4 — [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md)

Marta goes still for half a second, her eyes snapping to [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md)'s at close range; keeping her left palm pressed tight over the microphone head, she whispers back barely above a breath: *"One letter? We'll make the foundry recast it Monday—we are not stalling this hall in front of Samuel. Let go of the cord."* Smoothly lifting her palm from the gooseneck, she squares back behind the oak lectern, taps the top edge of her run-of-show card twice against the wooden rim, and looks down six feet to the reserved front row with a warm, steady smile. "Samuel, Tomi—thank you for letting your neighbors stand with you this afternoon," she says clearly into the live microphone. "Forty households gave from the heart so everyone who walks into Alder Street Hall knows the woman who built our Saturday table—Miriam Okafor." She steps half a pace back from the live microphone, angles her run-of-show card toward Theo beside the draped plaque, and extends an open left hand toward the braided brass draw-cord.

*(Through the wall speaker, the two card-taps carry softly before Marta's voice fills the hushed room. Six feet in front of the wall, Samuel inclines his head over his cane and lifts his eyes expectantly to the navy velvet cloth while Tomi rests a hand lightly on her father's sleeve; by the side window, the press photographer brings his viewfinder to his eye, finger poised on the shutter, as forty seated neighbors look straight to Theo at the cord.)*

### Turn 5 — [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md)

Theo glances once at [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md)'s extended left hand and does not release the cord—instead pressing his left palm flat over the braided brass draw-cord against the navy velvet's edge so the cloth stays shut. His shoulders lock rigid and his right thumb and forefinger pinch tight along the seam of his right jacket pocket; turning squarely toward the reserved chairs six feet away beside the live lectern microphone, he looks straight down to Samuel and Tomi Okafor and speaks clearly into the quiet room, his voice tight but steady: "Samuel, Tomi—please forgive me, I can't pull this cord today. Underneath this cloth, Miriam's name was cast *M-A-R-I-A-M*—not because of Marta or the committee, and not the foundry, but because I approved the rush proof alone on my phone without our second reviewer after promising I'd checked every letter. Miriam was the first neighbor to welcome me into this hall, and I won't uncover the wrong name in front of you or a camera."

*(Carried cleanly across the hushed timber-ceilinged hall—and picked up faintly along the side of the live gooseneck microphone—Theo's words draw a low, startled murmur across the forty chairs as several neighbors glance down at the printed programs on their laps. By the side window, the press photographer lowers his camera half an inch from his eye without clicking the shutter; six feet away, Tomi looks sharply up at Theo while Samuel sits very still with both hands resting atop his cane.)*

### Turn 6 — [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md)

Marta's extended left hand hangs suspended in midair for a beat, then lowers slowly to her side; her right hand lifts the printed run-of-show card half an inch above the oak lectern rim, stops short without tapping, and lays the card flat and silent against the wood. She glances once at [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md)'s palm pinning the brass draw-cord against the navy velvet, steps back up to the live gooseneck microphone, and looks straight down six feet to Samuel and Tomi Okafor. "Samuel, Tomi—as Events Chair, that final check belonged to me too, and Theo is right to keep the cloth closed," she says into the microphone, her voice quiet and unhurried. "The Civic Association will have the bronze recast properly at our own expense—not a dollar from the forty households who gave—before that velvet comes down. Every program in this hall carries her true name, Miriam Okafor, and with your permission, Samuel, we will simply read her dedication aloud for you now and keep our promise to make this afternoon brief." Resting one hand still atop the flat card on the oak rim, she falls silent behind the microphone and holds her gaze steadily on Samuel and Tomi.

---

## Immediate Consequence

Bolted to the front wall of [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](../02-company/place-hall.md), [the Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md) remains completely hidden beneath its weighted navy velvet cloth, [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md)'s left palm still pressed steady over the braided brass draw-cord—leaving [the Unveiling Ceremony](../02-company/process-unveiling.md) halted short of its centerpiece reveal before forty neighbors holding programs for a memorial wall that stays covered.

Six feet away in the reserved front row, Tomi Okafor exhales sharply, glances down for a beat at *Miriam Okafor (1948–2025)* printed across her folded program, then rises quietly, steps forward to the base of the front wall directly beneath the dark navy velvet, lays the white chrysanthemums on the hardwood floor under the veiled plaque, and returns to her chair to set her hand over her father's wrist. Samuel Okafor looks for a long, unhurried second from Theo's hand on the brass cord to [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md) waiting behind the oak lectern, then gives a single grave nod over the head of his walking cane. "Leave the cloth where it is, Theo," Samuel says quietly into the stillness, clear enough for the front rows to hear. "Read her words from the page, Marta—and spell her right when the new bronze comes." Across the forty folding chairs, the low murmur settles into a somber, attentive hush as neighbors hold their ivory programs open; by the side window ahead of his 2:20 p.m. cutoff, the weekly photographer lets the camera hang unclicked against his chest for the missed unveiling shot, raising his lens only once without flash toward the white chrysanthemums resting beneath the unopened velvet as Marta lifts the printed program to read.

***Curtain.***

# Director’s Note

## (a) Epistemic States, Shift in Action Costs, and Outcome

- **Opening into Event 1 (`Turns 1–2`, One Beat):** [plot.md](../02-company/plot.md) (`## Stage`) fired the **Discovery Cue** `First`. Immediately at 2:12 p.m., **only** [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md) saw **`MARIAM OKAFOR`** under the bottom corner of the velvet, while [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md) and the forty neighbors saw only a routine one-second hem check, and the Okafor family had not yet entered.
  - *Available and costly actions:* Because the front row was still empty, Theo could physically claim custody of the braided brass draw-cord and silently signal Marta (*"Hold on—don't cue it"*) in Turn 1 without immediately confronting Samuel and Tomi six feet away, though doing so broke his composed posture under forty watching eyes. Ignorant of both the casting typo and Theo's solo proof sign-off, Marta read his head-shake through her Shadow as a minor snag or stage fright: in Turn 2 she stretched her live-mic cadence by a single beat (buying a second on the photographer's lens), covered the mic head with her palm, and whispered for Theo to ease his grip before 2:20 p.m.
- **Event 2 into Open Exchange (`Turns 3–6`):** Immediately after Turn 2, the **Entrance Cue** placed Samuel and Tomi Okafor six feet directly in front of the draped wall.
  - *Available and costly actions:* Private coordination collapsed into breath-level whispers under the family's direct gaze, and halting the ceremony now meant disappointing Samuel to his face after Marta's morning promise. In Turn 3, Theo used the muffled mic gap to confess both **`MARIAM`** and his solo rush proof approval to Marta, erasing their epistemic asymmetry. Once Marta knew the truth, she faced a sharp split in Turn 4: halt her own program publicly, or gamble that a single wrong letter could be unveiled for the 2:20 p.m. photo and quietly recast Monday. Driven by her morning promise against a disorganized spectacle, Marta chose momentum—whispering *"We'll make the foundry recast it Monday... Let go of the cord"* and publicly hand-cueing Theo on the live mic. That pushed the entire cost of stopping onto Theo in Turn 5: with Marta's hand extended and the photographer's shutter poised, Theo could only save the family from seeing **`MARIAM OKAFOR`** by keeping his palm flat on the cord and confessing his solo error aloud to the room while explicitly clearing Marta and the committee. Faced with an already-halted reveal in Turn 6, Marta abandoned her card-tap Tell, stepped back to the mic to share institutional responsibility as Events Chair, pledged an Association-funded recast, and asked Samuel's permission to read the printed dedication aloud instead.

## (b) Director Direction and Mediation

- **Strict Context Partition:** Performers ran in isolated subagent contexts reading only their assigned cast files; Theo's onboarding supplied his 1:45 p.m. kraft-paper mounting and 4:45 p.m. solo PDF sign-off with zero mention of **`MARIAM OKAFOR`** until Turn 1 delivered the verbatim **Discovery Cue**.
- **Turn Selection (`Turn 3`):** Per [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md) (`## Collaboration`), Turn 1 went to Theo after the **Discovery Cue** and Turn 2 to Marta. When the **Entrance Cue** fired as Event 2 right on the heels of Marta's whispered question (*"Is the rod snagged?"*), I assigned Turn 3 to Theo as the volunteer most directly put on the spot—caught between Marta's waiting question half a pace right and Samuel and Tomi settling six feet ahead.
- **Room Physics and Hall Reactions:** Between turns, I voiced only brief observable acoustic and hall reactions grounded in [place-hall.md](../02-company/place-hall.md) and the Personas (the wall-speaker pickup of Marta's double card-tap, the photographer's ready nod and lowered camera, Tomi's glance at the leaning volunteers, and the hall's murmur when Theo spoke aloud), leaving every tactical and ethical choice unsteered.

## (c) Staging Friction: Director Choices vs. Gaps in the Play or Plot

1. **Initialization vs. `First` Discovery Cue (*Staging Choice* vs. *Plot Consequence*):**
   - Because [plot.md](../02-company/plot.md) fires the **Discovery Cue** `First` right after the Opening while [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md) (`## Knowledge`) requires withholding **`MARIAM OKAFOR`** before Discovery, combining performer setup with Turn 1 would have handed Theo the casting error from his very first token. *Staging choice:* I separated onboarding from Turn 1 so Theo first inhabited his pre-discovery stewardship before receiving the **Private Visual View**. *Plot consequence (structural, not a flaw):* With Discovery placed `First`, Theo never takes an onstage action under false confidence—that beat only occurs onstage when [plot.md](../02-company/plot.md) reverses the two cues.
2. **Palm-Muffling a Live Gooseneck Microphone (*Gap in Place / Promptbook* vs. *Staging Choice*):**
   - [place-hall.md](../02-company/place-hall.md) and [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md) specify that the switched-on gooseneck microphone carries even a sharp breath or index-card tap through the wall speaker and mentions "avoiding or covering" the live mic, yet neither file defines whether pressing a bare palm directly onto the live capsule cleanly mutes speech or sends a loud thump/muffled bleed to the room. *Staging choice:* I allowed Marta's firm palm-cover in Turns 2–4 to shield their breath-level whispers acoustically while exposing their heads-together posture visually to Tomi six feet away. If touching the live capsule is meant to carry audible speaker risk, [place-hall.md](../02-company/place-hall.md) should state that explicitly.
3. **Origin of the Typo After Withholding `## Yearbook` (*Gap Between Play Cue and Piece Backstory*):**
   - [piece-plaque.md](../02-company/piece-plaque.md) (`## Yearbook`) establishes both that the foundry mistyped **`MARIAM OKAFOR`** while re-keying the layout *and* that Theo missed it on the 4:45 p.m. PDF proof. However, [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md) withholds `## Yearbook` from Theo before Discovery and delivers only the visual **Discovery Cue** from [play.md](../02-company/play.md) at Discovery. *Result:* Keeping [play.md](../02-company/play.md) fixed, Theo never received explicit confirmation of whether the foundry introduced the typo onto the PDF proof or only during casting—so in Turn 5 he inferred from his `## Shadow` that the error had been on the PDF proof and even absolved the foundry aloud (*"not the foundry"*), despite carrying his phone and the foundry packing slip in his jacket pocket ([persona-plaque-lead.md](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md) `## Tell`). If the author wants Theo to know (or be able to check on his phone/slip) that the foundry re-keyed the typo onto the 4:45 p.m. proof, [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md) should clarify what Theo remembers or can verify at Discovery.
