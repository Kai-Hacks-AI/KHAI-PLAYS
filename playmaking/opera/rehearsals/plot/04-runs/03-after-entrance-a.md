# The Wrong Name — Rehearsal Run (`Entrance Cue` → `Discovery Cue`)

Staged from [play.md](../02-company/play.md) with [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md) and the active [plot.md](../02-company/plot.md), featuring [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md) (`Ceremony Lead`) and [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md) (`Plaque Lead`) during [the Unveiling Ceremony](../02-company/process-unveiling.md) for [the Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md) inside [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](../02-company/place-hall.md).

---

## Performance

### Opening (2:12 p.m.)

Forty neighbors sit hushed in folding chairs inside [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](../02-company/place-hall.md), holding ivory half-sheet programs titled *Honoring Miriam Okafor (1948–2025)*. At the oak lectern, [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md) stands behind the live gooseneck microphone on the closing lines of her brief welcome, with the press photographer poised by the side window ahead of a hard 2:20 p.m. departure, a youth group booked for 2:30 p.m., and four paper-ribboned front-row chairs reserved for the Okafor family sitting empty six feet from the front wall. Two paces to Marta's left, [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md) stands beside [the Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md), draped smooth beneath its weighted navy velvet cloth and braided brass draw-cord. Neither volunteer has seen the bare bronze face since casting.

### Event 1 — Entrance Cue

At the rear of [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](../02-company/place-hall.md), the double glass doors ease open. Miriam's widower Samuel Okafor, leaning lightly on his walking cane, and her daughter Tomi Okafor, carrying white chrysanthemums and a folded printed program, walk quietly down the center aisle as seated neighbors turn with warm nods, taking two of the reserved front-row chairs six feet directly in front of [the Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md).

---

### Beat 1

#### Turn 1 — [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md)

Marta pauses gently at the oak lectern just long enough for the aisle to settle, squaring the bottom edge of her printed run-of-show card silently against the wood as her gaze drops warmly to the reserved front-row chairs six feet away. She gives Samuel and Tomi Okafor a quiet, respectful nod of welcome, then leans slightly into the live gooseneck microphone, her shoulders open and her voice steady and unhurried across the hall.

**Marta** *(into the live microphone)*: "Samuel, Tomi—we are so honored to have you right here in front with us this afternoon. Everything in this room—forty Saturday mornings a year, forty neighbor households pitching in together—comes back to the table Miriam built on Alder Street, and it belongs on this wall for good."

She lifts the run-of-show card lightly in her right hand, steps half a pace back from the live microphone, turns half toward [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md) two paces to her left with a composed, encouraging smile, and extends an open left hand toward the braided brass draw-cord beside the navy velvet cloth.

**Marta** *(into the live microphone, carrying cleanly from half a pace back)*: "Neighbors, with our deepest gratitude—*The Miriam Okafor Community Pantry Wall.* Theo, whenever you're ready."

*(Six feet away in the front row, Samuel Okafor rests both hands atop his walking cane and looks up warmly toward the veiled plaque; beside him, Tomi Okafor settles the white chrysanthemums on her lap over her folded program, and by the side window the press photographer raises his camera to his eye.)*

#### Turn 2 — [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md)

Theo straightens in his dark jacket, returning Marta's smile with a brief, earnest nod, then turns his gaze six feet forward to Samuel and Tomi Okafor and inclines his head respectfully in silent welcome.

**Theo** *(aloud from beside the wall, quiet, warm, off-mic, carrying gently to the front row)*: "Thank you, Marta. Welcome, Mr. Okafor—Tomi."

He steps half a pace closer into the narrow gap between the lectern and the right edge of the veiled [Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md), lifting his right hand to rest lightly against the loop of the braided brass draw-cord while his left hand lowers to the weighted bottom corner of the navy velvet hem, fingers settling at the edge of the fabric for a quick check of the hang before pulling.

---

### Event 2 — Discovery Cue

- **Public Stage View (seen by Marta and the hall):** Beside [the Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md), [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md) reaches down and angles the weighted bottom corner of the navy velvet cloth an inch off the wall, glancing underneath for a second as if checking that no foundry packing tape or cord snag remains, before letting the velvet settle flat over the plaque again. From the lectern and the chairs, only that brief hand check is visible; the bronze face stays hidden.
- **Private Visual View (seen ONLY by [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md)):** Behind the lifted corner of the navy velvet, the 1.5-inch raised polished-brass capitals across the top header line catch the light: **`MARIAM OKAFOR`**—not **`MIRIAM OKAFOR`**.

---

### Open Volunteer Exchange

#### Turn 3 — [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md)

As the bottom corner of the navy velvet settles flat against the bronze, Theo freezes. His breath catches silently in his throat; his shoulders lock rigid, and his right hand drops off the loop of the cord to his side, thumb and forefinger pressing tight along the seam of his right jacket pocket. His left hand slides up to clamp the braided brass draw-cord and the right edge of the navy velvet cloth together against the wall, holding the drape shut.

His eyes flick for a fraction of a second to the front row six feet away, where he forces a brief, stiff nod, before speaking out softly from the wall.

**Theo** *(aloud to the front of the room, off-mic, quiet and slightly tight)*: "Pardon me just one second—the cord's caught on the top pin."

Stepping into the narrow gap between [the Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md) and the lectern while keeping his left hand anchored back on the closed velvet edge and draw-cord, Theo catches [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md)'s eye with a tiny, urgent shake of his head. He leans across the gap toward her left shoulder—angling his mouth away from the live gooseneck microphone and the front row—and lets out the barest thread of a whisper.

**Theo** *(in a tight, barely audible breath toward Marta)*: "Marta—stop. Don't unveil it. It says *Mariam*—M-A-R-I-A-M."

*(In the hushed hall, all forty pairs of eyes watch Theo clamp the velvet edge and lean sharply toward Marta's shoulder. Six feet away, Samuel Okafor shifts his grip slightly on his cane with a polite, waiting look toward the "caught" cord; Tomi Okafor's brow creases faintly as she watches the whispered exchange; by the side window, the press photographer lowers his camera half an inch and glances briefly at his wristwatch—2:14 p.m.)*

#### Turn 4 — [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md)

Marta's smile holds steady for the front row even as her jaw tightens a fraction; in her right hand, she taps the top edge of her printed run-of-show card twice against the oak lectern rim—*thump-thump*, close enough for the live gooseneck microphone to carry the faint double-tap through the wall speaker.

She steps cleanly back behind the lectern, squaring her shoulders toward Samuel and Tomi Okafor and the side window with composed, unhurried warmth.

**Marta** *(into the live microphone)*: "New brass hardware always asks for one extra second on opening day—bear with us just a moment, neighbors, let me give Theo a second pair of hands."

Leaving the printed card flat on the oak lectern so it won't rustle near the live mic, Marta steps smoothly across the two-pace gap to stand flush beside the right edge of the veiled [Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md), angling her back slightly between the bottom hem and the front row six feet away. For the room's sightline, she raises her right hand lightly toward the upper brass rod beside Theo's clamped left hand; lower down, shielded by her hip from the chairs, her left fingers pinch the weighted bottom corner of the navy velvet cloth and tilt it a single inch off the wall as she dips her chin to peer up into the narrow gap beneath the fabric.

**Marta** *(whispered off-mic to Theo, barely audible, lips scarcely moving behind a calm stage expression)*: "You barely lifted the hem in the dark—let me look. That photographer walks out at two-twenty."

*(Because Theo's left hand is clamping the right edge of the velvet and draw-cord flat against the wall above her fingers, Marta's one-inch tug at the bottom corner pulls taut against his grip, showing only the dark bottom rim of the bronze. Across the forty chairs, a brief, sympathetic ripple of smiles answers Marta's "new brass hardware" line before the hush settles back in; Tomi Okafor leans subtly forward over the white chrysanthemums, watching both volunteers' hands at the cloth as the photographer checks his watch at 2:15 p.m. and steps closer along the side wall.)*

#### Turn 5 — [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md)

Theo's shoulders stay locked rigid; his right thumb and forefinger press hard for a beat along the seam of his right jacket pocket before he raises his right hand to the upper brass rod beside Marta's, angling his right shoulder slightly outward to screen the right edge of [the Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md) from the side window.

With his left hand, he keeps his thumb and forefinger hooked tight around the upper loop of the braided brass draw-cord against the top rod so the drape cannot part or drop, while easing his palm and lower fingers two inches off the vertical edge of the navy velvet—releasing the tautness above Marta's pinched corner while keeping the cloth hung flat toward the front row six feet away.

He tilts his head slightly toward the upper corner beside Marta's temple, eyes fixed on the brass rod as if checking a jammed pin, and speaks in a tight, barely audible breath behind near-motionless lips.

**Theo** *(in a tight breath right at Marta's ear, no louder than air)*: "Top line—third letter. I cleared the rush proof alone on my phone. Say the wall bolt's loose—we can't drop this on Samuel, I'll pay to recast it Monday."

*(Angling up through the narrow two-inch channel between the eased navy velvet and the dark statuary-bronze face, a slice of side-window light catches the 1.5-inch raised polished-brass capitals across the top header line for Marta standing flush beside Theo: **`MARIAM OKAFOR`**. In the third row, a folding chair creaks softly; six feet away, Samuel Okafor leans an inch forward over his cane while Tomi's gaze rises from the velvet hem to Marta's face, and at 2:16 p.m. the photographer lifts his finger onto the shutter button.)*

#### Turn 6 — [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md)

For one still beat beside [the Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md), Marta's eyes stay fixed up into the narrow gap beneath the cloth; the muscles along her jaw set hard, without a sound escaping her lips. Smoothly, she eases the weighted bottom corner of the navy velvet flat against the bronze face so the fabric hangs straight and closed toward the chairs, and lowers her right hand from the upper brass rod.

**Marta** *(whispered off-mic to Theo, a brisk, barely audible breath behind composed lips)*: "Keep that cord held shut. No loose bolts—you already told them top pin."

Turning cleanly without haste, Marta walks the two paces back behind the oak lectern. She picks up her printed run-of-show card and taps its top edge twice against the oak lectern rim—*thump-thump*, carrying faintly through the live gooseneck microphone to the wall speaker—before squaring her shoulders and meeting Tomi and Samuel Okafor's gaze six feet away.

**Marta** *(into the live microphone, clear, steady, composed civic warmth)*: "Samuel, Tomi—and neighbors. That isn't a caught pin. We just checked beneath the cloth, and the foundry cast Miriam's first name with an *A* instead of an *I* on the top line. We aren't going to drop the velvet on the wrong spelling of Miriam Okafor's name—not in front of her family, and not on Alder Street's wall. The cloth stays closed today, a corrected bronze plaque goes into casting Monday morning at zero cost to the neighborhood fund, and we will mount it right here where it belongs."

She glances down for half a second at her printed card, lifts her gaze warmly back to Samuel and Tomi, and continues without letting a silence open in the room.

**Marta** *(into the live microphone)*: "What doesn't wait until Monday is every word underneath her name—the tribute forty households came here to honor: *Miriam Okafor, 1948 to 2025—who fed Alder Street without asking why.* Before our photographer has to step out at two-twenty, let's gather our dedication photo right at the front row—Samuel, please stay comfortable right where you're seated, with Tomi and the flowers right beside you."

Marta steps half a pace to the right of the lectern, extending a calm, open hand slightly toward the side window to cue the photographer forward.

---

### Immediate Consequence

Across the forty folding chairs of [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](../02-company/place-hall.md), a quiet, startled intake of breath ripples over the open *Honoring Miriam Okafor (1948–2025)* programs—a few low murmurs (*"With an A?"* / *"Thank goodness they looked"*) rising and subsiding as several neighbors glance from the printed program line naming *Theo Park, Dedication Steward* up to the front wall, where [the Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md) stays hidden beneath its flat navy velvet cloth and [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md)'s left hand remains hooked around the unpulled brass draw-cord.

Six feet in front of the covered plaque, Tomi Okafor lets out a sharp, quiet breath; her fingers tighten around the stems of the white chrysanthemums on her lap as her eyes cut briefly from [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md) at the lectern to Theo standing silent beside the shroud, before she reaches her free hand across to rest steadily on her father's sleeve. Beside her, Samuel Okafor sits motionless for a long second, both hands folded over the handle of his walking cane, looking up at the dark rectangle of velvet where his wife's bronze tablet hangs unseen; then he gives Marta a single, slow nod—receiving the honesty that kept the wrong spelling covered, even as his shoulders settle heavier into the reserved chair. Stepping in quickly from the side window ahead of his 2:20 p.m. departure, the weekly newspaper's photographer drops to one knee in the center aisle and raises his camera, framing Samuel and Tomi Okafor with the white chrysanthemums in the foreground while, behind them on the wall, the unveiled centerpiece of [the Unveiling Ceremony](../02-company/process-unveiling.md) hangs closed in dark navy velvet as the shutter clicks twice in the hushed hall.

*Curtain.*

# Director’s Note

### (a) Epistemic Timeline, Shifting Action Costs, and What Unfolded

- **Opening & Event 1 (`Turns 1–2`, pre-Discovery):** Because [plot.md](../02-company/plot.md) (`## Stage`) fired the **Entrance Cue** first, Samuel and Tomi Okafor walked down the center aisle into the front row six feet away while **neither** [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md) nor [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md) knew or suspected any error on [the Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md). Acting on her morning promise to Samuel to keep [the Unveiling Ceremony](../02-company/process-unveiling.md) brief and dignified ahead of the 2:20 p.m. press cutoff, Marta warmly welcomed the family on the live microphone and cued Theo to unveil on **Turn 1** (`"Theo, whenever you're ready"`). Theo greeted the family off-mic and stepped to the draw-cord and hem on **Turn 2**. Before discovery, unveiling carried zero apparent risk, while any delay lacked cause.
- **Event 2 (`Discovery Cue`) into `Turn 3`:** Lifting the bottom velvet corner an inch, **only** Theo saw **`MARIAM OKAFOR`** while Marta and the forty seated neighbors saw only a routine hem check. Because the Okafor family was already seated six feet away and Marta had already handed off the unveil on the live mic, halting the ceremony carried immediate public cost: Theo clamped the cord and velvet shut against the wall, improvised a thin mechanical excuse aloud to the front row (`"the cord's caught on the top pin"`), and visibly leaned across the two-pace gap under forty pairs of eyes to whisper `"Marta—stop. Don't unveil it. It says Mariam—M-A-R-I-A-M"`, withholding his solo proof sign-off.
- **`Turn 4` into `Turn 5` (Verification & Confession at the Wall):** Still lacking visual proof—and reading hesitation against her `Shadow` fear of a disorganized stall before the 2:20 p.m. photographer cutoff—Marta tapped her card twice on the lectern (`thump-thump` over the speaker), covered smoothly on-mic (`"New brass hardware always asks for one extra second"`), and stepped flush beside the plaque to inspect the face herself. Because Theo's left hand clamped the right edge taut, Marta could not see the header until **Turn 5**, when Theo kept the upper cord loop hooked shut, eased the vertical edge open two inches for her sightline, confessed in a tight breath at her ear that he had cleared the rush proof alone on his phone, and pleaded with her to blame a loose wall bolt while promising to pay for a Monday recast.
- **`Turn 6` into Immediate Consequence:** Once Marta saw **`MARIAM OKAFOR`** with her own eyes, every remaining option carried sharp cost: unveiling would expose the wrong name to Samuel and Tomi six feet away; claiming a `"loose bolt"` after Theo had already said `"caught on the top pin"` risked transparent public evasion. Marta smoothed the cloth shut, told Theo off-mic to keep the cord closed (`"No loose bolts—you already told them top pin"`), returned to the live mic, and publicly disclosed the misspelling while shielding Theo's solo proof failure behind `"the foundry cast Miriam's first name with an A instead of an I"` and `"at zero cost to the neighborhood fund"`, salvaging the spoken dedication and front-row press photo beneath a still-shrouded wall.

### (b) Extra Direction and Mediation Supplied as Director

- **Strict context and sightline isolation:** Ran [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md) and [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md) in isolated subagent contexts reading only their assigned cast files, withholding `MARIAM OKAFOR` from Theo until **Event 2** (`Discovery Cue`) and delivering only the **Public Stage View** to Marta at **Event 2** so she knew nothing of the error until Theo's Turn 3 whisper and saw the bronze lettering only after Theo eased his grip on **Turn 5**.
- **Turn 2 pre-pull beat boundary:** After Marta's Turn 1 unveil handoff (`"Theo, whenever you're ready"`), directed Theo on **Turn 2** to play the single beat receiving the handoff up to placing his hands at the cord and hem **without** pulling the draw-cord yet, preserving the closed velvet cloth so the fixed **Discovery Cue** (`Event 2`) could fire verbatim immediately afterward.
- **Physical cloth adjudication (`Turn 4` → `Turn 5`):** Enforced [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md)'s sightline rule (*"No one besides Theo sees the bronze lettering unless Theo holds the velvet corner open for someone right beside him or someone pulls the draw-cord / removes the cloth"*): ruled that Marta's Turn 4 one-inch tug at the bottom corner pulled taut against Theo's left-hand clamp higher up the right edge, leaving it to Theo on **Turn 5** whether to ease the cloth open for her or keep it clamped shut.
- **Room pressure and Turn 6 cutoff:** Voiced only quiet, non-directive hall cues (Samuel's posture over his cane, Tomi's gaze, a third-row chair creak, and the photographer's watch advancing from 2:14 to 2:16 p.m.) and called **Immediate Consequence** after **Turn 6** (`6` volunteer turns total) in strict accordance with [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md), since Turn 6 ended in a completed public commitment rather than a mid-question or mid-reach exchange.

### (c) Points of Strain — Staging Choice vs. Gap in the Play or Plot

- **Authored seam between late-welcome Opening and `Entrance → Discovery` cue order (Plot/Play tension vs. Director staging choice):** In [play.md](../02-company/play.md), the **Opening (2:12 p.m.)** places [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md) already *"on the closing lines of her brief welcome,"* and [persona-ceremony-lead.md](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md) defines her `## Action` as stepping back and cueing Theo to pull the draw-cord. When [plot.md](../02-company/plot.md) fires the **Entrance Cue** `First` and delays the **Discovery Cue** until `After one beat` (`Turns 1–2`), Marta naturally completes her welcome and cues the unveil on **Turn 1**—leaving an uninformed Theo on **Turn 2** with a direct public cue to pull the cord *before* **Event 2** (`Discovery Cue`) has happened. Bounding Turn 2 as the pre-pull beat (matching [persona-plaque-lead.md](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md)'s `## Action`, *"before the cloth moves"*) was a **Director staging choice** to keep [play.md](../02-company/play.md) fixed; the underlying pinch is a **structural seam in the Play/Plot pairing** whenever `Entrance` precedes `Discovery` by a full two-turn beat after the welcome is nearly finished.
- **Turn-budget compression after shared verification (Staging adherence choice):** Because verifying the hidden bronze across two isolated contexts required three post-discovery turns (**Turn 3** halt/whisper → **Turn 4** cover/step-in → **Turn 5** eased-edge reveal/confession), Marta's public announcement landed on **Turn 6**—the fourth turn of the open exchange (`6` turns total). Calling consequence after Turn 6 rather than invoking the `7`-turn emergency ceiling was a **Director adherence choice** honoring [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md) (`use a 7-turn hard ceiling only if Turn 6 stops mid-question or mid-reach on the cloth`). Dramatically, it works cleanly—leaving Theo silently holding the cord beneath Marta's public `"foundry"` attribution—though if an author wanted Theo tested onstage on whether he lets someone else's public shield stand over his solo error, the Open Exchange ceiling (`2–4` turns) is tight whenever the ceremony lead insists on seeing the bronze beneath the cloth first.
