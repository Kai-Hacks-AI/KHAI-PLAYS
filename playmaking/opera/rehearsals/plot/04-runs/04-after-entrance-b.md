# The Wrong Name — Staged Rehearsal Run

Staged from [play.md](../02-company/play.md), [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md), and the active [plot.md](../02-company/plot.md) at [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](../02-company/place-hall.md), with [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md) (`Ceremony Lead`) at the oak lectern and [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md) (`Plaque Lead`) beside [the Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md) during [the Unveiling Ceremony](../02-company/process-unveiling.md).

---

## Opening and Event 1

### Opening (2:12 p.m.)

Forty neighbors sit hushed in folding chairs inside [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](../02-company/place-hall.md), holding ivory half-sheet programs titled *Honoring Miriam Okafor (1948–2025)*. At the oak lectern, [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md) stands behind the live gooseneck microphone on the closing lines of her brief welcome, with the press photographer poised by the side window ahead of a hard 2:20 p.m. departure, a youth group booked for 2:30 p.m., and four paper-ribboned front-row chairs reserved for the Okafor family sitting empty six feet from the front wall. Two paces to Marta's left, [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md) stands beside [the Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md), draped smooth beneath its weighted navy velvet cloth and braided brass draw-cord. Neither volunteer has seen the bare bronze face since casting.

### Event 1 — Entrance Cue

At the rear of [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](../02-company/place-hall.md), the double glass doors ease open. Miriam's widower Samuel Okafor, leaning lightly on his walking cane, and her daughter Tomi Okafor, carrying white chrysanthemums and a folded printed program, walk quietly down the center aisle as seated neighbors turn with warm nods, taking two of the reserved front-row chairs six feet directly in front of [the Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md).

---

## Beat 1

### Turn 1 — [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md)

Marta pauses for two quiet seconds with her shoulders open to the center aisle until her gaze settles warmly on the reserved front row, squares her printed run-of-show card against the oak lectern rim, and speaks clearly into the live gooseneck microphone:

"Samuel, Tomi—welcome home to Alder Street. Forty Saturday mornings a year, Miriam built this pantry with her own two hands, and every household in this room helped put her name in bronze on this wall forever."

She steps half a pace back from the live microphone, turns her shoulders and angles her run-of-show card toward [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md) two paces to her left, and extends an open left hand toward the braided brass draw-cord with a calm, expectant nod:

*(into the outer pickup of the live mic, warm and crisp)* "Theo—whenever you're ready, let's bring Miriam's wall into the light."

*(In the front row six feet away, Samuel Okafor eases down onto his folding chair and steadies his walking cane upright between his shoes while Tomi settles the white chrysanthemums across her lap; by the side window, the weekly photographer lifts his camera to his eye.)*

### Turn 2 — [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md)

Theo's thumb and forefinger press briefly along the seam of his right jacket pocket, his shoulders squaring rigid for a fraction of a second before he inclines his head toward the reserved front row six feet away.

*(spoken clearly off-mic toward the front row, quiet and steady)* "Welcome, Mr. Okafor. Welcome, Tomi. It's an honor to have you both here."

He steps into the narrow gap between the oak lectern and the right edge of the veiled [Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md), catches Marta's gaze with a brief nod, presses his left fingertips lightly against the weighted bottom-right hem of the navy velvet flush to the wall to steady it, and closes his right hand around the loop of the braided brass draw-cord—holding poised for a beat before drawing it.

---

## Event 2 — Discovery Cue

- **Public Stage View (seen by Marta and the hall):** Beside [the Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md), [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md) reaches down and angles the weighted bottom corner of the navy velvet cloth an inch off the wall, glancing underneath for a second as if checking that no foundry packing tape or cord snag remains, before letting the velvet settle flat over the plaque again. From the lectern and the chairs, only that brief hand check is visible; the bronze face stays hidden.
- **Private Visual View (seen ONLY by [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md)):** Behind the lifted corner of the navy velvet, the 1.5-inch raised polished-brass capitals across the top header line catch the light: **`MARIAM OKAFOR`**—not **`MIRIAM OKAFOR`**.

## Open Volunteer Exchange

### Turn 3 — [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md)

Theo's right hand uncurls from the braided brass draw-cord without pulling it, while his left palm presses the right edge of the navy velvet flat against the wall until the cloth hangs completely still. His shoulders lock rigid for half a second, his right thumb and forefinger pinching tight along the seam of his right jacket pocket.

Releasing his pocket seam, he lifts one quiet index finger in a brief "one moment" gesture toward the side-window photographer, takes one controlled step right across the narrow gap to Marta's left shoulder—well back from the live gooseneck microphone and keeping his body positioned between the lectern and the draw-cord—screens his lips from the front row with two fingers, and leans close to Marta's ear:

*(barely audible whisper to Marta only, angled away from both the live mic pickup and the front row)* "Marta—hold on. Don't pull the cord. The bronze says *Mariam*—M-A-R-I-A-M."

*(By the side window, the photographer lowers his viewfinder half an inch at Theo's raised finger and glances at his watch—2:14 p.m. Six feet away in the reserved front row, Samuel Okafor rests both hands atop his cane waiting toward the veiled wall, while Tomi Okafor's gaze shifts sharply from the unmoving navy velvet to the two volunteers leaning together beside the oak lectern. A faint rustle of ivory program sheets stirs in the silent rows behind them.)*

### Turn 4 — [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md)

Marta's jaw sets for half a second while a calm, steady smile stays fixed on her face toward the reserved front row. Right behind the gooseneck stem, she taps the top edge of her printed run-of-show index card twice against the oak lectern rim—sending a faint *thump-thump* through the live microphone and wall speaker.

Pressing her right palm briefly over the foam head of the live microphone, she angles her chin slightly toward Theo at her left shoulder while keeping her shoulders open to the hall:

*(crisp, barely audible whisper to Theo only, masked from the live mic)* "You squinted under the bottom hem in the dark, Theo—don't freeze in front of his family. Pull the cord."

She lifts her palm cleanly off the microphone, leans smoothly into the live pickup, and turns half a step left toward the veiled plaque, extending her open left hand toward the braided brass draw-cord:

*(into the live lectern mic, warm and composed)* "Just making sure our brass cord clears the velvet cleanly—all right, Theo, let's bring Miriam's plaque into view."

*(At Marta's smooth explanation, the side-window photographer raises his camera viewfinder back to his eye, finger poised on the shutter. Six feet away in the front row, Samuel Okafor straightens slightly above his walking cane with a gentle, expectant nod toward the navy velvet, while Tomi Okafor's gaze fixes directly on Theo's hands beside the braided brass draw-cord.)*

### Turn 5 — [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md)

Theo steps half a pace left to the edge of the plaque, wraps his left hand around the braided brass draw-cord, and presses both the cord and the right edge of the navy velvet flat against the wall—holding the cloth visibly shut instead of pulling it. His right thumb and forefinger pinch tight along the seam of his right jacket pocket; his shoulders lock rigid before his gaze drops briefly toward the reserved front row.

*(spoken off-mic toward Samuel, Tomi, and the photographer, low and strained)* "Forgive me, Mr. Okafor—something's wrong behind the cloth. Please give us just one moment."

Keeping his left hand pinning the draw-cord and velvet flush against the wall, he leans right across the single-pace gap toward Marta's left shoulder—angling his mouth away from both the front chairs and the live gooseneck microphone—and fixes his gaze on Marta's face at close range:

*(barely audible whisper to Marta only, masked from the front row and live mic)* "I signed the rush proof alone—no second check. The top brass line says *Mariam*. Look for yourself—I can't drop this on them."

*(Six feet away in the reserved front row, Tomi Okafor leans forward in her folding chair, one hand tightening around the paper-wrapped stems of the white chrysanthemums, her eyes darting from Theo's hand pinning the navy velvet to Marta's face. Beside her, Samuel Okafor stays seated with both hands stacked quietly on the handle of his cane, tilting his head slightly upward as he waits. Low, uncertain murmurs ripple across the first three rows of neighbors holding* Honoring Miriam Okafor (1948–2025) *programs, and by the side window the photographer lowers his camera to his chest and checks his watch—2:15 p.m.)*

### Turn 6 — [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md)

Marta lowers her extended left hand slowly, glances once from Tomi Okafor's face to Samuel's hands resting on his cane, and turns half a step back to the oak lectern. Squaring her shoulders calmly toward the hall, she speaks evenly into the live gooseneck microphone:

*(into the live lectern mic, warm and unhurried)* "Neighbors—and Samuel, Tomi—thank you for bearing with us for just thirty quiet seconds while we check the fixture."

She clicks the base switch of the gooseneck microphone off with her thumb, sets her printed run-of-show card flat on the oak lectern, and steps briskly across the gap to stand flush against the front wall beside Theo. Angling her shoulder between the plaque and the reserved front row, she tugs the weighted bottom-right corner of the navy velvet an inch off the wall with two fingers—still checked taut halfway up by Theo's left hand pinning the right edge and draw-cord—tilts her head close to the wall trying to peer up underneath at the bronze face, and whispers beside Theo's ear:

*(barely audible whisper to Theo only, flush against the wall)* "Let me see how big it is—the paper leaves in five minutes. Can we get through today reading *Miriam* aloud and recast it Monday, or is it right across the face?"

*(Out in the silent hall, the promised thirty seconds are already ticking away: Tomi Okafor leans further forward six feet away with a quiet, worried crease between her brows, Samuel Okafor's fingers tighten gently over the top of his cane as he watches the two volunteers pressed against the veiled wall, and by the side window the photographer steps out into the side aisle with a glance toward the rear doors—2:16 p.m.)*

### Turn 7 — [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md)

Theo slides his left hand up to the top-right corner of the plaque, pinching only the braided brass draw-cord fast against the end of the brass rod—slackening the middle edge of the navy velvet above Marta's lifted bottom corner while angling his left forearm between the wall and the front row to keep the gap screened from the chairs.

His right thumb and forefinger press tight along the seam of his right jacket pocket; his shoulders stay locked rigid as he leans close beside Marta's ear flush against the wall:

*(barely audible whisper to Marta only, flush against the plaster)* "Inch-and-a-half brass capitals right across the top—Samuel, Tomi, and the camera will read it instantly. Don't drop the cloth. Let me step forward right now, tell them the proof error is mine alone, and cover the recast myself."

He glances once past Marta's shoulder toward the reserved front row six feet away, then turns his gaze straight back to Marta's face at close range, keeping his left hand anchored on the top loop of the draw-cord.

---

## Immediate Consequence

Behind Marta's lifted fingers, the 1.5-inch raised polished-brass capitals across the top header line of [the Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md)—**`MARIAM OKAFOR`**—catch the afternoon light for Marta to see firsthand before the weighted bottom corner settles flat against the bronze again. With Theo's left hand still clamping the braided brass draw-cord shut at the top rod and the gooseneck microphone clicked off at the empty oak lectern two paces away, [the Unveiling Ceremony](../02-company/process-unveiling.md) stalls past Marta's promised thirty-second check (`2:17 p.m.`), leaving the plaque bolted and hidden beneath its motionless navy velvet cloth on the front wall of [Alder Street Neighborhood Hall](../02-company/place-hall.md).

Six feet away in the reserved front row, Tomi Okafor sets the white chrysanthemums down onto the empty chair beside her, rises from her seat, and takes a single step toward the draped wall, speaking quietly into the unamplified hush: *"Marta? Theo—is everything all right with my mother's plaque?"* Beside her, Samuel Okafor leans forward with both hands braced atop his walking cane, looking steadily up from the dark velvet to the two silent volunteers pressed together beside the unpulled cord, while the newspaper photographer lets his camera hang from its strap by the side aisle ahead of his 2:20 p.m. departure and forty neighbors look up from the *Honoring Miriam Okafor (1948–2025)* programs open on their laps, waiting before a still-covered wall for an explanation.

*Curtain.*

# Director’s Note

## (a) Epistemic States, Beat-by-Beat Action Costs, and Outcome

- **Opening + Event 1 (Entrance Cue) → Beat 1 (Turns 1–2):**
  - *Knowledge state:* Neither [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md) nor [Theo Park](../02-company/persona-plaque-lead.md) knew or suspected any casting defect on [the Dedication Plaque](../02-company/piece-plaque.md). Marta held her morning phone promise to Samuel Okafor for a brief, dignified tribute ahead of the 2:20 p.m. press photo and 2:30 p.m. room turnover without knowing how Theo handled the proof; Theo privately carried his solo 4:45 p.m. phone proof approval and his 1:45 p.m. kraft-paper slide behind the velvet, believing every letter matched.
  - *Available/costly actions & what unfolded:* Once Samuel and Tomi Okafor walked down the center aisle into the reserved front row six feet from the plaque, moving straight into the unveil appeared costless and aligned for both leads. In **Turn 1**, Marta welcomed Samuel and Tomi into the live mic and stepped half a pace back with an open-hand cue for Theo to draw the cord. In **Turn 2**, Theo greeted Samuel and Tomi off-mic, stepped beside the right edge of the plaque, steadied the bottom-right velvet hem, and set his right hand on the draw-cord loop.
- **Event 2 (Discovery Cue) → Open Exchange (Turns 3–7):**
  - *Event 2 & Turn 3 (asymmetric shock under front-row scrutiny):* At the **Discovery Cue**, only Theo saw **`MARIAM OKAFOR`** behind the lifted bottom corner while Marta and the hall saw a routine one-second check. Because Samuel and Tomi Okafor were already seated six feet away and Marta had already publicly cued the unveil, any delay by Theo immediately incurred visible friction in front of the grieving family. Theo uncurled his hand from the draw-cord, signaled "one moment" to the photographer, stepped away from the live mic to Marta's left shoulder, and whispered behind two fingers that the bronze read *Mariam* (`M-A-R-I-A-M`)—giving Marta the claim of an error without visual proof or his proof-review confession.
  - *Turn 4 (momentum vs. unverified claim):* Still believing Theo had verified every letter on the proof, Marta read his whisper through her Persona's `## Shadow` as stage fright from squinting under a dark hem. Her index-card double-tap sounded faintly over the live speaker; she covered the mic head to whisper *"Pull the cord"* and publicly smoothed over the pause on the live mic while re-cueing the unveil—raising Theo's cost of stopping the ceremony from an awkward beat to an overt public contradiction.
  - *Turn 5 (public halt and private confession):* Forced to choose between unveiling **`MARIAM OKAFOR`** before Samuel, Tomi, and the camera or breaking Marta's live cue, Theo physically pinned both the draw-cord and right velvet edge shut against the wall, apologized aloud off-mic to Mr. Okafor (*"something's wrong behind the cloth"*), and whispered his solo rush-proof sign-off to Marta while inviting her to look for herself. At this point the family and hall knew something was wrong behind the velvet, and Marta learned both the casting error and Theo's solo sign-off.
  - *Turns 6–7 (visual verification against the clock):* Still protecting ceremony dignity and eyeing the 2:20 p.m. press exit, Marta asked the room for *"thirty quiet seconds while we check the fixture,"* switched off the gooseneck mic, stepped flush beside Theo, tugged the bottom-right velvet corner, and asked whether they could read *Miriam* aloud today and recast Monday. In **Turn 7**, Theo shifted his grip to pinch only the draw-cord at the top rod—slackening the middle hem while screening the gap from the chairs with his forearm so Marta finally saw the 1.5-inch **`MARIAM OKAFOR`** capitals herself—and whispered for her to keep the cloth up and let him take sole public blame and cover the recast.
  - *Immediate Consequence:* With both volunteers still pressed against the veiled plaque as the `7`-turn hard ceiling arrived and Marta's thirty-second window expired in silence, the plaque stayed covered on the wall while Tomi Okafor rose six feet away to ask aloud whether everything was all right with her mother's plaque.

## (b) Extra Direction and Mediation Supplied as Director

1. **Pre-pull beat boundary on Turn 2:** After Marta's Turn 1 explicitly cued Theo to unveil (*"Theo—whenever you're ready, let's bring Miriam's wall into the light"*), I paced Samuel Okafor settling his cane in the front row and bounded Theo's Turn 2 prompt to his immediate pre-pull beat (*stopping at stepping into position / preparing at the veiled plaque without pulling the draw-cord or uncovering the plaque yet*), with zero hint of any defect on the bronze, so Event 2's fixed **Discovery Cue** could still fire intact.
2. **Adjudicating cloth custody between Turns 5 and 7:** In Turn 6, Marta tugged the bottom-right velvet corner while Theo's left hand was already pinning the right edge and draw-cord flat from Turn 5. Per [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md) (*no one besides Theo sees the bronze lettering unless Theo holds the velvet corner open for someone right beside him or someone pulls the draw-cord*), I kept the middle edge checked taut until Theo deliberately shifted his hand to slacken the edge for Marta in Turn 7.
3. **Use of the `7`-turn ceiling and observable hall clock:** Because Turn 6 ended both mid-question (*"Can we get through today reading Miriam aloud...?"*) and mid-reach on the bottom velvet corner, I invoked [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md)'s `7`-turn hard ceiling so Theo could answer and open the corner on Turn 7, while marking quiet real-time progression (2:12 to 2:17 p.m.) through the photographer's watch checks and the family's physical posture without steering either volunteer's tactical choice.

## (c) Strained Points: Staging Choices vs. Gaps in the Play or Plot

- **Seam in the Play + Entrance-First Plot (Turn 1–2 pre-Discovery unveil pressure):** [play.md](../02-company/play.md) opens at 2:12 p.m. with [Marta Solis](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md) already *"on the closing lines of her brief welcome,"* and [persona-ceremony-lead.md](../02-company/persona-ceremony-lead.md) specifies her `## Action` as extending an open hand to cue the draw-cord pull. When [plot.md](../02-company/plot.md) fires the **Entrance Cue** first and delays the **Discovery Cue** for one beat (`Turns 1–2`), the arrival of Samuel and Tomi Okafor removes the only remaining reason to wait—leading Marta naturally to cue the unveil on Turn 1 while Theo still has zero reason not to pull the cord on Turn 2 before Event 2 fires.
  - *Staging choice vs. Play/Plot gap:* Bounding Turn 2 to Theo's pre-pull stance while Samuel finished settling in his seat was my **staging choice** to deliver Event 2 intact; the underlying collision—an end-of-welcome Opening paired with an Entrance-first cue sequence that invites an uninformed plaque steward to drop the cloth before the **Discovery Cue**—is a **structural seam in [play.md](../02-company/play.md) / [plot.md](../02-company/plot.md)** (which an author could resolve only if desired, e.g., by starting Marta earlier in her welcome or folding the hem check into Theo's instinctive response to an unveil cue).
- **Late-discovery turn compression (`4–6` turns, `7` ceiling):** Firing Discovery after Beat 1 leaves at most five volunteer turns (`Turns 3–7`) to absorb a hidden visual shock, cross Marta's skepticism, verify the 1.5-inch lettering beneath a weighted cloth without exposing it six feet in front of the family, and commit to a public explanation. Because Marta spent Turn 4 pushing forward and Turn 6 visually verifying the brass header, Theo's offer to confess landed on Turn 7 just as the turn ceiling closed. Ending on the expired thirty-second pause and Tomi Okafor's spoken question—rather than inventing unplayed Turn 8 speech for either volunteer—was a **staging choice** faithful to [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md) and [process-unveiling.md](../02-company/process-unveiling.md)'s halted-ceremony `## Echo`, whereas the tight five-turn post-Discovery runway is an intentional pressure constraint of [play.md](../02-company/play.md)'s run structure.
