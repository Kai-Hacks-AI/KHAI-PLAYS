## 1. Keep the Six `TO CAST` Chapters (`Taxonomy`, `Owner`, `Cue`, `Action`, `Stage`, `Tension`)

Holding `Taxonomy`, `Owner`, `Cue`, `Action`, and `Tension` byte-for-byte fixed while reversing only the event order inside `Stage` between [plot-before-entrance.md](../02-company/plot-before-entrance.md) and [plot-after-entrance.md](../02-company/plot-after-entrance.md) confirmed that `TO CAST` sets forces in motion cleanly while leaving the outcome to play:
- **`Cue`** anchored the `2:12 p.m.` hall hush, live mic, `2:20 p.m.` press exit, and six-foot front row identically across all four runs.
- **`Action`** kept Marta’s ceremonial momentum colliding directly with Theo’s custody of the draw-cord and velvet cloth (`Turns 4–5` in every run).
- **`Stage`** shifted the entire epistemic and tactical trajectory—from pre-arrival whisper channel + full public confession by Theo in [01](../04-runs/01-before-entrance-a.md)–[02](../04-runs/02-before-entrance-b.md) to post-cue cover excuses, Marta’s skepticism, and wall-side visual verification in [03](../04-runs/03-after-entrance-a.md)–[04](../04-runs/04-after-entrance-b.md)—without scripting what either volunteer chose.
- **`Tension`** supplied the private proof-approval shortcut and morning family promise that made every available move costly without spoiling post-discovery facts.

## 2. Proposed Refinements to `references/plot.md`

1. **`## Stage` — explicitly include the order of staged arrivals or turns when sequence matters:**
   - *Evidence:* `references/plot.md` currently prompts `## Stage` as *"Place the Personas and their Pieces, and say who enters with what."* That covers static `t = 0` placement and character entrances (Samuel and Tomi Okafor arriving with cane, flowers, and program), but leaves implicit how `Stage` orders multiple staged triggers or non-entrance events like Theo’s hem check ([authoring-note.md](../02-company/authoring-note.md#L14-L15)).
   - *Smallest edit (`## Stage`):*
     > `Place the Personas and their Pieces, say who enters with what, and set the order of any staged arrivals or turns.`

2. **`## Tension` — keep private stakes anchored at opening rather than mid-scene discoveries:**
   - *Evidence:* Because [03-after-entrance-a](../04-runs/03-after-entrance-a.md) and [04-after-entrance-b](../04-runs/04-after-entrance-b.md) played `Turns 1–2` *before* the **Discovery Cue**, placing **`MARIAM OKAFOR`** inside `Tension` would have leaked post-check knowledge into pre-check play ([authoring-note.md](../02-company/authoring-note.md#L16-L17)). Grounding `Tension` in what Personas already carry privately when the scene opens (Theo’s solo rush-proof sign-off; Marta’s morning promise to Samuel) kept `Turns 1–2` genuinely unspoiled while supercharging Discovery once it arrived.
   - *Smallest edit (`## Tension`):*
     > `Name what a Persona carries privately at the start that keeps the Action from going smoothly.`

## 3. Adjacent Cross-Class Seams (`Play` / `Process`, `Piece`, `Director`)

1. **Leave enough `Process` runway at Opening when `Plot ## Stage` can delay a discovery by a beat:**
   - *Evidence:* Both `After the entrance` Directors ([03](../04-runs/03-after-entrance-a.md#L138), [04](../04-runs/04-after-entrance-b.md#L144)) flagged the same structural pinch: opening [play.md](../02-company/play.md#L22) on the *"closing lines"* of Marta’s welcome worked seamlessly when `Discovery` fired first (`01`–`02`), but when `Entrance` fired first with a two-turn beat before `Discovery` (`03`–`04`), Marta naturally cued the unveil on `Turn 1`, requiring Directors to bound Theo’s uninformed `Turn 2` to his pre-pull stance at the cord so `Event 2` could fire before the cloth dropped. Starting a `Process` one step earlier (e.g., mid-welcome rather than final line) gives reordered `Stage` beats natural room without Director braking.
2. **Gate `Piece ## Yearbook` provenance that reveals a veiled defect (`Piece` + `Promptbook ## Knowledge`):**
   - *Evidence:* Keeping [piece-plaque.md](../02-company/piece-plaque.md) `## Apparent` strictly to the veiled velvet outline and gating `## Yearbook`’s foundry-typo line until Discovery prevented pre-check spoilers across all four runs, while `01`’s Director noted ([01](../04-runs/01-before-entrance-a.md#L100-L101)) that delivering the relevant `Yearbook` fact alongside the visual Discovery Cue lets the checking Persona know whether the typo came from foundry re-keying on the proof or post-proof casting.
3. **Update `khai-director/SKILL.md` to reference `Plot` (`TO CAST`):**
   - *Alignment note:* Adding `Plot` alongside `Play`, `Promptbook`, and `Pitch` in [khai-director/SKILL.md](../../../skills/khai-director/SKILL.md)—so the Director stages the `Cue`, `Stage` sequence, colliding `Action`, and private `Tension` of any active [Plot](../../../skills/khai-playwright/references/plot.md) while leaving the outcome to the cast—aligns the Director skill with `TO CAST`.
