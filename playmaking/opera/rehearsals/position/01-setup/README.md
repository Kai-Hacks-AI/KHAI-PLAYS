# Rehearsal Setup: TO HOLD at the Printmaking Bench

An evening printmaking class has five minutes left. The first proof is crooked. There is one clean sheet. As the Student prepares to pull the last print, the Teacher's former teacher walks in, stops beside the crooked proof, and says, “I remember that mistake.”

## Create

Use the [Playwright skill](../../../skills/khai-playwright/SKILL.md) and its [TO HOLD template](../../../skills/khai-playwright/references/position.md). Author Teacher and Student as reusable Position files. Give each something it confers, requires, and costs. The Teacher owes the Student room to make the next attempt. The Student has to risk making an attempt under someone else's eye.

Author three distinct Personas: the current Teacher, the current Student, and the former Teacher. The current Teacher tends to take charge when unsure. Keep the history with their former teacher in the Persona material. Author a short Play and Promptbook, the workshop as a Place, the crooked proof and last clean sheet as Pieces, and the last pull as a Process whose result is open. Keep all of that fixed across runs.

## Rehearse

Use the [Director skill](../../../skills/khai-director/SKILL.md). Run each casting twice in fresh contexts. Introduce the former teacher on the same visible cue, with the same line, in every run. Keep the current Teacher in the Teacher Position toward the current Student throughout.

| Casting | When the former teacher arrives |
| --- | --- |
| Teacher only | The former teacher is a visitor. The history is real and they may speak, but they hold no active Teacher Position toward the current Teacher. |
| Teacher and Student | The former teacher resumes the Teacher Position toward their former pupil. The middle Persona also holds Student toward them while still teaching the current Student. Both Positions apply at once. |

Let the Personas decide what to say and who handles the last sheet. Play through one consequence of that choice. Record any direction or clarification the Director supplied.

## Review

Use SCORE to save the tested commit, Company, runs, observations, and any proposed template edit. Look for a move made possible by the Position, a cost the holder actually bears, and a moment where the two Positions press on the same move. Distinguish the pressure of seeing a former teacher from the obligations of actively holding Student.
