# Authoring Note — Position Rehearsal (`One Clean Sheet`)

## Key Stageable Authoring Choices

1. **Concrete printmaking mistake that links tonight's proof to six-year-old history without leaking private `Shadow`:**
   - [The Crooked Proof](piece-crooked-proof.md) exhibits a specific, immediately legible physical flaw on a relief press: sighting only the near-left registration corner while letting the far-right edge drop early onto the inked block, leaving a counterclockwise skew and a heavy bead of squeezed ink along the right edge.
   - Placing the inspection blotter just inside the doorway of [the workshop](place-workshop.md) makes [Arthur](persona-former-teacher.md)'s stop beside [the crooked proof](piece-crooked-proof.md) physically natural on entrance and grounds `"I remember that mistake."` in observable ink and margins (`Apparent`).
   - Both [Nadia](persona-teacher.md) and [Arthur](persona-former-teacher.md) carry only the *observable* shared past from six years ago in Arthur's shop (when Nadia made that same closing-time near-corner drop and spoiled her last rag sheet rushing the correction under his watch), while keeping their conflicting inner interpretations strictly isolated in their respective `Shadow` sections and completely unknown to [Theo](persona-student.md) unless spoken aloud.

2. **Spatializing "who handles `piece-clean-sheet.md`" through `Place — TO SHOW`:**
   - In [place-workshop.md](place-workshop.md), `Withheld` establishes that the press apron fits only one body and that touching [the last clean sheet](piece-clean-sheet.md) cleanly requires clean hands or the two brass handling clips beside the tray. Stepping in to align or lay the sheet therefore physically displaces whoever occupies the bed, turning an internal authority struggle into unmistakable blocking.

3. **Cleanly separating the fixed entrance cue from initiating `Process — TO IDLE`:**
   - The entrance cue fires when [Theo](persona-student.md) steps into the apron, checks the block on the registration grid, and reaches for the brass clips beside [the last clean sheet](piece-clean-sheet.md) *before* lifting the sheet out of the tray.
   - [process-last-pull.md](process-last-pull.md) (`Initiated by`) starts only when someone actually lifts [the last clean sheet](piece-clean-sheet.md) clear of the tray toward the inked block. That gap preserves a live onstage decision window after `"I remember that mistake."` in which all three Personas can contest or yield who handles the sheet.

4. **Keeping `Teacher` and `Student` reusable across Personas and plays:**
   - [position-teacher.md](position-teacher.md) and [position-student.md](position-student.md) avoid both Persona names and printmaking-specific vocabulary, framing `Has`, `Orders`, `Loses`, and `Drives` around workspace standard, owed room for the next attempt, relinquishing direct takeover of the material, and visibly risking an attempt under scrutiny. That allows `Teacher` to fit both Nadia (toward Theo) and Arthur (toward Nadia), and `Student` to fit both Theo (toward Nadia) and Nadia (toward Arthur).

## Seams and Frictions Noticed Authoring `TO HOLD` Alongside the Other Templates

1. **Positions are directed edges (`X -> Position -> Y`), while `Persona — TO PAST` (`Taxonomy`) is unary and static:**
   - `references/position.md` describes what a Position gives, orders, and costs "whoever holds it," and `references/persona.md` asks `Taxonomy` to "Link its Position if one exists." In live staging, however, holding [Teacher](position-teacher.md) or [Student](position-student.md) is *relational* (held toward a specific counterpart) and *castable/temporal* (resumed upon arrival in one casting, absent in another, and held simultaneously with a second Position toward a different person).
   - Putting target-Persona names or arrival triggers inside `Persona.Taxonomy` would either break fixed-file reusability across castings or leak Arthur's un-occurred entrance cue before beat 1. Consequently, `Persona.Taxonomy` can only declare Position eligibility/links in general terms, while [play.md](play.md) and [promptbook.md](promptbook.md) (`System`) have to carry the directed binding `(holder, Position, counterpart, activation beat)`.

2. **Keeping temperament (`TO PAST`) separate from normative obligation (`TO HOLD`):**
   - When authoring a Persona who "tends to take charge when unsure" ([Nadia](persona-teacher.md)) or a veteran printer accustomed to pointing out flaws ([Arthur](persona-former-teacher.md)), there is strong gravity in `Action` and `Shadow` toward phrasing habits as duties ("feels responsible for giving the student a turn" or "claims a mentor's right to inspect").
   - Doing so would collapse the difference between **Teacher only** and **Teacher and Student**. Keeping `TO PAST` strictly descriptive of *habit, history, blind spot, and involuntary tell*—while keeping *standing, owed room, risked attempt, and surrendered takeover* strictly inside `TO HOLD`—is essential so that adding `Arthur -> Teacher -> Nadia` and `Nadia -> Student -> Arthur` introduces genuine new obligations (Nadia losing the standing to wave off Arthur's scrutiny and owing a risked attempt under his eye, colliding directly with owing Theo room at the only clean sheet).

3. **Cue-boundary friction between `Play` entrance cues and `Process — TO IDLE` (`Initiated by`):**
   - Naturalistic phrasing ("as the Student prepares to pull the last print") overlaps closely with starting the pull itself. Without an explicit physical threshold between *preparing tools at the tray* (entrance cue in `Play`/`Collaboration`) and *lifting the sheet from the tray* (`Initiated by` in `Process`), performers could accidentally trigger `process-last-pull.md` before the arrival cue or pre-empt the choice of who handles `piece-clean-sheet.md`.

4. **Open outcomes in `Process — TO IDLE` (`Echo`) require a `Promptbook` adjudication rule:**
   - `Echo` asks "what has changed in the world when the Process has run," which naturally pulls toward stating a single finished state, whereas an open Process needs multiple live outcomes without allowing a hopeful Persona performer to self-author "I pull a clean print." Pairing a state-transition `Echo` (the last blank chance is spent or forfeited at 9:00 PM, yielding a visible result tied to `Lever`) with an explicit `Agent`/`System` rule in [promptbook.md](promptbook.md) that only the Director/Process runner declares the resulting print keeps the outcome genuinely open in performance.
