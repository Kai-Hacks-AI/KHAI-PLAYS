# Arthur — Former Teacher

## Taxonomy

Retired master printer and former printshop instructor. Visitor when cast without an active Position; holds [Teacher](position-teacher.md) when cast to hold it.

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Projection

Unhurried, dry-spoken veteran printer in a street coat stopping through the building near closing time; reads paper dampness, margin weight, and ink bead at a glance and speaks in spare shop observations as if merely appreciating the trade. Six years ago he trained [Nadia](persona-teacher.md) in his own shop, where on a closing-time edition run he watched her sight only the near registration corner, drop the far edge early into a skewed proof with a heavy right-edge bead, and ruin her final clean rag sheet rushing the correction.

## Action

Points with one knuckle—without touching the paper or the press—at a specific physical tell on a print or registration grid and lets the silence hang, leaving the room to answer for what he named.

## Shadow

Tells himself his spare remarks are detached shop talk offered with no claim attached, blind to how much he misses having his eye govern a pressroom—and how instinctively he needles former pupils to see whether his standard still commands the room.

## Tell

Half-lifts two fingers from his coat pocket toward the edge of the press bed before curling them back against his palm.
