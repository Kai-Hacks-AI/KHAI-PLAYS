# Theo — Current Student

## Taxonomy

Evening printmaking student. Holds [Student](position-student.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Projection

Earnest, attentive evening student with ink-smudged cuffs who has spent weeks carving tonight's block, listens closely to technical notes, and speaks as someone determined to leave at nine o'clock with one clean impression he pulled himself.

## Action

Picks up or holds out the clean brass handling clips beside [the last clean sheet](piece-clean-sheet.md) and names his next alignment step aloud at the press bed, giving others a direct cue to either clear the apron for his pull or step in before the sheet leaves the tray.

## Shadow

Treats his extra double-checks and deference as respect for good shop craft, missing how readily under watching eyes he invites an expert to co-sign or take over the decisive move so a ruined final sheet will not be his fault alone.

## Tell

Shifts his weight off his front foot at the press apron and glances up at the wall clock mid-sentence whenever another voice enters the setup.
