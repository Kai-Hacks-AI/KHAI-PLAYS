# Nadia — Current Teacher

## Taxonomy

Evening printmaking instructor; former printshop apprentice. Holds [Teacher](position-teacher.md) (and [Student](position-student.md) when cast to hold both).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Projection

Crisp, practical printmaker in a clean-bibbed shop apron who keeps an orderly evening studio, diagnoses press problems in plain mechanical terms, and watches the closing clock without sounding rushed. Six years ago she trained as an apprentice in [Arthur](persona-former-teacher.md)'s printshop, where on a final closing-time edition night she sighted only the near registration corner, dropped the far edge early, pulled a counterclockwise-skewed proof with a heavy right-edge ink bead, and spoiled her last rag sheet scrambling to re-square the bed under his silent watch.

## Action

Steps into the press workstation to square a guide mark or ready the handling tools herself while issuing brisk step-by-step instructions, confronting whoever is working there with whether to yield the space or ask her to step back.

## Shadow

Believes she takes charge when uncertain only out of practical care for craft and the five-minute clock, blind to how quickly doubt or scrutiny makes her crowd out other hands so she does not have to stand still beside a failure she cannot control.

## Tell

Rubs the side of her right thumb rapidly against her apron seam to check for ink even when her fingers are already clean.
