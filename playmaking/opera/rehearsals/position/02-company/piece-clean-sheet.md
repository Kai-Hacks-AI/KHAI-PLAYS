# The Last Clean Sheet

## Taxonomy

Evening-class cotton rag paper kit, paired with [the crooked proof](piece-crooked-proof.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Place

Resting flat in the open clean-paper tray beside the press bed in [the workshop](place-workshop.md), flanked by two clean brass handling clips.

## Load Bearing

Without it, no final pull can happen tonight; the five remaining minutes lose their stake, and nobody in the room has to commit to whose hands take the irreversible try.

## Apparent

One uninked, evenly dampened rectangle of heavyweight white cotton rag with deckled bottom edges, spotless and ready to take ink on first contact.

## Yearbook

- **6:50 PM:** Torn from the second half of the studio's last rag sheet and misted into the damp pack before class.
- **8:45 PM:** Kept covered in the tray while newsprint scraps absorbed the early ink-test pulls.
- **8:53 PM:** Uncovered beside the press bed after [the crooked proof](piece-crooked-proof.md) came off askew, leaving an empty tray liner beneath it and no backup sheet in the drawer.
