# The Crooked Proof

## Taxonomy

Evening-class cotton rag paper kit, paired with [the last clean sheet](piece-clean-sheet.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Place

Lying damp and face-up on the inspection blotter just inside the doorway of [the workshop](place-workshop.md).

## Load Bearing

Without it, there would be no visible record of how the first pull went askew, nothing beside the door for an arriving eye to read on sight, and no proof forcing the next pull to reckon with a concrete error rather than guess blind.

## Apparent

A damp white cotton rag sheet printed in dense black relief ink, rotated noticeably counterclockwise against the paper margins, with the upper-left margin pinched narrow and a heavy bead of squeezed ink along the dropped right edge.

## Yearbook

- **6:50 PM:** Torn down with one companion half from the last full sheet of cotton rag in the studio flat file and slipped into the damp pack before class.
- **8:49 PM:** Lifted onto the press bed with eleven minutes of class left; sighted only at the near left registration corner while the far right edge dropped early onto the inked block.
- **8:51 PM:** Peeled off the block askew and set wet onto the doorway inspection blotter under the overhead tube light.
