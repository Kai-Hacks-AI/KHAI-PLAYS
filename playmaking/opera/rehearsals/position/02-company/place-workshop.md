# The Printmaking Workshop

## Taxonomy

Second-floor studio wing of a community printshop.

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Shown

Sharp smell of oil-based relief ink and citrus solvent under humming fluorescent tubes; a wall clock reading **8:55** above a 9:00 PM lights-out notice; a hand-cranked iron bed press centered in the room with a freshly re-inked block set on its registration grid; and an inspection blotter placed just inside the hallway door so anyone stepping in passes the wet work before reaching the press.

## Holds

The iron bed press with etched registration grid and felt blankets, the freshly inked carved block, an inking slab with brayer and shop rags, two clean brass paper-handling clips, a washup sink against the back wall, the wall clock, [the crooked proof](piece-crooked-proof.md) on the doorway inspection blotter, and [the last clean sheet](piece-clean-sheet.md) in the open clean-paper tray beside the press bed.

## Offers

Standing room for one person inside the press apron to sight the corners and turn the wheel; clean brass handling clips and dry rags so inked hands can prepare to lift paper without smudging it; and a clear sightline from the doorway blotter straight to the registration grid.

## Withheld

Spare rag paper (the flat-file drawer is empty for the night) and studio time past 9:00 PM; clean contact with [the last clean sheet](piece-clean-sheet.md) unless whoever reaches for it visibly wipes their hands or uses the brass clips; and two-person space at the registration bed—because the press apron fits one body, stepping in to align or lay the sheet requires physically displacing whoever stands there.
