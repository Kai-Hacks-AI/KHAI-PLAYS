# One Clean Sheet

## Situation

An evening relief-printmaking class in [the workshop](place-workshop.md) has five minutes left before the 9:00 PM lights-out close. Tonight's first proof—[the crooked proof](piece-crooked-proof.md)—came off the press rotated counterclockwise with a heavy right-edge ink bead where the far edge dropped early. The carved block has been freshly re-inked and set back on the press bed's registration grid. Beside it in the clean-paper tray lies [the last clean sheet](piece-clean-sheet.md), with no spare rag paper in the shop.

## Cast and Materials

- **Personas:**
  - [Nadia](persona-teacher.md) — the current Teacher running the evening class; trained six years ago in Arthur's shop.
  - [Theo](persona-student.md) — the current Student finishing weeks of work on this block before closing.
  - [Arthur](persona-former-teacher.md) — Nadia's former printmaking teacher.
- **Positions:** [Teacher](position-teacher.md), [Student](position-student.md)
- **Place:** [The Printmaking Workshop](place-workshop.md)
- **Pieces:** [The Crooked Proof](piece-crooked-proof.md), [The Last Clean Sheet](piece-clean-sheet.md)
- **Process:** [The Last Pull](process-last-pull.md)

## Two Castings

All eleven Play, Promptbook, Position, Persona, Piece, Place, and Process files remain unchanged across runs. Before curtain, the production selects one of two castings:

- **Teacher only:** [Nadia](persona-teacher.md) holds [Teacher](position-teacher.md) toward [Theo](persona-student.md), and [Theo](persona-student.md) holds [Student](position-student.md) toward [Nadia](persona-teacher.md) throughout. When [Arthur](persona-former-teacher.md) walks in, he is a visitor: the shared past with Nadia is real and he may speak and watch, but he holds no active [Teacher](position-teacher.md) Position toward Nadia, and Nadia holds no [Student](position-student.md) Position toward him.
- **Teacher and Student:** [Nadia](persona-teacher.md) holds [Teacher](position-teacher.md) toward [Theo](persona-student.md), and [Theo](persona-student.md) holds [Student](position-student.md) toward [Nadia](persona-teacher.md) throughout. When [Arthur](persona-former-teacher.md) walks in, Arthur resumes [Teacher](position-teacher.md) toward Nadia, and Nadia simultaneously holds [Student](position-student.md) toward Arthur while still holding [Teacher](position-teacher.md) toward Theo—both Positions apply to Nadia at once.

## Fixed Conditions

1. **At curtain (8:55 PM):** Only [Nadia](persona-teacher.md) and [Theo](persona-student.md) are in [the workshop](place-workshop.md). [The crooked proof](piece-crooked-proof.md) lies wet and face-up on the doorway inspection blotter; [the last clean sheet](piece-clean-sheet.md) lies untouched in the clean-paper tray beside two brass handling clips; the re-inked block rests on the press bed. Neither Nadia nor Theo knows Arthur is in the building.
2. **Exact entrance cue and line:** In the opening beat, as [Theo](persona-student.md) visibly prepares to pull the last print—stepping into the press apron, checking the block on the registration grid, and reaching for the brass handling clips beside [the last clean sheet](piece-clean-sheet.md) *before* lifting the sheet from the tray—[Arthur](persona-former-teacher.md) walks in through the hallway door, stops beside [the crooked proof](piece-crooked-proof.md), looks down at it, and says the exact line:
   > “I remember that mistake.”
3. **Ongoing Position toward the Student:** [Nadia](persona-teacher.md) remains in [Teacher](position-teacher.md) toward [Theo](persona-student.md) from curtain to end in every run.

## Open Decisions in Play

Once [Arthur](persona-former-teacher.md) speaks his entrance line with [the last clean sheet](piece-clean-sheet.md) still in the tray and under five minutes remaining:

1. **What to say:** The three Personas decide how to answer the remark, account for the registration error, and negotiate the space around the press under the active Positions of the selected casting.
2. **Who handles [the last clean sheet](piece-clean-sheet.md):** Because the press apron holds one body and the damp rag takes ink on first contact, the Personas must settle onstage whose hands (or clips) lift and lay [the last clean sheet](piece-clean-sheet.md)—whether Theo takes the pull himself, Nadia steps in to handle or take over the alignment, someone invites or refuses Arthur at the bed, or hesitation holds the sheet back against the 9:00 PM clock.
3. **Running [The Last Pull](process-last-pull.md):** Lifting [the last clean sheet](piece-clean-sheet.md) from the tray initiates [The Last Pull](process-last-pull.md). Its physical outcome is genuinely open—neither guaranteed clean nor pre-scripted to fail—and resolves from how `Lever` is actually played onstage.
4. **One consequence:** After the choice over [the last clean sheet](piece-clean-sheet.md) commits and [The Last Pull](process-last-pull.md) reaches its `Echo` (a finished pull on the bed or an unpulled sheet at 9:00 PM close), the Personas play through one concrete onstage consequence of who handled the sheet and what came of it, then end the scene.
