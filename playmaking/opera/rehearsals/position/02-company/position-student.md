# Student

## Taxonomy

Instructional pair, paired with [Teacher](position-teacher.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Has

First claim on the next attempt at the work in progress, and permission to still be learning rather than already finished.

## Orders

Visibly risk making an attempt—committing one's own hands or judgment to the work—under the [Teacher](position-teacher.md)'s eye rather than stalling, deflecting, or letting someone else absorb the try.

## Loses

Unwatched privacy around mistakes, the safety of leaving the attempt unmade, and the standing to wave off the [Teacher](position-teacher.md)'s scrutiny before the work is tested.

## Drives

Moves whoever holds it toward claiming the exposed attempt—putting real effort on the line while watched, even when an error will be immediately plain.
