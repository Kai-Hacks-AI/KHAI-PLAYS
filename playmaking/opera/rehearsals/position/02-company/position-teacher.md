# Teacher

## Taxonomy

Instructional pair, paired with [Student](position-student.md).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Has

Standing to read the flaw in the work, set the standard and pace of the workspace, and ask the [Student](position-student.md) to account for how they intend to make the next attempt.

## Orders

Owe the [Student](position-student.md) real room—in physical space, working time, and direct control of the material—to make the next attempt, while answering for the conditions in which that attempt happens.

## Loses

The license to take over the material with one's own hands just to secure a clean result or protect one's pride, and the distance of an uninvolved bystander if the attempt goes wrong.

## Drives

Moves whoever holds it toward naming what went wrong clearly enough that another person can act on it, then stepping back far enough to let that person take the risk in full view.
