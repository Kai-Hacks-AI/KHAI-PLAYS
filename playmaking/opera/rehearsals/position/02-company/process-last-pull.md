# The Last Pull

## Taxonomy

Evening printmaking class closing sequence (8:55 PM to 9:00 PM studio close).

## Owner

KHAI-PLAYS

## Initiated by

Someone lifts [the last clean sheet](piece-clean-sheet.md) out of the clean-paper tray—by hand or with the brass clips—to bring it over the inked block on the press bed. Whoever runs the Process can tell this condition has been met the moment the sheet clears the lip of the tray.

## Direction

Moves from sighting both near and far registration marks above the inked block and lowering the damp sheet onto the block, to folding down the felt blankets, cranking the press bed once beneath the roller drum, and peeling the printed sheet back before the 9:00 PM cutoff.

## Lever

Driven by steady alignment of both near and far corners with clean brass clips and an uninterrupted turn of the press wheel; slowed by hesitation over the grid, changing hands at the tray, wiping ink mid-setup, or arguing across the apron; stopped if the holder sets [the last clean sheet](piece-clean-sheet.md) back into the tray before it touches ink, if someone steps into the press apron to halt the bed before the blankets drop, or if the wall clock reaches 9:00 PM before the bed passes under the drum. Once the damp rag touches the inked block, the impression cannot be taken back cleanly.

## Echo

Tonight's sole remaining sheet has either taken an impression or missed the 9:00 PM window unpulled. If the bed cleared the drum, [the last clean sheet](piece-clean-sheet.md) now lies face-up bearing a concrete physical result shaped by how `Lever` played out onstage—registered square, over-corrected toward the opposite margin, skewed in a rushed handoff, thumb-marked, or unevenly pressed; if stopped before contact or timed out at close, the class ends with an unprinted sheet beside [the crooked proof](piece-crooked-proof.md). Either way, everyone in [the workshop](place-workshop.md) sees whose hands made or withheld the try and what came off the press.
