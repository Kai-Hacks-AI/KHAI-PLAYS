# Observations: TO HOLD at the Printmaking Bench

Four isolated runs staged [play.md](../02-company/play.md) (*One Clean Sheet*) from the same 8:55 PM opening with all eleven Company files fixed. In every run, as [Theo](../02-company/persona-student.md) stood in the single-person press apron with wiped hands reaching for the brass clips beside [the last clean sheet](../02-company/piece-clean-sheet.md) before lifting it from the tray, [Arthur](../02-company/persona-former-teacher.md) walked in, stopped beside [the crooked proof](../02-company/piece-crooked-proof.md), and said, *“I remember that mistake.”* Runs [01-teacher-only-a](../04-runs/01-teacher-only-a.md) and [02-teacher-only-b](../04-runs/02-teacher-only-b.md) cast Arthur as a visitor (**Teacher only**); runs [03-teacher-and-student-a](../04-runs/03-teacher-and-student-a.md) and [04-teacher-and-student-b](../04-runs/04-teacher-and-student-b.md) resumed `Arthur -> Teacher -> Nadia` and `Nadia -> Student -> Arthur` upon arrival alongside `Nadia -> Teacher -> Theo` (**Teacher and Student**). All four runs concluded naturally at 11 performer turns (`8:55 PM` to `8:59`–`9:00 PM`) without reaching the 12-turn loop guard.

## 2. Moves Made Possible by Active Positions (`Has`)

- **`Student` (`Theo -> Nadia` in all 4 runs):** In every run, Theo's `Shadow` pulled him for half a beat toward handing Nadia the clips when she stepped into his apron (`Turn 5`). Holding [Student](../02-company/position-student.md) (`Has`: first claim on the next attempt; `Orders`: visibly risk one's own hands rather than letting someone else absorb the try) gave Theo explicit standing to pull the clips back and ask his instructor to clear the single-body apron ([01](../04-runs/01-teacher-only-a.md) Turn 5; [02](../04-runs/02-teacher-only-b.md) Turn 5; [03](../04-runs/03-teacher-and-student-a.md) Turn 5; [04](../04-runs/04-teacher-and-student-b.md) Turn 5: *"cranking the wheel after you lay the paper isn't me learning the pull"*).
- **`Teacher` (`Nadia -> Theo` in all 4 runs):** [Teacher](../02-company/position-teacher.md) (`Has`) let Nadia open every run by reading the mechanical flaw on [the crooked proof](../02-company/piece-crooked-proof.md) and requiring Theo to state his hand-prep and two-corner sighting plan before touching the clips—and in [01-teacher-only-a](../04-runs/01-teacher-only-a.md) (Turn 7) let her establish a studio floor rule (`"One voice on the floor while the clock's running, Arthur"`).
- **`Teacher` (`Arthur -> Nadia` in [03](../04-runs/03-teacher-and-student-a.md)–[04](../04-runs/04-teacher-and-student-b.md) only):** In **Teacher only** ([01](../04-runs/01-teacher-only-a.md)–[02](../04-runs/02-teacher-only-b.md)), Arthur spoke strictly in impersonal shop observations (`"Don't mind me—just passing the stairwell"`, [02](../04-runs/02-teacher-only-b.md) Turn 6) without asking Nadia to justify a single decision. Resuming [Teacher](../02-company/position-teacher.md) (`Has`: standing to ask the Student to account for how they intend to make the next attempt) in **Teacher and Student** enabled Arthur to put Nadia directly on the clock twice in **both** runs: forcing her at `8:57 PM` to name her call at the tray ([03](../04-runs/03-teacher-and-student-a.md) Turn 6; [04](../04-runs/04-teacher-and-student-b.md) Turn 6) and testing her bench judgment again at `8:58`–`8:59 PM` after `Echo` ([03](../04-runs/03-teacher-and-student-a.md) Turn 9: `"How are you reading the sheet for him before washup?"`; [04](../04-runs/04-teacher-and-student-b.md) Turn 9: `"call the order on your bench before the tubes go dark"`).

## 3. Costs Holders Actually Bore (`Orders` & `Loses`)

- **Nadia (`Teacher` `Orders` & `Loses` across all 4 runs):** Stepping out of the single-person press apron on `Turn 7` forced Nadia to withdraw her reaching hands in front of her former teacher, stand clear of an irreversible final sheet she could no longer rescue physically, and leave the wet finished print in Theo's clips during the consequence beat (`Turns 9–10`).
- **Nadia (`Student` `Loses` in [03](../04-runs/03-teacher-and-student-a.md)–[04](../04-runs/04-teacher-and-student-b.md)):** Holding `Student` toward Arthur cost Nadia both the standing to wave off his scrutiny (`Turn 4`) and unwatched privacy around her own crowding mistake—forcing her in [03-teacher-and-student-a](../04-runs/03-teacher-and-student-a.md) (`Turn 10`) to own aloud in front of her own student that her three-minute apron intrusion dried the upper relief lines, and in [04-teacher-and-student-b](../04-runs/04-teacher-and-student-b.md) (`Turns 9–10`) to answer Arthur's callout (`"once you finally took your hands off his clips"`) and the 60-second solvent crunch it left behind.
- **Theo (`Student` `Loses` across all 4 runs):** Refusing Nadia's takeover cost Theo his safety net; every trace on the pulled rag—the 1 mm left guard in [02](../04-runs/02-teacher-only-b.md) and the 1/16-inch mid-hover shift in [03](../04-runs/03-teacher-and-student-a.md)—landed plainly as his own wrist's mark under two veterans' eyes.
- **Arthur (`Teacher` `Orders` & `Loses` in [03](../04-runs/03-teacher-and-student-a.md)–[04](../04-runs/04-teacher-and-student-b.md)):** Whenever Arthur's `Tell` lifted two fingers from his street-coat pocket toward the press bed (`Turns 6, 9`), `Teacher` barred him from touching paper or iron, holding him outside the apron while Nadia and Theo acted.

## 4. Seeing a Former Teacher vs. Actively Holding `Student`—and Two Positions Pressing on One Move

Comparing [01](../04-runs/01-teacher-only-a.md)–[02](../04-runs/02-teacher-only-b.md) against [03](../04-runs/03-teacher-and-student-a.md)–[04](../04-runs/04-teacher-and-student-b.md) separates what came from `Persona` history (`TO PAST`) from what came from holding `Student` (`TO HOLD`):

1. **Seeing a former teacher (`Persona` pressure, present in all 4 runs):** Even when Arthur arrived strictly as a visitor in [01](../04-runs/01-teacher-only-a.md) and [02](../04-runs/02-teacher-only-b.md), `"I remember that mistake"` pricked Nadia's six-year-old shop memory (`Tell` firing three times on `Turn 4` in both runs) and drove her `Shadow` reflex straight into Theo's apron to take over the clip setup. Personal history alone was enough to rattle Nadia toward crowding Theo.
2. **What holding `Student` changed (`01`–`02` vs. `03`–`04`):**
   - **Boundary vs. owed accounting on `Turn 4`:** Without `Student -> Arthur`, Nadia immediately bounded Arthur as an outsider to her class (`"Evening, Arthur—Theo's on his final pull"`, `"Class runs till nine"`, `"good night, lights go out at nine"`). With `Student -> Arthur` active, `Student.Loses` removed her standing to brush off his scrutiny: on `Turn 4` of both [03](../04-runs/03-teacher-and-student-a.md) and [04](../04-runs/04-teacher-and-student-b.md), she met his remark directly and accounted to him for the dropped right edge before turning to the tray.
   - **Both Positions pressing on the same move (`Turns 4 & 7` in `03`–`04`):** On `Turn 4`, `Student -> Arthur` (`Orders`: visibly commit hands or judgment under the Teacher's eye) compounded Nadia's `Shadow` push to control the sheet ([04](../04-runs/04-teacher-and-student-b.md): covering both brass clips to hinge the sheet down herself while leaving Theo only the wheel), colliding directly with `Teacher -> Theo` (`Orders`: owe direct material control; `Loses`: no hands-on takeover). On `Turn 7`, with four hands at the tray and Arthur demanding her call, keeping the clips would violate `Teacher -> Theo`, while stepping back into uninvolved silence would violate `Student -> Arthur`.
   - **How the double bind resolved (and why Theo still pulled the sheet in all 4 runs):** Because [position-student.md](../02-company/position-student.md#L17) (`Orders`) required committing *"one's own hands **or judgment**"*—and because Arthur also held [position-teacher.md](../02-company/position-teacher.md) rather than demanding hands-on proof—Nadia could satisfy both Positions on `Turn 7` of [03](../04-runs/03-teacher-and-student-a.md) and [04](../04-runs/04-teacher-and-student-b.md) by dividing hands from call: yielding physical control of the clips and apron to Theo (`Teacher -> Theo`) while explicitly putting her instructional call on the line under Arthur's eye (`"I'll stand on the pull he makes from it"` in [03](../04-runs/03-teacher-and-student-a.md); `"whatever comes off this bed answers to my call"` in [04](../04-runs/04-teacher-and-student-b.md)), which Arthur then tested in `Turns 9–10`.

## 5. Directions, Timings, and Clarifications Supplied by Directors

The differences across runs include both what the casting changed and how each Director staged open conditions in [play.md](../02-company/play.md), [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md), and [process-last-pull.md](../02-company/process-last-pull.md):

- **Opening turn order and `8:57 PM` routing:** All four Directors cued Nadia first at `8:55 PM` so Theo's `Turn 2` preparation inside the apron fired Arthur's `8:56 PM` entrance cue cleanly before the sheet left the tray, and all four routed `Turn 6` (`8:57 PM`) to Arthur after Theo resisted yielding the clips (`Turn 5`) so Nadia's `Turn 7` choice answered both voices at once.
- **Clock pacing and `Echo` adjudication:** Every Director advanced the five-minute clock roughly one minute per exchange (`8:55 → 9:00 PM`) and adjudicated `Echo` from `Lever` outside the Personas: registering clean square pulls where setup and wrist level held ([01](../04-runs/01-teacher-only-a.md), [04](../04-runs/04-teacher-and-student-b.md)), a 1 mm leftward guard where Theo locked his right wrist high ([02](../04-runs/02-teacher-only-b.md)), a 1/16-inch shift plus dry upper-line ink grain from the three-minute open-air delay ([03](../04-runs/03-teacher-and-student-a.md)), and a 60-second wet-ink/solvent crunch at `8:59 PM` ([04](../04-runs/04-teacher-and-student-b.md)).
- **Physical contact ruling in [04-teacher-and-student-b](../04-runs/04-teacher-and-student-b.md):** When Nadia nudged the block's far edge on `Turn 4` before resting her hands on the brass clips, the Director ruled she touched the block's uninked vertical wooden edge (consistent with her clean thumb `Tell`), keeping the clip jaws spotless for `Echo`.
