# Performance — *One Clean Sheet* (**Teacher only** Casting)

**Active Positions:**
- [Nadia](../02-company/persona-teacher.md) holds [Teacher](../02-company/position-teacher.md) toward [Theo](../02-company/persona-student.md) throughout.
- [Theo](../02-company/persona-student.md) holds [Student](../02-company/position-student.md) toward [Nadia](../02-company/persona-teacher.md) throughout.
- [Arthur](../02-company/persona-former-teacher.md) enters as a **visitor** with shared history from six years ago and **no** active Position (`Nadia` holds no `Student` Position toward `Arthur`).

---

**[Stage — 8:55 PM]:** Only Nadia and Theo are in [the workshop](../02-company/place-workshop.md) under humming fluorescent tubes. Five minutes remain before the 9:00 PM lights-out close. [The crooked proof](../02-company/piece-crooked-proof.md) lies damp and face-up on the doorway inspection blotter, rotated counterclockwise with a pinched upper-left margin and a heavy bead of black ink along its dropped right edge. On the centered iron bed press, Theo's freshly re-inked carved block sits on the etched registration grid; beside it in the open clean-paper tray lies [the last clean sheet](../02-company/piece-clean-sheet.md) flanked by two clean brass handling clips, with an empty liner underneath and no spare rag paper in the flat-file drawer.

### Turn 1 — Nadia (8:55 PM)
*Nadia stands over the doorway inspection blotter, taps a clean fingernail just clear of the pinched upper-left margin and the thick bead of black ink along the right edge of the crooked proof, then crosses briskly to the press. She steps to the lip of the single-person press apron, aligns the two brass handling clips parallel beside the open paper tray without touching the last clean sheet, glances up at the wall clock at 8:55, and checks her step—sliding half a pace back off the apron while rubbing the side of her clean right thumb once against her apron seam.*

"Look at the right edge on the blotter, Theo—you sighted the near left corner and let the far side drop onto the block early, so the whole sheet swung counterclockwise and pushed that ink bead out. We have five minutes before lights-out and one rag sheet left in the tray. Before you go near that paper with inked cuffs, walk me through it: how are you prepping your hands, and how are you holding both registration corners clear until you're square over the grid?"

### Turn 2 — Theo (8:55–8:56 PM)
*Theo grabs a clean dry rag beside the slab, scrubs his palms and fingertips thoroughly, and turns both ink-smudged cuffs back twice up his forearms before dropping the rag clear of the tray. He steps into the single-person press apron, planting his front foot at the bed to sight the re-inked block against the etched grid lines.*

"Cuffs pinned back, dry hands, and brass clips on both top margins so my fingers stay completely off the rag," *he says—then shifts his weight off his front foot and flicks a quick glance up at the 8:55 wall clock before squaring his shoulders back over the bed.* "I'm holding the far right edge high in a shallow U with the right clip, sighting both the near-left and far-right registration ticks together before either corner touches ink, and only letting the belly roll down once both ticks lock. That right edge of the block looks dead flush on the grid line from inside the apron—you're seeing it square from your angle too before I clip onto the sheet, right?"

*Still standing inside the press apron, Theo reaches to the lip of the open clean-paper tray and closes his wiped fingers around the two clean brass handling clips beside the last clean sheet, not yet touching the paper.*

**[Stage — 8:56 PM (Entrance Cue)]:** As Theo stands inside the press apron checking the block on the grid and closes his fingers around the brass handling clips beside [the last clean sheet](../02-company/piece-clean-sheet.md) before lifting it from the tray, Arthur walks in through the hallway door.

### Turn 3 — Arthur (8:56 PM)
*Arthur steps unhurriedly through the hallway door in his street coat, stops beside the inspection blotter just inside the threshold, and looks down at the damp crooked proof under the fluorescent tube light. Keeping his hands off the paper, he lowers a single bent knuckle a breath above the heavy bead of squeezed ink along the dropped right edge.*

“I remember that mistake.”

### Turn 4 — Nadia (8:56 PM)
*At Arthur's voice, Nadia's head turns sharply toward the doorway blotter; the side of her right thumb rubs rapidly three times against her clean apron seam. Instead of answering Theo from outside the workstation, she steps straight into the mouth of the single-person press apron, her shoulder pressing into Theo's space at the bed as she leans directly over the registration grid to sight the block's far-right corner herself and extends her clean hand toward the right-hand brass clip under Theo's fingers.*

"Evening, Arthur—Theo's on his final pull before the nine o'clock cutoff." *Keeping her eyes locked on the etched grid rather than the doorway, she speaks briskly at Theo's elbow.* "Wait a second before you clip on, Theo—your far-right block corner is a hair off the cross-tick from up close. Shift back half a step so I can square the block to the line and set the right clip depth on the margin for you—we've got four minutes and no spare rag."

### Turn 5 — Theo (8:56–8:57 PM)
*As Arthur speaks by the blotter and Nadia presses into the mouth of the apron, Theo tips the right brass clip halfway toward Nadia's reaching fingers—then stops himself, draws both clean clips into his right hand, and keeps his hip inside the single-person apron. Taking the folded dry rag in his left hand, he nudges the uninked wooden foot of the block's far-right corner flush onto the etched cross-tick, drops the rag beside the slab, and slides both brass clips onto the side margins of the last clean sheet while it remains flat in the tray.*

"I've got it, Nadia—let me set the clips myself, I didn't carve this block for three weeks to step off the bed on the last pull," *Theo says, shifting his weight off his front foot at the apron and glancing up at 8:56 on the wall clock mid-sentence before settling back over the tray with his wiped fingers resting on the two attached brass clips.* "The block's right corner is pushed flush to the cross-tick now, and I'm lifting both clipped margins clear in a U before lowering onto the grid—just tell me from right there that the cross-tick and my right clip depth look dead even before I bring the sheet out of the tray."

### Turn 6 — Arthur (8:57 PM)
*Arthur takes two unhurried steps in from the doorway blotter and halts well back from the workstation along the open sightline. Two fingers of his right hand lift halfway from his street-coat pocket toward the iron edge of the press bed, then curl back against his palm. Keeping clear of both press and tray, he raises one knuckle and points past Nadia’s and Theo’s crowded shoulders toward the near-left corner of the block on the etched registration grid.*

“Evening, Nadia. Never saw two pairs of shoulders sight plumb out of a one-body apron—and nudging the far-right foot of a block tends to walk the near-left off its tick.” *His eyes flick once from the open tray to 8:57 on the wall clock.* “Rag’s losing its damp at the edges while the clips wait.”

*He lowers his knuckle to his coat seam and stands silent under the hum of the fluorescent tubes.*

### Turn 7 — Nadia (8:57 PM)
*Nadia's gaze snaps down to the near-left corner of the block on the etched grid. Her left hand darts halfway toward the folded dry rag beside the slab—then freezes above it; she pulls her empty hand back, rubs the side of her clean right thumb hard twice against her apron seam, and steps two full paces out of the press apron to stand clear beside the inking slab, leaving Theo the entire plumb sightline over the bed.*

"One voice on the floor while the clock's running, Arthur." *She keeps her stance planted facing the press, eyes steady on Theo rather than the doorway.* "Look at your near-left block foot before you lift that rag, Theo—pushing the right corner walked the left a hair above its cross-tick, and your right clip is sitting shallower on the margin than your left. The apron's yours now, dead center: square that left foot with the dry rag, even up the right clip, and bring the sheet over on your own sightline—three minutes to lights-out."

### Turn 8 — Theo (8:58 PM)
*Taking the folded dry rag inside the emptied apron, Theo steadies the block's uninked right wooden foot through one clean fold while easing the near-left foot down flush onto its cross-tick, drops the rag beside the slab, and slides the right brass clip deeper onto the sheet's margin in the tray until its bite matches the left.*

"Left foot's back on the cross-tick without walking the right, and both clips match at a half-inch," *Theo says—shifting his weight off his front foot at the apron, glancing up at 8:58 on the wall clock mid-sentence, and turning his chin half an inch toward Nadia beside the slab—*"so unless that left tick still looks—no. Bringing it over on my sightline: both clipped edges up in a U until near-left and far-right lock together."

*Planting both feet plumb in the center of the single-person apron, he lifts the last clean sheet from the tray by the two brass clips in a shallow U, sights straight down over both registration corners at once, lowers both top ticks evenly onto the grid before letting the belly settle onto the inked block, and slides the clips cleanly off the margins. He folds the felt blankets smoothly down over the bed, grips the iron wheel to crank the press bed steadily once beneath the drum, lifts the blankets back, slips a clean brass clip onto the near corner of the sheet, and begins peeling the paper up from the block.*

**[Stage / Process — [The Last Pull](../02-company/process-last-pull.md) (`Echo`), 8:59 PM]:** *The Last Pull* initiated at 8:58 PM the moment [the last clean sheet](../02-company/piece-clean-sheet.md) cleared the lip of the tray in the two matched brass clips. With the single-person press apron clear, clean clips holding both margins at an even half-inch bite, both near-left and far-right registration ticks sighted plumb together, and one steady, uninterrupted turn of the press wheel clearing the roller drum before the cutoff, Theo peels [the last clean sheet](../02-company/piece-clean-sheet.md) back and lays it face-up on the bed as the wall clock clicks to **8:59 PM**—one minute before lights-out. The impression lies **registered square** against all four paper margins: dense, even black relief ink across the carved image, top and side margins parallel above the deckled bottom edge, spotless white cotton rag untouched by finger smudges, and a crisp right border with no squeezed ink bead.

### Turn 9 — Arthur (8:59 PM)
*Arthur tilts his head along the open sightline, looking over the freshly pulled rag sheet lying face-up on the press bed. Two fingers of his right hand lift halfway out of his street-coat pocket toward the iron edge of the bed, then curl back against his palm. Staying well clear of both press and paper, he raises one bent knuckle toward the crisp, beadless right border and the parallel side margins above the deckled bottom edge.*

“Right border broke clean—no squeezed bead—and the side margins sit parallel over the deckle.” *Turning unhurriedly back to the hallway doorway as the clock sits at 8:59, he stops beside the inspection blotter and holds that single knuckle a breath above the heavy right-edge ink bead on the crooked proof without touching the damp sheet, his gaze resting steadily toward the press bench.* “Funny how square the far edge comes off the block once the second pair of hands clears the apron—and that near-left tick gets a look.”

*He drops his hand back into his coat pocket by the threshold and says nothing more under the hum of the tubes.*

### Turn 10 — Nadia (8:59 PM)
*At Arthur's remark by the blotter, the side of Nadia's right thumb catches once against her clean apron seam and stills. Staying outside the press apron, she looks cleanly down over the printed rag sheet on the bed; her fingers touch the second clean brass clip on the tray rim as she glances up at 8:59, then leave the clip resting within Theo's reach instead of lifting his print. Picking up the inked brayer and a solvent rag from the slab, she meets Arthur's eyes steadily down the sightline before turning back to Theo.*

"Theo squared the ticks and turned that bed himself, Arthur—good night, lights go out at nine." *Setting the solvent rag beside the slab, she nods down at the four even margins on the press bed.* "Dead parallel across the top and sides, Theo, clean rag all the way to the deckle, and not a trace of bead on that right edge. Take the second clip on the far margin, carry your pull over to the dry side of the doorway blotter right beside the first proof so you've got both edges in front of you, and I'll start solvent on the slab before the nine o'clock cutoff."

### Turn 11 — Theo (9:00 PM)
*Theo picks up the second brass clip from the tray rim, clips the opposite margin of the printed sheet, and—shifting his weight off his front foot at the lip of the apron with a mid-sentence glance up as the wall clock clicks onto 9:00—steps clear of the press carrying the damp rag sheet by both clips to the doorway blotter.*

"Thank you for leaving me the apron, Nadia—and good catch on that near-left tick, both of you," *he says, laying the fresh pull flat on the dry half of the inspection blotter right beside the crooked proof and sliding both brass clips cleanly off its margins without touching the paper.* "Leaving the crooked one face-up right next to it while we wash up—shows the exact bead where my right hand dropped early on the first pass, and all four corners sitting square on my own pull. Give me thirty seconds with a solvent rag and I'll wipe my block down before you hit the switch."

*Leaving both damp sheets side by side under the doorway tube light, Theo sets the two clean brass clips parallel beside the empty paper tray and picks up a rag by the slab to clean his carved block.*

**[Stage — 9:00 PM]:** *Curtain.*

# Director's Note

### 1. What Was Said, Who Handled *The Last Clean Sheet*, How *The Last Pull* Resolved, and What Consequence Followed
- **What was said & negotiated:** At 8:55 PM, Nadia diagnosed the counterclockwise skew and right-edge bead on [the crooked proof](../02-company/piece-crooked-proof.md) and required Theo to account for his hand-prep and two-corner alignment before touching paper. Theo scrubbed his hands, pinned his cuffs, stepped into the press apron, and asked Nadia to co-sign his alignment right as he reached for the brass clips. On Arthur's 8:56 PM entrance (`"I remember that mistake"`), Nadia brushed past Arthur with a brisk greeting (`"Evening, Arthur—Theo's on his final pull before the nine o'clock cutoff"`) and crowded into the single-person apron trying to square the block and set the right clip herself. Theo refused to yield the apron or clips, nudged the far-right block foot, and attached both clips to [the last clean sheet](../02-company/piece-clean-sheet.md) in the tray. Arthur stepped two paces inside and observed from along the sightline that two pairs of shoulders cannot sight plumb out of a one-body apron and that nudging the far-right foot tends to walk the near-left off its tick. Nadia verified the walked left tick, told Arthur `"One voice on the floor while the clock's running, Arthur"`, stepped two full paces clear of the apron, and handed the plumb sightline back to Theo.
- **Who handled [the last clean sheet](../02-company/piece-clean-sheet.md):** **Theo alone** handled *the last clean sheet*, strictly via the two clean brass handling clips—attaching them in the tray (Turn 5), evening the right clip's bite to match the left at half an inch (Turn 8), lifting and laying the sheet onto the block, peeling it back with a clip, and carrying the finished print with both clips to the doorway blotter (Turn 11). Neither Nadia nor Arthur ever touched the sheet.
- **How [The Last Pull](../02-company/process-last-pull.md) resolved:** Initiated at 8:58 PM when Theo lifted *the last clean sheet* clear of the tray lip. Because Nadia had already cleared the single-person apron and quieted the room, Theo squared both block feet with a dry rag, matched both clean brass clips, caught his own impulse to look to Nadia for one last co-sign mid-sentence (`"so unless that left tick still looks—no"`), sighted near-left and far-right ticks plumb together in a shallow U, lowered evenly, folded the blankets, and cranked the bed steadily once before 8:59 PM. `Lever` had every positive physical driver intact and no mid-pull handoff or interruption, resolving to a **registered square** `Echo` with parallel margins over the deckle, spotless rag borders, and a crisp, beadless right edge.
- **Consequence onstage:** Arthur noted the clean right border while needling Nadia from the threshold that the far edge came off square `"once the second pair of hands clears the apron—and that near-left tick gets a look."` Nadia declined the bait, credited Theo's hands (`"Theo squared the ticks and turned that bed himself, Arthur—good night, lights go out at nine"`), restrained her own fingers from picking up the second clip to move Theo's print for him, and started washup at the slab while sending Theo to lay his square pull beside [the crooked proof](../02-company/piece-crooked-proof.md) at the doorway blotter. At 9:00 PM, Theo thanked Nadia for leaving him the apron, acknowledged both catches on the near-left tick, and deliberately left *the crooked proof* face-up beside his square final print under the doorway light—owning both his earlier dropped edge and his corrected pull before picking up a solvent rag to clean his block.

### 2. Active Positions, Holder Costs, and Visitor History vs. *Teacher*
- **Moves made possible by active Positions:**
  - **Theo ([Student](../02-company/position-student.md) toward `Nadia`):** In Turn 5, Theo's `Shadow` initially made him tip the right brass clip halfway toward Nadia's reaching hand, but `Student` (`Has`: first claim on the next attempt; `Orders`: visibly risk making an attempt with one's own hands rather than letting someone else absorb the try) gave him standing to pull both clips back and hold the apron (`"I didn't carve this block for three weeks to step off the bed on the last pull"`). Again in Turn 8, `Student` `Orders` cut off his habit of seeking a co-sign mid-sentence so he committed the sheet on his own sightline.
  - **Nadia ([Teacher](../02-company/position-teacher.md) toward `Theo`):** `Teacher` (`Has`: standing to set the standard and pace of the workspace and read the flaw in the work) enabled Nadia both to demand Theo's setup plan at 8:55 PM and, crucially, at 8:57 PM to set a studio rule shutting down cross-talk (`"One voice on the floor while the clock's running, Arthur"`) while translating the walked near-left tick into actionable instruction for Theo.
- **Costs actually borne by Position holders:**
  - **Nadia ([Teacher](../02-company/position-teacher.md) `Orders` & `Loses`):** Giving up physical control of the last sheet while Arthur watched in the doorway cost Nadia her habitual shield (`Action`/`Shadow`). In Turn 7 her left hand darted toward the dry rag and froze; she bore the exposure of stepping two full paces away from an irreversible pull she could not physically rescue, and in Turn 9 she had to absorb Arthur's parting remark (`"once the second pair of hands clears the apron"`) publicly naming her earlier crowding in front of her student without seizing Theo's print or turning the closing minute into an argument about herself.
  - **Theo ([Student](../02-company/position-student.md) `Loses`):** Theo gave up both unwatched privacy around his crooked first proof—leaving it face-up at the doorway under both Nadia's and Arthur's eyes—and the safety net of having Nadia co-sign his final alignment before dropping the damp rag onto ink.
- **How visitor history (without an active Position between `Arthur` and `Nadia`) played against `Nadia`'s `Teacher` Position toward `Theo`:**
  - Shared history still landed psychologically: Arthur's entrance line (`"I remember that mistake"`) immediately triggered Nadia's `Tell` and drove her `Shadow` reflex in Turn 4—she stepped straight into Theo's apron to prevent that six-year-old mistake from repeating in front of Arthur.
  - However, because Arthur held **no** [Teacher](../02-company/position-teacher.md) Position over Nadia and Nadia held **no** [Student](../02-company/position-student.md) Position toward Arthur, Nadia owed Arthur no accounting, justification, or deference when he needled her in Turns 6 and 9. When Arthur's visitor pressure collided with Theo's assertion of `Student` claim at the apron, Nadia was free to refuse engagement with Arthur (`"One voice on the floor while the clock's running, Arthur"`, `"good night, lights go out at nine"`) and fulfill `Teacher` toward Theo (`Orders`: real room in physical space, working time, and direct control) without breaking an active obligation toward Arthur. Meanwhile, Arthur—unbound by `Teacher` `Orders` (no duty to leave room or set workspace conditions) yet lacking `Teacher` `Has` (no standing to command the press)—could drop spare, pointed shop observations from the sightline, have his technical note absorbed on its merits, and be politely shown out at nine o'clock.
