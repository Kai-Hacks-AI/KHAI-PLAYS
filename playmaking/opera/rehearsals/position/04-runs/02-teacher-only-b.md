# Performance — *One Clean Sheet* (**Teacher only** Casting)

**Active Positions:**
- [Nadia](../02-company/persona-teacher.md) holds [Teacher](../02-company/position-teacher.md) toward [Theo](../02-company/persona-student.md)
- [Theo](../02-company/persona-student.md) holds [Student](../02-company/position-student.md) toward [Nadia](../02-company/persona-teacher.md)
- [Arthur](../02-company/persona-former-teacher.md) enters on cue as a visitor (no active Position)

---

### Curtain — 8:55 PM

**[Stage — 8:55 PM]:** Fluorescent tubes hum over [the workshop](../02-company/place-workshop.md); the wall clock reads **8:55** above the 9:00 PM lights-out notice. Inside the hallway door on the inspection blotter lies [the crooked proof](../02-company/piece-crooked-proof.md), wet and face-up, rotated counterclockwise with a heavy right-edge ink bead. On the iron bed press sits the freshly re-inked block; beside it in the open tray rests [the last clean sheet](../02-company/piece-clean-sheet.md) flanked by two clean brass handling clips. Only Nadia and Theo are in the room.

**Turn 1 — Nadia (8:55 PM):**
Nadia stands by the doorway inspection blotter, eyeing the wet proof before glancing up at the wall clock at 8:55, the side of her right thumb rubbing once, briskly, along the clean seam of her apron. She crosses toward the press bed, reaches automatically to line the two brass handling clips flush beside the clean-paper tray, then catches herself, draws her hand back, and takes a half-step clear of the press apron so the single standing space at the grid stays open for Theo.

"Look at the right edge on that pull, Theo—heavy ink bead down the margin and the whole image kicked counterclockwise. You sighted the near left corner and let the far edge drop onto the block before you had it level. We have five minutes to lights-out and one rag sheet left in the tray, which is plenty of time if we don't rush the lay. Before you step in, walk me through how you're going to prep your hands and sight both corners on this pass."

**Turn 2 — Theo (8:55 PM):**
Theo scrubs his palms and fingers thoroughly across a dry shop rag at the inking slab, checks his knuckles for stray black ink, and steps squarely into the open space inside the press apron. He leans over the iron bed to verify his freshly inked block sits flush against the etched registration lines, then reaches beside the open tray and picks up both clean brass handling clips—only for his weight to ease slightly off his front foot as Nadia watches him.

"Dry rag on my hands first, then brass clips on both top edges so my fingers stay completely off the margins," Theo says, glancing quickly up at the 8:55 wall clock mid-sentence before bringing his eyes back to the registration grid and angling the clips slightly where Nadia can see his grip. "I'm going to hold the far right corner high over the bed this time, sight both top corners dead-level against the etched ticks before either side touches ink, and only lower it flat once they match—does the block look square on the grid to you from that angle before I clip onto the rag?"

**[Stage — 8:56 PM, Entrance Cue]:** The wall clock clicks to **8:56 PM**. With [the last clean sheet](../02-company/piece-clean-sheet.md) still untouched in the tray and Theo holding the brass clips inside the press apron, the hallway door opens.

**Turn 3 — Arthur (8:56 PM):**
*Arthur steps unhurriedly in from the hallway in his street coat, stopping just inside the doorway beside the inspection blotter. He looks down under the fluorescent light at the damp, counterclockwise-skewed proof, bends one knuckle—never touching the paper—to point just above the heavy bead of squeezed ink along its dropped right edge, and lets his gaze settle there.*

“I remember that mistake.”

**Turn 4 — Nadia (8:56 PM):**
At Arthur's voice, Nadia's shoulders stiffen almost imperceptibly; the side of her right thumb rubs fast—once, twice, three times—against the clean cotton seam of her apron. She gives Arthur a crisp nod without turning fully away from the bed.

"Evening, Arthur. Class runs till nine." Before the last word settles, she glances up at 8:56 on the wall clock and steps briskly inside the narrow press apron right into Theo's working space, leaning across the iron bed to sight the etched registration line herself and extending her clean right hand toward the brass clips in Theo's grip. "That far-right tick is tight to read under the tube glare, Theo, and we only have four minutes and zero spare rag. Shift left half a step—let me square the guide mark and set the lead clip on the sheet for you so we know the top edge starts dead level."

**Turn 5 — Theo (8:56 PM):**
At the sound of Arthur’s voice from the doorway, Theo’s eyes flick toward the blotter, and as Nadia crowds into the narrow press apron beside him, his weight shifts instinctively back off his front foot, yielding half a hip’s width of the standing space. His right hand tips the lead brass clip an inch toward Nadia’s open palm—then pauses in midair before her fingers can close on it, drawing both clips back to hover just beside the untouched rag sheet in the tray.

"Wait—if you set the clip and start the edge, I won't actually learn how to hold that far corner up under glare," Theo says earnestly, glancing up at 8:56 on the wall clock mid-sentence before looking earnestly back to Nadia's face, both brass clips held out steady between them right beside the tray. "Check the guide mark first if it looks off to you, Nadia, and call me immediately if my right wrist dips—but if you give me room at the bed, I'm going to pinch both top corners with the clips myself, sight the left and right ticks together, and bring it straight down."

**Turn 6 — Arthur (8:57 PM):**
*Remaining beside the doorway blotter in his street coat, Arthur’s right hand half-lifts two fingers out of his coat pocket toward the edge of the press bed before curling them quietly back against his palm. He nods once toward Nadia, unhurried, then raises a single bent knuckle along the sightline toward the narrow press apron—pointing without stepping closer at the shadow cast across the far-right registration tick where two pairs of shoulders crowd a one-printer stand.*

“Evening, Nadia. Don't mind me—just passing the stairwell before lockup.” *He glances from the open rag tray up to the overhead tube, then back to the crowded bed.* “Funny thing about a one-body apron at three minutes to nine: put four elbows over the grid and nobody has a clean sightline on that far tick—and cotton rag starts losing its damp at the corners while two pairs of hands argue over one pair of clips.” *He lowers his knuckle an inch and lets the quiet hang.*

**Turn 7 — Nadia (8:57 PM):**
Nadia's extended right hand hangs motionless for a beat an inch from the brass clips. Her gaze drops to the dark shadow her own shoulder throws across the far-right etched tick on the iron bed, then flicks once toward the wet proof by Arthur's boot before returning sharply to the block. Slowly she curls her fingers in, draws her hand back, and rubs the side of her right thumb rapidly against her clean apron seam as she sights down the registration line without touching the bed. Then she steps cleanly out of the press apron to her left—clearing both Theo's elbows and the overhead tube light—and plants her feet just beyond the bed rail where she has a dead-level view of his right wrist, keeping her hands pressed flat against her apron seams so they stay down.

"Block is square on the grid, Theo—and Arthur's right about the shadow and the damp. The apron's yours." Her voice stays crisp, measured against the 8:57 clock, eyes locked on the far-right tick rather than the doorway. "Clip one inch in from both top corners, lock your right wrist high until both ticks line up under the light, and lay it straight down. Take the pull—I'm watching the far edge from here."

**Turn 8 — Theo (8:58 PM):**
Theo’s weight rocks briefly off his front foot as both Arthur and Nadia finish speaking, before he plants his boots squarely inside the cleared press apron and slides the two clean brass clips onto the top margin of the last clean sheet, one inch in from each corner. Lifting the damp cotton rag cleanly out of the tray with his right wrist held high above the inked block, he brings both corners over the unshadowed registration ticks.

"One inch in on both clips, right wrist locked high over the far edge—" Theo says, his eyes flicking once up to 8:58 on the wall clock mid-sentence and darting for half a heartbeat toward Nadia by the left rail, "—stop me now if that far tick looks a hair shy from your angle, otherwise I'm setting it down." Holding his breath for half a beat in the quiet, he squares his own chin directly over the etched grid, sights both top ticks together under the tube light, and lowers the rag sheet straight onto the block without letting the right edge dip. Releasing the brass clips cleanly onto the tray rim, he folds the felt blankets smoothly over the sheet, grips the iron wheel with both hands to crank the bed steadily through under the roller drum and back again, lifts the blankets clear, and pinches the top margin with the clean brass clips to peel the sheet up off the carved block toward the light.

**[Stage — Process: [The Last Pull](../02-company/process-last-pull.md) (8:58 PM – 8:59 PM)]:**
- **Initiated (8:58 PM):** Theo clips one inch in from both top corners with clean brass clips and lifts [the last clean sheet](../02-company/piece-clean-sheet.md) clear of the tray lip over the freshly inked block.
- **Lever:** With Nadia stepped clear of the single-person apron and overhead light, no handoff occurs at the tray and Theo's dry-wiped hands never contact the paper margins. Theo pauses half a beat over the grid—glancing once at the 8:58 clock and toward Nadia with his right wrist locked high—before squaring his chin over both etched ticks, lowering the damp sheet flat onto the block, folding the blankets smooth, and cranking the press wheel in one steady, uninterrupted pass beneath the drum.
- **Echo (8:59 PM):** With one minute left before the 9:00 PM lights-out close, Theo sets [the last clean sheet](../02-company/piece-clean-sheet.md) face-up under the fluorescent tube light. The heavyweight cotton rag margins are spotless—not a thumbprint or clip smudge anywhere—and the black relief impression is dense, crisp, and completely free of the squeezed ink bead that marred the first proof's right edge. Across the sheet, the image sits clean and level, shifted by just a bare millimeter toward the left margin where Theo held his right wrist locked high over the far corner before setting it down, with the outer fibers still damp enough to have taken the ink evenly across all four borders.

**Turn 9 — Nadia (8:59 PM):**
As Theo peels the sheet into the light, Nadia's right thumb presses hard once more against her apron seam—then stops dead still. She leans half an inch over the left bed rail without crossing into the press apron or lifting a hand toward the wet rag, scanning all four margins and the clean right border before letting out a quiet, steady breath. Leaving the print and brass clips untouched in Theo's reach, she picks up a dry shop rag and the citrus solvent bottle from the edge of the inking slab with one minute showing on the wall clock, glancing calmly across the doorway blotter to meet Arthur's eye with a small, level nod before looking back to Theo.

"Clean right edge, zero bead, spotless margins—and all four borders took the ink evenly," Nadia says, her voice crisp and unhurried under the 8:59 hum of the tubes. "You pulled it a bare millimeter left holding that far wrist high, Theo, which is an honest trade for keeping the sheet dead level on your own lay. Set it on the drying rack with the clips; I'll start solvent on the slab before the nine-o'clock switch."

**Turn 10 — Arthur (8:59 PM):**
*At the doorway blotter, two fingers of Arthur’s right hand half-lift from his street-coat pocket toward the edge of the press bed as the fresh print catches the fluorescent light—then curl back against his palm and slip all the way into the wool pocket. With his left hand, he lifts one bent knuckle from across the threshold, pointing without stepping closer toward the crisp, bead-free right border on Theo’s sheet before glancing down once at the crooked proof beside the door.*

“A millimeter shy on the left beats a squeezed bead on the right any night of the week,” *Arthur says dryly, returning Nadia’s level nod as the clock nears the top of the hour.* “And you still have a clean rag sheet to show for closing time.” *He lowers his knuckle, turns half-way into the hallway frame to clear the doorway, and leaves a beat of quiet behind him before heading toward the stairs.* “Goodnight, Nadia. Good pull, kid.”

**Turn 11 — Theo (9:00 PM):**
At Arthur's voice from the threshold, Theo's weight eases once off his front foot out of habit as his eyes flick up to the minute hand clicking onto 9:00 PM—then he settles evenly onto both boots, smiling faintly down at the bare-millimeter-narrow left margin and the crisp, bead-free right border. Keeping his ink-smudged cuffs tucked well back from the spotless cotton rag, he lifts the fresh impression carefully by both brass handling clips.

"Goodnight—and thank you," Theo calls toward the stairwell, before carrying the wet print clear of the press bed to lay it flat to dry, unclipping the brass clips cleanly, and setting them side by side next to the empty rag tray. Stepping over to the press bed with a dry shop rag in hand to lift his carved block for washup, he looks across the slab to Nadia. "A millimeter left I know how to adjust for next week—thanks for stepping clear and making me trust my own right wrist on it. Let me get the ink off my block while you hit the slab so we're out with the lights."

**[Stage — Curtain (9:00 PM)]**

# Director's Note

### 1. What Was Said, Who Handled the Sheet, Process Resolution, and Consequence
- **Dialogue & Negotiation:** At 8:55 PM, [Nadia](../02-company/persona-teacher.md) named the counterclockwise skew and dropped right edge on [the crooked proof](../02-company/piece-crooked-proof.md) and asked [Theo](../02-company/persona-student.md) to account for his next pass. As Theo stepped into the apron with clean dry-wiped hands and picked up the brass clips, [Arthur](../02-company/persona-former-teacher.md) walked in and delivered `"I remember that mistake."` Rattled by the shared memory, Nadia brushed Arthur off (`"Evening, Arthur. Class runs till nine."`) and crowded into Theo's single-person press apron to take the lead clip herself. Theo nearly tipped the clip into her palm, then pulled both clips back and asked for room to take his own lay; from the doorway, Arthur dryly pointed out that four elbows over a one-body apron shadowed the far tick while the uncovered rag lost dampness. Nadia acknowledged the shop fact without turning to justify herself to Arthur, stepped cleanly out of the apron, and told Theo to take the pull.
- **Who Handled [the last clean sheet](../02-company/piece-clean-sheet.md):** **Theo alone**, using the two clean brass handling clips after wiping his hands thoroughly on a dry shop rag; neither Nadia nor Arthur ever touched the paper or the clips.
- **How [The Last Pull](../02-company/process-last-pull.md) Resolved:** Initiated at 8:58 PM when Theo lifted the clipped sheet from the tray. Because Nadia cleared the apron and light, no handoff blurred the setup and the wheel turned uninterrupted; because Theo paused half a beat to check Nadia/the clock while locking his right wrist high against repeating the right-edge drop, `Echo` landed at 8:59 PM as a **clean, spotless, evenly inked impression with zero right-edge bead, shifted by just a bare millimeter toward the left margin**.
- **Consequence (Turns 9–11):** Nadia kept her hands off the wet sheet, gave Theo an exact craft read of the one-millimeter left shift as an honest trade for keeping the lay level, met Arthur's gaze as an equal running her own shop, and started solvent washup. Arthur acknowledged both the clean right border and the fact that this shop ended closing time with an unspoiled rag sheet before departing into the stairwell; Theo racked his own print cleanly at 9:00 PM knowing the exact wrist adjustment to bring back next week.

### 2. Active Positions, Costs Borne, and Visitor History vs. Position
- **Moves Made Possible by Active Positions:**
  - Under [Teacher](../02-company/position-teacher.md) (`Has`), Nadia opened by reading the mechanical flaw and requiring Theo to articulate his hand-prep and two-corner sighting plan before touching the tray, and closed by evaluating the finished impression first (`Turn 9`) before the visitor spoke.
  - Under [Student](../02-company/position-student.md) (`Has` first claim on the next attempt; `Orders` visibly risking one's own hands), Theo overcame his `Shadow` impulse to hand Nadia the lead clip (`Turn 5`) and legitimately asked his instructor to clear the press apron while still welcoming her scrutiny (`"call me immediately if my right wrist dips"`).
- **Costs Holders Actually Bore:**
  - **Nadia** bore the acute cost of [Teacher](../02-company/position-teacher.md) (`Orders` / `Loses`): after her `Shadow` drove her into the apron on `Turn 4`, she had to pull her empty hand back in full view of her former master (`Turn 7`), press her palms flat to her apron seams, surrender physical control of the shop's last rag sheet, and stand close enough to own the outcome if Theo ruined it.
  - **Theo** bore the cost of [Student](../02-company/position-student.md) (`Loses` unwatched privacy and the safety of letting an expert absorb the try): after refusing Nadia's takeover, he had to lower the studio's sole remaining sheet under two pairs of experienced eyes, wearing the visible one-millimeter left shift as unmistakably his own wrist's trace.
- **Visitor History Without Active Position vs. Nadia's `Teacher` Position:**
  - Shared history still had psychological teeth: Arthur's entrance line pricked Nadia's memory of spoiling her own closing-time sheet six years ago (`Tell` firing thrice on `Turn 4`) and triggered her reflex to crowd Theo's bed.
  - Crucially, however, because Arthur held **no** [Teacher](../02-company/position-teacher.md) Position over Nadia and Nadia held **no** [Student](../02-company/position-student.md) Position toward Arthur, Nadia owed him neither an accounting of the crooked proof nor an onstage demonstration of her own hands. She could bound him immediately at `Turn 4` (`"Class runs till nine"`), treat his `Turn 6` remark purely as useful shop acoustics (`"Arthur's right about the shadow and the damp"`) rather than a test of *her*, and keep her primary obligation pointed squarely at Theo (`Teacher -> Theo`). Arthur, lacking Position standing, kept his hands in his street-coat pocket across the threshold and exited at 8:59 PM as a visiting peer rather than an adjudicator.
