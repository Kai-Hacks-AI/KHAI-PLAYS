**[Stage — 8:55 PM]:** *Second-floor printmaking workshop ([the workshop](../02-company/place-workshop.md)), five minutes before the 9:00 PM lights-out close. Under humming fluorescent tubes, [the crooked proof](../02-company/piece-crooked-proof.md) lies damp and face-up on the doorway inspection blotter, rotated counterclockwise with its upper-left margin pinched narrow and a heavy bead of squeezed ink along the dropped right edge. Center-room, the freshly re-inked block rests on the iron press bed's registration grid; beside it in the open clean-paper tray lies [the last clean sheet](../02-company/piece-clean-sheet.md) flanked by two clean brass handling clips. Only [Nadia](../02-company/persona-teacher.md) and [Theo](../02-company/persona-student.md) are in the room.*

**Nadia:**
*Nadia glances from the doorway inspection blotter up to the wall clock at 8:55, then steps briskly to the edge of the press apron, nudging the two clean brass handling clips square beside the open paper tray with one clean fingertip before folding her hands back against her apron bib—her right thumb giving a quick, dry rub along the seam.*

"Look at the right edge on that blotter proof, Theo—you sighted only the near left corner and let the far side drop early onto the ink, which pulled the whole margin counterclockwise and squeezed out that heavy bead. We have five minutes before lights-out and one rag sheet left in the tray. Wipe your hands down first, then tell me how you're going to sight both registration corners before you pick up the clips."

**Theo:**
*Theo scrubs the black ink from his fingers and palms with a dry shop rag, steps squarely into the single-person press apron to check the freshly inked block against the etched grid, and picks up the two clean brass handling clips beside the open tray—pausing above the spotless rag sheet as his weight eases slightly off his front foot.*

"Right—I let my right wrist dip before I ever checked the far mark on that one. This time I'll bow the sheet high in a tight U between both clips, touch the near-left and far-right registration ticks cleanly against the grid lines—" *he glances quickly up at the wall clock at 8:55 before looking back to Nadia at the edge of the apron* "—and hold the belly clear of the ink until both corners match; does the block look square enough on the crosshair from your angle before I pinch the deckle?"

**[Stage — Entrance Cue, 8:56 PM]:** *On Theo's visible preparation inside the press apron with the brass clips poised above [the last clean sheet](../02-company/piece-clean-sheet.md) still untouched in the tray, [Arthur](../02-company/persona-former-teacher.md) enters through the hallway door. At this beat, `Arthur -> Teacher -> Nadia` and `Nadia -> Student -> Arthur` activate alongside `Nadia -> Teacher -> Theo`.*

**Arthur:**
*Arthur steps unhurriedly through the hallway door in his street coat, hands settled in his pockets, and comes to a stop just inside the room beside the inspection blotter. Under the hum of the overhead tubes, his gaze drops to the damp proof—taking in the pinched upper-left margin and the thick black bead along the right edge—before he tilts one knuckle lightly in the air above that dropped edge without touching the sheet, letting the quiet hold a beat before glancing down the sightline toward the press.*

“I remember that mistake.”

**Nadia:**
*At Arthur's voice, Nadia's right thumb rubs rapidly twice against her clean apron seam; she meets his gaze straight down the sightline from the blotter, then steps directly into the narrow press apron beside Theo—crowding his eased stance at the bed—to sight down the etched grid from center and set her clean fingertips on the rim of the paper tray inches from his poised brass clips.*

"Arthur—we dropped the far edge early sighting only the near mark, and we're down to four minutes and the last sheet of rag in the shop. Your block sits dead square on the crosshair from center, Theo, so don't touch the bed. Give me half a step at the tray so I can set the clip spacing on the deckle with you first, then hold that U-bow high and sight the far-right tick before the near corner comes anywhere near the ink."

**Theo:**
*At the voice from the doorway, Theo's weight rocks back off his front foot and his right hand tilts one brass clip half an inch toward Nadia's waiting fingertips before he catches himself against the press frame, plants his boot back inside the narrow one-person apron rather than yielding the tray, and slides both clean clips onto opposite margins of the spotless rag sheet himself without lifting it yet.*

"I've got the clip bite set two fingers above the bottom deckle on both sides, Nadia—if I step out of the apron now I'll lose my sightline on that far-right tick." *He flicks a quick glance toward the man in the street coat by the blotter and up at the wall clock at 8:56 mid-breath before meeting Nadia's eyes across the tray.* "Check my spacing right here if it looks uneven, then let me have the bed space so I can bow it up and set both corners myself before nine."

**Arthur:**
*As the wall clock clicks to 8:57, Arthur takes a single unhurried pace past the blotter—two fingers of his right hand half-lifting from his street-coat pocket toward the iron press bed before curling back tight against his palm. He stops well short of the tray, raises one bent knuckle without coming near the press, and points strictly at the wedged gap where Nadia’s hip and fingertips crowd Theo’s elbow inside the single-person apron.*

“Three minutes is plenty of time to pull one clean sheet, Nadia—not enough for four elbows in a one-man apron.” *He lowers his knuckle half an inch toward her fingertips on the tray rim and lets a dry beat settle.* “You’ve got damp cotton waiting and fresh ink skinning on the block. Are you taking the clips off him and pulling this proof yourself, or are you giving your printer the apron to make his drop?”

**Nadia:**
*Nadia's fingertips linger half a beat on the tray rim under Arthur's pointed knuckle before she sights sharply across Theo's two brass clips, lifts her hand clear, and steps cleanly out of the single-person press apron—her right thumb rubbing fast against her clean apron seam until she presses her palm flat against her hip, standing in open view beside the bed with the sightline from the blotter left clear.*

"I'm giving my printer the apron, Arthur—and I'll stand on the pull he makes from it. Your clip bite is even on both margins, Theo, and the bed is yours with three minutes on the clock. Lift high in that U-bow, match the far-right tick to the grid before you bring the near corner down, and make your drop."

**Theo:**
*Theo's weight rocks briefly off his front foot at the two voices before he plants both boots squarely inside the cleared press apron, lifts the spotless rag sheet from the tray in a high, taut U-bow between the two brass clips, and sights past the near corner straight to the far-right registration tick on the etched grid.*

"Thank you—far-right tick on the grid first, keeping the belly clear—" *he flicks one mid-breath glance up at the wall clock nearing 8:58 and half-turns his chin toward Nadia as if to ask for a nod, then locks his jaw back onto the grid himself.* "—near-left matched, and coming down now." *Holding both corners steady on the ticks without waiting for a co-sign, he lowers the sheet cleanly onto the inked block, unclips the margins, folds the felt blankets smooth, cranks the iron bed steadily once beneath the drum and back, throws the blankets open, and catches the top margin with a clean brass clip to peel the sheet straight back off the block.*

**[Stage / Process — *The Last Pull* Initiated (8:58 PM)]:** *The moment Theo lifts [the last clean sheet](../02-company/piece-clean-sheet.md) clear of the tray lip in a taut U-bow between the two clean brass clips, [The Last Pull](../02-company/process-last-pull.md) initiates in a single-body apron.*

**[Stage / Process — *The Last Pull* Echo (8:58 PM)]:** *Turned face-up on the press bed under the fluorescent tubes with two minutes left before the 9:00 PM cutoff, [the last clean sheet](../02-company/piece-clean-sheet.md) bears a clean, upright black impression on spotless cotton rag—deckled bottom edge untouched by ink or thumbprints, top and bottom margins registered level to the sheet, and the right edge crisp with zero squeezed ink bead. Only two faint physical traces mark how the pull unfolded: the side margins sit a sixteenth of an inch narrower on the left than the right where Theo guarded away from the dropped right edge during his brief mid-hover pause over the grid, and the finest upper relief lines hold a slightly dry, velvet grain from the three minutes the re-inked block stood open while the apron was contested.*

**Arthur:**
*Arthur walks slowly down the clear lane beside the press bed, stopping an arm’s length outside the apron so Theo’s stance at the iron frame stays untouched. Two fingers lift halfway out of his right coat pocket toward the edge of the bed as his eyes track across the face-up rag, then curl back against his palm. Without touching sheet or iron, he raises one knuckle—tipping it first toward the inkless right edge and narrower left margin, then shifting a fraction toward the upper relief lines—and lets a beat of fluorescent hum sit in the room.*

“No bead on the right edge, left margin sitting a sixteenth tighter than the right, and a dry grain in those top lines from three minutes of open air while we talked over the apron.” *He lowers his knuckle, slips his hand back into his street coat, and looks across the bed at Nadia.* “Clock’s at 8:58, Nadia, and you’re the one standing on the pull. How are you reading the sheet for him before washup?”

**Nadia:**
*As the wall clock clicks to 8:59, Nadia steps only to the outer rail of the iron bed—staying clear of Theo's single-person apron across from Arthur—to sight the margins and upper relief lines under the fluorescent tubes; her right thumb gives one quick rub against her clean apron seam before she stills it, checks the clock once, and looks straight across the face-up rag from Theo to Arthur without reaching for the brass clips.*

"Top and bottom margins sit level on clean rag with zero bead on that right edge, Theo—you fixed the early drop. You pulled a sixteenth tight on the left margin guarding away from the right side mid-hover, which is yours to square up on the next roll. And that dry velvet grain in the fine upper lines is mine, Arthur—three minutes of ink skinning in open air because I crowded his apron instead of giving him the bed. We have one minute to lights-out, Theo: carry your proof to the clean side of the blotter with the clips, and tell me how you'll steady that hover while we get solvent on the slab."

**Theo:**
*Theo's weight eases briefly off his front foot inside the apron as Nadia finishes, then he clips both clean side margins with the brass clips, carries the damp cotton sheet down the lane to lay it flat on the clean half of the doorway inspection blotter right beside the crooked proof, and unclips the edges cleanly before turning back toward the inking slab.*

"I pulled it that sixteenth left when I broke my sightline mid-hover to look up at you instead of trusting my marks." *Stepping back toward the press bed to reach for a rag by the slab, he flicks a quick mid-sentence glance up at 8:59 on the wall clock before meeting Nadia's eyes.* "Next roll I keep my chin down on both grid ticks straight from the tray lip to the drop with zero pause in the air—hand me the citrus solvent and I'll clear the block while you take the slab before lights-out."

**[Stage — Curtain (8:59 PM)]**

## Director's Note

### 1. What Was Said, Who Handled *The Last Clean Sheet*, Process Resolution, and Consequence
- **What was said & negotiated:** At 8:55 PM, Nadia diagnosed the counterclockwise skew and right-edge ink bead on [piece-crooked-proof.md](../02-company/piece-crooked-proof.md) and asked Theo to wipe his hands and state his alignment plan. Theo wiped his palms, entered the press apron, picked up the brass clips, and asked Nadia to co-sign the block's centering just as Arthur walked in at 8:56 PM and delivered `"I remember that mistake."` Under Arthur's gaze, Nadia answered with `"we dropped the far edge early"` and stepped into the single-person press apron, asking Theo for `"half a step at the tray"` to set his clip spacing on the deckle herself. Theo nearly tilted a clip toward her (`Shadow`), caught himself, held his stance inside the apron, clipped the margins in the tray without lifting the sheet, and asked Nadia to check the bite where it lay and clear the bed. Arthur pointed at `"four elbows in a one-man apron"` while fresh ink skinned on the block and forced Nadia to name whether she was taking the clips herself or giving her printer the apron. Nadia cleared the apron and staked her standing on Theo's pull.
- **Who handled [piece-clean-sheet.md](../02-company/piece-clean-sheet.md):** **Theo alone** touched and lifted the sheet, handling it exclusively with wiped hands and the two clean brass clips (Nadia touched only the clips before setup and the tray rim during the standoff; Arthur never touched paper or press).
- **How [process-last-pull.md](../02-company/process-last-pull.md) resolved:** Initiated at 8:58 PM when Theo lifted the sheet clear of the tray lip in a high U-bow. Driven by wiped hands, even brass-clip placement, single-body space in the apron, sighting both far-right and near-left ticks, refusing to wait for a verbal co-sign, and a steady crank before 9:00 PM—tempered by two physical drags in `Lever`: Theo's brief mid-hover chin-turn toward Nadia and the three minutes the inked block stood open during the apron dispute. The resulting `Echo` was a clean, spotless, level impression with zero right-edge ink bead, bearing a 1/16-inch cautious shift narrower on the left margin and a slightly dry velvet grain in the fine upper relief lines.
- **Consequence:** Arthur read the physical marks on the pulled sheet and turned the shop critique back onto Nadia (`"How are you reading the sheet for him before washup?"`). Nadia cleanly separated Theo's error from her own—crediting Theo's corrected drop, assigning him the 1/16-inch hover shift for next session, and publicly claiming the dry upper-line grain as her own fault for crowding his apron for three minutes. Theo laid the new print beside the crooked proof on the doorway blotter, owned breaking his sightline mid-hover to look for Nadia's nod, and joined Nadia in the 8:59 PM washup.

### 2. Active Positions, Costs Borne, and Nadia's Dual-Position Collision
- **Moves made possible by active Positions:**
  - **Theo ([Student](../02-company/position-student.md) toward Nadia):** When his `Shadow` pulled him to hand a clip to Nadia at 8:56 PM, `Student.Has` (first claim on the next attempt) and `Student.Orders` (commit his own hands rather than letting someone else absorb the try) gave him standing to plant his boot in the apron, set the clips himself, and ask his Teacher for bed room—and again mid-lift to lock his jaw back onto the grid without waiting for Nadia's nod.
  - **Arthur ([Teacher](../02-company/position-teacher.md) toward Nadia):** `Teacher.Has` gave Arthur direct standing—not merely a visitor's nostalgia—to step past the blotter, halt the four-elbow stall at 8:57 PM, demand Nadia account for whether she was taking over or yielding the apron, and require Nadia at 8:58 PM to read the finished print aloud under his eye.
- **Costs borne by holders:**
  - **Arthur** bore `Teacher.Orders` and `Teacher.Loses`: twice his fingers half-lifted from his coat pocket toward the press bed (`Tell`), yet he had to curl them back and stay an arm's length outside the apron without touching sheet or iron.
  - **Theo** bore `Student.Loses`: giving up the safety of Nadia's co-sign so the 1/16-inch leftward guard from his mid-hover glance landed plainly as his own mark.
  - **Nadia** bore both `Student.Loses` (losing unwatched privacy around mistakes and any standing to wave off Arthur's scrutiny) and `Teacher.Loses` (giving up license to secure a clean sheet with her own hands and losing bystander distance from the dry-ink grain her crowding caused).
- **Where Nadia's two simultaneous Positions (`Teacher -> Theo` and `Student -> Arthur`) pressed on the same move:**
  1. **Turn 4 (8:56 PM at the tray rim):** The instant Arthur spoke `"I remember that mistake"`, `Student` toward Arthur stripped Nadia's ability to brush him off and drove her (`Student.Orders` / `Drives`, amplified by her `Shadow`) to step forward and visibly put her own hands and judgment on the line under Arthur's eye—while `Teacher` toward Theo barred her from outright taking the pull away from Theo (`Teacher.Loses`). Caught between both, she crowded halfway into Theo's single-person apron and reached for `"half a step at the tray"` to set the clip spacing *"with"* him—straining `Teacher.Orders` onstage until Theo and Arthur both pushed back.
  2. **Turn 7 & Turn 10 (8:57 PM clearing the apron & 8:59 PM reading the proof):** Forced to satisfy both Positions openly without dropping either, Nadia could only obey `Teacher.Orders` toward Theo (stepping out of the apron) by satisfying `Student.Orders` toward Arthur through **exposed judgment rather than hands**—declaring `"I'll stand on the pull he makes from it"` in Turn 7, and then in Turn 10 publicly owning the dry upper-line grain in front of both her former Teacher and her current Student (`"that dry velvet grain in the fine upper lines is mine, Arthur—three minutes of ink skinning in open air because I crowded his apron"`).
