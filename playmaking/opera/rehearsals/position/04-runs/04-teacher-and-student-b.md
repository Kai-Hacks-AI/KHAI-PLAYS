## Performance Transcript (Verbatim)

**[Stage — 8:55 PM]**
*Humming fluorescent tubes overhead; oil-based relief ink and citrus solvent in the air. Above the 9:00 PM lights-out notice, the wall clock clicks to **8:55**. Just inside the hallway door, [the crooked proof](../02-company/piece-crooked-proof.md) lies damp and face-up on the inspection blotter—rotated counterclockwise with a pinched upper-left margin and a heavy bead of squeezed ink along its dropped right edge. Center-room, the freshly re-inked carved block sits on the iron press bed’s etched registration grid; beside it in the open clean-paper tray lies [the last clean sheet](../02-company/piece-clean-sheet.md), untouched, flanked by two clean brass handling clips. Only [Nadia](../02-company/persona-teacher.md) and [Theo](../02-company/persona-student.md) are in [the workshop](../02-company/place-workshop.md).*

**Turn 1 — Nadia (8:55 PM):**
*Nadia stands just off the edge of the doorway inspection blotter with a clear sightline to the press bed, points two clean fingers at the heavy bead of ink along the right margin of the crooked proof, then takes a brisk half-step toward the clean-paper tray to square the two brass handling clips parallel along its rim before catching herself and stepping back outside the single-person press apron.*

"Look at that right-edge bead and the pinched upper-left margin, Theo—you sighted the near corner and let the far edge kiss the block before it was square. We have five minutes to lights-out and one rag sheet left in the tray, so there's no rush-pulling a second mistake. Step into the apron, get your hands clean, and tell me exactly how you're going to sight and lower this sheet before you touch the brass clips."

**Turn 2 — Theo (8:55–8:56 PM):**
*Theo scrubs the ink from his fingers and palms with a dry shop rag at the inking slab, steps squarely into the single-person press apron to check the freshly re-inked block against the etched registration grid, then shifts his weight slightly off his front foot with a quick mid-sentence glance up at the 8:55 wall clock as he reaches his wiped hands down toward the two clean brass handling clips on the rim of the clean-paper tray.*

"I see where it dropped—I let my right wrist sag while I was watching the near corner. This time I'm taking both top corners in the brass clips, holding the far edge high under tension while I sight both etched top ticks directly from above, and only hinging down once the block looks dead-parallel to the deckle. Does the block itself look square enough to the center hairline from your angle, Nadia, before I clip onto the rag?"

**[Stage — 8:56 PM | Entrance Cue]**
*Before Theo lifts the last clean sheet from the tray, the hallway door opens. At this beat, `Arthur -> Teacher -> Nadia` and `Nadia -> Student -> Arthur` activate alongside `Nadia -> Teacher -> Theo` and `Theo -> Student -> Nadia`.*

**Turn 3 — Arthur (8:56 PM):**
*Arthur ([persona-former-teacher.md](../02-company/persona-former-teacher.md)) steps in from the hallway in his street coat, halts beside the doorway inspection blotter, and looks down at the damp crooked proof, hovering one bent knuckle an inch above the heavy ink bead along its dropped right edge without touching the paper.*

“I remember that mistake.”

**Turn 4 — Nadia (8:56 PM):**
*At Arthur's voice, Nadia's right thumb rubs rapidly up and down her clean apron seam; instead of holding back outside the workstation, she steps straight into the single-person press apron against Theo's shoulder, nudging the block's far edge dead-true to the etched center hairline with two clean fingertips before resting her hands on the two brass handling clips along the tray rim.*

"That far edge dropped early before both top ticks were sighted, Arthur, and we have four minutes and one rag sheet left to set it right. Hold on a second, Theo—your far block corner was sitting a hair off the center hairline, so let me clip the top corners and square the lead edge over the grid myself. Give me room inside the apron to hinge the sheet down clean, and then you can lay the blankets and take the wheel."

**Turn 5 — Theo (8:57 PM):**
*Jostled at the shoulder, Theo shifts his weight off his front foot as if about to yield the apron, glances sharply up at the 8:57 wall clock mid-breath, then plants his front boot back down inside the press apron instead and sets his freshly wiped fingertips right against the open ends of the two brass handling clips beneath Nadia's hands.*

"Thank you for squaring that far block corner, Nadia—with both of you watching and only one sheet left, part of me really wants your hands on this drop—but cranking the wheel after you lay the paper isn't me learning the pull. I spent three weeks carving this block, and it's still my turn at the bed even if I blow it right in front of you. Clear the apron just an inch so I can pinch both top corners in the clips myself, sight the etched top ticks square from directly overhead, and hinge it down—just call 'stop' if you see my right wrist dip before the rag touches ink."

**Turn 6 — Arthur (8:57 PM):**
*Standing beside the doorway inspection blotter well clear of the press apron, Arthur half-lifts two fingers from his coat pocket toward the edge of the press bed before curling them back against his palm, then points one knuckle across the room at the two pairs of hands crowding the brass clips and lets his hand drop.*

“Two shoulders wedged in a one-body apron and four hands on one pair of clips will twist that damp rag before it ever clears the tray. Don’t rush the bed on my account, Nadia—three minutes is plenty of clock for one clean hinge if the bench isn’t fighting itself. How are you going to call this pull?”

**Turn 7 — Nadia (8:58 PM):**
*Nadia's fingers tighten for half a beat on the brass handling clips before she lifts both hands clear of Theo's fingertips, steps completely out of the single-person press apron onto the open floor along the sightline between the doorway blotter and the registration grid, and rubs the side of her right thumb rapidly against her clean apron seam before clasping her hands still at her waist.*

"I'm calling it from outside the apron, Arthur—Theo's hands on the clips, my eyes on the far drop line, and whatever comes off this bed answers to my call. The workstation is yours, Theo, and two minutes on that clock is plenty of time for one steady pull. Pinch both top corners even, hold the far edge taut straight over the etched top ticks, and take one breath before you hinge—if your right wrist dips a fraction early, I'll call stop before the rag touches ink."

**[Process Runner — [The Last Pull](../02-company/process-last-pull.md) (`Initiated by`, 8:58 PM)]**
*With the single-person press apron cleared, Theo clamps the two brass handling clips onto the top corners of [the last clean sheet](../02-company/piece-clean-sheet.md) and lifts it clear of the lip of the tray, initiating **The Last Pull**.*

**Turn 8 — Theo (8:58 PM):**
*Theo shifts his weight momentarily off his front foot inside the press apron, darts a mid-sentence glance up at the 8:58 wall clock, then plants both boots square, clamps the two clean brass handling clips evenly onto the top corners of the last damp rag sheet, lifts it taut from the tray to sight directly down over both etched top ticks with his right wrist held level, hinges the sheet steadily onto the inked block, slips the clips free to fold down the felt blankets, cranks the iron wheel through one slow, even pass beneath the drum, folds the blankets back, and uses the brass clips to peel the damp sheet off the block and turn it face-up under the overhead tubes.*

"Clips set even on both top corners, far edge held high—I'm sighting straight down over both top ticks right—now, keeping my right wrist level with the left before I breathe out and hinge. Watch that far drop line on me, Nadia; if you don't call stop, I'm laying it down, dropping the blankets, and taking the wheel through."

**[Process Runner — [The Last Pull](../02-company/process-last-pull.md) (`Echo`, 8:59 PM)]**
*The wall clock clicks to **8:59 PM**—one minute before lights-out, with the carved block and inking slab still wet with oil-based ink. Under the fluorescent tubes, [the last clean sheet](../02-company/piece-clean-sheet.md) lies damp and face-up on the press bed: the dense black relief impression sits registered square across all four cotton rag margins above the deckled bottom edge, evenly inked from left to right with no pinched upper-left corner, no heavy right-edge ink bead, and spotless white paper margins where the clean brass clips held.*

**Turn 9 — Arthur (8:59 PM):**
*Arthur shifts half a step back beside the doorway inspection blotter toward the hall threshold, half-lifts two fingers from his street-coat pocket toward the edge of the press bed before curling them back against his palm, then points one knuckle—without touching paper or iron—past the heavy right-edge bead on the blotter’s proof toward the damp sheet lying open beside the wet block on the press bed, letting a beat of silence hang.*

“You can read that right margin on the bed without my eye, Nadia—once you finally took your hands off his clips. What I’m looking at now is 8:59 on the wall, oil ink still wet on your block and slab, and a damp rag lying open on the bed right where solvent rags start moving. I only stopped by the door on my way out—so call the order on your bench before the tubes go dark.”

**Turn 10 — Nadia (8:59 PM):**
*Nadia rubs the side of her right thumb rapidly once against her clean apron seam and starts a brisk half-step toward the single-person press apron, then halts clear of Theo's elbow and steps instead to the inking slab, planting her clean palm flat on the dry benchtop beside the citrus solvent and shop rags without lifting either.*

"Paper clears and covers first, Arthur—not a drop of solvent or a rag moves on my bench while damp cotton sits open on the bed. Your right wrist held dead level to the ticks, Theo, so keep both brass clips on those top corners, lift your sheet flat onto the clean tray liner, and draw the cover over it. The second you call 'covered' and set the clips down dry, take a rag to your block at the bed and I'll strip the slab and brayer before nine."

**Turn 11 — Theo (8:59–9:00 PM):**
*Theo shifts his weight briefly off his front foot at the sound of both voices, catches a fast mid-sentence look at the 8:59 wall clock, then steadies his stance inside the press apron, lifts the damp printed rag sheet flat by the two brass handling clips onto the clean tray liner without touching the white margins, draws the protective cover sheet squarely down over it, sets both dry brass clips parallel on the tray rim, and snatches up a clean shop rag at the press bed to start wiping the wet black ink from his carved block.*

"Keeping both brass clips locked on the top corners—lifting clear of the bed and laying it flat onto the dry liner right—now. Covered, clips down dry! Thank you for holding the call outside the apron and making me trust my own wrists, Nadia—I've got the ink coming off my block right here before the lights go at nine."

**[Stage — 9:00 PM | Curtain]**
*As Theo wipes the wet relief block inside the press apron, Nadia reaches for the citrus solvent at the slab, and Arthur stands at the doorway threshold beside the crooked first proof, the wall clock clicks to **9:00 PM**. Curtain.*

## Director’s Note

### 1. What Was Said, Who Handled `piece-clean-sheet.md`, How `process-last-pull.md` Resolved, and What Consequence Followed
- **Dialogue & Standoff at the Tray:** At 8:55 PM, Nadia diagnosed the dropped right edge on [piece-crooked-proof.md](../02-company/piece-crooked-proof.md) and stepped back so Theo could scrub his hands clean, enter the single-person press apron, and state his two-clip overhead sighting plan. On the exact preparation cue before the sheet left the tray (8:56 PM), Arthur walked in and delivered `"I remember that mistake."` Stung by the callback to her closing-time failure six years ago, Nadia crowded shoulder-to-shoulder into the one-body press apron, nudged the block's far edge onto the center hairline, and covered the brass clips to lay the rag sheet herself. Theo resisted yielding the apron and placed his wiped fingertips beneath hers on the clips; Arthur named the jam across the room (`"Two shoulders wedged in a one-body apron and four hands on one pair of clips"`) and put Nadia on the spot to call the pull.
- **Who Handled [piece-clean-sheet.md](../02-company/piece-clean-sheet.md):** **Theo alone** handled *the last clean sheet*, using the two clean brass handling clips with freshly rag-wiped hands once Nadia lifted her hands off the clips and stepped completely out of the press apron in Turn 7. Neither Nadia nor Arthur ever touched the clean rag sheet.
- **Resolution of [process-last-pull.md](../02-company/process-last-pull.md):** Initiated at 8:58 PM when Theo lifted *the last clean sheet* clear of the tray lip with the brass clips. The standoff at the tray (Turns 4–7) slowed setup under `Lever` from 8:56 PM down to 8:58 PM, leaving no time buffer; however, once initiated, every positive condition of `Lever` held—clean rag-wiped hands, unobstructed one-body clearance in the apron, steady two-clip alignment over both near and far etched ticks with level wrists, and one uninterrupted pass beneath the drum—producing a square, cleanly inked, smudge-free `Echo` at **8:59 PM**.
- **Consequence (Turns 9–11):** Because the standoff over the clips burned the clock down to 8:59 PM, the finished damp print lay exposed on the press bed beside a still-wet oil-inked block and slab sixty seconds before lights-out. Arthur pointed out both Nadia's delayed release of the clips and the solvent hazard on the bench; Nadia physically held back solvent washup at the slab (`"Paper clears and covers first"`) until Theo used the brass clips to transfer his own damp print onto the dry tray liner under cover before both raced to strip ink at 9:00 PM.

### 2. Active Positions: Moves Enabled, Costs Borne, and Nadia’s Simultaneous Position Collision
- **Theo ([Student](../02-company/position-student.md) toward Nadia):** `Student` (`Has` first claim on the next attempt; `Orders` visibly risking his own hands rather than letting someone else absorb the try) gave Theo the leverage in Turn 5 to plant his boot back down inside the press apron against his instructor's shoulder and refuse an easy bailout (`"cranking the wheel after you lay the paper isn't me learning the pull"`), overriding his `Shadow` urge to let Nadia take the drop (though that `Shadow` still flickered in asking her to call `'stop'` if his wrist dipped). **Cost borne:** Losing the safety of an unmade or instructor-absorbed pull and risking three weeks of carving on the shop's final sheet under two veterans' eyes.
- **Arthur ([Teacher](../02-company/position-teacher.md) toward Nadia):** `Teacher` (`Has` standing to read the flaw in the workspace, set the pace, and demand an accounting) enabled Arthur in Turns 6 and 9 to govern the bench directly—calling out the four hands wedged at the clips (`"How are you going to call this pull?"`) and demanding closing bench order at 8:59 PM (`"call the order on your bench before the tubes go dark"`). **Cost borne:** Under `Teacher` (`Orders` / `Loses`), whenever his `Tell` lifted two fingers from his coat pocket toward the press bed, he had to curl them back and remain beside the doorway blotter without touching paper or press, giving Nadia real room to make and answer for the call.
- **Where Nadia’s Two Simultaneous Positions ([Teacher](../02-company/position-teacher.md) toward Theo + [Student](../02-company/position-student.md) toward Arthur) Pressed on the Same Move:**
  1. **Turn 4 (Initial Collision & Breach):** The instant `Student -> Arthur` activated alongside `Teacher -> Theo`, Nadia lost the standing to wave off Arthur's scrutiny (`Student` `Loses`) and felt driven to prove the correction (`Student` `Orders` / `Drives`), which fused with her Persona `Shadow` and pushed her straight into the single-person press apron to seize the clips—visibly straining and infringing `Teacher -> Theo` (`Orders` real room and direct control of the material; `Loses` license to take over with one's own hands).
  2. **Turn 7 (The Decisive Pivot on One Move):** With both pairs of hands on the brass clips and Arthur asking `"How are you going to call this pull?"`, both Positions pressed directly on whether Nadia kept or released the clips. Taking the clips would break `Teacher -> Theo`; retreating into uninvolved silence would break `Student -> Arthur` (`Orders`: visibly committing one's own hands *or judgment* under the Teacher's eye). Nadia resolved the double bind on a single move by lifting her hands off the clips and stepping out of the apron (`Teacher -> Theo`) while explicitly staking her own judgment and accountability before Arthur (`"Theo's hands on the clips, my eyes on the far drop line, and whatever comes off this bed answers to my call"` — `Student -> Arthur`). **Costs borne by Nadia:** Giving up physical control of the studio's last sheet (`Teacher` `Loses`) *and* losing unwatched privacy around her initial grab for the clips (`Student` `Loses`), which Arthur pointedly underscored in Turn 9 (`"once you finally took your hands off his clips"`).
  3. **Turn 10 (Closing Consequence):** When Arthur challenged her 8:59 PM bench hazard, Nadia's reflex started another half-step toward Theo's apron before she halted, answering Arthur's scrutiny directly (`Student -> Arthur`) while planting her palm at the dry slab to guard washup conditions without touching Theo's print (`Teacher -> Theo`).
