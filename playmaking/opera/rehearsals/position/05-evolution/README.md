# Evolution: TO HOLD Position

Smallest proposed edits to [references/position.md](../../../skills/khai-playwright/references/position.md) and adjacent authoring/staging guidance, each tied to concrete evidence from [02-company/authoring-note.md](../02-company/authoring-note.md) and [04-runs/](../04-runs/).

## 1. Keep the Six `TO HOLD` Chapters (`Taxonomy`, `Owner`, `Has`, `Orders`, `Loses`, `Drives`)

Across [01-teacher-only-a](../04-runs/01-teacher-only-a.md)–[04-teacher-and-student-b](../04-runs/04-teacher-and-student-b.md), each operational `TO HOLD` chapter carried distinct onstage weight while remaining reusable across Personas:
- **`Has`** gave Theo standing to hold the single-body apron against Nadia's takeover (`Turn 5` in all four runs) and gave Arthur standing in **Teacher and Student** to demand an explicit call and bench reading from Nadia ([03](../04-runs/03-teacher-and-student-a.md)–[04](../04-runs/04-teacher-and-student-b.md) `Turns 6, 9`) that he did not claim as a visitor ([01](../04-runs/01-teacher-only-a.md)–[02](../04-runs/02-teacher-only-b.md)).
- **`Orders`** required Nadia to clear the apron (`Turn 7` in all four runs), required Arthur to keep his hands off the press despite his `Tell` ([03](../04-runs/03-teacher-and-student-a.md)–[04](../04-runs/04-teacher-and-student-b.md)), and required Nadia in [03](../04-runs/03-teacher-and-student-a.md)–[04](../04-runs/04-teacher-and-student-b.md) to stake her judgment visibly under Arthur's eye.
- **`Loses`** separated visitor discomfort from Position obligation: Nadia brushed Arthur off at the door in both **Teacher only** runs ([01](../04-runs/01-teacher-only-a.md)–[02](../04-runs/02-teacher-only-b.md) `Turn 4`), whereas holding [Student](../02-company/position-student.md) (`Loses`: forfeiting the standing to wave off scrutiny and unwatched privacy around mistakes) made her account directly to Arthur on `Turn 4` and own her three-minute crowding aloud on `Turn 10` ([03](../04-runs/03-teacher-and-student-a.md)).
- **`Drives`** oriented each holder's push across turns without scripting dialogue.

## 2. Changes to `references/position.md`

- **`Taxonomy`:** Name the immediate group or paired Position this belongs to and link it if it has a file. Both [Teacher](../02-company/position-teacher.md#L5) and [Student](../02-company/position-student.md#L5) act toward a counterpart Position while staying free of Persona names so Nadia and Arthur could both hold `Teacher` and Theo and Nadia could both hold `Student`.
- **`Orders` (authoring note on scope):** State what holding the Position requires the Persona to do. In [Student](../02-company/position-student.md#L17), writing *"committing one's own hands or judgment"* let Nadia reconcile simultaneous `Teacher` and `Student` Positions on `Turn 7` by yielding the clips to Theo while staking her call before Arthur ([03](../04-runs/03-teacher-and-student-a.md), [04](../04-runs/04-teacher-and-student-b.md)); when an author wants two active Positions to force an irreconcilable breach rather than a split between hands and oversight, `Orders` should name the same contested physical act.

## 3. Adjacent Cross-Class Seams (`Play`, `Promptbook`, `Persona`, `Director`)

1. **Bind `(holder -> Position -> counterpart)` and activation timing in `Play` / `Promptbook`, not in `Persona` or `Position`:**
   - *Evidence:* Noted in [authoring-note.md](../02-company/authoring-note.md#L22-L24): keeping Position files free of Persona names and keeping `Persona -> Taxonomy` free of un-occurred arrival cues allowed all eleven Company files to stay byte-for-byte fixed across **Teacher only** and **Teacher and Student** while activating `Arthur -> Teacher -> Nadia` and `Nadia -> Student -> Arthur` cleanly on the `8:56 PM` entrance cue ([play.md](../02-company/play.md#L18-L24), [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md#L27-L29)).
2. **Keep temperament and shared past (`Persona — TO PAST`) separate from normative standing and duty (`Position — TO HOLD`):**
   - *Evidence:* Because Nadia's crowding reflex and six-year-old history lived in [persona-teacher.md](../02-company/persona-teacher.md) while owed room, risked attempt, and forfeited wave-off lived in [position-teacher.md](../02-company/position-teacher.md) and [position-student.md](../02-company/position-student.md), **Teacher only** still produced Nadia's `Turn 4` flinch and apron intrusion without collapsing into active `Student` accountability.
3. **Update `khai-director/SKILL.md` to name all fixed Company classes:**
   - *Alignment note:* [khai-director/SKILL.md](../../../skills/khai-director/SKILL.md) currently says *"Carry forward what the Personas establish, including what they leave unresolved"*; extending that framing to the full Company (`Persona`, `Piece`, `Place`, `Process`, and active `Position` bindings) matches what Directors now hold fixed onstage.
