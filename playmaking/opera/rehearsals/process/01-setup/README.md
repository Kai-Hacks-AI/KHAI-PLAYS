# Rehearsal Setup: TO IDLE in Der Zauberlehrling

Use [Goethe's original German ballad](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Der_Zauberlehrling_(1798)) as the source for a short Play. The apprentice enchants a broom to fetch water. Repeated trips raise the water until the apprentice must act.

## Create

Use the [Playwright skill](../../../skills/khai-playwright/SKILL.md) to author the rehearsal material. Use every class currently supported:

| Class | What to author |
| --- | --- |
| Play and Promptbook | An opening just before the spell takes effect, with brief conditions for how cues and changes become known. |
| Persona | The apprentice and the master, each with their own pressure. |
| Piece | The broom and the bucket. |
| Place | The room where the water accumulates. |
| Process | The broom's repeated fetching and the changing state it leaves behind, written with TO IDLE. |

Keep the source's pressure. Give the apprentice room to intervene. If the master enters during the rehearsal, agree on the cue before staging and keep it fixed across runs.

## Rehearse

Use the [Director skill](../../../skills/khai-director/SKILL.md). Stage the same opening in fresh contexts with the same Personas, Pieces, Place, and Promptbook. Give performers their own material and relay changes as they become observable. The Human sets the opening once.

1. Stage the authored Process twice. Play through the first meaningful intervention and show what the next move inherits.
2. Change one sentence under **Initiated by** and run twice more. Give the variant a different observable threshold while keeping **Direction**, **Lever**, and **Echo** fixed. The shared opening must make both thresholds reachable.
3. Compare when repeated fetching starts, what advances it, what an intervention changes, and what physical state remains. Record any timing, stop condition, or consequence the Director supplied because the text left it open.

Use SCORE to save the exact tested commit, the authored material and variant, the runs, observations, and any proposed template edit. Judge the Process by whether its threshold and consequences stay legible while the apprentice's choices vary.
