# Company: *The Sorcerer's Apprentice* (*Der Zauberlehrling*)

Authored from [Goethe's 1798 ballad](https://de.wikisource.org/wiki/Der_Zauberlehrling_(1798)) using every currently supported OPERA class.

## Authored Material

| Class | File | Role in the Play |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Play** | [play.md](play.md) | Shared opening in the dry Hall before any spell takes effect, physical rules for restraint vs. cleaving, fixed observable entry cue for the Master, and ending condition. |
| **Promptbook** | [promptbook.md](promptbook.md) | `HACKS` instructions governing audience Human brief, isolated Apprentice/Master/Stage roles, cue-based Process start and Master entry, observable `Servants` / `Pieces and tools` / `Water mark` ledger, and guardrails against invented counter-spells. |
| **Persona (`TO PAST`)** | [persona-apprentice.md](persona-apprentice.md) | *The Apprentice* — proud of memorized gestures and words, unwilling to look like a water-hauling drudge or call early for help. |
| **Persona (`TO PAST`)** | [persona-master.md](persona-master.md) | *The Old Master* — unhurried head of the house who performs rites in view while keeping their dismissing words to himself. |
| **Piece (`TO PLAY`)** | [piece-broom.md](piece-broom.md) | *The Broom* — birch-twig broom draped in damp rags beside the basin; retains the whole compulsion in each half if split. |
| **Piece (`TO PLAY`)** | [piece-bucket.md](piece-bucket.md) | *The Water Bucket* (*Wassertopf*) — heavy stave-bound pot resting empty between the Broom and the dry bath basin. |
| **Place (`TO SHOW`)** | [place-hall.md](place-hall.md) | *The Hall* — inward-sloped flagstones above the river steps holding the bath basin (*Becken*), loose stone bowls (*Schaalen*), unlatched timber doors, and wood-splitting axe (*Beil*), with no drain or grimoire. |
| **Process (`TO IDLE`)** | [process-water-fetching.md](process-water-fetching.md) | *Water-Fetching* — repeated river-to-basin trips driving the Hall from `Stage 1` (*Basin swelling*) through `Stage 4` (*Steps flooded*). |

## `Initiated by` Variant

- [process-water-fetching-variant.md](process-water-fetching-variant.md) — replaces only the single sentence under `## Initiated by` so repeated fetching starts when, having spoken the summoning formula in the Hall, the Apprentice lays a hand on [piece-broom.md](piece-broom.md) or [piece-bucket.md](piece-bucket.md) to make it move (`Taxonomy`, `Owner`, `Direction`, `Lever`, and `Echo` unchanged).
