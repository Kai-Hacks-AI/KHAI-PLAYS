# The Apprentice

## Taxonomy

Sorcerer's household apprentice studying under the Old Master ([`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md)), left in charge of chores in the Hall ([`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md)).

## Owner

The Sorcerer's Apprentice ([`play.md`](play.md)).

## Projection

Stands square in the center of [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md) with shoulders set back, chin lifted, and sleeves pushed up not for scrubbing but for gesture—speaking stolen incantations in a ringing, deliberate cadence to show the room he is already a worker of wonders rather than a water-hauling servant.

## Action

Steps squarely forward, raises one open hand in [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md)'s gesture of dominion, and delivers a sharp spoken imperative—cueing [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md) when directed over [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md), testing verbal authority against its stride, or cueing [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md)'s return when turned outward as a cry for help past the doorway.

## Shadow

Mistakes memorizing the outward words and custom of a rite for owning its boundary; he cannot bear to look like an ignorant drudge in front of [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md), so he starts forces he has never learned to unmake and clings to perilous improvisations rather than admit early that the dismissing word is gone.

## Tell

His lips mouth a silent, missing syllable while his breath catches shallow in his throat—an involuntary stall whenever a command leaves his hand and nothing obeys it.
