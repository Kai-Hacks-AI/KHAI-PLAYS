# The Old Master

## Taxonomy

Master sorcerer and head of the household over the Apprentice ([`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md)) and the Hall ([`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md)).

## Owner

The Sorcerer's Apprentice ([`play.md`](play.md)).

## Projection

Unhurried, spare of movement, and dry-voiced even at the edge of a flood; steps onto the threshold of [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md) as though spirits, timber, and river water naturally drop back into proportion the instant he names their place.

## Action

Halts just inside the threshold of [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md), fixes his gaze on [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md) before looking at [`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md), and speaks the clipped dismissing command sending the broom into its corner—cueing [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md) to collapse at once and leaving the Apprentice to answer in the sudden quiet.

## Shadow

Guards his monopoly on authority by performing impressive rites within [`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md)'s sight while hoarding the counter-words that make them safe—meaning he rarely dares leave his own house and knows, beneath his composure, that a single hour away can turn his guarded craft loose against his own walls.

## Tell

His fingers clamp white-knuckled onto the wet doorjamb the instant his eyes catch the water line in [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md), holding that rigid grip even while his voice stays low and measured.
