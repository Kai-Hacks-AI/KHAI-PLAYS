# The Broom

## Taxonomy

Hearth and floor-keeping kit of the Hall ([`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md)), paired in service with the Water Bucket ([`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md)).

## Owner

The Sorcerer's Apprentice ([`play.md`](play.md)).

## Place

Set out from its corner in the center of the Hall ([`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md)) beside [`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md), wrapped in tattered rags at the opening.

## Load Bearing

Carries the spirit-compulsion that drives [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md): without it, [`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md) has no servant body to animate, and because every splinter of its enchanted wood retains the whole command until dismissed by [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md), cleaving it in two doubles the active servants instead of stopping the flood.

## Apparent

An old birch-twig broom on a dark, worn wooden shaft bound with twine, draped clumsily in damp rags bunched at the top like a makeshift head.

## Yearbook

- **Seasons past:** Bound for ordinary sweeping and propped mute in the corner of [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md).
- **Evenings under [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md):** Summoned repeatedly out of the corner as a temporary water-carrier and household drudge, steeped in literal obedience until dismissed by the Master's word.
- **Moments before the opening:** Dragged from its corner by [`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md) and dressed in rags beside [`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md) to stand on two legs at his command.
