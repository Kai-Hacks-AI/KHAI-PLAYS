# The Water Bucket

## Taxonomy

Bath and water-hauling vessels of the Hall ([`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md)), worked alongside the Broom ([`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md)).

## Owner

The Sorcerer's Apprentice ([`play.md`](play.md)).

## Place

Resting empty on the flagstones of the Hall ([`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md)) between [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md) and the dry bath basin at the opening.

## Load Bearing

Turns the river outside into measurable rising water inside [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md) on each cycle of [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md): heavy enough when full to make [`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md) shirk manual hauling, large enough to swell the bath basin in two pours, yet backed by the Hall's loose bowls if wrested away or if a second servant rises.

## Apparent

A wide-bellied earthen and stave-bound water pot (*Wassertopf*) with a damp rim, river-silted base, and a thick handle worn smooth by wet hands.

## Yearbook

- **Weeks prior:** Carried up the river steps trip after trip by [`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md), leaving aching arms that soured him on manual chores.
- **Upon [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md)'s departure:** Left empty beside the dry bath basin in [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md) as the day's unfinished task.
- **Just before curtain:** Positioned at the foot of [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md) so the summoned servant can seize it on the first stride.
