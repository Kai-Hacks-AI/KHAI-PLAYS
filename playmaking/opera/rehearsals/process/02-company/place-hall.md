# The Hall

## Taxonomy

Ground-floor work and bath chamber of the Old Master's house, opening directly onto the stone steps down to the riverbank.

## Owner

The Sorcerer's Apprentice ([`play.md`](play.md)).

## Shown

Cool river draft through an open outer doorway, dry flagstones sloped slightly toward the inner room thresholds, a deep empty bath basin (*Becken*) at the center, stone benches lined with dry bowls (*Schaalen*), an empty broom-corner, and no floor drain wide enough to clear fast water.

## Holds

- The rag-draped Broom ([`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md)) and empty Water Bucket ([`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md)) beside the central bath basin.
- Several loose stone water bowls (*Schaalen*) along the benches.
- A sharp wood-splitting axe (*Beil*) resting by the chopping block near the wall.
- Heavy timber doors at the outer river-step threshold and the inner room thresholds, latching only when held or wedged against pressure.

## Offers

- **To [`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md):** Privacy from [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md) at the opening; loose bowls to catch brief overflow; doorways narrow enough to brace with body weight; the sharp axe within reach; and an open outer threshold that carries a shout straight toward the street and river path.
- **To [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md):** A short, unobstructed sprint down the steps to the river during [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md), plus spare bowls to haul with if [`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md) is withheld or a second servant rises.
- **To [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md):** An immediate sightline from the entry threshold across the steps, floor, basin, and broom-corner.

## Withheld

- Any sluice, drain, or written grimoire holding the dismissing word: nothing inside the room can secretly swallow the water or supply the forgotten formula.
- Concealment once the bath basin overflows: to shed water out of the room or obtain [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md)'s dismissal, [`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md) must either send water across the outer threshold onto the public steps or cry aloud past the doorway—giving up any chance of hiding what he summoned.
