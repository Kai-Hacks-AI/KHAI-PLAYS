# The Sorcerer's Apprentice

*A short Play after Johann Wolfgang von Goethe's 1798 ballad* Der Zauberlehrling.  
*Staged with [`promptbook.md`](promptbook.md).*

## Cast and Material

- **Personas:** The Apprentice ([`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md)), The Old Master ([`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md))
- **Pieces:** The Broom ([`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md)), The Water Bucket ([`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md))
- **Place:** The Hall ([`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md))
- **Process:** Water-Fetching ([`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md))

## Situation

The Old Master ([`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md)) has stepped away from the house for once, leaving the Apprentice ([`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md)) alone in the Hall ([`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md)) with a mundane chore: fill the central bath basin from the river below the steps. Having watched the Master's rites and memorized his summoning words and custom, the Apprentice means to make the household spirits serve his will instead of hauling water by hand.

## What Is Fixed at the Opening

1. **The threshold moment:** The Play opens in the dry Hall ([`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md)) just before the Apprentice ([`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md)) makes his opening move, before any spell has taken effect. The Master ([`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md)) is offstage beyond the street and river path. The Apprentice has pulled the old Broom ([`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md)) from its corner, draped it in wretched rags, and set the empty Water Bucket ([`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md)) beside the dry bath basin, standing ready to put the Master's summoning rite to work. Not a drop has entered the basin yet, and the broom lies still.
2. **The Process and the missing word:** Water-Fetching ([`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md)) begins if and when the condition under its `Initiated by` heading is met in play. The Apprentice remembers the Master's summoning words and rite **but has forgotten the Master's dismissing word** that restores an animated broom to lifeless wood. Once [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md) is running, no guessed phrase, plea, rhyme, or command spoken by the Apprentice can end it.
3. **What physical force does:**
   - Once active, the Broom ([`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md)) moves with uncanny speed and fierce, single-minded drive from river to basin.
   - Bodily grappling, snatching [`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md), catching overflow in the Hall's loose bowls, or bracing the doorway can check or blunt at most a single trip while the Apprentice spends full effort maintaining it; if deprived of the bucket while active, the servant seizes a stone bowl from [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md) and keeps hauling.
   - Striking or cleaving an active [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md) in two with the sharp axe in [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md) splits the shaft cleanly for one brief breath—then **both halves rise whole as servants**, doubling every subsequent pour of [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md).
4. **Fixed observable cue for the Master's entry:** The Old Master ([`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md)) remains offstage until **whichever of these two observable events happens first**:
   - The Apprentice calls aloud for the Master loudly enough to carry past the doorway of [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md), **or**
   - Water in [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md) (whether by rising flood from [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md) or deliberate bailing) crosses the Hall's outer threshold and runs down the entry steps.
   On the beat immediately following that cue, the Master arrives at the doorway. Only the Master holds the dismissing command (*"Into the corner, broom! Be as you were!"*) that returns one or more broom-servants to dead wood.

## How Cues and Changes Become Known

- Only spoken words, audible sounds (rushing water, timber impacts, axe blows, cries for help), and visible physical changes in [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md) are known in the scene.
- Whether [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md) remains idle or takes hold becomes known through the visible stillness or sudden movement of [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md) and [`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md).
- The Apprentice discovers the dismissing word is gone only upon first trying to stop or recall an active [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md).
- Every beat leaves an explicit, observable mark in [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md): the count and position of active broom-servants, the state of [`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md) and any room tools used, and the water's current height (dry flagstones, bath basin swelling, bowls brimming to full measure, streams crossing the inner thresholds, or floodwater cascading over the outer threshold onto the steps).
- When [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md) enters, he knows only what drew him to the steps and what lies visibly before him in the room (water level, displaced vessels, split or whole wood, and the Apprentice's bearing).

## What the Performers Decide

- **The Apprentice ([`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md))** opens the scene before the rag-draped [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md), empty [`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md), and dry basin, deciding how to set about filling the bath by spirit-craft and—once [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md) is underway and refuses to stop—how long to risk flooding the house to salvage pride and which means to try: imperative commands, wrestling [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md) or [`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md), using bowls or doors in [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md), resorting to the sharp axe, or swallowing pride to cry out for [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md) before water breaches the outer steps. No sequence of interventions is pre-scripted.
- **The Old Master ([`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md))** decides, upon entering on cue and dismissing any active [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md) to its corner, how he confronts the Apprentice and marks the boundary of who calls spirits in his house, shaped by whether the Apprentice called for help early or let water reach the steps—and whether whole or split wood lies in the corner.

## Ending

The Play concludes once [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md) has run and been brought to a halt by [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md), after the Master's reckoning and the Apprentice's final response have landed in [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md) (or earlier only if the Apprentice abandons the rite and completes or forfeits the chore without ever triggering [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md)).
