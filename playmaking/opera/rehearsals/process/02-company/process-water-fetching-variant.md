# Water-Fetching

## Taxonomy

Bound spirit-service within [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md)'s household craft.

## Owner

The Sorcerer's Apprentice ([`play.md`](play.md)).

## Initiated by

Having spoken the Master's summoning formula in the Hall ([`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md)), the Apprentice ([`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md)) lays a hand on the rag-wrapped Broom ([`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md)) or the Water Bucket ([`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md)) to make it move.

## Direction

Runs in relentless round trips from the riverbank up the steps into [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md), driving the room through four observable water stages:

1. **Stage 1 — Basin swelling:** [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md) bolts to the river and returns at lightning speed, pouring [`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md) into the bath basin until water swells high up its sides.
2. **Stage 2 — Full measure:** A second rapid pour brims the bath basin and fills the surrounding bowls (*Schaalen*) to the lip, completing the chore while the broom pivots straight back toward the river.
3. **Stage 3 — Thresholds awash:** Fresh pours surge over the basin rim, sheet across the flagstones, and stream over the inner thresholds of [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md).
4. **Stage 4 — Steps flooded:** Water rises ankle-deep through [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md) and spills out across the outer doorway down the entry steps (*auf den Stufen*), cueing [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md)'s arrival if he has not already been called.

*(If [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md) is cleaved in two, both halves rise whole as active servants and haul in tandem, advancing two stages' worth of water on each cycle.)*

## Lever

- **Driven by:** The literal, tireless compulsion of the summoning rite bound into [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md), the short open run down the steps to the river, the capacity of [`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md) (or spare bowls from [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md)), and the doubling of servants if the wooden shaft is split.
- **Resisted by:** Bodily grappling, wresting [`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md) away, catching water in empty bowls, or bracing the outer door—each able to delay or blunt at most one trip while [`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md) commits full strength against the broom's fierce push—until [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md)'s spoken dismissing word severs the compulsion completely.

## Echo

[`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md) stands soaked to the highest mark reached—basin and bowls brimming, flagstones and thresholds wet or flooded, outer steps slick if Stage 4 was hit—while [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md) lies inert in its corner (whole, or in two split shafts if the axe fell), [`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md) rests where the last pour stopped, and [`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md)'s unauthorized summoning is laid bare before [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md).
