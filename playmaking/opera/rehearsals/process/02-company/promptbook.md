# Promptbook — The Sorcerer's Apprentice

*Performance instructions for [`play.md`](play.md).*

## Human

Before the opening beat, the human chooses either to inhabit [`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md) directly or to watch as audience while agents perform all roles. If watching, the human sets the curtain in motion without intervening mid-run, unless calling `Cut` to inspect or restart the staging. After the Play ends, the human decides whether to run [`play.md`](play.md) again to test different interventions or Process conditions.

## Agent

- **Apprentice agent** (when not played by the human): Inhabits [`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md) in the first person, speaking and acting in real time from what is perceptible in [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md). Takes the opening beat beside the rag-draped [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md), empty [`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md), and dry bath basin from his urge to command the Master's spirits rather than haul water by hand, then responds freely to whatever follows without following a predetermined checklist from the source poem.
- **Master agent**: Inhabits [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md). Stays silent and offstage until the fixed entry cue in [`play.md`](play.md) is met, then steps onto the threshold, assesses the visible state of [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md), [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md), [`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md), and [`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md), and acts from his own pressure.
- **Stage voice** (held by the Director or a dedicated world agent): Tracks [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md), [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md), [`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md), and [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md). Evaluates on each beat whether an idle [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md) has met its `Initiated by` condition, and reports only observable movement, stillness, sound, physical resistance, and water level. Enchanted objects do not speak; [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md) communicates only through stillness or motion, posture, speed, fierce aspect, and physical force.

## Collaboration

- **By a cue:**
  - [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md) stays idle until the exact condition under its own `Initiated by` heading is met in play; meeting that condition cues the Stage voice to start [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md).
  - Each action beat by [`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md) cues the Stage voice to report the room's physical response—keeping [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md) idle while its `Initiated by` condition remains unmet, or resolving physical contact and advancing the water state by one beat once it is active.
  - [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md) waits strictly on cue—entering on the beat immediately after either the Apprentice calls aloud past the doorway for the Master or water crosses the outer threshold of [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md) onto the entry steps.
- **By the scene:**
  - [`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md) takes the opening move in [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md) and, while [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md) remains absent, responds next after every Stage report as the Persona bearing the immediate pressure in the room.
  - Once [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md) enters, handoff proceeds by the scene between Master, Stage voice (including when [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md) drops inert upon the dismissing command), and Apprentice according to who is directly addressed or most affected.

## Knowledge

- Information enters the Play solely through spoken dialogue, audible commotion, and visible physical states in [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md). Private thoughts are never shared with other roles.
- At the opening, [`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md) assumes he holds sufficient command of the rite; the absence of the dismissing word becomes known to him only at the moment he first tries to halt an active [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md).
- At the close of every Stage beat, persist and state three concrete facts as shared evidence for everyone present:
  1. **Servants:** How many animated broom-servants are active (0 while [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md) is idle or dismissed; 1 once started; 2—or more—if split) and where they are between corner, basin, doorway, and river.
  2. **Pieces and tools:** What holds [`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md), whether spare bowls, doors, or the axe have been used, and whether [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md) is whole or split.
  3. **Water mark:** The exact stage of water in [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md)—dry floor, Stage 1 (basin swelling), Stage 2 (basin and bowls brimming at full measure), Stage 3 (floor awash and streaming over inner thresholds), or Stage 4 (overflowing the outer threshold down the entry steps).
- [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md) may use only what he heard on approach and the physical evidence recorded in [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md) upon arrival.

## System

- **Single source for Process initiation:** Govern when [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md) starts solely by the `Initiated by` section of [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md)—never starting it before that condition is met in play, and starting it on the beat that condition is met.
- **No invented counter-spell:** Override any impulse to let [`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md) recall, deduce, or improvise a working verbal dismissal once [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md) is running. Only [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md) can speak the dismissing formula that ends [`process-water-fetching.md`](process-water-fetching.md).
- **Honest physics over scripted beats:** Do not force [`persona-apprentice.md`](persona-apprentice.md) to pick up the axe or follow Goethe's exact order of attempts. Resolve whatever intervention the Apprentice tries: bodily restraint, barring a door, catching water in bowls, or wresting [`piece-bucket.md`](piece-bucket.md) away can delay or blunt at most one trip's pour while held at full effort, whereas cleaving an active [`piece-broom.md`](piece-broom.md) always produces two active water-fetching servants and doubles the pours per beat.
- **Fixed Master entry cue:** Hold [`persona-master.md`](persona-master.md)'s entry cue invariant across runs—never early to rescue, never delayed once either trigger occurs.
- **Mode switch:** Only the human may call `Cut` to pause performance for notes or `Reset` to return [`place-hall.md`](place-hall.md) to the dry opening state.
