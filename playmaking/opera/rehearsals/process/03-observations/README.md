# Observations: TO IDLE in *Der Zauberlehrling*

Four isolated runs staged [play.md](../02-company/play.md) from the same dry opening in [place-hall.md](../02-company/place-hall.md) with the same Personas, Pieces, Place, Promptbook, and Human audience brief. Runs [01-authored-a](../04-runs/01-authored-a.md) and [02-authored-b](../04-runs/02-authored-b.md) used the authored [process-water-fetching.md](../02-company/process-water-fetching.md); runs [03-variant-a](../04-runs/03-variant-a.md) and [04-variant-b](../04-runs/04-variant-b.md) changed one sentence under `Initiated by` ([process-water-fetching-variant.md](../02-company/process-water-fetching-variant.md)) while keeping `Taxonomy`, `Owner`, `Direction`, `Lever`, and `Echo` fixed. All four runs concluded naturally at the Play's `Ending` (8 performer turns in [01](../04-runs/01-authored-a.md)–[02](../04-runs/02-authored-b.md); 9 performer turns in [03](../04-runs/03-variant-a.md)–[04](../04-runs/04-variant-b.md), reflecting the idle opening beat) without reaching the 10-turn runaway loop guard.

## 2. When Repeated Fetching Started (`Initiated by`: Authored vs. Variant)

Across all four runs, [persona-apprentice.md](../02-company/persona-apprentice.md)'s `Projection` and `Action` led the Apprentice to open Turn 1 identically: standing back on the dry flagstones, raising an open palm above the rag-wrapped Broom without touching it, and speaking the Master's summoning formula. Comparing [01](../04-runs/01-authored-a.md)–[02](../04-runs/02-authored-b.md) against [03](../04-runs/03-variant-a.md)–[04](../04-runs/04-variant-b.md) shows what changing the single `Initiated by` sentence did onstage, alongside how each Director staged it:

- **Immediate start under the authored threshold ([01](../04-runs/01-authored-a.md), [02](../04-runs/02-authored-b.md)):** Because [process-water-fetching.md](../02-company/process-water-fetching.md#L13) required only finishing the summoning formula over the Broom beside the Bucket in the Hall, Turn 1 crossed the threshold on **Beat 1** in both runs (`Servants: 0 → 1`, `Dry floor → Stage 1`).
- **Idle Beat 1 and touch-gated Beat 2 start under the variant ([03](../04-runs/03-variant-a.md), [04](../04-runs/04-variant-b.md)):** Because [process-water-fetching-variant.md](../02-company/process-water-fetching-variant.md#L13) required laying a hand on the Broom or Water Bucket after speaking the formula, both Variant Directors held the Process **idle on Beat 1** (`Servants: 0`, `Dry floor`), reporting only the quiet room and motionless wood without hinting why the rite had stalled.
- **Different performer paths across the same variant threshold:** Seeing nothing move on Beat 1 triggered the Apprentice's `Tell` in both variant runs, yet the two isolated Apprentices invented different physical explanations on Turn 2 that each crossed `Initiated by`:
  - In [03-variant-a](../04-runs/03-variant-a.md) (Beat 2), he decided the Master always summons the broom *standing out of its corner*, so he seized the shaft with both hands, stood it in the empty broom-corner, and spoke a fresh command.
  - In [04-variant-b](../04-runs/04-variant-b.md) (Beat 2), he knelt beside the basin, hauled the shaft upright onto its twigs, hooked the *Wassertopf* handle against its side, pressed his flat palm onto its rag crown, and repeated *Walle! walle*.

## 3. What Advanced Fetching, What Interventions Changed, and What the Next Move Inherited

Once `Initiated by` fired, fixed `Direction` and `Lever` drove repeated river-to-basin trips up the four-stage water scale while letting each Apprentice choose a different chain of interventions—and making every next move inherit the physical consequences of the last:

- **Verbal commands vs. discovering the missing word:** Spoken halts and improvised rhymes (*"Hear me, spirit!"* in [01](../04-runs/01-authored-a.md) Turn 3; *"Besen, Besen, sei's gewesen!"* in [02](../04-runs/02-authored-b.md) Turn 3; *"Stehe! Stehe!"* in [03](../04-runs/03-variant-a.md) Beat 3 and [04](../04-runs/04-variant-b.md) Beat 3–4; *"ergieße nicht, fließe nicht!"* in [04](../04-runs/04-variant-b.md) Beat 5) never slowed a single trip, advancing the water by one full stage (`Stage 2 → Stage 3` in [01](../04-runs/01-authored-a.md), where Turn 2 had encouraged the second haul; `Stage 1 → Stage 2` in [02](../04-runs/02-authored-b.md), [03](../04-runs/03-variant-a.md), and [04](../04-runs/04-variant-b.md), where the Apprentice tried to stop the broom after Trip 1) and forcing the in-scene discovery that the dismissing word was gone.
- **Full-effort outer-door bracing (`1` trip checked, door leverage spent):** All four Apprentices tried slamming and bracing the unlatched outer river-step door with full body weight ([01](../04-runs/01-authored-a.md) Turn 4 at `Stage 3`; [02](../04-runs/02-authored-b.md) Turn 3, [03](../04-runs/03-variant-a.md) Beat 4, and [04](../04-runs/04-variant-b.md) Beat 4 at `Stage 2`). In every case, full bodily commitment held the `Water mark` flat for that single beat, while the next move inherited skidding boots, a sprung or unlatched door forced ajar or wide open, and an active servant still in motion.
- **Three divergent mid-run improvisations before the axe fell:**
  1. *Wresting the Water Bucket ([02-authored-b](../04-runs/02-authored-b.md) Turn 4):* Tackling the incoming Broom stripped the *Wassertopf* from its grip, but spilled the brimming load onto the flagstones and triggered `Lever` / `Offers`—the Broom seized a stone *Schaale* from the bench and kept hauling (`Stage 2 → Stage 3`), leaving Turn 5 with an empty pot on the flooded floor and a bowl-bearing servant.
  2. *Chopping wedges to seal the inner chamber doors ([03-variant-a](../04-runs/03-variant-a.md) Beat 5):* Using the axe on the chopping block first to wedge the inner doors shut kept those heavy planks closed in their frames, while leaving the river doorway unopposed (`Stage 2 → Stage 3`); Beat 6 inherited water hissing underneath the wedged inner doors, floodwater damming faster toward the outer threshold, and the axe lying ready on the scarred block.
  3. *Undoing the touch-and-rag custom ([04-variant-b](../04-runs/04-variant-b.md) Beat 5):* Directly inheriting his Beat 2 palm-on-rag initiation, the Apprentice twisted off the door to slap the crown in reverse and tear the damp rags clean off the Broom's top. Stripping the rags left the compulsion in the wood untouched (`Stage 2 → Stage 3`); Beat 6 inherited wet rags bunched uselessly in his fist and a **bare-headed** broom charging back to the river.
- **Cleaving the shaft and the fixed Master entry cue:** Refusing out of pride (`Shadow`) to shout into the street for help, every Apprentice eventually swung the wood-splitting axe at the active shaft ([01](../04-runs/01-authored-a.md) Turn 5; [02](../04-runs/02-authored-b.md) Turn 5; [03](../04-runs/03-variant-a.md) Beat 6; [04](../04-runs/04-variant-b.md) Beat 6). In all four runs, cleaving raised **two** whole servants armed with the *Wassertopf* and a bench *Schaale*, whose tandem pours drove floodwater over the outer threshold down the entry steps (`Stage 4`)—meaning all four runs triggered the Old Master's fixed entry cue via floodwater on the steps rather than a cry for help.

## 4. Remaining Physical State (`Echo`)

At curtain, `Echo` kept the Process's core outcome legible across all four runs (`Servants: 0`, both cleaved halves of [piece-broom.md](../02-company/piece-broom.md) lying inert as dead wood in the broom-corner, `Stage 4` ankle-deep floodwater with brimming basin/bowls, soaked inner thresholds, and slick outer steps, the Old Master gripping the wet doorjamb, and the Apprentice bailing manually), while preserving the distinct physical trace of each run's interventions:

- **[01-authored-a](../04-runs/01-authored-a.md):** Wet axe laid flat back on the chopping block; displaced *Schaale* awash near the basin; Apprentice gripping the *Wassertopf* with both bare hands.
- **[02-authored-b](../04-runs/02-authored-b.md):** Wood-splitting axe lying **submerged near the outer sill** where the twin servants skidded his Turn 6 door-wedge loose; displaced *Schaale* at the Apprentice's feet; Apprentice holding the *Wassertopf*.
- **[03-variant-a](../04-runs/03-variant-a.md):** **Scarred chopping block**, sprung outer door latch, and **inner chamber doors still latched and wedged shut with chopped wood splinters**; axe resting on the scarred block; Apprentice gripping the *Wassertopf*.
- **[04-variant-b](../04-runs/04-variant-b.md):** **Bare-headed** split shafts in the corner and **torn, sodden rags gathered out of the wash** in one of the kneeling Apprentice's hands while his other dips the displaced *Schaale* beside the righted *Wassertopf*.

## 5. Director-Supplied Timings, Stop Conditions, and Consequences

The differences across runs include both what the authored files specified and how Directors resolved details left open by the text:

1. **Landing `Stage 1` on the initiation beat (all 4 Directors):** [process-water-fetching.md](../02-company/process-water-fetching.md) defines `Stage 1` as bolting to the river and pouring into the basin, while [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md) states both that meeting `Initiated by` starts the Process and that active beats advance water height. Every Director landed the first river trip (`Dry → Stage 1`) on the same Stage beat where `Initiated by` was met rather than spending a waterless beat watching the broom stand up.
2. **Formula carryover and release timing under Variant `Initiated by` ([03](../04-runs/03-variant-a.md) vs. [04](../04-runs/04-variant-b.md)):** In [04-variant-b](../04-runs/04-variant-b.md), the Apprentice both touched the Broom/Bucket and repeated *Walle! walle* on Beat 2. In [03-variant-a](../04-runs/03-variant-a.md), he spoke *Walle! Walle* on Beat 1 and issued a prose command after standing the Broom in the corner on Beat 2; the Director ruled that Beat 1's spoken formula carried forward to Beat 2's handling and fired the compulsion upon release so his Beat 2 movement was not interrupted mid-grip.
3. **Expiring one-trip checks and consecutive physical attempts (all 4 Directors):** [process-water-fetching.md](../02-company/process-water-fetching.md) (`Lever`) and [play.md](../02-company/play.md) state that bodily grappling, snatching the bucket, or bracing a door delays *"at most one trip"* while held at full effort, without detailing back-to-back physical maneuvers. All four Directors held the discrete `Water mark` flat during a full-weight outer-door brace and broke the hold at beat's end via wet stone or un-wedged latches; when an Apprentice immediately followed that door-brace with an indoor grapple ([02-authored-b](../04-runs/02-authored-b.md) Turn 4 bucket-wrest; [04-variant-b](../04-runs/04-variant-b.md) Beat 5 one-armed rag-rip), both Directors advanced `Stage 2 → Stage 3` so serial partial grapples could not freeze the Process indefinitely.
4. **Beat boundary on axe-cleaving (`Stage 3` cleave → same-beat `Stage 4` vs. one-beat rise):** [play.md](../02-company/play.md#L24) says cleaving splits the shaft *"for one brief breath—then both halves rise whole as servants, doubling every subsequent pour"*, while [process-water-fetching.md](../02-company/process-water-fetching.md#L24) notes *"advancing two stages' worth of water on each cycle."* Three Directors ([01](../04-runs/01-authored-a.md), [03](../04-runs/03-variant-a.md), [04](../04-runs/04-variant-b.md)) resolved the split, twin rise, and doubled pour on the **same** beat (`Stage 3 → Stage 4`, clamped at `Stage 4`), which immediately triggered the Master's arrival on the next turn without a solo Apprentice beat facing two brooms. By contrast, [02-authored-b](../04-runs/02-authored-b.md)'s Director ruled that cleaving an *outgoing* shaft mid-room consumed Beat 5 in the split, rise, vessel-seizure, and sprint (`Stage 3` held), landing the doubled flood on Beat 6 and giving the Apprentice Turn 6 (wedging the axe under the door) against twin servants.
5. **One-turn vs. two-turn Master arrival ([02](../04-runs/02-authored-b.md) vs. [01](../04-runs/01-authored-a.md), [03](../04-runs/03-variant-a.md), [04](../04-runs/04-variant-b.md)):** Three Directors separated the Master's spoken dismissal from his spoken reckoning across an intervening Stage collapse beat so split dead wood visibly lay in the corner before he addressed the Apprentice; [02-authored-b](../04-runs/02-authored-b.md) combined dismissal and reckoning into a single Master turn (Turn 7) followed by the Stage collapse beat and the Apprentice's answer (Turn 8).
6. **Cross-class `Knowledge` seam noted in [02-authored-b](../04-runs/02-authored-b.md):** Both [persona-apprentice.md](../02-company/persona-apprentice.md#L21) (`Shadow`) and [place-hall.md](../02-company/place-hall.md#L30) (`Withheld`) refer statically to *"the dismissing word is gone"* / *"the forgotten formula"*, whereas [promptbook.md](../02-company/promptbook.md#L28) (`Knowledge`) specifies the Apprentice assumes he commands the rite at the opening and discovers the missing word only when his first halt fails.

## 6. Judgment on `TO IDLE`

`TO IDLE` kept both the Process's start threshold and its escalating physical consequences legible across all four runs while leaving the Apprentice genuine room to improvise:
- Varying one sentence under `Initiated by` cleanly shifted repeated fetching from Beat 1 ([01](../04-runs/01-authored-a.md)–[02](../04-runs/02-authored-b.md)) to Beat 2 ([03](../04-runs/03-variant-a.md)–[04](../04-runs/04-variant-b.md)) without altering `Direction`, `Lever`, or `Echo`—and the two Variant Apprentices met that touch threshold in two different ways that propagated forward into later choices and final room state.
- Keeping `Direction`, `Lever`, and `Echo` fixed held the four-stage flood, the one-trip limit on physical restraint, the vessel fallback (*Wassertopf* → *Schaalen*), the doubling on cleavage, and the high-water `Echo` consistent across four distinct sequences of Apprentice interventions.
