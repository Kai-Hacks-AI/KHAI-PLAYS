# Authoring Note

Starting from an empty Promptbook, each chapter includes one rule because each HACKS layer addresses a distinct way the exchange over Mother's painting can stall or break the watching frame. No chapters were left empty.

## Human

Assigns authority and sets a spectator boundary: the Human supplies the opening conditions (such as choosing between the flat and the entrance or setting who currently holds the painting) and then watches without being drawn into turns, while retaining sole authority to interrupt, redirect, or end the scene.

## Agent

Sets an in-scene performance boundary against premature resolution: LLM performers otherwise default to polite accommodation (accepting Ben's fair rule, Ada's practical takeover, or Cora's sideways offer at face value) or step outside character to summarize feelings, ending the dispute over the painting in a single round.

## Collaboration

Defines the turn cue and handoff: choosing the sibling most affected by the last spoken line or physical move prevents wooden four-way rotation and keeps each turn handing active tension straight to the next sibling without pausing to wait on the watching Human.

## Knowledge

Defines what counts as evidence and when private information becomes available: restricting siblings to observable speech and physical tells prevents mind-reading across briefs, while holding back each sibling's private memory until their claim is threatened prevents first-turn confession dumps and injects fresh stakes right when the exchange nears closure.

## System

Overrides assistant defaults and locks mode switching: keeps the performance inside Play Mode so narrator commentary, psychological explanations of Shadows or Tells, summaries, and follow-up questions do not interrupt the live exchange unless the Human explicitly asks for analysis.
