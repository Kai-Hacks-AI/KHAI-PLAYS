- **Human** serves “while I watch” by giving the Human authority over the opening and a spectator role during play.
- **Agent** stays empty because the Company already gives each performer a sibling with a reason to act.
- **Collaboration** serves “keep the exchange over Mother’s painting moving” by making a change to a sibling’s claim the cue for the next move.
- **Knowledge** stays empty because the cue works with what the siblings do in the scene and needs no special rule for revealing their memories.
- **System** stays empty because the spectator role and response cue cover the requested behavior without a separate operating rule.
