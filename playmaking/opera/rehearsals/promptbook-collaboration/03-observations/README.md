# Observations: HACKS from an Intended Effect

**Goal:** “I want the siblings to keep the exchange over Mother's painting moving while I watch.”

Eight runs staged both scenes after a single Human opening. All continued with separately played Personas and a Director outside the fiction. The painting's final destination stayed unsettled in all eight runs. Each run had its own Director; the reported cues are staging choices, and the same rule can admit several plausible choices.

## What each sentence did

- **Human.** The sentence makes the observer role explicit. The Human stayed outside in both Human-only runs, and both empty runs did the same. The rehearsal itself held Human participation fixed, so these runs do not isolate an effect of that sentence. A separate probe with a Human move would give it an occasion to make a difference.
- **Collaboration.** The sentence gave Directors a local reason to revisit an affected claim, including across the cut. It did not select one universal speaker order: Ada initially drew Dan in [A](../04-runs/05-collaboration-a.md) and Ben in [B](../04-runs/06-collaboration-b.md). When two claims were equally affected, Directors still made the cue decision. The empty runs already had responsive handoffs and carryover.
- **Together.** Both combined runs stayed with Ada and Dan through the flat and into the entrance, then drew other siblings in when custody or the terms of deciding changed. The outcomes varied. In A the painting came down and changed hands; in B it stayed upstairs. The combination supported the desired play, with no clear additional effect attributable to the Human sentence under this setup.
- **Agent, Knowledge, System.** The Playwright left them empty. This goal did not test any added rule in those chapters.

## Rehearsal limits

Two runs per condition give examples of behavior, not a reliable frequency. Directors all staged the transition and decided when to rest. They sometimes supplied extra framing or recaps. Those choices, the shared thread limit, and the observed continuity and wording slips are recorded in the Director notes. The sample shows how the sentences can work and where the existing Play and Director guidance already produced the desired behavior.
