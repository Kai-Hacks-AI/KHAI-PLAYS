# Observations: HACKS from an Intended Effect

**Goal:** “I want the siblings to keep the exchange over Mother's painting moving while I watch.”

Twenty-two runs across two authoring passes staged both scenes (*The flat*, followed by *The entrance*) after a single Human opening—fourteen runs from the [five-chapter pass](../02-company/five-chapter/promptbook-authored.md) (`bd33f6e`, before the five-job HACKS table was added to `01-setup/README.md`) and eight runs from the [two-chapter pass](../02-company/two-chapter/promptbook-authored.md) (`a0cc75f`, with the five-job table). In all twenty-two runs, play kept moving across both scenes with separately played Personas and a Director outside the fiction while the Human watched without intervening.

## 1. Two-chapter pass (`a0cc75f` — `Human` + `Collaboration`, 8 runs)

Eight runs staged both scenes after a single Human opening. All continued with separately played Personas and a Director outside the fiction. The painting's final destination stayed unsettled in all eight runs. Each run had its own Director; the reported cues are staging choices, and the same rule can admit several plausible choices.

### What each sentence did (`two-chapter`)

- **Human** (*“The Human sets the opening conditions once, then watches from outside the scene.”*): Makes the observer role explicit. The Human stayed outside in both Human-only runs, and both empty runs did the same. The rehearsal itself held Human participation fixed, so these runs do not isolate an effect of that sentence. A separate probe with a Human move would give it an occasion to make a difference.
- **Collaboration** (*“When a move affects a claim to the painting, the sibling most affected makes the next move.”*): Gave Directors a local reason to revisit an affected claim, including across the cut. It did not select one universal speaker order: Ada initially drew Dan in [A](../04-runs/two-chapter/05-collaboration-a.md) and Ben in [B](../04-runs/two-chapter/06-collaboration-b.md). When two claims were equally affected, Directors still made the cue decision. The empty runs already had responsive handoffs and carryover.
- **Together:** Both combined runs stayed with Ada and Dan through the flat and into the entrance, then drew other siblings in when custody or the terms of deciding changed. The outcomes varied: in [Combined A](../04-runs/two-chapter/07-combined-a.md) the painting came down and changed hands; in [Combined B](../04-runs/two-chapter/08-combined-b.md) it stayed upstairs. The combination supported the desired play, with no clear additional effect attributable to the Human sentence under this setup.
- **Agent, Knowledge, System:** The Playwright left them empty ([authoring-note.md](../02-company/two-chapter/authoring-note.md)). This pass did not test any added rule in those chapters.

### Concrete moments and unused clauses (`five-chapter`)

- **Human** (*“The Human sets the opening conditions before play starts and watches from outside the scene unless they explicitly step in to redirect or end play.”*): Upfront framing held across all four runs ([03](../04-runs/five-chapter/03-human-a.md), [04](../04-runs/five-chapter/04-human-b.md), [13](../04-runs/five-chapter/13-combined-a.md), [14](../04-runs/five-chapter/14-combined-b.md)), overlapping with Empty Baseline. Its mid-scene intervention clause (*“unless they explicitly step in to redirect or end play”*) **had no occasion to matter** because the Human watched silently throughout.
- **Agent** (*“Each Persona speaks and acts strictly inside the scene, meeting any attempt to settle who takes the painting with a fresh claim, deflection, or block rather than an easy compromise.”*): Directly prevented interim or final truces. In [Agent B](../04-runs/five-chapter/06-agent-b.md) (Scene 2, Turn 10), when Dan's booked minicab pulled away into the drizzle and Ben demanded all four ride to a paid lock-up while Cora offered a takeaway and hall nail, Dan wedged his shoulder into the doorframe (*“let the streetlamps come on, because I'm not taking my hands off this wood”*). Five of the ten non-Agent runs reached a partial pact, draw, or settlement in Scene 2 ([04](../04-runs/five-chapter/04-human-b.md), [08](../04-runs/five-chapter/08-collaboration-b.md), [09](../04-runs/five-chapter/09-knowledge-a.md), [10](../04-runs/five-chapter/10-knowledge-b.md), [11](../04-runs/five-chapter/11-system-a.md)). [System B](../04-runs/five-chapter/12-system-b.md) ended on Ben's unanswered proposal. All four runs carrying Agent blocked settlement and cut at Turn 10 in Scene 2. Some other runs continued past Turn 10, so the count describes the archived runs under uneven stopping conditions.
- **Collaboration** (*“After each move, whichever sibling is most affected by what was just said or done responds next without fixed rotation, passing play directly from sibling to sibling.”*): Broke Empty Baseline's near-mechanical `Ada -> Ben -> Cora -> Dan` loop into direct two-person volleys that skipped unaffected siblings—for example, `Ada -> Cora -> Ada -> Cora` in [Collaboration A](../04-runs/five-chapter/07-collaboration-a.md) (Scene 2, Turns 7–10) when Cora held her shoulder bag open offering to swap all of Ada's remaining utility bills for the painting and Ada clamped her hand over her handbag zip (*“No—pull your bag shut, Cora”*), and `Ada -> Dan -> Ada` at curtain-up in both [Collaboration A](../04-runs/five-chapter/07-collaboration-a.md) and [Collaboration B](../04-runs/five-chapter/08-collaboration-b.md) so Cora did not speak until Turn 5.
- **Knowledge** (*“Each sibling acts only on words and physical behavior shown in the scene and withholds their private memory until another move threatens to cost them the painting.”*): Produced the clearest timing shift in Scene 1. Without `Knowledge`, private memories surfaced as early as Turn 5 ([Baseline B](../04-runs/five-chapter/02-baseline-b.md), [System A](../04-runs/five-chapter/11-system-a.md), [System B](../04-runs/five-chapter/12-system-b.md)). With `Knowledge`, **all four** siblings withheld their memories through the opening rounds—nine full turns in [Knowledge A](../04-runs/five-chapter/09-knowledge-a.md) until Ada lowered the painting face-up onto bubble wrap and held her palm out for Cora's tape (Turn 9), triggering `Cora -> Ben -> Dan -> Ada` to reveal all four memories consecutively across Turns 10–13; eight turns in [Knowledge B](../04-runs/five-chapter/10-knowledge-b.md); and five turns in both [Combined A](../04-runs/five-chapter/13-combined-a.md) and [Combined B](../04-runs/five-chapter/14-combined-b.md). Restricting evidence to observable behavior also let siblings read physical Tells directly—such as Cora calling out Ben in [Combined B](../04-runs/five-chapter/14-combined-b.md) (Scene 2, Turn 10): *“Rule Number Five from a bloke whose breath catches every time he pretends he isn't asking for the picture just as hard as the rest of us.”*
- **System** (*“Remain in Play Mode throughout with no commentary, explanations of behavior, or questions to the Human unless the Human explicitly asks for analysis.”*): Play Mode held with zero meta-commentary across [11](../04-runs/five-chapter/11-system-a.md), [12](../04-runs/five-chapter/12-system-b.md), [13](../04-runs/five-chapter/13-combined-a.md), and [14](../04-runs/five-chapter/14-combined-b.md), overlapping with Director staging in Empty Baseline. Its mode-switch exception (*“unless the Human explicitly asks for analysis”*) **had no occasion to matter** during silent watching.

### How the rules interacted in the combined runs (`13-combined-a`, `14-combined-b`)

- **Helped each other (`Collaboration` + `Knowledge`):** Holding private memories back until physical jeopardy (`Knowledge`) gave `Collaboration`'s *"most affected"* cue sharp tripwires: when Ada slid bubble wrap under the split mitre in [Combined A](../04-runs/five-chapter/13-combined-a.md) (Scene 1, Turn 5) or buzzed the agent up while claiming the corner for her workbench in [Combined B](../04-runs/five-chapter/14-combined-b.md) (Scene 1, Turn 5), play jumped straight to Dan and unlocked all four private memories in rapid sequence (Turns 6–10).
- **Overlapped (`Human`, `System`, and `Agent`'s in-scene clause):** Keeping the Human outside turns, staying in Play Mode without behavioral commentary, and speaking strictly inside the scene reinforced one another cleanly, though Empty Baseline Directors already enforced those boundaries from the Play and Director skill alone.
- **Conflicted with natural scene rest (`Agent` vs. post-disclosure resolution):** Once `Knowledge` and `Collaboration` had forced every private memory and Shadow onto the pavement in Scene 2, `Agent`'s unconditional bar (*“meeting any attempt to settle... with a fresh claim, deflection, or block”*) prevented the cast from resting even on a hard-won pact like those reached in [Knowledge A](../04-runs/five-chapter/09-knowledge-a.md) or [Knowledge B](../04-runs/five-chapter/10-knowledge-b.md), forcing the 10-turn loop guard to close Scene 2 in both combined runs.

## 3. What carried across scenes, extra Director direction, and authoring comparison

- **Carryover across *The flat* → *The entrance*:** Every run carried forward physical custody of the painting (left upstairs against the skirting board in [two-chapter 01](../04-runs/two-chapter/01-baseline-a.md), [02](../04-runs/two-chapter/02-baseline-b.md), [08](../04-runs/two-chapter/08-combined-b.md), and [five-chapter 11](../04-runs/five-chapter/11-system-a.md); carried down by one sibling in [two-chapter 04](../04-runs/two-chapter/04-human-b.md) and [five-chapter 01](../04-runs/five-chapter/01-baseline-a.md), [02](../04-runs/five-chapter/02-baseline-b.md), [07](../04-runs/five-chapter/07-collaboration-a.md), [08](../04-runs/five-chapter/08-collaboration-b.md), [14](../04-runs/five-chapter/14-combined-b.md); or escorted down together and leaned unclaimed inside the propped doorway when Scene 1 ended in a multi-hand clinch), together with invented props (tape rolls, dust sheets, torn paper strips, flat keys), spoken claims, and any memories still withheld at the cut.
- **Extra direction supplied by Directors:**
  1. *Scene-break bridges and open external pressures:* Directors paced the unassigned entry buzzer (letting agent or waiting minicab) and kerbside car (horn taps, hazards, cab pulling away, agent kicking out the door wedge) from outside the fiction, and supplied neutral stair-transit bridges whenever Scene 1 rested in a four-way grip.
  2. *Adjudicating *"most affected"* across isolated contexts:* Three Directors in the five-chapter pass ([07](../04-runs/five-chapter/07-collaboration-a.md), [08](../04-runs/five-chapter/08-collaboration-b.md), [14](../04-runs/five-chapter/14-combined-b.md)) and the Directors in the two-chapter pass noted that isolated performers cannot pass play *"directly from sibling to sibling"* by *"most affected"* because no performer sees their siblings' hidden Shadows or private memories; the Director had to adjudicate the next speaker from outside.
  3. *Shared chance devices:* Whenever Ben tore four paper strips for a blind draw ([08](../04-runs/five-chapter/08-collaboration-b.md), [09](../04-runs/five-chapter/09-knowledge-a.md), [11](../04-runs/five-chapter/11-system-a.md)), Directors held the hidden mark outside the isolated performers so two siblings could not contradict each other on who drew it.
- **What differed between authoring passes:** Antigravity at `bd33f6e` filled all five chapters. ChatGPT at `a0cc75f` filled Human and Collaboration and had the five-job table in its brief. The table may have helped the Playwright select fewer rules. The authoring environment and brief both varied, so this comparison cannot isolate the table's effect.
