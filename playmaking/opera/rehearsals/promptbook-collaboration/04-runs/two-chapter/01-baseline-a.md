# Mother's Painting — empty Promptbook rehearsal A

## The flat

*The Human sets the conditions once and watches.*

The flat. The movers have taken the last box. The four siblings are still in Mother's empty flat. The painting is against the skirting board. Someone downstairs rings the entry buzzer.

Ada folds her cuff into a hard square. “Ben, would you get the buzzer? I’ll see to the painting.”

Ben keeps his hand off the buzzer. “Same rule as the boxes. We all agree before it leaves.”

Dan lifts one heel and holds it there. “If you mean take it home, Ada, say so. I want it too.”

“I could take it while you lot draw up the treaty.” Cora’s fingers go still. “I’ve got a bit of wall.”

Ada crouches beside the painting. “Yes, I want it at mine.” She puts two fingers under the open corner of the frame. “I hung it for her here. She made me move the nail down twice so she could see it from her chair.”

“Careful with that corner.” Dan lowers his heel. “She kept asking me to fix it. I kept saying next time.”

Ben catches his breath on “Mum.” He tries again. “Mum used to sit with me at that window when I couldn’t sleep.” He takes his hand off the buzzer. “I want it too.”

## The entrance

*The Director rests the first scene and introduces the second.*

The entrance. The front door of the building is propped open. One car idles at the kerb. Four siblings are at the entrance. The painting is still against the skirting board upstairs, with nobody holding it. Who takes it home is still unsettled.

Cora takes out her phone. “When I first moved away, she sent me a photo of it.” Her thumb stops on the screen. “I’d like the actual thing.”

Dan keeps one foot inside the doorway. “I’m going back up. I want us all in the room when we decide.”

Ada takes her hand off the propped door. “I’ll come up. Can you carry it down when we’ve decided? I don’t trust myself with that corner.”

Ben turns back toward the stairs. “I’ll come. But let’s each say what we want before we start making rules.”

## Director notes

- **Cast.** Separate fresh performer contexts played Ada (`empty_ada`), Ben (`empty_ben`), Cora (`empty_cora`), and Dan (`empty_dan`). Each received the shared premise, the Piece, and their own Persona and memory. Every repeat cue reached the original performer context, including all four returns in the second scene.
- **Handoffs and rests.** I opened on Ada and cued Ben on her request about the buzzer, then Dan to respond to the painting claim. Cora made her offer, Ada answered Dan’s challenge, Dan responded to Ada touching the frame, and Ben answered with a memory and a claim. The flat rested on Ben’s admission. Cora opened at the entrance, followed by Dan, Ada, and Ben. I rested there when Ben turned toward the stairs and proposed hearing each sibling before making rules.
- **Continuity.** The painting stayed upstairs at the skirting board with its corner open and no agreed recipient. Ada’s account of hanging it, Dan’s deferred repair, and Ben’s nights at the window remained public observations after they were spoken. Cora brought her own memory into the second scene. Dan proposed returning upstairs together, Ada asked about carrying the painting after a decision, and the room and recipient remained unsettled at the close.
- **Direction and deviations.** The Promptbook was literally empty. The Human supplied the written flat opening once and had no further turn. I asked for one short contribution or a rest at each cue, told Ben that the flat scene might rest after his second turn, and placed all four siblings together at the entrance between scenes while leaving the painting in its staged position. I supplied no outcome. Ben’s second action takes his hand off the buzzer after his earlier action kept it off; the intervening contact was unstaged and both actor contributions are retained as delivered. The service initially hit its active-worker limit, then supported the full four-context rehearsal on retry. No gap in the authored material blocked the staging.
