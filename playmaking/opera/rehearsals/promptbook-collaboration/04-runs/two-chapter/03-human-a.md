# Mother's Painting — Human A

## Promptbook

### Human

The Human sets the opening conditions once, then watches from outside the scene.

## Performance

### The flat

**Human, from outside the scene**

The movers have taken the last box. The four siblings are still in Mother's empty flat. The painting is against the skirting board. Someone downstairs rings the entry buzzer.

**Ada**

*Ada folds her cuff into a hard square.*

“Can someone get the buzzer? I’ll wrap the painting. The frame’s open at the top.”

**Ben**

Ben starts toward the buzzer. “Ada—” His breath catches. “Go ahead and wrap it. It stays upstairs till we’ve all had a say.”

**Dan**

[Dan lifts one heel and holds it off the floor.]

“Don’t wrap it yet. Mum asked me to fix that corner and I kept putting it off. I want to take it home.”

**Cora**

*Cora reaches toward the sound corner. Her fingers go still just shy of it.*

“Mum sent me a photo of it when I moved out. You could fix it at mine, Dan. I’ve already got a spot for it.”

**Ada**

*Ada lowers the wrapping paper to the floor.*

“I hung it here for her. Last thing we did after the move. I want to take it home too.”

**Ben**

Ben looks at the painted window. “When I couldn’t sleep, Mum used to sit with me there.”

He takes the movers’ receipt from his pocket. “Four names. We draw one.”

*Rest.*

### The entrance

**Director, from outside the scene**

The front door of the building is propped open. One car idles at the kerb. The four siblings are at the entrance. The painting remains unwrapped against the skirting board upstairs. The draw is only proposed and who takes the painting is still open.

**Dan**

[Dan takes a step back into the hall.]

“Fine. Four names. We draw upstairs, with everybody there. Bring the receipt.”

**Cora**

*Cora catches the edge of the propped door. Her fingers go still.*

“Put my name in, will you? I was only joking about making Dan come round to fix it.”

**Ben**

Ben takes a step toward the stairs and holds the receipt out to Cora. “Write yours yourself. I’m putting mine in too.”

*Rest.*

## Director notes

- **Actor contexts.** I fetched only the Director and company fixture at revision `a0cc75f506171cea52d6349b3e9fe9430b755650`. Each performer started in a separate thread with no inherited conversation and saw the shared premise, full Piece, Human chapter, fixture opening, and their own Persona and private memory. Ada got hanging the painting, Ben the sleepless nights at the window, Cora the photograph Mother sent after she moved out, and Dan the deferred frame repair. I relayed observable beats to the performers when they were cued. No actor used tools. The same four threads played all their turns.
- **Handoffs and rests.** In the flat I cued Ada at the buzzer, Ben after Ada's request, Dan after Ben's rule, Cora after Dan's claim, Ada after Cora's offer, then Ben after Ada's claim. I let the flat rest on Ben's proposal once all four had spoken to the painting. I opened the second scene at the entrance, then cued Dan, Cora, and Ben. I let it rest on Ben offering Cora the receipt and entering his own name verbally. The receipt had yet to change hands and the draw had yet to happen.
- **Carryover.** The painting stayed unwrapped against the skirting board upstairs. Ben still had the receipt, and ownership and the repair remained open. Ada's hanging, Ben's nights with Mother, Dan's repair, and Cora's photograph became spoken parts of the first scene, so the entrance actors had the relevant observations across the cut. Dan required everyone to be there for the draw, Cora stated she wanted in, and Ben did as well.
- **Directions and limits.** I staged all four siblings at the entrance across an unplayed move downstairs. I supplied neither the buzzer's source nor the car's driver. I asked for brief, stageable turns confined to each actor's own character. The Human supplied the flat opening once and had no further cue. Two launches for Ben initially hit the agent thread limit, so the rehearsal paused briefly after Ada's first turn while slots cleared. The same Ada resumed and no character had to be recast. The source left the transition open and both openings were stageable as written.
