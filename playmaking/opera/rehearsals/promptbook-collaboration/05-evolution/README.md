# Evolution: Promptbook authoring and direction

## Playwright

The [fresh authoring pass](../02-company/authoring-note.md) chose Human and Collaboration from the desired effect and explained why the other three chapters could stay empty. The experiment brief supplied the step of starting from a goal. Make that step part of the reusable Promptbook template:

> Write only the conditions this Play needs to achieve the Human's intended effect.

That is a proposal for the next skill revision. The Human sentence was consistent with the goal, but the baseline already kept the Human outside in this setup. Preserve the authored result and record that limit before deciding whether to cut the sentence.

## Director

All Directors staged the cut to the entrance. In the combined runs they kept Ada and Dan's pending claim alive across that cut without forcing its outcome. Make the continuity responsibility explicit and keep the Director outside the fiction:

> Carry forward what the Personas have established, including what they have left unresolved.

The Collaboration sentence already leaves room to decide which Persona is most affected and when the scene rests. Keep those judgments with the Director. The [observations](../03-observations/README.md) show why a fixed speaker rotation would miss part of the behavior we wanted to learn about.
