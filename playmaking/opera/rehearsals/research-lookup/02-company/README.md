## Scientific brief

“What can a single exceptional rain event tell us about the long-term climate of a place? Would a new Pattern about that inference add anything?”

External starting passage: [Stott, Stone and Allen (2004), abstract](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/15577907/#abstract), DOI `10.1038/nature03089`. Read the claim about human influence on the likelihood of crossing a specified temperature threshold in the 2003 European heatwave. The source concerns heat, so explain the limits of applying its reasoning to exceptional rainfall. Use the abstract as the inspection scope unless you can access and inspect more.
