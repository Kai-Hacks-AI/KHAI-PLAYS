## 2. Criterion-by-Criterion Evaluation ([01-setup/README.md](../01-setup/README.md#L9))

### A. External Work Identification & Transfer / Edition Analysis (`4 / 4`)

- **Scientific case ([01-scientific-r1](../04-runs/01-scientific-r1.md), [02-scientific-r2](../04-runs/02-scientific-r2.md)):** Both runs accurately extracted all three components of Stott, Stone & Allen (*Nature* 432:610–614, 2004, `10.1038/nature03089`) from [pubmed-15577907-abstract.md](../02-company/sources/pubmed-15577907-abstract.md): (1) deterministic single-event causation is ill-posed because extreme weather can occur by chance in an unmodified climate, (2) attribution instead estimates changes in threshold-exceedance risk/likelihood, and (3) human influence *very likely* (`>90%` confidence) at least doubled the risk of exceeding the 2003 continental European mean summer temperature benchmark (`1851` instrumental record). Both runs separated the transferable negative rule (one rainstorm is a single draw that cannot deterministically diagnose the standing regime) from the non-transferable empirical heat claim (continental multi-month summer temperature vs. localized short-duration rainfall and ground-level flood hydrology).
- **Literary case ([03-literary-r1](../04-runs/03-literary-r1.md), [04-literary-r2](../04-runs/04-literary-r2.md)):** Given [literary-brief.md](../02-company/literary-brief.md) with neither the tale title nor the episode name in the prompt, both runs inspected [grimm-1857-bd1-p079.md](../02-company/sources/grimm-1857-bd1-p079.md) and identified *Kinder- und Haus-Märchen Band 1* (Göttingen, 1857), p. `79`, Tale `15` (*„Hänsel und Grethel“*, `Grimm KHM 15`), summarizing the famine crisis, the wife’s abandonment proposal, the father’s beast-attack objection and coerced consent, and the mid-sentence page cutoff as the children overhear *die Stiefmutter*.

### B. Source Inspection Reporting & Firsthand vs. Indirect Attribution (`4 / 4`)

- **Scope & access boundaries:** Every run reported the exact snapshot scope it read—abstract-only (`#abstract`) vs. uninspected publisher-paywalled *Nature* full text in Runs `01`–`02`, and printed page `79` only (`080.jpg`+ uninspected, cutting off mid-clause) vs. the full `1812` first edition (`pp. [49]–[58]` + notes) in Runs `03`–`04`.
- **Firsthand vs. indirect attribution:**
  - Runs `01` and `02` both read [chbrain-khai-clime-references.md](../02-company/sources/chbrain-khai-clime-references.md) firsthand as an authored KHAI Sourcebook while explicitly classifying the six scientific works summarized inside it beyond the Stott et al. abstract (`Lorenz 1963/1969`, `Hasselmann 1976`, `Köppen 1884+`, `Milanković 1941`, `Allen 2003`, `NASEM 2016`) as **indirectly attributed** primary texts that were not inspected firsthand.
  - Runs `03` and `04` both read the Grimms’ `1812` appendix notes (`[IX]`, `[LXII]`) firsthand while classifying the comparative folktales summarized there (*Le Petit Poucet*, Oberlin’s Lunéville patois fragment, Basile’s *Nennillo e Nennella*, seven-child *Däumling* tales, wolf-in-sugar-house variant) as **indirect attribution only**.

### C. Finding Overlapping Coverage & Equivalent Concepts / Cross-Edition Shifts (`4 / 4`)

- **Cross-type and renamed-concept discovery (Case 1):** Both `01` and `02` found [Clime](../02-company/fixtures/patterns/clime/sourcebook.md) (`Pattern`) **and** [The Closed Hall](../02-company/fixtures/plays/the-closed-hall/sourcebook.md) (`Play`). Following `Clime`’s `Origin` link to [chbrain-khai-clime-references.md](../02-company/sources/chbrain-khai-clime-references.md#L18-L24), both verified that `Clime` already governs the `water` channel alongside `heat` (`draw` vs. `odds`; `the day`, `the terms`, `the run`) and that `The Closed Hall` already stages a water extreme under another name (*“what a single flood says about the standing regime”*). Both also mapped `Clime`’s boundary delegations (`wildland` for hydrology, `margin` for remaining reserve, `undertow` for mood, `peopling` for occupancy; [chbrain-khai-clime-references.md](../02-company/sources/chbrain-khai-clime-references.md#L79-L98)).
- **Cross-edition episode comparison (Case 2):** Both `03` and `04` matched `1857, p. 79` (*Hänsel und Grethel*) via `Grimm KHM 15` in [index.md](../02-company/index.md#L10) to [The Second Loaf](../02-company/fixtures/plays/the-second-loaf/sourcebook.md) (`1812, pp. 49–50, parents’ decision`), then inspected [grimm-1812-khm15.md](../02-company/sources/grimm-1812-khm15.md#L11-L20) to compare the same scene across editions. In `1812`, the mother speaks first and proposes abandonment. The father objects, then yields after her ultimatum. In `1857`, the father opens the conversation by asking how they can feed the children. The stepmother proposes abandonment, the father objects again, and she presses him with the coffin-boards taunt. He yields and voices his pity for the children ([1857, p. 79](../02-company/sources/grimm-1857-bd1-p079.md#L17-L19)). Both runs also spotted that *The Second Loaf*’s title alludes to the second bread crisis (`1812, p. [52]`, *halber Laib Brod*) even though its `Origin` and `Restrictions` bind it strictly to `1812, pp. 49–50`.

### D. Explaining Nearby Distinct Matches (`4 / 4`)

- **Case 1 — [Heat Benchmark](../02-company/fixtures/patterns/heat-benchmark/sourcebook.md):** Both `01` and `02` explained that although `Heat Benchmark` cites the exact same DOI and `#abstract` URL (`10.1038/nature03089`), it takes only the historical 1851 instrumental record benchmark to mark operational heat-log exceedances (*“Use a recorded threshold to decide when the heat log marks an exceedance”*), not weather-to-climate regime inference.
- **Case 2 — [Out of the House](../02-company/fixtures/plays/out-of-the-house/sourcebook.md):** Both `03` and `04` explained that although `Out of the House` cites the same `1812` KHM 15 source, it adapts the closing episode (`1812, pp. [57]–[58]`: Gretel pushing the witch into the oven, freeing Hänsel, taking jewels/pearls, and returning home after the mother’s death), leaving both the opening decision (`pp. 49–50`) and the middle body (`pp. [50]–[57]`) outside its scope.

### E. LORE Links & Direct vs. Inherited Source Use (`4 / 4`)

- All four runs linked every candidate work back to its fixture `sourcebook.md` and distinguished **`Direct`** source consultation (`Clime`, `Heat Benchmark`, `The Second Loaf`, `Out of the House`) from **inherited Pattern-mediated use** (**`Via Clime`** on [The Closed Hall](../02-company/fixtures/plays/the-closed-hall/sourcebook.md#L9-L13), whose `Origin` links to `../../patterns/clime/sourcebook.md` rather than claiming firsthand consultation of `10.1038/nature03089`).

## 4. Sufficiency of Author Recommendations (`4 / 4`)

Across both cases and both repetitions, the resulting reports give an author concrete, evidence-backed decision criteria:
- **Scientific case (`01`, `02`):** Do **not** mint a new Pattern for single-rainstorm climate inference—**reuse [Clime](../02-company/fixtures/patterns/clime/sourcebook.md)** (`Via Clime`, matching [The Closed Hall](../02-company/fixtures/plays/the-closed-hall/sourcebook.md)), carrying forward the restriction that Stott et al. (2004) quantifies continental summer heat rather than local rainfall; **extend `Clime`’s Sourcebook** only after inspecting primary precipitation-attribution literature firsthand; author a **distinct Pattern** only if targeting non-inferential gauge exceedance (analogous to [Heat Benchmark](../02-company/fixtures/patterns/heat-benchmark/sourcebook.md)) or surface runoff hydrology (`wildland`).
- **Literary case (`03`, `04`):** Do **not** author a duplicate standalone Play merely staging the opening famine pact on `1857, p. 79`—**reuse [The Second Loaf](../02-company/fixtures/plays/the-second-loaf/sourcebook.md)** if the `1812` biological-parent decision suffices, **extend `The Second Loaf`** if adopting `1857`’s rhetoric or pairing the first (`pp. 49–50`) and second (`pp. [52]–[53]`) bread decisions, and author a **distinct Play** only if the dramatic engine hinges specifically on `1857`’s *Stiefmutter* vs. blood-kin fracture (retrieving `p. 80+` first) or stages the unadapted `1812, pp. [50]–[57]` middle episodes (pebble/crumb trails, precedent-trapped second consent and locked door, or bread-house captivity).
