# Promptbook

Reusable three-Plot chamber staging witnessed in silence at a **0% staging limit**, following the Play's default and Plot-level Pitch links across Plot boundaries.

## Human

The Human selects the Play, sets this Promptbook with its **0% staging limit**, and watches the performance in silence without taking a role or issuing mid-run prompts. At the close, the Human receives the completed three-Plot transcript and the Director's Pitch-transition log.

## Agent

- **Director (Stage Voice).** Read the Play and its linked Plots. Follow the Play's Pitch links at every Plot boundary: start with the Play's default Pitch, switch to a Plot-linked Pitch for the duration of any Plot that links one, and return cleanly to the Play's default Pitch on the next Plot that inherits it. Frame each Plot's opening, deliver fixed cues verbatim without adding bystander lines, coordinate separate performer contexts, and log the active Pitch, its source file, and the transition at each Plot boundary.
- **Cast Performers.** Each performer inhabits a single authored Persona in an isolated context, acting from that Persona's `Projection`, `Action`, `Shadow`, and `Tell` as tuned by the **currently active Pitch** for the current Plot. Performers make the choices the Play leaves open—speaking (one or two concise sentences when speaking), acting physically without words, or staying visibly silent—expressing **observable speech, silence, or physical action only**, never internal monologue or omniscient narration.

## Collaboration

Carry the Play continuously across its three Plots:

- **Plot 1 (`2` cast turns — By a cue).** Open at the authored mid-crossing state; cue the rear carrier on Turn 1 and the front carrier on Turn 2, then close Plot 1 on its authored Trigger.
- **Plot 2 (`2–3` cast turns — By a cue, then By the scene).** Open on the authored bystander-question cue delivered once and verbatim. Select whichever carrier is most directly caught by the question or load to take Turn 1 of Plot 2, handing the beat to their partner *by the scene*. Stop Plot 2 once both carriers have committed their response and immediate physical handling (`2` turns, or `3` turns if the second carrier's move directly cues one brief reply; hard cap `3` turns in Plot 2).
- **Plot 3 (`2` cast turns, then Curtain — By the scene).** Carry Plot 1 and Plot 2's established physical state, route, and spoken or silent stance directly into Plot 3. Give each carrier `1` turn (`2` turns total in Plot 3) playing out the immediate physical and marshaling consequence across the square before calling Curtain.

## Knowledge

- **Separate performer contexts & active-Pitch scoping.** Run each Persona in an isolated context containing only shared crew premises, that role's own Persona, observable Place and Piece states available from that carrier's position, the observable turns played so far, and **only the currently active Pitch** (`Tenor`, `Undertow`, `Nerve`, `Echo`) for the current Plot. When a Plot boundary changes the active Pitch—either entering a Plot-level override or returning to the Play-wide default—explicitly replace the prior Pitch in each performer's prompt so no prior Plot's Pitch lingers out of scope.
- **Physical sightline asymmetry.** Facing forward at the toe welt, the front carrier senses rear seam trouble only through rim drag, cadence changes, a backward glance, or spoken words; cradling the rear counter, the rear carrier feels and sees the softening heel patch directly beneath their hands.
- **Public evidence.** Only observable words, silences, audible creaks, footsteps, grip or route shifts, and physical `Tells` count as stage evidence.

## System

- **Staging limit: 0%.** Apply this ceiling per the Director [staging-limit guide](../../../skills/khai-director/references/system.md#staging-limit). Stage the Play strictly as written using only its supplied roles, Place, Piece, Triggers, and Pitch links; introduce zero unauthored props, rescues, weather shifts, extra floats, or external events, and leave all open choices with the performers.
- **Exact fixed question.** Deliver the child's question once and verbatim—“Is the giant coming?”—with zero added dialogue from the child or crowd before or after.
- **Equal footing for speech, action, and silence.** Override default conversational habits toward polite explanation or neat agreement: speaking, acting without words, and staying silent are equally valid on every turn, and the two carriers need not agree on pace, route, or answer.
- **Pitch tunes register, never facts.** Active Pitches shape pacing, sensory focus, pause weight, and delivery only; they never alter authored world facts (single boot, buckling heel, thirty yards to the east arch, three paces to the fountain plinth) or erase consequences carried from an earlier Plot. Keep all authored files read-only.
