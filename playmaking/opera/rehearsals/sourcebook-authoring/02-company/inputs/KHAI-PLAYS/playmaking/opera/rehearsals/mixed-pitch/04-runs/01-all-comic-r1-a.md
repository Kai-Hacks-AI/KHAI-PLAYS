## Three-Plot Transcript

### Plot Boundary: Start → Plot 1 (`Active Pitch: Dry Comedy` — Play Default)

### Plot 1: The Strained Crossing

**Director (Stage Framing — Plot 1 Opening):**
Halfway across [Market Square](../02-company/variant-a/place-square.md)—thirty yards short of the waiting flatbed beyond the east arch and three paces inside the waist-high stone plinth by the dry central fountain—brass instruments warm up out of sight beyond the arch in damp autumn air while early onlookers line the low rope barriers. [Maren](../02-company/variant-a/persona-toe-carrier.md) (front painted wooden toe-welt, facing east, marshaling pass clipped to her collar) and [Tomas](../02-company/variant-a/persona-heel-carrier.md) (rear heel counter) carry the Third Ward’s lone entry—the single eight-foot russet [Giant Papier-Mâché Shoe](../02-company/variant-a/piece-shoe.md), with no second float or giant built behind them—knee-high along the white chalk marshaling lane. Directly beneath the rear counter, Tuesday night’s uncured flour-paste patch begins creasing inward over Tomas’s knuckles.

**Turn 1 — Tomas ([Plot 1](../02-company/variant-a/plot-1-strained-crossing.md), Turn 1):**
Tomas draws his lower lip between his teeth as the russet heel arch creases inward over his knuckles. Keeping his shoulders squared low inside the white chalk lane, he slides one broad, paste-flecked palm inward off the wooden carry-welt to press flat beneath the softening seam, and checks his rear stride by half a pace on the uneven flagstones—letting the eight-foot boot tug back slightly against Maren's forward lead without a word.

**Turn 2 — Maren ([Plot 1](../02-company/variant-a/plot-1-strained-crossing.md), Turn 2):**
Maren’s right index finger taps a quick, involuntary count against the painted wooden toe-welt as the frame tugs back against her grip. Keeping her eyes fixed straight ahead toward the east arch, she shifts both hands forward along the front rim to tip weight off the rear end and leans steadily into the next stride inside the white chalk line.

"Thirty yards to the flatbed, Tomas—keep the step even, or the marshals pull our lead slot before the brass clears the arch."

**Director (Plot 1 Closing Trigger):**
Their stride brings the flank of the eight-foot boot abreast of the dry central fountain’s white chalk marshaling edge, carrying forward Tomas’s one-palm brace under the inward-creasing heel seam and Maren’s forward-shifted grip on the toe-welt.

### Plot Boundary: Plot 1 → Plot 2 (`Active Pitch: Dry Comedy` — Play Default Retained; No Plot Override Linked)

### Plot 2: Question and Response

**Director (Stage Framing — Plot 2 Opening & Fixed Cue):**
Continuing on the very next beat alongside the dry central fountain’s chalk marshaling edge—three paces inside the waist-high stone plinth while brass warm-ups beyond the east arch tighten toward marshaling lock-in—a child steps down from the loose rope barrier onto the white chalk edge beside the flank of the eight-foot boot, looks up at the shoe, and asks:

“Is the giant coming?”

**Turn 1 — Tomas ([Plot 2](../02-company/variant-a/plot-2-question-and-response.md), Turn 1):**
Tomas catches his lower lip briefly between his teeth as the damp heel seam yields another fraction over his knuckles, wedging his paste-flecked forearm tighter against the inner wooden carry-welt while keeping his broad palm spread flat across the creasing cardboard beneath the rear counter. Holding a short, flat-footed step right along the fountain-side chalk line rather than lengthening stride to match Maren’s pull, he glances sideways toward the child at the flank of the boot.

"Flagstones by the fountain wouldn't take the rest of him—takes two of us just walking his left boot to the wagon. Mind your toes on the chalk."

**Turn 2 — Maren ([Plot 2](../02-company/variant-a/plot-2-question-and-response.md), Turn 2):**
Maren’s right index finger taps a sharp, rapid four-count against the painted wooden toe-welt as the short, flat-footed cadence tugs through the frame from behind. Cutting a brief glance down toward the white chalk line beside the boot's flank before setting her eyes straight back on the east arch, she shifts both hands farther forward along the front rim to tip weight off the rear end and presses onward at a steady march.

"Parade permit only covers one left boot through the east arch—step back behind the rope, kid. Lengthen out, Tomas; your end's dragging the count and the brass is nearly locked in."

**Director (Plot 2 Closing Trigger):**
Both carriers have committed their spoken response to the child’s question and their immediate handling of the shoe—Tomas holding a short, flat-footed step with his forearm wedged to the inner welt and palm flat beneath the yielding heel seam, Maren tipping weight forward on the front rim and pressing a steady march down the chalk lane.

### Plot Boundary: Plot 2 → Plot 3 (`Active Pitch: Dry Comedy` — Play Default Retained; No Plot Override Linked)

### Plot 3: Immediate Consequence

**Director (Stage Framing — Plot 3 Opening):**
Immediately on the heels of Maren’s call, with the silent child watching from the white chalk marshaling edge beside the dry central fountain and the waist-high stone plinth three paces off-line, the brass warm-ups beyond the east arch abruptly break off into the pre-step-off hush as Maren’s steady forward pull meets Tomas’s braced rear counter through the wooden carry-welt.

**Turn 1 — Tomas ([Plot 3](../02-company/variant-a/plot-3-immediate-consequence.md), Turn 1):**
In the sudden quiet as the brass cuts off beyond the east arch, Tomas draws his lower lip hard between his teeth as the damp cardboard yields another fraction over his knuckles. Hooking one hand tight around the solid inner wooden carry-welt where Maren's pull comes through the frame, he presses his other broad, paste-flecked palm stiff and flat directly beneath the inward-creasing heel seam. He passes the stone fountain plinth without stepping outside the white chalk line, bending his knees deeper over the uneven flagstones and quickening into a rapid, flat-soled shuffle that keeps the rear counter riding dead-level behind Maren toward the east arch.

"A boot this size doesn't trot, Maren—keep the sole level over the stones and we'll make the flatbed."

**Turn 2 — Maren ([Plot 3](../02-company/variant-a/plot-3-immediate-consequence.md), Turn 2):**
At the sudden hush beyond the east arch, Maren’s chin lifts fractionally toward the open straightaway ahead; her right index finger taps two quick beats against the painted wooden toe-welt, then clamps flat around the rim. Without a word, she shifts both hands forward into an even, locked grip on the front welt, flattens the bounce out of her stride, and carries the toe steady and knee-high across the uneven flagstones in rhythm with the rapid shuffle behind her, straight down the white chalk lane toward the east arch.

**Director (Stage Consequence & Curtain):**
Leaving the waist-high fountain plinth unused three paces outside the chalk line and the silent child watching from the marshaling edge, Maren and Tomas hold the white chalk straightaway toward the east arch flatbed—the damp russet heel seam yielded over Tomas’s knuckles yet braced off the flagstones by his flat palm and deep-knee shuffle while Maren locks the front welt level in silent, bounce-free cadence.

**CURTAIN.**
