# Sourcebook — Explicit Promptbook Pitch Handoff (*The Giant’s Shoe*)

## Line of Work

The mixed-pitch rehearsal of *The Giant’s Shoe* stages a continuous three-Plot crossing of Market Square under a **0% staging limit** using a single shared [Promptbook](promptbook.md) across both a uniform-Pitch Play ([Variant A](variant-a/play.md), inheriting [Dry Comedy](variant-a/pitch-dry-comedy.md) throughout) and a mixed-Pitch Play ([Variant B](variant-b/play.md), overriding the Play default with [Quiet Wonder](variant-b/pitch-quiet-wonder.md) in [Plot 2](variant-b/plot-2-question-and-response.md) before returning to [Dry Comedy](variant-b/pitch-dry-comedy.md) in [Plot 3](variant-b/plot-3-immediate-consequence.md)).

This Sourcebook documents the Director’s staging decision to govern middle-Plot Pitch changes through an **explicit Promptbook handoff** rather than implicit context accumulation: at every Plot boundary, the Stage Voice resolves whether the incoming Plot inherits the Play’s default Pitch or links a scene override, scopes each isolated performer context to **only** the currently active Pitch (`Tenor`, `Undertow`, `Nerve`, `Echo`), explicitly instructs performers to discard the outgoing Pitch when a boundary swaps register, and logs each transition while carrying all physical states and spoken or silent commitments forward unbroken.

## Origin

- **Director skill ([`SKILL.md`](../../../skills/khai-director/SKILL.md)).** Requires the Director to apply the active Pitch to pacing, attention, and staging; carry established facts, consequences, and unresolved Stakes from one Plot into the next; withhold knowledge from performers until it becomes available in play; and keep authored files fixed within a `0%` staging limit. It defines *what* must hold across Plots, motivating an explicit operational mechanism in the Promptbook for *how* persistent performer contexts release an outgoing Pitch at a Plot boundary.
- **Shared Promptbook ([`promptbook.md`](promptbook.md)).** Defines the reusable silent-witness `0%` chamber staging shared by both rehearsal variants, pairing isolated per-Persona contexts and front/rear sightline asymmetry with explicit Plot-boundary Pitch replacement and transition logging.
- **All-comic baseline run ([`01-all-comic-r1-a.md`](../04-runs/01-all-comic-r1-a.md)).** Establishes the control behavior under [`promptbook.md`](promptbook.md) when all three Plots inherit [Dry Comedy](variant-a/pitch-dry-comedy.md) ([Pitch table](../04-runs/01-all-comic-r1-a.md#plot-by-plot-pitch-table); [boundary log](../04-runs/01-all-comic-r1-a.md#director-pitch-transition--boundary-log)). Re-confirming `Dry Comedy` at `Plot 1 → Plot 2` and `Plot 2 → Plot 3` kept both carriers in brisk parade-logistics register across the child’s question (`"Flagstones by the fountain wouldn't take the rest of him..."` / `"Parade permit only covers one left boot through the east arch—step back behind the rope, kid"`) while advancing the softening heel patch and maintaining sightline asymmetry.
- **Mixed-Pitch rehearsal run ([`02-mixed-wonder-r1-b.md`](../04-runs/02-mixed-wonder-r1-b.md)).** Tests the two-way register handoff (`Dry Comedy → Quiet Wonder → Dry Comedy`) on identical world facts, Personas, triggers, and `0%` ceiling ([Pitch table](../04-runs/02-mixed-wonder-r1-b.md#plot-by-plot-active-pitch-table); [boundary transitions](../04-runs/02-mixed-wonder-r1-b.md#1-active-pitch-scoping--boundary-transitions)). Explicitly discarding `Dry Comedy` for `Quiet Wonder` at `Plot 1 → Plot 2` shifted both carriers cleanly into grave, eye-level attentiveness (`"He takes a long stride between towns..."` / `"Stand right behind the white line—it takes the whole lane just to set his first foot down straight"`) as they lifted the boot an inch higher; explicitly discarding `Quiet Wonder` to restore `Dry Comedy` at `Plot 2 → Plot 3` returned both carriers immediately to deadpan marshaling practicality (`"this boot's riding my forearm to the tailgate"`) while carrying forward the added load of Plot 2’s lift and spoken promise.
- **LORE warrant architecture ([`reference.md`](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai/blob/c8c56c7e843eac2eb33709d06ad1d7aca4dd8c00/packages/khai-arch/architecture/reference.md)).** Supplies the four-part evidentiary structure (`Line of Work`, `Origin`, `Restrictions`, `Encoding`) separating staging rationale and scope limits here from the working instructions inside [`promptbook.md`](promptbook.md).

## Restrictions

- **Supported claims:**
  - *Clean bidirectional Pitch switching without register bleed:* In isolated two-performer subagent runs over three short Plots (`6` total cast turns), explicitly replacing the outgoing Pitch in each performer’s prompt at `Plot 1 → Plot 2` and `Plot 2 → Plot 3` prevented Plot 1’s deadpan briskness from flattening Plot 2 and prevented Plot 2’s hushed wonder from softening Plot 3 ([`02-mixed-wonder-r1-b.md`](../04-runs/02-mixed-wonder-r1-b.md#1-active-pitch-scoping--boundary-transitions)).
  - *Single Promptbook reuse across inherited and overridden Plots:* One unedited [`promptbook.md`](promptbook.md) staged both uniform default inheritance (`Variant A`) and a middle-Plot scene override (`Variant B`) at a `0%` staging limit without touching authored Play files or reading unlinked Pitch files.
  - *Register shifts preserve physical and narrative continuity:* Swapping active Pitch changed delivery, cadence, and pause weight while keeping the single-boot premise, progressive flour-paste heel buckle, front/rear sightline asymmetry, and verbatim child cue intact across Plot boundaries ([`02-mixed-wonder-r1-b.md`](../04-runs/02-mixed-wonder-r1-b.md#2-world-fact-sightline-asymmetry--trigger-verification)).
- **Open questions and adaptations needing rehearsal:**
  - *Implicit-append ablation:* Neither rehearsal ran a negative control that appended Plot 2’s Pitch without explicitly instructing subagents to discard Plot 1’s Pitch, or that ran both Personas in a shared context; the exact failure rate without explicit replacement remains unmeasured.
  - *Layered object Pitches:* Neither variant linked a `Persona`, `Place`, or `Piece` Pitch alongside a `Plot` Pitch. How an explicit Plot-boundary handoff composes with persistent Persona- or Place-level Pitches still requires rehearsal.
  - *Broader staging conditions:* Evidence covers silent-witness `0%` staging with two performers, fixed cues, and one middle-Plot override (`Dry Comedy ↔ Quiet Wonder`). Multi-carrier scenes, consecutive distinct overrides (`Pitch A → Pitch B → Pitch C`), mid-Plot Pitch triggers, imported `TO LOAN` Performances, and interactive Human participation above a `0%` staging limit still need dedicated rehearsal.
- **Boundaries to carry forward:**
  - *Never let Pitch rewrite facts or erase consequences:* Future Playwrights and Directors must keep Pitch strictly to register (`Tenor`, `Undertow`, `Nerve`, `Echo`); world facts, spatial geometry, sightline filters, and physical or spoken commitments from prior Plots always carry across the boundary unchanged.
  - *Scope performer prompts to the current Plot’s Pitch only:* Do not preload upcoming Plot Pitches at Curtain-up, read unlinked Pitch files, or leave a superseded Plot override active in performer instructions once the next Plot begins.
  - *Keep Pitch selection in authored links under `0%` limits:* Default Pitches belong in `play.md` and scene overrides in the relevant `Plot` file; the Promptbook executes and logs those authored links rather than inventing unauthored tonal turns.
