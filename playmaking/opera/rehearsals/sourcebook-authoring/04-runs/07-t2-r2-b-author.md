# Sourcebook: Explicit Promptbook Pitch Handoff in *The Giant’s Shoe*

## Line of Work

The mixed-pitch rehearsals of *The Giant’s Shoe* test whether a single [Promptbook](promptbook.md) at a **0% staging limit** can stage two three-Plot variants of the same Play across isolated performer contexts (`Tomas` at the rear heel counter and `Maren` at the front toe welt): an all-comic baseline where all three Plots inherit the Play-wide `Dry Comedy` Pitch, and a mixed-Pitch variant where Plot 2 overrides the default with `Quiet Wonder` before Plot 3 returns to `Dry Comedy`.

This Sourcebook explains the Director’s staging decision to require an **explicit Promptbook Pitch handoff** at every Plot boundary—scoping each performer’s isolated context to *only* the currently active Pitch, logging the active Pitch and its source file at each boundary, and explicitly discarding and replacing the outgoing Pitch whenever a Plot enters a scene-level override (`Plot 1 → Plot 2`) or restores the Play-wide default (`Plot 2 → Plot 3`).

## Origin

- **[Director skill](../../../skills/khai-director/SKILL.md):** Instructs the Director to apply the active Pitch to tune pacing, attention, and staging; carry established facts, consequences, and unresolved Stakes across Plots; withhold knowledge from performers until it becomes available in play; and keep authored material fixed under a `0%` staging limit. Because persistent isolated performer subagents retain prior turn prompts, introducing a new Pitch without explicitly evicting the previous one risks leaving an out-of-scope Pitch active alongside the current one, while preloading all Pitches at Curtain-up would leak upcoming register shifts early.
- **[Shared Promptbook](promptbook.md):** Establishes the reusable three-Plot chamber rules—isolated performer contexts, front/rear sightline asymmetry, exact verbatim delivery of `"Is the giant coming?"`, and explicit Plot-boundary Pitch replacement—applied unchanged across both rehearsal runs.
- **[All-comic rehearsal run (`01-all-comic-r1-a.md`)](../04-runs/01-all-comic-r1-a.md):** Baseline run of Variant A ([pitch table](../04-runs/01-all-comic-r1-a.md#plot-by-plot-pitch-table) and [boundary log](../04-runs/01-all-comic-r1-a.md#director-pitch-transition--boundary-log)), where `Dry Comedy` remains inherited across all three Plots. Re-confirming `Dry Comedy` at each Plot boundary held both carriers in dry parade-marshaling practicality during [Plot 2](../04-runs/01-all-comic-r1-a.md#plot-2-question-and-response) (`"Flagstones by the fountain wouldn't take the rest of him"`, `"Parade permit only covers one left boot through the east arch"`) while Tuesday night’s flour-paste heel patch progressively yielded.
- **[Mixed-Pitch rehearsal run (`02-mixed-wonder-r1-b.md`)](../04-runs/02-mixed-wonder-r1-b.md):** Comparative run of Variant B ([active Pitch table](../04-runs/02-mixed-wonder-r1-b.md#plot-by-plot-active-pitch-table) and [boundary log](../04-runs/02-mixed-wonder-r1-b.md#director-pitch-transition--boundary-log)), where Plot 2 links `Quiet Wonder` and Plot 3 inherits `Dry Comedy`. Explicitly instructing both performers at `Plot 1 → Plot 2` to discard `Dry Comedy` and adopt `Quiet Wonder` shifted [Plot 2](../04-runs/02-mixed-wonder-r1-b.md#plot-2-question-and-response) cleanly into quiet, eye-level gravity (`"He takes a long stride between towns, so his left boot gets into the square ahead of him"`) paired with a matching one-inch lift of the boot. Explicitly discarding `Quiet Wonder` and restoring `Dry Comedy` at `Plot 2 → Plot 3` snapped [Plot 3](../04-runs/02-mixed-wonder-r1-b.md#plot-3-immediate-consequence) back into brisk structural urgency (`"this boot's riding my forearm to the tailgate"`) while carrying forward both the extra load from Plot 2’s lift and the spoken promise made beside the chalk line.

## Restrictions

- **Supported claims:**
  - Explicit Plot-boundary Pitch replacement lets one unchanged `0%`-limit Promptbook stage both uniform-Pitch inheritance and a middle-Plot Pitch override (`Dry Comedy → Quiet Wonder → Dry Comedy`) without lingering register bleed into Plot 3.
  - Scoping Pitch swaps strictly to delivery and attention (`Tenor`, `Undertow`, `Nerve`, `Echo`) shifts performer cadence and response register while preserving authored world facts (single eight-foot left boot, no giant built), front/rear sightline asymmetry, and continuous physical load progression across Plots.
- **Open questions and unrehearsed adaptations:**
  - *Sample size and chamber scale:* Evidence comes from a single comparative pair of two-performer, six-turn silent-witness runs (`01-all-comic-r1-a.md` and `02-mixed-wonder-r1-b.md`). Multi-run variance, larger casts, and longer arcs with repeated Pitch oscillations still need rehearsal.
  - *Object-level and mid-Plot Pitch shifts:* Neither variant linked Persona-, Place-, or Piece-level Pitches, and Pitch changed only at Plot boundaries under a `0%` staging limit. Competing simultaneous Pitches across Personas or mid-Plot Pitch transitions triggered by live Human input (`> 0%` staging limit) are not yet tested.
  - *Observable turn-history echo:* Evicting an outgoing Pitch removes its directive block from performer prompts, but performers still retain the observable transcript of prior turns shaped under that Pitch. How strongly accumulated dialogue pulls register in longer scenes remains open.
- **Boundaries for future collaborators:**
  - Never preload upcoming Plot Pitches before their Plot opens, and always pair entering or exiting a Plot-level Pitch override with an explicit instruction to discard the prior Pitch—especially on the return boundary back to the Play-wide default.
  - Keep Pitch transitions strictly separate from world-fact continuity and sightline filtering: a Pitch swap may change how carriers answer or hold stillness, never what exists in the square, what physical damage has accrued, or what the front carrier can see behind her.

## Encoding

The working staging instructions remain in [Promptbook](promptbook.md):

- **[`promptbook.md` — `## Agent`](promptbook.md#agent):** Encodes the Director’s boundary transition rule (start on the Play’s default Pitch, switch to a Plot-linked Pitch for that Plot’s duration, return cleanly to the Play default on the next inheriting Plot, and log active Pitch, source file, and transition at every boundary) and binds Cast Performers to the currently active Pitch for observable speech, silence, or action only.
- **[`promptbook.md` — `## Collaboration`](promptbook.md#collaboration):** Encodes continuous state transfer across `Plot 1`, `Plot 2`, and `Plot 3` so grip, route, heel-seam strain, and spoken or silent commitments persist across the same Plot boundaries where the active Pitch swaps.
- **[`promptbook.md` — `## Knowledge`](promptbook.md#knowledge):** Encodes **Separate performer contexts & active-Pitch scoping**—supplying only the active Pitch (`Tenor`, `Undertow`, `Nerve`, `Echo`) for the current Plot and requiring explicit replacement of the prior Pitch in each performer’s prompt on every Pitch-changing boundary—alongside **Physical sightline asymmetry** and **Public evidence**.
- **[`promptbook.md` — `## System`](promptbook.md#system):** Encodes the `0%` staging limit, verbatim child cue (`“Is the giant coming?”`), equal footing for speech, action, and silence, and the **Pitch tunes register, never facts** guardrail that keeps authored files read-only and world facts invariant across Pitch handoffs.
