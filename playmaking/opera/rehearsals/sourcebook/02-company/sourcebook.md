# Sourcebook

## Line of Work

- The nine standard Tenors give Playwrights and Directors a starting register for authoring or choosing a Pitch. A Pitch can be linked at Play, Plot, or element scope.
- The mixed-Pitch example gives the Director a way to direct attention to the square, Tomas, and the shoe at different registers within one scene.

## Origin

- The [legacy KHAI Pitch defaults](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai/blob/c8c56c7e843eac2eb33709d06ad1d7aca4dd8c00/packages/khai-arch/defaults/pitch.md) supply the nine labels used in Opera. The legacy page gives no citation for their relationship to Navarasa. [Phillip B. Zarrilli’s article on Indian performance](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0266464X11000455) compares nine rasas with their associated performer states in the table on page 258.
- [*The Giant’s Shoe* rehearsal observations](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS/blob/068605d97afa13f6ff07d7cb74ada52c92a61474/playmaking/opera/rehearsals/pitch/03-observations/README.md) record four isolated runs. The [Company and Pitch files](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS/blob/068605d97afa13f6ff07d7cb74ada52c92a61474/playmaking/opera/rehearsals/pitch/02-company/README.md) identify dry comedy and quiet wonder as the two Tenors tested.

## Restrictions

- The correspondence with Navarasa supports a comparison. The available sources leave the origin of the KHAI list unconfirmed.
- Each rehearsal run used one shared Pitch. The records support observations about those runs. They leave open how distinct Play, Place, Persona, and Piece Pitches work together.

## Encoding

- The opening and nine-item list in [`pitch-defaults.md`](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS/blob/dc24d4e71ff8364061afe18bfdc75187b6287787/playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/references/pitch-defaults.md#standard-tenors) turn the legacy Tenors into starters for authoring a Pitch. The file asks the author to supply Undertow, Nerve, and Echo.
- The [mixed-Pitch example in the same file](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS/blob/dc24d4e71ff8364061afe18bfdc75187b6287787/playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/references/pitch-defaults.md#several-pitches-in-one-scene) proposes comic for the Play, dread for the square, tender for Tomas, and wondrous for the Piece. The Piece cue gives the shoe a moment to register at the child’s eye level. This is proposed staging guidance.
