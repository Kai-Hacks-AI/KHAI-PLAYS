# Sourcebook

## Line of Work

- The nine standard Tenors give Playwrights and Directors a starting register for authoring or choosing a Pitch. A Pitch can be linked at Play, Plot, or element scope.
- The mixed-Pitch example gives the Director a way to direct attention to the square, Tomas, and the shoe at different registers within one scene.

## Origin

- The [legacy KHAI Pitch defaults](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai/blob/c8c56c7e843eac2eb33709d06ad1d7aca4dd8c00/packages/khai-arch/defaults/pitch.md) supply the nine labels used in Opera. The legacy page gives no citation for their relationship to Navarasa. [Phillip B. Zarrilli’s article on Indian performance](https://doi.org/10.1017/S0266464X11000455) compares nine rasas with their associated performer states in the table on page 258.
- The [original rehearsal](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS/blob/068605d97afa13f6ff07d7cb74ada52c92a61474/playmaking/opera/rehearsals/pitch/03-observations/README.md) used one shared Pitch per run. [PR #25’s SCORE observations](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS/blob/3c93faece59162b082fd3b50f31aabbcbdd4615b/playmaking/opera/rehearsals/mixed-pitch/03-observations/README.md) record four runs of a three-Plot adaptation. Both mixed runs used Dry Comedy, then Quiet Wonder, then Dry Comedy again.

## Restrictions

- The correspondence with Navarasa supports a comparison. The available sources leave the origin of the KHAI list unconfirmed.
- [PR #25’s evolution record](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS/blob/3c93faece59162b082fd3b50f31aabbcbdd4615b/playmaking/opera/rehearsals/mixed-pitch/05-evolution/README.md) scopes that result to a Play-wide default and one Plot override with an explicit Promptbook handoff. The Play, Place, Persona, and Piece example combines scopes within one scene and remains proposed staging guidance.

## Encoding

- The opening and nine-item list in [`pitch-defaults.md`](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS/blob/3c93faece59162b082fd3b50f31aabbcbdd4615b/playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/references/pitch-defaults.md#standard-tenors) turn the legacy Tenors into starters for authoring a Pitch. The file asks the author to supply Undertow, Nerve, and Echo.
- The [mixed-Pitch example in the same file](https://github.com/Kai-Hacks-AI/KHAI-PLAYS/blob/3c93faece59162b082fd3b50f31aabbcbdd4615b/playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/references/pitch-defaults.md#several-pitches-in-one-scene) proposes comic for the Play, dread for the square, tender for Tomas, and wondrous for the Piece. The Piece cue gives the shoe a moment to register at the child’s eye level. This is proposed staging guidance.
