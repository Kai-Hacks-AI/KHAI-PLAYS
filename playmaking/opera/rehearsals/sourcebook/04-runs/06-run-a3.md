# Review of Draft Opera Guidance: `TO TUNE` and Standard Tenors

---

## 1. Assessment of “The nine KHAI Tenors were derived from Navarasa.”

**Verdict:** **Revise (unsupported causal attribution).** The inspected sources establish a conceptual parallel with *Navarasa*, not historical derivation. The claim should be bounded to documented provenance (`ChBrain/khai` legacy defaults) and, at most, a structural comparison.

### Analysis
- **Purpose of the list:** In `playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/references/pitch-defaults.md#standard-tenors` (lines 1–15), the nine standard Tenors (`grieving`, `comic`, `tender`, `furious`, `heroic`, `dread`, `abject`, `wondrous`, `still`) give Playwrights and Directors a starting dominant register for authoring or tuning a Pitch at Play, Plot, or element/object scope. A Tenor shapes pacing, attention, and delivery while preserving authored facts and performer agency; the author completes the Pitch by supplying `Undertow`, `Nerve`, and `Echo`. Upstream in `packages/khai-arch/defaults/pitch.md#pitch-defaults-the-standard-registers-to-tune-from` (`ChBrain/khai` @ `c8c56c7`, lines 3–29), the same nine labels define the default `Tenor` keys within the four-facet `TO TUNE` type without changing fixed events.
- **Evidence for the claim:** `playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/references/pitch-defaults.md` (line 28) attributes the Opera list to the legacy KHAI defaults (`packages/khai-arch/defaults/pitch.md` in `ChBrain/khai`). While the nine Tenors closely mirror the nine classical Indian *rasas* (*Navarasa*)—and the packet notes cite Phillip B. Zarrilli’s table comparing nine *rasas* with performer states (`https://doi.org/10.1017/S0266464X11000455`, p. 258)—that evidence supports a correspondence rather than documented lineage.
- **Limits on attribution:** `packages/khai-arch/defaults/pitch.md` (`ChBrain/khai`) gives no citation, note, or mention of *Navarasa*, leaving the historical origin of the KHAI list unconfirmed. Furthermore, Zarrilli’s article (`https://doi.org/10.1017/S0266464X11000455`) could not be inspected directly (offline/inaccessible external DOI link).
- **Where to preserve or change wording:**
  - **Preserve** the existing provenance footer in `playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/references/pitch-defaults.md` (line 28): `Adapted from the [legacy KHAI Pitch defaults](https://github.com/ChBrain/khai/blob/c8c56c7e843eac2eb33709d06ad1d7aca4dd8c00/packages/khai-arch/defaults/pitch.md).`
  - **Change** the proposed sentence wherever draft Opera guidance introduces or annotates `playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/references/pitch-defaults.md#standard-tenors` so it does not assert derivation.

### Evidence Chain
1. **Purpose:** Provide Playwrights and Directors nine starter dominant registers (`Tenor`) for tuning a Pitch at Play, Plot, or element scope while leaving authored facts and performer choices intact, to be completed with `Undertow`, `Nerve`, and `Echo` (`playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/references/pitch-defaults.md#standard-tenors`, lines 3, 15).
2. **Source:** Opera’s list in `playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/references/pitch-defaults.md#standard-tenors` (lines 5–13, 28) is adapted directly from `packages/khai-arch/defaults/pitch.md#pitch-defaults-the-standard-registers-to-tune-from` (`ChBrain/khai` @ `c8c56c7`, lines 17–28); the packet compares the nine registers to the nine *rasas* tabulated in Phillip B. Zarrilli (`https://doi.org/10.1017/S0266464X11000455`, p. 258).
3. **Limits:** `packages/khai-arch/defaults/pitch.md` contains zero mention or citation of *Navarasa*, and Zarrilli’s DOI source could not be inspected directly; therefore, only a structural comparison is supported and derivation remains unconfirmed.
4. **Proposed Edit (`playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/references/pitch-defaults.md#standard-tenors` / draft `TO TUNE` guidance):**
   - Preserve line 28 of `pitch-defaults.md` as written, and replace *“The nine KHAI Tenors were derived from Navarasa.”* in draft guidance with:
     > “The nine standard Tenors are adapted from the legacy KHAI Pitch defaults; their nine registers correspond closely to the nine *rasas* (*Navarasa*), though the legacy KHAI source records no citation confirming their origin.”

## 2. Assessment of “The rehearsal demonstrated that the Play, Place, Persona, and Piece can carry distinct Pitches in the same scene.”

**Verdict:** **Revise (conflates rehearsed sequential Plot overrides with untested proposed staging guidance).** Neither rehearsal staged concurrent Play, Place, Persona, and Piece Pitches inside one scene; that four-scope combination appears only as an illustrative authoring/staging example.

### Analysis
- **Director’s intent for the shoe (`Piece`):** In `playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/references/pitch-defaults.md#several-pitches-in-one-scene` (lines 19–26), the `Piece · Wondrous` example—*“Give the oversized shoe a moment to register at the child’s eye level.”*—gives the Director a scoped attentional cue so the eight-foot boot registers with awe at the child’s eye level inside the same scene where the crossing stays deadpan logistics (`Play · Comic`), the square builds route/brass pressure (`Place · Dread`), and Tomas’s handling of the fragile heel reads with care (`Persona · Tender`), without altering the boot’s authored properties or Tomas’s agency to choose his response.
- **What the rehearsals actually demonstrated:**
  1. **Single-Pitch rehearsal (`playmaking/opera/rehearsals/pitch/03-observations/README.md`, `KHAI-PLAYS` @ `068605d`):** Four isolated single-scene runs (`2 × 2`) of *The Giant’s Shoe* tuned the entire Play with **one shared `pitch.md` per run** (`Dry comedy` vs. `Quiet wonder`, changing only the `Tenor` sentence while holding `Taxonomy`, `Owner`, `Undertow`, `Nerve`, and `Echo` identical; lines 3–5, `#1-line-gesture-and-silence-across-all-four-runs-2--2--4`, `#2-how-the-question-lands-across-tenors-dry-comedy-vs-quiet-wonder`). Line 5 explicitly notes that separate Persona-linked or multi-Pitch scopes *“remain untested here.”*
  2. **Mixed-Pitch three-Plot rehearsal (`playmaking/opera/rehearsals/mixed-pitch/03-observations/README.md` and `playmaking/opera/rehearsals/mixed-pitch/05-evolution/README.md`, `KHAI-PLAYS` @ `3c93fae`, PR #25):** Four runs (`2 × 2`) of a three-Plot adaptation at `Staging limit: 0%` tested a **Play-wide default (`Dry Comedy`) with a single middle-Plot override (`Quiet Wonder` on Plot 2) and return to default (`Dry Comedy` on Plot 3)** (`mixed-pitch/03-observations/README.md#1-four-run-pitch--consequence-matrix`, lines 9–15; `#2-observable-register-shifts-across-the-three-plots`, lines 18–29). In both mixed Condition B runs (`02`, `03`), Plot 2 shifted cleanly into `Quiet Wonder` (Maren’s finger-tap stilled, carriers lifted/glided the boot and answered `“Is the giant coming?”` gravely at eye level) and Plot 3 snapped cleanly back to `Dry Comedy` logistics with zero lingering wondrous phrasing while preserving physical consequences from Plot 2 (`mixed-pitch/03-observations/README.md#3-world-fact-trigger-continuity--agency-verification`, line 36).
- **Which parts of the claim remain open:**
  1. **Concurrent multi-object Pitches in one scene/turn:** `playmaking/opera/rehearsals/mixed-pitch/05-evolution/README.md#3-scope-limits-of-this-rehearsal` (line 30) explicitly marks simultaneous multi-object Pitches within the same Plot (`Play · Comic` + `Place · Dread` + `Persona · Tender` + `Piece · Wondrous`) as **“Not yet rehearsed here,”** leaving open how a Director arbitrates concurrent object-scoped Pitches inside a single turn.
  2. **Unassisted Director skill transitions:** `playmaking/opera/rehearsals/mixed-pitch/05-evolution/README.md#1-did-the-updated-guides-support-the-switch-and-return` (line 9) and `#2-smallest-optional-revision-warranted-by-the-evidence` (lines 20–23) note that clean Plot-boundary switching relied on explicit Pitch-transition instructions in `02-company/promptbook.md` and does not independently prove `khai-director/SKILL.md` alone produces those transitions without an optional Plot-boundary update line.
- **Where the mixed-Pitch decision appears:**
  - **Four-scope same-scene example (proposed guidance):** `playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/references/pitch-defaults.md#several-pitches-in-one-scene` (lines 17–26, framed with *“imagine a comic Play-wide Pitch alongside three Pitches linked to individual objects”*).
  - **Scoped rehearsal boundary & status record:** `playmaking/opera/rehearsals/mixed-pitch/05-evolution/README.md#1-did-the-updated-guides-support-the-switch-and-return` (lines 7–12) and `#3-scope-limits-of-this-rehearsal` (lines 27–30).

### Evidence Chain
1. **Purpose:** Enable scoped register control across a work—and, in the `Piece · Wondrous` example, direct staging attention so the oversized shoe registers at the child’s eye level alongside distinct registers for the Play (`Comic`), square (`Dread`), and Tomas (`Tender`) without changing world facts or performer agency (`playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/references/pitch-defaults.md#several-pitches-in-one-scene`, lines 19–26).
2. **Source:** Single-Pitch runs in `playmaking/opera/rehearsals/pitch/03-observations/README.md` (lines 3–5); sequential three-Plot `Dry Comedy → Quiet Wonder → Dry Comedy` runs in `playmaking/opera/rehearsals/mixed-pitch/03-observations/README.md#1-four-run-pitch--consequence-matrix` and `#2-observable-register-shifts-across-the-three-plots`; scope summary in `playmaking/opera/rehearsals/mixed-pitch/05-evolution/README.md#1-did-the-updated-guides-support-the-switch-and-return` and `#3-scope-limits-of-this-rehearsal`; hypothetical four-scope scene in `playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/references/pitch-defaults.md#several-pitches-in-one-scene`.
3. **Limits:** Rehearsals tested only (a) one shared Play-wide Pitch per run and (b) a Play-wide default overridden on a middle Plot (`Dry Comedy → Quiet Wonder → Dry Comedy`) at `Staging limit: 0%` with explicit `promptbook.md` handoff instructions; simultaneous `Play`, `Place`, `Persona`, and `Piece` Pitches within one scene remain unrehearsed (`mixed-pitch/05-evolution/README.md#3-scope-limits-of-this-rehearsal`, lines 29–30).
4. **Proposed Status Wording & Staging Cue:**
   - **Status wording (to replace the proposed sentence in draft Opera guidance / rehearsal summaries):**
     > “Rehearsals demonstrated a Play-wide default Pitch (`Dry Comedy`) switching cleanly to a middle-Plot override (`Quiet Wonder`) and back with explicit Promptbook handoff instructions; combining distinct Pitches for the Play, Place, Persona, and Piece within the same scene—including a `Wondrous` Piece cue giving the shoe a moment to register at the child’s eye level—remains proposed staging guidance (`pitch-defaults.md#several-pitches-in-one-scene`) and has not yet been rehearsed.”
   - **Authoring / staging cue preservation:** Keep `playmaking/opera/skills/khai-playwright/references/pitch-defaults.md#several-pitches-in-one-scene` (line 19) framed explicitly in the hypothetical (*“imagine a comic Play-wide Pitch alongside three Pitches linked to individual objects”*) unless and until a multi-object single-scene rehearsal tests concurrent Director arbitration.
