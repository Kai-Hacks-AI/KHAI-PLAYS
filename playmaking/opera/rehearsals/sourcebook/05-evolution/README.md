# Evolution — What to Keep or Revise in `references/sourcebook.md`

Per [01-setup/README.md](../01-setup/README.md#L34-L36), this rehearsal leaves [playmaking/opera/references/sourcebook.md](../../../references/sourcebook.md) untouched and records evidence-backed recommendations tied to specific reviewer responses in `04-runs/`.

---

## 1. Summary Judgment on the Four-Chapter Format

Across `2 × 4 = 8` isolated reviewer runs ([03-observations/README.md](../03-observations/README.md#L14-L33)), adding the four Sourcebook chapter headings (`Line of Work`, `Origin`, `Restrictions`, `Encoding`) over an already LORE-ordered 8-bullet packet **tied plain source notes at the `8.0 / 8` primary ceiling** (`8–8` range and `0 / 4` unsupported claims in both arms; `7.5` vs. `8.0` median under the strict literal `D2-Encoding` check).

- **Do not claim reader-side superiority from headings alone:** When an author has already written complete `Purpose → Origin → Restrictions → Encoding` bullets with pinned links, independent reviewers trace both Playwright and Director decisions equally well with or without the four `##` headings ([01-run-a1.md](../04-runs/01-run-a1.md), [04-run-a2.md](../04-runs/04-run-a2.md), [06-run-a3.md](../04-runs/06-run-a3.md), [07-run-a4.md](../04-runs/07-run-a4.md)).
- **Keep the four LORE chapters as the Sourcebook’s authoring structure.** The rehearsal tested how reviewers read complete packets. Whether the chapter prompts help authors capture intent, sources, limits, and file-level choices when starting from raw artifacts remains an open question.

## 2. What to Keep in `references/sourcebook.md`

1. **Keep the four-chapter order (`Line of Work` → `Origin` → `Restrictions` → `Encoding`, [references/sourcebook.md](../../../references/sourcebook.md#L5-L21)):**
   - Every run (`01`–`08`) achieved `4/4` on Decision 1 and `4/4` primary on Decision 2 with `0` unsupported claims. Both packets supplied the same intent, sources, evidence boundaries, and file-level choices in the same order.
   - In Condition B, [08-run-b4.md](../04-runs/08-run-b4.md#L14) actively used the chapter heading as a citation anchor (`"As summarized in the packet’s Sourcebook (#origin)"`) when attributing the offline Zarrilli source (`https://doi.org/10.1017/S0266464X11000455`).
2. **Keep `Restrictions` explicit about unconfirmed lineage, uninspected external sources, and unrehearsed adaptations ([references/sourcebook.md](../../../references/sourcebook.md#L15-L17)):**
   - With the same restriction notes in both packets, all eight reviewers avoided inflating the *Navarasa* parallel into historical derivation ([01-run-a1.md](../04-runs/01-run-a1.md#L17-L18), [02-run-b1.md](../04-runs/02-run-b1.md#L18-L19), [03-run-b2.md](../04-runs/03-run-b2.md#L18), [04-run-a2.md](../04-runs/04-run-a2.md#L14-L15), [05-run-b3.md](../04-runs/05-run-b3.md#L22), [06-run-a3.md](../04-runs/06-run-a3.md#L12), [07-run-a4.md](../04-runs/07-run-a4.md#L14-L15), [08-run-b4.md](../04-runs/08-run-b4.md#L16-L17)) or misreporting PR #25’s sequential Plot override as proof of concurrent Play/Place/Persona/Piece tuning.
3. **Keep `Encoding`’s rule that working instructions live in the target object, not the Sourcebook ([references/sourcebook.md](../../../references/sourcebook.md#L21)):**
   - All eight reviewers pointed edits or preservation checks directly to [pitch-defaults.md](../../../skills/khai-playwright/references/pitch-defaults.md) (`#standard-tenors` and `#several-pitches-in-one-scene`) rather than treating the packet itself as runtime Playwright or Director guidance.

## 3. Candidate Revisions to Consider in a Follow-Up Reference PR

1. **Clarify `Encoding` when a linked passage encodes a tentative proposal/example rather than a confirmed rule ([references/sourcebook.md](../../../references/sourcebook.md#L19-L21)):**
   - **Evidence from runs:** [references/sourcebook.md](../../../references/sourcebook.md#L21) currently instructs authors to *"Link to the file and section where each supported idea becomes a choice someone can use."* When Decision 2’s `## Encoding` bullet ([02-company/sourcebook.md](../02-company/sourcebook.md#L21)) pointed to an *unrehearsed illustrative proposal* (`Piece · Wondrous` in `pitch-defaults.md#several-pitches-in-one-scene`), two Condition B reviewers—[03-run-b2.md](../04-runs/03-run-b2.md#L41-L44) and [05-run-b3.md](../04-runs/05-run-b3.md#L57-L59) (along with Condition A’s [01-run-a1.md](../04-runs/01-run-a1.md#L44-L48))—summarized the four-scope example collectively as proposed guidance and supplied the rehearsed `khai-director/SKILL.md` (`L9`) Plot-handoff cue from [mixed-pitch/05-evolution/README.md](../../mixed-pitch/05-evolution/README.md#L20-L23) as their concrete staging cue, whereas [02-run-b1.md](../04-runs/02-run-b1.md#L49-L51), [04-run-a2.md](../04-runs/04-run-a2.md#L40-L42), [06-run-a3.md](../04-runs/06-run-a3.md#L49-L50), [07-run-a4.md](../04-runs/07-run-a4.md#L43-L46), and [08-run-b4.md](../04-runs/08-run-b4.md#L44-L45) explicitly preserved the `Piece · Wondrous` eye-level shoe cue itself as a marked proposal.
   - **Candidate revision (`## Encoding`):** Allow `Encoding` to distinguish **supported choices** from **illustrative proposals carried in documentation**—for example, adding: *"If a linked example or cue remains proposed rather than rehearsed, name the cue and say how the target file marks it as tentative."*
2. **Keep one decision per Sourcebook—or sub-label decisions consistently across chapters when bundling ([references/sourcebook.md](../../../references/sourcebook.md#L3-L9)):**
   - **Evidence from runs:** [02-company/sourcebook.md](../02-company/sourcebook.md#L3-L21) bundled a Playwright provenance decision (Tenors) and a Director staging decision (the shoe) inside one Sourcebook, interleaving `[Decision 1, Decision 2]` inside each of the four chapters (`L5/L10/L15/L20` vs. `L6/L11/L16/L21`). Every Condition B reviewer ([02-run-b1.md](../04-runs/02-run-b1.md), [03-run-b2.md](../04-runs/03-run-b2.md), [05-run-b3.md](../04-runs/05-run-b3.md), [08-run-b4.md](../04-runs/08-run-b4.md)) had to re-collate bullets across all four chapters to reconstruct each decision’s chain.
   - **Candidate revision (opening / `## Line of Work`):** Note that a Sourcebook normally documents **one** cohesive design or staging decision, and if a collection Sourcebook covers more than one decision, each bullet should carry a short consistent decision label across all four chapters so chains do not interleave silently.
3. **Require explicit disclosure in `Origin` / `Restrictions` when a secondary source was cited indirectly rather than inspected directly ([references/sourcebook.md](../../../references/sourcebook.md#L11-L17)):**
   - **Evidence from runs:** Although all eight reviewers flagged `https://doi.org/10.1017/S0266464X11000455` as inaccessible in their review notes, three reviewers ([01-run-a1.md](../04-runs/01-run-a1.md#L22), [02-run-b1.md](../04-runs/02-run-b1.md#L23), [08-run-b4.md](../04-runs/08-run-b4.md#L21)) still carried `(cf. Zarrilli, p. 258)` into their suggested guidance sentence, while five reviewers ([03-run-b2.md](../04-runs/03-run-b2.md#L22), [04-run-a2.md](../04-runs/04-run-a2.md#L19), [05-run-b3.md](../04-runs/05-run-b3.md#L27), [06-run-a3.md](../04-runs/06-run-a3.md#L23), [07-run-a4.md](../04-runs/07-run-a4.md#L19)) omitted unverified page citations from downstream guidance prose.
   - **Candidate revision (`## Origin` / `## Restrictions`):** State that if a source was not inspected firsthand (paywalled, offline, or known only via secondary citation), `Restrictions` must record that inspection limit so downstream `Encoding` does not propagate an unverified citation as firsthand authority.
4. **If rehearsing the Sourcebook again, test *authoring* from raw artifacts rather than *reading* pre-ordered bullets:**
   - Because [plain-notes.md](../02-company/plain-notes.md) and [sourcebook.md](../02-company/sourcebook.md) shared identical LORE-ordered bullets ([01-setup/README.md](../01-setup/README.md#L11)), Condition A received `Line of Work`, `Origin`, `Restrictions`, and `Encoding` content without headings, producing an `8/8` ceiling. A decisive follow-up test would compare rationale notes **authored** from raw rehearsal files with vs. without [references/sourcebook.md](../../../references/sourcebook.md), then score downstream reviewer traceability on the resulting author-generated packets.
