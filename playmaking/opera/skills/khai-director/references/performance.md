# Performance — TO LOAN

Use these headings in order. Make one card per captured Performance. Keep `performance.md` beside the captured `transcript.md` in one Archive folder.

## Taxonomy

Link the Play that was performed.

## Owner

Name the Director responsible for the Performance.

## Lodged

## Occasion

Identify this Performance by its date, venue, active Pitch, and the Human’s conditions where supplied.

## Aftermath

Record what the Performance settled, what failed, and what remains open. Use what the Performance established.

## Note

Record what the lenders or a later production should know.
