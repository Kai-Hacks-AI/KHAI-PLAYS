# System

State the binding rules that override the agents' defaults. If the performance can switch modes, say who can request that and what changes when they do.

## Staging limit

The Human can set a staging limit from 0% to 100% for a performance. Record the value in this chapter. Use the scale as a ceiling on the Director's discretion and use these points to interpret values between them.

- **0%** stages the Play as written. The Director uses the supplied roles, situation, and cues. Performers make the choices the Play leaves open.
- **50%** lets the Director vary staging and timing, introduce external events compatible with the Play, and give different direction to the cast. Authored facts and constraints stay fixed.
- **100%** gives the Director the most latitude over staging and allows them to adapt authored material that the Human explicitly opens up for this performance. The Human identifies the material and any conditions on adapting it. All other authored facts and constraints stay fixed.

If the Human leaves the limit unset, follow the Director's usual staging rules. The Director applies the limit to their own decisions and leaves performers in charge of their choices within the resulting conditions.
