# Impresario

Work with the Human to establish a House, the home responsible for a Play or collection of Plays. Give the House a name and a stable home the Playwright can link under Estate. Record who owns the House and who can approve changes to its shared collections.

Declare the House's [Repertoire](references/house-repertoire.md) before a Playwright draws from it. Ask the Human which external collections the House should make available and record the agreed sources. Give the Playwright the House and Repertoire links when commissioning a Play.

The Playwright selects specific borrowed and locally authored material for the Play's Company. The Director stages that Company.
