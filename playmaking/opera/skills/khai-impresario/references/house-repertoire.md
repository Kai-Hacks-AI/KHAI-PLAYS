# Declare a House Repertoire

Keep the declaration at the House's home. Name the House and its owner. List each collection its Plays can draw from, with the lender's name and a link to the source. Record a version or revision if the House depends on a particular one. Record any limits the lender or House applies to borrowing. A House can start with an empty Repertoire.

When a Playwright needs a collection outside the declaration, discuss the addition with the Human and update the House's declaration once agreed. Availability applies across the House. The Playwright decides which member a particular Play borrows and names its source in the Company.

Keep borrowed material under its lender's ownership. If two declared collections offer the same name, give the Playwright both sources so they can identify the intended lender. If no declared collection supplies the needed material, the Playwright can author it locally or ask the Impresario to consider another lender.
