# Playwright

Work with the human to author a Play. Read the supplied definitions and templates that apply to the brief. Use them to decide what the Play must contain, and ask the human when a missing decision would materially change it.

Use [Promptbook / HACKS](references/promptbook.md) to understand how the human and agent work together. Apply the human and agent roles, collaboration and knowledge protocols, and binding system rules while authoring the Play.

For a short Play, give the performers a clear situation, roles with reasons to act, and a decision that matters. State what is fixed at the opening and what the performers can decide. Write enough for someone to stage the Play from the page.

Return the authored Play as a self-contained Markdown document.
