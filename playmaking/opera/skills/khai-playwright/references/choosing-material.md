# Choosing material

| Reference | Why use it | What it is for | Where it fits |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Play · ENACTS](play.md) | Make several moments one telling. | The Arc, Company, Triggers, and Stakes of the whole work. | Links the Plots and names what the Company can draw on. |
| [Plot · TO CAST](plot.md) | Put the Company under pressure. | One situation with an open choice. | The Play’s Arc places it. Its actions draw on the Company. |
| [Persona · TO PAST](persona.md) | Give a performer a particular character. | What the character projects, does, hides, and gives away. | The Company casts the Persona. A Plot can give them a Position to hold. |
| [Position · TO HOLD](position.md) | Give a role standing and obligations. | What holding the role grants, demands, and costs. | A Persona can hold it across a Play. A Process can assign it temporarily. |
| [Place · TO SHOW](place.md) | Let the setting change what people can do. | What the cast can sense, find, use, or be denied. | A Plot stages the Company there, with the Pieces and Processes in reach. |
| [Piece · TO PLAY](piece.md) | Give an object weight in the action. | What the object affords or puts at stake. | A Place can hold it, a Persona can carry it, and a Process can use it. |
| [Process · TO IDLE](process.md) | Let a change unfold in play. | What starts the change, what drives it, and what it leaves behind. | A Plot sets the conditions, and the result can carry into the next Plot. |
| [Plan · TO DOIT](plan.md) | Give someone a course of action. | What a production, group, or individual aims to do and how they can assess it. | Attach it to whoever pursues it. Several Plans can compete in the same Plot. |
| [Pitch · TO TUNE](pitch.md) | Shape how the material is played or felt. | The tone, counter-current, and effect on the audience. | Link it where its tone applies. A Plot can change the Play’s Pitch. |
| [Promptbook](../../khai-director/references/promptbook.md) | Give the Director and cast conditions they can act on. | How the Human and agents collaborate, what they can know, and the rules in force. | The Director selects or writes it. A Play can link to it, and several Plays can use it. |
| [Performance · TO LOAN](../../khai-director/references/performance.md) | Bring something that happened into another Play. | A captured occasion, its consequences, and what remains open. | The Director captures it. The Playwright links the card in the receiving Play’s Triggers and states when it enters; the Promptbook governs who can learn what. |
| [Dramaturgy · TO GROW](../../../references/dramaturgy.md) | Bring a reusable Pattern into a Play. | What the Pattern offers and what the Play must supply. | Link the Pattern and author its requirements in the Play, Plot, or Company member that owns them. |
