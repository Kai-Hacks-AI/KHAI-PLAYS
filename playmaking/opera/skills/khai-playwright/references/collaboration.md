# Collaboration

Set how the human and agents work together, and how agents work with one another. Specify what makes someone respond and how they hand the scene over. In [Late Night Radio](https://github.com/ChBrain/KAIWorlds/blob/main/late_night_radio/instructions.md), the Persona who can carry the moment’s pressure picks up. In [Silence](https://github.com/ChBrain/KAIWorlds/blob/main/silence/instructions.md), a process determines when a Persona can speak.
