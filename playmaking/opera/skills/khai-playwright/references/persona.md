# Persona — TO PAST

Use these headings in order. Give the performer enough to act, then cut what they can supply in play.

## Taxonomy

Name the role this Persona fills. Link its Position if one exists.

## Owner

Name the project responsible for this Persona.

## Projection

Show what the Persona presents to the room. Make it concrete.

## Action

Give the Persona one deliberate move that gives someone else a cue.

## Shadow

Give the Persona a blind spot that conflicts with what they present in Projection. Make it specific enough to account for the Tell under pressure.

## Tell

Give one involuntary physical sign, distinct from the deliberate move in Action. It can surface in the same beat. Let others notice that something is off and draw their own conclusions.
