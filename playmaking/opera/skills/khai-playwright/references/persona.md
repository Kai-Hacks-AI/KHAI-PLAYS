# Persona — TO PAST

Use these headings in order. Give the performer enough to act, then cut what they can supply in play.

## Taxonomy

Name the role this Persona fills. Link its Position if one exists.

## Owner

Name the project responsible for this Persona.

## Projection

Show what the Persona presents to the room. Make it concrete.

## Action

Give the Persona one deliberate move that gives someone else a cue.

## Shadow

Name the blind spot that puts pressure on the Projection. The Persona can see it only in part, if at all.

## Tell

Give one involuntary physical sign that leaks when the Shadow is under pressure. Let others notice the sign and draw their own conclusions.
