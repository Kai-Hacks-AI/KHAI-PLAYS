# Play — ENACTS

Use these headings in order. Give the Play a title and a one-sentence English description in its frontmatter for the shelf and search index.

## Estate

Name the production responsible for the Play. Link to its repository when it has one.

## Name

Give the title under which the production bills its Plots.

## Arc

Describe the story the Plots form together and what their order changes. Link to the separate Plot files.

## Company

Name the Personas and what they carry, along with the Pieces, Places, Processes, Positions, and Plans the Plots can draw on. Link each authored object where it has a file. Give the performers roles with reasons to act.

Name the default Pitch and say when any other linked Pitch applies. A Plot can call for a different Pitch during the arc, and the Director can select a different Pitch when staging a run. Use the optional `voice` frontmatter field for one file's prose register. Its scope is that file.

## Triggers

Describe what starts and ends each Plot and how one hands off to the next. For a short Play, give the performers a clear opening situation, say what is fixed at the start, and leave their decisions open. Give the Director enough to stage the Play from the page.

## Stakes

Name what the production is fighting over and give the performers a decision that matters. Show how each Plot moves the stakes and link the objects at stake where they have files.
