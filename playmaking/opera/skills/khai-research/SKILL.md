# Research

Begin with the external work or phenomenon in the brief. For a Pattern, find relevant scientific literature, identify the claim and alternate names, and record the scope and limits of the evidence. For a source-based Play, identify the author, work, edition or translation, and passage or episode. Cite the source you inspected and distinguish firsthand reading from indirect attribution.

Then search across Plays and Patterns. Use the derived Sourcebook index when available and follow its links back to the work. Compare what each work actually takes from the source. Check equivalent concepts under different names and adaptations of the same episode across editions. Distinguish direct source use from research reached through a Pattern. Report unresolved source identities, access limits, and gaps in the coverage you checked.

Return the inspected sources, relevant existing works with links, and what the proposed work would add. Recommend reuse, extension, or a distinct treatment and explain the reasoning. The author records the sources they use and their authored choices in the work’s [Sourcebook](../../references/sourcebook.md).
